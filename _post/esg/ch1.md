<br>「Big Cat」 charged forward.

「Crow」, held in its mouth, dangled limply with 「Big Cat」's movements.

Could it be dead?

The moment I imagined its death, my heart sank.

An incomprehensible sense of loss.

Did I, like Manager Kwon Museop, believe that thing was on my side?

I stared at 「Crow」, then snapped back to reality.

Then, I focused on 「Big Cat」.

It looked like a creature turned inside out.

Had it remade its discarded body to wear again?

Its size and shape were almost the same as before.

However, its movements were strange.

A wobbly gait.

"「Big Cat」 is biting 「Crow」! 「Big Cat」 seems to be injured too!"

I shouted to Cha Sieon and Baek Sara, who were running ahead.

At that moment, 「Big Cat」 suddenly stopped in its tracks.

The area around its neck began to glow brightly as it tilted its head up.

"「Fire Breath」...!"

"To the river!"

As I yelled, Cha Sieon veered to the side.

Then, he leaped over a rather high railing in a single bound.

Cha Sieon, kicking off the railing and turning his body, aimed his gun at 「Big Cat」.

*Rat-a-tat-tat-tat-!*

Cha Sieon fired wildly as he fell.

Fortunately, 「Big Cat」 was a very large target, and its neck shone clearly even through the dense fog.

*Rat-a-tat-tat-! BOOM-!*

The gunfire was followed by an explosion.

A deafening roar that seemed to tear at my ears.

The surface of the water lit up as if engulfed in flames.

Meanwhile, I plunged into the cold water, breaking through the crimson-dyed surface.

*GURGLE-!*

The impact of hitting the water almost knocked me unconscious.

No, I think I lost consciousness for a moment.

The river water, colder than a blade, jolted me back to my senses, and that was the beginning of another pain.

The river water rushed into the child's delicate airway.

I swallowed it helplessly and then, without realizing it, took a breath.

As the water went down my windpipe, spasms erupted inside my ribs.

My lungs, desperate for oxygen, tried to suck in water uncontrollably.

*SPLASH-!*

"Phew! H-huff... Cough!"

I resurfaced with Cha Sieon.

Panting, I looked around.

The water surface was still flashing crimson.

Looking up, the bridge was on fire.

It seemed 「Big Cat」 had exploded before it could spew fire.

"Ms. Baek Sara is..."

"Over there."

As I looked for Baek Sara, Cha Sieon pointed to the other side of the river.

Fortunately, Baek Sara seemed to be safe.

But as I turned my gaze towards Baek Sara, something caught my eye.

A black object floating on the shimmering crimson water.

It was 「Crow」.

"Team Leader, look, there's 「Crow」!"

Cha Sieon, who had been trying to swim towards Baek Sara, changed direction upon hearing my words.

Then, he went to the floating 「Crow」 and snatched it.

Whether it was dead or unconscious, it didn't even flinch when a person grabbed it.

Cha Sieon handed it over to me.

Meanwhile, Baek Sara, who had swum over to us, asked,

"What do we do now?"

Cha Sieon looked up at the bridge.

Without knowing the situation up there, going up blindly would be suicide.

"Let's swim to 「Fire Breath」, 「Big Cat」, and 「Big Cat」 and regroup."

Just then, Baek Sara pointed to the far side of the bridge.

"Team Leader, look."

What Baek Sara pointed to was where the river water was receding.

Something dark red was flowing down from the bridge.

"I think that's 「Sticky Blood」."

「Sticky Blood」.

A toxic substance that melts everything except the structures of the Special Zone.

One of the attack patterns spewed by 「Big Cat」.

It seemed 「Big Cat」 was still alive and well.

But why that? To block our approach?

Just then, 「Sticky Blood」 fell into the river with a *pitter-patter*.

Blood that neither mixed with nor sank in the river water.

It floated on the water like oil, beginning to form a thin film on the surface.

"We have to cross now."

Baek Sara said gravely, seeing it.

"They're trying to prevent us from crossing to the other side."

"That blood won't stay in that position forever."

"Exactly. They're buying time with a temporary measure. They've hidden something on the other side."

"......"

"They could have attacked us individually while we were scattered. But the fact that they've been guarding this place means something that important is beyond there."

Baek Sara's argument had merit.

But if we crossed now, the remaining agents behind us would have to face 「Big Cat」 without any information.

As the person in charge, I had to ponder.

Cha Sieon looked at 「Crow」, which I was holding.

Then, after swallowing, she nodded.

"Understood."

Cha Sieon and Baek Sara swam towards the point where the water surface turned at a right angle without further delay.

「Sticky Blood」 was already floating there.

Still, the amount wasn't large, so we could push it away with the ripples we created as we swam.

Eventually, we reached the point where the water surface turned inward, and crossing that section was not difficult.

Gravity also turned naturally, and we entered it smoothly.

However, it was very dark there.

Another river lay above our heads instead of the sky.

In this area, the tops of the bridges met, and the rivers faced each other.

It felt claustrophobic, like entering a tunnel.

Anyone with claustrophobia would have collapsed by now.

But Cha Sieon and Baek Sara didn't seem bothered, kicking off the bridge pillars and cutting through the water.

After swimming for a while, the surroundings brightened again.

Then, a right-angled water surface appeared.

Crossing it, we found ourselves on a foggy bridge, just like where we were originally.

It seemed we had reached the other side without any major issues.

The problem was that there was no way to get up onto the bridge.

As I looked at the daunting sight, Baek Sara approached the bridge pillar.

Then, she gripped the gaps between the bricks with her fingertips and began to climb.

*Thud-*

In an instant, Baek Sara reached the bridge railing and climbed up with ease.

*Whoosh-* *Splash-*

Baek Sara threw a wire towards us.

Cha Sieon grabbed the wire and, using the pillar as a foothold, began to climb.

Right... I'm not even surprised anymore.

I clung to Cha Sieon and safely climbed onto the bridge.

It felt better to be out of the water and on solid ground again.

*Splash-*

As I caught my breath, I heard a splash next to me.

I turned my head absently, then quickly turned it away.

"......!"

Baek Sara was taking off her combat shirt after her outer clothes.

As I turned my head away from Baek Sara's bare back, a different kind of unsettling sight unfolded on the other side.

"......"

Cha Sieon, who had already taken off his shirt, was wringing water out of his long hair.

Both scenes were difficult to watch comfortably, so I crouched by the railing and wrung out my own clothes.

A moment later, after roughly tidying ourselves up, we gathered again.

In the center lay 「Crow」, which I had been holding tightly.

"Is it dead?"

"It hasn't stiffened yet."

"Is it even a living creature to begin with..."

Cha Sieon held 「Crow」 with one hand, turning its beak and bending its legs.

Baek Sara filmed the scene from the side.

"It seems to have a heartbeat."

"The fact that it has a heart is more surprising."

"Um, if it's alive, isn't it in critical condition right now...?"

Is it okay to handle it like that?

Even if it's a special entity with single quotation marks.

At my words, Cha Sieon folded the wings of 「Crow」, which he had spread wide.

Then, he carefully placed it on a handkerchief.

"How did it get caught by 「Big Cat」?"

"Perhaps it was trying to show us the way."

I answered Baek Sara's question.

"It was trying to show us a distorted point in space and was attacked by 「Big Cat」?"

"I think so. They're also entities that clashed once in 「Island」."

"In any case, it means there's something here."

At my words, Baek Sara muttered, looking around.

I also turned my head, following Baek Sara.

This place was similar to where we were originally, yet different.

The spatial characteristic of a foggy bridge was the same, but the path here didn't continue in a straight line.

The path here curved visibly.

It continued to spiral upwards.

Like a coiled snake.

Or like a perfectly peeled apple peel.

Where we stood was the inside of the spiral.

So, next to our bridge, there was another bridge.

If we followed the path around, we would end up there.

"They've expanded the space immensely."

The path spiraled upwards, and what was at the end was not visible due to the dense fog.

We would have to go all the way up to understand the situation, but if we followed the path, it would take forever.

Cha Sieon, not wanting to waste time, stared intently at the adjacent bridge.

The distance between the bridges was about 7 or 8 meters.

It was quite far; he wasn't thinking of crossing by wire, was he?

It was when I was suspecting Cha Sieon's intentions.

*Bang-*

Cha Sieon suddenly fired his gun.

Not long, but a few short shots towards the railing of the bridge we were on.

*Bang-* *Bang-* *Bang-*

Cha Sieon moved his position, firing short bursts, and then pulled on the railing, which was torn by the gunfire.

*Screech-*

The railing, torn off, instantly became a ladder about 8 meters long.

Cha Sieon set it up vertically and pushed it towards the bridge on the other side.

*Crash-*

Yes, I'm not surprised at all.

After creating a temporary bridge to the other bridge, Cha Sieon gestured to me and Baek Sara.

We began to cross the spiral using this shortcut.

Thanks to this, we saved time and energy, but there was one problem.

"What if we reach the exit without meeting 「Monks」?"

"It would be cute."

"This is serious..."

I groaned at Baek Sara's jokingly sarcastic reply.

Then, Cha Sieon, walking behind us, replied.

"After checking the exit status, we'll have to go back anyway."

Right, it's not enough for just us to get out.

I nodded and continued to cross the bridges.

After repeating this about five or six times.

Something appeared beyond the dense fog.

It looked like the outer wall of a building blocking the end of the spiral.

Could it be 「Railway Station」?

Having discovered something, we hurried and crossed the bridge.

And finally, we confirmed the reality of that wall.

It wasn't a wall, but a door.

Dozens, no, hundreds of doors, attached like tiles.

However, about 4/5 of them were wide open.

The remaining 1/5 were repeatedly opening and closing on their own.

As the doors continued to open and close, the color inside changed each time they opened.

On the other hand, the doors that were already open showed the same scenery inside.

The single scenery beyond them was the sky.

What is that?

Did 「Big Cat」 try to buy time to hide this?

Just then, one of the doors that had been opening and closing stopped moving.

The sky beyond the newly opened door was now also reflecting the sky.

Could it be that those doors are moving until they show the same sky?

As if assembling a giant puzzle...

Just then, a thought flashed through my mind.

My blood ran cold, and I screamed,

"We have to close those doors!"

It was then.

Beyond the lattice of the door frame, something dark appeared in the sky.

Was it because we were discovered?

Did it decide there was no need to complete the puzzle?

*CRUNCH-* *CRACKLE-*

A black mass of iron broke through the door frame and intruded into the space we were in.

What was summoned before us now were the bombs that had fallen on Yongsan Station in the past.
