<br>The first thing that came to mind was a single word.

*'Why?'*

CEO Cha was unpredictable, but fundamentally, he was cold and calculating. If he had decided to clear the game with a Normal Ending instead of the True Ending, he would have acted precisely according to that goal.

Killing off a few annoying NPCs along the way would be one thing, but he wasn't the type to commit such an inefficient act as a total massacre.

For a moment, I was so stunned that I couldn't even think. This wasn't the CEO Cha I knew.

My mind filled with questions, but now wasn't the time to dwell on them.

I had to find CEO Cha.

Though his actions were insane, his movements themselves were calm.

In fact, CEO Cha was moving stealthily.

If Kwak Hanmuk hadn't diligently leveled up his Prison Guard Ability, or if the System Window hadn't informed me of the situation...

...Then I would have met my end in my sleep without ever knowing why.

*'The Warden's endings include one where all the Creatures are exterminated.'*

I tried to guess CEO Cha's train of thought.

If CEO Cha had decided to kill all the NPCs, how would he have moved?

There was no reason to kill the prison guards first.

His role was the Warden, and he held absolute authority within the prison.

If he wanted to, he could have summoned all the guards and dealt with them in one go.

Yet, if he had gone through the trouble of killing them, he must have targeted the guards guarding the Prison Automaton.

His first target was likely the Creatures of Zone X.

He would have wanted to deal with the most troublesome ones first.

The moment I realized this, a sense of foreboding washed over me.

Orca was still recovering from the gunshot wound he'd sustained today and couldn't fight properly. The other Creatures were in no better shape, all weakened.

No, even at their peak, they wouldn't have stood a chance against Representative Cha.

Recalling the sight of Daeil Yeorae slashing through the Creatures, I spoke urgently.

"Representative Cha is trying to kill all the NPCs right now. We need to go to the Prison Automaton."

I'd blurted it out without any explanation, but Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk didn't ask any questions.

*Clang.*

The sound of metal echoed.

It was Je Hyun-oh unfurling the wings he'd been storing.

The moment the six wings filled the cramped Medical Officer's Quarters—

*CRACK!*

The stone floor beneath Je Hyun-oh's feet shattered.

*K-BOOM!*

A black figure burst through the wall of the Medical Officer's Quarters.

A massive hole was blasted through the wall of the quarters.

The dark figure, its metallic wings unfurled, shot into the air like a projectile. At the same moment, Kwak Hanmuk grabbed me.

He wrapped his prayer beads around me and leapt through the hole in the wall.

We were three stories up, but that was no problem. He flung the prayer beads out like a rope, snagging them on nearby structures to cushion our fall.

The moment we hit the ground, Kwak Hanmuk and I sprinted toward the Prison Automaton.

As we ran, a sudden chill ran down my spine.

Dominic had been too quiet.

I had been committing a buffet's worth of things he despised, yet he hadn't said a word.

Dominic, in particular, hated it when his subordinates were disrespectful.

By his standards, Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk were openly defying me.

He should have at least said something, yet he remained silent.

I glanced at my shoulder.

The Pudding Slime was stuck firmly, not budging even with my rough movements.

I was about to try calling it with the strange voice I'd just learned, but Dominic noticed first and spoke.

"Subordinates can hear their master's voice."

I immediately shut my mouth. Kwak Hanmuk was right beside me—I'd almost made a mistake.

*'So those I've registered can hear that voice.'*

It seemed that becoming a subordinate moved them slightly beyond Dominic's classification of "mere creatures."

"Of course, they don't hear *every* voice, but you'd better be careful, Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

He meant that since I was still young, I didn't know how to distinguish my voices yet.

I'd only just gained the ability to use that voice.

With no way to tell them apart, I kept my mouth shut.

Then, I asked him in my mind why he was being so quiet.

I was certain that a quick-witted Slime would surely answer.

After I kept glancing at him, the Pudding Slime finally spoke.

"I'm always watching over you, so you don't need to worry."

The words "always by your side" were gentle.

But that wasn't the answer I wanted.

I wanted to know why he hadn't said anything, and what he had been thinking while silently watching me.

However, the Pudding Slime didn't speak again.

A strange sense of unease crept over me.

But given the circumstances, I had no choice but to endure the Pudding Slime's silence.

The Prison Automaton wasn't far, so we arrived quickly.

By the time I treated Orca, finished my remaining duties as a Medical Officer, and returned to the Medical Officer's Quarters, it was already evening.

It was well past 6:00 PM, and the hour was closer to night. The area in front of the Prison Automaton was deserted.

Aside from the occasional sweep of a distant searchlight, there was only pitch-black darkness and heavy silence.

In that chillingly quiet space, two men stood facing each other.

The first thing that caught my eye was the man holding a sword that glowed white even in the dark.

At CEO Cha's feet lay a prison guard, bleeding neon green liquid.

The body still twitched slightly, as if it had only just been killed.

It seemed CEO Cha had killed the guard in front of the Prison Automaton and was trying to enter when he ran into Je Hyun-oh.

He probably never expected to be discovered so quickly.

*'I have to stop him here.'*

I might still be able to persuade CEO Cha.

I called out to him.

"Representative Cha."

The white mask, which had been staring at Je Hyun-oh, slowly turned its gaze toward me.

I had spent so much time with CEO Cha in the game. I thought I knew him well, but now it felt like I was facing a stranger.

Perhaps it was because he was acting so completely out of character.

*'Could he really be doing this because of me?'*

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"Because you don't want to regret it—you start trembling over whether a mere NPC might die."</span>

In our last conversation, CEO Cha had shown clear displeasure at how I was being manipulated by the NPC.

If he had decided to kill all the NPCs because of that aversion, the logic would almost hold up.

But for that to be true, the premise itself had to be valid.

I would have to be more than just useful to him—I would have to be someone who truly mattered.

I knew that.

The only reason I was able to break through CEO Cha's distrust of people and enter his inner circle in the game was because it was a special space where we had repeated our encounters a thousand times.

But here, the CEO Cha from the game—the one who had been with me a thousand times—doesn't exist.

The man before me is the CEO Cha I met before I had earned his trust. A man I'd only seen for a few hours at most.

In reality, we could never achieve that level of trust or understanding.

Yet, CEO Cha's strange behavior kept making my mind wander.

*'What if he remembers the game?'*

No, that's impossible. If he remembered, he wouldn't be leaving me alone.

And the real-life version of him shouldn't have the memories of being a game NPC anyway...

A new thought seized me.

*'Then what is CEO Cha, the man before my eyes?'*

I looked at CEO Cha with a strange sense of unfamiliarity.

What did he feel from my gaze?

For a moment, it seemed as if CEO Cha, even behind the mask, wavered.

Just as I slowly closed and opened my eyes, before I could even say a word, Kwak Hanmuk couldn't hold back and cursed.

"Fuck, CEO Cha. What the hell are you doing right now?"

A vein bulged on his forehead, showing just how pissed off he was.

"If we entered because of Trainee Han, you should have just quietly supported us. Now you're fucking around trying to get a True Ending."

But CEO Cha didn't even spare Kwak Hanmuk a glance.

He kept his gaze fixed on me, as if waiting to see what I would say.

And I knew instinctively.

In this moment, no matter what offer I made to CEO Cha, it would be useless.

No matter how groundbreaking or brilliant the proposal, he wouldn't be persuaded.

I slowly opened my mouth.

"I'm going to clear this with the True Ending."

"Do whatever you want," CEO Cha replied, his voice so cold it sent a chill through me. "Because I'll be doing whatever I want, too."

Just then—

*Blink.*

The light on Je Hyun-oh's goggles flickered on.

CEO Cha and Kwak Hanmuk both stared at Je Hyun-oh with identical expressions of utter disgust.

Je Hyun-oh called out CEO Cha's name.

"CEO Cha."

The metallic wings fluttered once, slowly.

The moment the smooth, metallic sound ceased, a low, rough voice spoke.

"You talk too much."

*Bang!*

A powerful impact sound immediately exploded.

*Screech, screech.* A menacing sound echoed from the white sword that had blocked Je Hyun-oh's metallic hand.

The white and black figures flung themselves apart as if shaking each other off, only to clash again.

In the span of just a few seconds, an immeasurably fierce exchange of blows took place.

The trajectory of the pure white light moved elegantly, blocking the encroaching darkness.

While the white lines were drawn so chaotically they were hard to follow with the eye, the black lines also violently slashed through the air.

The black streak, which had soared into the sky, plummeted rapidly. Upon colliding with the white one, a thunderous roar shook the surroundings.

At that same moment, the metallic wings disintegrated into blades, blanketing the sky.

The dense swarm of black blades was nearly invisible against the darkness, but they glinted menacingly whenever they caught the white light.

With a piercing shriek, the blades tore through the air and rained down.

The white sword easily deflected the black blades.

However, it couldn't completely evade the metallic hand hidden among them.

*Screeeech!*

The sound of metal grinding against metal erupted.

Beast-like claw marks were left on CEO Cha's Cognitive Decline mask.

"..."

CEO Cha, who had leaped back, touched his mask and let out a short, dry laugh.

Je Hyun-oh's metal hand creaked.

I didn't even need to open the Status Window to know his current state.

Je Hyun-oh looked utterly thrilled.

I swallowed dryly as I watched him gradually become consumed by the euphoria of battle.

"Captain Kwak, we need to evacuate the Creatures."

I didn't think Je Hyun-oh would lose.

But CEO Cha wouldn't just stand by, either. If they both started fighting recklessly, the surrounding area would be reduced to a wasteland.

The Creatures could be killed in the crossfire.

"Damn it... Let's go."

Kwak Hanmuk cursed as he opened the door to the Prison Automaton.

I entered the Prison Automaton with Kwak Hanmuk.

The interior was a scene of utter chaos.

Human inmates clung to the iron bars, screaming insults and threats.

"Open the doors right now!"

"You bastards, open the doors!"

The unsettling sounds from outside were shaking the very building, and the inmates were on the verge of rioting.

But it didn't matter. They had no way of opening the prison doors.

The prison guards would be here soon, and they would deal with the inmates themselves.

As I ran down the long corridor, I thought, *'Can I even get the True Ending if I save the Creatures?'* This time, I truly lacked confidence.

With a heavy heart, I entered Zone X.

There, Kwak Hanmuk and I ran into an unexpected figure.

The Security Chief was standing motionless inside Zone X.

It wasn't as if he had any precognitive abilities, and there was no reason for him to come straight to Zone X when trouble broke out so suddenly.

The air in Zone X was chilling. The damp, gloomy energy that clung to us was downright unpleasant.

The creatures were all awake, yet they held their breath, not even stirring.

Something was wrong.

The Security Chief was a being the creatures feared, but not to this extent.

Right now, they were acting as if... an ultimate apex predator stood before them.

I tried to approach the Security Chief, but a hand gripped my shoulder.

"..."

It was Kwak Hanmuk.

But he wasn't looking at me.

His gray eyes, frozen with tension, were fixed on the Security Chief.

His hand on my shoulder trembled slightly.

Kwak Hanmuk was not the type to get nervous over just anything. For him to react this way...

It was a tension laced with fear, as if he had just encountered an old nightmare.

I felt strangely detached, as if I were the only one out of place.

Suddenly, a low chuckle reached my ears.

Dominic was laughing.

Then, the Security Chief, who had been standing as still as a statue, slowly turned around.

He looked at me.

Stunned by this incomprehensible sequence of events, I hesitated, unsure of how to react to the Security Chief.

*'Should I attack him right away? Or should I just greet him first and read the situation?'*

As I weighed my options, the grip on my shoulder tightened even further.

"Captain Kwak...?"

Just as I was about to panic at his clearly abnormal state, the Security Chief removed his metal mask.

His hidden eyes were revealed, but they weren't eyes at all. Instead of irises and pupils, there was only a single, solid neon green glow.

The Security Chief's lips slowly curved into a smile.

Then, he spoke.

*"Roasted…Chestnut."*

When I didn't answer, he tilted his head and called out another name.

*"Little... lamb?"*

An overwhelming joy, so intense it felt like my head would burst, washed over me.

Feeling my heart race, I shouted,

"Mr. Octopus Leg!"
