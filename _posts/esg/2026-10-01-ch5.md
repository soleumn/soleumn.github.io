<br>"Hah...!"

I took a deep breath and opened my eyes.

What came into my gradually sharpening view was the waiting room of 「Railway Station」.

Did I make it back...?

I steadied my breathing and looked around.

In the vast waiting room, I was alone.

And the glass ceiling, still perfectly holding up the sky.

It was peaceful, as if nothing had happened at all.

I stared blankly at the restored 「Railway Station」 before snapping back to my senses.

This was no time to be distracted.

First, I had to find a door.

Fortunately, there was a suitable door right next to the ticket booth.

A frosted glass door that the ticket booth staff would use.

That should do.

I walked over and opened it right away.

At that moment.

*Vroooom—! CRASH—!*

A bike charging from inside slammed into the glass door.

"Aaaah...!"

"......!"

A loud scream followed.

As I stood frozen beside the door, having nearly been caught in the rush, the bike that had burst into the waiting room swerved sharply and came to a stop.

*SKREEEECH—!*

The bike tilted as if it were about to tip over, then stopped dead.

Two men in combat uniforms sat on it.

"What the—what now! Is this 「Railway Station」?"

The man shouting noisily was none other than Jeong Jinu.

And the man sitting behind him, scanning their surroundings, was Han Seungyo.

"Is it Yongsan Station? Is it 「Railway Station」? What the hell is going on?!"

Jeong Jinu, practically snapping, turned his head.

Then his eyes went wide when he saw me.

"Agent Doun?"

"Yes. Are you both unharmed?"

Giving up on the broken door, I walked over to where the two had stopped their bike.

"We're not hurt, but... did we die?"

Jeong Jinu asked urgently.

I smiled, glad to see him, and answered.

"No. You're not dead."

"Then what happened? Wait, why are you alone, Agent Doun?"

Jeong Jinu looked around again, and upon realizing there was no one here but me, he frowned slightly.

Then, with a surprisingly serious face, he said.

"Got it."

"This is a senior's heart, Sara."

"Is this really Agent Otter...?"

I smiled again at Jeong Jinu's muttering.

Then I spoke to the two, who were still confused.

"A moment ago, the bomb that came out of 「Yongsan Station」 fell onto 「Great Bridge」."

"Whoa, so we weren't seeing things?"

"No. It really happened."

"But the fact that we came out here means..."

"We used the characteristics of a nascent-phase Special Zone to call you here. You were thirteen seconds from death, agents."

"Like the Director?"

"Yes."

"So we did die...?"

"But you're alive now."

At my answer, Jeong Jinu's expression went blank.

He looked like he had a lot to say, but I didn't have the time to explain in detail right now.

"I'll explain everything at once once everyone's gathered. We still have people to find, so please give the others a brief explanation as they come out."

At my words, Jeong Jinu set aside his questions and nodded.

I looked around for a new door to replace the one Jeong Jinu had smashed.

A glass door wouldn't work—something might come charging out of it again like just now.

That was when a steel door caught my eye.

Yeah, that should do.

I walked over to the steel door and opened it.

Then, one by one, the agents who had died inside began to come out.

"Seo Doun?"

"Didn't your leg just get crushed?"

"Park Jeonghu died..."

"I'm right here, you know?"

"Ahh, fuck!"

Fortunately, the only one who came out on a bike was Jeong Jinu, so there were no further incidents.

Before long, everyone who had been caught in the bombing on the Ring side of 「Great Bridge」 had come out.

Now, all that remained were Baek Sara, who had died fighting on the Spiral side, and Cha Sieon.

Those two were a different story.

I quietly swallowed.

Then I said to the agents bustling around behind me.

"It's better if you all keep your distance."

After warning the agents, I leaned my back against the wall beside the door.

Then, stretching out only my arm, I opened the door.

*CLANG—!*

Sure enough.

The moment the door opened, the sound of a powerful strike rang out.

"......"

Even though I had braced myself, the sound sent a chill down my spine.

At the same time, the steel door burst open as if it were about to be knocked off its hinges.

My wrist nearly got caught and broken along with it.

The one who appeared next was Baek Sara.

"......?"

Baek Sara, as if she had just struck the steel door with her sword, stood with one foot forward, the tip of her blade lowered.

"Agent Otter?"

Baek Sara's sharp eyes swept the area before landing on me.

Then she tilted her head and added.

"An adult...?"

For some reason, Baek Sara seemed more surprised that I was an adult than anything else.

As Baek Sara took in my appearance, I too carefully examined hers.

Fortunately, she didn't seem to be injured.

She was breathing naturally.

I couldn't check her pulse directly anymore, but it was probably beating smoothly too.

Relieved by that, I smiled.

Then I muttered idly.

"So much for being cute."

"......?"

Baek Sara still didn't seem to understand the situation.

Normally, she would have fired right back at my joke, but now she just blinked.

I looked at her for a moment, then spoke.

"Thank you."

"Huh?"

"You probably don't remember, but you risked your life to save me, Ms. Baek Sara."

"I did?"

At my words, Baek Sara's eyes went wide.

As if she couldn't believe she had done such a thing.

But denying it wouldn't change anything.

Because I had seen it with my own eyes.

"Yes. Thanks to you, all the other agents survived too."

"Well... that's a relief."

Baek Sara spotted the agents gathered at the far end of the waiting room and muttered.

Then, noticing one person missing, she asked again.

"What about Team Leader Cha?"

"Now we have to save him. Team Leader Cha is the last one."

"Team Leader Cha died too?"

Baek Sara asked, even more shocked than before.

Her expression was one of utter disbelief this time, so I let out a wry laugh again.

Yeah, I understand.

When someone you'd never expect to die suddenly dies, it's truly baffling.

I empathized fully with Baek Sara's shock and explained.

"We drove off 「Big Cat」 and 「Alpha」. But then a 「River Crosser」 appeared again, so he stayed behind to send me off."

"Ah......"

Baek Sara nodded, finally understanding.

"It seems like they come back in the order they died."

At my added explanation, Baek Sara thought for a moment, then asked.

"Who came back first?"

"Agents Jeong Jinu and Han Seungyo."

"Ah."

Baek Sara nodded again.

This time, her face showed no surprise at all.

I smiled once more at Baek Sara, whose trust and distrust were both so firmly set.

Then I asked her, like the other agents, to keep her distance.

Now, the last one.

It was Team Leader Cha Sieon's turn.

When the door opens, the dead return to their state from thirteen seconds before death.

The rule I had confirmed at 「Hotel」 was being applied here identically.

It wasn't the work of 「Crow」.

That thing was out cold right now, and some other being was helping.

Anyway, that was why Jeong Jinu had crashed through the door with his bike, and Baek Sara had struck the door with her sword.

So what about Cha Sieon, thirteen seconds before his death?

That would be the moment he was firing his gun wildly.

And his injuries had been severe too.

"There might be gunfire. Everyone stay alert, and get first aid ready."

At my words, the agents nodded.

I closed the steel door that Baek Sara had struck with her sword.

Then I leaned my back against the wall and carefully turned the doorknob.

*Click.*

"......"

There was no sound at all.

I had expected gunfire to ring out immediately.

A sense of foreboding washed over me as I pulled the door open.

And I came face to face with—

Cha Sieon's back, standing straight.

Cha Sieon was looking at something with his back to the door.

"Team Leader Cha."

Only at my call did Cha Sieon turn his head.

His expression was calm.

And his hair had been cut short again, down to his shoulders.

At the same time, he was still covered in blood.

His appearance was strangely jumbled, so I asked.

"Are you alright?"

"I just ran into 「Monks」."

"Here?"

"Yes. As the aging disappeared, the injuries I had just sustained also vanished."

The voice in which Cha Sieon said this was oddly stiff.

No—that was probably just his usual way of speaking.

What I had briefly grown accustomed to was the trial version of Cha Sieon from eight years later.

"So you're fine now?"

"Yes."

Thank goodness.

I'd been wondering how I was going to get a critically wounded man out of here, but he'd even recovered on his own.

I felt a little grateful to that woman.

"Give me a situation report."

Cha Sieon said as he stepped out into the waiting room.

He must have realized that he had died and come back to life.

I smiled weakly at Cha Sieon's dry demeanor.

Cha Sieon glanced at me.

But I pretended not to notice and spoke.

"After we split up, I also ran into 「Monks」. And then I arrived here."

I decided to just omit the fact that I had died.

And it was impossible to even mention the woman I had met.

If I couldn't explain it properly, bringing it up would just lead to endless rambling.

So I quickly got to the point.

"Our guess was right. All the agents who died have returned."

Cha Sieon swept his gaze over the agents, including Baek Sara, and nodded.

Then he looked around the waiting room and asked.

"Is the 「Giant Siren」 taken care of?"

Cha Sieon had immediately noticed that this wasn't the ordinary Yongsan Station but the 「Railway Station」 Special District.

But since no siren was ringing, he must have found it strange.

"No. This is a section with no entities."

"What do you mean?"

"First, we need to get out of here."

"......?"

"We don't have much time, Team Leader. If we want to save the agents, we need to leave right now."

Cha Sieon looked at me as if expecting an explanation.

So I had no choice but to lie.

"I'll explain in detail once we're out."

Cha Sieon's eyes narrowed.

This guy and his uselessly sharp instincts.

I kept my face perfectly straight under his suspicious gaze and said.

"You said the top priority of this operation was everyone returning alive. For now, we need to get out."

In principle, I was right.

Right now, this was an extremely dangerous Special District, where strange traits were tangled together, recreating even the bombing.

Getting out was the urgent priority.

"All agents, attention."

In the end, Cha Sieon called the agents instead of pressing me further.

"Due to unexpected variables, we're pulling out for now."

No one objected.

They had all been wiped out once and brought back to life, so saving their own lives came first.

"But how do we get out?"

The agents asked, though.

This was 「Railway Station」, but it wasn't the one we knew.

No siren rang, and no 「Railway Police」 appeared.

So there was no way the escape method would work the same as before.

That was why I answered the agents who asked.

"Same as before—take the train from the platform."

"Is that still valid?"

"Yes, it's valid for now. But we don't know how long it'll stay open, so we need to hurry."

The agents didn't doubt my words.

After receiving our tickets, we all headed to the platform together.

As we ran toward the platform, I felt a strange sensation.

Memories of running down this path with Lee Sol overlapped with the present.

It was a similar yet different situation.

No—actually, it was the exact same situation.

A moment later, when we arrived at the platform, a train was waiting for us.

The agents all boarded the train with their tickets.

I got on first and counted the agents as they came through the entrance.

And when Cha Sieon boarded last, I watched him walk deeper into the train, then called out to him.

"Team Leader Cha."

Cha Sieon turned only his head to look at me.

I hesitated for a moment, wondering what to say.

But then I thought—why bother saying anything to someone who does well on his own?

So I smiled wryly and muttered.

"Please discard my will."

"What—"

I took a step back and got off the train.

Cha Sieon's eyes went wide.

He turned his body as if to grab me, and I closed the train door.

*Clack.*

Cha Sieon's final expression was quite fierce, but I didn't care.

The moment the train door closed, the space that had been barely held together collapsed like shattered glass.
