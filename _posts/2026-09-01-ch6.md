The true ending of The Cell X is the ‘true freedom’ of the creatures.

There is one prerequisite for this: the creatures must kill all the prison guards.

All twelve creatures had to participate in the slaughter of the guards.

This was because they needed to reclaim and absorb the Neo flowing through the guards' bodies. However, it was no easy feat for the creatures, weakened by long periods of incarceration, to kill guards who possessed enhanced bodies.

Since some individuals among the creatures lacked the capacity for killing, it was difficult to execute the plan through sheer force alone.

In the game, I used a method of dealing with the guards by utilizing Neo.

I would force an overdose of Neo extracted from the creatures into the guards, turning them into ‘dregs.’

Once they became dregs, it was much easier for the creatures to absorb the Neo.

*‘It was effective against the regular guards.’*

The Security Chief, however, seemed to have a strong adaptability to Neo; he didn't turn into dregs even after an overdose.

Therefore, I had dealt with him in a different manner.

Regardless, I needed to verify if the methods I attempted in the game would work in reality.

As it happened, I found myself collecting Neo while only one guard was following me.

It was a perfect situation for a test.

I stopped in front of Solitary Cell No. 2. I swallowed a smile that threatened to surface.

It was because No. 2 and No. 3 were stuck together.

Born from the same root, their solitary cells were connected.

They had to stay together to survive.

No. 2 and No. 3, consisting of fluffy white balls of fur like cotton candy, looked at me with large, black eyes.

Two antennae, perched on their heads like ribbons, tilted curiously.

Then, static electricity began to crackle across their fur.

No. 2 and No. 3 tucked their antennae into their fur and began to jump frantically.

They bounced off the walls, the floor, and in every direction, rolling like rubber balls.

As electricity sparked everywhere, the guard behind me looked on with disgust.

“What the hell is wrong with those bastards again?”

*‘They’re cute, though.’*

I countered the guard internally as I took out insulating gloves from my medical bag and put them on.

Then, I gently slid my hand through the bars.

No. 2 and No. 3, who had been bouncing mindlessly, came to a dead stop. They popped their antennae back out and hopped toward me.

I stroked No. 2 and No. 3 with both hands. At that, they both melted and became docile.

I looked back at the guard and spoke.

“Please open the door.”

The guard opened the cell door with a look of apprehension.

He looked ready to bash them with his baton the moment the creatures showed the slightest sign of resistance.

However, No. 2 and No. 3 were as gentle as could be.

A metallic clank sounded behind my back as the iron bars closed.

I didn't pay it much mind and crouched down in front of No. 2 and No. 3.

They were small individuals with relatively simple structures.

I only needed to check the antennae on their heads and the core in their center.

As I checked to see if they had been injured during yesterday's jailbreak, I noticed one of the antennae was slightly bent.

I gently straightened it with my hand and then wrote 'Normal' on the patient record chart.

“I’ll come back again.”

As I stood up, No. 2 and No. 3 clung to my pants, hindering my movement.

Seeing their antennae twitch, I couldn't help but let out a small laugh and stroked them both once more.

In my heart, I wanted to play with them, but there were still many patients left for my rounds.

After managing to detach No. 2 and No. 3, I headed toward Cell No. 12.

Rather than visiting in numerical order, it was more efficient to visit based on the characteristics of the individuals, so I planned to treat No.12 first.

Having seen the sight of No. 12 bound in chains, my steps naturally quickened as I sought out the solitary cell.

No. 12's cell was exceptionally dark.

It was already dim, but a translucent curtain had been drawn over the small window.

The moment I stepped inside the cell, something sticky clung to the soles of my feet.

It was the remnant of a spiderweb.

“…!”

My body was suddenly yanked upward and slammed against something.

Caught in a radial web stretched across the solitary cell, I struggled to let my eyes adjust to the darkness.

*Scritch, scratch*. I heard the sound of sharp, jointed legs approaching.

Soon, a woman with long, waving hair appeared before me.

No. 12 had the lower body of a spider and the upper body of a human female; her eyes consisted only of black pupils, without any whites.

With clear eyes, she looked at me, smiled, and reached out with a human arm.

A hand made of soft skin, not a spider's hard exoskeleton, caressed my cheek.

I involuntarily shuddered at her chilly body temperature.

And then, she suddenly… quite suddenly…

Kissed my cheek….

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Affection has reached 100!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

“Pa-Patient!”

Startled, I struggled within the web, and No. 12 laughed with her mouth open.

Her voice consisted of a song.

Listening to the clear melody, my face flushed hot.

There had been an event like this in the game.

It was an event where she would greet you with a kiss on the cheek once the affection level reached a certain point.

But that was a game, and this was reality.

I tried to assert my dignity as a medical officer.

“I need to examine you, so you mustn't do this. Please let me down.”

Even if she didn't understand the language, she should have known what I wanted, but No. 12 pretended not to and continued to sing.

While doing so, she stroked me and even kissed the top of my head.

After being kissed several times while completely immobilized, I let out a whimper.

“Patient No. 12….”

Only then did No. 12 pull me out of the web and set me down on the floor.

I quickly performed the examination before she could play around again.

I turned on my flashlight and meticulously checked each joint of her legs and her exoskeleton.

I had worried she might have been seriously injured while being forcibly dragged up in chains yesterday, but seeing her play like this, she seemed to have mostly recovered.

There were no visible injuries.

After barely coaxing and soothing her as she kept trying to wrap me in webs and sing, I exited Cell No. 12.

“You've got a strong stomach.”

The guard, standing some distance away, looked at me with a disgusted expression.

I replied with a professional business smile.

“It’s my job.”

Then, for some reason, a notification suddenly popped up.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Affection +3</strong>
  </div>
</div>

…Why is the affection increasing?

I gave up on trying to understand.

The next place to examine was Solitary Cell No. 6.

Standing before Cell No. 6, I looked at Orca beyond the bars.

Orca was sitting quietly in a chair.

He had been staring blankly up at the palm-sized window, but he turned his head upon sensing my presence.

A hint of gladness flickered in his eyes, but he didn't make it obvious.

This was because the guard was right nearby to open the cell door.

Orca waited for me in silence, maintaining an expressionless face.

The guard opened the door and stepped back again.

It was blatantly obvious that he didn't want to be anywhere near the creatures.

Entering the cell, I stood before the iron bars and looked at Orca.

Orca gave a playful wink with the eye that wasn't covered by a patch.

Then, he rose from the chair.

He approached me, limping.

Having been exposed to the violence of the guards for a long time and forced to have his Neo extracted, one of Orca's legs could no longer function properly.

One of his eyes had also been removed for research purposes.

Orca… always described himself as broken.

A slight tingling lingered in my left wrist.

Because of last night's dream, I seemed to be unnecessarily conscious of my wrist.

I clenched and unclenched my hand as I looked at Orca.

“Mr. Chestnut.”

Conscious of the guard, he spoke in a lowered voice.

“Were you alright last night? It pained my heart to part with you in such a manner. I was so worried about you, Mr. Chestnut.”

I hadn't yet managed to bring my affection level with Orca to 100.

If it was the same as the game, the moment his affection for me reached 100 was predetermined.

It happened when reaching the True Ending.

Unless it was then, it would rise to 99, but it would never hit 100.

When I played The Cell X as a game in the past, I had resented Orca.

That he only gave me his complete affection once I finally saw the True Ending.

Still, it was fine this time because I was going to create a new True Ending.

After catching my breath for a moment, I smiled at Orca.

“I’m fine. Are you injured anywhere, Mr. Orca?”

Though he said he returned to Sector X on his own, the guards might have subjected him to violence again during the process.

Since I wasn't a real medical officer, I didn't have great skills, but I could perform simple treatments.

*‘I did study the treatment methods for each creature in the game.’*

At my words, Orca grinned and held out his arm.

“Please treat this!”

His forearm was so full of scars from self-inflicted wounds that it was hard to tell where the injury was.

Orca pointed to a wound with his finger.

Looking where his finger pointed, there was a small scratch.

It was a wound barely deeper than a hairline crack.

“…….”

Without a word, I rummaged through my medical bag and pulled out a bandage.

It was a penalty bandage I usually only brought out when the human prisoners became too annoying.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Chick Bandage: A children's bandage with a cute chick drawn on it. Usable by both humans and creatures.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

As I slapped the bright yellow bandage over the wound, Orca's eyes widened.

A notification of value change appeared.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Suspicion -10</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Affection +6</strong>
  </div>
</div>

To think a single chick bandage would reduce suspicion by 10.

I should have put about ten of them on him.

Orca spoke, suppressing a laugh.

“Mr. Chestnut…. Do you know how old I am? I don't believe I told you in the dream.”

“Roughly three digits, I assume.”

“How did you know?”

I had guessed, and I was right.

Since he was a creature, I figured he'd be old, but I was honestly surprised that it was actually true.

*‘He was a total grandfather, wasn't he?’*

No wonder he used an elderly manner of speaking.

Perhaps because my surprise was evident, Orca chuckled and spoke teasingly.

“It may seem ancient to you, Mr. Chestnut, but in my race, I am still a youth! In my prime. Let's see, in human years, I would be around my mid-to-late thirties.”

Listening to Orca's kind explanation made me curious about Dominic's age as well.

However, I quickly discarded the curiosity, thinking that age was likely meaningless for him.

Orca looked down at the bandage on his forearm and muttered.

“This didn't happen in the dream…. Reality is certainly different.”

I had never put a chick bandage on him in the game either.

I placed my stethoscope against him and lowered my voice.

“Mr. Orca. Since there is only one guard today, I intend to test a plan. Are you okay with that?”

At that, Orca leaned his head closer to me and replied.

“Of course, Mr. Chestnut. But I am still worried. What if you are in danger?”

A faint scent of the sea wafted by.

The sea breeze was flowing into this place through the tiny window.

I gave him a small smile and said something else.

“Later… would you like to go to the sea with me? Please teach me how to swim.”

Orca's eyes widened. Soon, he laughed as if collapsing.

“I am not such a good teacher.”

“I'm not exactly a model student either.”

“Haha…. If Mr. Chestnut wishes it, I would like to teach you.”

  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Suspicion -20</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">

  </div>
</div>

I checked Orca's stats as the notifications popped up.

<br>
<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Creature No. 6 (Orca)

<br><br>- Suspicion: 0

<br><br>- Affection: 99</strong>
  </div>
</div>
<br>

As expected, the final 1 remained unfilled.

“If Mr. Chestnut gives me freedom, it will be difficult for us to swim together.”

I grit my teeth. But Orca eventually said the words I didn't want to hear.

His hopeful voice cried out as if dreaming.

“You know what freedom I desire, don't you, Mr. Chestnut!”

Creature No. 6 of The Cell X, Orca.

The true freedom he desired was death.
