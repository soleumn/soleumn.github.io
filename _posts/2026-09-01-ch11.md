<br>The medic's weekly schedule had transformed into a Correction Cleric's duty roster.

No matter how many times I reread it, the words remained unchanged.

I even pulled out the operational manual to double-check.

But the manual was still the same. Only the weekly schedule had been altered.

As I kept staring at the schedule, Dominic, who had been watching me, chimed in.

"Mr. Roasted Chestnut, has your role changed to a cleric?"

"No, it hasn't," I replied to Dominic.

As I answered, a sudden thought struck me.

*‘Could it be the Octopus Tentacle?’*

The Octopus Tentacle had tried to claim Je Hyun-oh as its subordinate but failed.

Perhaps it was only natural that it would then turn its attention to the master of that subordinate.

I asked Dominic, my heart filled with a slight sense of dread.

"Mr. Dominic."

"Yes, Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

"By any chance... if a high-ranked god takes a low-ranked god as a subordinate..."

"Then all of the low-ranked god's subordinate also becomes their property."

The answer hit the exact point I was worried about, leaving me speechless.

As if mocking me, the System Window appeared.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You, as a young god, swear to protect yourself and your subordinates.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

Ignoring the System Window's obvious statement, I sank into serious thought.

*‘This is why I hated the registration.’*

This was a maddening situation.

I wondered if I could win if those octopus tentacles ever came for me.

The conclusion was reached instantly.

Impossible.

The abilities I possessed as a god were nothing more than a Message and a Restriction that I could use only on those I had registered.

It would be difficult to face another god with just this.

But even so, I couldn't just sit idly by.

The biggest problem was actually Dominic.

He wouldn't like me becoming a subordinate of the Octopus Leg.

Since the Octopus Leg seemed quite strong, Dominic might have to exert considerable power to face a god of that caliber, and if he were to regain his memories in the process...

"..."

I tried not to think too much in the worst-case scenario.

Just then, Dominic asked me, "Are you going to the prayer room?"

"I don't plan to."

I answered firmly.

Even if it was a mission, I had no desire to perform the "Octopus Tentacle Worship Four-Course Meal." I absolutely refused.

My heart felt like it was burning, and I rubbed my chest with my hand.

*‘I should go see Lieutenant Won.’*

Since he was a Correction Cleric, he might be able to give me a clue.

"Ah, and about the octopus tentacle mark you mentioned before. I think I can find it for you today."

Dominic, who had seen the octopus tentacle carving in the prayer room before, had asked me to let him know if I found anything similar.

Yet, for some reason, he politely declined.

"It's alright, Mr. Roasted Chestnut. I don't need it anymore."

He told me he'd resolved his doubts thanks to the subordinate made of black metal.

I had no idea what he'd learned from seeing Je Hyun-oh emerge from the seawater, though.

For now, I decided to try contacting Samra.

"I'm going to contact a friend for a moment."

After asking the Pudding Slime for permission, I activated my hologram badge.

"Samra."

After calling out his name, I suddenly wondered if he might be too busy. The outside world must be in chaos because of the new meteor.

‘But since it's Samra, couldn't he handle his work while talking to me?1

I waited for a moment, holding onto my unwavering faith in the state-of-the-art android.

Sure enough, a voice soon replied.

—Mr. Han Goyo.

"Do you have a moment? If you're busy, I can call back later..."

—I can handle multiple tasks at once. Your concern is unnecessary.

"Ah, yes, sir."

Having been scolded for worrying about an android, I quickly gave a report on the current situation.

I reported that things were proceeding according to plan, and if no major issues arose, we would be able to achieve the True Ending by next week at the latest, based on The Cell X's time.

I didn't mention the error in today's weekly schedule. I was afraid Samra would tell me to use the escape function immediately. Besides, the text in the System Window wasn't corrupted, so I couldn't use the escape function anyway.

Before Samra could ask me a difficult question, I asked first.

"How are things outside?"

—We have established an international organization to respond to the Trial. This is for more efficient international cooperation.

Even now, global cooperation is underway to face the Trial, but there has been no central organization to coordinate it.

Establishing an international organization was a good choice.

But then, Samra said something completely unexpected.

—I will be taking a key position in that organization.

"What?!"

I blurted out, my voice louder than intended in my shock.

Samra spoke calmly, as if he knew exactly what I was worried about.

—I will not be leaving Era Agency. I will be holding both positions concurrently.

"Oh..."

I wondered if that was even allowed, but then I realized Samra wasn't human, so it should be fine.

He has no desire for power, nor does he prioritize the interests of the Republic of Korea over humanity. He is an android capable of making fairer judgments than any human ever could.

I wondered what position Samra would be taking, but I was also a little worried.

"Samra. Aren't you overextending yourself?"

He already had so much on his plate; I worried his CPU might burn out if he pushed himself too hard.

*‘What if he overloads?’*

I didn't know Samra's internal structure, so maybe that wasn't possible...

But Samra answered my concern calmly.

—This is work I have always done.

Then again, considering Samra's influence, he was likely already doing work on par with an international organization. Now, he would simply be given a position that came with honor and power.

—Han Goyo.

"Yes?"

—Will you transfer your affiliation to the soon-to-be-established international organization?

I was speechless for a moment. Samra had surprised me several times today.

Samra calmly added a well-reasoned explanation.

—It will be difficult for Director Jang Seokyun to exert significant influence within that International Organization. Furthermore, it would grant access not only to the Trials in the Republic of Korea but also to those in other countries.

I had given some thought to overseas Trials before.

My affiliation was listed as "Earth," so I had wondered if it was acceptable to only close off the Trials within my own country.

But that was still a far-off concern. Unless a quest specified a particular region, I had no intention of leaving.

After all, everyone I wanted to protect was here.

Yet, I understood all too well why Samra was making this proposal.

He was unconditionally cooperating for my sake, just as he had promised at the very beginning.

Since Era Agency and Paseong seemed to be obstacles for me, he had brought an alternative: the International Organization.

Would it be too arrogant to think that his desire to secure a position there was also for my sake?

Still, I couldn't shake the feeling.

"Thank you, Samra," I said sincerely.

—...

Samra didn't respond for a moment. He had realized my greeting was a rejection.

"Still, I want to stay at Era Agency. I also have a promise to keep to Representative Cha."

Becoming part of an international organization would have many advantages, but in the end, I would only grow distant from the people at Era Agency.

The meteorite embedded in Mo Haein, the god contained within Kwak Hanmuk, and Je Hyun-oh's <Zero>.

With none of these resolved, I didn't want to leave Era Agency.

—...I understand. Please let me know whenever you change your mind.

Samra didn't try to persuade me any further.

As always, he ended the call after leaving a final reminder to prioritize my own safety.

I switched the hologram badge to off.

I headed to the prison cafeteria, thinking that closing down The Cell X and stepping outside would bring about many changes.

Previously, I should have faced an event where I got beaten up by the Prison guard for failing a mission, but CEO Cha had stopped that from happening.

Since then, I've grown arrogant.

I actually decided to have breakfast in the cafeteria.

Normally, I would have sneaked in after the mealtime was over and stolen the leftovers.

Of course, I wasn't going to walk in boldly. I still planned to keep a low profile and be cautious.

I walked down the hallway with a light heart.

*‘I wonder if Captain Kwak is in the cafeteria. It would be nice to eat with him.’*

Just as I was debating whether to send him a message, it happened.

"Lamb."

I had run straight into the Security Chief.

The Medical Officer's Quarters were in the most secluded corner of the Prison guard's Building. There was no reason to cross paths with other officers unless they came specifically looking for me.

Just as I registered the sense of dread, Dominic spoke.

"Mr. Roasted Chestnut. Be careful. Around you..."

But I never heard the end of his sentence.

A presence materialized behind me. I spun around instantly, but seeing the threat and avoiding it were two very different things.

*Thud!*

A dull pain slammed into the back of my head, and the world went black.

When I finally came to, the first thing I noticed was the smell of cigarettes.

The smoke made me cough. I sputtered and gasped, slowly regaining consciousness.

It was an interrogation room in the prison.

The harsh white light from the ceiling fixture fell directly onto the table in the center of the room. The table was made of gray steel, bolted firmly to the floor. Its surface was scarred with scratches, cigarette burns, and what looked like fingernail gouges.

I slowly lifted my gaze from the table.

A man sat there, his face cast in deep shadow by the overhead light. He took a drag of his cigarette and slowly exhaled the smoke.

I swallowed a curse.

It was the Security Chief.

Two Prison guards  stood behind him.

I first checked to make sure the Pudding Slime was still attached. Dominic was on my shoulder. He spoke to me in a low voice.

"You're bleeding."

Only after Dominic spoke did I notice the damp sensation.

My head must have been split open. The blood that had trickled down my temple had soaked the collar of my medical gown and shirt. Still, the bleeding seemed to have mostly stopped; I didn't feel any new blood flowing.

My body, bound in rope, felt a bit stiff and uncomfortable. I managed an awkward smile and offered a greeting.

"Good morning, Mr. Security Chief."

The man in the gray metal mask stared at me, a cigarette dangling between his fingers.

"A Prison guard has gone missing."

"...Excuse me?"

"The CCTV footage we need to review has vanished, and the Warden told me to just let this matter drop."

"..."

"Did you do this, Lamb?"

"Me?" I asked, sounding bewildered.

"I haven't even taken Neo... How could I possibly stand against the Prison guard with their enhanced bodies?"

I feigned ignorance, and the Security Chief smirked.

"That's right. Little Lamb, you are just an ordinary human."

Could he be doing this because he lost out in the power struggle with CEO Cha?

I knew the Security Chief wouldn't just sit idly by. But it made no sense for him to act like this toward me, a mere Medical Officer.

If he wanted to stab CEO Cha in the back, that would be one thing.

Just as I was trying to figure out why he had brought me here, the Security Chief spoke again.

"It is certainly an impossible task..."

He continued slowly.

"But there are those who can make the impossible possible. And I know such a person."

Just as I was wondering why the Security Chief was speaking in such airy, unfocused riddles—so unlike his usual self—the lips beneath his sightless metal mask moved.

"I once had a dream where you appeared, Little Lamb."

"...!"

The Security Chief took a slow drag of his cigarette and blew the gray smoke directly into my face.

"In that dream, your name wasn't Little Lamb," he said with a smirk. "It was Roasted Chestnut."

I kept my face a mask of indifference, suppressing every flicker of surprise.

But it was all in vain.

His next words shattered my composure.

"In that dream, you were the perfect comrade."

"You dealt with the creatures in a way so cruel, even I hadn't thought of it... Ah, truly impressive."

The Security Chief had dreamed of the time I played the role of a Prison guard.

A dream where, in order to collect various endings, I had faithfully followed his orders as a

Prison guard and abused the creatures.

Staring into my trembling eyes, the Security Chief displayed a notification of a change in his stats.

Affection +10

"When I woke up, I felt such a pang of regret. Since then, no matter how I train other officers, none can become like you in my dreams."

The Security Chief crushed his cigarette into the ashtray.

"It seems this wasn't just my dream alone... What do you think?"

And then he smiled at me.

"We had fun, didn't we, Roasted Chestnut?"
