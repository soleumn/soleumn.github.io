<br>Silence fell as soon as Samra finished speaking.

Despite it being a video conference with twenty countries participating, no one opened their mouth at that moment.

There were no follow-up questions asking if it was certain.

Such a question would have been meaningless to Samra Man-sang.

Jang Seok-yoon looked at Samra with trembling eyes. This was a fact even he had been unaware of.

Samra added a short explanation to aid their understanding.

“This is the result of a data analysis completed 56 seconds ago. The accuracy is 100%.”

The voice, claiming there wasn't even a 0.01% margin of error, contained no emotion.

“It is presumed to be a chain reaction triggered by existing meteors reacting to the new meteor.”

The locations of the meteors scheduled for hatching and explosion were varied.

They were scattered randomly across the Northern and Southern Hemispheres, making it impossible to deduce any geographical correlation.

At the very least, since there was clear data for the Trial meteors scheduled to explode, they analyzed based on that information, but similarly, no connection was found.

Samra displayed the locations of the hatching and exploding Trials on each participant's screen.

Since every country had at least a few included, the atmosphere darkened instantly.

Countries where exploding Trials were located, in particular, could not hide their anxiety.

Jang Seok-yoon was no exception. A deep frown creased his brow.

South Korea had five meteors scheduled to hatch and two Trials scheduled to explode.

Currently, the number of meteors that had fallen worldwide reached hundreds of thousands.

By country, it ranged from hundreds to thousands, and the majority had not yet hatched.

The news that a new meteor had fallen was certainly shocking, but for countries that already had thousands of meteors piled up, they had no choice but to focus on other concerns.

However, the fact that a new meteor could cause fluctuations in the BT levels of existing meteors, triggering hatchings and explosions...

This might very well be the beginning of a human extinction scenario.

Cruelly, every single meteor had fallen only in human settlements.

Not a single instance of a Trial meteor being discovered in a remote area devoid of human traces existed.

In particular, the higher the population density, the higher the probability of a meteor strike, leading them to fall concentratedly in urban centers.

If a Trial exploded, the damage would inevitably be substantial.

Controlling the civilians was also a problem.

Currently, the situation was being managed somehow through memory erasure and information manipulation.

But if simultaneous hatchings and explosions occurred like this, word about the Trials and the BT virus would inevitably leak out.

Jang Seok-yoon let out a deep groan and spoke.

“Samra. Is there a way to solve this?”

Everyone looked at Samra.

Within those gazes directed at him, Samra detected a multitude of emotions.

“There is.”

“…!”

“This is a numerical fluctuation caused by the newly fallen Trial meteor. If this Trial is closed, there is a possibility that the BT levels will stabilize. However, even if stabilization is successful, if another Trial meteor falls, the same chain reaction will occur.”

Converting the sight of humans swinging between joy and despair into data and saving it, he continued.

“Therefore, as Samra Man-sang, I propose a countermeasure.”

As always, it was not difficult to move these irrational creatures according to his intentions.

Samra always achieved the optimal result he had designed.

Except for one single being.

Thinking of the sole exception, Samra spoke for the sake of 'humanity.'

“The establishment of an international organization for Trial response.”

I don't know how Je Hyeon-oh cleared &lt;Zero&gt;.

&lt;Zero&gt; is a game with no ending.

An infinite defense game where you protect a castle against endless waves of monsters.

As time passes, more powerful and numerous monster waves surge in.

There is no set story or ending.

It simply records how long the player can hold out in an eternally continuing battle.

It is a game where breaking the record is the only goal.

I received &lt;Zero&gt; from the creator and played it, breaking the records of previous users to achieve the all-time high score.

After breaking my own record a few more times, there was nothing left to do.

I poked around to see if there were any easter eggs and then closed the game.

When the creator asked for feedback, I told him it was fun, but a bit disappointing because there was no content other than combat.

Je Hyeon-oh... survived a place like that.

By creating an ending for a Trial that cannot be cleared.

Currently, &lt;Zero&gt; was being maintained in a peculiar state following Je Hyeon-oh's clear.

Originally, once a Trial hatches, it must be cleared at regular intervals to prevent it from exploding.

The standard procedure was for a Compatible to enter and clear it once the BT level had accumulated to a certain point.

However, after Je Hyeon-oh's clear, the BT level of &lt;Zero&gt; stopped rising and remained at a certain level.

Since it wasn't closed, entry was possible at any time.

Because of that, there were successive accidents where Compatibles, wanting power like Je Hyeon-oh's, secretly entered &lt;Zero&gt; and died.

Currently, the Trial grade of &lt;Zero&gt; was rated as six stars, and the Era Administration strictly prohibited unauthorized entry.

And yet, he asked me to enter &lt;Zero&gt; with him.

I could only ask with a somewhat foolish expression.

“Right now...?”

“Later.”

He looked down at me with eyes shaded by long eyelashes and spoke.

“You’ll die if you go in there now.”

It was a very kind explanation.

It was also a statement implying that the option of 'not going in' did not exist.

If so, I wanted him to tell me more clearly when exactly we would enter.

I threw an ambiguous question at him.

“Do you want to close &lt;Zero&gt;?”

“No.”

Just as I was flustered by the answer that closing it wasn't what he wanted.

Je Hyeon-oh spoke to me in a rough, low voice.

“I like &lt;Zero&gt;, Goyo.”

That 'like' was different from the 'like' of ordinary people.

Because the moment Je Hyeon-oh uttered those words... his face was distorted with all sorts of emotions.

At that moment, I felt a strange, intense intuition.

The happy memory I had seen while under an illusion spell in the SL2PL dungeon.

It was an intuition that perhaps Je Hyeon-oh's happy memory was the moment he spent in &lt;Zero&gt;.

I wanted to know more.

But the conversation with Je Hyeon-oh ended there.

He put his goggles back on, displayed only a smiling icon, and said nothing more.

As if today's quota of conversation was finished.

I hovered around, wanting to ask more, but eventually, I had to give up.

Afterward, I discussed future operations with Je Hyeon-oh.

Since Je Hyeon-oh's role was important in my plan, I informed him of various details, finished the tasks necessary for the strategy, and returned to the medic's quarters.

I lay on the bed with the Pudding Slime on my chest, continuing to chew over the conversation with Je Hyeon-oh.

*'So, what was that frustration?'*

At first, I wondered if it was because of another god, but that didn't seem to be the case.

I opened Je Hyeon-oh's Status Window to check his current state.

The state had now changed to joy.

Staring blankly at the word *'joy,'* I thought.

Mo Haein, Kwak Hanmuk, Je Hyeon-oh...

The things I had recently learned were their private secrets.

They were extremely intimate parts that the vast majority would never know, yet I felt as empty as a blank sheet of paper.

I still felt like I knew nothing.

I wanted to know more, but I didn't know how, to the point where my head felt itchy.

Even though the phrase 'invasion of privacy' was floating around in one corner of my mind.

My state had definitely become strange.

*'I even blurted out nonsense in front of Lieutenant Colonel Je.'*

I had said all sorts of crazy things, like why he met another god.

I thought I was fairly patient, but lately, I felt like I had become impulsive.

I wondered if it was because of the Children's Nutritional Supplement Dominic fed me, but for now, I felt I should try to think more calmly.

I forced myself to think of something else.

*'Is the problem the octopus leg?'*

The reason Je Hyeon-oh suddenly disappeared on the night the prison break occurred was likely because of the octopus leg.

I suspected that the octopus leg had dragged him into the sea because it wanted to make him its subordinate.

“Mr. Dominic.”

“Yes, Mr. Roasted Chestnut.”

“The octopus leg...”

Dominic smiled immediately. It was Black Block Volume 2.

Feeling embarrassed by my slip of the tongue, I corrected myself.

“The fact that the god of this place tried to steal a subordinate... does that mean such a thing is possible between gods?”

“It is.”

“He seems like a fairly strong god, so I'm curious why he couldn't steal him.”

“The octopus leg stealing Mr. Roasted Chestnut's subordinate?”

“Yes...”

Hearing it in Dominic's voice, the term 'octopus leg' didn't seem so bad.

He explained kindly from atop my chest.

“There could be many reasons, but the most primary factors would likely be loyalty and rank.”

Je Hyeon-oh and loyalty.

As I struggled to process such an unconventional combination of words, Dominic continued steadily.

“The deeper the heart that sincerely serves the master, the harder it is for another god to interfere. Of course, if there is a difference in rank great enough to ignore all that, it is possible.”

The Pudding Slime stopped talking for a moment and squirmed.

He moved freely across my face and spoke.

“It seems the octopus leg does not possess that level of rank.”

I couldn't understand everything Dominic said, but I could grasp the general situation.

Anyway, it meant it wasn't easy to lay a hand on Je Hyeon-oh recklessly.

I was about to thank him for the explanation, but he wasn't finished yet.

“There are cases where a high-ranking god takes a lower god as a subordinate.”

I just thought of something scary...

When I couldn't hide the immediate suspicion and wariness, this time Dominic smiled longer than before.

“You are still too young to be made my subordinate, Mr. Roasted Chestnut.”

The slime's body gradually began to sparkle.

I stared blankly at the sight, shining like the infinite stars of the universe.

“And wouldn't it be a waste to become merely such a being? Considering the rank Mr. Roasted Chestnut will grow into in the future.”

I had no idea what kind of rank I possessed for him to package me like this.

In the midst of that, I wanted to take just one bite of the Pudding Slime passing over my cheek.

I asked when the Pudding Slime moved back down to my chest.

“Then, could the octopus leg approach me? To make me a subordinate...?”

“That would be possible. However, he will not be able to act as he wishes.”

I couldn't ask why. Because the Pudding Slime spoke in a mock-stern voice.

“Mr. Roasted Chestnut always goes to sleep late.”

At his words, I realized it was already close to midnight.

Since it didn't seem like Dominic would explain further, I decided to go to sleep for tomorrow's sake.

*'I should communicate with Samra tomorrow morning.'*

Since the weekly schedule told me that the Residue wouldn't visit today, I closed my eyes in relief.

As the Pudding Slime patted me softly on my chest, sleep naturally washed over me.

I drifted into a deep slumber, scattering my worries about the octopus leg.

As soon as I opened my eyes, I saw the Pudding Slime's blue body.

I greeted him in a sleepy voice.

“Good morning, Mr. Dominic.”

I placed him back on the bed and stood up.

I planned to take a bit of a risk today.

Since I was out of food, I had to go to the cafeteria for breakfast.

With Kwak Hanmuk and CEO Cha around, I figured the prison guards wouldn't be able to mess with me easily.

Thinking of my reliable backing, I checked the weekly schedule.

I intended to check Wednesday's tasks.

“…?”
