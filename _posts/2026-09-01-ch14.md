<br>I reached out to it.

The fishy smell of the sea filled the air as hundreds of tentacles lunged toward me.

Just before they touched me...

"Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

A large hand reached from behind and pressed down on my forearm.

I tilted my head back.

The three-meter-tall man standing behind me looked down and said, "You can't."

*'Why?'*

"You can't digest something that size."

*'I can eat it.'*

The corners of his blue eyes curved into a smile.

"The Young god is quite greedy, isn't he? Even though he's still so small."

He kept trying to stop me, saying I couldn't even digest ■■■■.

I hated the way he spoke to me as if I were a child.

I wasn't a child.

Upon hearing my words, Dominic burst out laughing.

His laughing eyes truly seemed to be looking at something small and young.

My frustration softened slightly.

I knew what kind of being Dominic was.

Before him, everything would simply be young.

"Let's see. How about we do this, then?"

Still positioned behind me, Dominic wrapped his arms around my shoulders, his hands cupping them in a gentle arc.

I looked down at the thing I had momentarily forgotten.

At my feet, it continued to stretch its tentacles toward me.

But as if blocked by something, they slipped through the air time and again.

The writhing movements were repulsive.

Staring at it, a sudden question struck me.

*'Why did I ever want to eat that thing?'*

I had simply wanted to punish the one who dared to covet my subordinate.

I could have done that without eating him.

But my train of thought was cut short.

A gentle voice, like an enchanting dream, tickled my ear.

"He is neither small, nor weak, nor virtuous. He is a suitable opponent for you, Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

He encouraged me, saying that I could definitely defeat him if I just learned a few tricks, and that I could do it because I had strong subordinates.

"Once you've had your fill of playing with him, I'll cut him up so you can eat him."

*'I don't want it to hurt like ■■■■.'*

"It won't hurt this time."

*'Really?'*

"It will help cool down your heat. It will make digesting ■■■■ much easier."

……

"I promise."

If that were the case, I thought it might be alright. I gave a small nod.

Dominic moved his fingers lightly. He tapped me playfully and laughed.

"Once you've digested it all, you'll be able to take a few steps."

Then, he covered my eyes with one hand. His large hand seemed to envelop my entire face.

"Let's go back. There's nothing good about staying in a place like this for too long."

In the pitch-black darkness, I heard a call.

It was a mass of corrupted sound, mixed with distorted, grotesque syllables.

It was calling to me.

It tried to call my name several times, but it was all in vain.

Neither *'Roasted Chestnut'* nor* 'Little Lamb'* was a name that held my true essence.

Leaving the discordant noise behind, I slipped away to the outside.

***

<br>The moment I opened my eyes, the dream scattered.

I tried to grasp it, but it slipped through my fingers like grains of sand, leaving nothing behind.

I felt the heat in my eyes.

They were probably flashing gold again.

"An Octopus Leg has approached Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

The Pudding Slime was guarding my bedside.

Still half-dazed, I asked, "Did it enter my dream...?"

"It is common for gods to dwell in dreams."

Dominic added the advice that it would be best not to try and recall the dream. He explained that even if I had successfully driven the Octopus Leg away, remembering the dream could re-establish the severed connection.

I slowly sat up.

And waited for the throbbing heat in my eyes to subside.

A god that interferes with the human mind.

I'd thought this place was strange ever since I saw their religious broadcasts, but I never expected them to approach me this way.

I had a guess as to why the Octopus Leg was able to enter my dreams.

Unlike before, I had downed three bottles of Neo in a row. That must have somehow allowed the Octopus Leg to find me.

I couldn't remember the dream well.

But... the feeling of pleasure remained. It was the kind of joy I'd felt upon finally discovering something I had been searching for all along.

As I rubbed my eyes with my hand, I flinched at the dry, flaky blood. I had fallen asleep without washing up.

Just as I was getting up, thinking I needed to wash quickly, I heard someone outside.

I had practically rolled into the room earlier, leaving the door ajar.

The approaching footsteps were heading straight for the Medical Officer's Quarters.

My head still throbbing from being cracked open just hours ago, I couldn't help but be on guard. I gripped the iron pipe I'd tucked away under the bed.

'This time, I'm the one cracking a head.'

Clutching the pipe, my eyes flew open—only for my lips to part in a silent "Oh."

"Lieutenant Won?"

It wasn't an intruder; it was Won Taebeom.

I'd sent a message through Kwak Hanmuk, asking him to explain the general situation to CEO Cha. I'd also requested that Lieutenant Won be sent to the Medical Officer's Quarters if possible.

But I never expected him to show up this quickly.

As I lowered the iron pipe, I sensed something was off.

Won Taebeom stood frozen in the doorway, staring down at me.

*'Is he looking at me like that because I'm so filthy?'*

I was a mess. The blood from my head wound had soaked my face and clothes.

To make matters worse, the Security Chief had smoked so much that the smell of tobacco seemed to have seeped into my skin.

I brought my sleeve to my nose for a quick sniff, but Won Taebeom's voice cut through the air, hard and sharp.

"Report your status, Trainee Han."

I snapped to attention, trying to look as soldierly as I thought I should.

"I had an altercation with the Security Chief," I reported. I explained that he had kidnapped me to turn me into a prison guard, and that I had escaped by selling out CEO Cha.

"Why didn't you get medical treatment?"

"I'm all healed up."

I told him that the Security Chief had forced me to drink Neo, but that I had solved the problem using the neutralizer I had on me.

Of course, I hadn't taken a single drop of the neutralizer, but Won Taebeom had no way of knowing the truth.

Just as I was about to explain why I had called him...

Something extremely embarrassing happened.

*Grumble.*

A notification popped up, warning me that my hunger had surpassed its limit.

Now that I thought about it, I hadn't eaten a thing since yesterday morning's cereal.

"Lieutenant Won... I'm really sorry, but..." I said, feeling my ears burn with shame. "Do you have any spare food? If you do, could you give me a little?"

Won Taebeom stared at me in disbelief.

I would have endured it if I could, but I needed to eat now because starving too much would trigger a Bad Ending.

Fortunately, Won Taebeom immediately pulled some food from his inventory.

"Thank you!"

I bowed deeply and politely accepted the food.

It was a peanut butter and jelly sandwich.

Having eaten nothing but expired cereal for days, I savored every bite of this heavenly meal.

I devoured the sandwich at first, but my pace gradually slowed. I could feel Won Taebeom's gaze fixed on me.

*'Maybe I was eating too ravenously,' *I thought. I began to chew the sandwich as politely as possible.

After finishing, I gently brushed the crumbs from my lips.

"Trainee Han."

"Yes, sir?"

I jumped, startled like a guilty criminal. He sighed, his voice weary.

"Go wash up first. We'll discuss the mission after that."

"Uh, but—"

"That's an order."

"Yes, sir!"

Truth be told, I'd been desperate to wash up, so Won Taebeom's order was music to my ears.

I quickly showered in the small bathroom attached to the Medical Officer's Quarters.

After changing my clothes, I felt like a new person.

I re-equipped the Slime I'd left outside for a moment and hurried back to Won Taebeom.

Won Taebeom spoke bluntly.

"Dry your hair."

"Yes, sir."

Water droplets were constantly rolling off my wet hair.

In my haste, I had only roughly rubbed it with a towel, leaving my hair looking like a soggy piece of seaweed.

Following my superior's order, I grabbed another dry towel and vigorously shook out my hair.

Won Taebeom waited patiently for me.

When I finally faced him again, his eyes held a complicated expression.

I wondered if I had been too arrogant, not acting like a proper recruit.

During our brief reflection period, he called me over.

"Trainee Han. How have you been eating?"

"I've been rationing some cereal. But I ran out of food yesterday morning... You saved me, Lieutenant Won."

"..."

After a long silence, he handed me five energy bars.

"If you have any more food-related issues, report them to your superior immediately. Understood?"

"Yes, sir."

I obediently accepted them and stored them in my inventory.

Feeling like I'd just struck it rich, I began to seriously consider my operation.

My original plan had been a "Prison Rebellion Centered on the Creatures."

The goal was to choose a date, overthrow the prison all at once, use Neo to slaughter the guards, and then feed their remains to the Creatures.

This was the standard True Ending walkthrough.

I had already discussed it with Orca and hidden Creature No. 1 in the basement. I'd also asked Kwak Hanmuk and CEO Cha to complete their quests and maximize their Prison Guard and Warden abilities, respectively. I had originally assigned Je Hyun-oh to the underground maze, but since the situation had changed drastically, I had to pivot.

My new plan was a "Prison Rebellion Using the Security Chief's Coup."

After mulling it over, I asked Won Taebeom, "Lieutenant Won, have you completed the Correction Cleric quest?"

"Yes. I've raised the ability to Level 3."

The Correction Cleric's ability is Brainwashing.

It allows the user to brainwash a chosen target and turn them into a Fanatic. A brainwashed human prisoner's labor productivity increases significantly, and a brainwashed Creature's Neo production skyrockets.

It was an ability that condensed the effects of the religious broadcast.

As its level increased, the duration of the brainwashing also extended; at Level 3, it lasted for about two days.

I nodded and checked something else.

"Have you noticed any anomalies in the mission log?"

"Today's mission on the pastoral schedule was to brainwash the Medical Officer."

*'That damn Octopus Leg.'*

I had expected him to target the Correction Cleric first, but it seemed I was right.

"Everyone else's missions have changed too. What's going on?"

"Well... the god here is calling for me."

Since Won Taebeom's role was the Correction Cleric, it was difficult for him to lie about this.

When he spoke honestly, my eyes narrowed.

"Are you saying... a being called 'god' actually exists?"

"Just think of him as a boss."

It was actually quite similar.

I cursed Octopus Leg under my breath, recalling my weekly schedule, which had become monstrously complicated.

At this rate, any plan I devised would be ruined by these missions.

Failing the same mission twice would result in a Bad Ending.

Even if I managed to escape this time, I'd be completely trapped if the same mission were assigned tomorrow.

In the end, Octopus Leg was targeting me.

After pondering a solution, I came up with a plausible method.

A perfect way to stop Octopus Leg's tyranny and also reset the Security Chief's suspicion level to zero.

*'But there's something I want to ask Dominic...'*

Without thinking, I opened my lips.

"Mr. Dominic."

*Gasp.*

I flinched, startled by my own voice.

The moment I decided to speak to Dominic, a strange voice suddenly came out of my mouth.

As I flinched, Won Taebeom gave me a puzzled look.

Dominic smiled, reassuring me. "It's all right. Your eyes haven't changed; they're just as they were. You can now hold casual conversations with ease, so there's no need to worry."

He swayed gently, as if pleased with me. "Lesser beings can't hear you, so speak freely."

Indeed, Won Taebeom, sitting across from me, seemed completely unaware of my voice. It felt like using a whisper function in an online game.

Taking advantage of this strange but useful voice, I cautiously asked Dominic, "Does the Octopus Leg need to make a contract with me to take me in as its subordinate?"

"That's right. Taking in a god as a subordinate is quite a complicated process."

"Then I suppose it's fine if I praise the Octopus Leg for a few days, right?"

Dominic, having figured out what I was up to, replied as if speaking to a mischievous child.

"Mr. Roasted Chestnut... I really can't take my eyes off you for a second."

At least it meant I had his permission.

I came to a conclusion.

"Lieutenant Won."

"Yes, sir."

"Brainwash me."

To become a fanatic of the Octopus Leg.
