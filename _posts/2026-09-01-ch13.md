<br>If I had to pick the most baffling moment of the last few years, it would be right now.

For a second, I thought I'd heard things.

Won Taebeom immediately went on high alert. He had expected the white blade to be aimed at either Kwak Hanmuk or himself.

But CEO Cha silently sheathed the Daeil Yeorae.

Kwak Hanmuk raised an eyebrow. "CEO Cha... are you feeling sick? Did you get smacked with the gauntlet before I arrived?"

Even with Kwak Hanmuk openly provoking him, CEO Cha's voice remained cold. "Where is Han Goyo?"

"Our Trainee Han is busy."

Kwak Hanmuk and CEO Cha stared each other down. Kwak Hanmuk grinned, baring his fangs, but his eyes remained cold.

"If you play the part of the Warden well, Representative Cha, things will get a lot less busy for me. Just pretend to lose when the Security Chief stages a coup. That's it. Easy, right?"

Kwak Hanmuk shrugged his shoulders, then deliberately scratched at the wound again.

"I heard you've decided to support Trainee Han. If you can't handle it, tell me now. I'll pass it on to him."

CEO Cha let out a dry laugh.

"Me?"

He tilted his head, his gaze fixed on Kwak Hanmuk.

"Captain Kwak. I'm playing along. Don't get on my nerves."

His voice, distorted by the mechanical filter, spat out a sarcastic warning.

"Act like the lapdog you are, chained to the Era Agency."

As if he'd been waiting for the cue, Kwak Hanmuk let out a mock bark. "Woof woof."

Yet CEO Cha didn't even show a hint of disbelief.

"Sure. Good boy."

With that single remark, he turned and walked straight out, as if his business here was finished.

Behind CEO Cha's back, Kwak Hanmuk mouthed a silent curse.

Then, he turned to Won Taebeom and spoke.

"Lieutenant Won. Go to the Medical Officer's Quarters."

He added a ridiculous comment, saying he had to do the task Trainee Han had given him, and even asked if Lieutenant Won could check if Trainee Han had eaten breakfast.

As Won Taebeom tried to brush it off and walk away, Kwak Hanmuk waved his hand and insisted again.

"I'm not just saying this. Make sure you check on Trainee Han."

"What kind of check are you talking about?"

"He looks gentle, but he's a complete lunatic. It looks like he's about to cause trouble right now, so keep a close eye on him."

"…right?"

Did he talk to Han Goyo?

His tone was strange, as if he had only been relayed the situation without meeting him in person.

But Won Taebeom didn't ask.

The gray eyes embedded in the expressionless face turned toward Won Taebeom.

"Lieutenant Won, you don't want to die a dog's death here either, do you? Since we need

Trainee Han to get out, don't do anything reckless for now. Just focus on Trainee Han as your top priority. Got it?"

"Understood."

Following his superior's orders, Won Taebeom headed for the Medical Officer's Quarters.

The hem of his black suit fluttered with each of his wide strides.

As he walked alone down the dimly lit hallway, a tangle of complicated thoughts churned in his mind.

The first question that surfaced was about CEO Cha.

He had found him strange since the Era Agency terror attack, but this latest incident had confirmed it.

CEO Cha treated Han Goyo with unusual leniency.

No matter how useful Han Goyo was, it was strange for someone of CEO Cha's temperament to act this way.

It was also difficult to fathom Han Goyo's true intentions in summoning him now.

Until now, Han Goyo had only ever asked him to gather information. It was a clear instruction: don't reveal too much to me.

He had practically been sidelined from this mission, yet Han Goyo was suddenly seeking him out. It was baffling.

Just then, a whistle echoed.

Wheeew. The whistle dissolved into the hissing of a snake and a low, mocking chuckle.

Won Taebeom ignored it, as he always did.

The twisted, grotesque sound of his own voice lingered in his ear.

`"Whose turn is it this time? Who will you sacrifice to become a righteous butcher all alone?"`

Ever since he'd been infected with the BT-DFH virus, Won Taebeom had been hearing voices.

He hadn't told anyone about it.

After all, speaking up would only get him quarantined at the Era Agency as an infected subject.

Won Taebeom acted as if he heard nothing and reached the Medical Officer's Quarters.

"......"

He was momentarily speechless.

Han Goyo was covered in blood.

Han Goyo's eyes were wide with tension, clearly thinking the visitor was an intruder. He was even clutching an iron pipe in his hand.

He stared at Won Taebeom with a pale face, then immediately smoothed out his furrowed brow upon recognizing him.

"Lieutenant Won?"

The sharp, wary look in his eyes softened, revealing a hint of familiarity toward the man he knew.

Not a hint of negativity showed on his face, despite the unfair and terrifying situation he was in.

Even in his disheveled state.

At that moment, Won Taebeom felt a crushing weight on his chest.

Emotions are irrelevant to the greater good.

The sacrifice of a few for the many is inevitable.

If the misfortune of a single person could save countless others, there is all the more reason not to hesitate.

Yet, no matter how clear his rational judgment was, he couldn't suppress the sudden surge of emotion.

The phantom pain of a Roman collar tightening around his neck choked off his breath.

As if it had been waiting, a cruel whisper drifted into his ear.

`"So, that child is your new sacrifice!"`

<br>I had drunk Neo at the new prison guard training center, but my memory of that time was hazy.

I figured that my return to normal must have been a stroke of luck caused by a system error.

Therefore, I thought there was a very high probability that I would become a prison guard if I were to ingest Neo again.

But it wasn't 100 percent.

I decided it was worth taking the bet.

In fact, it was my only option.

Kwak Hanmuk had the neutralizer, but circumstances made it difficult to meet him before the transformation into a prison guard was complete.

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"You've already done it once, haven't you? The second time will be even easier. Perhaps it won't even feel like anything."</span>

The moment Dominic spoke, I thought of DeZonDeal's Zombie Virus.

The Zombie Virus had neutralized itself inside me.

What if Neo's earlier neutralization had also been my doing?

I decided it was a gamble worth taking and drank the Neo.

A full three bottles.

The Security Chief hadn't been satisfied with just one bottle and had forced me to drink three.

When I reached the last bottle, I was so nauseated I couldn't even swallow, but the Security Chief had kindly poured it directly into my mouth.

There was only one benefit to drinking the Neo.

It instantly healed the gash on my head.

Other than that, everything was terrible.

I had barely managed to feign composure in front of the Security Chief, but the moment I left the interrogation room, I could no longer endure the pain.

A searing heat erupted from my chest, spreading through my entire body.

Drenched in cold sweat, I barely managed to reach the Medical Officer's Quarters. The moment I opened the door, I collapsed to the floor.

Curled into a tight ball, I trembled violently, gasping for breath.

"Hah... hah..."

It felt as if someone had set my heart on fire—a scorching, agonizing pain. How did I endure this when I drank Neo at the new prison guard training center?

But whenever I tried to recall the memory, it remained as hazy as fog. The pain was so intense

I couldn't even think straight.

The agony wasn't confined to my heart alone. The heat radiating from it surged through my veins, incinerating my entire body. Even my eyes throbbed with a burning sensation.

I gasped for breath, tormented by the unyielding heat.

*I couldn't bear this foul sensation.*

*I wanted to expel it right now.*

"Mr. Roasted Chestnut."

I looked at Dominic, gagging from the heat.

A blue light filled my blurred vision. A sparkle, like a cosmic nebula, stole my gaze.

"You want to get rid of this unpleasant feeling quickly, don't you?"

*It feels awful.*

"That's only natural. After all, it doesn't belong to you."

His pleasant voice was low and soothing.

"You can do it. Go ahead. It will be easier than before."

Drunk on the heat, I thought.

I had been desperately searching for a way to eliminate Neo, but suddenly, an instinct guided me in a different direction.

Realization struck me like a light switch being flipped.

There was no reason for me to act.

For a moment, my eyes stung.

I commanded,

*"Get out."*

The pounding in my heart subsided. The heat that had been searing my veins gradually vanished.

I knew instinctively: Neo, who had been churning inside me, had evaporated.

A faint smile touched my lips.

At that moment, a soft touch brushed my cheek.

The sudden coolness against my feverish skin sent a shiver through me, and I winced.

"You did wonderfully. You're becoming more proficient with each attempt. As expected, you learn quickly."

I opened my mouth to respond to the infinitely gentle praise.

*"Dominic."*

And then I froze in horror.

*"Wh-what is this? My voice...!"*

The shock was so intense I felt the hair on my head stand on end.

It was definitely my voice, yet it sounded utterly bizarre.

No, perhaps "heard" was the wrong word.

It wasn't a voice.

It felt as if something with a tangible form was pouring its will directly into my brain. And for some reason, the voice sounded infinitely young.

Dominic let out a low chuckle as he watched my astonishment.

"It will return to normal soon, so it won't interfere with your daily life."

*"Are you sure...?"*

"Of course. It will also make it easier for you to converse with me from now on."

He praised my rapid progress once again.

I listened to his praise, waiting for my voice to return.

"...Ah."

Thankfully, I regained my normal voice shortly after.

I let out a huge sigh of relief. My heart was still pounding from the shock.

After calming down a bit, I first sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh.

After dealing with the urgent matters, I took a moment to think with a calm mind.

This was the second time I had encountered Neo, the first being at the new prison guard training center.

Yet, once again, I had not been infected.

Just as I had recovered from DeZonDeal's Zombie Virus.

The infection mechanism, the residue, and the zombies...

There was a strange similarity between DeZonDeal and The Cell X.

I pondered it deeply, but couldn't find any immediate clues.

*'It's kind of funny that the virus vanished just because I spoke in a strange voice.'*

It seemed as if I had expelled Neo using my own power.

But the more I chewed on it, the stranger it felt.

My mind had been a complete blur from the fever, yet the moment I said, "Get out," my consciousness had been crystal clear.

At that instant... it felt as if something...connected.

But I couldn't tell what had been linked.

I thought about it for a while, but found no answer. In truth, it was a question with no solution.

Hoping things wouldn't get any weirder, I shifted my thoughts elsewhere.

The bait I'd offered the Security Chief was simple: I'd act as a double agent.

The main point was that I would help him oust the Warden and become the one in charge here.

However, one thing still puzzled me.

"Mr. Dominic, why did Octopus Leg keep a subordinate like the Security Chief in his position?"

I couldn't understand why Octopus Leg had left him there, even though the Security Chief wanted the Warden's job. If I were Octopus Leg, I would have put him in the highest position possible.

Because of that, I hadn't realized sooner that the Security Chief was Octopus Leg's subordinate. Come to think of it, I didn't seem to hear the Voice of god that often.

I had never noticed any of this while playing the game, nor had I sensed the slightest hint of it after entering The Cell X in reality.

As someone who used the message function several times a day, almost like chatting, I couldn't understand it.

Dominic explained it to me simply.

"Because subordinates are merely tools to be used."

It meant there was no need, nor any reason, to carefully attend to every single detail and treat them well.

I felt a strong surge of resistance.

They've entrusted their lives to me, so shouldn't I obviously treat them well?

I believed it was right to do everything I could for them.

But telling Dominic this would be useless, so I kept the thought to myself and let it go.

Now, I had to slowly get up and start solving this disastrous mess one by one, but...

I just wanted to close my eyes for a little while—just ten minutes.

I had been running on empty since morning, skipping breakfast and getting run ragged. I didn't even have the strength to knead a Pudding Slime.

I glanced at the bed, then decided to just flop onto the floor. It was too filthy for me to even consider lying on the bed.

"Mr. Dominic... please wake me up if I sleep too long," I murmured, then practically passed out.

I fell into a deep, absorbing sleep, only to snap awake.

At first, I thought I had woken up. But I soon realized I was still dreaming.

I was floating above a vast ocean.

Suspended in mid-air, I surveyed my surroundings. Under a dark sky, the world was filled with deep blue water. It was a boundless sea, its width and depth immeasurable.

Just looking at it made me feel strange.

Suddenly, the surface of the water rippled. I looked down at my feet.

From the deepest part of the churning sea, something shot upward with terrifying speed.

A colossal shadow loomed closer, then tore through the surface to reveal its true form.

It was like a mountain.

Between the shattered waves, hundreds of tentacles writhed and coiled, but they were only a

fraction of its whole. I could easily imagine the rest of its form descending endlessly into the depths.

And its eye.

A deep-sea eye, revealed between the tentacles, stared at me.

Its pitch-black pupil dilated to take in my existence.

Ancient terror, a living abyss, a darkness that swallowed all the light in the world.

It had *sensed* me.

Having fully realized my presence, it extended a tentacle.

The reach extending toward me was rapacious, yet I felt no threat.

Instead, I couldn't help but burst into laughter.

Golden light flashed, piercing the darkness.

I, too, had *recognized* it.

This was the one.

The one who had dared to touch my subordinate.
