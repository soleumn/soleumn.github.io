<br>72 hours was an excruciatingly long time.

I felt no hunger, but I writhed in boredom.

Being trapped alone with my thoughts was difficult; dark, intrusive ideas kept surfacing. I deliberately tried to think of other things.

*'Why am I trapped here for seventy-two hours?'*

I suspected it had something to do with the children's supplements.

I rubbed my eyes.

Though I couldn't feel any distinct changes yet, I could perceive more than I ever had before. I could even sense Infernum and ■■■ mixing and being digested within me.

When I had only taken in Infernum, the heat had been agonizing. Now, it was not.

Thanks to ■■■, I would be able to assimilate Infernum even faster.

I wanted to grow stronger as quickly as possible.

*'I need to be careful about this...'*

The thoughts that kept surfacing on their own felt completely alien to my human self.

It was definitely different from before. The fact that I was now thinking and speaking like a god felt a little unsettling.

I needed to be careful not to let my mindset lean too far into the divine.

Lost in these thoughts, I spent my time coming and going from the Galactic Trading Post.

And finally, the 72 hours were up.
