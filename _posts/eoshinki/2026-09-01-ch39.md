<br>The Administrator seemed to have decided to warn me via a message rather than a direct communication.

But there was one thing he didn't know.

I was already with the Master of the Butlers.

Worse yet, since I was sharing Dominic's vision, he saw the Administrator's message as well.

I tensed up, but the tension quickly drained away.

Dominic didn't care at all.

I thought I knew what he was thinking.

*'He probably doesn't see any reason to care about the words of a trivial being.'*

Yet, Dominic was attentive to me.

Sensing my apprehension, he withdrew the vision share.

I felt the power that had settled within me slip away.

"Good work, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic's voice was calm as he praised me.

I thought for a moment about the Pudding Slime on my shoulder.

I had always known he was a being of immense power.

It was impossible not to. The moment I caught even a glimpse of Dominic, I knew instantly.

But the oldest god?

It seemed I had touched upon a being far greater and more dangerous than I had vaguely imagined.

Suddenly, my shoulders felt heavy.

It was no longer just a question, but a complete mystery why such a being would take an interest in me and stay by my side.

It was only natural that I, who had lived for a mere twenty-four years, could not understand a god so ancient...

Yet, there was still something I simply couldn't accept.

*'Why is Dominic so indifferent to his subordinates?'*

He showed no reaction, even with the Cat Butler and the Rabbit Butler right before his eyes. He had recovered a fair amount of his power, so he must have recognized his subordinates.

Just as he had done with the Administrator, he simply concealed his presence so the Butlers couldn't see him.

His attitude was so indifferent and cold that I wondered if they were even his subordinates.

"...Mr. Dominic."

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

After much thought, I asked him an ambiguous question.

"What kind of people are your subordinates, Mr. Dominic?"

At that, Dominic chuckled as if he'd heard a funny story and gave a very brief answer.

"I'm not sure."

It was as if they didn't even enter the realm of his thoughts.

I had at least expected him to say he had no interest in them, but it wasn't even that.

I knew how desperately the Butlers had waited for their master. Their devotion, with which they had cherished and maintained the Mansion for their master who never returned, was a pure sincerity, untainted by any impurities.

Dominic must have known their affection was nothing short of worship, yet he remained so utterly indifferent.

"A god is not meant to be a benevolent being, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic seemed to have already guessed what I was feeling and what was on my mind. He explained it to me patiently.

"You're still young, Roasted Chestnut, but you will eventually realize it too. A subordinate is meant to serve and revere their master."

I hated the way he spoke, as if this were an unchangeable fate.

Clinging to my defiance, I asked, "Are other gods like you, Dominic?"

"They aren't as soft-hearted as you are."

"But..."

A squishy sensation pressed against my cheek. The slime, who had gently tugged at my cheek as if playing a prank, whispered to me.

"When a god takes in a subordinate, there's a first thing they always do."

Without realizing it, I found myself leaning in to listen.

Dominic casually uttered something horrific.

"They pluck out the subordinate's eyes. So they can see only their master. It's the best way to implant blind faith."

**It was madness.**

I didn't need to hear any more. I should have changed the subject.

But... for some reason, I couldn't bring myself to interrupt him.

"After that, they usually modify them according to their preferences. Roasted Chestnut, how was it for you when you were briefly a believer of ■■■?"

*'At that time, I wanted to change the people around me.'*

I'd even felt that *not *modifying my body was somehow irrational.

The emotions I felt in that moment remained etched in my memory.

"I won't scold you for your affection for your subordinates, Roasted Chestnut. But right now... well, I feel you're neglecting them too much."

*Me? Neglecting them?*

"Why not take a more active role as their master in caring for them?"

*Would they only look to me then?*

*Without a single thought for any other god?*

"Of course. They would become immutable beings, devoted solely to you, thinking only of you forever. Ah, and it would be much better for defending Chestnut’s Planet, too."

*'That didn't sound bad at all.'*

I especially liked the word "unchanging."

After all, humans are fragile beings, always in flux.

A low voice gently shook me.

"Chestnut’s subordinates must be eagerly awaiting their master's touch."

Should I start by gouging out their eyes?

Then, I'll swap their bodies so they don't feel any pain.

Something that suits each of them.

When I was a fanatic of ■■■, I almost turned everyone into seafood, but now that I've regained my senses, I won't do anything strange.

*What kind of crazy talk is this—me wanting to adorn my subordinates in the most beautiful way?*

I hastily peeled the Pudding Slime off my cheek.

The slime, squishing softly in my hand, stared at me in confusion.

Drenched in a cold sweat, I spoke to him.

"...Thank you for the advice, Dominic. But I'm still scared of fully taking responsibility for my subordinates. I'd like a little more time."

Dominic didn't press me. He simply told me to do as I wished and praised me instead.

"There's no need to be anxious. Young Chestnut is growing wonderfully, after all."

But his praise only made me more uneasy.

Because I knew what kind of god he desired.

Back when I ate ■■■'s eye...

I had vowed to protect myself.

But perhaps...

I might have already started to change.

The next morning, Kwak Hanmuk came to my room to help me move into the official residence.

Dressed in casual clothes, his physique looked even more impressive than usual. The simple cotton T-shirt, replacing his sharp uniform, accentuated his muscles far better.

As I stared in envy, Kwak Hanmuk glanced around the room and asked, "Are you all packed?"

My belongings were minimal. I carried my uniform on a hanger to prevent wrinkles, and the rest was packed into a single duffel bag.

As Kwak Hanmuk inspected my meager luggage, he let out a sharp, "Ugh."

He had spotted the Pudding Slime on my head.

The Pudding Slime put on a sparkling reunion show, which only made Kwak Hanmuk's expression darken.

I lied for his sake. "It's actually my pet slime. It's like a pet item that only gods can keep."

If the Butlers had heard that, they would have fired beams of light at me for sure this time. But since they were on another planet, I lied with confidence.

Dominic just smiled at my claim about the pet item.

Kwak Hanmuk let out a dry laugh and muttered, "Sure... If Earth has gods, it can definitely have a pet slime..."

He then took me to the residence where I would be staying from now on.

Since the repairs on the Seoul Headquarters were complete, the residence I was moving into was located in Seoul. I had also heard that I would be commuting to the Seoul Headquarters again. It seemed Samra's help had expedited the repairs.

As we drove in the car Kwak Hanmuk was driving, he brought up the display case.

"I've already moved that to the residence."

After visiting the studio apartment, I had told Samra that I only wanted to take the display case. Apparently, he had already finalized everything with Kwak Hanmuk and moved it to the residence.

I worried that the display cabinet's size would be a nuisance, but Kwak Hanmuk assured me it wasn't.

"The living room's completely empty. There's nothing in the house."

He just told me I'd see for myself when I arrived, leaving me wondering just how empty it really was.

The Seoul Residence was a clean, modern officetel building. It was only a ten-minute walk from the Era Agency, which seemed like it would make the commute incredibly convenient.

Kwak Hanmuk stepped up to the front door and punched in the passcode. Then, he hummed a strange melody —*da-da-da-dan*— as he opened the door.

When I stared at him in confusion, he looked a bit awkward.

"Don't kids these days know this? You're supposed to sing it when you move into a new place."

It sounded vaguely familiar. I made a mental note to look it up later as I stepped inside.

And there, I realized that Kwak Hanmuk's statement had been the absolute, unvarnished truth.

The place was devoid of even basic furniture like a TV or a sofa.

A six-person dining table stood alone in the kitchen, looking conspicuously new—clearly bought just because I was moving in.

When I opened the door to my room, it was the same story: just a bed, a desk, and a small chest of drawers.

"So this is Roasted Chestnut's new residence? It's an improvement over the last one," the Pudding Slime remarked.

I set my bag and uniform on the desk, ignoring the Slime's one-line review.

"One of the rooms is too small, so I'm using it as a walk-in closet. Goyo keeps his clothes there too. Want to see my room?"

"May I?"

"You're welcome to come and go as you please whenever you're bored."

Kwak Hanmuk's room was even worse than mine. It literally contained nothing but a bed.

The only other things were a couple of dumbbells tucked away in a corner.

I'd heard this was originally Kwak Hanmuk's place, but it was so bare that it wouldn't have looked out of place if he'd just moved in today.

*'Actually, since I'd only be sleeping at the residence, the sight wasn't all that strange.'*

After looking around, I returned to the living room.

A large glass display case stood in an empty corner. Kwak Hanmuk gestured toward it with his chin, just as I'd done in the studio apartment, where it was covered with a cloth.

"Hey, Chestnut. What's that? Can I look?"

Since we'd be living together from now on, it felt wrong to keep it hidden.

I uncovered the display case with him. His eyes widened in surprise.

"...Wow."

After letting out an instinctive exclamation, he immediately glanced at me.

He must have known I'd retired due to my injury.

Sensing his hesitation to react, I smiled and spoke first.

"These are gifts I received from my fans a long time ago."

"Goyo, you were amazing back then."

Kwak Hanmuk examined the items in the display case one by one, admitting he hadn't known much because he'd never been interested in games.

"What's this? Chestnut played such a cute character?"

"Yes. It was a character I liked."

"Whoa, this is a total green chestnut. Look at him in that uniform."

"I was nineteen back then."

I felt a bit shy. Still, his lighthearted jokes helped me feel more at ease as I looked at the display case.

*'Ah, the supplements.'*

Just as I was reminding myself to order the supplements I'd promised Kang Byeokrin, Kwak Hanmuk suddenly flung open the display case.

Before I could even react to his abrupt movement, he held something out to me.

"Is this... you?"

It was a photo of my childhood self, my face photoshopped onto a player's jersey.

"Yes. That was when I started elementary school."

Kwak Hanmuk's expression went blank.

The fans had made it as a gift, praising how cute it was, but it was true that my appearance back then was very different from how I look now. I was small and thin, with only my cheeks being chubby.

*'Still, it's not so strange that it would cause a reaction like this...'*

Just as I was getting flustered by Kwak Hanmuk's reaction, a crackling sound was heard.

Blue sparks were flying from the prayer beads.

Kwak Hanmuk flinched, grabbing his wrist. But his face remained frozen.

Finally, he spoke slowly.

"...Ah, sorry."

Kwak Hanmuk put the photo back in its place and grinned.

"I was just so surprised by how tiny Chestnut was."

But his hand was still trembling slightly.

I wanted to ask why, but I couldn't bring myself to do it. His complexion was poor.

Kwak Hanmuk ran a hand over his face and took out his phone. Then, as if nothing had happened, he asked me, "You haven't saved my number, have you?"

On the day I went to Paseong, I had only been invited to the group chat, so I didn't know the Special Team's phone numbers. I saved Kwak Hanmuk's number for the first time and observed him again.

Tapping his phone screen, Kwak Hanmuk raised an eyebrow. "Let's have the housewarming party tomorrow. Captain Mo said he's free tomorrow. Want to eat Jajangmyeon?"

"...Anything is fine with me."

"You'll eat Tangsuyuk, right? Matbami, do you prefer the sauce poured over or dipped?"

"I don't mind either way..."

"Then let's order the Kung Pao Chicken, too."

"Captain Kwak, I'll take the order."

"No, no. Just keep unpacking."

Kwak Hanmuk patted my shoulder twice.

I agreed and went into my assigned room.

Then I closed the door.

"..."

Leaning my back against the door, I thought.

*'Why was he so surprised?'*

Moreover, blue sparks had actually crackled from his prayer beads. Something like that couldn't happen just from being startled.

I squeezed my eyes shut in anguish. Every time this happened, I wanted to open my Status Window.

*I want to know everything.*

I hoped nothing would be hidden from me.

I felt as if my eye color was about to change.

Only after repeatedly reminding myself that I must not act strangely in front of the Captains did I finally manage to calm down.

Fortunately, I managed to eat the jajangmyeon without incident.

Kwak Hanmuk had ordered enough food to fill the six-person table, and I ate until I was so stuffed that I wouldn't crave Chinese food for a while.

After that, I was able to act as if nothing was wrong.

Until I lay down in bed to end the day.

I pulled the blanket up to my chin and stared at the ceiling, unable to sleep.

This wasn't the studio apartment I used to live in, nor was it the Era Agency's Quarantine Facility.

It was a shared residence, yet I still felt as if I were living alone.

Even though I knew Kwak Hanmuk was in the room right next to mine...

The Slime on my chest called my name.

"Roasted Chestnut."

My response was delayed, my thoughts a tangled mess.

"...Yes, Dominic."

Why had blue sparks flown?

I racked my brain, wondering what I had done wrong, but I still couldn't figure it out.

I had even asked Kwak Hanmuk directly why the photo had startled him. But he refused to answer, brushing it off as a joke as usual.

Of course, Kwak Hanmuk had no reason to tell me everything. He was keeping the god sealed within him a secret, after all.

But this... this felt like it concerned me...

*After all, he was my subordinate...*

"Roasted Chestnut did nothing wrong."

The Pudding Slime, always saying the sweetest things, climbed onto my face.

"It is all the fault of the subordinate who tries to hide everything from his master."

He comforted me.

He continued to offer words of comfort, but they brought me no solace. I simply listened in silence.

Still, his voice was pleasant to hear.

A languid drowsiness soon washed over me.

"Roasted Chestnut. Do you wish to know what your subordinates are hiding?"

"..."

I didn't answer. I knew if I opened my mouth, I would simply say *yes*.

A soft, supple touch traced patterns across my face.

"There is no reason for you to suffer because of your subordinates. You need only to do whatever you desire."

His words were a magnificent temptation.

"No matter what their master does, the subordinates will still worship their god."

I...

*I wanted to know everything.*

*I wanted to dig out every single detail, so my subordinates could hide nothing from me.*

*But merely looking in wasn't enough.*

*They might just keep secrets from me again.*

A golden light emerged from the darkness.

As the room brightened as if a star had fallen, I slowly rose from the bed.

I set my feet on the floor. The sensation against my bare soles was chilly.

I walked calmly and deliberately, taking my place before Kwak Hanmuk's door.

*Knock, knock.* I tapped on the door and called out to him.

"Captain Kwak. May I come in for a moment?"

No matter how much I thought about it, there was only one conclusion.

*First, let's pluck out his eyes.*
