I was almost dragged straight to the Chief's Office by the Special Team.

They were eager to get approval for the team's permanent establishment, but I managed to stop them.

After all, I hadn't even signed a contract with the Era Agency yet.

If they pushed for the team's formation before I even became an employee, the whole process would get messed up.

Besides, I had things to discuss with Chief Jang Seokyun, so I insisted on handling this matter myself.

In the midst of this, Kwak Hanmuk asked about my dispatch contract with Paseong, making me break out in a cold sweat.

After I explained the situation, he scolded me a little...

But he seemed to understand, since that had been the best option at the time.

Of course, a separate scolding was still on the horizon, but I managed to dodge it for now.

So, Samra and I decided to visit Chief Jang Seokyun together, but first, we needed to change our clothes.

Returning to my room for the first time in ages, I changed into a spare uniform and let my thoughts wander.

*'I want to check my Status Window.'*

I fought the urge.

More than that, I was curious why he'd offered me a spot on the team. 'Was it because I'd come back from the dead?' That seemed likely, but it wasn't a completely satisfying explanation.

I was used to hostility. The more I faced it, the faster I could sense it and the easier it was to react.

But kindness without strings attached? That was unfamiliar and difficult. To me, kindness was a reward you only received after paying a sufficient price. Most of the goodwill I'd experienced in the past had been granted only after I won a match or claimed a championship.

*'Perhaps this is... happening because everyone on the Special Team is my subordinate.'*

I had become even more of a Young god than before.

My influence over my subordinates had grown stronger, so it was highly likely they had offered me the team because of it.

*It was only natural for my subordinates to like me.*

...Though it wasn't really a matter of "liking" or not.

Anyway, if it was due to my divine power, it was a rather bitter reason.

Checking my current status through the Status Window would solve most of my worries and questions, but I suppressed the urge.

They were the ones who had believed me even when I said the strange thing about being a god.

At the very least, it was only right for me to show them a corresponding level of trust.

Besides, maintaining the Special Team had been my greatest wish since I decided to stay at the Era Agency.

I truly wanted to do well for the team in the future.

*'I need to be careful around Captain Mo and Captain Kwak.'*

At The Cell X, every time I recalled the moments I acted as the *Young god*, I vowed over and over again:

*'I won't let the Young god control me too much.'*

I never wanted to show that side of myself to the Captains. In fact, they might have accepted me precisely because they didn't know the full details about the *Young god*. Now that we had finally become an official team, the mere thought of ruining it with my own hands was horrific.

Dressed in my full uniform, I looked in the mirror and glanced at my bare shoulders.

*'But why hasn't Dominic returned yet?'*

At this point, I was starting to get anxious. I could feel him constantly watching me. But I only felt his gaze; I had no idea what he was doing, which made me even more uneasy. The timing of his disappearance—right after I had regained some of my existence—made me even more impatient.

Even so, there was no way for me to find him.

With no other choice, I hugged my anxiety close and headed to the Chief's Office with Samra.

Jang Seokyun had to leave soon for an urgent business trip, so the meeting was scheduled to be brief. I didn't plan on taking much of his time either.

And the moment I entered the Chief's Office and came face-to-face with Jang Seokyun—

"...Trainee Han, you're here."

I felt a very strange sensation.

Before, I had always been incredibly nervous in front of Jang Seokyun. I had considered him a formidable opponent and tried my best to act bold.

But now...

*The thought crossed my mind that killing him would be a very simple solution.*

"..."

The Young god's way of thinking seemed to be closer to that of a social misfit. To be honest, I had also been shut in my room for four years, so my own personality couldn't exactly be called "social" either.

I'm sure I still have my quirks, but compared to the Young god, I was under the illusion that I seemed quite decent.

After trying hard not to come off as too arrogant, I adopted a more human-like mindset and spoke.

"Hello, Director. I've come to discuss the contract."

I could see a hint of undisguised agitation in Jang Seokyun's eyes as he looked at me.

Even for someone like Jang Seokyun, it must have been hard to act normal when a person declared dead had come back to life.

"I knew Trainee Han was extraordinary... but this time, I'm truly stunned."

Jang Seokyun stared at me for a moment before speaking.

"Samra informed me that Trainee Han is a different kind of being from the existing Transcendental Beings. This fact will henceforth..."

"This will be managed as a top-secret matter of the Era Agency. We will share it with the highest-ranking officials of the soon-to-be-established International Organization, but we intend to relay the information after a certain amount of time has passed."

He also explained that both inside and outside the Era Agency, they planned to announce that the recent death pronouncement was a temporary state caused by the closure of the Trial.

I stared quietly into Jang Seokyun's eyes and thought.

*'How would others perceive the existence of a 'god'?'*

The Special Team had experienced all sorts of things by my side and were also my subordinates, so they seemed to have come to understand the concept of a god relatively quickly.

Samra was close to being omniscient, knowing everything. Since he was already tracking the existence of Outer gods, he would have accepted it most easily, without any prejudice or preconceptions.

But I wondered if Jang Seokyun and the other humans who would eventually be informed of this fact would ever truly understand what a 'god' is.

I wondered if they would simply see the Young god as a mere anomaly and covet his utility.

And the moment Jang Seokyun held out the contract, my thoughts grew even clearer.

It was still a contract where the Era Agency held all the power.

"We will meet any conditions you desire, Trainee Han. We can even grant you a special promotion to Captain."

I didn't take the contract. I just kept my eyes lowered.

Even before entering The Cell X, I had resolved to sign a contract with Jang Seokyun that would reverse our roles—one where I held the upper hand.

I had even dragged Paseong into this for that very reason.

But now, the situation had changed completely.

The Special Team had chosen me.

I no longer had any reason to struggle to stay by their side.

What I wanted right now was the power to protect the team, and for that, I had to suppress Jang Seokyun a little.

"Director Jang."

I slowly lifted my gaze from the contract and met Jang Seokyun's eyes directly.

"The one I desperately need is* you*, Director."

Jang Seokyun failed to grasp my meaning immediately and replied, "To close the Trial in the Republic of Korea—"

"Isn't it more than just that?"

*I despised his arrogance. He had dared to speak recklessly of my subordinate's secret.*

*I wanted to make it impossible for him to ever torment my subordinate again.*

*I felt the Young god stirring within me.*

If I wanted to, I could suppress this being that was eager to burst out at any moment.

But for now, I thought it might be alright to play the part of a Young god.

After all, Jang Seokyun needed to know exactly what kind of being he was trying to restrain.

I continued speaking, feeling the color of my eyes shift.

"Isn't my presence essential if the Republic of Korea is to secure a pivotal position in the newly formed International Organization?"

I savored the shock written all over Jang Seokyun's face.

A thrilling sensation washed over me. Just a little further, and I would...

*I wanted to tear into his mind and crush it.*

I suppressed the impulse. But I couldn't stop the faint, involuntary curl of my lips.

"And I understand you signed the Dispatch Agreement with Paseong without consulting me."

The contract with Paseong that the directors had presented to me earlier was not something I had signed with CEO Cha.

It was a contract drawn up between Jang Seokyun and CEO Cha.

Samra had manipulated Jang Seokyun into believing that I would be the one to contract with Paseong, and this was the result.

Both Samra and CEO Cha were merely acting on my orders, but Jang Seokyun was completely in the dark.

"If you don't want to be chased by the International Organization and Paseong... I think you should be asking me for a favor instead of shoving a contract in my face."

I stood before the desk, looking down at Jang Seokyun.

The fear etched on his frozen face brought me pleasure.

I gave the wretched creature the exact words he needed to say.

"Say, *'Please help me.'*"

The air turned icy in an instant. Jang Seokyun dared not say a word to me.

He just stared at me, his lips parted as if he were in a trance.

The silence stretched on, unnervingly long.

Suddenly, a knock sounded at the door.

"Chief, we need to leave." an urgent voice called from outside.

Jang Seokyun snapped back to his senses.

His gaze flickered to the door for a moment before returning to me.

"...Han Goyo."

Ignoring his words, I pulled an item from my inventory.

It was a contract I had drafted myself.

Naturally, I was Party A, and the Era Agency was Party B.

As I laid the contract on the desk, I repeated the same words he had once said to me.

"I trust you will make a wise decision, Director Jang."

***

After the negotiation with Jang Seokyun, I intended to discuss our future plans with Samra.

But I couldn't.

In the Chief's Office, Samra had merely watched silently, as I'd requested, while gathering information on me. Yet, the moment my conversation with Jang Seokyun ended, he began pouring out a barrage of incredibly difficult questions, as if he'd been waiting for this exact moment.

"Han Goyo-ssi. I believe it is only right that I, too, be included within the scope of the abilities you employ as part of the Special Team."

His tone, though demanding something perfectly reasonable, felt heavy with pressure.

I hoped the System Window would appear, asking if I wanted to register Samra, but it remained silent. It hadn't shown up since The Cell X, so it seemed it hadn't fully recovered its strength yet.

I remembered the System Window that had appeared the first time I tried to register Samra.



<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">Non-human beings cannot be registered yet.</span>



The System Window remained silent, but I could still feel it.

I still couldn't take Samra as a subordinate.

I confessed the truth to him. "My abilities are still too limited. I can only use them on humans."

Samra's reaction suggested he found my statement utterly unconvincing. "I can think beyond human capacity and even experience emotions. Please tell me the exact criteria that define a human."

Even if I'd spent my life studying instead of playing games, I wouldn't have known how to answer that. I briefly wondered if a humanities or science major would be better equipped to handle such a question, then confessed my ignorance once more.

"I don't know either."

Samra fell silent.

He just stared at me. I apologized and promised to share more information with him, but he still didn't respond.

I hesitated before making a cautious promise.

"If you can just wait a little longer... I will make sure you can use your abilities too, Samra."

Samra, who had been staring at me, slowly closed and opened his eyes.

*'As if imitating a human.'*

"Understood."

His brief reply made me read his expression even more intently. But unless Samra chose to reveal his feelings, I had no way of knowing them.

With the impassive face of an android, Samra spoke calmly.

"My priority is your well-being, Han Goyo. Please do not overexert yourself. Furthermore, if I judge that you are taking dangerous actions, I will intervene."

The absolute calmness of his threat to intervene made it even more terrifying.

Unbidden, an image formed in my mind: me, trapped inside something like an incubation capsule.

I promised him I'd be absolutely careful. At the same time, I resolved to buy Samra a gift from the Galactic Trading Post once I earned some Star Fragments. I couldn't think of any other way to soothe the android.

After discussing our future plans, Samra and I went to bed early. Though my body felt fine, the day's events had left me mentally exhausted. I fell into a deep, dreamless sleep.

The next morning, I dressed in the casual clothes I'd asked Samra to prepare and took the phone he had made for me. The device, a product of Samra's advanced technology, offered no privacy, but I wasn't concerned—regular phones were the same anyway.

Today, to celebrate my newfound freedom, I planned to run a few errands. My first stop was the house I used to live in.

Samra had been managing the place for me all this time, and since I wouldn't be using it anymore, I figured I should clear it out. After all, I'd be staying at the Era Agency from now on.

Samra had mentioned, however, that they would soon provide me with a space other than the Quarantine Facility. I didn't really mind staying there, but I was hoping for a slightly larger room.

After that, I planned to visit CEO Cha in the hospital. I doubted he'd actually be lying in a hospital bed, but it was officially a "sick visit." I'd told him I would come, but since he wasn't the type to wait around, I'd already contacted Mok Saehwa.

Because I was visiting Paseong, a member of the Special Team would be accompanying me on my outing today.

I waited for the person who would pick me up, feeling incredibly awkward in my jeans and hoodie.

"...?"

I couldn't believe my eyes.

I almost asked, "Who are you?" but managed to stop myself just in time. Still, my mouth hung open, refusing to close.

As I stood there blankly, the man holding the door tilted his head slightly.

"Aren't you coming?"

His voice wasn't the low growl of a beast, but a pleasant baritone.

I answered, completely dazed. "Oh, no... I'm coming..."

It was Je Hyun-oh in human form.

I'd heard from Samra before that he had an item that could hide his infected form.

But... to run into him like this, without any warning...

I couldn't tear my eyes away from Je Hyun-oh.

His golden pupils, his metallic hand, and his wings were all gone. Dressed in light clothing instead of his usual all-black techwear, he felt like a completely different person.

But, most tragically, he was still wearing a black mask. It wasn't a gas mask, but it still obscured the lower half of his face.

*'Please, just take off the mask for one second.'*

The words I wanted to ask rose to my throat, but I couldn't bring myself to say them. Instead, I asked a different question.

"Are you okay without wearing the Crow Set?"

Je Hyun-oh stared at me, his eyes seeming to ask why I would even ask such a thing.

I gazed at his pupils, now devoid of their golden hue, and spoke.

"Ah, I thought you were wearing it because of the infection..."

Nothing was known about the Crow Set items he used.

It was only speculated that they suppressed &lt;Zero>'s infection.

After all, the Crow Gloves also transformed his metallic hand into a human one.

I worried a bit about what side effects might occur if he didn't wear the Crow items.

"Goyo."

"...Yes."

My response was a little slow because I was so unfamiliar with the sound of a human voice.

Je Hyun-oh slowly leaned toward me.

"I'll tell only you."

His whisper felt strangely chilling.

As I reflexively shrank back, he gently curved his eyes and said, "I've never been infected."
