<br>There was a hidden item in the second stage.

It was a reward granted only if every other player fell to their deaths.

After knocking Banana off the edge with the Marshmallow Glove, I threw another Jelly Ball at the ceiling fan.

I kept throwing them until I hit the fan six times. A rattling sound echoed, and the item dropped.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>【Legendary Lollipop】<br><br>
    A legendary lollipop said to have been crafted by the Primordial Sugar Artisan from a lightning bolt that struck a giant cotton candy cloud. Only a true Sugar Champion can wield it.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

The massive lollipop, with its round pink candy and white stick, was an invincible item.

Though its hit detection was difficult, a single successful strike would blast other players out of bounds.

I usually avoided using this because it would ruin the fun, but today was an exception.

I pulled a lollipop from my back and summoned the Sheep-Head Slime.

"Dominic. I'm about to get a bit violent."

I worried he might dislike the commotion, but the Sheep-Head Slime didn't seem to mind.

"Don't worry about me," he said, clinging squishily to my shoulder. He was wearing an orca plushie headband, which kept him perched there.

"By the way, Roasted Chestnut," he asked, his tone light but his curiosity genuine, "how much longer do you plan on keeping company with these creatures?"

To him, even my subordinates were little more than disposable tools.

Lately, he'd been tolerating my behavior, knowing how much I cherished my subordinates.

But now, seeing me brawl with mere humans—not even subordinates—Dominic must have found my actions utterly incomprehensible.

Even his words now felt heavily restrained.

I offered another excuse, playing the part of the alien again.

"I'm not trying to fit in. I'm just... observing the ecosystem. Studying the local fauna, you know?"

"I see," Dominic replied with a gentle admonishment. "At your age, Chestnut, curiosity is only natural. However, I advise you not to spend too much time on this."

I promised to be careful, yet a seed of anxiety took root in my chest.

I feared the day he would finally give up on understanding me and simply impose his will.

Even though Dominic always listened to my opinions.

My stomach churned as I imagined Dominic, his existence restored, crushing the Earth with a single gesture.

I had to calm my wounded heart.

"Dominic..."

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

"I need your urgent help."

As I held out my hands, I heard a soft *Hmm*.

I waited patiently, and the Sheep-Head Slime hopped right into my palms.

I took a moment to enjoy the squishy, healing sensation.

"Thank you, Dominic. This really helped a lot."

After thanking him profusely, I moved on to the next stage.

I breezed through to the Final Stage.

The countless obstacles and traps meant nothing to me.

It was even easier since I didn't have to teach Angel Wings how to play or worry about what others thought.

In an instant, I reached the Final Stage and stood before the finish line.

I swung my lollipop in the air a couple of times, waiting for the recruits.

Hopdpop was crawling with trolls. I'd dealt with enough of those bastards who deliberately messed with other players.

Thanks to that, I knew exactly how to make someone absolutely lose their shit.

After a rather dull wait, the recruits finally came into view.

They were sprinting in a final burst of speed, ecstatic at the prospect of taking first place. But when they saw me standing at the finish line, their eyes widened in shock.

"What the hell? What's he doing there?"

"Get that bastard out of here!"

The recruit leading the pack charged at me.

I decided to give my lollipop its first real workout.

*Wham!*

With a crisp thud, the recruit was sent flying.

After that, it was just a repeat performance.

The recruits tried teaming up to take me down, but it was useless. I sent every single one of them back to the starting line.

Just as the number of recruits who had lost all their Hearts and become Spectators was growing, Banana and his few remaining friends charged at me again.

Seeing them swarm from all sides, it seemed they'd come up with a plan, but I just sent them flying again.

I made sure to hit Banana extra hard, and he soared far and high into the air.

Just as I was savoring the satisfaction, I felt an intense gaze fixed on me.

Thinking it was just another batch of baseballs, I turned my head, only to flinch in surprise.

"...Lieutenant Won?"

Won Taebeom was here, in the Final Stage.

I had been told that once we entered Hopdpop, instructors would not intervene. They were to become spectators, only stepping in if they judged the stage impossible to clear.

So, I had been swinging my lollipop without a care in the world, only for Won Taebeom to suddenly appear.

I hid the lollipop behind my back.

Come to think of it, this was a training exercise for recruits, and I had been trolling way too much.

With a belated sense of remorse, I looked up at Won Taebeom.

"Um... the other recruits were interfering so much, I just... for a moment..."

I mumbled excuses, warily reading his expression.

I thought I might get scolded, but an unexpected question was asked instead.

"Are you injured?"

Hopdpop was a Trial so safe that the very concept of injury didn't even apply.

I answered, a bit flustered. "No, sir."

"Confirmed."

His blunt reply brought me a sense of relief. It seemed I wasn't in trouble after all.

He probably came over thinking something had happened, since the recruits were still Spectators.

I quietly looked up at Won Taebeom.

He was a man who clashed terribly with this cute world made of sweets.

In the sweet Hopdpop, Won Taebeom was like a piece of 99% cacao chocolate.

Right now, it was just him and me here.

It would take a considerable amount of time for the recruits I had knocked off the ledge to respawn and reach this spot.

I figured this was the best time to talk quietly. If we were seen together outside, people might think Lieutenant Won was giving me special treatment.

Besides, he looked like he had something to say to me, too. He hadn't taken his eyes off me for a while now.

I cautiously spoke up.

"Um, Lieutenant Won."

I tried to get ahead of him by immediately bowing my head, but—

"About The Cell X, I really—*Gasp!*"

I failed.

Won Taebeom had grabbed my shoulder, preventing me from bowing. Startled by the grip's strength, I remained half-bent as I belatedly finished my sentence.

"...I'm sorry."

Won Taebeom said nothing.

He seemed to be waiting for me to speak, so I stumbled through an apology.

"The... ability I used should be deactivated by now. If you're experiencing any issues, please let me know."

*Blind Faith* was an ability that manipulated the mind. Even if it had been deactivated, there could still be side effects.

He looks fine at a glance, but...

*He clearly wants to serve me as his master.*

Anyway, *he* just keeps popping out and saying strange things whenever *he* gets the chance.

If *he* had a physical form, I would have definitely smacked *him*.

Ignoring the thoughts of the social misfit, I said, "I will make sure this never happens again."

At that moment, Won Taebeom's face hardened completely.

His eyes met mine as he murmured softly, "Ever since then, I can't stop thinking about god."

*'I'm screwed.'*

The side effects of Blind Faith had taken hold.

Even though I'd been prepared for this, seeing it happen right in front of me made my head spin. I had done something terrible to a man who had once been a priest.

I finally managed to open my mouth.

"It's a side effect of my ability."

Won Taebeom's brow furrowed slightly. He asked slowly, "Is this... a side effect?"

"Yes, it's a side effect!"

I pleaded with him, "I'll definitely fix this. There has to be a cure."

I was going crazy with guilt for making a perfectly sane man act so strangely.

I apologized over and over, promising to restore him to his original state and to find a cure no matter what.

But the more I apologized, the darker Won Taebeom's eyes grew.

*'Is it because my promises are so uncertain?'* I thought I saw a flicker of despair in his gaze.

*He must be upset that he couldn't become my subordinate.*

I brushed off my own crazy thoughts. Just as I was at a loss, consumed by guilt...

Won Taebeom suddenly swayed violently.

"Lieutenant Won!"

I rushed to support him.

His complexion had turned noticeably pale, and he was drenched in a cold sweat. His chest heaved, his breath coming in ragged, shallow gasps.

His hand, clenched so tightly that the veins stood out blue, was trembling slightly.

I never expected to see this side of him—a man who had always been as steadfast as a rock.

*The snake is tormenting him... He's desperately searching for me...*

*'Snake?'*

The words were different from *his* usual delusions, and they nagged at me.

For a brief moment, my attention was diverted as I scanned the area for a place where Won Taebeom could sit.

He slowly straightened his posture and grabbed my arm.

"It's nothing," he said, his voice low and strained.

I looked at him. He didn't look okay at all.

But he quickly regained his composure. The darkness that had sunk into his eyes and the tremors of agony vanished without a trace as he spoke to me.

"What just happened has nothing to do with Trainee Han, so you don't need to worry about it."

"Lieutenant Won..."

"They said there's a way to treat it, so I'll wait."

He cut me off sharply, as if he didn't want to hear another word.

Another strange thought crossed my mind.

*I should give him a chance.*

*He will prove his faith to me.*

...I wanted to press a button and turn off my thoughts.

This crazy social misfit was way too confident. I had no idea where *his* unshakable belief that everyone liked *him* came from. Now, *he* was even acting like Won Taebeom was *his* believer.

While I was trying to shake the crazy social misfit's thoughts out of my head, Won Taebeom sent me toward the finish line.

"Trainee Han. Complete the race and wait for me."

"But..."

"That is an order from your instructor."

I kept looking back at Won Taebeom over and over again.

But his resolute gaze left me no choice but to cross the finish line.

Rainbow sprinkle fireworks exploded as a first-place congratulatory notification appeared.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>The Master of the Sweet Throne has arrived!<br><br>
    The victorious Sugar Champion, 'Little Lamb,' has crossed the finish line in first place!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I sat on the three-tiered sugar cake chair—the exclusive throne for the first-place winner—and waited for the remaining recruits to finish.

I killed time playing with a Sheep-Head Slime as the recruits arrived one by one.

Since I had sent so many of them to the Spectator area, there weren't many recruits left.

The recruits who arrived, panting for breath, shuddered at the sight of the lollipop on my back and kept their distance.

Whether they did or not, I waited for Angel Wings.

He never showed his face the entire time I acted as the Gatekeeper in the Final Stage.

It seemed he had the skill to pass the remaining stages, though.

Even after all the surviving recruits had arrived, Angel Wings still hadn't appeared.

After a while, Won Taebeom crossed the finish line.

I stepped down from the cake chair and saw Angel Wings following behind him.

Angel Wings immediately spotted me and gave a bright smile.

"All surviving recruits have finished the run. The practice session is now over," Won Taebeom declared.

A System Window appeared, announcing the completion.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>&lt;Hop Drop Pop> Clear!<br><br>
    - Sugar Champion Ending.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Han Goyo's name is now known in Akasha!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>■■ ■■■ cheers! He watches you with a sense of refreshing satisfaction.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Current Recognition: 10.003%</strong>
  </div>
</div>

The previous PTB only increased by 0.001%, but this time it gave me 0.002%.

Considering it was an easy Trial and not a True Ending, it seemed like a pretty generous boost.

*'But does that Black Block have nothing to do?'*

I'd been thinking this since the Day News, but it felt like he was staring at me 24/7.

*'Could he be an unemployed alien?'*

Even if he were unemployed, watching me 24/7 must be tough. At this point, I found it impressive.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Trial: &lt;Hop Drop Pop> has ended.<br>
    The Trial will close in 80 seconds.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You are the Top Contributor of &lt;Hop Drop Pop>.<br>
    You may take '1' item.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

Even though it was just a Normal Ending, I'd been secretly hoping for more, but it was only one item again.

If it were up to me, I'd take all the decorative items related to the Trial.

I stared at the System Window with regret before selecting an item.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Item selection complete.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

It was the Orca Plushie Headband.

The lollipop would have been more useful, but I chose the orca anyway.

After finalizing my choice, I glanced around and saw the other recruits. The sight felt strange.

Ever since &lt;Happy Smile Factory>, I had always been left alone at the end of each Trial.

PTB had been with others, but that was an abnormal ending...

*'Maybe it's because this isn't the True Ending,'* I thought.

Just as I was waiting for the Trial to close, Angel Wings approached me.

"Trainee Han Goyo! Congratulations on taking first place!"

I looked up at him. Staring at the man who seemed more delighted than if he had won himself, I asked, "Tell me your name."

"Huh? My name?"

Angel Wings flinched, his wings fluttering. His reaction made it seem as if he couldn't possibly dare to tell me his name.

When I just kept staring, Angel Wings spoke shyly.

"Then... I'll tell you outside."

With only a few seconds left before the Trial closed, I nodded.

Angel Wings suddenly leaned in toward me.

"Trainee Han Goyo."

His voice dropped an octave as he called my name, sending a strange shiver through me.

He met my eyes and said, "Next time we meet, I'll introduce you to my friends. They're all good people, and they're eager to meet you. Ah, though their first impressions might be a bit... off."

The eyes that had been half-hidden by his bangs curved into a gentle smile.

"You'll end up liking them too, Trainee."

His eyes held no trace of timidity or shyness.

In that instant, the world seemed to melt away.

I found myself outside the Trial.

"..."

As soon as I regained consciousness, I slowly pushed myself up.

The faint, final words of Angel Wings weighed heavily on my mind.

I scanned the other trainees scattered around, intending to ask him directly what he meant.

...But he was gone.

Just then, Won Taebeom stirred. I immediately approached him and reported, "Lieutenant Won. One of the trainees is missing. Thirteen of us entered, but there are only twelve in the classroom now."

Despite having just woken up, Won Taebeom's gaze was sharp as he raised an eyebrow.

"Trainee Han."

Then, he told me something unbelievable.

"The number of recruits who entered Hopdpop was twelve."
