<br>The moment I saw Je Hyun-oh, only one thought crossed my mind.

*My subordinate!*

I wasn't the least bit curious about how he'd managed to enter this Trial, which had a strict limit on the number of participants.

*He missed me that much.*

*So much that he couldn't wait a single moment.*

Though a considerable amount of time had passed inside the Trial, it must have been just an instant in the outside world. The fact that he couldn't even endure that short wait and came looking for his master brought an involuntary smile to my face.

Smiling, I gazed at my subordinate.

He approached me with slow, deliberate steps.

Bloody footprints stained the floor.

The man who had left a trail of crimson marks came to a halt before me, his eyes scanning me from head to toe. I could feel his gaze sweeping over me.

*I loved the moment my subordinate thought only of me.*

And as expected...

*His original form was the best.*

The appearance he had used to mimic a human was not his true essence. I preferred the strongest and fastest metallic wings, the metallic hands that could tear through anything, and the golden pupils embedded in the center of his eyes.

My subordinate, who had been staring intently at my smiling face, finally turned his gaze away.

CEO Cha stood frozen, sword in hand, at my subordinate's sudden appearance. Even for him, this must have been a first.

He had forcibly broken through the Trial System. This was an act that defied all standards, shaking the very foundations of order and law.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je... You had a trick I didn't know about."

My subordinate showed no reaction to CEO Cha's words.

He merely stared at CEO Cha through his dark, unlit goggles.

*K-boom!*

In an instant, he swung his metallic hand.

The white sword and black metal clashed.

After a brief, tense friction, Je Hyun-oh swung his metallic wings.

CEO Cha threw himself backward, but the sharp blade had already grazed his skin.

Blood sprayed, staining the subject's clothes.

Je Hyun-oh pursued the retreating CEO Cha, swinging his metallic hand again.

CEO Cha's upper body lurched forward as he tried to dodge the blow.

Je Hyun-oh seized the opening, kicking CEO Cha squarely in the solar plexus.

CEO Cha's body slammed straight into the wall. Around him, the wall buckled and cratered like a meteor impact site.

As he approached the figure embedded in the wall, his metal hand screeched and groaned.

CEO Cha briefly lifted the bottom of his mask and spat out a mouthful of blood.

After readjusting the mask, he rose silently, gripping his sword.

And just as they clashed again—

*This is delightful.*

The brilliantly exploding exchange of light was entertaining.

CEO Cha threw his head back to dodge, but the fingertips still left a shallow, long scratch beneath his chin.

But before the blood could even stain his collar, the next attack came.

Blue veins bulged on the back of CEO Cha's hand as he gripped his sword.

"...!"

A black metallic feather finally embedded itself in CEO Cha's thigh.

As if possessed by a will of its own, the feather burrowed into his flesh and scraped against his bone. CEO Cha's body lurched violently.

Immediately after, feathers struck his other thigh and both arms.

The thick scent of blood snapping me back to my senses.

I really had to stop him.

*Why? Let's play a little longer.*

Just as I was about to ignore the social misfit's thoughts and impose a restriction, the world began to warp and melt in a kaleidoscope of colors.

It was time to go back outside.

<br>To think I found it amusing to watch Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha fight...

The mind of a social misfit was truly insane.

I had a feeling the two would continue their brawl even after we got out.

The moment my consciousness returned, I snapped my eyes open.

"...?"

Prayer beads were tangled everywhere.

"I knew it. I knew this would happen."

Kwak Hanmuk stood in the center of the ruined CEO's office, arms crossed.

The prayer beads, radiating from him, had bound me, Je Hyun-oh, and CEO Cha tightly in mid-air.

Je Hyun-oh was strangely docile, as if his mood had improved slightly.

CEO Cha, however, was bleeding profusely.

Blood gushed from the limbs where the feathers had been embedded, and the pool at his feet was steadily widening.

Beside him, Mo Haein pointed the Black Moon Sword at CEO Cha, her expression cold.

"You really are something, Representative Cha," Kwak Hanmuk said, glaring at him with a frown. "Where did you learn such manners—throwing a visitor who came to wish you well into a Trial?"

"..."

CEO Cha remained silent.

Mo Haein, still aiming the sword at him, spoke softly. "Representative Cha, the Era Agency is authorized to use force."

She glanced to the side. "The responsibility is clear. We expect you to handle the aftermath properly."

Mok Saehwa and Seo Munchan stood by the door to the office. Their expressions suggested they had expected this outcome from the moment they planned the visit.

Seo Munchan stepped forward with a genial smile.

"My sincerest apologies, Captains."

He offered a polite request, apologizing directly without any excuses.

"Paseong will compensate you separately for this incident. If you're willing, could you please return our Representative to us?"

As if annoyed, Kwak Hanmuk flicked his prayer beads, and CEO Cha was instantly transported.

Mok Saehwa and Seo Munchan immediately caught him.

CEO Cha refused the directors who tried to help him up and looked at me.

He stared quietly into my eyes, which must have returned to their normal color, no longer gold.

"Representative Cha."

I spoke his name first, then said calmly, "I hope nothing like this ever happens again."

As if the moment I had shown him my rock bottom during the Trial had never existed.

CEO Cha let out a short laugh.

That was all. In the end, he gave no answer.

Under Kwak Hanmuk's arrangements, I was sent back to the Era Agency.

Je Hyun-oh was loaded into a container that Kwak Hanmuk had prepared in advance. Mo Haein decided to accompany him, just in case.

Before boarding the container, Je Hyun-oh gestured to me with his metallic hand. I hurried to his side and whispered the words I had been wanting to say all along.

"I'm sorry for making you wait..."

At that, Je Hyun-oh lifted his goggles, pushing them up onto his forehead. The man who had just revealed his golden-pupiled eyes stared intently at me.

"Goyo."

He smiled, his eyes narrowing into thin slits.

"Aren't you curious how I got in?"

"Will you answer if I ask?"

"No."

"..."

"But keep wondering anyway."

"What...?"

Je Hyun-oh pulled his goggles back on and disappeared into the container.

While I was talking to Je Hyun-oh, Mo Haein exchanged a silent look with Kwak Hanmuk. After their unspoken conversation, she shut the container door.

Then, I was driven back in the military vehicle Kwak Hanmuk had brought. It was the same model as the sedan Je Hyun-oh had driven, but the ride quality was worlds apart.

Sitting in the passenger seat, I praised Kwak Hanmuk's driving skills while glancing at the back seat. It was piled high with the desserts I had planned to buy. There was an endless supply of paper bags and cake boxes, all stamped with the cafe's logo.

*'So this is what he meant when he said he was going to buy them himself.'*

It seemed he had planned to come to Paseong all along.

"Chestnut, I bought you coffee. Drink up."

Kwak Hanmuk jerked his chin toward the iced americano in a cup holder and turned the steering wheel. Next to the coffee, a refreshing fruit smoothie—or at least a cool-looking drink—was tucked side by side in the holder.

I sipped the coffee and stared at the System Window floating before my eyes.

By closing The Cell X and PTB, I had completed two out of the seven. But I couldn't shake the feeling that PTB was far from enough. The modest rise in my recognition score alone proved it.

I felt a little dazed.

Je Hyun-oh tearing through the Trial and even speaking informally to CEO Cha in his anger—it all felt surreal, like a dream.

*'How on earth did Je Hyun-oh get in?'*

To forcibly enter a Trial with a strict participant limit... The image of his metallic hand seemingly slicing through space was still vivid in my mind.

It didn't even look like he'd done this for the first time.

*'And yet, it's not an infection...'*

I tried to cool my overheating brain with coffee, but I knew I wouldn't find an answer. There was just too much I didn't know.

I fought the urge to peer deeper into Je Hyun-oh and forced myself to think about something else.

Still, his appearance in PTB had helped me snap out of it.

Until then, I had been acting completely irrationally.

I had momentarily lost control of my emotions and yelled at CEO Cha.

Even if he had dragged me through the dirt, my reaction was excessive. I regretted it a little, but I knew I wouldn't have been able to hold back even if I went back to that moment.

Still, one thing became clear.

Both CEO Cha and I were hiding something, but neither of us had any intention of being the first to lay our cards on the table. In a situation where we couldn't trust each other, even if I were to tell him everything first, he wouldn't reveal his own secrets.

For someone who had even earned the Disbeliever Trait, there was only one way to get anything out of him right now.

*'He'll only open up if I sign the Hamster Contract.'*

Setting aside the impossible method, I slowly continued my train of thought.

That wasn't the only irrational thing I'd done.

As a young god, I had peered into the mind of the Paranoid Researcher.

Because I'd looked without restraint, the NPC had completely lost his mind.

Yet, I felt nothing in particular.

Instead, since he was already broken, I only thought about delving deeper into his mind.

*'What if I did that to Lieutenant Colonel Je or the Captains?'*

Above all else, that was my main concern.

I was terrified that I might do something strange the moment I lost control.

The Captains didn't even clearly understand what a god was...

I was just weakly sipping my coffee, my mind a jumbled mess of worries and thoughts.

"Goyo."

"Yes, sir."

"Are you okay?"

"Yes. I'm fine."

Kwak Hanmuk glanced at me sideways and sighed for some reason.

"You went to visit a patient, but you're the one who went through a Trial and came out."

"...I'm sorry."

"No, CEO Cha is the crazy one. Captain Mo and I were shocked."

He examined my face again and spoke.

"Tell me what happened."

I recounted the events in Paseong as briefly as possible.

The information would have been shared with Samra through the phone I carried anyway, but I also told him about Geumcheon and reporter Sa Gongyeon.

And the story about CEO Cha and the Trial... I explained it in a bit more detail.

Kwak Hanmuk listened to the whole thing, only offering short acknowledgments.

Only after I finished speaking did he say in a low voice, "About the dispatch contract. If it's a burden, I'll see if I can resolve it."

But the dispatch contract with Paseong was also for my own benefit.

When I told him again that I was fine, Kwak Hanmuk fell silent for a moment.

He tapped the steering wheel with his fingers, his brow furrowed slightly.

"Captain Kwak."

"Hmm?"

"Thank you for coming today. We survived because you and the other captains came."

"Ah. I was planning to come anyway."

He had been waiting nearby, but Samra had sensed the Trial's onset and sent Kwak Hanmuk up to the Representative's Office.

After giving me a brief explanation of the situation, Kwak Hanmuk drove in silence for a while.

Then, as if suddenly remembering, he spoke.

"You have to move out of your quarters tomorrow. Pack your bags tonight."

Having already heard about this from Samra, I replied immediately, "Yes, sir."

"Where am I going?"

"You'll be moving to the Era Agency's official residence, Brother Chestnut."

*'...Is that really okay?'*

They hadn't kept me in the Quarantine Facility for no reason. Even if it served no practical purpose, they needed to maintain the appearance of keeping me confined for others to see.

I had naturally assumed the new place would be similar to the Quarantine Facility.

Sensing my bewildered gaze, Kwak Hanmuk smirked and recited a six-digit number.

"The passcode to the house."

I memorized the numbers instantly and asked again.

"By the way, Captain Kwak. Is it alright if I stay in a regular officer's quarters?"

"No, it's not."

"Then..."

"I've decided to take charge of Foot Bomb," he said, his gaze fixed straight ahead. "It's a three-bedroom place, so it won't be cramped with just the two of us... but there's nothing in the house besides a bed. Let me know if you need anything."

"...Am I staying in the same quarters as you, Captain Kwak?"

"Uh-huh. That way, if you cause trouble, I can bind you up with prayer beads."

My eyes widened in shock, and Kwak Hanmuk let out a soft chuckle.

"Deal with it, even if you're unhappy. Otherwise, you're heading back to the Quarantine Facility."

"Ah, no. I have no complaints."

I truly had no complaints at all. Which was exactly why I was even more worried.

"I'm fine with it, but I'm worried Captain Kwak might find it uncomfortable..."

After all, he'd been living alone and comfortably, and now he had to take in someone he was supposed to be monitoring. It was bound to be a nuisance.

I watched his expression, worried I was causing trouble, but Kwak Hanmuk just smiled.

When the car stopped at a red light, he leaned over the steering wheel to look at me.

Then he said playfully, "Get ready. I'm going to work you like a dog, recruit."

"Yes, sir. I'll handle all the cleaning and everything else."

After that, Kwak Hanmuk and I talked about the move. He mentioned that Captain Mo lived in the same building, so we could occasionally grab late-night snacks together. By the time we finished our casual chat, we had already arrived at the headquarters.

We agreed that Kwak Hanmuk would pick me up tomorrow morning to move out, and I returned to my quarters with a heart full of anticipation.

I had just closed the door and was about to set down my duffel bag when...

"..."

The strength left my hands.

*Thud.* The bag hit the floor.

"Roasted Chestnut?"

A voice, tinged with surprise, called out.

The figure, who had been leaning down, brought his face close to mine and examined me. I couldn't say a word; I just trembled.

He let out a short laugh, then caused his form to collapse. The three-meter-tall man shed his human shape, twisting into a grotesque mass. Countless eyes of every imaginable color stared at me, then slowly shrank.

And finally...

He returned to a form I recognized.

A small, blue-glowing slime sat on the table, its squishy body calling out to me.

"There, Roasted Chestnut. It's alright now."

"..."

"See how small I am? In this form, I can't harm you at all."

"......"

"Roasted Chestnut."

I forced my feet to move, each step a struggle. I slowly approached him.

Staring at the small slime, my heart gradually began to calm.

As I drew near, Dominic looked up at me and spoke.

"I've just returned from exploring the area around Roasted Chestnut's planet. Forgive me for disappearing without a word."

He offered a gentle apology, his form shimmering.

I tried to ask if his journey had gone well, but the words wouldn't come.

Just as I was struggling to even part my lips, the one who shone like an infinite universe of stars whispered to me.

"Roasted Chestnut is holding something that belongs to me."

I immediately thought of the signet ring I had received from Clean3's butlers.

Then, I heard his voice—a gentle, low tone, as if he were trying his best not to frighten me.

"Will you return it to me now?"
