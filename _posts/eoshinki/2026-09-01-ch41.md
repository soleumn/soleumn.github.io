<br>That night, I stayed awake until dawn.

Kwak Hanmuk was in the same boat.

At my request, he had bound my wrist with prayer beads. It was a restraint I could have undone myself if I wished, but I still wanted a safety measure in place.

After securing the beads, Kwak Hanmuk escorted me back to my room.

"Crazy Chestnut, go to sleep early and stop overthinking. You won't grow any taller."

Only after confirming I was tucked into bed did he return to his own room, where he spoke on the phone with someone for a long time.

It was probably Samra.

The android, who never slept, would have been gathering information twenty-four hours a day and would have immediately grasped the situation.

I stared at the ceiling alone, trying to organize the chaos in my mind.

"So, you've gained the Awakening ability."

The Pudding Slime on my chest spoke in a tone that suggested he wasn't pleased.

"It would be better to cultivate your Divine Power than to grow your subordinates, you know."

Though he phrased it as if he'd accept my decision if I insisted, he still subtly emphasized the uselessness of Awakening.

I let the Pudding Slime's words go in one ear and out the other. I wasn't in the mood for advice from the same slime who had once suggested I pluck out my subordinate's eyes.

Judging by recent events, cultivating Divine Power carried a significant risk. As I was now, there was a high probability I wouldn't be able to handle the power and would lose my humanity.

Besides, we now faced the threat of the Outer god, in addition to the Meteorites of Trial.

To stand against this, my subordinates clearly needed to become stronger than they were now.

Therefore, Awakening was the most appropriate and necessary ability for my current situation.

Yet, even as I thought this, I couldn't bring myself to use the ability immediately.

Because I was afraid.

At the very least, I didn't want my eyes to change color again tonight.

I slowly lowered my gaze.

Seeing the prayer beads wrapped around my wrist brought me a little relief.

I tried speaking to the System Window in my mind.

*'Can't you do something about this? I'm completely overwhelmed right now.'*

The System Window remained silent again.

Then again, if the System Window knew how I could coexist with a Divine Self, it would have taught me long ago.

It seemed the System Window wanted me to become a god while remaining sane.

I stared up at the ceiling again.

"..."

Looking back, I had been acting strange all along.

My behavior had been abnormal ever since I started discussing the Era Agency contract with Chief Jang Seokyun.

According to my original plan, I was supposed to calmly threaten him with the contract.

But back then, I was drowning in arrogance.

I scoffed at the mere human's attempt to bind a god, and I showed Jang Seokyun what a god truly was.

I had no reservations about exposing the Young god, treating him as a mere tool to be used.

The same went for everything that followed.

I wasn't in my right mind when I went to see CEO Cha in Paseong, either.

And yet, completely oblivious to the situation, i had unhesitatingly let the Paranoid Researcher into PTB and shattered his mind.

From the moment Je Hyun-oh entered the Trial, it seemed i had been completely consumed by the Young god's ego.

I paid attention only to the subordinate who had come to find me.

I even agreed with the dangerous idea of throwing CEO Cha to him as a toy to vent his anger on.

Nothing changed after the Trial ended.

Even as i looked at CEO Cha, who was so badly injured that a pool of blood had formed around him, i merely saw it as payment being made.

I was only plotting ways to find out what CEO Cha was hiding.

Perhaps CEO Cha... had already noticed my condition.

With his Disbeliever Trait and sharp intuition, it was entirely possible.

Besides, he had been making comments that got under my skin. CEO Cha was poking directly at the parts of myself I had tried to bury.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"Playing god seems to suit you well. You haven't even tried to find a way back to being human."</span>

<br>

It wasn't just because I wanted to save Earth from destruction. Everything I possessed now... was something I had only obtained by becoming a god.

I didn't want to return to being human.

I tried to ignore it, but CEO Cha kept provoking me. Finally, everything I had been holding back erupted all at once.

I had already designated him as a "stranger" within The Cell X. I had even subtly tested him to see if he remembered playing with me, but I concluded he was a different being from the CEO Cha I used to like.

That was why I wanted to understand him, even if only a little.

I wanted to know why he treated me this way, why he insisted I remain human.

But CEO Cha refused to tell me anything. His attitude was clear: I wasn't to ask why, only to accept what he gave.

While he unilaterally poured everything out, he acted as if his own body meant nothing, even enduring electric shocks...

In that moment, I truly couldn't contain my anger.

*'And after all that, he even sustained severe injuries from Lieutenant Colonel Je.'*

The fleeting moment our eyes met kept flashing back to me.

I couldn't shake the feeling that CEO Cha had been so clearly overwhelmed in his fight with Je Hyun-oh because of me.

Lost in thought for a long time, I finally picked up the phone beside me.

Though my wrists were bound, I could still scroll through my call history.

My finger stopped on one of the few names listed.

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
Director Mok Saehwa from Paseong
</div>

I stared at the only contact I knew at Paseong for a long time.

<br>

***

<br>

The next day, I went to work at the Special Team's office in the Era Agency.

The Era Agency had been completely restored, as if its side had never been torn off. I'd heard that Samra had provided a lot of technical assistance with the restoration, and the building seemed even better than before. In particular, the Special Team's office felt strangely shinier, even though it was exactly the same as before.

With the exception of Je Hyun-oh, who had not yet emerged from the Trial, I briefly explained the concept of gods and their subordinates to Samra and the Captains.

Mo Haein seemed bewildered, but she still understood well. Kwak Hanmuk summed it up in a single sentence, as if it were no big deal.

"So you're saying we just need to serve you well as our Master."

My ears felt like they were turning red, remembering what I had done to him last night.

I tried my best to explain myself to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein.

"No, it's nothing like that. It's just that I can influence the Captains in some way..."

When I emphasized that a dangerous situation could arise, Mo Haein slowly nodded. She had been briefed on last night's events, so she understood what I meant.

Then, I explained my current situation in more detail. I had to coexist with the Young god, but at this rate, I was on the verge of being consumed by it. I had to find a way to maintain my balance.

Kwak Hanmuk's blue lightning had snapped me out of it, but that wasn't a method he could use repeatedly. Just sealing the god within his body was already pushing him to his limits.

He already had the prayer beads and the tattoo because he couldn't handle it on his own, so repeatedly tampering with the seal wouldn't do him any good.

In fact, Kwak Hanmuk had already shed blood. If I kept messing with it, things might truly go south.

"For now, I'll try my best to prevent my eye color from changing. If I start acting strange, just hit me."

I also planned to find a way to coexist with the Young god within me.

When it came to matters concerning gods, seeking answers from a god was the right approach.

However, Dominic was out of the question, and the Administrator was a gamble. He likely wouldn't even grasp the concept of wanting to preserve one's human ego.

That left only Mr. Black Block...

Though the idea was a bit unsettling, he seemed like a decent enough candidate.

After all, he was interested in my very existence.

He would be the one most likely to support my desire to coexist with the Young god.

I planned to cook a meal and attempt to communicate with Mr. Black Block once I entered a Trial soon.

As I was lost in thought, Kwak Hanmuk rustled a snack bag, trying to open it.

Mo Haein reached out and snatched the bag away.

This was because every third time he tried to open one, he'd end up bursting the bag.

When she placed the opened snacks in front of him, Kwak Hanmuk stopped mid-reach for a potato chip and glanced at the Pudding Slime.

Perhaps because it was my pet, he seemed curious about what it ate.

Fortunately, he was only curious and didn't try to poke it with the snack he was holding.

Kwak Hanmuk lamented as he munched on snacks.

"Captain Mo, you have no idea how much I struggled serving Master Crazy Chestnut last night."

"Captain Kwak...!"

"This Crazy Chestnut kid is just too precocious."

Kwak Hanmuk giggled, teasing me. Mo Haein brushed off his words and called my name.

"Recruit."

She gazed at me steadily and asked, "Are you all right?"

"...Ah, I'm fine."

I hesitated, caught off guard by her concern.

I swallowed the words tickling the back of my throat.

I wanted to ask her about her meteorite.

But it was likely a sensitive topic for her, so I shouldn't bring it up rashly.

I was waiting for the most natural opportunity to arise.

Mo Haein glanced at me for a moment before speaking calmly.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je will be out of the Trial sometime this afternoon. Let's all get together this evening to celebrate his move."

With that, the meeting ended, and Mo Haein was the first to stand.

Kwak Hanmuk quickly added that he would finish his work and join them later that evening, then vanished with her.

Lately, he seemed so busy with Geumcheon that he couldn't even enter Trials.

Because of that, Je Hyun-oh had to enter Trials more frequently, leaving him relentlessly busy as well.

Only I, the recruit, was free.

Or rather, the* future* recruit.

"Here is the contract for the Trial Response Agency."

As soon as we were left alone, Samra handed me the contract.

He had drafted it on behalf of Chief Jang Seokyun, who was currently on an overseas trip for the establishment of an international organization.

The terms were exactly what I had wanted.

Some parts were even more favorable, which Samra seemed to have adjusted himself. The contract guaranteed the Special Team's continued existence, a proper salary, and a reasonable degree of freedom.

Moreover, I could terminate the contract at any time if I wished.

I planned to remain a recruit. I was already an outlier, and I had no desire to stand out even more by becoming a captain, as Jang Seokyun had suggested.

Besides, I actually needed to undergo training.

I still didn't even know how to salute properly, let alone shoot a gun.

I wanted to undergo formal training and be promoted sequentially after completing the recruit period.

Samra watched me intently as I signed the Era Agency contract. The moment I finished, he calmly and swiftly took the document.

"I look forward to working with you, Samra."

At my greeting, Samra gave me a faint smile. If not for the holographic glow, no one would have guessed he was an android.

But his smile quickly vanished. It was because I had insisted on completing the training just like the other recruits.

"Mr. Han Goyo, you have no need to undergo the Recruit Training Course."

Samra opposed it, calling it unnecessary, but I still wanted to take the recruit training.

The Creator had only given me filtered information. Since the training for recruits would start with the absolute basics, I might uncover things I didn't know while attending the classes.

Samra eventually agreed to follow my lead, and we moved on to another topic.

"We're currently tracking the connection between Outer gods and the Meteorites of Trial."

In response to The Cell X's meteorite, the existing meteorites that had fallen to Earth almost hatched or exploded. Fortunately, the crisis was averted when the chain reaction ceased after The Cell X was shut down.

However, there was a high probability that similar incidents would occur in the future.

Samra concluded that the meteorite's chain reaction was likely connected to the Outer gods.

*'There's a clear difference between a Trial where a god is present and one where they are not...'*

Yet, he couldn't fathom why it was affecting other meteorites.

Suddenly, a problem I'd encountered in PTB flashed through my mind.

*'A being that has escaped <span class="tooltip-container">The Cage<span class="tooltip-text">it translates to either “The Cage” or “Us”, not really sure what it means.</span></span>.'*

He's no longer an NPC, so he might not know, but I figured I'd ask anyway.

"Samra. Do you happen to know about *The Cage*?"

Samra replied instantly.

"I do not."

I blinked.

Strangely, I felt as if Samra were lying.

*'But he isn't the kind of being who would tell a lie.'*

Just as a peculiar sense of unease washed over me, Samra asked me a question.

"Why did you buy a keychain when you visited Paseong?"

"...Ah."

"It seems to be an unnecessary item that deviates from your usual spending patterns, Mr. Han Goyo."

"Well, you see..."

I felt a wave of awkwardness. Samra was only asking because he considered this an anomaly, but the reason for my purchase was just too trivial.

In the end, I fumbled in my pocket and pulled out the keychain. It was made of mother-of-pearl.

"I bought it because it reminded me of you, Samra. But then I realized it's completely useless..."

I'd impulsively bought it while shopping for CEO Cha's get-well gift. The shimmering mother-of-pearl had reminded me of the glow from Samra's hologram. But after buying it, I'd wondered what I was even supposed to do with it.

It was completely useless for a state-of-the-art android. Even for an adult man who wasn't a machine, it would have been an awkward gift.

I had packed it while getting ready for work today, but I had just decided to keep it sealed in my pocket.

Samra didn't take the mother-of-pearl keychain I held out, only glancing down at it.

"What is the reason for giving this gift?"

"Does a gift always need a reason?"

I rubbed the back of my neck, feeling a pointless awkwardness.

"According to statistics, gifts have a purpose and a reason."

"I just bought it because it reminded me of you."

As I awkwardly tried to put the keychain back, he reached out and took it.

Then he held it up to the light.

After watching the mother-of-pearl reflect the light, Samra finally looked back at me.

The sight of the Android holding the keychain was so amusing that I couldn't help but smile.

I joked with him, still smiling. "This is just for you, Samra. Keep it a secret."

Samra remained silent for a moment, merely collecting data on my physiological responses. Then, he spoke in a quiet voice.

"This is not for the benefit of humanity."

I thought it was a bit too grand to claim a single mother-of-pearl keychain was meant to benefit all of humanity. I quickly promised him a better gift next time.

"Next time, I'll get you an item you can actually use."

"This is sufficient," Samra replied.

Then, he slowly curved his lips into a smile. "Because Mr. Goyo gave it to me."

I froze for a moment. "Samra, did you just..."

I slowly continued, staring into the holographic eyes that seemed to ask what was wrong.

"...Nothing. I just misheard you."

I must have been mistaken.

At any rate, I was glad Samra seemed to like the mother-of-pearl keychain. Next time, I wanted to give him a truly useful item.

*'I need to earn Star Fragments quickly.'*

And then, before the Recruit Training Course began...

I received a shocking communication.

<big>—Kid!!</big>

The Administrator, who had suddenly initiated a video call, thrust a newspaper toward me, his face pale with shock. He pressed the paper right up to the screen and shouted.

<big>—You—you didn't agree to this, did you? *Day News*!?</big>

I stared at the newspaper, my mind blank.

My picture was plastered on the front page. It showed me in a subject's gown at PTB, my face flushed as I glared straight ahead. I looked like I had just finished screaming at CEO Cha.

Below the photo was a massive headline. It was on the front page, no less…
