<br>At the same moment I gained a new ability, I regained consciousness.

*Regained* might not have been the right word.

It wasn't as if I had fainted.

Every action I had taken as the Young god flooded back to me with vivid clarity.

"..."

I stared at the creatures.

At the creatures, now nothing more than melted, wretched fragments of flesh and eyeballs.

As I took in the remains at my feet, my body began to tremble.

A tide of horrific thoughts surged through my mind.

I gagged, but nothing came up.

Shivering, I hugged the golden sphere tightly.

Then, I forced my thoughts to a halt.

Even thinking such thoughts was a luxury I couldn't afford right now.

I focused on a single thought.

*'I have to do this.'*

I had created a new ability, but these creatures were not my subordinates.

I had only bound them with the flimsy shackle of *Temporary Possession*, but there was still a chance.

I had used *Punishment* on a ■■■ who wasn't my subordinate, so *Resurrection* should have been possible too.

But that was only theoretically possible. Nothing was certain.

I felt my heart pounding violently as I activated the skill.

*Resurrection.*

I tried to revive Orca, the first one to die, but the first attempt failed.

Cold sweat drenched my entire body. Sweat dripped continuously from my chin. My cheeks were damp.

"Please..."

I kept trying to use Resurrection, but I failed again and again.

I watched Orca gradually fade away. Only a tiny fragment of him remained.

My vision kept blurring, and I blinked repeatedly.

I had to find another way.

I... broke myself.

I heard a phantom sound, as if something in my deepest depths was shattering.

The heat I had locked away in my heart surged through my veins.

But it wasn't beyond my control.

I spoke as a Young god.

*Resurrection.*

A faint light formed.

The light, sprouting like a new bud, gradually grew as clear as a star.

Just as it sparkled with a brilliant golden hue—

"...!"

In an instant, a flash erupted, and the entire area was engulfed in light.

It lasted only a few seconds, yet it blanketed the entire world in gold before vanishing.

A System Window appeared.

The moment I saw the neat, square window, the strength drained from my legs.

I couldn't even collapse to the ground. I lay sprawled on my stomach, gasping for air.

"Haa, haa..."

I couldn't even twitch a finger.

It wasn't physical exhaustion; it felt as if something deep inside me had been damaged.

A dull ache began to throb, but I deliberately ignored it.

Still, as I stopped thinking about it, the pain seemed to gradually subside.

Gritting my teeth, I reached out with a trembling hand.

I barely managed to grab the golden sphere I had dropped when I fell, pulling it back into my embrace.

The sphere, softly radiating light, remained as serene as ever.

I let out a sigh of relief.

I had actually considered using Resurrection on Captain Kwak.

But since he wasn't dead, I was too afraid of what might happen and decided against it.

I stared at the sphere for a while before turning my gaze away.

A System Window had appeared, stating that Resurrection was in progress, but the percentage refused to budge from 1%.

Even after staring at it for a long time, the number remained unchanged.

I knew it instinctively.

*'This is the Domain of ■■■.'*

For my power to take effect, I would have to subdue ■■■ and seize control of the domain.

I forced myself to stand up.

I thought I wouldn't be able to move at all, but when I forced it, I managed to stir.

I looked around.

Endless eyes rolled beneath my feet, and above my head, the Sea of Neo surged.

Nothing else was visible.

It was as if everything, living and non-living alike, had melted away.

*'Lieutenant Colonel Je, Lieutenant Won... Where is CEO Cha?'*

I tried sending a message to Je Hyun-oh, but it felt pointless.

I brought the System Window, tucked away in the corner of my vision, to the center of my sight.

Designed like a game UI, it displayed a concise summary of characters, health, skills and their cooldowns, as well as buffs and debuffs.

Je Hyun-oh, Kwak Hanmuk, CEO Cha, and Won Taebeom appeared in the list with cute character portraits.

I quickly checked their current status.

Kwak Hanmuk's slot was grayed out, which seemed to indicate he was incapacitated.

As for the others, their health bars were more than half empty.

CEO Cha was in particularly critical condition. His health had dropped below 30%.

It was all because of Light Modulation.

Light Modulation forced opponents to expose their weaknesses, dealing fatal damage. But such immense destructive power came at a steep price.

The skill generated light by burning the user's own life force.

CEO Cha had found the truth among tens of thousands of false eyes, and the enraged ■■■ had overturned the world, leading to this moment.

According to the System Window, everyone was still alive.

Yet, they were nowhere to be seen.

Je Hyun-oh even had wings. I should have been able to see him flying around, searching for me. But no matter how hard I scanned the sea above my head, there was no sign of the black crow.

I watched helplessly as the HP displayed on the System Window continued to drain.

Frustrated, I stumbled around, running on top of the giant eyeball.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je... *ugh*."

I tried to shout his name, but my voice wouldn't come out properly. I could only manage a whisper as I called for the others.

"Haah... Representative Cha... Where are you? Lieutenant Won..."

But only a suffocating silence answered.

A sharp pain shot through my chest. I tripped over my own feet and fell.

"..."

The fear I had tried so hard to suppress rushed in, as if it had been waiting for this exact moment.

What if I can't find them?

Even if I use Resurrection after they die... I think I need to be right in front of them for it to work.

Will I even be able to use Resurrection again before then?

The thought that it was impossible kept hammering at me.

*'No.'*

I shook my head violently and forced myself back up. Just as I was trying to banish the negative thoughts and regain my composure...

A memory of the new prison guard training ground flashed through my mind.

*The hologram of Creature #13.*

The moment the thought formed, I gazed into the ■■■.

The fallen being was shrouded in a colossal lie. It had concealed its true essence, but the illusion was erased beneath the light. I took in the sight of its exposed weak points and the method to subdue it. But it wasn't enough. I wanted to know its true form. I peered deeper. And then...

*'Why is there so little left?'*

"!"

I slammed my eyes shut in horror.

Too deep.

Had I looked too deeply? The searing heat felt as if it would scorch my eyes.

The thoughts I'd been having until just now swirled chaotically in my mind. I barely managed to quell them as they threatened to run wild.

I stumbled, nearly falling, before catching my balance. A sharp pain shot through me, perhaps from overexerting my strength.

Yet, a faint, wry smile crept across my lips.

In the new prison guard training hall, I had faced an entity made of illusions. It felt as real as the flesh, delivering every impact with crushing force, but I had learned how to subdue it—for it was nothing more than a fake.

This was no different.

That thing had no true form. It was no more than a phantom.

The moment I looked into it, the moment I fully perceived it...

The world collapsed.

The sea in the sky and the eyes on the earth all melted away, giving way to a darkness so absolute I couldn't see an inch in front of me.

The darkness was so thick and absolute that I couldn't even see my hand held right in front of my face.

The golden sphere I had been cradling preciousy was gone.

I recoiled in shock but quickly forced myself to calm down.

Ever since the world turned upside down, ■■■ had been using illusions to blind me and everyone else, isolating me all alone.

There was no question why it had left the Creatures alone. To a god, Creatures were mere insects, incapable of causing any real trouble.

The same was true now.

The darkness ■■■ had woven was a lie.

But even if it were real, it didn't matter.

No matter how deep the darkness that trapped me, I could always generate my own light.

I called out to it.

"■■■."

The way to subdue ■■■ was to acknowledge it.

I called its name, dragging it out into the open so it could no longer hide.

A neon green light flickered into existence in the space that had been nothing but darkness.

The light gradually expanded, forming a massive eye. It was an eye with a horizontal pupil, nearly three meters tall.

The eye, fixed upon me, spoke continuously.

The voice, transmitted directly into my mind rather than through my eardrums, kept trying to burrow into my consciousness.

In a desperate effort, I twisted my lips into a smile.

Even though I had subdued ■■■, I was still within his domain.

I had to make this place my own.

I approached the eye and placed my hand upon it.

I slowly traced the surface, feeling a texture as hard as iron.

Though it seemed smooth at a glance, I could sense a deep indentation. It had used an illusion to conceal the wound it had sustained in the previous battle.

I shoved my hand into the crack.

The Eye shrieked, a sound that threatened to melt my brain.

With an expressionless face, I watched its agony and pushed my arm deeper.

Once I had inserted it far enough, I recalled the moment I had expelled Neo from my own body.

I retraced that memory perfectly, but instead of within my own body, I unleashed the power into the eyeball.

The Eye began to crumple.

Centered around my embedded hand, it collapsed pathetically, as if being squeezed by something colossal.

The sight of the flesh being carelessly crushed and pulped was a grotesque spectacle.

The pupil, which had been collapsing inward, suddenly ceased its contraction.

Then, a blinding explosion of golden light erupted.

The pupil let out a final, silent scream before vanishing without a trace in the golden blast.

All that remained was a fragment of shimmering light.

I realized it.

This place is now...

My Domain.

The System Window appeared, and the world shifted once more.

It was no longer a place of darkness, a world where earth and sky were overturned, or a prison shrouded in gloomy sea fog.

My Domain is a remote island with a clear, bright sky, dazzling sunlight, and a rippling blue sea.

I stood on the beach and looked up at the sky.

The breeze was cool and the weather was perfect for a swim.

A golden curtain descended from the sky. I smiled faintly, captivated by the aurora-like shimmer.

It was time for those waiting to return.

In that moment, I even forgot the pain. I truly felt no suffering at all.

But the first thing that met my eyes, just as I was anticipating a reunion, was...

A gray metal mask.

The Security Chief, clad in a black uniform, materialized amidst the golden light. Then, countless more black-uniformed figures began to appear behind him.

Staring in shock, I realized:

*'Was it an area-of-effect skill?!'*

The Resurrection I had cast had spread across the entire space, reviving not just the Creatures, but the Security Chief and all the prison guards as well.

Even the Scraps had been revived as their original prison guards, their numbers seemingly endless.

*'No wonder it hurt like I was dying,'* I thought. *'There was a reason for it.'*

The once peaceful little island was now stained black with their uniforms.

I could only gape as the Security Chief strode toward me.

"Roasted Chestnut!"

His face was alight with ecstasy.

"I knew you'd choose us!" he exclaimed, his voice trembling with emotion as he reached out to me. "Yes, I knew you'd make the right choice. Roasted Chestnut, I knew you would definitely...!"

But the Security Chief never reached me. A vine had already coiled tightly around his forearm.

Then, a deep, powerful voice boomed.

"Us?"

A massive Orca looked down at the Security Chief. Behind him, other Creatures emerged, all perfectly healthy and completely unscarred.

Number 1, as if enraged, twitched its vines and tugged at the Security Chief's forearm.

The Security Chief looked at the taut vine, then back at me. Even through his metal mask, I could feel his intense gaze. He was waiting for me to say the words he wanted to hear.

But I showed no reaction. I didn't even feel the need to speak. He wasn't worth the effort of a denial.

Orca spoke to me politely.

"Would you mind closing your eyes for a moment?"

I closed my eyes as he asked.

Then came the sounds—crushing, tearing, swallowing. I waited patiently as the noises continued.

After a while, I grew tired and sat down on the sand to wait.

Finally, Orca called my name.

"Mr. Chestnut!"

I gently opened my eyes and looked up at him.

Orca, drenched in neon green liquid and black flesh, stared at me with his piercingly bright eyes.

The other Creatures also gently approached me. Surrounded by twelve of them, I took each one in with my eyes.

Last, I looked at Orca.

Two pebble-like pupils, and two sturdy legs. His black-and-white leathery skin was smooth without a single scar, and the fin on his back was perfectly straight, without a single bend.

The Orca, brimming with vitality, faced me.

A smile escaped my lips without me even realizing it.

"Mr. Orca," I said, beaming at him. "Please teach me how to swim."

But Orca did not smile back. He looked down at me with a grim expression.

The other Creatures were the same. None of them looked pleased.

In the heavy atmosphere, they all pressed their bodies close to me.

Orca slowly knelt down in front of me.

Even so, his gaze was still so high that I had to look up at him.

"Mr. Chestnut. You must receive treatment before you swim."

Orca let out a deep sigh and took something out.

"This is serious... Please take this for now. It contains our power. I don't know if it will be of much help, but..."

What Orca handed me was a small child's bandage. It was a different design from the chick bandage I had put on him.

The moment I read the item description, I burst out laughing.
