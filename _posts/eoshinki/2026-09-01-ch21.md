<br>Won Taebeom swallowed a dry laugh as he stared at the neon green sea.

The sight reminded him of the Trial he had been trapped in before.

That place hadn't been a sea, nor was it neon green.

But it was the same in one way: it was a ruin built of endless despair.

The System Window, which had appeared the moment he was drenched in Neo, displayed the current situation perfectly.

This was the first time anything like this had happened since he was infected with the BT-DFH virus.

A giant tentacle had shattered the entire Trial Space, causing everything to plummet into the depths.

Even though he had experienced it firsthand, it still felt like a dream.

Won Taebeom summoned and equipped the Paladin's Oath.

The moment he donned the Silver Gauntlet, he felt a slight resistance.

It seemed the gauntlet, while accepting the BT-DFH virus, would not permit the Neo infection.

The Gauntlet's rejection was natural, in a way, since the Scrap had not been infected by his Conviction.

But it didn't matter.

After all, once someone fully turned into a Scrap, items would become useless anyway.

Won Taebeom thought of the first thing he had to do.

He had to find the Special Team and join them.

*'I need to find Trainee Han first.'*

If possible, he intended to prioritize searching for Han Goyo.

Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk had plenty of means to deal with such a situation, but Han Goyo had nothing.

He had even been brainwashed, leaving him out of his right mind.

The fact that Won Taebeom himself had played a part in this kept scratching at the back of his mind.

It had always been him who forced the few to sacrifice themselves for the many.

He had never seen anyone like Han Goyo, who chose to sacrifice himself out of his own free will.

Perhaps it was because this was such an exceptional case that I was even more concerned.

If Han Goyo's physical abilities were lower, the residue transformation caused by Neo might occur even faster.

It would be best to search as quickly as possible.

I was using floating debris on the sea to move around and check the nearby area first.

Sensing someone approaching, I turned my head.

Immediately, a large black figure filled my vision, and I heard the sound of metallic feathers folding.

*Thud*. Je Hyun-oh landed on a piece of a building, unusually without his goggles on.

"Lieutenant Won."

The first words out of his mouth were not about the current situation.

"Let's not report anything that happens from now on to Commissioner Jang Seokyun."

Je Hyun-oh, whose voice had changed due to the infection, rarely spoke aloud.

Han Goyo was surprised that Je Hyun-oh, who rarely spoke, was now speaking at length. The content of his words was even more unbelievable.

It was something a Lieutenant Colonel should never say.

Je Hyun-oh had always been a man who wouldn't care what Han Goyo did. Yet here he was, issuing an order specifically to protect him.

This, combined with Kwak Hanmuk's behavior and their own inexplicable actions during Han Goyo's temporary disappearance, seemed to be intensifying.

*'What is it about Han Goyo that draws them in so strongly?'*

Won Taebeom slowly opened his mouth and asked, "What happens if I defy your orders?"

"Then Trainee Han won't be at the Era Agency anymore."

A hollow laugh escaped Won Taebeom's lips. It wasn't a threat to kill him. He could feel that Je Hyun-oh knew him well.

There was a reason the Era Agency had appointed Je Hyun-oh as the Director of the On-site Response Headquarters.

Won Taebeom didn't care which side he took, as long as it meant Han Goyo would stay at the Era Agency.

"I'll follow your orders."

The moment he heard the agreement, Je Hyun-oh grabbed him and took off into the sky.

"Lieutenant Won. The boss battle will start in twenty minutes. Find the Creatures and return to Trainee Han—"

Je Hyun-oh stopped abruptly in mid-air, cutting off his briefing to stare up at the sky.

Then, he suddenly accelerated with explosive speed, streaking across the ocean. The water's surface split apart in his wake.

As he flew, his body suddenly lurched.

His wing feathers rattled with a metallic clang, and Je Hyun-oh dropped Won Taebeom onto a piece of floating debris.

Won Taebeom nearly missed his mark, but he had already accounted for that and managed to land safely.

Yet, he wondered why Je Hyun-oh had faltered mid-flight.

Why did he...

...suddenly...

...do that...

"..."

His train of thought was severed. A sensation, sharp as a blade, slashed through his entire body.

It was a survival instinct.

But he couldn't escape.

His body moved against his will.

He didn't want to lift his head, yet he found himself staring at the sky, as if someone were pulling a string.

Something colossal existed in the sky.

A form so contradictory it seemed to mock the laws of nature.

He wanted to look away, but he was not allowed.

Held captive, he gazed up at the heavens.

Hideous information, too vast for a human brain to contain, shattered his retinas and crushed his mind as it flooded in.

Too many. Eyes. Gazes. Eyes. Gazes. Eyes. Gazes. Dozens. Hundreds. Thousands. Tens of thousands. Hundreds of millions of eyes.

*Please stop. Stop staring. Stop.*

The eyes... the pupils... looking at me...

**Light!**

"...!"

He gasped for air.

A blindingly white light filled his vision.

If the light hadn't forcibly blocked his sight, his brain would have been crushed.

Won Taebeom felt blood streaming from both eyes.

He realized too late that he was convulsing, vomiting blood.

He lifted his head, blood trickling from between his teeth.

The white light, momentarily turning night into day, was the radiance emitted by CEO Cha's Daeil Yeorae.

A semicircular arc of white sword energy was soaring into the sky.

Yet, the man who had leaped high and swung his sword was now falling backward.

As if he had made one last desperate struggle, CEO Cha fell helplessly, his blood-choked voice calling out to Han Goyo.

Je Hyun-oh snatched something from the air just as CEO Cha plunged into the Sea of Neo.

A massive pillar of water erupted.

Moments later, a drenched CEO Cha emerged, hauling himself onto some floating debris.

He lifted the lower part of his mask and vomited blood.

The hand gripping the Daeil Yeorae trembled and convulsed.

After coughing up blood several more times, CEO Cha muttered in disbelief.

"That thing... is the Boss? Ha..."

Won Taebeom felt exactly the same way.

They had faced countless Trials, but no Boss had ever exuded such an overwhelming presence. Even Won Taebeom, who had already witnessed the very depths of hell, had never seen anything like this.

And yet, that thing was not the end.

Won Taebeom watched as black figures began to emerge one by one from the Sea of Neo. The shapes, appearing like filth, were Scraps. They were the same creatures that originally inhabited the underground maze, now joined by the Prison guards who had fallen into Neo and rapidly transformed into Scraps themselves.

The black figures grew in number. It was obvious they would soon fill the entire sea.

Above them loomed an entity of unknown origin; below, the Scraps were swarming. The suffocating pressure of being completely surrounded forced a cold sweat down his back.

`"Ah! So this is where you'll be buried! Finally!"`

A high-pitched laugh, an auditory hallucination that pierced through the tinnitus, tore through his mind.

Won Taebeom managed to ignore the auditory hallucinations and turned to CEO Cha.

The CEO was readjusting his mask.

Judging by the way his hands were still trembling, he too seemed to have been severely affected by the Being of the Sky.

Just a single glance had done this.

Won Taebeom didn't even want to imagine what would happen a second time.

The moment he realized death was closing in on him, he felt a force once again compelling him.

His head was forced upward. Just as he was about to tremble in pain that would crush his brain, a golden streak shot across the sky.

It was a meteor shower with long, trailing tails.

But the light did not fall; it soared, ascending toward the heavens.

The moment it struck the Being of the Sky head-on, a scream that felt like it was scraping his brain crashed down from all directions.

As blood streamed from his ears, he finally saw the form of the Being of the Sky, which he hadn't even been able to properly perceive before.

Within its massive eye, countless smaller eyeballs were packed tight.

A meteor shower had struck its eyes.

The Being of the Sky, struck directly by the golden light, closed some of its eyes. As eyelids shut over dozens of them, the crushing pressure that had weighed down Won Taebeom's body eased considerably.

Won Taebeom stared blankly at Je Hyun-oh, who was descending from the air with graceful wingbeats.

Je Hyun-oh placed something shimmering upon the floating debris in the sea.

Without realizing it, Won Taebeom's lips parted.

*Ah...*

It was utterly flawless.

The object, shimmering with pure golden light, was beautiful. It was so radiant that he stared, mesmerized, forgetting the moment his brain had nearly been crushed by the Being of the Sky.

Yet, this was no salvation.

It was the same as the Being of the Sky, yet completely different. It was composed of only warm and soft things, making him want to accept it unconditionally, without even a hint of resistance.

It evoked a pitifully desperate instinct to protect, yet it was a being that would immediately erode one's mind upon contact.

The moment he registered the strangeness, a chilling dread swept over his entire body.

He instinctively backed away, nearly slipping as he lost his footing.

*'But as long as he could escape that thing, falling into the sea would be fine.'*

*'I'd rather just go inside Neo...'*

"...!"

A sharp pain stung his forearm.

He saw a metallic feather, having scratched his skin, retreating.

The golden light that had enveloped his entire consciousness slowly faded, allowing him to discern his opponent.

Won Taebeom slowly blinked, as if waking from a dream.

The surrounding objects gradually came into focus.

Je Hyun-oh stood atop a massive piece of a building across from him.

And beside him stood something else.

The moment Won Taebeom saw its eyes, which radiated a brilliant light, he recognized it as the same golden presence that had just ensnared him. At the same time, a terrifying question etched itself into his mind.

*'What in the world is this?'*

One thing was certain: it was not human.

Staring blankly at the figure he suspected to be Han Goyo, Won Taebeom shifted his gaze.

Je Hyun-oh seemed perfectly fine. Though he had stumbled during their flight, he appeared completely unaffected by the Being of the Sky or the creature standing beside him. He looked no different than usual.

In fact, Je Hyun-oh was even touching it. Like a crow who had found the shiniest of treasures, he held the creature's wrist in a firm grip.

Far from fearing the non-human being, Je Hyun-oh's eyes gleamed with ecstasy.

It was a joy that seemed to say he had been waiting for this very moment.

It was astonishing enough that Je Hyun-oh was showing so much emotion, but...

*'How could he not be afraid?'*

His complete lack of repulsion was nothing short of miraculous.

Wondering if he was the only one feeling this primal terror, he glanced to his side.

Though CEO Cha's face was hidden behind a mask, it was clear he was also deeply shaken by the creature.

Won Taebeom's lips trembled as he spoke.

"What... what is it?"

The question was fragmented, but the subject was unmistakable.

He dared not name it directly, yet the answer Je Hyun-oh gave was anticlimactically simple.

"He's Trainee Han."

"..."

***That*** was Han Goyo?

The words, defining his existence without a moment's hesitation, sparked a fierce surge of resentment.

In that instant, CEO Cha shot to his feet and leaped over the floating debris in a single bound.

He closed the distance to Han Goyo in the blink of an eye.

He seemed poised to seize the man by the collar, but a metallic hand blocked his path.

CEO Cha glared at Je Hyun-oh, his voice dripping with menace.

"Explain."

The smile in Je Hyun-oh's eyes deepened.

If he had been wearing goggles, he probably would have displayed an emoticon.

"Explain what?"

"Explain what the hell is going on right now, Lieutenant Colonel Je."

From behind the metallic hand, Han Goyo gazed up at CEO Cha.

Meeting that golden gaze head-on, CEO Cha's murderous aura immediately subsided.

That being was even more dangerous precisely because it did not provoke hostility.

However, CEO Cha seemed a little more flustered than the others.

The reason was clear.

Han Goyo had always shown a very friendly attitude toward CEO Cha.

But the current Han Goyo, though he looked the same on the outside, seemed like a completely different being.

His golden eyes held only indifference, along with a slight sense of question regarding the person blocking his path.

Watching this scene, Je Hyun-oh replied with an annoyed expression.

"Is CEO Cha my superior?"

When met with the retort that there was no reason to explain, CEO Cha gritted his teeth and spoke again.

"Then just tell me how to turn things back to the way they were."

At that moment, Won Taebeom also read the thought that flashed through Je Hyun-oh's eyes.

*'Is that really necessary?'*

Je Hyun-oh made no effort to hide his thoughts, and CEO Cha couldn't have missed them.

Just as he placed a hand on the Daeil Yeorae, Han Goyo's lips parted.

He seemed to be speaking, but for some reason, not a single word reached anyone's ears.

Yet Je Hyun-oh appeared to hear him perfectly, replying casually.

"Just the two of us? It would be better if there were more."

Throughout their exchange, Han Goyo's gaze remained fixed solely on Je Hyun-oh.

Je Hyun-oh, speaking in a strangely indulgent tone as if to a child, relayed Han Goyo's words.

"He says let's kill the thing floating in the sky. CEO Cha, are you going to cooperate?"

Won Taebeom felt like he was losing his mind at the nonchalant words.

Yet Je Hyun-oh spoke as if discussing a routine operation.

"If that thing dies, we might be able to return to normal."

CEO Cha remained silent for a moment. He kept his eyes fixed not on Je Hyun-oh, but on Han Goyo, before finally speaking.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je."

Veins bulged on the back of the hand gripping the sword's hilt. A chilling voice followed.

"You'd better take responsibility for the words you've spoken."

Then, CEO Cha immediately drew the Daeil Yeorae.

Watching the scene, Won Taebeom couldn't help but wonder.

*'But how?'*

The sea was swarming with Scrap, and the Being of the Sky was too terrifying to even look at. The moment his gaze met it, his brain would be crushed.

Though the pressure eased as several of his eyelids fluttered shut from the golden meteor shower, it had not disappeared completely.

The pressure was still there, crushing his body even though he wasn't facing the Young god.

Even breathing was a struggle. Everyone except Je Hyun-oh, who seemed unaffected, was completely incapacitated.

Just as Won Taebeom concluded that death was their only option, a System Window of a form he had never seen before appeared before his eyes.

It felt as if he had escaped the influence of the Being of the Sky and fallen under the influence of another entity.

The tinnitus and hallucinations that had relentlessly tormented him also cleared away completely.

For the first time in a very long time, he was enveloped in silence.
