<br>At my cry, Mr. Octopus Leg laughed aloud.

With each of his laughs, the very space trembled, and every being in Zone X was crushed by his majesty.

Agonized groans echoed from all around.

They were all so utterly weak. I alone stood unscathed, gazing at Mr. Octopus Leg.

I had desperately wished to see him in person, but I couldn't believe my dream had actually come true.

The fact that he had descended upon me, inhabiting the body of a subordinate, was overwhelming.

My face flushed at this miraculous moment.

Strange things kept appearing before my eyes, but I ignored them.

I had no time to spare for such trifles when I had so little time to feast my eyes on Mr. Octopus Leg.

I tried to approach Mr. Octopus Leg, but something held me back firmly.

When I looked to see what it was, I saw a hand gripping my shoulder.

The hand, with its tendons and bones bulging, was trembling so violently it was pitiful.

I slowly raised my gaze to see the other person's face.

*'Ah, it's Captain Kwak.'*

In my joy at meeting Mr. Octopus Leg, I had momentarily forgotten about him.

I looked at Kwak Hanmuk with questioning eyes.

Kwak Hanmuk's face was deathly pale, and sweat was dripping down it.

It was strange to see him unable to suppress his instinctive fear and repulsion.

What could possibly make him so afraid?

What a magnificent god Mr. Octopus Leg is.

To have even seen His direct manifestation was an honor I couldn't achieve even if I devoted my entire life to it.

I didn't know why I was trembling like this.

"Fuck..."

Kwak Hanmuk forced out a curse.

*Clatter...* His prayer beads slowly unfurled.

The beads, extended as if stumbling, wrapped around my wrist.

"Cough!"

Fresh red blood gushed out. Kwak Hanmuk collapsed to his knees, spitting blood again.

A pool of blood quickly formed on the floor.

The metallic scent of blood made me droop my eyes in pity.

If only I had modified his body sooner, this wouldn't have happened.

It was all my fault.

With the prayer beads wrapped around one wrist, I approached Mr. Octopus Leg.

As I drew closer, his neon green eyes glowed with an eerie, shimmering light.

When I finally stood before him, Mr. Octopus Leg asked me a question with a faint smile.

"■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■?"

His voice sounded like a funeral dirge echoing from the deep sea. The muffled resonance pounded against my brain, yet I couldn't understand a single word.

As I looked up at him with a confused expression, his smile deepened.

"Y...oung."

This time, I could hear him, but I still didn't understand the meaning. He didn't seem to be calling me a "Little Lamb."

*'Does he mean I'm young?'*

After a moment's thought, I remembered Dominic. Dominic always spoke to me as if I were young.

*'Come to think of it, Mr. Octopus Leg should be able to see Dominic.'*

Even Infernum, who wasn't a god, had seen Dominic.

Mr. Octopus Leg should have been able to perceive Dominic.

As fellow gods, they were bound to at least exchange greetings, yet he remained strangely silent.

I glanced sideways at the Pudding Slime.

Mr. Octopus Leg followed my gaze, but he showed no reaction.

It was as if he couldn't sense Dominic at all.

*'Could Dominic have concealed himself?'*

I couldn't understand why he would go to such lengths to hide from Mr. Octopus Leg.

As I pondered, Mr. Octopus Leg called out to me again.

"Little... lamb."

The shadow cast at my feet writhed, though there wasn't a breath of wind.

The humanoid shape gradually split into countless wriggling tentacles.

Then, it detached from the shadow and took on a three-dimensional form.

The briny scent of the sea pierced my nostrils.

Tentacles, dripping with sticky mucus, wriggled and coiled around me.

I smiled with pleasure at the sensation of them slowly creeping up from my feet.

It felt as if I were gradually sinking into the deep sea.

*'Ah... I wanted to cut off my useless limbs and attach Mr. Octopus Leg's instead...'*

Just as I was drowning in this hazy euphoria—

*Crack.*

A sharp sting shot through me.

A small blue spark was flickering from the wooden bracelet on my wrist.

I turned my head to see the bracelet's string trailing far behind.

The tentacles had already covered everything.

They surged and swayed like the waves of a crashing ocean.

I spotted a flash of red hair amidst the writhing tentacles.

A man, slowly being crushed to death, called out to someone.

"Goyo... Han Goyo..."

Blood streamed from his eyes. He wept tears of blood, like someone who had witnessed something never meant for mortal eyes.

"That's a god... fuck... get your head straight..."

*Crackle.*

Sparks erupted from the wooden bracelet, and the red-haired man coughed up a mouthful of blood.

For some reason, I couldn't tear my eyes away from him.

Just as I was staring intently at the man—

"■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■..."

A hint of displeasure tinged Mr. Octopus Leg's voice.

It seemed he was displeased that I was paying attention to someone else—even with a god standing right before me.

A slimy tentacle touched my cheek.

Mr. Octopus Leg forced me to look at him, then smiled gently.

But...

I glanced at Mr. Octopus Leg for a moment before turning my head back toward the man.

He was dying, crushed by the power of god.

Just as the man's lips parted to speak to me—

"...!"

He let out a pained groan.

Mr. Octopus Leg's tentacles tightened around him as if to crush him to death, and in an instant, the man vanished into the wave of tentacles.

As if swept away by the deep sea.

Now, all that met my eyes was the endless, undulating wave of tentacles.

The wooden bracelet wrapped around my wrist lost its strength and slid off.

Soon, the wooden bracelet too was buried in the waves of tentacles.

I…*felt a wave of disgust.*

The tentacle that had been touching my cheek froze.

With it came a cool breeze.

I looked up.

A flash of white and black flickered, and the ceiling vanished.

Not a single piece of rubble fell. It was as if the ceiling had been deleted, instantly revealing the night sky.

The dry sky, once shrouded in thick sea fog, was now bare.

*Flash!*

A fractal pattern of brilliant blue light etched itself across the heavens.

Then, a deafening thunderclap shook the world, tearing through my eardrums.

The moment I heard the roar, the ecstatic sensation that had enveloped me just seconds before vanished completely.

A searing heat flared in my heart.

*Thump, thump.*

My pulse quickened, and heat welled in my eyes.

Amidst the rapidly rising heat, I felt a surge of anger.

I locked eyes with the neon green glow and commanded:

*"Get out."*

The undulating wave of tentacles froze instantly.

The neon green light vanished from the Security Chief's eyes, leaving only hollow voids behind.

His body swayed before collapsing like a puppet with its strings cut.

The tentacles that had filled every inch of the space vanished as cleanly as a shadow under sunlight.

"Captain Kwak...!"

I rushed to Kwak Hanmuk's side.

He lay on the floor, drenched in blood.

My vision narrowed, and my breath quickened. With trembling hands, I checked his pulse first.

*'Thank god, he's just unconscious. His heart is still beating.'*

He's alive.

I finally let out the breath I'd been holding.

The prayer beads, which had been limp until then, returned to their master.

I clenched my teeth, slowly watching the prayer beads wrap around Kwak Hanmuk's wrist.

The sight of him—so utterly broken—was more than I could bear.

*He's my subordinate.*

*I was supposed to protect him.*

Rage, mingled with a storm of other emotions, threatened to split my head open. I clenched my fists so hard my nails dug into my palms.

*I wanted to burn it all down. Every last bit.*

*I wanted to tear through the deep sea, drag it out, and make it pay for daring to lay a finger on my subordinate.*

Just as the shimmering heat was about to consume me, a voice roared out.

"Han Goyo!!"

Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha leaped down from the gaping hole in the ceiling, landing beside me.

CEO Cha started to shout at me again but froze mid-sentence.

"Your... your eyes..."

Before he could finish, a thunderous *KABOOM* erupted.

The ocean surged into the sky like a bomb had detonated.

Colossal tentacles erupted from the sea, blotting out the sky.

And there wasn't just one. Hundreds of thick stalks surged from all sides of the island. The thousands of suckers on their surfaces sucked in the air, creating a chilling, grating sound.

The tentacles swung down in unison, slamming into the prison building with crushing force. Thick reinforced concrete crumbled like rotten fruit.

Then, the tentacles coiled around the prison, binding it tight.

*Crr-ack! Crunch!* Steel and concrete crumpled violently, and the once-massive prison building collapsed in an instant.

All of this happened in a matter of seconds.

Je Hyun-oh could barely shield them from the falling debris with his metallic wings.

But it wasn't over yet.

A bizarre voice echoed from the deep sea. After a sound that felt like it was scratching the inside of their brains, the tentacles struck the island again.

The ground gave way, and everything began to fall.

Je Hyun-oh quickly donned his Crow Gloves and grabbed Kwak Hanmuk.

Beneath the prison lay an underground labyrinth.

But as I looked down, I saw something else entirely.

A Neon Green Sea stretched across the depths. The liquid, a color so vivid it stung the eyes, greedily swallowed everything falling from above.

Je Hyun-oh tried to defy the fall and fly back up, but protecting both the unconscious Kwak Hanmuk and me while dodging falling debris was too much.

He abandoned his flight and wrapped his metallic wings around us.

A massive chunk of the shattered building struck Je Hyun-oh squarely.

The impact accelerated our fall.

*Splash!*

We plunged into the Neon Green Sea.

Immersed in the viscous liquid, I couldn't let my guard down. Debris continued to rain down into the sea.

Each time a heavy object struck the metal wings, we sank a little deeper, slowly but steadily.

Je Hyun-oh checked on me. I nodded, signaling that I was okay.

Neo isn't water. You won't drown in it. But the more you drink, the worse you'll change.

As the falling debris gradually ceased, Je Hyun-oh folded his wings tight against his body, streamlining his form. Then, using that shape, he swam toward the surface.

"...Pwah!"

The Neon Green Sea before my eyes was a complete mess.

I scanned my surroundings, searching for something to cling to.

I spotted a human prisoner standing on a large wooden plank.

"Lieutenant Colonel! Over there...!"

Je Hyun-oh immediately understood what I wanted. He fired a metal feather.

The human prisoner died instantly, his throat pierced through.

I quickly swam over and dragged the body off the plank. Je Hyun-oh then hoisted Kwak Hanmuk onto it.

Only then could I finally catch my breath. I turned to Je Hyun-oh.

"Lieutenant Colonel, are you alright?"

He displayed an emoticon on his goggles.

*'Are the Creatures all right? What about Lieutenant Won?'*

CEO Cha was surely safe.

I was most worried about Won Taebeom and the Creatures who had been imprisoned.

Won Taebeom, in particular, must have fallen into the Neon Green Sea like a bolt from the blue. He might have been injured by falling debris.

*'What should I do?'*

Just as I was struggling to find a way to resolve this insane situation—

*Ding.*

A glitched System Window appeared before my eyes.
