<br>Kwak Hanmuk stood before me, his presence completely blocking my view.

I took a cautious step to the side, trying to assess the situation.

Despite Kwak Hanmuk's rude remarks, Ham Jiwol and the Paseong directors showed no reaction. They simply stared at me, looking utterly stunned.

I couldn't fathom what was happening.

Judging by the glass pillar I'd been inside, it seemed my condition had been critical. I always lost consciousness for a long time after completing a Trial...

*'Wait, wasn't it 72 hours this time?'*

If so, I'd actually woken up faster than usual.

As I was thinking, a grim whisper reached my ears.

"He's alive..."

Ham Jiwol's nose began to bleed profusely.

When I looked at her again, the blood vessels in her eyes had burst, leaving the whites bloodshot and the skin beneath them bruised black. She looked as if she hadn't slept for days and nights. Blood streaming down her face, she cried out, "He's really alive... Mr. Goyo, you're alive...!"

"Excuse me?"

I wanted to ask what she meant, but Ham Jiwol's intensity was so overwhelming that I couldn't even open my mouth. The way she looked at me was more than just fervent.

It's a bit embarrassing to say, but it was as if she had fallen in love with me. There was no other way to describe the look in her eyes.

Her face flushed as she gasped for air, her body swaying. Then, she collapsed to the floor. She had fainted.

I stared at the back of her head as she lay there, my mouth hanging open.

I still had no idea what was going on, but for now, Ham Jiwol's unconscious state was a good thing.

"Lieutenant Won Taebeom."

Samra's voice broke the silence.

"Please remove Director Ham Jiwol."

He meant for her to be carried outside.

But Won Taebeom didn't respond to Samra's request.

His eyes were still fixed on me.

It was an intense gaze, but entirely different from Ham Jiwol's.

I couldn't help but feel flustered by his stare.

Since I had just messed with her mind, it would have been more appropriate for him to look at me with disgust or fear of the unknown.

But I saw none of those emotions in Won Taebeom's eyes.

I couldn't be sure, but it seemed closer to confusion.

*'He likes me after all.'*

...I'm definitely not agreeing with the Young god's way of thinking, though.

But it seemed certain that he didn't hate me.

*'Lieutenant Won, I'll be on my knees apologizing to you soon.'*

As I was busily sending him apologies in my mind, Samra called his name again.

"Lieutenant Won Taebeom."

Only then did Won Taebeom slowly tear his gaze away from me.

He seemed to be thinking for a moment, then silently grabbed Ham Jiwol and casually draped her over his shoulder.

Just then, a cute cell phone ringtone pierced the chilly atmosphere.

The owner of the ringtone, a song by a popular girl group, was Mok Saehwa.

As she answered the call, her face immediately lit up.

"Representative!"

Seo Munchan's face also lit up with joy.

Mok Saehwa continued in a clear, bright voice.

"Yes. Goyo is alive. Ah, he's right here with me, so let me put him on—"

*Slash.*

Mok Saehwa's phone was sliced in half.

The metallic feather that had just grazed her fingers returned to Je Hyun-oh.

"...I was going to, but I guess I can't now."

Mok Saehwa sucked the bleeding tip of her finger and stuffed the severed phone into her pocket.

The two directors turned to look at me at the same time. Then, they suddenly burst into applause and cheered in unison.

"Goyo! Congratulations on your resurrection!"

*Clap clap clap clap.*

The pouring applause almost made me say "Thank you" without thinking.

While I was still reeling from the congratulations on my "resurrection"—something I'd never received in my life—Seo Munchan abruptly pulled a neatly folded handkerchief from his suit jacket.

He removed his glasses and pretended to dab his eyes.

*'But he's not shedding a single tear.'*

I waited, curious about what kind of performance this was.

After a few more moments of feigning tears, Seo Munchan finally spoke.

"Ms. Goyo. Representative Cha has just woken up."

"What?"

"He's been in a coma. He's barely regained consciousness, but his condition is still very poor."

My mind went blank.

That's impossible.

*I'm the one who resurrected him.*

Resurrection brings the dead back to life, and it heals the living.

Since the skill was an area-of-effect ability that covered the entire space, I assumed CEO Cha would have emerged fully recovered.

After all, I had regained consciousness after seventy-two hours and felt completely fine.

*'Could it be because of the Disbeliever Trait?'*

The thought that CEO Cha's Disbeliever Trait might have blocked the healing hit me.

My heart sank.

When a Trial ends, the virus that infected the participants vanishes, but any physical injuries remain. CEO Cha had taken continuous damage even after losing more than half his life force to Light Modulation. To think he had been brought out without any healing...

"..."

While trapped in that black space for seventy-two hours, I had thought about countless things. It was because there was so little I could do, left all alone.

It was a painful time, filled with thoughts that clung to me so relentlessly I wished I could stop thinking them.

Among those thoughts was CEO Cha.

My heart felt as heavy as a stone.

Just as I was unable to hide the darkening expression of worry on my face, Samra spoke, his eyes flashing with light.

"Director Seo Munchan's statement was significantly exaggerated."

"It is true that he was unconscious, but CEO Cha's vital signs have now returned to perfectly normal levels. He is in a condition to enter the Trial immediately without any issues."

It seemed Samra had hacked the hospital's equipment to retrieve the information.

He then declared to the Paseong directors, "I will restrain any further statements that may cause Han Goyo confusion."

Despite his words, the directors remained steadfast.

"It's hard to lie before the eyes of the entire world, isn't it?"

As Seo Munchan shrugged his shoulders with a playful grin, Mok Saehwa called my name.

"Goyo-nim."

She clasped her hands together, her eyes pleading.

"Please, just visit our Representative in the hospital once."

At that, Kwak Hanmuk couldn't hold back any longer.

"Wow, how much longer do we have to wait?"

He tilted his head mockingly and raised his voice.

"Directors, isn't it about time you all get lost?"

Mok Saehwa quickly cut in.

"Of course, Paseong has suffered a very great loss! And the losses will only grow even larger in the future!"

She flashed a bright smile.

"But if Goyo-nim would just visit him in the hospital, none of that will matter at all."

"The Representative will be waiting for you, Goyo-nim."

Seo Munchan chimed in quickly.

The directors lined up, staring at me like a row of pitiful kittens.

It seemed they were trying to appeal to my sympathy, but having faced sword-teeth and flower-bullets, I remained completely unmoved.

But I gave them the answer they wanted.

"Yes. I'll go visit him in the hospital."

Everyone was stunned by my reply. Even the Directors looked shocked.

Kwak Hanmuk stared at me with an incredulous expression.

"Chestnut... Is he still sick? He's saying strange things because he's mentally unstable."

"Han Goyo, that is an unnecessary comment."

Samra looked ready to recite CEO Cha's physical data to prove just how perfectly fine he was.

Je Hyun-oh rattled his metallic hand, while Mo Haein gently admonished me.

"You don't have to do that."

But... *I* wanted to go.

I felt like I could only be at ease if I saw CEO Cha moving and breathing with my own eyes.

*I wish I had made him my subordinate.*

*If I had made him my subordinate, I could have inspected every inch of him right now.*

Once again, I felt that the Young god's way of thinking was a bit peculiar.

I disagreed with this thought as well.

*'And besides, CEO Cha didn't seem to like gods much.'*

It was wrong to forcibly register someone who didn't want it.

At any rate, the Young god had a history of throwing tantrums in front of him, so I would have to talk to him about that.

I politely asked the directors.

"I will visit you soon, so I would appreciate it if you could leave for now."

"Of course. We'll get lost immediately."

"Call us anytime, Mr. Goyo!"

Having gotten what they wanted, the directors bowed politely as if nothing had happened.

But before they could even vanish, the world before my eyes shimmered with holographic colors.

Samra had created a virtual space and summoned the entire Special Team.

It was an exact replica of our office in the Special Team's main headquarters, which had been destroyed.

I wanted to feel a flicker of nostalgia at the familiar sight, but I couldn't.

I... was surrounded by the Special Team....

*'This is a little scary.'*

Everyone except Mo Haein was taller than me.

As they all looked down at me, memories of The Cell X naturally resurfaced.

It was the memory of me groveling before Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk.

*Click.* Je Hyun-oh removed his goggles.

When he revealed his eyes and stared at me without a word, I became twice as scared.

If he had at least called my name—*Goyo*—I could have mentally prepared myself, but his silence made it even more terrifying…

I was trembling inside.

Just then, Samra spoke.

"Mr. Han Goyo."

Then he said something unbelievable.

"You have recovered vital signs 72 hours after being pronounced dead."

I froze for a moment before slowly asking, "...Are you saying I died and came back to life?"

"That is correct."

"..."

*'Died and came back to life...'* Even I would be horrified by that.

Now, the reactions of the people around me—which had seemed not just strange, but downright grotesque—all started to make sense.

I had no idea that "connecting to the body" meant *this*. It was absolutely maddening.

And to do it so publicly, no less.

If I'd known, I would have hidden in a corner somewhere to be resurrected.

Between the real-name registration for subordinates and everything else, it seemed gods simply had no privacy.

Just as I was feeling overwhelmed by the events to come, Samra said something that left me even more stunned.

"I had already concluded that Outer gods—Transcendental Beings from beyond Earth—exist, and I have been searching for information on them. Therefore, I judged it highly probable that a Transcendental Being could also exist within Earth."

Samra's pupils dilated and contracted like a camera's aperture. He stared at me without blinking and asked:

"Mr. Han Goyo, you are a god. Am I correct?"

I froze.

And I realized that my reaction had already given Samra the answer he needed.

No... even if I had managed a perfect lie, it wouldn't have mattered.

Samra was already convinced.

Honestly, it was too ridiculous to even try making further excuses.

Reluctantly, I forced myself to speak.

"Um... just as Samra said... I am..."

I could feel my face flushing as I murmured, my voice barely audible.

"...the god of Earth."

It was an incredibly difficult confession to make.

After the words left my mouth, I stole glances at the others. I had expected them to stare at me like I was a lunatic, but their expressions were unexpectedly grave.

It was as if... they had already discussed something while I was unconscious for those seventy-two hours.

Of course, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein showed clear signs of shock, but they remained remarkably calm for having heard such a startling revelation.

Je Hyun-oh, already knowing the truth, showed no particular agitation.

But the way he had been staring at me for a while now was starting to give me the creeps.

I fought the urge to check his Status Window to see what was wrong.

"Recruit."

Mo Haein called my name, her tone stiff.

"Yes, ma'am..."

Knowing I was in the wrong, I watched her face warily as I asked, "Should I... get down and give you push-ups?"

At that, Kwak Hanmuk muttered under his breath, averting his gaze. "This is driving me crazy."

*'That's not it.'*

I stood there awkwardly as Mo Haein bit her lip hard, then snapped at me.

"Report on what happened while you were clearing The Cell X."

She glared at me, her eyes promising to grab me by the collar and shake me.

"What could you possibly have done to be dead for seventy-two hours all by yourself?"

While I hesitated, not knowing how to answer, Je Hyun-oh spoke for the first time.

"Goyo overdid it. Right up to the brink of death."

Well, he's not wrong.

But there were unavoidable circumstances, considering the situation...

*'And he was the one who helped me out so enthusiastically when I was a god.'*

As I mumbled, feeling a slight sense of injustice, Kwak Hanmuk narrowed his eyes.

"I assume the fact that I'm fine is also thanks to Goyo."

"That's true..."

"And what do you mean you 'made it into a void'?"

"I was trying to protect you, Captain Kwak..."

"So, you used your powers as a god, overexerted yourself, and died for seventy-two hours, right?"

"Yes, that's correct..."

A heavy silence descended after my answer.

I felt a strange sensation under their gazes.

Even though I was clearly being scolded, I didn't dislike it. It was because they were treating me like an ordinary recruit, not some alien being.

Just as I closed and opened my eyes, Mo Haein suddenly spoke.

"This won't do."

She strode toward me and grabbed my wrist in a firm grip. Then she turned to Samra.

"Samra. Take us outside."

*'Am I being arrested?'*

I froze stiffly, bracing myself to be handcuffed, but Samra said something to me in a language I couldn't understand.

"After some discussion, we've established the top-priority measure to effectively protect Mr. Han Goyo."

I was still struggling to process what that meant when Je Hyun-oh finally called my name.

"Goyo."

I looked at him, bracing myself for the inevitable.

When our eyes met, his long eyes curved slightly.

His low, gravelly voice whispered to me.

"I'm going to make you act like a proper god."

"What...?"

"That's right, Honey. If you've caused a mess, you have to take responsibility."

As I stood there in bewilderment, Kwak Hanmuk let out a heavy sigh.

Then, erasing all traces of playfulness, he looked at me with a serious expression.

"Join our team."
