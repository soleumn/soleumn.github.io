I recalled the quest I had received from Dominic in *SL2PL*.

The System Window had been just as broken then.

I remembered its contents perfectly.

The corrupted System Window had repeated the same characters over and over.

Dominic’s reaction to it was still vivid in my mind.

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"Hmm, the craftsmanship is a bit poor. I'll bring you a better-looking one next time."</span>

And now, the quest window was indeed "better-looking," with the text displayed much more normally than before.

A chill ran down my spine as I reached up to touch my shoulder.

The soft touch was gone.

Dominic was gone.

*'When did he disappear?'*

I frantically retraced my steps.

He had been there when the Octopus Leg used a subordinate to find me. I vaguely remembered wondering why Dominic was hiding himself from the Octopus Leg.

But now, he had vanished from my side, leaving only the quest window behind.

I forced my mind to work, even as it froze with dread.

*'It can't be that he has recovered his memories.'*

If I had, he would never have given me a quest like this.

*'What does Dominic want? Does he want me to complete the quest without his help?'*

Unable to contain my anxiety, I bit my lip.

Time was running out.

I only had thirty minutes.

If I didn't resolve this within thirty minutes, all my subordinates would be turned into Scraps.

*'First, the quest.'*

I prioritized and called out to Je Hyun-oh.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je."

But even knowing the urgency, I couldn't bring myself to get straight to the point. The moment I saw him completely drenched in the neon green liquid, I couldn't even mention the plan. Other words came out instead.

"I'm sorry. Things didn't go as planned..."

Everyone had trusted me and followed my lead, but I'd ruined everything. Worse, they were now all infected with Neo and facing the risk of becoming Scraps.

The moment the thought struck me that I had messed everything up for them, my tongue froze.

Clutching the board, I couldn't bring myself to look Je Hyun-oh in the eye. Instead, I stared at the undulating waves of neon green.

I wished I could sink deep into the ocean and never come back.

The one who should have been injured and unconscious wasn't Kwak Hanmuk—it should have been me. If it had, things would be much better.

"I'm sorry..." I muttered again.

I could feel Je Hyun-oh's gaze fixed on me.

He looked ready to lash out with criticism and curses. This time, he might even hit me.

*'If it ends with just a beating, maybe that's a relief.'*

I needed to convince him to continue the strategy, no matter what, to stop the residue transformation. But as I realized I wouldn't even get a chance to try, a crushing sense of despair washed over me.

Just then, a *click* echoed.

Je Hyun-oh pushed his goggles up, revealing his eyes, and called my name.

"Goyo."

Unable to meet his gaze, I answered cautiously.

"Yes..."

"Are you the Team Leader?"

"No."

Je Hyun-oh narrowed his eyes. "Then why are you acting like this?"

"...That's..."

"Trying to commit insubordination again?"

"Absolutely not."

He slowly opened his metallic hand, neon-green liquid dripping from his fingers.

"Tell me the next operation. If there isn't one, I'll handle this my way."

*'There's still a chance.'*

A wave of relief washed over me, followed by a strange feeling. The idea of having a chance felt unfamiliar. In the past, when things reached this point... there was never a next time.

Either way, I couldn't afford to fail this time. I had to do well. No, I ***had* **to succeed.

I desperately racked my brain, then finally spoke.

"We have to take down the god of this place. The one who approached Lieutenant Colonel Je."

Je Hyun-oh immediately frowned.

"However, everyone is currently overexposed to Neo. If we don't deal with the god within 30 minutes, we'll all become Scraps."

After midnight, Scraps would spawn in the underground maze and ascend to the surface. The only way to completely block them was to eliminate all the pits that formed randomly throughout the maze. Je Hyun-oh had wandered the underground maze, fighting Scraps to clear every single pit, so he knew exactly what kind of creatures they were.

If everyone turned into Scraps, it would be a total wipeout.

After a moment of assessing the situation, Je Hyun-oh asked curtly, "Can the two of you do it?"

Je Hyun-oh was undeniably strong. But for just two people to charge in and take on a god was a suicide mission.

"I don't think it's possible."

"What if we add CEO Cha and Lieutenant Won?"

"Then it might be worth a try. If you could also find the Creatures..."

"Wait."

Je Hyun-oh kicked off from the neon green liquid. His figure, wings of metal unfurled, vanished from my sight in an instant.

I found another piece of floating debris and climbed onto it.

I thought and rethought until my head throbbed.

I had asked Je Hyun-oh to find the others, but I still hadn't come up with a solution for the current situation.

When I encountered the Octopus Leg, I had been brainwashed.

But my mind had been shaken far more violently than any brainwashing applied by the System. It seemed the god had exerted its influence directly upon me from inches away.

Now, I was completely free of the brainwashing.

I had an intuition, a conviction that I had escaped the System's judgment.

Its crude puppet play would no longer work on me.

But if we encountered the Octopus Leg again—especially if we faced the god directly rather than through its subordinates—there was no guarantee we wouldn't suffer a similar fate.

I wasn't the only one with a problem.

Kwak Hanmuk had been severely damaged by the god's presence. It was a being that humans were never meant to behold.

"..."

I stared silently at Kwak Hanmuk.

My Medical Officer Items were useless against his injuries. Our only option was to Clear the area quickly and get him outside for proper treatment.

If others were to suffer the same damage as Kwak Hanmuk upon encountering the god, then the battle itself would be impossible from the start.

*'Wait. How is Lieutenant Colonel Je completely unscathed?'*

He had encountered the Octopus Leg earlier, yet he had taken no damage at all.

*'Had he already faced the gods many times?'*

Even so, this wasn't the kind of thing one could simply get used to.

As I weighed the possibilities and pondered a strategy, I suddenly noticed the strangeness in my own thinking.

I was only considering *how* I would fight.

I wasn't considering the possibility of defeat at all.

Amidst a quietly seething, strange exhilaration, I was certain.

*'I can win.'*

My eyes grew hot.

I could do it right now…

I hoped the System Window would provide some clue.

But its response was as vague as a passing cloud.

"What have I learned?"

The System Window remained silent.

Knowing that further questioning would be futile, I pondered the words it had left behind.

There was only one place in The Cell X where the word "learned" could possibly apply.

The new prison guard training center.

But all I had learned there was basic knowledge about Neo and how to subdue creatures.

There was no way I could use that against a god.

"—!"

Lost in thought, I was suddenly yanked backward.

My body was being dragged, even though there was nothing visible around me.

I immediately realized what was happening.

"Representative Cha!"

I shouted, figuring out what he was trying to do.

"Killing all the NPCs won't clear the stage!"

But CEO Cha didn't stop.

Fury surged through me as I thought about how his actions would mess everything up when every second counted. I couldn't hold it back any longer and screamed, "You crazy bastard! We won't get the ending!"

CEO Cha froze. Then, he lowered his Cognitive Decline and revealed himself.

I glared at the white mask, panting heavily.

Only when I felt his steady gaze on me did I finally snap back to my senses.

*I* was the crazy bastard...

I had just treated him exactly like I did the CEO Cha in the game.

I braced myself to be sliced to pieces by Daeil Yeorae, but CEO Cha's response was a single, short word.

"Why?"

It was a surprisingly calm question. I'd expected to be chopped up for my insolence.

I quickly switched to formal speech, explaining as if I hadn't just been spewing curses and insults.

"The god of this place—the Trial Boss—is causing an anomaly, and the Trial System itself has collapsed. Even if we kill all the Creatures, there's no chance of a normal ending."

"..."

"We have to team up with the Creatures to fight the Boss."

CEO Cha suddenly asked something else.

"Did you get infected here?"

"...Pardon?"

"Will your eyes go back to normal if we kill the Boss?"

He must have concluded that my golden eyes were a result of the infection.

If he didn't know about the god's existence, that was practically the most logical conclusion.

He had made a very convenient assumption, so I naturally played along.

"Yes. I was infected at The Cell X."

I pressed him again. "We need your help, CEO Cha, to get out of here. Please cooperate."

I looked up at him with desperate eyes.

CEO Cha was a flawed individual, but no one could deny his abilities.

Even now, that was true.

In the sea surrounded by Neo, CEO Cha alone remained unscathed. He had come to find me without a single drop of the neon green liquid staining him.

I had firmly believed he would be safe, but seeing him not just unharmed, but completely untouched by even a single drop of Neo...

A familiar sense of relief washed over me.

Recalling old memories, I called out to him again as he remained silent.

"CEO Cha..."

Then, CEO Cha slowly opened his mouth to answer.

"You called me a crazy bastard, didn't you?"

"...I apologize for that. Anyway, you're going to cooperate, right?"

"Depending on how you behave."

Though his reply carried a hint of temper, it seemed he would cooperate after all. If he hadn't liked my proposal, he would have thrown me into the Neo Sea long ago.

"Thank you," I said, thanking him presumptuously.

'How much time do I have left?'

Just as I was thinking that I would need to act immediately once Je Hyun-oh returned with Won Taebeom and the Creatures—

Suddenly, everything went dark.

It was a darkness so absolute that even the neon green liquid seemed to lose its glow.

I looked up at the sky.

There was…

An eye.

A colossal eye gazed down upon the subterranean sea.

I wasn't sure if "eye" was the right word to describe it. Its form defied all conventional notions.

Within the massive eye, countless other eyes, large and small, were packed together.

The eyeballs moved independently, scanning the surroundings like prison searchlights in search of something.

The sight was beyond mere grotesqueness; it evoked an instinctive loathing.

Amidst the hundreds of pupils scanning every direction, my gaze met one of them.

The moment it spotted me—

Every single gaze converged on me at once.

As the stares rained down like a shower of arrows, I...

...Slowly smiled.

A sudden surge of heat enveloped me.

The thought of burning all those eyeballs to ash filled me with boundless joy.

I reached out toward them, dangling myself like a piece of prime bait.

*Come here.*

They whispered to me over and over, beckoning me to approach...

But then, the entire world was flooded with a brilliant white light.

The white light cast down like the sun, banishing the darkness and illuminating everything as bright as midday.

I looked at the man who had created the light.

He shouted something to me, but I couldn't hear him clearly.

A streak of black darkness flew through the white light and snatched me away.

It was my *subordinate*.

I burst into a delighted laugh and spoke to my own.

*"Let's kill him together."*

I wanted to share the fun and excitement.
