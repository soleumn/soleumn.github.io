<br>"You have a sibling?"

Cecilia stared at me, her eyes wide with surprise.

I nodded. It was a lie I'd hastily cobbled together, but it was the best choice I could make.

The only relationship the jealous Cecilia would ever tolerate was that of a sibling. She had been forcibly separated from her own younger sister long ago, and that trauma had left a deep scar. It was why she tended to become obsessively attached to those she held dear.

That was also why she hated it when I built bonds of camaraderie with the Stringers in the game.

But a sibling wasn't a comrade. She would probably let that slide.

Besides, I couldn't think of any other words that started with the same syllable.

Anyway, it was the best lie for now, but dealing with the fallout later would be the real problem.

Je Hyun-oh folded his wings and landed slowly.

*Clatter.* The metallic wings retracted into his Crow-themed hoodie.

Cecilia watched him approach, her gaze still sharp with lingering suspicion.

*'He did look suspicious...'*

As always, Je Hyun-oh defied the Trial's rules. He was the only one not wearing the standard COF hoodie, sporting the full Crow Set instead. His all-black attire blended strangely well with the back alleys of Hyper Eden.

*'Come to think of it, he said it would take an hour.'*

I had entered COF first, since he'd told me it could take an hour or more to reach the broadcasting station.

Still, this was practically like he'd been right on my heels the whole time.

I wondered if he'd flown here instead of moving by container.

While I was lost in thought, Je Hyun-oh walked up to me.

I hastily offered Cecilia an explanation.

"He's my younger brother. We grew up together in the orphanage."

I told her he was as good as a biological brother, even if we weren't blood-related, but Cecilia's eyes remained suspicious.

Honestly, it was probably because no one could see Je Hyun-oh as my brother.

While Cecilia scanned Je Hyun-oh with a sharp gaze, I sent him a desperate message.

I explained in detail that she was an important NPC and that she wouldn't accept any relationship other than him being my brother. As I did, Je Hyun-oh looked at me.

I was on edge, half-expecting to be struck down by that metal hand at any moment.

Then, an unbelievable word reached my ears.

"Hyung."

My jaw dropped. I thought I had misheard. It had to be a mistake—otherwise, this made no sense.

As I stood frozen in shock, Je Hyun-oh tilted his head slightly. A slow question followed.

"What are you doing here?"

I had set this whole situation in motion, but it was still hard to handle. I had only imagined him staying silent and still; I never expected him to play along so actively.

But if I hesitated any longer, I would never dispel Cecilia's suspicion.

She still held the pulse gun in her hand.

I forced my voice out, barely managing to speak. "I... I was meeting a friend..."

My voice cracked.

*'What a disgrace. I'm the one who'll have to bow and apologize to Je Hyun-oh later.'*

Since the cat was already out of the bag, I quickly told Cecilia about the Captains too.

When I revealed they were four siblings from an orphanage, a deep frown creased her brow.

"You're the eldest...?"

I nodded, and she narrowed her eyes, her expression one of utter disbelief.

"Roasted Chestnut. Why on earth would you put your siblings through the danger of Stringer work? Tell them to stay home and rest comfortably."

Then, she threw an arm around my shoulders and smiled brightly.

"You only need me, right?"

Her hand gently pinched my cheek, a gesture full of affection. I was about to smile back unconsciously when—

A deep, rough voice spat out a single word.

"What about your younger brother?"

"..."

It felt like not only my words but my very breath had been choked out.

When I hesitated to answer, Je Hyun-oh slowly rattled his metal hand and muttered, "Big brother is wandering around on his own... not even looking after his little brother..."

He looked ready to tear his older brother apart with that metal hand.

I answered quickly. "That's not true! We always have to be together!"

At that, the lights on his dark goggles flickered on.

Having barely managed to resolve the situation, I turned back to Cecilia and realized something I'd missed.

There were three of us, but both of them had been staring only at me this whole time.

I forced an awkward smile and called out to Cecilia.

"Noona..."

When I looked up at her pleadingly, she rolled her eyes in disapproval.

She placed one hand on her hip and said, "Fine. We can't keep talking in the middle of the street, so let's head back to the base first."

Every Stringer in the city would be hunting for me, so staying outside like this was a bad idea.

I agreed with her, but the biggest problem was getting back to the base.

Cecilia's teleportation ability had limitations.

There was a set distance she could teleport each day, and she couldn't exceed that limit.

She had to save her power since we didn't know what might happen next.

It would be best to try and reach the base on our own first, and use her ability as a last resort if we failed.

I took off my hooded jacket and pulled it over my head.

It covered me generously enough to hide my face, but it still looked incredibly suspicious. It was the perfect look to attract reporters desperate for a scoop.

I needed something more reliable.

*'If only I had something like CEO Cha's Cognitive Decline Mask,'* I thought.

*Click.*

The sound of an item being unequipped echoed.

Startled, I looked at Je Hyun-oh. He was taking off his Raven Goggles.

He hooked the goggles on his metal fingertips and held them out to me.

"This is perfect, Roasted Chestnut," Cecilia said approvingly. "This will cover half your face."

It was indeed the best item for concealing my face. But I hesitated, unsure if I should really be using it.

As I stood there dumbfounded, Je Hyun-oh gave a silent nod of encouragement.

I politely accepted the goggles with both hands.

*'Wow...!'*

I had only taken them because I had no choice, but I was secretly thrilled to get to wear Je Hyun-oh's goggles.

Seizing the opportunity, I checked the item description.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Raven Goggles: What does the black eye gaze upon?</strong>
  </div>
</div>

The description for the Crow Gloves had been abstract, and the goggles were no different.

I wanted to check the item descriptions for Kwak Hanmuk's prayer beads and Mo Haein's Black Moon Sword later.

With the goggles equipped, my curiosity was itching to get out. I wanted to ask how to display emoticons, but I held back with all my might since Cecilia was right beside me.

The journey to the base went smoothly.

The goggles concealed my face, and having Cecilia and Je Hyun-oh escort me on either side was highly effective.

No one seemed to guess that I had just been frantically running away.

Thanks to that, we arrived at the base without any problems.

"How could this be!"

Cecilia screamed the moment she saw the dilapidated apartment building, which looked like it was about to collapse.

"I can't let my Castañita sleep in a place like this!"

Come to think of it, I had never brought Cecilia to the base in the game. I had always visited her at her place, so this must have been the first time she learned I lived in a place like this.

"Roasted Chestnut. I'll get you a house. I'll get one right away, so let's move."

"But I don't want to be a burden to you, Sister..."

"How can I leave you in a place like this!"

Cecilia urgently tried to persuade me. Even the usually docile Pudding Slime chimed in.

"You should know how to accept a favor, Roasted Chestnut."

It seemed he was very displeased that I was staying in that old apartment.

After some thought, I decided to accept their help.

If it were just me, I wouldn't have minded, but staying in such a run-down place with Je Hyun-oh and the Captains was a bit much.

"Thanks, Cecilia."

When I thanked her, Cecilia seemed even more pleased than I was.

Just then, a beeping sound broke the moment. It was Cecilia's work pager.

Cecilia checked the message and frowned.

"...Sigh. Of all days, it had to be today."

She had been contacted by someone she had a prior engagement with. Explaining that she couldn't back out since they were a business partner, Cecilia let out a heavy sigh.

"I'll find a place for us by tomorrow night, so we can move in then. Got it?"

She gently stroked my hair, telling me to stay here for just one more night and that she'd see me tomorrow.

"Oh, Cecilia. I have one favor to ask."

Before we parted ways, I made a request that sent her into fits of laughter, clutching her stomach.

After she left, Je Hyun-oh and I walked back into the apartment.

I walked up the stairs to the sixth floor and opened the old front door. A System Window appeared, announcing the quest's completion.

  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You have arrived at the base!</strong>
  </div>

A new quest immediately followed, instructing me to begin my first assignment.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein shot up from their chairs to greet me.

"Goyo!"

"Recruit!"

Their faces were a chaotic mix of bewilderment and worry.

I had sent a message ahead to explain the situation, but it was still a shocking sight. They seemed even more startled by the Raven Goggles I was wearing.

I took them off, handed them back to Je Hyun-oh, and immediately apologized.

"I'm sorry. This is all my fault."

"...No, don't apologize."

Mo Haein held out the newspaper she had been reading. Her expression was tinged with anger as she spoke.

"You didn't do anything wrong."

It was the Day News...

I scoffed as I read the headline.

<br>

<kbd>BREAKING NEWS! THE YOUNG GOD DESCENDS UPON HYPER EDEN!</kbd>

<br>

The article bluntly declared that the Young god was currently in Hyper Eden, urging stringers to seize the scoop.

So that's why they'd rushed in with such unwavering conviction.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je."

Kwak Hanmuk, arms crossed, posed a short question to Je Hyun-oh.

"Was she alright?"

Though it sounded like a casual inquiry, the underlying wariness was immediately apparent.

It was Cecilia.

She was an NPC who showed me an abnormally strong fondness, much like the butlers of Clean3.

Even if she'd received a message from me, it was only natural for them to be suspicious.

"She'll be fine."

Surprisingly, Je Hyun-oh readily offered a positive opinion.

He had judged Cecilia to be a useful NPC.

I was relieved, thinking she'd be essential for conquering COF.

"As long as Brother Goyo doesn't do anything weird."

"..."

Je Hyun-oh's comment made me want to hide.

Kwak Hanmuk silently clenched his fist, pressing it against his mouth. He was clearly holding back laughter.

Then, Mo Haein dropped a bombshell in her plain, matter-of-fact voice.

"Then, in front of the NPC, the recruit should be called—"

"No! I mean, yes, but... that's..."

I couldn't help but shout before she could finish the title.

As I stammered, Kwak Hanmuk finally burst out laughing.

I wished he'd just keep laughing, but Mo Haein's expression was so dead serious that my cheeks burned with a fierce blush.

I hesitated for a moment before bowing my head low and replying, "Please... I'm counting on you."

"Got it. Don't worry. I can handle that much."

"Wow, I've been calling you 'Hyung' all this time, and now you've actually become a real Chestnut Hyung. This is fucking crazy."

I watched Kwak Hanmuk, who had collapsed over the back of the sofa, wheezing with laughter. I tried to change the subject by bringing up our strategy.

"I'm thinking we'll just enter the First Stage tonight and leave immediately, then proceed with the coverage in the Second Stage."

Since I was already getting help, I had also borrowed the next piece of filming equipment—a camera—from Cecilia. 

I planned to pay the rental fee with the money I would earn tonight.

Mo Haein, who had been nudging Kwak Hanmuk to stop laughing, turned to me and asked,

"So, what's our first reporting quest?"

"I'm planning to finish it in advance."

As we arrived at the base, the first reporting quest popped up. It instructed us to go down into the sewers and record the sound of the monster said to live there.

But there are no monsters there. Even if you struggle all night, you'll only record the sound of the wind. Even if you succeed in the quest, you'll just be mocked on the late-night radio. It's a reporting task that is both meaningless and draining.

We entered with the Special Team, and I didn't want to waste time on something like that.

Therefore, I...

"Um... Lieutenant Colonel Je, Captain Kwak."

...decided to try actively utilizing the talent at hand.

"May I ask you for a favor?"

It was the roar of a monster, rising from the abyss.

Interspersed with the monster's guttural whispers was the unsettling clatter of metal, heightening the dread. At the peak of this chilling atmosphere, the victim's desperate screams erupted.

The DJ spoke in a somber tone.

I turned off the radio and smiled with satisfaction.

"This... actually worked."

Mo Haein muttered, her voice laced with disbelief. Kwak Hanmuk, clearly pleased with his own performance, spoke up.

"Captain Mo, Director Chestnut was a total hardass. He really ran Lieutenant Colonel Je through the wringer."

"Thank you so much."

I bowed deeply to Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh again.

They were the stars of the monster and victim sounds broadcast today.

We had just scammed our way through the first quest.
