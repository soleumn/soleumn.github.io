<br>His mind went blank.

There was no need to doubt Samra's words. After all, Samra never lied.

Kwak Hanmuk immediately sprang from the bed.

As expected, not much time had passed since he'd cleared the trial; he was still in his uniform, not a patient's gown.

Since there were no major issues, they'd simply moved him to a hospital room for now, while all available personnel seemed to be swarming around Han Goyo.

He quickly slipped into the combat boots left beneath the bed and bolted out the door.

He knew the Research Department had taken over the basement of the Gyeonggi Regional Office.

With no time to wait for an elevator, he headed straight for the emergency stairs.

The emergency staircases at the Gyeonggi Regional Office were designed with an open center, with the stairs wrapping around it in a rectangular shape.

The central shaft was open from the basement all the way to the top floor.

*Swish!* The prayer beads on his wrist coiled around the staircase railing.

Kwak Hanmuk leaped into the central void in a single bound.

The prayer beads unfurled, their string slowing his descent. The moment he hit the ground floor, he burst through the door as if to break it.

The basement was in total chaos.

Researchers were dashing about, shouting orders, while others were locked in heated arguments. The Research Department was no longer the only one here; personnel from other headquarters had swarmed the area, adding to the turmoil.

Kwak Hanmuk seized the nearest researcher by the shoulder.

"Where is Trainee Han Goyo?"

The researcher turned, ready to snap in annoyance, but flinched upon meeting Kwak Hanmuk's gaze and immediately led the way.

Following him with anxious strides, Kwak Hanmuk finally found Han Goyo.

Han Goyo was immersed in preservation fluid.

He must have been placed in it immediately after being declared dead to prevent any bodily damage.

The sight of him with his eyes closed, as if sleeping inside the massive glass cylinder, was familiar. Mo Haein would sometimes fall into this kind of sleep because of the Meteorites of Trial implanted in her body.

But... he never expected to see Han Goyo like this.

Submerged in the preservation fluid, Han Goyo looked exceptionally pale, which made him seem even younger than usual.

He looked around for the small slime that was always by Han Goyo's side, but it was nowhere to be seen.

As he gazed up at the figure who seemed to be in a deep sleep, he checked the vital signs monitor right next to the glass cylinder.

The waveforms all drew flat lines.

The pronouncement of death was unmistakable.

Yet, out of nowhere, a thought surged through his mind.

*'He's still alive.'*

Though it was a baseless thought, Kwak Hanmuk was certain. Han Goyo was alive.

"Hanmuk."

A voice whispered his name. Mo Haein had approached him.

She, too, seemed to have rushed here. Shock from the unbelievable news was etched on her face, yet her gaze remained steady and unwavering.

The moment their eyes met, both Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein realized it.

They were thinking the exact same thing.

Han Goyo was not dead.

They shared an inexplicable conviction.

"Hae-in. Are you okay?"

The brief question held a multitude of meanings. Perhaps because of the time they had spent together, Mo Haein immediately understood his intent.

She nodded silently.

Kwak Hanmuk shifted his gaze, a flicker of relief washing over him. Only then did his surroundings come into focus.

The first person to catch his eye was Je Hyun-oh.

He stood directly in front of the glass pillar. With his metallic hand hanging at his side, he looked like a guardian for the one trapped inside.

Even without seeing the completely dark goggles, Hanmuk could guess Je Hyun-oh's state of mind.

He had fully deployed his metallic wings.

Je Hyun-oh usually kept them retracted and hidden unless necessary. He almost never revealed them outside of a Trial.

Je Hyun-oh, poised for combat, flexed his metallic hand with a sharp* clack*.

Before him, Ham Jiwol of the Research Analysis Headquarters and the directors of Paseong stood in a tense standoff.

Unlike Mok Saehwa and Seo Munchan, who were impeccably dressed in suits, Ham Jiwol was covered in blood. She appeared to have been lightly struck by Je Hyun-oh while attempting to approach the glass pillar. Yet, despite the blade wounds all over her body, her eyes gleamed with a manic intensity.

A short distance away stood Chief Jang Seokyun, Lieutenant Won Taebeom, and Samra. Jang Seokyun's complexion was so ghastly that even a polite lie couldn't describe it as "good." Beside him, Won Taebeom looked somehow even worse.

His face, which rarely betrayed any emotion, was now etched with clear despair.

It was as if his entire world had crumbled.

Samrado was also in an unstable state.

His eyes flashed repeatedly, as if he were constantly calculating something.

Ham Jiwol spoke in a very clear voice.

"Goyo's body belongs to the Era Agency."

She continued to state her claim with precise articulation.

"It is data that the Republic of Korea, and the entire world, absolutely needs to defend against the Trial. Lieutenant Colonel Je, Directors, please just give up."

"Haha, Director Ham. I'm sorry. What power do we have? We're just salaried employees. To CEO Cha—"

"Se Mi-ri already gave us strict instructions. You know what our CEO is like, don't you?"

Seo Munchan wore a sly grin. He spoke with the slippery charm of an eel, pleading for them to consider his own company's position.

"Paseong will be taking Goyo's body."

"Absolutely not!" Ham Jiwol retorted the moment Seo Munchan finished speaking. "He must be returned to the state. We cannot hand him over to a private company!"

Mok Saehwa, bleeding profusely, watched Ham Jiwol's unwavering stance with an amused, almost affectionate gaze. Yet, she remained ready to draw her weapon at any moment.

Mok Saehwa had no intention of backing down.

Seo Munchan immediately turned to face Jang Seokyun.

"Director Jang, the recruit's contract is over. The Era Agency has no right to claim ownership of Han Goyo. On the other hand, Paseong has signed a dispatch agreement. In this case, wouldn't Paseong have a bit more priority?"

Seo Munchan pulled a contract from his inventory and waved it.

*'What is that now?'*

Kwak Hanmuk barely suppressed the curse that was about to escape his lips.

What kind of trouble had Han Goyo gotten into for Paseong to have something like that in their hands?

If he were by his side, he would have hit him on the head ten times with his prayer beads.

"Director Seo, I understand what you want, but that's impossible. The contract doesn't specify post-ownership, does it?"

As Jang Seokyun replied slowly, Mok Saehwa immediately let out a deep sigh.

She deliberately acted rude to draw his attention before speaking.

"Chief. Listen to me. Right now, Representative Cha is the only one in a coma, isn't he?"

*'A coma?'*

Kwak Hanmuk immediately checked Je Hyun-oh and Won Taebeom again.

They must have faced the god too, yet they were perfectly fine, just like him.

Unlike Han Goyo, who had even been pronounced dead.

He concluded that Han Goyo must have done something to create this situation.

Otherwise, he and the others wouldn't be so intact.

His gaze drifted toward Han Goyo inside the glass pillar without him even realizing it.

The memory of Han Goyo, unable to even meet his eyes before the meteorite fell, flashed through his mind.



<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);"><i>“Can't you just keep... teaming up with me?”</i></span>



*'Fuck.'*

Kwak Hanmuk cursed himself, barely managing to keep his train of thought.

If so, why was only CEO Cha in a coma?

It was certain that an accident had occurred during the Clear.

But that wasn't the only thing that was strange.

Even though CEO Cha was in a coma, the directors showed no fear or worry. They only had an unwavering faith that CEO Cha would definitely regain consciousness and recover.

He knew they were fanatics of CEO Cha, but this was almost at the level of worshipping a god.

"We entered the Era Agency's Trial, but the Representative ended up like this, and you're ignoring the dispatch contract. You're not telling Paseong to bear all the losses and dedicate itself to the state right now, are you?"

Following Mok Saehwa's remark, Seo Munchan drove the point home.

"You'll have to at least hand over Goyo's body so we can recover some of our losses."

Ham Jiwol's eyes flashed with fury. Her voice rose as she shouted, "What can Paseong even do with the body? They don't have research facilities like the Era Agency—at best, they have a few medical clinics. Claiming ownership is pointless!"

Ham Jiwol clenched her fists, her words pouring out in a rapid torrent. "The Era Agency will analyze Goyo-ssi down to the nano-level. They'll use everything—from a single strand of hair to a sliver of a fingernail—as data for the future. Goyo-ssi's value to the future of humanity...!"

"Director Ham, what the Era Agency does is irrelevant. What matters is that we are following the Representative's prior orders."

Seo Munchan's gaze was as unyielding as Ham Jiwol's. Mok Saehwa then spoke, a beaming smile on her face.

"The Era Agency is only thinking about dissecting Goyo's body, isn't it? Goyo wouldn't have wanted that. Am I right?"

But it wasn't Ham Jiwol who answered the directors.

"...The Trial Response Agency," Samra said, his tone slightly slower than usual, his eyes flashing repeatedly. "It might be possible to revive Han Goyo. We are calculating a method to connect his brain to an artificial life-support system and transplant it into an android body. We will prevent Director Ham Jiwol from using Han Goyo's body."

"Samra!"

"Director Ham Jiwol. Humanity needs Han Goyo."

Just as Samra's intervention turned the situation into complete chaos...

*Screeeech!*

The sound of metal scraping echoed through the space.

Every eye turned toward the source of the sound.

Je Hyun-oh, who had just scratched the life-sign monitor with his metallic hand, whispered, "He's not dead."

In that instant, Kwak Hanmuk immediately threw his prayer beads.

With a sharp *clatter*, the beads wrapped around the glass pillar.

After concealing Han Goyo from everyone's sight, he came to stand beside Je Hyun-oh.

"You may find this hard to believe," he began, his gaze sweeping over the others who were still struggling to grasp the situation.

He wanted nothing more than to tear down Ham Jiwol and Paseong for everything they'd done. But knowing how absurd his claim sounded, he forced himself to speak as calmly as possible.

"As Lieutenant Colonel Je said, Trainee Han Goyo is still alive."

Kwak Hanmuk wasn't the only one. Mo Haein had also rushed to the glass pillar.

She stood there, her small frame partially shielding it, and drew her Black Moon Sword.

Gripping the hilt so tightly that the knuckles on the back of her hand turned white, she echoed his claim.

"He's alive. Please, believe me."

A heavy silence descended.

The directors scoffed in disbelief.

Seo Munchan exchanged a glance with Mok Saehwa before speaking.

"I'd like to know the basis for your judgment. Or, perhaps you could show us Goyo waking up."

Just then, something appeared before Je Hyun-oh, Kwak Hanmuk, and Mo Haein.

It was a vision granted only to them, invisible to everyone else.

A light flickered on the dark goggles.

The sudden emoticon left everyone speechless.

Je Hyun-oh extended his metallic hand.

Just before the directors could draw their weapons, a short sentence was heard.

"Three days."

Je Hyun-oh rattled his outstretched metallic hand and spoke lazily.

"If he doesn't wake up after three days, then you can take him to Paseong."

His words were filled with conviction, as if he had received a divine revelation.
