<br>I will change all my subordinates.

If I pluck out their eyes and make them look only at their master, they will never be able to hide anything from me again.

Truthfully, I had always been anxious.

Even though they were my subordinates, I never felt I fully possessed them.

Kwak Hanmuk had allowed another god into his body, and Mo Haein had the Meteorites of Trial embedded in her stomach.

Je Hyun-oh's body had already undergone a transformation.

And yet, I didn't even know the reason why they had become that way.

The fact that I knew nothing always left me feeling restless and anxious.

But it's okay now.

Because I've found the solution.

I just need to make them completely mine, even if it's late.

What a ridiculous thought.

In truth, I was regretting how I had neglected my subordinates all this time. I had to take care of them immediately.

I kept ignoring the System Window that flashed before my eyes.

*'I'll start with Captain Kwak, then send a message to Captain Mo and Lieutenant Colonel Je.'*

Je Hyun-oh had entered a Trial, so I would have him come the moment he returned. And then Mo Haein...

*Click.*

The sound of the door opening cut short my thoughts.

"What's up? You said you were going to bed, Chestnut, but you're already bor—"

Kwak Hanmuk, who had appeared with a playful grin, trailed off, his expression hardening.

"I'm not bored. I have something urgent to tell you."

I reported my business quickly, but for some reason, Kwak Hanmuk didn't respond.

He frowned slightly, crossing his arms. Then, leaning his shoulder against the doorframe, he just stared at me.

Normally, he would have asked what was wrong, Chestnut, but now he just watched me in silence.

Since I was the one in a hurry, I asked him again.

"May I come in?"

Kwak Hanmuk gazed at me intensely for a moment before answering.

"Come in."

He took a step back, and I finally entered the room.

As soon as we entered the room, I pointed to the bed.

"Please sit here."

I thought it would be best for him to have his eyes removed in a place where he felt comfortable and familiar.

Kwak Hanmuk complied without resistance and sat on the bed. He leaned back on his hands, looking up at me.

I stood before my subordinate, who was seated at an angle, and scrutinized his eyes with a grave expression.

As expected, I loathed those gray irises. I wanted to pluck them out and embed gold ones in their place.

As I continued my examination, Kwak Hanmuk spoke.

"Goyo. What are you trying to do?"

Only then did I realize I hadn't explained anything.

I spoke to him in a serious tone.

"I am about to remove your eyes, Captain Kwak."

Kwak Hanmuk let out a short, scoffing laugh.

"My eyes?" he asked.

"Yes."

"Well, are you taking both?"

"It wouldn't look very good if I only took one, would it? It won't affect your vision."

"Why are you taking them?"

I paused, wondering how to explain. I worried that telling the truth might make me seem too narrow-minded. But since I couldn't think of a good excuse, I decided to be honest.

"Because you seem to be hiding too much from me."

"So you're taking my eyes because I have too many secrets?"

"Yes. That's why I'm going to take care of you as your master from now on."

"As my master..."

Kwak Hanmuk muttered my words back to me, raising an eyebrow. "That sounds like it's going to hurt a lot. How am I supposed to trust my master's skill?"

If I just looked into his mind a little, it should be fine.

If I gently kneaded his consciousness so he wouldn't feel any pain before pulling the eye out, it wouldn't hurt much.

I diligently soothed my frightened subordinate.

"I'll make it hurt as little as possible."

"But you know... there's nothing in it for me if you pull my eye out."

"What? How could you say that?"

Of course, there was a huge advantage.

I smiled faintly and said to him, "You will become completely mine."    

That was a joyful thing for the subordinates. After all, a life devoted solely to their master was the greatest happiness they could desire.

Pleasant fantasies followed. I imagined adorning my subordinates, whom I would fashion to look only at me.

*'Come to think of it, how should I change their bodies? Should I buy some items?'*

I'd seen several body modification items in the subordinate section of the Galactic Trading Post.

I hadn't added them to my cart, thinking I'd never need to buy them, but I remembered their names well enough to find them again quickly.

The financial issue could be solved with a sponsorship contract.

Even if my account was frozen, I could just receive the items instead of currency, so I wasn't particularly worried.

*Dominic would give me anything I wanted.*

And if Dominic were to refuse, I still had Mr. Black Block.

Mr. Black Block might make some rather unreasonable demands in exchange for his sponsorship, but...

Still, this was for my subordinates.

It was truly a perfect plan.

I smiled and reached out. My fingertips were about to touch the corner of Kwak Hanmuk's eye when—

"...!"

I grabbed the prayer beads he had thrust forward.

*'Why is he trying to bind me?'*

As I stared at him in confusion, Kwak Hanmuk let out a short laugh.

"So you don't want to be bound, huh?"

Before I could react, he slammed into me. In the blink of an eye, I was sprawled on the floor.

I put up a brief, desperate fight against him on the ground, but he quickly overpowered me.

Pinned face-down, I felt his knee crush into my back.

Kwak Hanmuk immediately twisted my arms behind me and seized them.

He tried to bind me with the prayer beads, but my resistance made them slip and slide.

Kwak Hanmuk spat a curse, then yanked the struggling beads tight, binding my arms like a rope.

Still pinned to the floor, I strained to turn my head back toward him.

"Captain Kwak..."

His eyes drooped as he asked, "Are you very scared? If you can bear it for just a moment, I'll pull it out quickly. It'll be over in a flash."

*'I was even going to praise you when it was all over.'*

It was a pity to see him so frightened by a pain that would only last a fleeting instant.

Kwak Hanmuk swept back his disheveled hair with one hand.

"Wow... Crazy Chestnut is driving me crazy." 

The lights-out room had only been lit by the faint glow from outside.

But now, it wasn't dark at all—it was brilliant. The light was streaming from my own eyes.

Kwak Hanmuk muttered, his gaze lost in a tangle of complex thoughts.

"So *this* is a god..."

His solitary murmur was laced with bewilderment. He reacted as if this was nothing like the god he had known.

A creeping sense of displeasure washed over me.

*'Is he comparing me to the god inside Captain Kwak?'*

*I hated that.*

At that moment, blue sparks crackled from the prayer beads.

Kwak Hanmuk frowned slightly and pressed his hand over his heart. I flinched too.

"Captain Kwak. This hurts a little. I'm having trouble breathing..."

It didn't actually hurt much—just a sharp sting—but I said it anyway, hoping he might take the prayer beads off.

But Kwak Hanmuk didn't buy it.

"Suffer through it. You're the one who tried to gouge out someone's eyes."

"..."

I had no comeback, so I kept my mouth shut.

Still, I could feel him adjusting the weight of his knee on my back to make it easier for me to breathe.

Kwak Hanmuk sighed in lamentation and called my name.

"Goyo."

"Yes."

"Even if you gouge my eyes out, you won't get what you want."

"Why not?"

"Because I'm not going to tell you anything. I'll keep hiding it, keeping it secret, and keeping you in the dark."

*'That won't be possible, will it?'*

Kwak Hanmuk seemed to be under the delusion that he could retain his ego even after his eyes were removed. He didn't understand that he would be reconstructed as a being dedicated to me.

But I didn't tell him the truth.

I just... felt like I shouldn't.

"But if you still want to gouge my eyes out..."

*Crackle, crackle.* Blue light flashed continuously from the prayer beads.

As sparks flew, a faint tremor coursed through our touching bodies.

I felt puzzled by the tremor, which seemed to be a sign of pain.

That was because this wasn’t pain I had inflicted on him.

Kwak Hanmuk spoke, his voice laced with amusement. "A master should learn to resist a little."

*Me?*

The moment a wave of intense repulsion washed over me, a burst of golden light erupted. The prayer beads that had been binding me finally shattered, flashing with a brilliant blue light.

As the gold and blue lights mingled, the force pinning me down from behind momentarily weakened. I shoved Kwak Hanmuk away and scrambled out from beneath him.

We now faced each other, standing just a few steps apart. The room was small enough that we could reach each other with a single stretch of our arms.

I glared at Kwak Hanmuk, my eyes cold.

A faint glow shimmered beneath his sleeveless shirt. The fractal-patterned lightning tattoo was radiating a vivid blue light.

That wasn't all. A vivid blue light seeped into his gray eyes.

Sparks continuously crackled around him.

*I wanted to strip away every last trace of that blue light.*

"Fuck, Lieutenant Colonel Je... They said you were young and weak..."

Kwak Hanmuk looked at me, gasping for breath. His lips trembled as he forced a smile.

"This is some serious violence, huh?"

Dominic, who had been watching from above, casually chimed in.

"Roasted Chestnut. You don't need to listen to every single word your subordinates say."

He reasoned that subordinates never truly know what they want, so Roasted Chestnut, as their master, should be the one to lead and teach them.

I agreed with him to some extent.

His resistance was so fierce that I felt I had to break his spirit first.

I'd only intended to touch his mind enough to make him feel no pain from the eye gouging.

But if I looked any *deeper*...

"..."

I stopped dead, as if someone had pressed a pause button, just as I was about to examine Kwak Hanmuk.

The image of the Paranoid Researcher flashed through my mind.

The one whose mind I had crushed...

At that moment, my wrist was seized.

The instant a tingling sensation seeped in, the wall-wood prayer beads wrapped themselves around me with all their might, and Kwak Hanmuk spat blood as a burst of blue light erupted.

The sound of a thunderclap echoed through the cloudless night sky.

The brilliant blue light seared itself into my vision.

I realized.

"...Captain Kwak!"

What I had been about to do.

The wall-wood prayer beads slipped off, and Kwak Hanmuk's body swayed. I quickly supported him and helped him sit on the bed.

Kwak Hanmuk stared at me, trembling, and began to laugh. He wiped the blood trickling down his chin with the back of his hand.

"Being the person in charge of Gwangbam-i is fucking hard..."

For a split second, I had glimpsed *inside* him.

Because of that, I now clearly understood his condition.

Kwak Hanmuk had partially undone the seal that imprisoned the god within him just to subdue me.

In that state, I had tried to peer inside, and I collided with the god residing in his body.

If I hadn't stopped, Kwak Hanmuk's mind would have shattered, just like the Paranoid Researcher's.

*'Why did I do that?'*

I had always considered the Young god and myself to be separate beings.

But my past self had been a jumble of both. I had been thinking like the Young god while simultaneously making judgments as "me." I hadn't even realized how strange that was.

*'When did this start?'*

The moment I recognized the dissonance, I retraced my actions since I had eaten the ■■■.

The more I recalled, the more I realized I had been deluding myself. I had resolved to protect myself by eating the ■■■, plunging into a gamble with slim odds. And I had believed I was fighting well enough in my own way.

But that was pure delusion. I might have already lost.

"Goyo."

"..."

My trembling eyes fixed on him, covered in blood.

Kwak Hanmuk looked up at me and spoke.

"It's okay."

It was anything but okay.

When he asked me to join his team, and when he pulled me out of the Quarantine Facility as the one in charge...

Kwak Hanmuk could never have imagined this would happen.

I had almost destroyed him.

Come to think of it, ever since he registered as my subordinate, he'd done nothing but get hurt.

The fact that I was his god offered Kwak Hanmuk no benefit.

Instead, it was a colossal disaster.

"...I'm sorry, Captain Kwak. I... I don't know how to undo this registration... Because of me..."

The words that spilled from my lips were nothing more than a pathetic excuse.

I was frozen in the face of an irreversible mistake.

Kwak Hanmuk called my name again.

"Chestnut."

"Yes..."

"I'm sorry."

I stared at him blankly, not understanding why he was apologizing.

Kwak Hanmuk met my eyes and continued.

"You must be incredibly frustrated with the situation, and I've only made it harder by not being able to explain properly. But seriously, okay? Let's not gouge out any eyeballs. Just give me a little time..."

He paused, hesitating for a long moment as if he needed to judge for himself whether the words he was about to say were right.

Finally, he spoke to me.

"I'll tell you everything."

Then, he reached out and gripped my forearm.

I felt a slight weight pulling me down.

He spoke to me, a recruit, as a captain of the same team.

"It's just that I don't know much right now, so I can't say anything."

"..."

"Well, probably... Captain Mo will be about the same. She'll tell you everything if you just wait. Ah, as for Lieutenant Colonel Je, I don't know about him. He's completely out of his mind."

I just stared blankly at Kwak Hanmuk, who was joking around.

As I stood there dazed, he lightly shook my arm to get my attention.

"About the registration. I'm registered under you, right?"

"...Yes."

"Since you're the one in charge, of course I should be registered."

Then, as a subordinate, he spoke to his god.

"Don't even think about kicking me out."

I felt a sharp pang in my heart.

A strange sensation I'd never experienced before washed over me.

I... wanted to answer him firmly.

That as a teammate, and as a god, I would not be his calamity.

Just then, a System Window appeared before my eyes.
