<br>It wasn't entertaining in the least.

At the very least, it was certain that this was not a pastime for me.

I had endured until now because I believed that Black Block wouldn't do anything bad to my subordinate.

But he had finally crossed the line.

Giving some pathetic excuse that my broadcasting had messed up his plans.

My head, boiling with all sorts of thoughts and emotions, felt like it was going to burst.

The Young god and I wanted the exact same thing.

*I….*

*I wanted to make everything disappear.*

I glared at the screen. At the same time, the surface of the screen flickered.

Rough noise arose, as if the hologram was about to turn off at any moment.

It was the moment when the buzzing sound gradually grew louder.

A soft voice could be heard.

“■■■■■.”

The very second I thought it sounded like a comforting remark.

*Pop!* The overheated device burst, and the holographic screen vanished in an instant.

Exposed, CEO Cha and I froze in place.

However, there was no one there.

The hallway was quiet without a single trace of presence.

As if only CEO Cha and I had existed there from the very beginning.

Sparks flickered from the blown projection device, only announcing that what had just happened was real.

CEO Cha carefully pulled his hand away from me.

Then, in a low, sharp tone, he snapped.

“Han Goyo. Are you insane?”

I stood still, unable to even answer.

At my half-dazed state, CEO Cha started to say something, then stopped.

“…….”

I covered my eyes with my hand. It was to regain my composure by at least blocking my vision.

Otherwise, the Young god felt like it would swallow me whole right this instant.

*‘Stay still, please.’*

I was suppressing the Young god with difficulty, but my own condition wasn't that great either.

At this point, I could roughly guess Black Block's thoughts.

He said he was a fan of mine, but it didn't seem to be by human standards, after all.

Black Block was continuing to provoke me, wanting to get a reaction.

I felt like I knew the reason why he had bothered to bring my subordinate, who had been locked in the Tower, back to the masquerade ball.

It must have been to drive me mentally into a corner.

*‘So that I wouldn't get distracted by other Sponsors.’*

There had been several clashes with Black Block so far.

Every time, I pulled off things that greatly deviated from his expectations.

Black Block probably wanted to make sure I couldn't do that during this masquerade ball.

Considering it was a place where other gods gathered as well.

Now, my objectives included not only reporting, but also finding my subordinate somewhere out there.

Thanks to that, it became a situation where I absolutely could not afford to leisurely look around for potential Sponsors.

Does Black Block really think he will be chosen as a Sponsor even while pulling this kind of crap on me?

My current self was filled with nothing but the desire to obliterate him.

Suppressing my emotions as much as possible, I replayed Black Block's words in my mind.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">“It likely won’t be in the form the Lamb remembers!”</span>

<br>

I tried to guess what he might have done to my subordinate.

It wouldn't simply be inflicting physical damage.

There was no way my subordinate would feel such negative emotions over something like that.

*‘He was lonely.’*

It was the emotion my subordinate had felt.

I tried to locate my subordinate, but as expected, I couldn't feel him.

The moment my power was blocked, I deliberately cut off my thoughts. If I thought about it any further, it felt like it would be hard to remain rational.

Dominic reflexively came to mind.

He had reprimanded Black Block for taking my subordinate away and uttered words that seemed to comfort me.

But that was all. He hadn't done anything beyond that.

Talking in a language that couldn't be heard even while knowing I was behind the screen was all he did.

What on earth does Dominic want from me?

I can roughly guess the reason behind Black Block's actions, but what Dominic wants remains completely opaque.

It became even harder to figure him out ever since he split the Young god and me fifty-fifty.

If he wanted to make me a god that badly, he could have just fused the Young god and me in a ratio of around 9 to 1, to begin with.

Even though I knew I shouldn't consider him entirely on my side….

Being driven into a situation like this kept making me feel a sense of bitterness.

Pudding Slime had always been kind to me, which probably made the comparison stand out even more.

I took a deep breath and let it out.

Slowly lowering the hands that had covered my eyes, I looked at CEO Cha in front of me.

CEO Cha silently and steadily watched me. Under his confirming gaze, I first offered a word of thanks.

If it hadn't been for him, I surely would have caused trouble of some sort.

"Thank you for holding me back earlier."

"……."

"As you can see, my condition is strange. I am not normal."

"I know."

He truly didn't have a single drop of idle small talk in him.

I made him aware of my urgent situation.

"I need to go look for my subordinate right now, so…."

"Your subordinate?"

For some reason, CEO Cha cut in, interrupting what I was saying.

I retraced my steps to see if I had made a slip of the tongue, but since there didn't seem to be anything particularly odd about it, I continued speaking.

"Yes. Therefore, it seems difficult to search for Kang Byeokrin together. I will repay you later. I'm sorry, but I'm in a rush, so I'll be going now."

“…Ha.”

Leaving behind CEO Cha, who let out an empty laugh, I busily moved my footsteps.

Yet, for some reason, I felt footsteps following behind me.

When I turned around in bewilderment, CEO Cha spoke crookedly.

"Where are you going? The conversation isn't over."

What else did he want to talk about? But immediately following that, a short, abrupt remark dropped.

"Ask for help."

It was a remark so out of the blue that I wondered if I had misheard.

He repeated it as if giving an order.

"Hurry."

You could offer help in a way like this, too. It was a method I was hearing for the very first time.

Anyway, I hadn't planned on entrusting hard labor to a fragile human.

It would be fine just to use the position of a Sponsor candidate holding a Number Tag.

*'Cecilia's face is known, so CEO Cha will be more useful.'*

Just as I was about to readily accept him, the Young god pushed past me and popped out.

"Even so, i won't make you a Sponsor or a subordinate."

He went out of his way to talk like that to someone offering to help.

I tried my best to restrain the Young god, whose social skills were shattered.

While I wrestled back and forth with the Young god, CEO Cha stared intently at the Young god and said.

"I'm not interested in you."

"It's a lie… no, wait…. I'm sorry…."

I swallowed a sigh and asked.

"I keep being in this state, would it really be alright for you to come along with me?"

"Deal with it quickly."

CEO Cha said to me, crossing his arms.

“You have to give it what it wants; that’s the only way it’ll calm down, right?”

Referring to the young god as “it” caused another uproar inside me, but I managed to quell it somehow.

I gratefully accepted CEO Cha's help.

"Then I will count on you."

Having gained an unexpected helper, I calmly organized the current situation once again.

"I plan to search for my subordinate while proceeding with the reporting."

However, Black Block wouldn't have made it easy to find them.

*'As I approach Black Block, it will be revealed.'*

Since he was hoping for me to draw near, the probability of that was high.

I had a gut feeling that he had designed it so I would run into my subordinate on the way to finding him.

I fidgeted with the collar equipped with a ultra-miniature camera.

COF's reporting required digging into the truth.

At first, they sold manufactured points of interest, even if forged.

However, the higher the stringer climbed into the upper tiers of the city, the closer they got to the truth, and they came to aim for publishing a "real scoop" in the media.

Succeeding in reporting on Hyper Eden's biggest mystery, the Mad Messiah—meaning Black Block—did not mean anything simple at all.

It wasn't just about selling his photos.

It was akin to meaning that I would find out what kind of existence he was.

Then where would Black Block be right now?

Now seemed like the exact time to use the item purchased with 10 Star Fragments.

When I was trapped in a black space for 72 hours, I had frequented nothing but the Galactic Trading Post to kill time.

As a result of examining them until my eyes fell out because the recommended items weren't up to snuff.

I came to know what value 10 Star Fragments held at the Galactic Trading Post.

It meant that only trash could be bought.

The items recommended by the Trading Post System were the best ones, but even those weren't all that great.

The most absurd item on the recommended list was a sticker to put on a spaceship.

It was a space-version of a "Baby on Board" sticker.

However, not only did I not have a spaceship, but I also doubted whether other ships would actually practice safe navigation just because I slapped this on.

Anyway, after much deliberation, I finally picked an item from the recommended list.

I chose it thinking it was at least the best of the bunch, and it turned out to be the most useful item for me right now.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">

    <strong>Acorn Compass Detector: The needle spins in circles and stops in the direction of your friend. You can escape the tagger in a game of hide-and-seek.</strong>

It was an item used for a game of hide-and-seek.

*'It was even on discount.'*

It was originally a Star Fragment item worth 100, but I bought it after getting a 90% children's special discount.

If you designate a tagger, it tells you the direction where that person is, but since it was simply a toy item, various restrictions were attached.

First of all, the effective radius was 15 meters. It could only search an area roughly the size of a basketball court.

Also, because it was a single-use item, it could only be used once, and crucially, the target that could be designated as the tagger was limited to a "god".

Because of the strict conditions, it was bound to be awkward to use anywhere.

It was undoubtedly put on a 90% discount for inventory clearance because it wasn't selling at all.

When I took out the compass, a System Window popped up.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Please register the tagger!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

*'Come to think of it, I still don't know his name.'*

The invitation had 'Mad Messiah' written on it, but that didn't seem to be the Black Block's name.

While I was pondering what to do while thinking of the Black Block, the tagger was registered on its own.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Tagger: ■■ ■■■</strong>
  </div>
</div>

It was the familiar arrangement of the Black Block. For now, it seemed like the registration went through properly according to my intent.

CEO Cha looked a bit bewildered when he saw the acorn compass I pulled out.

"...You're using something like this?"

I explained the usefulness of the cute compass.

"If the target approaches within 15 meters, we can figure out their direction."

I placed the compass on my palm and carefully watched the needle.

The needle hasn't stirred at all yet. Since it had to fall within the radius for the needle to move, it didn't seem to be around here.

First, I dropped by the smoking room, which was my original goal, but there were no memos left by the subordinates.

*'Should I approach the banquet hall side?'*

This was right when I was feeling a bit worried because of what happened with the Eyeball Alien.

"Follow me."

I looked at CEO Cha while holding the acorn compass.

"I have a terrace seat assigned to me."

His supplementary explanation made me even more flustered. To think they gave CEO Cha a terrace seat that would usually only go to an alien VIP.

Come to think of it, I hadn't heard how he managed to get a number tag yet....

Anyway, the terrace seat was not only the best place to survey the banquet hall at a glance, but it also made it easy to check the second-floor area.

I headed to the terrace seat in the banquet hall together with CEO Cha.

While obediently following behind him while pretending to be the executive secretary of the No. 6 alien guest, conversations of aliens flew in from here and there.

There were languages I didn't know, but most of them were understood.

There was a remark that pierced my ears while I was quietly moving my steps.

"Is it true? There's a story that He has returned...."

I almost turned my head without realizing it and barely managed to fix my gaze on CEO Cha's back.

Instead, I slowed my pace just a little bit. CEO Cha adjusted to it on his own without me even saying anything.

Glancing sideways, aliens dressed up like gentlemen were having a serious conversation.

They had human-like forms, but their flashy hair and the look in their eyes made them unmistakably aliens.

"I don't know either. There are no witnesses who saw it firsthand, and only rumors are rampant. When I looked into it privately myself, it seems to be because of the butlers."

"The butlers? I only heard the news that they smashed some cleaning company's spaceship the other day."

"Well, that's already old news. The butlers recently conquered a new territory."

"This can't be. Aren't we in danger too?"

“No, it isn’t. It’s a completely random place. You’d be surprised if you heard the coordinates. Why on earth would a place like that….”

The murmuring aliens abruptly stopped talking.

As they turned toward my direction, I quickly pretended not to notice, and CEO Cha shielded me from their view.

Fortunately, we were right in front of the stairs leading up to the terrace seats, so we quickly slipped out of the aliens' line of sight.

The second-floor terrace seats were accessed via a separate staircase rather than through the banquet hall.

A security Android scanned CEO Cha's Number Tag and guided us to the terrace seats.

As soon as I came up to the second floor, I checked the acorn compass, but the needle was still quiet.

And upon entering the terrace seats, I immediately looked down at the floor below.

“…….”

My face turned somewhat weary.

While I was away, screens had popped up all over the banquet hall.

The screens were all playing videos featuring the Young god. Some of them were even videos without the golden eyes.

There was a great variety of content, ranging from personal broadcast videos to footage of me conquering trials.

I winced for a moment and then scanned every corner of the banquet hall.

Still, at this rate, it was going to be difficult to find the Black Block.

I needed to move around and search for it myself, but there were simply too many dangerous aliens for that, and….

Just as I was wondering if there was a way to clear these damn aliens out all at once,

I came up with a rather decent idea.

“CEO Cha.”

And so I made a request to the person who would brilliantly execute my idea.

“Could you play the role of the Young god for a moment?”
