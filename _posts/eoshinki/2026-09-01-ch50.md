<br>Dominic looked slightly puzzled, perhaps because I had greeted him with such unusual enthusiasm.

Instead of landing on the dirty street, he touched down softly on my shoulder. "What is it, Roasted Chestnut?"

*'I can probably ask him to help me with this.'*

I knew the Creator's promised sequel wouldn't be an adult-themed game, but I still wanted to check the description. After all, it featured elements I'd never seen in the game before.

I explained the situation to Dominic.

Honestly, it was ridiculous for someone my age to need age verification.

Dominic, however, hummed in disapproval. "There is no need for you to experience such provocative and low-quality content."

"I just want to read the description," I insisted.

And I truly meant it.

I had already discussed the topic with the Special Team, so I couldn't just pick a new one out of the blue.

Even after hearing my explanation, Dominic still seemed reluctant, but he eventually agreed to verify my identity.

The verification process was simple.

As Dominic stared at the System Window, a glitch flickered across the prompt requesting adult verification.

Immediately after, a window confirming completion appeared.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    V-V-Verification C-C-Completed!
</div>

*'It's not going to break, is it?'*

With a hint of anxiety, I tapped the Adult button.

Finally, the description window appeared.


<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <i>“The public is hypocritical. They preach justice with their lips, but their fingers click on lewd and scandalous headlines.”</i><br>

Adult is the fastest and most reliable reporting field for capturing the public's attention in Hyper Eden.<br>

Primal and sensational articles guarantee overwhelming attention, regardless of their depth.<br>

A single scoop can propel even a bottom-tier stringer to the top of the media world overnight.<br>

- <strong>Primary Targets:</strong> Start with ordinary people. Then, escalate to scandals involving unknown celebrities, public figures, and VIPs from politics and business.<br>

- <strong>Investigation Style:</strong> Focus on stealth, eavesdropping, super-telephoto paparazzi shots, and infiltrating VIP rooms rather than physical confrontation. You must hold your breath and wait for the perfect moment to capture that one decisive photo.<br>

- <strong>Risk Factors:</strong> While stimulating the public's basest instincts ensures the fastest rise in the media world, you also risk becoming a target for the powerful whose secrets you've exposed, potentially turning you into a fugitive hunted across the entire city.<br>

<i>After all, the next scandal's star might just be you!</i>
  </div>
</div>


In short, they wanted me to uncover sex scandals and sell them as stories.

The pros and cons were clear, but for me, the downside was too severe. In Hyper Eden, opposing those in power meant death.

I cleanly gave up on any lingering attachment to the Adult topic.

*'But why did this suddenly appear?'*

Even the last Clear report from a Civilian Qualified person mentioned nothing about a new topic. It seemed something had indeed changed in COF.

I chose the topic I had originally promised the Special Team.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Selecting ‘Mystery.’</strong>
  </div>
</div>

This topic involved covering strange phenomena and bizarre incidents within the city that defied common sense. I chose Mystery because its reporting method was the most intuitive and controllable.

Also, while it garnered less public interest than sports or crime, there was almost no competition, making it possible to secure exclusive stories.

*'Because it's dangerous.'*

Covering paranormal phenomena meant I'd often be dealing with monsters.

It was a subject that could get you killed in an instant.

In Hyper Eden, reviving after death required a massive sum of money.

It was an amount my shallow pockets could never afford.

Naturally, Hyper Eden's stringers gravitated toward subjects with a lower risk of death.

But as long as I had a modicum of skill, there was no chance of dying while covering mysteries.

Crucially, sports and crime could trigger events involving powerful figures right from the start if you were unlucky.

Since that could ruin me completely before I could even try to stop it, I chose mysteries.

Actually, there were other reasons too, but...

I checked the Main Quest window that had appeared in my field of vision.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>‘Han Goyo (Little Lamb)’ has entered &lt;City of Frame>!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>◆ &lt;City of Frame> Main Quest: Become the most famous Stringer in Hyper Eden and uncover the truth of the city.<br>

The beings of Akasha are watching the Young god.<br>

Amidst their growing interest, you must prove yourself by achieving the True Ending. You must show that you are a being whom as the master of Planet Earth can never be treated lightly, and that you possess the power to reject their grasp.<br>

Quest Success: Survival!<br>

Quest Failure...</strong>
  </div>
</div>

In place of the word "failure," an ellipsis was printed.

I waited for a moment, but no letters appeared, and the ellipsis only continued to lengthen.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Fail...ure........................</strong>
  </div>
</div>

Just as I was watching the ellipsis stretch on, a new System Window suddenly popped up with a *thud*.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You must not fail.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I'd expected a drop in fame or an explosion of the Trial, but the message was completely different.

As I stared at it in confusion, the System Window sent another message.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You resolve to never fail this quest.<br>

For if you do, a terrible future awaits you.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You swear to succeed at all costs. <span style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">ABSOLUTELY!!!!!!!!!</span></strong>
  </div>
</div>

Of course, I planned to shut down the Trial since it was about to explode. I'd already steeled myself for the worst, as this was the Trial Sa Gongyeon had wanted to enter.

But this was the first time the System Window had issued such a dire warning.

There must be something in Hyper Eden.

*'By the way, why have you been so quiet lately?'*

I tried to ask about the meteorite, but as expected, the System remained silent.

I thought about picking a fight with it just to get a response, but I decided against it.

Closing the quest window, I asked Pudding Slime, "Did you choose your theme too, Mr. Dominic?"

"Hmm, you chose Adult."

He had used the auto-select feature, and it had landed on Adult.

Overjoyed, I asked him to let me know what quests appeared later. Then, I stowed the voice recorder I had been holding into my inventory.

In COF, initial reporting is done using a voice recorder. My goal was to get accepted by late-night radio. As I secured bigger scoops, I would upgrade my equipment, move on to newspapers, and eventually break into television.

Soon, the first quest appeared.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>◆Main Quest: Find the Base Using the Map<br>

Find the Old Apartment, the Stringer's base, using the map.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

COF featured a map function.

This was a basic quest to get used to using the map.

I checked the map for a moment.

Hyper Eden was divided into districts centered around a tower in the middle of the city.

Broke and poor Stringers ended up basing themselves in the slums.

Checking the map, I saw that the base wasn't too far from my current location.

I had agreed to meet the Special Team at the base.

Estimating it would take about 15 minutes, I started walking and caught a glimpse of my reflection in a shattered window.

COF's default outfit—an oversized hooded jacket—featured neon-colored lines. The color changed randomly with each playthrough, and this time it was a fluorescent sky blue. It looked quite good, matching the Pudding Slime perched on my shoulder.

*'Can I not put a hooded jacket on the slime?'*

I figured it would be difficult since it didn't have arms.

I was pondering what kind of cyberpunk accessories would suit Pudding Slime.

"......"

A sudden, ominous feeling washed over me.

Passersby kept staring.

It wasn't just casual glances. More than a few were glaring at me, their eyes wide and blatant.

*'No way.'*

A terrible thought crossed my mind.

Are they actually...?

Please, let it not be true.

But as usual, my worst guess was always right.

The Pudding Slime on my shoulder had also noticed something was off.

"They're after Roasted Chestnut," it said. "You'd better be careful."

I swallowed hard and gradually picked up my pace.

As I sped up, the people around me began to close in.

I swallowed a curse and took a deep breath.

Then I bolted.

"He's running!"

"Get him!"

"Film it first!"

Flashes burst from the crowd that swarmed around me like ants.

I ran frantically, but it seemed I would be surrounded soon enough. I desperately scanned my surroundings, but there was nowhere to hide.

My flustered expression as I was cornered was captured repeatedly on camera.

Just as I was running like mad,

"...!"

I spotted a woman.

A stunning Latina beauty stood by the roadside, yawning in boredom. She seemed to be waiting for someone, and her towering height—nearly 2 meters—made her stand out.

She was even more conspicuous in her all-white outfit: a cropped top, a hooded jacket, and tight-fitting shorts.

She ran a hand through her long, dark, wavy hair that cascaded over her healthy tan skin, then turned her gaze indifferently toward the commotion.

Her black eyes locked onto mine.

In that instant, her eyes widened.

A shiver coursed through my entire body.

The woman reached out to me, and I ran toward her with all my might.

The moment our hands met—

A spark flew, and she and I vanished from the street.

We reappeared in an alley more than a kilometer away from where we had been.

As soon as she let go of my hand, she immediately pulled me into an embrace.

"Roasted Chestnut!"

I wrapped my arms around her in return and cried out, "Noona!"

The woman was Cecilia, the main NPC of COF.

Cecilia acted as an information broker, charging a small fee to sell stories gathered by Stringers to various media outlets. She also sold them rumors worth investigating.

Throughout the game, she was a reliable ally who supported players from the sidelines, even using her teleportation ability to rescue them from desperate situations. I, too, had received much help from her during my own playthrough.

> "Roasted Chestnut, don't stop. Keep climbing. You will reach the summit of Hyper Eden."

Cecilia was deeply intertwined with the player's journey. In fact, there were sections of the game that could only be cleared with her help. After playing COF, it was impossible not to fall in love with her.

Cecilia's final farewell to me was still vivid in my mind.

The fact that she remembered me filled me with unbearable joy. It was a welcome sight, especially since I was in such a desperate situation.

Cecilia cupped my cheeks in her hands and asked, "My Castañita! What on earth has happened? You promised you'd never come back here again."

Hearing my old nickname, "Castañita," after so long made me blush with embarrassment. But she was the one person who could be my strongest ally in my current predicament.

"Something came up... By the way, Cecilia. Is this Day News?"

I asked abruptly, but she understood my meaning immediately.

She nodded gravely.

My world went dark.

I had my suspicions, given that Sa Gongyeon had thrown me into this, and the Trial's setting was Media City. But to find Day News already spreading throughout COF...

I'd let my guard down because no news agency with that name existed when I played the game.

What's more, Cecilia, an NPC, even remembered me.

A single conclusion immediately presented itself.

*'COF is a Trial where a god exists.'*

But unlike my experiences with Orca or the Security Chief, Cecilia recognized me right away. This meant either she was incredibly powerful, like the Butlers of Clean3, or that the god's influence here was substantial.

Since the former was out of the question, it had to be the latter.

*'Does that mean the god is nearby?'*

In The Cell X, ■■■ wasn't present from the very beginning. He had gradually drawn closer as I progressed through the walkthrough, finally revealing himself at the very end.

*'In Edel's case, Dominic had always seen himself as an NPC.'*

He had only realized his true existence at the very end, so there would be a difference.

I glanced at the Tower.

The Tower, soaring high enough to touch the sky, was visible from anywhere in Hyper Eden.

"Roasted Chestnut."

Lost in thought, I met Cecilia's eyes.

Her pupils were trembling slightly.

"Is what they said in that newspaper true?"

The "Young god" in the Day News must have been a completely different being from the "Roasted Chestnut" she knew.

I gave Cecilia a troubled smile.

"It just... happened that way."

"..."

"I'm sorry, Noona. I changed suddenly myself..."

I hesitated before adding, "I wasn't lying to you."

"I know. You'd never lie to me."

Cecilia's unwavering trust brought a faint smile to my lips.

She narrowed her eyes and asked, "But Roasted Chestnut... If you're a god, shouldn't you be... different?"

As an information broker, Cecilia knew far more than the general public. My current state, so starkly different from the knowledge she possessed, seemed to have left her bewildered.

"Well... I still don't know how to do much," I replied sheepishly.

"Don't tell me you came back to Hyper Eden because you ran out of money?"

"Something like that," I murmured, my voice barely audible.

Cecilia sighed, her gaze filled with pity. "Were you really so desperate that you had to start working as a Stringer again? But you know this city is..."

"Noona."

I looked up at Cecilia and pleaded, "Help me."

"Roasted Chestnut, you really—!"

Cecilia started to shout, then abruptly pulled a portable pulse gun from her coat. Without even glancing sideways, she pulled the trigger.

*Zzzzt!*

A beam of light shot out, followed by a heavy* thud*.

Following the beam's trajectory, I saw a man with a camera lying face-down, a hole blasted through his forehead.

Cecilia let out a soft sigh and tucked the pulse gun back into her coat. "I'm not mad at you. I'm just worried because you're in danger."

Then, as a professional information broker from Hyper Eden, she got straight to the most important question.

"So, what did you choose this time?"

She wanted to know first, as the nature of the mission would change drastically depending on the topic.

"Mystery."

"But that's dangerous!"

"I have people to help me."

Just as I was about to explain the Special Team, Cecilia's expression suddenly hardened, and she pulled me into a tight embrace.

A short-range teleportation followed.

Immediately after moving just about a meter, black metal feathers thudded into the spot where she had been standing.

Cecilia drew her pulse gun, her face a mask of stoicism.

Black wings filled the narrow slice of sky above the alleyway.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Lieutenant Colonel, wait! Just a second!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I sent the message at super-speed and called out to Cecilia.

"Sister! Wait!"

Cecilia, her finger already on the trigger, shot me a sharp glare.

I spoke hastily before she could pull it.

"He’s my—!"

"My?"

I choked on the word. Her icy tone sent a cold sweat running down my back.

In my joy at seeing her again, I had momentarily forgotten a crucial fact:

Cecilia was... incredibly jealous.

She hated it when I mingled with other Stringers. Calling him a "colleague" would only make things worse.

Considering I'd need her help in the future, I squeezed my eyes shut and shouted,

"My precious... younger brother...!"
