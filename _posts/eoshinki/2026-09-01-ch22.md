A massive golden roulette wheel materialized before me. It was the same one I had seen before, but its appearance had changed.

The golden wheel was now engulfed in roaring flames.

I watched the spinning wheel with a frown. 

*'What a useless, tedious process. I'll get what I want anyway.'*

As if reading my thoughts, the rapidly spinning roulette was consumed by the fire. The crimson flames devoured it completely, and I obtained what I desired.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    You have acquired the skill: 'Punishment'!
</div>

I reached my hand toward the sky.

*"Punishment."*

A golden beam shot forth, striking the eyes that filled the heavens. The light, soaring like a star, sparkled brilliantly—but it still wasn't enough for me.

I knew.

I knew I could shine much brighter than this.

I wanted to create a star that burned larger and more brilliant. I wanted *my subordinates* to see what I truly was.

Just as I was about to manifest the star again, something urgently blocked me.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Warning! Incomplete Body.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

Another square window appeared as I tried to channel my power once more.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You are attempting to cool your heat and regain your calm reason.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I was just thinking how annoying this interference was when my subordinate placed me down on a floating platform.

I wondered why we had descended to the ground when the eyes of the sky still numbered in the countless thousands.

I tried to understand my subordinate's actions.

After speaking with someone, a white-haired man approached me.

*'What is this?'*

I watched him silently, wondering if he intended to harm my subordinate.

He didn't seem to have any intention of causing harm, but...

I grew a little impatient.

*'I have a target to tear apart right above my head, yet I have to wait?'*

I told my subordinate to hurry up and kill that thing.

My subordinate looked down at me and smiled faintly.

"Just the two of us?"

"Just the two of us."

"It would be better if there were more."

I wasn't particularly keen on the suggestion to share the fun with others. But just as I was hesitating over my subordinate's words, a blue square window appeared.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You decide to yield the fun and exciting task to your subordinate.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I couldn't ignore it since it mentioned my subordinate, so I ended up reading it. The window, having captured my attention, immediately filled my vision.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Because giving your subordinate the opportunity to showcase their abilities is also an important part of being a master.</strong><br>
    <strong>This is a basic quality a master who commands subordinates must possess.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

*'This... seemed to be true.'*

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You resolved never to use 'Punishment'.</strong><br>
    <strong>Instead, you decided to help your subordinate show off its talents before its master.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I wanted to be a good master, and I also wanted to see my subordinate flaunt its abilities.

Since my subordinate had just asked me to use other things, I decided to follow its request.

However, this was the Domain of ■ ■ ■, so to use other things, I had to make them mine for a moment.

This was something I hadn't obtained permission for from my fast-asleep subordinate.

I was a little worried it might get jealous, but there was no other choice.

I thought I should get it over with before it woke up.

I scanned the Neon Green Sea and chose the things I could use.

Then I *commanded* them to follow me.

I saw my command floating above them.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Event Quest: Follow the Young god.
</div>

The white-haired man standing right in front of me slowly shifted his gaze to look at it.

Since he was just staring without choosing an option, I forced him to accept.

With the sound of a fanfare, the white-haired man became my temporary possession.

I thought he would feel at ease now that he was free from ■ ■ ■'s influence, but for some reason, he seemed even more unstable.

This was the exact opposite of the gray-brown man, who had found great stability after settling a short distance away.

"...Can I go back?"

The white-haired man muttered in a trembling voice.

"Can I... go back from this?"

He looked at me as if seeking an answer.

Fear clung to his gaze. Ironically, it wasn't fear of me.

The man was searching for something in me.

I gave up trying to understand.

His condition was a bit strange, but it seemed it wouldn't be a problem for the battle.

*'This might take a little longer.'*

I had thought I'd burn it all down in one go, but if this was the case, it would take some time.

I decided to bring my sleeping subordinate to my side.

However, I didn't have a suitable ability, so I generated a new one.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    【Summon】 has been acquired! 
</div>

As soon as I obtained the new ability, the square window urgently popped up.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>T h a t's&nbsp;e n o u g h!!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

There was no reason to listen to the words of anything that wasn't a subordinate.

Enduring the Punishment alone had already been enough.

*Summon.*

Light shimmered in the air, and my sleeping subordinate appeared before me.

I gazed down at my subordinate.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Current Status: Lost in a dream of the deep past, where blue lightning strikes. Yearning desperately for the Young god.
</div>

Even in sleep, they thought only of their master.

*'My subordinate.'*

I smiled softly and wrapped them in a golden veil, shielding them from the eyes of the sky. One layer was not enough, so I wound the veil around them several times. I wrapped them so tightly in gold that not a single strand of red hair could be seen, then shrank their size.

I held them in my arms, now no larger than a ball, and gently patted them.

Here, they would sleep undisturbed.

*'Let's get this over with while they're asleep.'*

I finally tore my eyes away from the subordinate in my arms.

Then, I checked the list of temporary possessions.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Cha ■ ■<br>

Won Taebeom<br>

Orca<br>

Number 12<br>

Number 5<br>

Number 8....
</div>


Fourteen individuals in total.

As I scanned the list of temporary possessions, sorted by their stats from highest to lowest, I tilted my head.

There was one individual whose name was barely legible.

I looked at the White Man.

The man wearing the white mask had hair so pale it had a faint blue tint.

For a moment, curiosity piqued, and I considered peering inside. But I decided against it.

There was no reason to waste time on something like that.

I began utilizing my temporary possessions to create an environment where my subordinates could roam freely.

My first priority was to clear the filth floating in the sea. I issued a command to the twelve subordinates, excluding the two higher-ranking ones.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Event Quest: Clean the Sea of ■ ■ ■.
</div>

As I gave the order, the once-still Neon Green surface rippled.

One by one, the filth that had filled the sea began to vanish. Between the Neon Green waves where the debris was silently dragged under, I saw black fins with curved tips.

Then, a spiderweb as fine as a hair spread across the sea like a massive fishing net.

A spider, having gathered the filth and hauled it from the sea, sang as it devoured the waste atop the ruins.

A massive vine plant extended its stems in all directions, plucking the filth like a fishing line, while a small tuft of fur dispatched them with bursts of electricity.

A black shadow that lingered near them completely swallowed the remains, making the filth vanish as if it had never existed in the first place.

Their flawless cleaning was satisfying to behold. They seemed poised to complete the remaining tasks with equal skill.

I issued orders to the last two subordinates.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Event Quest: Assist the Followers of the Young god.
</div>

I specially bestowed the golden veil upon them, as they had to approach ■ ■ ■.

*Golden Veil.*

The two of them would no longer have their minds eroded even if they met those eyes.

As the fluttering light enveloped them, the two individuals reacted with confusion.

Only my subordinate, accustomed to the Golden Veil, smiled at me from within it.

I was quite pleased with the golden light scattered across the black metal.

However, the white-haired man repeatedly tried to reject the Golden Veil.

"Stay still."

"He said stay still, CEO Cha."

When my subordinate relayed my words, the white-haired man's resistance somehow grew even more fierce.

In the end, I imposed a restriction to force the veil upon him.

The white-haired man, now forced to wear the golden veil, glared at me and resisted again, so I imposed another restriction.

I bound the White Man and examined him.

*Status Window.*

I checked the individual abilities of the two. Unlike my subordinates, neither of them could fly, and both focused on melee attacks.

The White Man could perform some ranged attacks, but his true strength lay in his devastating melee strikes, with his ultimate skill being *Light Modulation*.

The Gray-brown Man relied heavily on his items, which granted a damage boost only after he had branded his target.

Still, that alone wasn't enough to make him a good target. He possessed a truly remarkable trait.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Trait: Fanatic.<br>

Mad faith numbs the mind and transcends the limits of the body.<br>

All abilities increase in proportion to faith.
</div>

The stronger the faith in the god one serves, the higher the attack power. But right now, he did not believe in any god.

Then I just had to create one.

*'He likes me anyway.'*

His Affection Level for me was already quite high.

Since he was also under my Golden Veil, albeit temporarily, he was the perfect candidate to revere me as his god.

I generated the necessary ability.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    【Blind Faith】 has been acquired!
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Stop.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Please.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I used it on the gray-brown man.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>NO!!!!!</strong>
  </div>
</div>

*Blind Faith.*

The skill's effect was simple: it just made him like me a little more.

Without a moment's struggle, the gray-brown man accepted me.

I summoned him before me and checked his status.

A golden light swirled in his hazy eyes.

The gaze he fixed on me was nothing short of complete blind faith.

It was a temporary state, lasting only as long as he obeyed my commands, and he wasn't even a subordinate...

Still, the sight of my light permeating him was quite pleasing, and I felt satisfied.

*'This should be enough. The preparations are complete.'*

I looked up at the sky. It was time to bring *that* down to earth for my possessions.

The eyes cast their ravenous gazes upon me.

I called out.

*Come here.*

*Come to me.*

As I whispered with feigned tenderness, it could not refuse my summons.

*Screeeech…*

Space twisted, and the eyes drew closer.

It was as if the sky itself were descending.

The closer the eyes came, the stronger their power grew.

They writhed, trying to penetrate my mind, but they could not affect my subordinates in the slightest.

I whispered to the eyes once more.

*Closer.*

As it gradually approached, my subordinate let out a metallic *clack*.

Before I issued the full command to engage, I felt a slight sense of emptiness.

Soon, I realized what was missing.

There was something I couldn't leave out of any fun and exciting event.

Following my will, a faint drone sound began to fill the space. A dreamy voice, like an auditory hallucination, tickled my ears over a low beat set against a backdrop of dark and cold synthesizer pad sounds.

I smiled slightly and intuitively adjusted my vision.

A window displaying the health and available skills of my subordinate and possessions floated before me.

Checking the skill cooldowns and stacks, I spoke to my subordinate.

"Don't get hurt."

In response, a low chuckle returned to me, as if he had heard something amusing.

My subordinate then let go of my wrist, which he had been holding until that moment.

Black crescent moons began to form in his grasp.

The music's tempo accelerated sharply, its aggressive bassline crushing the space. A sharp, synthetic sound, like the grinding of machinery and metal, accompanied the drum beat as it tightened its grip.

I waited.

For him to draw a little closer...

For the perfect moment.

At the exact instant the distorted electric guitar riff exploded.

I *commanded.*

My message struck directly into the minds of my subordinates and my possessions.

A waterfall of intense electronic sounds, layered with a grand orchestra, cascaded down.

The first to attack was my subordinate.

Spreading his metallic wings, he took flight and scattered black crescent moons.

Hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of black crescent moons rained down upon the eyeball.

From within the black crescent moons, my subordinate surged forward, closing the distance to the eyeball. It struck the surface with its metallic hand, attempting to scratch it.

Yet, the eyeball remained completely unscathed. Only the screech of metal scraping metal echoed through the air. The black crescent moons themselves shattered upon impact, unable to pierce the surface.

I narrowed my eyes.

My first, tentative attack had been completely blocked. It seemed a direct assault would be useless after all.

"■ ■ ■ ■ ■!"

The enraged ■ ■ ■ let out a roar.

I paid it no mind, having no desire to understand its meaning.

At the ■ ■ ■'s cry, the filth from the sea wailed in terror. But their cries were drowned out by my temporary possessions, which were already tearing them all to shreds.

But the filth weren't the only ones to react to the noise.

A whirlpool formed at my feet.

In the blink of an eye, a pillar of Neon Green water surged up and trapped me. It was a pillar of water that plunged its captive into an eternal nightmare.

But it was a futile gesture.

How could something like this possibly plunge *me* into a nightmare?

I laughed aloud within the Neon Green pillar of water. Bubbles scattered with my laughter.

My subordinates and temporary possessions, caught in the pillar of water under my influence, fared no differently. The moment it realized it couldn't inflict any damage, the pillar of water vanished.

Immediately after, a giant tentacle began to plunge from the sky at an oblique angle.

The tentacle plunged into the sea like a heavy anchor, crushing the building's debris and sending high waves surging. As the raging waters swept in, the floating debris beneath my feet swayed violently.

I stabilized the space around me to prevent it from shaking and commanded the White Man and the Gray-brown Man.

The Gray-brown Man leaped onto the tentacle. He charged up it, treating the massive, mucous-covered limb like a bridge, showing no fear or hesitation.

My Fanatic swung his gauntlet toward the eyes of the sky.

A golden mark was seared onto the eye.

*'The mark was applied perfectly.'*

I tried a simple strike, but as expected, it dealt no effective damage.

It was only natural that a human could not reach a god.

However, the fact that my subordinates and possessions under my influence were unable to launch any attacks was a problem.

To identify the cause of the issue, I issued a command to the White Man.

But the White Man did not move.

I had ordered him to move together with the Gray-brown Man earlier, and he had completely ignored my commands from then until now.

*'His condition seemed strange. Was he broken? He wasn't disabled to the point of being unable to fight.'*

I used an ability I had been holding back, worried it would be a waste of power.

*Blind Faith.*

I activated the ability, but the blue square window did not appear again.

I waited for the golden glow to settle over the White Man's eyes.

But there was no reaction. Far from Blind Faith, the White Man remained as defiant as ever.

*'Is it because of his Disbeliever Trait?'*

I thought his higher Affection Level compared to the Gray-brown Man would allow me to ignore the trait.

Just as I was about to examine the White Man again—

"Do you want to order me around?"

I looked up.

The White Man, drenched in neon green liquid, was furious.

He brandished his snow-white sword and spoke roughly.

"Then do it while you're sane, Han Goyo."

Before I could even react to the sheer insolence of a mere Possession, the White Man charged forward.

As he swung his sword, he whispered like a passing breeze.

"Light Modulation."

And the world was overturned.
