<br>I froze, clutching my phone.

Only a few seconds had passed.

A glitch flickered across the screen, and the messages began to vanish. They disappeared in a blur, and before I knew it, the entire chat room with Reporter Sa Gongyeon was gone.

Staring at the empty screen, I hastily pulled out the phone Samra had given me.

I was about to ask him if he knew Reporter Sa Gongyeon from Day News, but I stopped.

There was no way Samra hadn't searched my phone. He had already dug up all my information the moment I was first dragged to the Era Agency.

If he had seen the messages from Reporter Sa Gongyeon, he would have definitely investigated it.

The fact that he hadn't mentioned a word about it meant Samra was completely unaware of them.

Virtually all electronic data on Earth belonged to Samra.

Missing a message transmitted through the network was an absurdity.

It was highly likely that this was an issue involving an Outer god, much like the Creator Samra was currently tracking.

*'Is Reporter Sa Gongyeon from Day News also a being connected to the Outer gods?'*

Given the mention of the title "Little Lamb," there was no other explanation.

A chill ran down my spine.

The timing of my retirement coincided with the Meteorites of Trial falling to Earth.

Yet Reporter Sa Gongyeon had been contacting me immediately after I retired.

The probability was high that he had known about me even before then.

That meant an Outer god had been watching Earth—and me—before the Meteorites of Trial ever fell.

Just how long had it been?

And why me?

My mind was a tangled mess of confusion.

*'I need to tell Samra about this first.'*

He might be able to help track down the Creator.

I steadied my ragged breathing.

The information was a lot to process, but I wasn't as shocked as I expected to be. Je Hyun-oh's declaration—"I'm not infected"—had acted like a preemptive shot. Thanks to that, my mind was functioning rationally.

Suddenly, the name ***Day News*** felt familiar.

The newspaper the butlers in Clean3 had shown me, and the one I'd found in CallOVo, were both called *Day News*. In CallOVo, Mr. Black Block had helped me through the *Day News *lifestyle section.

*'Now that I think about it, when did Mr. Black Block start watching me?'*

It must have been since DeZonDeal shut down.

Come to think of it, this was strange too.

How did he even notice my existence and start watching me? And he even called himself a fan every time he contacted me.

It felt like this was connected to the current situation.

*'Should I try calling him through Interstellar Communication?'*

After all, he said I could use a collect call.

But I soon abandoned the thought.

Contacting him might just give him the wrong idea and make him hopeful about a sponsorship contract.

It was best to avoid dealing with a pervert like that as much as possible.

In any case, I shouldn't waste any more of Je Hyun-oh's time, which was more precious than gold.

After taking a deep breath, I started packing my things.

I packed a few changes of clothes and my old phone into a large sports duffel bag. Even after adding smaller items like my stamp, bankbook, ID, and cards, the bag still had plenty of room.

There was nothing else to pack, so I should have been ready to visit CEO Cha.

But I... I kept hesitating.

I couldn't bring myself to leave, as if I were bound by invisible ropes. I paced aimlessly around the bedroom before finally heading to the living room.

Je Hyun-oh happened to be standing in front of the display cabinet, so I took the opportunity to ask for his help.

"Could you help me for a moment? I'd like to take this cloth off."

Since he had human hands, he was in the perfect condition to assist me.

With Je Hyun-oh's help, I removed the cloth that had been covering the display cabinet.

And for the first time in four years, I faced the glass display case.

The massive cabinet, stretching from floor to ceiling, held gifts and letters from fans I'd received during my playing days.

I took in each item—drawings, plushies, keychains, photo books. A faint smile touched my lips as I looked at a plushie of my signature Champion and a photo of my childhood face photoshopped onto a pro jersey.

Everyone else had probably forgotten, yet here I was, the only one still clinging to these memories. But I couldn't even face them openly; I'd been too scared, so I'd kept the cabinet covered with a cloth.

I'd vowed to finally clear it all out this time... but I knew I couldn't. I wanted to be the one to remember.

*'Can I take all of this with me?'*

I had no idea if my new dorm would have enough space. If it was anything like my old room in the Quarantine Facility, this would never fit.

If I couldn't fit it all in the new place, I'd have to leave it here and keep paying rent.

As I searched the display cabinet, I spotted something tucked away in the bottom corner.

It was the championship ring from my last competition. I had thrown away all my trophies and plaques, but I'd kept this one after a long struggle.

Now, I felt I could finally let it go.

I opened the cabinet and took out the ring. Its weight seemed to drag my hand down. After holding it for a moment, I tossed the entire box into the trash.

I felt Je Hyun-oh's gaze fixed on me, but I ignored it and slung my duffel bag over my shoulder.

"I think we can go now."

I turned to leave, but stopped just as I reached for the front door handle.

I stood frozen before the exit.

Long fingers gripped the back of my neck.

I flinched at the touch, which tightened slightly, as if he were carefully controlling his strength.

I looked up at the owner of the hand. A pair of dark violet eyes stared back at me.

Only then did I realize my breathing was ragged. My body was trembling slightly, too.

I pressed my hand firmly against my chest and focused on steadying my breath. Once I calmed down, I finally noticed the gaze observing me.

His eyes seemed to be weighing something.

I must have made a mistake.

Je Hyun-oh wanted a strong being to enter &lt;Zero> with him. Recalling his preference for a Young god, I forced myself to speak as calmly as possible.

"...I forgot something. I'll be right back after I grab it."

I returned to the bedroom, put on a baseball cap and a mask, and then came back out.

The obstructed view brought a familiar sense of security.

Finally, I could go outside.

It seemed that spending so much time at the Era Agency had truly cost me my sense of reality.

Stepping into my studio apartment felt like waking from a dream.

I adjusted the brim of my hat, wishing I could hurry back to the Era Agency.

Closing the front door, I led the way down the hallway and asked, "Lieutenant Colonel, can we walk to Paseong? I have some things to buy on the way."

Mok Saehwa had sent me the location in advance. The excuse was a hospital visit, but the destination was, of course, Paseong's headquarters.

It wasn't far from where I lived.

It would take about twenty minutes on foot, so I suggested walking, partly to buy a get-well gift.

I meant for us to leave the car behind.

The car left in front of the studio building would likely be retrieved by the Era Agency if I just asked them to.

I wanted to take a taxi back.

Just as I was about to turn around when Je Hyun-oh didn't answer, my bag strap was yanked.

Since I was wearing it diagonally across my body, I was pulled right along with it.

I stumbled back and looked at Je Hyun-oh in confusion.

"Goyo."

"Yes, sir."

"Can you walk?"

"Huh? Of course."

Je Hyun-oh swept his gaze over me. I stammered, bewildered.

"I can walk...?"

His eyes traced my hat, then my mask, before returning to my eyes.

"Right, then."

He let go of my bag strap with a soft thud and said, "Walk."

"Yes, sir," I replied, quickly moving my feet before he could change his mind.

As I walked down the street, I suddenly realized how long it had been since I'd walked like this.

Before, I had only gone back and forth between my studio apartment and the nearby convenience store.

After joining the Era Agency, I had only moved between the dormitory and the Trial.

I glanced at him, thinking he might be in a similar boat.

He seemed somehow more on edge than usual.

Nothing obvious showed on his face, but I'd grown so used to reading his moods that I just felt it.

*'Is he annoyed because of the crowd?'*

I kept an eye on Je Hyun-oh as I hurried along, quickly picking out a get-well gift.

Once I had it, Je Hyun-oh—who had been silent the whole time—finally spoke.

"You're buying that?"

"Yes. I think this will be fine."

It wasn't the most typical get-well gift, but CEO Cha would still like it. Je Hyun-oh seemed to approve of my choice, too.

With the large box tucked under my arm, we headed back toward Paseong. At a cafe near the area, I bought coffee and desserts for Mok Saehwa and Seo Munchan. I casually asked Je Hyun-oh if he wanted a drink, but he just gave me that same inscrutable smile again.

While I was waiting after making the payment, my phone buzzed.

*'Samra said he'd block spam messages...'*

Curious, I turned on the screen, only to have my eyes widen.

—I'll be right out. I'm sending Director Seo to get you. Please wait in the lobby!

I went inside the building, took off my hat and mask, and stuffed them into my duffel bag.

As I waited, glancing around the lobby, a luxurious sedan pulled up in front of the Paseong building.

Through the glass doors, I happened to look over and saw some unexpected visitors.

A woman with mid-length hair and a young woman with a striking appearance stepped out of the sedan.

The moment my eyes met the woman entering the lobby, I hastily bowed.

"Hello, Chairman. It's been a long time."

It was Kang Cheonseo, the Chairman of Cheongang, and her granddaughter, Kang Byeokrin.

Kang Cheonseo, who had been speaking with someone who looked like a secretary, widened her eyes in surprise and exclaimed,

"Look who it is! If it isn't Goyo."

She approached me, her face beaming with a wide smile.

"It's been way too long, hasn't it? I was hurt, Goyo."

Kang Cheonseo stood before me and feigned a sigh.

"If I lent you *Yongmo Danjeong*, you should have come to return it in person. Instead, you just sent the item back without a word."

In the past, I had visited the Chairman of Cheongang's residence to request items for the Clean3 raid.

It was true that I was in the wrong, but so much had happened after Clean3. I simply hadn't found the time to visit Cheongang. Besides, the Era Agency wouldn't have allowed it anyway.

"I'm sorry. I should have visited you sooner..."

"It's fine, it's fine. I'll take care of Goyo. Instead, come visit us for a meal again sometime. Since I'm not even getting an allowance, I should at least feed you."

"But what's with the box you're clutching under your arm?"

At my words, Kang Cheonseo burst out laughing, and Kang Byeokrin also looked at me with startled eyes.

"Ah, I'm dying. Life will never be boring with Goyo around."

After laughing heartily, Kang Cheonseo shifted her gaze to the side.

She looked at Je Hyun-oh, who was just standing there expressionlessly, without even a greeting or a word.

I thought she'd say something like, 'Who's your stiff-backed friend?' But surprisingly, Kang Cheonseo said nothing.

She simply watched for a moment, as if intrigued.

"Rin."

Kang Cheonseo spoke to Kang Byeokrin, who stood frozen with a flushed face, a smirk playing on her lips.

"You should greet us. After all, we've run into Mr. Goyo so unexpectedly!"

"Was it truly a coincidence, Chairman Kang?"

A cool, mechanical voice cut in.

CEO Cha crossed the lobby with slow, measured strides.

I breathed a sigh of relief inwardly.

Though I knew it was unlikely, I had been worried, but he looked perfectly fine.

Without his headphones or the Daeil Yeorae, CEO Cha wore only his mask and a suit.

The custom-tailored suit fit him perfectly, with no flaws along his firm shoulder line.

The suit fit him so perfectly that not even a blade could slip between the fabric and his body. Yet, in stark contrast to the impeccable fit, his shirt was not buttoned to the top; he had left the top button undone.

It seemed he had chosen a suit over his usual training wear because he was meeting Kang Cheonseo. I had found it striking when I saw him in his Warden's uniform, but seeing him in a suit for the first time felt completely new.

Looking at CEO Cha, I was struck by a strange feeling. I didn't know his exact age, only guessing that he was older than me. After all, in the time I had known him, he had often acted like an adult—just as he was doing now.

Yet, there were rare moments when he seemed my age, or even younger.

*'He never told me his age, even in the game.'*

And yet, we had been so close.

Anyway, CEO Cha was unmistakably an adult today.

He fixed his gaze on me, staring for a long time. I got the feeling he was confirming that I was alive and breathing.

Then, he turned his eyes to Je Hyun-oh and openly showed his disbelief. He seemed to recognize Je Hyun-oh even in his human form.

Je Hyun-oh just stood there, too lazy to even react.

It was a strange sight—CEO Cha and Je Hyun-oh, both dressed completely differently from their usual attire, facing each other.

*'Since we're outside the Trial, they probably won't clash...'* I thought, but I still watched them with a sense of unease.

Fortunately, CEO Cha silently turned away, his gaze finally coming to rest on Kang Cheonseo.

"You're here way too early for our scheduled meeting."

"What? You don't like it?"

Kang Cheonseo looked up at CEO Cha and smiled brightly.

"After all, CEO Cha, you broke into my house last time."

...She's using a metaphor, right?

The metaphor was so violent that I tensed up a little, but Kang Cheonseo casually linked her arm with mine.

"Let's include Goyo in our meeting today. It's about time he learned the truth, don't you think?"

"Chairman Kang."

"Oh, my old legs are aching. CEO Cha, how much longer are you going to keep us out here?"

As she said this, she held me even tighter.

When CEO Cha still didn't respond, she just started talking to me as if he weren't there.

"Goyo. Have you heard of Geumcheon?"

Geumcheon was the most infamous criminal organization formed by Adaptees.

The gang had expanded globally, committing all sorts of crimes, including the smuggling of Trial Items.

They operated illegal underground financial schemes using Trial Items and Adaptees, engaged in lobbying and contract killings targeting political and business figures, and even trafficked Adaptees.

Their transnational criminal activities were so extensive that the Era Agency had to cooperate with Interpol.

"They're targeting us, Cheongang. I'm scared to death. I immediately asked CEO Cha for help, but then Rin got this strange message last night."

Out of the blue, a reporter had contacted her, offering information on Geumcheon.

"His name is Sa Gongyeon. I have no idea who he is. CEO Cha, do you know any Adaptee by that name?"

*'Sa Gongyeon...?'*

I flinched at the unexpected name.

"Not an Adaptee," CEO Cha said, looking at me with a nonchalant expression. "But I know the NPC."
