<br>No matter how deep one hides in the darkness, there is no place the light of Daeil Yeorae cannot reach.

Where the light falls, there is no place to hide, no place to flee.

The light that erases the darkness also caused even the shadows to vanish.

An overwhelming radiance blanketed the world, transforming all creation into light.

But the Light Modulation was no mere white glow.

It was a light that revealed the truth and slashed through illusions, forcibly exposing the enemy's true form and weaknesses.

The reason Je Hyun-oh and Won Taebeom's previous attacks had failed was simple.

The eyes that covered the sky were nothing but a falsehood.

As the Being of the Sky revealed its true essence, the eye beyond the mask keenly observed it.

CEO Cha ascended the sky, riding the tentacles that had been thrust downward.

And from the countless, innumerable eyes of falsehood, he found the truth.

It was the eye at the very center.

Unlike the other eyes with their circular pupils, this one alone possessed a slender, elongated horizontal pupil.

That one was real, not an illusion.

The light he had unleashed upon the world returned as a sword.

Devouring the darkness, the light grew even more immense as it converged upon Daeil Yeorae.

The blade vibrated faintly from the energy condensed to its absolute limit.

The hand gripping the hilt tightly swung the sword down.

A single strike.

A pure white trail was drawn along the sword's elegant arc.

It was a strike of unparalleled precision, slashing across the enemy's most fatal weakness.

Yet, it was the power of a mortal against a god.

Not even one-tenth of the true power of Light Modulation was unleashed.

Despite possessing the power to cleave the sea and tear the heavens, the attack managed only to leave a long, shallow scratch on the horizontally slit pupil.

A single drop of azure blood welled in the scratch-like gash.

At that moment, every single eye fixed its gaze upon CEO Cha.

The collective stare was a mental assault, designed to shatter one's sanity.

"...!"

The golden veil above his head blocked the gaze, but CEO Cha's body still staggered helplessly. If not for the veil, his brain would have been crushed instantly.

CEO Cha landed on a tentacle and skidded to a halt. He managed to stop by driving the Daeil Yeorae into the slimy surface to slow himself down. Beside him, a black figure shot forward like an arrow.

Je Hyun-oh, his metallic wings fully spread, swung his hand toward the wound CEO Cha had inflicted.

A black line was drawn.

Though he had only scratched the wound again, the gash deepened noticeably.

As Je Hyun-oh and Won Taebeom identified the only vulnerable spot among the tens of thousands of eyes, a message flashed through their minds.

Won Taebeom immediately unleashed a flurry of blows with his gauntlet on the wound.

The first was a Golden Mark, followed by a Heavy Strike.

*K-boom!*

The moment the gauntlet struck the eye, a vibration rippled through the air as if a fortress wall had been struck.

The force was clearly weaker than the previous attack.

Yet, because the blow landed directly on the already sliced outer shell, the shock and vibration were absorbed fully, crushing the tissue beneath.

The wound, once a mere hairline fracture, tore open.

Je Hyun-oh immediately lunged at the eye, his metallic hand gripping the wound and ripping it wide.

At that exact moment, blue blood erupted from the forced opening.

*Clang, clang.*

With a chilling metallic ring, the six metal wings disassembled into blades.

They plunged into the wound.

The blades moved as if possessed by their own will, burrowing deeper into the Eye and tearing it apart.

The sound of metal clashing against metal echoed repeatedly. Just as the mere scratch was about to become a fatal wound—

A dark, brooding wrath poured forth.


*"■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■."*


It was the Voice of god, unlike anything heard before.

Before anyone could even realize something was wrong, the world was stained black.

Blackout.

And when the darkness, which had lasted only a few seconds, lifted—

The world had flipped.

It was a sight that shattered the laws of nature.

The ocean now hung above their heads, while tens of thousands of eyeballs stared up from beneath their feet.

The awe of the inverted world—the sea above, the earth below—was short-lived.

A living hell unfolded.

From the sea that had become the sky, everything began to plummet.

The eyeballs embedded in the ground rolled their pupils, greedily devouring whatever fell upon them.

An iron door, once part of the prison, struck an eyeball directly. The door, marked *Zone X*, sizzled and melted the moment it touched the eye's surface, vanishing without a trace.

It wasn't just inorganic matter. Living beings fared no better.

The scraps drifting in the sea dissolved into neon green liquid, smoke rising as they touched the eyeballs.

Though the agony must have been excruciating, no screams were heard. Their already broken brains had been crushed by the erosion.

The Scraps died, laughing madly from mental overload.

*Kkihahak, hahak, kkuahahak!*

The maniacal laughter echoing from all around pierced my ears.

"..."

I caught my breath atop the eyeball, clutching the golden sphere.

The moment I saw the pure white light created by the White Man, I felt a twisting sensation. The heat that had been boiling throughout my body seemed to cool instantly. Even as I issued orders for battle, the feeling wouldn't fade.

In the instant I was distracted by this unfamiliar sensation, a sudden, powerful force surged in.

■■■ had poured his power into the area, transforming the entire domain.

In the inverted world, I lost my balance and fell onto the eyeball.

Because I faltered for a brief moment, my subordinates and possessions under my influence also took damage and fell with me.

I immediately checked my subordinates' condition.

Their health bars had been chunked down.

Worse, their health was still steadily draining. Fortunately, no other debuffs were applied.

The other two entities I had protected with the Golden Veil were in a similar state. They had lost a significant amount of health and were now taking continuous damage.

The real problem, however, was the rest of the entities that weren't protected by the Golden Veil. Their health was dropping much faster.

The moment they touched the eyeball, it began to drain their life force while simultaneously eroding their minds.

Even if I cast a Golden Veil over them right now, their inherent weakness would prevent them from being fully protected.

They had become useless in battle.

I thought as I felt the unpleasant power of the ■■■ constantly trying to burrow into me.

*'Should I use Punishment?'*

I would have to end this in one blow with the strongest power I could muster.

My subordinate was in the middle of showing off, but I couldn't watch any longer, worried that things would take a dangerous turn.

To maximize the power of Punishment, I needed to gather my strength.

I decided not to waste any power on unnecessary things.

*'I'll have to dispose of all the temporary possessions.'*

If I withdrew my influence from everything except my subordinates, they would simply melt away on their own, consumed by the eyeballs.

It was a pity to lose the top two entities, but their utility paled in comparison to my subordinates' well-being.

Just as I was about to withdraw my power, a song drifted in from somewhere.

A woman's voice, singing what sounded like a sweet love song, drew closer and closer.

Strangely, I found myself... halting everything I was about to do, just to listen.

I stood there, listening without knowing why, when I suddenly felt a soft touch.

Looking down, I saw small, white balls of fluff nestled at my feet.

It was Number 2 and Number 3, the ones I had registered as temporary possessions.

They were slowly melting away.

Smoke continuously rose from where they touched my eyes, and a neon green liquid flowed.

*'Did they come to beg for their lives?'*

But they did not plead with me. They did not beseech god for anything.

They simply clung to my side.

With their bodies in agony and their minds being eroded, they must have been suffering immensely.

Yet, it was strange how they didn't even let out a single groan, as if they were already accustomed to the pain.

They were low-tier individuals with poor stats.

As mere creatures with no special talents, they should have suffered even more. I couldn't understand why they clung to me like this, without any apparent purpose.

As I watched the smaller creatures, a massive vine plant approached me.

It was Number 1, the same one I had registered as a temporary possession.

The moment I saw the vine plant, a stray thought struck me.

*'It used to be much bigger than this.'*

Its size had shrunk considerably. It must have melted away while pushing through the space between the eyeballs.

No longer fitting the description of "massive," the vine plant now moved toward me with a desperation so palpable it seemed frantic.

I stared coldly, wondering if it was about to beg for its life again.

But as it reached me, the vine plant did something completely unexpected.

It hurriedly shoved its stems beneath my feet, as if offering to become a stepping stool.

It seemed to be trying to keep me from touching the eyeballs, even if only by a little. But doing so would increase the surface area of Number 1 that was in contact with the eyeballs, draining its stamina even faster.

I swallowed a dry laugh.

*'Is it in such a hurry to die?'*

Number 1 was also a low-tier specimen. It possessed no strength to accomplish anything.

I deliberately stomped on Number 1, which was already melting away.

At my touch, its stem flinched. I thought it was writhing in pain, but I was wrong.

Number 1 extended a single stem and gently wrapped it around my ankle.

It was trying to prevent my foot from accidentally touching its eyeball.

The Neon Green liquid that had melted from Number 1 left a long trail on my pant leg.

I slowly narrowed my eyes.

The singing grew closer.

A woman, with only her upper body remaining as she was buried among the eyeballs, leaned her face against my leg.

It was Number 12.

Her lower body had already melted away. She had lost her spider's form and could no longer even spin a web.

Driven to the brink of death, she rubbed her cheek against mine and sang a happy song of love.

As I watched her...

I felt a truly strange sensation.

The temporary possessions were gradually gathering around me. All eleven of them clung to me, acting insolently by surrounding a god, despite being mere creatures.

*'I should be using Punishment.'*

Yet I couldn't tear my gaze away from those melting things.

It was while I was staring stupidly at them.

"Mr. Chestnut!"

Number 6 approached me.

Its skin, which had slightly curved fins on its back, was the hide of an orca.

But the thick hide was riddled with mottled holes.

Number 6, his body melted away in patches, greeted me cheerfully while leaking a neon green liquid.

"So you were here. I'm glad you're safe."

Number 6's lower body was invisible.

At a glance, it seemed as if he were submerged in an eyeball, but I knew. Just like Number 12, it had already melted away.

Number 6 pushed through the eyeball with his arms, approached me, and shouted brightly.

"I've had my fill of swimming in the Sea of Neo. It's all thanks to you, Mr. Chestnut!"

He said that being immersed in Neo had allowed him to regain his true form—the one that wasn't humanoid—and that although his broken legs remained as they were, he had been able to swim freely in the vast sea for the first time in a long while.

"We will protect you, Mr. Chestnut, until we die. Don't worry and focus on recovering your strength," Number 6 promised gently, looking up at me.

I wasn't trying to recover. I was trying to take my power back from them.

But... I couldn't do a thing.

I looked down at Number 6. A stray thought clouded my mind again.

*'He was much taller than this. He towered over me.'*

As I stared, Number 6 met my gaze and smiled.

"I have never once feared death. In fact, I've always awaited it!"

*'Liar.'*

I read the hidden terror within him.

It reached out toward me. But his scarred hand fell short.

As if he had never intended to touch me in the first place, Number 6 simply kept his hand outstretched and continued speaking.

"But it's a bit of a shame. I never had any lingering attachments before... but for a fleeting moment, it was truly joyful to revere you as a god, Mr. Chestnut."

His eyes, gleaming like polished black pebbles, sparkled as if he had just woken from a wonderful dream.

"I couldn't help but imagine what it would have been like if you truly were our god. Ah, of course, I know that could never happen! I was just indulging in a brief fantasy."

Number 6's gaze dimmed slightly.

The health bars of the temporary possessions were nearly empty.

Their deaths were imminent.

"You know? While swimming in the Sea of Neo, I realized something. I can't swim as gracefully as I used to—I'm clumsy and pathetic now—but still, I..."

Number 6 smiled broadly and said,

"I love swimming, Mr. Chestnut!"

The sound of the ocean reached my ears.

There was no blue sea here. Only an inverted world teeming with eyeballs.

Yet, I heard the sound of waves rolling in a vast ocean.

I slowly parted my lips.

*"Don't go."*

But Number 6 could not hear my voice.

He was a mere temporary possession, not a subordinate.

Number 6's eyes widened in surprise, then he gave a weak smile.

"Don't cry, Mr. Chestnut. Isn't this a pretty wonderful farewell?"

It wasn't wonderful at all. I clenched my teeth and shook my head.

Number 6 spoke to me in a gentle tone.

"I will miss you, Mr. Chestnut."

*"No. Don't go."*

"I hope Mr. Chestnut... will miss us, just a little."

I called his name.

*"Orca!"*

But Orca never heard my voice.

The hand he had reached out to me fell limp, then crashed to the floor.
