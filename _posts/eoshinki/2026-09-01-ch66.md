<br>Kwak Hanmuk hooked a finger into the knot of his bow tie and tugged at it.

As he loosened the tight knot, he once again felt a sense of absurdity.

A banquet like this, amidst the Trial of a sci-fi setting?

The tailcoat he wore—featuring fiber-optic piping along the seams—was of an old-fashioned design.

They were supposed to be aliens, yet their behavior was no different from that of medieval aristocrats.

Notifications kept flooding in incessantly from the metal bracelet on his wrist.

Orders from his superiors and requests from customers were pouring in, but Kwak Hanmuk ignored them all and strolled leisurely toward the smoking room.

It was the place where he had promised to meet with Han Goyo.

Although he hadn't found any significant clues yet, he couldn't just sit still because he was worried about Han Goyo.

Even though he had received a message, he wanted to see Han Goyo's face at least once, and it would put his mind at ease.

As Kwak Hanmuk walked, he realized that he was walking too hastily, unbecoming of a waiter.

He slowed down and blended in with the surroundings.

Kwak Hanmuk was feeling an uncharacteristic confusion.

He had painfully realized through COF that the beings outside the Earth were after Han Goyo.

Even the Masked Ball was an event that openly targeted Han Goyo.

It had come to this point, and Kwak Hanmuk couldn't help but wonder.

Why didn't Han Goyo run away?

From the moment Je Hyun-oh was taken away by the god, Han Goyo had many other options.

He could have abandoned Je Hyun-oh and escaped safely, but Han Goyo didn't.

He had risked himself to save Je Hyun-oh without any hesitation.

There was no sign of wavering or hesitation in his choice.

However, as a god, Han Goyo was still immature, and as a result, he became a target for other gods to hunt.

Then, what could Kwak Hanmuk, as a subordinate, do for Han Goyo?

Having experienced the existence of a god, Kwak Hanmuk knew it well.

No matter how much of an Adaptee he was, he was still human, and it was far from enough to confront the vast existence of a god.

Was a subordinate, like the aliens said, just a mere plaything for children?

Unable to even protect their master...

*'You're really something else.'*

The frustration that had been clinging to Kwak Hanmuk continued to torment him.

A feeling of powerlessness that he didn't want to experience again.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">“Hyung, I’m okay.”</span>

<br>

The image of a young boy from the past, who had said those words despite being in a terrible state, overlapped with the present.

Kwak Hanmuk involuntarily distorted his face and swallowed his curse.

At that time, struggling was pointless, but this time, he wanted to do something about it.

He needed a change, no matter what.

Kwak Hanmuk suddenly missed Je Hyun-oh.

Although he didn't expect Je Hyun-oh to give him a clear instruction, he was a superior who gave orders in situations like this.

Kwak Hanmuk wanted to get Je Hyun-oh out of the tower as soon as possible.

As he arrived at the smoking room, he found it empty.

In the COF world view, smoking using cigarettes was a behavior that only the working class would do, so it was natural that the room was empty.

It was a space that existed in name only.

That's why they had chosen this place, far from the banquet hall, as their meeting spot.

As Kwak Hanmuk looked around the room, the door to the smoking room opened.

The waiter, who had been pretending to clean, relaxed his posture.

However, he didn't let his guard down.

The one who entered, wearing a white dress, was Cecilia.

She was also part of the reconnaissance operation, so it was natural for her to come here.

However, neither Cecilia nor Kwak Hanmuk made any attempt to acknowledge each other.

Cecilia took out a pack of cigarettes from her small bag and opened her mouth.

"Want one?"

Kwak Hanmuk's expression changed, and he looked at Cecilia.

The moment their eyes met, Kwak Hanmuk smiled and replied.

"Please, give me one, sister. I won't forget your kindness."

Cecilia handed him a cigarette, and Kwak Hanmuk lit it with a plasma lighter.

As he took a puff, Cecilia narrowed her eyes.

The synthetic cigarettes that the working class in Hyper Eden smoked contained a chemical that induced a slight sense of awakening.

It was a cigarette that was hard to endure for those who weren't used to it, but Kwak Hanmuk smoked it without any issues.

As he felt Cecilia's gaze, he turned his head to the side and exhaled.

"Ah, don't get me wrong. I'm a non-smoker. I'm just smoking one now."

Cecilia chuckled at his unnecessary excuse.

They smoked in silence for a while, and the fluorescent smoke from the synthetic cigarettes spread out.

Kwak Hanmuk had wanted to have a conversation with Cecilia at least once.

It was to understand the unusual favoritism that NPCs showed to Han Goyo.

Especially Cecilia, who openly displayed her close relationship with Han Goyo.

What had Han Goyo done to make the NPCs so fond of him?

As Kwak Hanmuk thought about what to talk about, Cecilia suddenly spoke up.

"I had hoped that Roasted Chestnut wouldn't come back here. He had a hard time."

Cecilia looked at Kwak Hanmuk and continued.

"But if he comes back... I thought I should stay with him. He doesn't like being alone."

The implication was clear: they were useless to Roasted Chestnut.

However, Kwak Hanmuk pretended not to understand.

"You don't have to worry about that now."

"......"

"It's not like he doesn't have any siblings. He has three younger siblings who will take care of him. And, well, you know."

Kwak Hanmuk replied with a mischievous tone.

"I'm his subordinate, after all."

Cecilia's eyes turned sharp, and she smiled.

"But despite being his sibling and subordinate, you don't know much about Roasted Chestnut, do you?"

Kwak Hanmuk was taken aback by her words.

Cecilia started talking about her time with Roasted Chestnut.

She was sharing her memories, but her intention was clear: she was emphasizing her close relationship with Roasted Chestnut.

"Roasted Chestnut sleeps in a lot, you know. He can't wake up easily."

As Kwak Hanmuk listened to her story, he remembered what Mo Haein had said.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"Was I weird?"</span>

<br>

It was a question Mo Haein had asked Kwak Hanmuk after the convenience store quest.

Mo Haein had clashed with Cecilia over Han Goyo.

Although Cecilia had provoked her first, Mo Haein hadn't remained calm and had countered.

She had acted as if she was showing off her closeness to Han Goyo.

Mo Haein had confessed that she had felt an overwhelming impulse at that moment.

A feeling that was amplified to an abnormal level.

Now, Kwak Hanmuk was experiencing a similar feeling.

"I see. He's especially hard to wake up on rainy days."

Kwak Hanmuk nodded and continued.

"He's always messy, and... Ah, last time, he even came to my room because he didn't want to sleep alone."

Even though the real reason for the visit was to gouge out eyeballs.

Kwak Hanmuk was talking about Han Goyo, but inside, he was scolding himself.

*‘What the hell am I doing?’*

He couldn't stop talking, even though he knew how incredibly childish it was.

He was acting just like a brat who didn't want to lose their master's attention.

It was probably because he and Mo Haein were connected to Han Goyo as his subordinates, or maybe it was for another reason.

In any case, instead of thinking deeply, Kwak Hanmuk was just chatting away.

As Cecilia listened to Kwak Hanmuk's story, she exhaled a long stream of smoke. She smiled mysteriously and asked.

"Why weren't you by his side back then?"

Kwak Hanmuk hesitated for a moment before Cecilia continued.

"When that kid came to Hyper Eden alone, struggled to complete quests, and earned money by any means necessary, and cried secretly at night because he was hurt..."

Cecilia asked again, her tone biting.

"Why did no one stay by Roasted Chestnut's side?"

She stubbed out her cigarette in the ashtray, crushing the still-unburned tobacco.

She whispered.

"I was the one who stayed with Roasted Chestnut when he was struggling the most. I saved him when he was dying, and he saved me too."

Cecilia's eyes grew darker. It was a face she never showed in front of Han Goyo.

"I had sent him away from Hyper Eden, hoping he wouldn't get hurt again, and now he's back..."

Cecilia's eyes seemed to gleam with a fierce light, as if she might pull out a pulse gun and start firing at any moment.

“I keep getting the feeling that this is all because of his siblings, you know?”

Kwak Hanmuk looked at Cecilia silently for a moment.

He stubbed out his own cigarette and slowly opened his mouth.

"...There were circumstances back then."

His tone was serious, without a hint of joking.

"I'll make sure it doesn't happen again. Absolutely."

"......"

Cecilia's eyes flickered for a moment.

It seemed she hadn't expected Kwak Hanmuk to react this way, and the two just stared at each other in silence.

Just then, the door to the smoking room burst open.

“…!”

Mo Haein stood there with a pale face.

Kwak Hanmuk and Cecilia's expressions turned serious.

Mo Haein looked around the smoking room and muttered.

"The trainee has disappeared."

<br>

The holographic wall painting, which covered one side of the wall, was opaque, and the other side couldn't be seen.

However, I could imagine the being outside.

A 3-meter-tall man walking leisurely.

The faint heat emanating from the holographic screen's projection device couldn't be felt now.

Instead, I felt a chill.

Even the young god, who had been tormenting CEO Cha, was silent.

Why?

Why was Dominic with the Black Block?

I had suspected that they knew each other, given their previous conversation.

However, Dominic had ignored the Black Block.

The only time he had spoken was when the Black Block had threatened me.

So, I had unconsciously assumed that Dominic would always help me or take my side.

But now, he had suddenly disappeared and was with the Black Block.

Did this mean that everything was back to normal?

Or, at the very least...

Had Dominic found what he had lost and was now with the Black Block?

"Don't jump to conclusions. Not yet."

I tried to think positively and looked at CEO Cha.

The gap between the holographic screen and the wall was just enough for one person to fit.

CEO Cha and I were standing side by side, clinging to the wall.

CEO Cha, who had grabbed my arm, was more tense than ever.

His expression was one that I had never seen before, not even when he faced the Eyeball Alien or the young god.

We were hiding behind the thin holographic screen, listening to the gods' conversation.

"I haven't told anyone outside, as you requested. If they find out you're here, it would be troublesome."

The Black Block was being unusually polite.

Most of the time, the Black Block was talking alone, and Dominic was only responding briefly.

However, Dominic was speaking in a language that I couldn't understand, a language that seemed to be tailored to him.

"I see."

The Black Block explained the holographic screen's image.

"This is a newly added spiral galaxy."

Of course, neither Dominic nor the Black Block was unaware that I was hiding here.

They were pretending not to know, and there must be a reason for that.

I held my breath and waited for their next words.

"The planet where the Little Lamb is, is similar to this galaxy! If the Little Lamb's planet is destroyed, we'll give this galaxy as a gift!"

The Black Block said, smiling, as if hoping that the Little Lamb would like it.

And then, he added in a low voice.

"I wonder if the Little Lamb's planet will survive this time."

This time...

The implication was that it had been destroyed once before.

At that moment, I realized why Dominic and the Black Block had made me eavesdrop.

It was to reduce the cost of obtaining information.

However, I couldn't understand why they were being so kind and giving me this information.

A primal suspicion arose.

"Am I allowed to hear this?"

If I had a System Window, I would have seen a warning message telling me not to listen.

Just then, I heard an incomprehensible word.

"■■■■."

It seemed to be related to Je Hyun-oh, but I wasn't sure.

The Black Block let out a strange, chuckling sound.

"It couldn't be helped! If it hadn't happened, you wouldn't have been interested in me. But the Little Lamb started broadcasting..."

The Black Block complained to Dominic, saying that his plan had failed, and he hadn't gained anything.

And then, he exclaimed.

"So, I've made it more interesting! You'll definitely like it."

At the same time, the screen flickered.

I stared at the thing in front of me, my eyes wide open.

A hand in a glove was waving the holographic screen like a toy.

The screen was wavering like a wave, but the other side was still not visible.

"A gift for the main character, the Little Lamb! The Little Lamb's subordinate is also here. However..."

The Black Block said, looking at me through the screen.

"The Little Lamb won't remember it that way!"

At the same time, I tried to rush out.

Or, rather, I would have if CEO Cha hadn't grabbed me.

The Black Block said, as if it was a promise.

"The hide-and-seek game for the young god will be fun."

As I struggled to breathe, CEO Cha covered my mouth.

The Black Block pulled his hand out of the screen, and his last words lingered in my ear.

"Let's play hide-and-seek."
