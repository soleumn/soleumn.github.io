<br>*'What's this?'*

I stared at the being before me, my eyes wide with bewilderment.

The figure, a perfect mirror image of myself, was a Young god.

*'Why is it out here?'*

The moment our eyes met, my last hazy memory snapped into focus.

I had been using my power to find Black Block.

The instant I seized control of every screen in Hyper Eden and located Black Block, I felt an insatiable hunger, even though I had achieved my goal.

I wanted to use more power.

I wanted to claim all of Hyper Eden as my domain.

But my body couldn't handle it.

Before I could even act on the impulse, my consciousness snapped.

And when I opened my eyes again, I was in this situation.

To be honest, I could only think that this felt like a fever dream.

It wasn't surprising, given that the surrounding scenery was anything but normal. The place was filled with ancient machines I'd never even seen in real life. To make matters more confusing, state-of-the-art equipment was mixed in among them.

As I scanned my surroundings, my gaze returned to the Young god.

The Young god tilted his head.

I think I knew what he was thinking. He found it strange that I was looking around instead of focusing on him. It was as if he were asking, 'How could you ignore me and look away?'

*'He's a total social misfit.'*

His way of thinking was completely beyond my comprehension.

As I stared at him, speechless, the Young god tugged on my wrist, urging me to hurry.

"Let's go kill Black Block, hurry."

I looked at the young god anew.

His golden eyes, with their crimson pupils, were filled with the absolute certainty that he could do anything he desired. As if he could kill Black Block right this instant.

When I didn't respond, the young god tugged my wrist again and again, his movements urgent.

"My subordinate is waiting."

I knew that. I shared the young god's desperate wish to reclaim the one who hated waiting, even for a single moment.

For the first time, I spoke.

"We have to get out of here first."

Before he could even ask if I knew how to leave, I answered.

"Yes. Let's go."

The young god stared at me, his gaze convinced that I knew the way out.

I had to bring him back to reality.

"I don't know how to get out," I said.

"Why?" the young god asked, leaning in close. "You're the one who created this place!"

"...?"

His words made no sense. We were speaking completely different languages.

I felt like I was dealing with a preschooler, and a slow dread began to settle in. 'I can't let this thing out into the world,' I thought. 'But how do I put him back inside me?'

For now, I scanned my surroundings to find an exit.

The space was a chaotic jumble of machinery from every era.

A flat, handheld game console glided past me. I snatched it out of the air and pressed the power button. But the screen only displayed the word "START" and refused to advance.

Right beside it sat an ancient rotary dial telephone. I pulled it closer, lifting the receiver of the antique device, which featured a wooden body and a brass frame. I pressed it to my ear.

Though I hadn't dialed a number, a steady, rhythmic dial tone pulsed through the receiver. But it never connected; the tone just continued endlessly.

I also tried turning on an old television set, but its screen only showed a flickering, grainy black-and-white void, like a swarm of static.

No matter how long I waited, the broadcast never started.

I was convinced we'd never find a way out with just these things.

I was about to tell the Young god to do something, anything, when he suddenly gasped and sprang away from me.

Then, he scrambled to hide behind a nearby TV.

*'Does he really think he's hidden back there?'* I wondered. *'And why is he acting like this all of a sudden?'*

As I stared in bewilderment, something shot out from behind my back.

The moment I saw it brush past my cheek, I froze.

A black form, studded with countless eyes, seized the fleeing Young god.

It wrapped itself tightly around his wrist, like a hand catching a misbehaving child.

The Young god froze, unable to even let out a single cry.

A voice drifted from behind me.

"Roasted Chestnut."

It was the same low, elegant voice I had always known.

But I... I couldn't bring myself to turn around.

"It is difficult to take the small form you prefer here. Even the one that mimics a human."

The countless eyes embedded in the black mass holding the Young god all fixed their gaze on me at once. A multitude of stares pierced me like arrows.

"There is no need to be so afraid. My appearance is simply a bit different."

The gentle voice I recognized continued to soothe and reassure me.

I began to breathe again, taking in tiny, shallow gasps I hadn't realized I'd been holding. My face must have been deathly pale.

I felt an amorphous presence moving behind my back, but I couldn't muster the courage to turn around.

To him, this was probably nothing more than removing a thin layer of wrapping. But for me, it was an unbearable burden.

When I knew nothing, I could look at it without a second thought. I had simply considered it a grotesque shape. But now, when I could see more than ever before, I was terrified.

I saw a scream.

It was the shape of the desperate cries of beings slowly fading away.

*No! It is not a scream! It is the ecstasy of those who are becoming one with Him, basking in His glory!*

I did not want to become a part of Him and share in that joy.

Barely regaining my senses, I felt a slow, slithering touch coil around my waist.

"If you're too afraid to look back, we can just talk like this for now."

I froze, my gaze fixed on the floor.

Despite the soothing tone of his voice, the thing wrapped around my waist held me tight, preventing any attempt to escape.

It let out a displeased *hmm*.

"You shouldn't separate yourself like this, Roasted Chestnut. You're at a crucial stage of growth, aren't you?"

His words were a stern warning that this was a grave mistake.

As he continued his lecture, I began to piece together what had happened. In a momentary surge of power, the Young god's ego had nearly consumed me. Terrified of losing my humanity, I had forcibly separated him from myself.

It was an unconscious action.

I, who had been silently listening to the lecture, finally managed to part my lips.

"Where... is this?"

At my question, it hesitated for a moment before answering.

"This is Roasted Chestnut's mental world."

That didn't seem to be an accurate description. It had simply combined words I could understand.

It extended a part of its form.

As it lightly touched a flickering television, the screen, which had been boiling with noise, turned white.

A small animal was busily running around inside the screen.

It was a young lamb.

A voice filled with faint curiosity and interest spoke to me.

"So this is what's inside Roasted Chestnut."

I couldn't believe it. For this to be my interior, it was filled with things I knew nothing about.

It was just as I was watching the bouncing lamb.

"Stop it!"

The young god, who had been standing some distance away, was suddenly yanked in front of me with a shout.

From the looks of it, he must have been caught while trying to untie the thing wrapped around his wrist and run away.

Now tied up to his waist, the young god looked at me with a tearful face.

"Let's untie this... I hate the pain..."

When Dominic fed me ■■■'s eyeball, I refused to go to sleep and endured the pain fully.

The young god seemed even more terrified because he remembered that moment. He looked as if he would burst into tears at any moment.

It felt a bit strange since it was my face.

As I stared at him with an unreal feeling, ***it*** comforted the young god.

"I have no intention of hurting you."

"Liar."

His trust had fallen to rock bottom; he seemed to have no intention of believing ***it*** at all.

Then, ***it*** extended a part of itself in front of the young god's eyes and said,

"I'll give you a gift if you stop crying."

"...A gift?"

"Yes, I'll give you one of my eyes. Go ahead and pick whichever one you want."

*'What kind of insane offer is this?'* I thought, but the Young god's eyes sparkled with interest.

"You'll really give it to me? I can pick any one?"

"Of course. And if you behave while I treat your wounds, I'll give you another one."

The creature swayed its body, making the countless eyes embedded in its flesh shimmer like jewels. The Young god, who had been sobbing in terror just moments ago, was now completely captivated.

"Hey. Hold on a second."

I frowned, looking at the Young god who was practically window-shopping for eyeballs right in front of me.

"You're not actually going to pick one, are you?"

"Why not? This one's pretty."

"...No, seriously, *sigh*."

It was beyond suspicious—it was downright sinister. Yet here he was, thrilled by the prospect of a "gift" and ready to snatch it up.

He was acting like a total child. Suppressing the urge to rap his knuckles, I addressed the figure behind me.

"Mr. Dominic, I appreciate the gesture, but I have no place to store this..."

"I'll keep it!" the Young god interjected, cutting me off.

"Hey!" I finally snapped, unable to hold back.

The Young god shot back without missing a beat, "What! You love cute and pretty things too!"

I was about to retort, *'What kind of nonsense is that?'* when a memory flashed through my mind—me, indulging my own secret desires by adorning a Squishy Pudding Slime with all sorts of decorations.

I felt the gaze of the figure behind me, watching our argument unfold.

Quickly clearing my throat to brush off the awkward moment, I seized the chance to unleash a torrent of criticism on the Young god.

"Are you going to keep acting like this? Do you plan on doing whatever you want without consulting me first? You did the exact same thing when you tried to gouge out Captain Kwak's eyes!"

I decided I'd have to teach him a lesson right then and there.

But as soon as I spoke, the Young god's eyes widened.

"We did it together."

"...What?"

The Young god stared at me intently before speaking again.

"From now on, you just stay inside. I'll handle everything."

He boldly declared that he would be the one to save the subordinates and kill Black Block.

It was utter nonsense, considering he didn't even know how to get out of here.

"Everyone likes me better anyway."

The Young god reached out to me. He cupped my cheek in his hand and smiled.

"You like me too, don't you?"

Seeing him do that with ***my*** face made it even more unbearable.

Finally, I couldn't take it anymore and smacked him on the head.

The Young god shrieked in pain. Then, out of nowhere, he lunged at me and started swinging his fists wildly.

His punches were pathetic. I easily blocked the Young god's clumsy strikes.

But that only seemed to fuel his stubbornness. He redoubled his efforts, flailing wildly at me. Watching him, a suffocating feeling rose in my chest. All the times he'd made my life miserable flashed through my mind. I'd even thought about getting rid of him—more than a few times, in fact.

But now that he was right here in front of me...

*'I just want to hit him one more time.'*

Still, I held back, forcing myself to be the adult. I was managing my temper well enough, but then the Young god did something truly insane.

When his punches failed, he suddenly bit my wrist.

*'This little—'*

I delivered a knuckle rap to his head, harder than the one before. The Young god, who had been trying to chew through my flesh, let out a sharp groan and clutched his head with both hands.

"It hurts..."

The Young god's deflated voice drained my strength. He was just a child in an adult's body. I wondered what I was doing.

Then, a soft sigh echoed.

"Both of you need a scolding."

The moment the stern voice dropped, ***it*** filled the space.

Countless eyes stared at me.

<br>

***Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes!***

<br>

The Young god and I were crushed together by it.

An overwhelming pressure threatened to flatten us, and its voice rattled my brain.

<br>

*"If I leave you like this, you might separate completely. I will force you to merge."*

<br>

The Young god screamed.

I screamed too. I wasn't sure, though. It felt as if ***it*** was absorbing even my cries.

*It entered me.*

*Nausea surged as the endless sensation flooded in. Yet I could vomit nothing. I **must not** vomit. I had to simply accept it. Just as He desired!*

<br>

*"Don't cry. If you can't have the eyes, I'll give you another toy."*

<br>

It was unclear whether the entity was speaking to the Young god or to me.

Perhaps it was speaking to both of us.

As I was gradually being eroded by the entity, I suddenly felt a soft, warm aura.

I instinctively clung to the only source of warmth. Only then did I realize who it was.

It was the Young god.

His golden eyes looked up at me.

"I'm scared..."

It was my own face, just with different colored eyes. I stared at the being before me with a strange, unfamiliar gaze.

"It hurts so much... I'm scared," the Young god whimpered. "I want to see my subordinates..."

Hearing the terrified voice of a child, I suddenly realized.

I was hurting and scared, too. I missed the Special Team.

"..."

I swallowed a sigh and pulled the Young god into an embrace, shielding him from ***it*** as much as I could.

As if he had been waiting, the Young god hugged me back tightly.

As I held his trembling body, I suddenly heard ***it*** laugh.

As if praising my act of embracing the Young god, the being that had been crushing me gently stroked my hair.

It felt like a caress.

Yet, I felt no peace. I could only swallow my nausea at the eye thrust before me.

<br>

*"You must not do such dangerous things again. Do you understand?"*

<br>

I didn't even know how I had separated myself from the Young god, but I nodded for now.

A soft whisper followed.

<br>

*"Though you won't be able to, even if you want to."*

<br>

Before I could react to the chilling remark, the being spoke to me again.

<br>

*"Roasted Chestnut needs to be a little more spoiled. After all, he's still young. I'll help you with that."*

<br>

And then, I felt myself being crushed.

<br>

***

<br>

I opened my eyes.

A slight drowsiness washed over me.

The first thing I saw when I lifted my eyelids was my subordinates.

They must have been anxiously waiting for their master, and they greeted me with joy.

"Goyo...!"

"Recruit, are you okay?"

I looked at them gathered around my bed.

For some reason, they all seemed to falter the moment our eyes met.

I slowly sat up and gave the Captains a soft smile.

"Did you wait long?"

But no answer came. Just as I was wondering why, I realized what I had forgotten.

I reached out as far as I could, stroking Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein's heads, praising them for waiting so well.

As I smiled while petting their heads...

My sanity returned.

*'Crazy!'*

But my hands and mouth wouldn't stop. My tongue moved on its own, saying, "Good boy and girl."
