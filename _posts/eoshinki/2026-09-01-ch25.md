<br>A blue light unfolded like a star cluster.

The distant yet brilliantly sparkling feast of stars seemed to blind my eyes.

When the light finally faded, I found myself on a beach where deep blue waves crashed.

The dazzlingly shattered emerald surface was gone, replaced by dark, heavy swells that filled my vision. The sky was thick with storm clouds, as if rain would fall at any moment, and the beach was composed of sharp rocks and pebbles.

The hard, piercing sensation beneath my feet, replacing the soft and warm white sand, jolted me awake.

This was a strange space, completely different from the peaceful place I had just been.

Yet, I felt a peculiar sense of déjà vu. As if I had been here before, even if only in a dream.

A man stood on the beach.

His towering figure, visible even from a great distance, was something that could not be called human.

He was gazing at the sea.

Though nothing but fierce waves were visible, he seemed to see something clearly.

His blue eyes scattered a brilliant radiance.

He slowly raised his hand.

Then, the sea parted.

Something. Colossal. Emerged. Overwhelming. Terrifying.

*'Huh?'*

I flinched and shut my eyes.

The moment my vision was blocked, a delayed terror surged through me. It was the fear of having seen something no human should ever behold.

In the darkness, I heard its mournful shriek.

It was a language I could not understand in the slightest.

Yet, for some reason, I felt it was pleading.

Its voice sounded as if it were confessing its sins and begging for its life.

On impulse, I opened my eyes.

*'I could look at this much,'* I thought.

And then I witnessed it.

The moment a colossal being was annihilated by an even more terrible calamity.

A cosmic nebula blanketed the dark blue sea.

The being of impure form desperately resisted the starlight that engulfed it.

Its final struggle shook the sea, but it was futile. Before that being, even the ocean was but a single drop of water.

The creature's movements, stained in blue starlight, grew still.

Then it was sucked into a black space that had appeared in the void.

I stared into the black space.

Within that space lay a universe filled with beautiful stars.

Yet, looking deeper, it was ultimately a void where nothing existed.

The split sea merged back into one, its waves soon undulating as if nothing had ever happened.

I gazed at the being who had accomplished all of this.

"..."

I tried to call out his name but couldn't.

I wasn't sure if this was the same being I had once known.

I felt no fear or terror. He had simply transcended the realm of my comprehension.

My brain felt paralyzed as I stared at him, unable to move.

He slowly turned toward me.

Our eyes met.

A voice, smooth and resonant like a finely tuned double bass, called my name.

"Roasted Chestnut."

He called my name just as the Rabbit Butler and Cat Butler did...

When I remained rooted to the spot, unable to move, he simply watched me.

With a faint sigh, he spoke sternly, as if addressing a misbehaving child. 

"Come here."

I had no choice but to approach him.

It wasn't a conscious decision.

My body moved against my will, driven by something akin to a survival instinct.

I stumbled toward him and lifted my head.

Even meeting his gaze was not something I had chosen.

"Are you surprised?"

His tone was gentle.

He soothed me, acting as the very person I had always known—and secretly relied on.

"I'm sorry. If I'd known you'd be this startled, I wouldn't have commanded you."

My lips parted with agonizing slowness.

"Mr. Dominic..."

I thought he might not answer.

But he did.

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

It was a different way of addressing me than before.

He looked down at me quietly. He was waiting for me to speak, but I... didn't know what to say.

Countless questions flooded my mind, yet I couldn't bring myself to ask any of them.

The longer our gazes met, the more I felt as if my throat were slowly being strangled.

Just as I thought I would suffocate from the tightening sensation, I finally managed to force out a voice.

"Have your... memories returned?"

"Ah, my memories."

Amusement flickered in his eyes, as if my question were a joke. He leaned down toward me.

A blue universe filled my vision.

"Not yet. See? I'm still Dominic, aren't I?"

He was right.

He was still Dominic.

Dominic gave me a faint smile.

"Now that Roasted Chestnut has grown a bit, he should be able to understand. I called it 'memory' to fit human standards, but it is closer to something else."

He explained it to me patiently.

"It is about reclaiming one's existence. Because Roasted Chestnut lost a part of himself when he summoned me."

In the past, I would have found his words completely incomprehensible.

But now, I thought I understood what he meant.

When I played the game, I had summoned him, and he had answered my call to come to CallOVo.

But he was a being that no mere Trial could ever contain.

Forcing himself to fit into that mold, he had lost his memories—a part of his very existence.

My lips trembled.

"I'm... sorry... Because of me..."

"Because of what?"

"The... the things you lost... If I hadn't summoned you..."

Dominic chuckled low as I stammered my apologies.

My mind screamed warnings of danger, but I was frozen, unable to move. I could only stare at him, completely captivated.

"Actually, I'm curious too."

Dominic leaned toward me. Though he only tilted his upper body, the sheer difference in our sizes made it feel as if he were falling over me.

"No one should have been able to summon me..."

His voice, deceptively gentle, posed a question.

"How did Roasted Chestnut manage to call me? "

I blinked blankly. My tongue moved of its own accord as I answered him.

"I... I don't know either..."

If I knew anything, I would have told Dominic everything. But I truly didn't know.

*‘To think such an immense being was summoned and trapped in a Trial just because of my gameplay.’*

I was the one who wanted to know how such a thing could even happen.

Dominic gazed down at me.

I found myself staring directly into his eyes.

Blue eyes where stars drifted. His pupils, resembling the cosmos, were so unfathomably deep that the longer I looked, the more I felt I could never escape.

The infinite void choked the breath from my lungs...

After what felt like an eternity, Dominic finally averted his gaze.

Dominic watched me with amusement as I gasped for air, like someone who had just been pulled from deep water.

"It's truly fascinating how curiosity can arise," he remarked. 

"..."

"Perhaps when you've grown a bit more, Roasted Chestnut, we will understand why."

He brushed a strand of sweat-soaked hair from my face, soothing me with the assurance that reclaiming my existence was an eventuality I needn't worry about.

Then, he changed the subject.

"Now, Roasted Chestnut. There is a more pressing matter than such trivialities."

Dominic asked, a faint smile playing on his lips.

"Was ■■■ pleasant to play with? I was busy cutting it into a small piece that would be easier for you to handle—so I couldn't stay by your side."

*That* was "just a little"?

The thing that overturned the world, melted and devoured all existence, and plunged us into an illusion?

"Ah, what I just dealt with was the remainder of ■■■. Thanks to that, I've also recovered a bit of what I lost."

He continued, saying that while he would have recovered more if he'd encountered a greater being, this was not bad. He also didn't fail to praise me for dealing with ■■■, even if I was only a young god.

His explanation, tailored to my level, was kind.

When I failed to react, he gently placed a hand on my shoulder.

"Roasted Chestnut."

I flinched and shook off his hand.

Stepping back, I stared at Dominic with eyes full of wariness.

"What did you just do?"

I felt the exact same sensation as when ■■■ tried to delve into my mind.

Dominic replied softly, as if to reassure me he wasn't a threat.

"I was only trying to help you relax, Roasted Chestnut."

"I'm fine. I'd prefer to just talk like this."

Only after the words left my lips did a wave of dread wash over me. Had I been too insolent? Strangely, however, Dominic seemed amused.

"Very well, Roasted Chestnut. I won't touch you."

He gave me his word readily, a smile gracing his lips. His blue gaze fixed on me—a silent command to approach.

I wanted to flee, to run as far as I could, but there was nowhere to escape. I knew running wouldn't solve anything.

I gritted my teeth and took a cautious step toward him.

He knelt down. Meeting my gaze at the same level, he held something out to me.

The marble, roughly the size of a piece of candy, looked utterly harmless.

Yet the moment I saw its neon green color, I thought it looked like someone's eyeball.

"Since you found it difficult to eat last time, I've made it a little differently this time."

After staring at the round marble for a long time, I finally realized.

"...Mr. Dominic."

And I asked him.

"Did I eat the heart and eyeball of Infernum?"

"Yes."

His prompt answer, without even a hint of denial, drained the strength from my body.

After SL2PL, I suffered from a fever.

I had always felt heat whenever my eye color changed, but this was completely different.

It was a flame that burned me from the very core.

Caught in the heat, I had committed violent actions and thoughts.

But this was completely different. It was a flame that burned me from my very core.

Swept up in the heat, I'd committed violent thoughts and actions.

I recalled a time when I was a Young god, overwhelmed by an intense heat.

Filled only with the desire to incinerate my enemies, I had created abilities that were nothing short of divine punishment.

I completely ignored the warnings from the System Window.

I was in a state of utter irrationality.

I concluded that the cause was the flame's heat, an overwhelming power I couldn't handle, which had been unleashed by consuming the Infernum's essence.

What would happen if I ate this now, this thing Dominic was offering, which was probably made from ■■■?

I doubted anything good would come of it.

At the same time, a fundamental question arose.

What was Dominic trying to do?

He had already fed me Infernum. Now, he was going through the trouble of preparing ■■■ just to feed it to me. His goodwill toward me was unmistakable.

He wanted me to grow as a god.

But why?

Was it because I was a Young god and his contractor? Or was he simply trying to satisfy his curiosity?

Many thoughts crossed my mind, but none of them seemed to be the right answer.

I carefully chose my words, trying my best not to offend him.

"Mr. Dominic... thank you for your consideration, but I'll pass this time."

The flames of Infernum had almost made me lose my reason. I couldn't afford to accept something new again.

Dominic's eyes widened slightly in surprise at my refusal.

"It won't hurt. I promised you, didn't I?"

I had no memory of promising him anything.

"I just feel like... something strange will happen if I eat it," I replied, knowing he assumed I was refusing because of the pain.

"I see."

Dominic lowered his gaze for a moment, rolling the bead around in his palm. The bead looked incredibly small against his large hand.

"Roasted Chestnut."

"...Yes?"

"You're badly hurt."

"..."

I bit my lip at his reproaching tone. I knew it, too. The pain was still throbbing through me even now.

When I didn't answer, Dominic slowly closed and opened his eyes.

He took my hand and placed the bead on my palm. Then, holding my hand with the bead between us, he said, "Look. You can see Roasted Chestnut now."

I was puzzled, not understanding what he meant, but then a jolt of shock made the hair on my neck stand on end.

It was because I had looked *inside* myself.

I stammered out bewildered words. "This, what is this..."

Golden lines crisscrossed my entire body. The irregular lines looked precarious, as if they were barely holding together a body that had been shattered into pieces.

As if I would shatter into pieces again if touched even slightly.

"It's been like this ever since I first looked into Roasted Chestnut. It's only gotten worse. Using your power this time must have caused severe internal damage... Let me check for a moment."

"…!"

He reached out with his other hand. I screamed.

*'No! It hurts!'*

The hand that had plunged into my chest caressed my heart. His touch, as if searching for something, was somewhat rough.

I struggled, trying to escape somehow, but I couldn't get away because his other hand held me firmly.

Seeing me in pain, Dominic frowned.

But he didn't stop.

"As expected... To think you're this broken."

He rummaged through my insides, his expression showing clear displeasure.

*'Stop it!'*

His low voice kept falling against my ear.

"You've done something truly foolish. Why did you choose me? Your condition has only grown more dangerous."

A large hand gripped my heart.

*Thump, thump.* I could feel every beat trapped within his grasp.

Dominic spoke, his tone as solemn as a final judgment.

"At this rate, Roasted Chestnut will be destroyed."
