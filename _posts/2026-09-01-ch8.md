“If I sign a formal contract with the Era Management Agency...”

“You signed it?”

“Not yet, but I intend to.”

“...What did the Director tell you?”

“No, this is my own decision.”

Before the meteor from The Cell-X fell, while talking to Han Goyo on the rooftop, Kwak Hanmuk had a gut feeling.

He sensed that Han Goyo had been pressured by Director Jang Seok-yoon in some form.

He knew Jang Seok-yoon all too well.

Jang Seok-yoon was a man who fit the role of the head of a national agency perfectly.

He was the type of person who believed that the sacrifice of an individual for the state, and the misfortune of a Compatible for the sake of responding to the Trials, was inevitable.

He was more than capable of doing something wretched to Han Goyo.

Kwak Hanmuk knew this better than anyone because he had experienced it himself.

Kwak Hanmuk had also remained at the Era Management Agency because of Jang Seok-yoon.

Of course, the final choice was his own, but it was a fact that Jang Seok-yoon had exerted a profound influence.

If he had done the same thing to Han Goyo...

*‘Damn it.’*

Kwak Hanmuk swallowed his deep irritation.

The kid was only twenty-four.

He had even heard that the boy had been shut inside his house for four years as soon as he became an adult.

As far as social experience went, all he had was a single year as a pro gamer during his teens.

There was no way a kid like that could handle someone as seasoned as Jang Seok-yoon.

If it weren't for The Cell-X, he would have grabbed Han Goyo and grilled him for answers...

But since clearing the Trial was the priority, he endured it for now.

While suppressing the rising irritation, Kwak Hanmuk thought of something else.

“Um, Captain Kwak. If I enlist in the Agency... could you please keep being on a team... with me?”

In that moment, he almost failed to keep his expression in check.

From the first time they met, he had been strangely concerned about Han Goyo.

After the boy was registered in the Third Clear and covered by the golden veil, he paid even more attention to him.

The reason he hadn't kicked away the shackles of the 'Special Task Team' imposed by Jang Seok-yoon sooner was largely due to Han Goyo.

What was that longing he felt the moment he wore the veil?

It was definitely not an emotion forced upon him by someone else.

When he wore the veil for the second time in SL2PL, he felt that same longing again.

Kwak Hanmuk had even gone so far as to investigate thoroughly to see if he had any connection to Han Goyo in the past.

Of course, there was nothing. They were at a level where they would never have accidentally crossed paths if not for the Agency.

Ultimately, without the mystery being solved, he had entered The Cell-X.

For now, Kwak Hanmuk moved diligently according to Han Goyo's plan.

He moved Creature No. 1, gathered information from the guards, tampered with the CCTV, and so on.

After working his ass off, he went to find Won Taebeom.

It was to ask if he knew anything about Je Hyeon-oh's whereabouts.

However, he ended up learning something else.

What Jang Seok-yoon had done to force Han Goyo into a contract.

“...To a trainee?”

He asked back, unable to believe it even though he had heard it clearly.

Won Taebeom replied with his usual expressionless face.

“That is correct.”

“Ha, fuck.”

Kwak Hanmuk, who had been sitting crookedly on a pew in the prayer room, immediately spat out a curse.

He ran his hand roughly through his hair, but his anger wouldn't subside.

Mo Haein's condition was a secret of the Agency.

Won Taebeom only knew because he acted as the acting team leader of Team 2 in Mo Haein's absence; otherwise, it was a secret shared only among high-ranking officials.

And he had shown that to a trainee.

The intention was so obvious it made him nauseous.

“Our Director Jang... his habits haven't changed.”

As he muttered lowly and grit his teeth, Won Taebeom, who was standing opposite him, asked.

“Will you discourage Trainee Han from signing the contract?”

“Of course I should.”

He shouldn't sign a contract under these circumstances.

Since Han Goyo was an adult, he had tried to stay quiet to avoid seeming too intrusive, but he felt he couldn't just let this slide.

If left alone, the kid would probably just walk in and sign a lifelong contract with the Agency.

“The Agency needs Trainee Han.”

Kwak Hanmuk, who was wondering if he should have a showdown with Jang Seok-yoon, paused at the words he heard.

He slowly shifted his gaze toward Won Taebeom.

And he stared at him in silence.

Won Taebeom did not avoid his eyes. Meeting his gaze directly, he spoke again.

“Please ensure that Trainee Han can contract with the Agency.”

Kwak Hanmuk asked expressionlessly.

“You're talking like a piece of shit, aren't you?”

“Trainee Han is currently the only existence capable of closing the Trial. If such a person were to belong to a private company rather than the state...”

“Lieutenant Won.”

“...Various problems will arise. Furthermore, the Agency is the place where Trainee Han's abilities can be maximized. While I cannot say Director Jang's methods are right...”

Won Taebeom finally crossed the line.

“I believe it is a necessary action.”

*Crash!*

Kwak Hanmuk bolted upright. The pew he had been sitting on flipped backward with a loud noise.

*Thwack!*

A clean punch sent Won Taebeom's head snapping to the side.

“Fuck, you want to keep talking?”

“......”

Won Taebeom slowly straightened his head. He glared at Kwak Hanmuk.

“This is official business, Captain Kwak. Please exclude your personal feelings.”

“Exclude? I fucking can't exclude them.”

Just as he let out a hollow laugh and was about to swing his fist again.

“Captain Kwak!!”

At the sharp shout, Kwak Hanmuk froze his hand in mid-air.

He saw Han Goyo, who had flung open the door to the prayer room.

Startled, the boy's already large eyes had grown even wider.

Of all times, he had to be caught punching his junior.

Cursing inwardly, Kwak Hanmuk opened his clenched fist wide and smiled.

“Hey, Acorn, you're here.”

I had seen Kwak Hanmuk beat up NPCs or monsters in the Trials many times, but this was the first time I saw him hit someone from the Agency.

Moreover, he didn't stop at one hit; he was about to hit him again.

“Captain Kwak!!”

Fortunately, at my shout, Kwak Hanmuk stopped his hand and greeted me with a grin as if nothing had happened.

I hurriedly approached them.

“What's going on? Why did you hit Lieutenant Won?”

One of Won Taebeom's cheeks was swelling bright red.

His lip seemed slightly torn, making me worry if a tooth had been knocked out.

As I stared with wide eyes, Won Taebeom spoke first.

“It is nothing.”

Is getting punched once not a big deal in the military?

His tone was so calm that the thought actually crossed my mind.

I glanced at Kwak Hanmuk.

Perched carelessly on the overturned pew, Kwak Hanmuk said nonchalantly.

“Oops, my hand slipped. Maybe it's because it's humid by the beach.”

“......”

Still, Kwak Hanmuk wasn't the type to hit a subordinate for no reason.

It seemed like Lieutenant Won had made some kind of serious mistake...

*‘But Lieutenant Won isn't the type to pick a fight first.’*

No matter how much I speculated, I couldn't find the answer.

As I just rolled my eyes between the two, Won Taebeom spoke first again.

“I have been briefed on the current situation by the Captain. I also do not know Lieutenant Colonel Je's location.”

...Does it not hurt?

I couldn't bring myself to ask him and wondered internally.

I wanted to ask what had caused this one-sided punching incident.

However, neither Kwak Hanmuk nor Won Taebeom were people who would answer easily.

I just gave up and brought up the strategy.

“Lieutenant Won. While performing the Correctional Clergy mission, were there any additional unusual findings? Please tell me anything related to those octopus leg wood carvings.”

“Are you aware of the religious broadcasts that air on the prisoners' TVs?”

“I am.”

I had watched them when I played the role of a prisoner in the game.

The only channel on the black-and-white TVs was the religious broadcast.

Even then, it wasn't aired every day; it could only be viewed once every few days.

It clearly bore a cross symbol, but the content of the religious broadcast was very strange.

The content was as follows:

A steamship navigating a sea of black waves suddenly encounters a violent storm and sinks into the deep sea.

Then, a giant eyeball, which didn't seem human, is zoomed in on, filling the screen.

Music that sounded like it came from a distorted pipe organ played, and the hymn proceeded amidst a humming sound, as if dozens of people were murmuring underwater.

“Praise! Worship! O Great Master of the Deep! The tentacles of salvation He extends shall lead us to eternal rest!”

“Cast aside your imperfect reason and embrace the divine madness that shakes the brain! Finally, find rest in the deep, black abyss where there is neither pain nor sin!”

They were words that made no sense.

However, it was eerie enough that I immediately realized it wasn't a normal religious broadcast.

Whenever the broadcast turned on, the prisoners would all stare at the TV as if possessed.

And I was the only one watching blankly.

“Prisoners who watch that broadcast become docile for about a day.”

“Ah, that broadcast?”

Kwak Hanmuk chimed in.

“The prisoners worked hard on the days they watched that.”

Meaning the productivity during the daily labor hours increased noticeably.

Was some kind of brainwashing happening through the broadcast?

If so, I wondered why they didn't air it every day.

*‘Maybe it's because it causes brain overload... or something like that.’*

Since it was an act that caused mental damage.

Afterward, I obtained information about the guards from Kwak Hanmuk and heard about CEO Cha.

It seemed he had succeeded in reigning perfectly over the guards.

*‘The Security Chief isn't the type to stay quiet.’*

I still shuddered remembering how much I loathed the Security Chief's tenacity when I played the role of the Prison Warden.

After gathering various pieces of information, I... should have left the prayer room...

“......”

Neither Kwak Hanmuk nor Won Taebeom had finished their missions for today.

I had to find Je Hyeon-oh on my own, and I wasn't sure if it was okay to leave them behind.

I worried that they might start punching each other again if I wasn't there.

Reading my thoughts from my gaze, Kwak Hanmuk spoke immediately.

“I've done all my punching for today.”

“Yes... please try to use words if possible...”

Even as I left the prayer room, I glanced back anxiously.

Then, Kwak Hanmuk approached me with a mischievous grin.

“Want me to walk you?”

I hadn't said anything like that.

But I just answered, “Yes.”

After walking side by side in the hallway for a while, Kwak Hanmuk spoke to me casually.

“It's nothing, so don't worry, Chestnut.”

However, the current state of Kwak Hanmuk shown in the Status Window was still 'Irritated.'

*‘I wish he would tell me why.’*

I wanted to ask, but since I wasn't a team member, I felt I shouldn't pry.

I just thought I should find him some snacks later and said,

“Captain Kwak. Please don't overdo it.”

At that, Kwak Hanmuk chuckled and patted me on the back.

“Let me know when you find Lieutenant Colonel Je.”

“Yes, sir.”

I said goodbye to him and set off in earnest to find Je Hyeon-oh.

Although it was a remote island, the prison was quite large and the structure was complex.

Trying to find Je Hyeon-oh in a place like this without any clues already felt daunting.

I checked Je Hyeon-oh's Status Window, and his current state was still displayed as 'Despair.'

I scolded the system window in my heart.

*‘Do something.’*

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You should acquire the abilities of a young god as soon as possible...</strong>
  </div>
</div>

*‘No, not that. Tell me the direction Lieutenant Colonel Je is in, or something.’*

It seemed that as I grew as a god, I would be able to know the current location of my subordinates, but wasn't that a matter for the future?

I needed help right now.

The system window remained silent. Just as I thought I shouldn't have expected anything, a rectangular window popped up.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>You are amazed by the incredible power of the system window.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

At the same time, a golden arrow appeared in front of me.

It was an arrow like the ones that guide beginner users in games.

*‘This is useful. Good job.’*

After giving it a moderate compliment, I began to move in the direction the arrow pointed.

I had to wander around here and there because some paths were blocked by walls, but it was much easier with the direction indicator.

As I got closer to Je Hyeon-oh, my anxiety grew bit by bit.

It was because the state of 'Despair' kept weighing on my mind.

And finally, I found him.

A black figure was standing at the edge of a coastal cliff.

There was so little movement that at first glance, he looked like a statue placed at the edge of the cliff.

Positioned in a precarious spot where he looked as if he might fall at any moment, Je Hyeon-oh was staring at the sky.

I approached him cautiously.

Even though he must have felt my presence, he did not move.

His goggles were also dark.

I realized belatedly that he was drenched in seawater.

He looked as if he had just emerged from the ocean.

What on earth had he been doing...

Feeling a hint of frustration, I called out to him.

“Lieutenant Colonel Je.”

But Je Hyeon-oh did not look back at me.

For some reason, I felt I knew what I had to do for him.

*‘Protection.’*

I placed the golden veil over him.

Only then did Je Hyeon-oh slowly turn his head toward me.

Within the golden protection, he called my name.

“Goyo.”

“Yes...”

Just as I resolved to accept whatever he said.

Je Hyeon-oh asked me in a whisper.

“You're a god, aren't you?”
