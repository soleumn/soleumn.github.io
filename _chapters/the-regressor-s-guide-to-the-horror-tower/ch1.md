<br>“I'm heading out.”

“Right, see you tomorrow.”

I checked the clock with hollow, tired eyes.

10:00 p.m.

Overtime.

And what made it even scarier was that I wasn’t the only one left behind.

As the team leader waved goodbye looking exhausted, and I exchanged farewells before heading out,

“Hey, you know that thing lately. That famous missing persons case. What was it again? People just saying they’re going home and vanishing.”

"People going missing isn't exactly something new."

“Still, that could end up being you.”

“You really know how to say the nicest things to your subordinates, don’t you?”

“I’m begging you not to disappear. If you go missing, my workload doubles.”

The team leader chuckled as he spoke, and I offered a faint smile in return before stepping out into the hallway.

Walking down the hallway, I checked the news on my smartphone.

True to the team leader’s words, there had indeed been a string of mysterious disappearances trending on video-sharing sites recently.

Disappearing after walking down the stairs, disappearing after boarding an elevator.

In any case, they vanished.

Maybe the government was deleting them or something, because every video that got uploaded was quickly taken down.

Still, the rumors kept spreading.

If there was one common thread, it was that the culprits could never be found.

“….”

A video had been uploaded this time, too.

A man walking slowly down a stair landing, only to vanish at a certain point.

“…It gets creepier every time I watch it.”

I muttered to myself as I stepped onto the elevator.

It was bizarre.

The stairs, and the elevator.

People vanishing as if suddenly kidnapped into another world.

Standing blankly in the elevator and staring at my smartphone, I fell into thought.

It was a thought I’d had on occasion.

What would happen if, when I got off the elevator on my way home, the scenery that met my eyes was entirely different from the one I knew?

What a ridiculous thing to wonder about.

I let out a dry, self-deprecating laugh as I stepped inside the elevator.

With a cheerful *DING!* sound, the elevator doors closed.

My office was located on the seventh floor.

Slowly, the elevator began to descend.

7… 6…

5… 4…

“….”

Staring blankly at the elevator doors, I dropped my gaze to my smartphone.

Listening to weird stories really got into my head for no good reason.

I repeatedly fiddled with my phone, then looked away.

The elevator continued its descent smoothly as always.

Just as I was alternating between playing with my phone and glancing at the elevator,

*DING.* With a chime, the elevator stopped.

I raised my gaze to look ahead.

The elevator had stopped on the first floor.

With a soft, sliding sound, the elevator doors opened—

“….”

An entirely unfamiliar sight unfolded before my eyes.

It was the hallway of a building with all the lights out.

A strangely disgusting smell pierced my nose.

It smelled like something was rotting.

“….”

It's different.

This wasn’t the first-floor hallway of the company I knew.

Slowly, I turned my gaze toward the elevator’s digital display board.

The indicator read 1F.

It was the first floor.

It just wasn’t the first floor I knew.

“…What is this?”

My bewildered mutter barely left my lips when—

In an instant, goosebumps broke out all over my skin, and cold sweat poured down.

Those frequent missing persons cases.

Could it be…

Me?

In a panic, I pulled out my phone to check.

No signal.

I tapped the phone app to call emergency services, but the sound echoing in my ear was, naturally, the mechanical automated notice stating that I was in an area where the signal could not reach.

“Damn it, damn it…”

Sweating profusely, I stood on my tiptoes and desperately pressed my phone against the very top corner of the elevator, but not a single bar of signal could be caught.

How long had I struggled, desperately searching for a connection?

In the end, I had no choice but to accept that no signal was going to be found.

“….”

After spending a long time staring blankly in utter despair, I began tapping various apps, clutching at them like a final lifeline.

The calculator, the notepad, or even a game app I rarely ever opened.

Naturally, nothing happened.

It was hopeless.

After opening countless apps, the last thing I tried out of sheer desperation was the internet browser.

It was a ridiculous idea.

Obviously, if the signal didn’t reach, the internet wouldn’t either—

“…Huh?”

And right as I opened the internet app, an unidentified website that suddenly popped up grabbed my attention.

The site looked old.

There was only a single sentence written on it.

[Check the notebook.]

…A notebook?

Slowly turning my gaze, my eyes landed on a notebook attached to the elevator wall.

Hanging there as if it had been in that exact spot from the very beginning.

[Look at the notebook.]

“…?”

A leather notebook worn with age.

I carefully took the notebook down, and—

Opened it.

And beneath that, the records of numerous people were written.

Records of those who came here before.

[150. Where is this? Who are you people?]

It's important to understand the situation first, isn't it?

If so, this smell I'm smelling right now...

"...Ugh."

I press down on my mouth to forcefully hold back the rising nausea, and shift my gaze forward.

In the darkness, the scenery begins to slowly come into view.

It was a typical unlit building hallway.

What is this ominous feeling?

"...."

Should I say I instinctively feel that this place is dangerous?

"...."

I turn my gaze back to the notebook.

Let's organize things first.

There is a strategy to this place.

It seems like you shouldn't stay in the elevator for too long, either.

However...

"...."

I am genuinely terrified of going outside.

To think this is a place where you could die the moment you step out.

I shift my gaze outward.

A dim light.

The floor was clean, and not a single corpse was in sight.

The scenery seen from inside the elevator and the scenery seen from the outside are different.

What does this mean?

First, let's get out.

I slowly take a step.

The moment I step outside, an unpleasant air wraps around my body.

They said to move when the lights go out.

Along with a precariously flickering sound, the light bulb goes out,

and I, too, take my first step forward.

A splashing sound echoes.

A splashing sound?

I lower my gaze.

"...."

It was a splashing puddle.

It's too dark to see well, but I didn't even want to find out what this liquid was.

Thinking that, I moved forward.

Thud.

Something caught against my foot.

It felt like a log.

At the same time, the faint light turns back on.

I lower my gaze,

"...."

And made eye contact with a man staring at me with wide-open eyes.

It was the man in the pink hoodie.

A man whose neck was bent backward, staring into empty space with bulging eyes.

No, calling him a corpse would be more accurate.

"...."

I forcefully suppress my pounding heart and look away.

I must not look at that.

I mustn't make a sound, because if I look at that again, I feel like I'll scream.

Tightly shutting my eyes, I repeat the contents of the notebook in my head.

Absolutely do not make a sound.

And only move when the lights are out.

Head left at the fork in the road.

If I just think about those things, I can survive.

I take a deep breath and move my feet.

Right, as long as you keep your wits about you, you can survive even if you're dragged into a tiger's den.

I can escape this place.

Once again, with a dry sound, the lights go out.

Biting down hard on my lower lip, I take a step,

and at that moment, I step on something and slip.

—*CRASH!*

My body slides straight across the floor, landing hard on my bottom.

A loud noise echoes out, and I freeze right there.

*...!?*

Cold sweat pours down.

All sorts of thoughts instantly begin to race through my head.

Does this count as making a sound?

Or is this not considered a sound?

Should I move?

Should I run?

Which bastard left this down here in the first place—

*SNAP!*

The lights turn on.

I lower my gaze, and pooling blood floods my vision.

Blood.

I hastily turn my gaze away.

Blood was pooling in various places across the floor.

And, countless marks of slipping.

Without fail, right between those slip marks, a corpse lay resting.

Things like this weren't written down in the notebook.

Just as I felt a wave of bewilderment and tried to push myself up,

A chill crawled over my skin.

A splashing sound rings in my ears.

I hurriedly cover my mouth and hold my breath.

I realized it for the first time.

That when a person falls into extreme terror, their legs can freeze completely in place.

But what should I do?

Even before I could fully finish that thought,

—*SPLASH.*

The wet footsteps stopped directly in front of me.

Before I could look up at whatever stood there...

—*SNAP!*

Along with the sound of something snapping right in my throat and a momentary surge of intense pain, my consciousness ended right there.
