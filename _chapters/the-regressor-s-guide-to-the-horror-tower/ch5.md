<br>“….”

I reread the contents of the notebook before my eyes.

No. 151, No. 152?

Does that mean someone followed behind me?

Does that even make sense?

Wasn’t there supposed to be no one after me?

To begin with, I didn’t even die.

So how could I possibly see records of those who came after… No. How could I see records of people I didn't even know followed me in the first place?

Don't tell me…

“….”

I rushed out of the elevator, and the mascot costume, which had remained quiet until then, trotted right behind me.

“Do you know anything about this? That people get abducted into this place even if I don’t die?”

“…?”

At my question, the mascot costume tilted its head.

“I mean, whether I’m not the last, and there’s actually No. 151.”

“…?”

Meeting the eyes of the mascot costume, which stared back as if having no idea what I was talking about, I let out a sigh.

Right, my mistake for expecting an answer.

Let’s think for a moment.

It said there was someone below me.

And that they were heading up to the 2nd floor.

It sounds insane, but someone is on the 1st floor.

And that someone is coming up to the 2nd floor.

It’s a low probability, but what if, just what if…

What if someone comes up?

What if I meet the person who came up from the 1st floor?

Wouldn't that make things a little easier to solve?

At the very least, I’d have someone to talk to.

The mascot costume understands what I say, but we can’t hold an actual conversation.

“….”

Let’s read more of the notebook first.

I skimmed all the way through it, but the contents remained identical.

It’s like a maze.

And there is no way to find the path other than wandering around.

If I were to look for suspicious parts by rereading it multiple times…

[83. Seems like there's a slight pattern. But walking around trying to find the way feels the same either way... And walking is really hard because of the steam.]<br>
[121. I'm ■■, so I'm just keeping on walking. But when it gets past a certain heat, aren't the pipes making way too much noise?]<br>
[130. Seems like there might be cool spots too. But I probably won't make it out.]

About three entries like that.

“….”

I tapped my fingers against the elevator wall, lost in thought.

There is a pattern.

Once it gets past a certain heat, the pipes make noise.

There are cool spots.

“I have no idea.”

With just this, the information is far too lacking.

While I furrowed my brow deeply in thought, an announcement rang out as if it had been waiting for this moment.

&lt;Further stay is not permitted.><br>
&lt;We repeat. Due to the nature of the 2nd floor, further stay is not permitted.><br>
&lt;Furthermore, if you remain inside the elevator until the doors close, we bear no responsibility for any accidents that may occur hereafter.>

At the same time, the doors began to close, and the mascot costume, which had been standing still again, grabbed my wrist and dragged me outside.

“I told you, I can walk just fine on my own.”

“….”

Without an answer, it merely tugged my wrist, moving outside the elevator.

The moment I stepped out, the elevator doors began to slide shut, and shortly after, closed with a heavy *thud*.

“…*Phew*.”

Looking at the closed elevator doors, I let out a small sigh.

Let’s wait just ten minutes.

I’ll wait exactly six hundred seconds.

Who knows? Someone else might come up in the elevator.

Thinking that, I stared silently at the elevator.

The mascot costume beside me simply stared at me in silence without a word.

After waiting for exactly six hundred seconds,

“No dice. Not a chance.”

In the end, it seems impossible to meet No. 151 or anyone with later numbers just by waiting.

Perhaps…

“…Is it something like parallel worlds?”

It’s a theory I don’t know much about, but I’ve seen it often enough in movies and books.

And if not that, well, it could be some other rule I’m unaware of.

For now, the important thing is that there are people following behind me.

And that they act after reading what’s written in my notebook.

“….”

Are people’s lives at stake because of my writing?

“Let’s handle this a bit more calmly.”

“?”

“Just talking to myself.”

In the end, I had no choice but to push forward again this time.

But it’s fine.

I’ve already memorized the path.

“The path I took last time was left, right, right, left….”

I just need to add a few variations this time.

Time is on my side anyway.

If I die, I can just try again.

For someone rich in time like me, clearing this floor is ultimately no different from just putting in the hours.

Though dying will hurt a bit, of course.

“Hey, if things get too tough for me, could you kill me?”

“….”

At my words, the mascot costume shook its head side to side.

As if it couldn't possibly do such a thing.

“Is that so?”

That's a shame, then.

* * *

No matter how I look at it, this place really is easy to get lost in.

Since every path looks pretty much the same, I would have gotten lost in no time if I hadn't memorized it.

Feeling the slightly rising temperature, I kept moving my feet without rest.

“*Phew*….”

It’s hot.

Watching the steam hiss out, I paused for a moment to catch my breath.

When I stopped, the mascot costume following behind me stopped as well, quietly gazing at me.

“Isn’t it tough for you, too?”

“….”

Well, it’s sweating less than me, so it’s probably fine.

It doesn’t seem human to begin with.

Or is it?

“They say any part of you that touches the steam melts, so watch your step.”

“…!”

The mascot costume dodged the steam with exaggerated motions.

Come to think of it, the mascot costume is much thicker than a human body, so how does it dodge so well?

Watching the mascot costume dodge this way and that around the steam, I opened the notebook again.

Wonder if there are any new updates.

None.

Still, it gives me strength.

While it’s deeply regrettable that people were dragged in after me, on the flip side, knowing those who came after me solved problems more easily thanks to me is a great source of comfort.

I steadily drew the map inside my head.

“Right from here, and next is left….”

“….”

“A dead end. Then since I went back this time, rule out the left and go right….”

Remembering all this is a chore in itself. A real chore.

Just how long did I walk?

Gradually, the heat began to rise again.

“…It’s hot.”

When I took off my jacket and wrapped it around my waist, the mascot costume reached out its arms.

“What is it?”

“…!”

“Ah, you want to carry it for me?”

“!”

The mascot costume nodded its head vigorously.

It's surprisingly helpful, honestly.

“Thank you.”

“!”

Pumping its fist into the air, the mascot costume looked at me and took the jacket.

Well, if it’s willing to help.

Wiping my sweat with my dress shirt sleeve, I moved my feet again.

Though I'm starting to get the feeling that this run is ruined.

I kept moving forward, and the heat steadily grew more intense.

But it’s fine.

“We just need a path turning right from here. Then—”

A newly walked…

“…?”

A completely blocked wall appeared before my eyes.

Wait.

A blocked wall?

“…That’s strange.”

It’s not time for a dead end yet.

I should have at least four more turns to go.

In an instant, goosebumps erupted across my entire body.

“…Don't tell me.”

Don't tell me the layout resets with every Regression?

Then does that mean memorizing it was completely pointless?

“….”

My thought that this might be more troublesome than expected lasted only a moment.

—*Psssshh…!*

Along with the sound of steam venting from the pipes, the heat grew even worse.

“…No.”

I shook my head, pulling my thoughts together.

It's no big deal.

Nothing has really changed as much as I think.

I just need to wander around.

The number of times I get lost might increase, but even so, I can keep trying again and again.

There’s no disadvantage for me.

Right, at this level…

“….”

Noticing the oddly quiet atmosphere, I turned my gaze.

The mascot costume was staring blankly at something.

“Where are you looking?”

“….”

I followed the mascot costume's gaze,

and a path came into my view.

“Isn’t that path pretty much the same as the others?”

“….”

The mascot costume looked there for a moment, turned back to look at me, and then pulled my arm.

Straight in the exact opposite direction of where it had been looking.

“Wait, weren't you taking me that way?”

“…!”

Without a word, the mascot costume simply pulled me along.

“Hold on…!”

This was the first time this guy had ever dragged me along so openly.

The air remained burning hot.

“….”

The mascot costume turned its gaze toward me again, and after staring back at it for a moment, I looked away.

“Let’s keep walking.”

It wasn't as if we had any other choice anyway.

* * *

“….”

*Thump.* My body tilted again,

“…!”

and before I knew it, the mascot costume hurriedly caught my swaying frame.

“Right, thank you.”

“….”

The mascot costume wiped away my dripping sweat with its hand.

I'd walked plenty this time too.

I didn't know how many hours I'd been walking, but my knees creaked, and heat seethed through my body as if it were boiling.

Am I going to die like this again?

“…Seriously, would it hurt to let me die a little easier for once?”

I muttered, undoing the buttons of my completely soaked dress shirt.

Looks like this run is finished after all.

It might go without saying, but ever since falling here, I haven't died peacefully a single time.

My gentlest death so far was getting my neck snapped, so that really says it all.

And this death would undoubtedly be utterly miserable, too.

“….”

I don't want to die.

No matter how you look at it, dying is terrifying and agonizing.

Even if I come back to life, the pain that strikes in the meantime torments me endlessly.

That’s why, even if I have to lean on the mascot costume, I keep putting one foot in front of the other.

Holding onto hope, wishing that an elevator will appear around the next corner.

With that thought, I rounded the turn,

“….”

and at the same time, my vision blurred, and I collapsed to the floor.

Before I realized it, I had stopped sweating altogether.

It felt like my head was being cooked alive.

Turning my gaze sideways, I saw the mascot costume looking down at me while holding the coat it had taken from me.

What is it planning to do this time?

That thought lasted only a moment.

The mascot costume knelt down and met my eyes.

“…?”

I tried to say something, but no sound came out of my mouth.

I merely stared quietly at the mascot costume,

“….”

when it slowly reached out both hands and gripped my throat.

I felt a chilling sensation for only a brief moment.

The mascot costume twisted its hands, and—

*—*SNAP!**

With the sound of something snapping in my neck, my consciousness instantly cut out.

Did it kill me? Me?

Why?

Such questions flashed through my mind, but the last thing I saw was the mascot costume's unreadable face.

&lt;――――This is a violation.>

At the same time, a bizarre voice echoed.

A violation?

A violation of what…?

Along with crackling static, my consciousness sank once more.

* * *

“….”

I pushed myself upright.

Naturally, my eyes met those of the mascot costume, which was staring quietly at me.

The moment our eyes met, the mascot costume tilted its head vigorously.

I recalled the mascot costume's face from when it had just killed me.

Did it understand what I said?

But I told it not to.

Besides, what was that about a violation?

All sorts of thoughts swirled in my head, and I hurriedly opened my mouth.

“Just now, no. I mean. No. Do you even understand what I’m saying?”

“…?”

In response to my question, the mascot costume merely tilted its head.

“….”

First, I tried to recall what the mascot costume had done in the previous run.

It killed me, and in the run before that, it supported me.

Overall, every action the mascot costume took was for my sake.

To put it accurately, it could practically be considered a helper.

If that’s the case, assuming there was some hidden meaning behind those actions…

Inside the now-familiar elevator, I began pacing in circles while reading through the notebook.

Aside from killing me and supporting me, what else did the mascot costume do?

There was one thing.

The path the mascot costume had stared at without saying a word.

A singularity—if there was an anomaly…

“…!”

I hurriedly flipped through the notebook.

Three entries that had caught my attention.

[83. Seems like there's a bit of a pattern. But wandering around to find the way is the same either way... And walking is really tough because of the steam.]

[121. I'm ■■, so I'm just keeping on walking. But when it gets past a certain level of heat, the pipes make way too much noise, don't they?]

[130. Feels like there might be cool areas, too. But I probably won't make it out.]

Steam coming from the pipes.

Yet, the path the mascot costume had been looking at…

“….”

Snapping my back straight, I met the eyes of the mascot costume staring at me with an indifferent expression.

“Excuse me.”

“…?”

The mascot costume tilted its head.

“Thanks to you, I think I know how to get out of here.”

“???”

At my words, the mascot costume tilted its head vigorously.

Fundamentally, the layout of this place is a time attack.

No. Let's be precise.

It’s a layout designed to make you *think* it’s a time attack.

Unlike me, others only had one life to lose, so they just aimed to escape within the time limit.

That wasn't it.

“Thanks to you, I figured out how to find the exit.”

If I thought about it carefully, there was a way to get out.

And an extremely simple one, at that.
