<br>With a mechanical chime, the elevator doors opened.

However, I had only one thought.

2nd floor.

It was the 2nd floor.

Utter despair.

That was the only thought in my head.

Not an exit, but another floor.

Did that mean once I left this place, the 3rd floor might be waiting instead of an exit?

Did an exit even exist in the first place?

Cold sweat dripped down.

"…"

No one likes it when an ominous premonition comes true.

They only curse their own intuition.

"Damn it."

"…"

For now…

"…Why did you come along?"

"…"

And standing next to me was the mascot costume, tilting its head as it stared at me.

It was the bear mascot that had shared a cucumber sandwich with me earlier—back on the 1st floor.

"…?"

"Can you even understand what I'm saying?"

"…?"

"Nod if you understand me."

"…!"

The mascot costume nodded its head vigorously.

"Why did you come here?"

"?"

At my question, it simply shrugged its shoulders without saying a word.

"You don't know?"

To that question as well, the mascot costume merely shrugged.

"…Why did they even send you if you don't know anything?"

Right. Let's check something else first.

The ticket. There was a ticket.

I checked the ticket.

A ticket torn in half.

What does it say?

I narrowed my eyes and inspected the ticket.

[…vival Ticket]

"Vival Ticket?"

What kind of ticket is that?

"…"

I turned it over in my mind, but nothing came to me.

Giving me only half probably means I can't use it right now, right?

Just in case, I held up the ticket and waved it in the air, but nothing happened.

"I'll use the ticket!"

&lt;A half ticket cannot be used.>

I didn't expect an answer.

"Do I just need to bring the whole ticket?"

Unfortunately, there was no reply.

"…"

Whatever. Let's assume that's the case for now.

I turned my gaze back to the mascot costume.

The mascot costume was still quietly staring at me.

A gift, or a perk.

Either way, it was definitely something positive.

"You came to help me, right?"

"…!"

The mascot costume nodded.

"How?"

"?"

I still couldn't tell.

Nothing I can do about it. Let's read the notebook first.

Just as I thought that and went to open the notebook—

&lt;Remaining any longer is not permitted.>

An announcement came from the elevator, which had remained completely silent whenever I asked something before.

Not permitted to remain?

What is that supposed to mean?

&lt;We repeat. Due to the nature of the 2nd floor, remaining any longer is not permitted.>

&lt;If you remain inside the elevator until the doors close, we take no responsibility for any subsequent accidents.>

*'We'?*

My confusion was short-lived.

&lt;The doors are closing.>

With a mechanical sound, the doors began to close with a creak.

I have to get off?

Here?

I haven't even had a proper look yet.

Before I could even finish that thought—

"Ack!"

The mascot costume, perfectly still until just a moment ago, grabbed my arm and dragged me straight out.

How is it this strong…?!

As soon as it dragged me outside—

*THUD!*

The elevator doors were already shut.

"…"

Before I could even process the sudden turn of events—

—*Ssssshhhh…*

An ominous sound echoed out.

Where is this?

I quickly scanned my surroundings.

Inside the dark, narrow passage, only a flickering light greeted me from time to time.

It looked like… a basement.

—*HISSS!*

Straining my ears at the sound once more, I desperately looked around.

Countless pipes were tangled like a spiderweb, and that hissing sound was coming from those very pipes.

Other than that, it looked like an ordinary…

Well, could this place really be called ordinary?

No, this isn't the time.

Now that I've been kicked out this far, my only option is to check the notebook quickly.

I hastily flipped open the notebook and began checking the contents.

[9. Did I escape first?

I'm just going to die here. Good luck to whoever comes next.]

[11. Are we continuing with our own numbers? Anyway, you definitely shouldn't touch the steam coming from the pipes. The spot it just touched on me melted away.]

[13. Found a body. Don't know who it is, but rest in peace. Looks like they died recently.]

[15. Setting it on fire doesn't work either?]

[17. Don't touch the steam coming from the pipes, and just move as much as you can for now. Other than the pipes, there doesn't seem to be anything particularly dangerous. Unlike the 1st floor, there's no rule like only moving when the lights are off, or only moving when the lights are on. But it gets hotter and hotter.]

[21. Can't find the exit. The pipes aren't the problem—the layout here is just too complicated. From what I've walked so far, the path is as follows:]

[22. Writing with one arm is damn hard. How the hell are we supposed to find it?]

[22. It's getting hotter and hotter, isn't it?]

[24. Hello, I am ■■■ of the Management Bureau. If the 1st floor was basically a place to teach you the structure and rules of this location, then this place seems to test your adaptability.

I tried to break through here using my ability, ■■, but it doesn't seem to work this time.

Also, please have hope. The path you have walked will become hope for others.]

[24. For now, it seems the only way to find the exit is to wander until you stumble across it by chance. There are surely gimmicks I am unaware of, but for now, you will have to wander.]

[28. Just like No. 21 said, finding the exit is pretty tough.]

[30. This place is pretty rough. If you don't find the exit, the lights slowly start to dim. It's hot.]

[30. It's hot.]

[30. It's too hot. I want to die.]

[30. Hot.]

[31. Think I found the exit. Like No. 24 said, you basically have to wander... I kept following the left wall. Isn't there some rule that says you'll eventually find the exit that way?]

[35. Trusted No. 31 and kept following the left wall. Got lost. It's hot.]

[38. The longer you wander, the hotter this place seems to become. I can't walk anymore.]

[45. Just saying "walk until you escape"... how the hell are we supposed to escape?]

[51. Looks like you have to escape within roughly 12 hours. After that, the temperature becomes too high for a human to survive.]

"..."

I snapped the notebook shut.

I wanted to read more, but for now...

"Let's walk."

"..."

The mascot slowly moved behind me.

"...You're coming with me?"

"...!"

It nodded enthusiastically.

I looked at it curiously.

"It's supposed to get hot."

"...!"

The mascot struck some incomprehensible pose.

I had no idea what that meant.

Apparently, it could handle the heat?

"..."

I thought for a moment, then nodded.

"...Fine. But help me if you can."

"...!"

With that, I began walking.

The mascot fell into step beside me.

*Pssshhh!*

Steam burst from a pipe.

I jerked my body away.

"Damn it."

"..."

Fortunately, the mascot beside me wasn't a burden.

If anything, it was extremely good at dodging.

It was already hard enough looking after myself, so I'd worried about having to take care of that thing too, but it didn't seem like there was any need.

As much as I wanted to read the notebook while walking...

"..."

*Pshhh.* I watched the steam hissing out from all over the place.

If I walked carelessly and got blasted by that steam, it would be a total disaster.

For now, let's find the exit.

Dodging the spurting steam, I strode forward.

"...Ah."

Right.

First, let's make the most of my ability.

Because my ability is Regression.

Probably... if I die, I'll revive back at the second-floor elevator.

"Should I memorize the route...?"

Even if I tried mapping it out, it'd all be for nothing once I died anyway.

It's better to just memorize it.

Alright. Let's memorize it.

Having made up my mind, I moved forward.

"Left. Right, right, left..."

"..."

I didn't turn back.

Turning back was strictly for when the path was blocked.

Because I had plenty of time.

If I died, I could just take a different path instead of the one I'd taken.

I kept walking and walking; how long had I been on the move?

"...Phew."

It was starting to get hot.

Beads of sweat formed on my skin.

Just like late-spring weather.

I took off my suit jacket and tied it around my waist.

I glanced at the mascot costume, but unfortunately, because it was wearing a mascot costume, there was no telling whether it was hot or cold.

"Are you hot?"

"...!"

The mascot costume waved both hands in denial.

Well, whatever...

If it claimed it wasn't hot, who was I to argue?

I started walking again.

<br>

***

<br>

"...Whew."

A dead end.

That's fine.

I can just turn back.

The mascot costume's pace was slowing down as well.

"If you're hot, take off some clothes."

"..."

At my words, the mascot costume hurriedly shook its head.

As if it would never take off the mascot costume.

"Yeah, whatever. Suit yourself."

It won't be my fault if you die of heatstroke.

<br>

***

<br>

"...*Huff*... *pant*..."

Sweat poured down.

Every now and then, the mascot costume wiped my sweat with its fabric, but that didn't help in the slightest.

"...*Whew*."

To hell with it, fine.

If I die, so be it.

<br>

***

<br>

"..."

My head pounded.

I felt nauseous.

Now, I was barely sweating anymore.

"H-Hot."

"..."

"So... hot..."

The words slipped out incoherently.

"..."

The mascot costume stared at me quietly,

and smiled.

No, was it crying?

My vision blurred.

My body wouldn't obey me.

With a dull thud, I heard the sound of something collapsing.

"..."

Before I knew it, I was lying on the floor.

"Ah, ugh..."

My head hurt so badly.

It felt as though my brain were cooking.

Something entered my blurry field of view.

Crouching down, the mascot costume was looking at me.

"..."

I tried to say something, but lacked the strength to speak.

"Uh, ugh..."

"..."

When it was this hard for me to move, how could the mascot costume move so well?

They said that once your temperature rises past a certain point, you can't even sweat anymore.

Is this what they were talking about?

"..."

Slowly, the mascot costume walked toward me,

and pulled me to my feet.

I wanted to say something, but I couldn't.

Because I didn't even have the energy to talk.

Without letting out so much as a pant or a groan, the mascot costume supported me as we walked.

Even so, that couldn't stop the suffocating heat from rising.

In the parched, drying air, I felt death approaching.

After enduring a headache that felt like my skull would burst for a little longer, my consciousness sank away.

The last thing I saw was the mascot costume, desperately taking step after step while still holding me up.

Why had it supported me?

Did it have a reason, or else...

With a sensation of my mind drying out completely, my consciousness plummeted to the depths.

That was my first death on the second floor.

<br>

***

<br>

"*Gasp!*"

Clutching my pounding heart, I pushed myself upright.

A *ding* sound, and the doors opening.

"..."

I felt a gaze.

Before I knew it, the mascot costume was standing beside me.

"..."

I stared silently at the mascot costume, but it simply looked back at me and gave a thumbs-up.

"Why did you do that?"

"...?"

The mascot costume threw its arms up as if asking what on earth I was talking about.

It didn't seem to retain memories across the Regression.

Why was I feeling a mix of relief and disappointment?

I sighed, caught my breath, and turned my gaze.

The notebook on the wall, and the mascot costume staring blankly at me.

And lastly...

*―*DING!**

The elevator doors opened, and the scenery of the second floor revealed itself before my eyes once again.

The eviction order hasn't come down yet.

In that case, let's read it again.

Letting the familiar rushing sound of air go in one ear and out the other, I began skimming through the notebook.

Nothing really different.

In the end, the path was randomized, and there was no other way than wandering endlessly until finding it.

Since I had the Regression ability, it was something I could clear eventually given enough time.

Good.

Just as I was skimming through the notebook with that thought,

"..."

a note further down caught my eye.

[151. ? What the hell. There's nothing here. Can't you just walk out?]<br>
[152. Didn't No. 150 clear it?]<br>
[153. I think No. 150 cleared it. Otherwise, the tone feels way too different from what the earlier people wrote.]<br>
[154. So No. 150 did what the previous 149 people couldn't? Who the hell is this guy? Anyway, heading to the next floor.]

"..."

Entries from those who followed after were written there as well.
