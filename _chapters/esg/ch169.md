<br>“I never knew an editor was such a great profession.”

Cha Sieon finally opened his mouth after a long silence.

His attitude of finding me absurd was so blatant that I calmly nodded as well.

“Every profession is great.”

“That is not what I mean.”

Cha Sieon countered, looking as if he found it absurd.

“Are you really trying to lump everything together under the label of ‘editor’?”

Cha Sieon gave up on the veiled sarcasm and asked back.

To that, I nodded again, driving Cha Sieon up the wall.

If I say that is how it is, what is he going to do about it?

Team Leader Cha grew agitated once again at my attitude, then abruptly called out to me with a serious posture.

“Seo Doun.”

“Yes.”

“Have you read the response manual for isolation situations within a Special Zone?”

“I have read it.”

“People experience extreme mental exhaustion in an isolated environment. Therefore, when a citizen is discovered in an isolation situation, checking their mental state first is the principle.”

“However, Team Leader, I am not a citizen.”

“Are you naturally an editor normally and only an agent when you need to be?”

Ah…….

“If your editor ego comes first, doesn't that mean your identity is still that of a civilian?”

This was impossible to refute.

When I was left speechless just like that, Cha Sieon spoke again.

“So please cooperate.”

“……What and how do you want me to cooperate?”

“Please explain so that I can understand what on earth your crisis response capability has to do with your identity as an editor.”

I finally realized why Cha Sieon was acting like this.

Team Leader Cha, a man who insists on doing everything by the book and keeping tight control over everything.

He had tried to check my mental state first according to the manual, but it seemed he was displeased because my state of mind far exceeded the scope of his expectations.

He was the type who couldn't be satisfied unless he grasped everything and placed it in the palm of his hand before responding to a situation.

So he had gone so far as to demand that I make him understand.

Now that Team Leader Cha had come this far, it was difficult to gloss it over vaguely.

So I pondered for a moment before opening my mouth.

“An editor is a profession that checks and reviews the causal relationships in a story. Therefore, as you read various stories, your range of understanding about situations broadens as well. Once you acquire situations and causality in that manner, your responsiveness naturally increases.”

“And?”

“Therefore, even when facing a crisis situation, you can step back and make more rational judgments. Because I’ve seen similar situations before. It becomes possible to respond by thinking, ‘If I get scared here, I'll die, if I get excited over there, I'll die, and this is foreshadowing and buildup meant for ruin.’”

“Are you saying you are controlling your own actions by taking the failures of characters within stories as examples?”

As expected, Team Leader Cha also had a talent for summarization.

I nodded at Cha Sieon's concise summary.

Cha Sieon asked me back.

“Leaving all that aside, are stories and reality the same?”

It was a question thrown out mindlessly.

It was merely a question thrown out in ignorance by a layman who didn't look particularly close with books, let alone movies or comic books.

So, instead of asking back, “Are you looking down on our industry?” I calmly smiled and explained.

“They aren't different. Stories and reality aren't separate; stories simply reflect reality. Furthermore, masterpieces with verisimilitude are almost indistinguishable from reality, and sometimes they reveal reality even better than reality itself. That's why they are often more helpful than a few years of experience or a handful of human relationships. A truly well-written story has sufficient cause and effect to apply anywhere in reality, and this world overflows with stellar stories that humanity can use as cautionary tales. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that the tragedies of our world and humanity's reading volume are inversely proportional.”

“……”

“……Don't look at me like I'm some weirdo after you're the one who got me talking.”

Cha Sieon looked at me with a strangely wary gaze, which slightly annoyed me.

When I seemed displeased, Cha Sieon corrected his expression and asked back.

“So your composure was secured through your reading volume?”

I nodded again.

However, Cha Sieon still wore a face that didn't understand, so I elaborated a bit more.

“If you think, ‘My life is ultimately just one piece, like a story,’ you don't get overwhelmed by the situation. Because only my choices regarding the situation are left.”

“……”

“As you said, I feel fear too. But since it doesn't help much, I sort it out. ‘I might die here. People die anyway. Then I suppose it doesn't matter if I die. Since it's come to this, let's do whatever I want, fighting.’ Roughly along those lines.”

“……”

“And as they say, if you seek death you shall live, and if you seek life you shall die…….”

“I get it, so please stop.”

“Now do you understand?”

“It's a realm that defies understanding, so I'm letting it go.”

Cha Sieon made me talk all he wanted, yet wore a sour expression.

I looked at Cha Sieon with the feeling of, what on earth do you want me to do?

Team Leader Cha thought about something to himself, then abruptly opened his mouth.

“Among my team members, there are a few who occasionally do or say absurd things. So when I ask them what on earth was going on in their heads, do you know how most of them react?”

“‘I will correct it’?”

“No, they ask back, ‘Is it really only me doing this?’”

“….”

“Seo Doun is the first person to immediately explain a rational reason.”

I was virtually at a loss for words.

I was quite flustered by the feeling of being caught off guard, and Cha Sieon glanced at me and continued.

“Anyway, it doesn't seem different from usual, so I'll let it slide.”

The voice of Cha Sieon as he said that was calm.

It wasn't particularly sarcastic, nor did it sound like he was doing me a favor.

It was simply the tone of someone who had finished what he needed to do.

Yet, I felt as though I had been found out about something.

<br>

A giant pile of scrap metal was visible.

A pile of scrap metal where rusted iron was piled high.

That mountain-like thing was slowly approaching my direction.

*Screeech- Screeech-*

The shape, as if it would collapse on me at any moment, was threatening.

Fortunately, the speed wasn't all that fast, so I could have avoided it however I wanted.

Yet I merely stared at the pile of scrap metal drawing near, as if nailed to the spot.

Then, a man pinned beneath the pile of scrap metal came into my field of vision.

A figure I could now say I knew.

He was Lee Seop.

-...ry.

Lee Seop said something to me.

But I couldn't hear it well.

What was he saying?

I stepped closer to hear that voice.

-Ki...

The moment Lee Seop tried to speak again.

*Boom-!*

A loud roar rang out.

Startled, thinking the pile of scrap metal was collapsing, I looked up.

However, that sound was not something from my dream.

*Boom-!*

This was a sound heard in reality.

Surprised by that sound, I woke up from my sleep.

“That’s the sound.”

When I snapped my eyes open, Cha Sieon said from beside me.

“You can sleep 40 more minutes.”

“……”

I calmed my shock at Cha Sieon’s added words.

Then, I buried my head back into my coat and closed my eyes again.

12 hours since we were isolated here.

To secure sleeping time, we were taking turns standing watch.

And that unknown loud roar continued to echo.

<br>

24 hours since being isolated in the 「Railway Station」 waiting room.

Cha Sieon had gone inside the tunnel connected to the door to inspect the interior.

Because of the continuous tremors, we decided to secure an evacuation route.

Because if an actual bombing were to drop, we’d be finished.

So right now, we’ve opened one door close to the waiting room and are exploring.

The space being explored was a relatively manageable ‘tunnel with tracks laid in it’, but even that only looked manageable while being just as suffocating.

Fifteen minutes had passed since Cha Sieon went inside with a wire tied around him.

Yet it seemed he still hadn't found the end of the tunnel.

Thanks to that, I was feeling the hopelessness of isolation with my entire body.

It was then.

*Boom-!*

A loud roar echoed from afar.

And the tremors that followed.

Not again…….

“Hoo…….”

Startled by the roar, I let out a sigh.

That sound had no set cycle; it rang out again just when you were starting to forget it.

That unpredictable pattern was quite stressful.

But anyway, it was just making noise.

……That was what I was thinking.

*Rumble-!*

What?

Where was that sudden sound of water coming from?

Abruptly, the sound of a waterfall was heard.

However, from where I was standing, nothing could be seen.

At the same time, I couldn't recklessly leave the doorway with Team Leader Cha left in there, so I shouted toward the inside of the tunnel.

“Team Leader Cha!”

Worried my voice wouldn't reach, I also pulled on the wire a couple of times.

“Please come out! There is suddenly a sound of water!”

Then, the wire started winding back up.

About five minutes later, Cha Sieon came running out from the other side of the tunnel.

Without even asking me for details, he faintly frowned.

“The sound of water?”

Leaving the tunnel door secured, we moved back to the center of the waiting room.

Then, we looked around searching for the sound of water.

Our search didn't take long.

The floor on the other side of the hallway branching off from the waiting room was soaked with water.

Moreover, that water was gradually spilling out into the waiting room.

It looked as though a flood had broken out due to torrential rain.

“What in the…….”

We splashed through the water as we ran down the corridor.

And then, we were met with it.

*Rumble-rumble-rumble-!*

A massive torrent of water pouring out from the door.

What is going on?

Did a loud roar just echo and the door opened?

No, even if the door opened, why is water suddenly gushing out?

All sorts of things had come out from beyond the door until now.

However, they had never arbitrarily invaded this territory.

Doubts ran rampant, but there was no time to ponder leisurely.

“The door!”

Cha Sieon shouted and ran, and I followed right behind him.

We tried with all our might to close the door.

However, it was impossible to block the water pouring entirely out of the door with the strength of just two people.

“The water pressure is too strong!”

The water was literally pouring down like a waterfall.

Trying to block it with brute force was impossible.

Then, should we just let the water fill up and then close the door?

Since it’s impossible to defy the water pressure, it might be possible after matching the water level.

But this is the waiting room on the second floor above ground.

To fill this entire space with water, it would have to rise all the way to the platform where you go down the stairs.

Given how wide the space was, there was no immediate fear of drowning.

However, once the water filled up that much, there was no way for us to drain it back out.

The more the water filled up, the more our movement routes shrank.

*Rumble-rumble-rumble-!*

While looking around, completely soaked in water.

*Thud-!*

Another loud roar echoed.

*Bang-!*

Two more doors opened.

From there too, water began to pour out like crazy.

We looked back at the other doors with a dizzying feeling.

They were closed doors, but they looked like they could open at any moment.

And if water were to pour from those doors as well.

“…….”

It seemed this space had decided that our end would be a watery burial.
