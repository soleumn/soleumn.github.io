<br>Ta-dadada-daa—!

Hello, trainee agents!

Today, we will learn about the danger grades of Special Management Zones!

The Special Zones you will manage from now on are classified into 5 grades in total: ‘Caution’, ‘Alert’, ‘Danger’, ‘Quarantine’, and ‘Pending’.

Then shall we look at the features of each classification one by one?

════════

■ Caution (C) Grade

1\. Both an exploration rate of 63% or higher and a return-alive rate of 45% or higher must be met.

2\. At least 1 stable escape route, or at least 2 unstable or somewhat risky escape routes, must be secured.

3\. The danger factors within the Special Zone must be at a level that a non-disabled civilian aged 14 or older can sufficiently avoid if they exercise caution.

4\. 「Giants’ Board Game Café」, 「Rainbow Cake Shop」, etc.

════════

Caution grade refers to zones being successfully controlled.

As long as you respond according to the manual, major accidents will not occur.

If a citizen is pulled into a Caution-grade Special Zone, the duty team can deploy immediately without separate permission.

So if you discover a citizen in a Caution-grade Special Zone, make sure to rescue them actively!

════════

■ Alert (B) Grade

1\. Exploration rate of 45% or higher, or return-alive rate of 27% or higher.

2\. At least 2 unstable or somewhat risky escape routes must be secured.

3\. There are fatal danger factors within the Special Zone, but if conditions are met, response and avoidance must be possible.

4\. 「Sports Complex Where Corpses Run」, 「Spring of Reincarnation」, etc.

════════

Alert-grade Special Zones are zones where exploration has progressed considerably and escape routes have been secured, but the danger level of the space is still high.

Permission is required to enter such zones.

So if you discover a citizen in an Alert-grade Special Zone, make sure to report it quickly.

════════

■ Danger (A) Grade

1\. Exploration rate of 27% or higher, or return-alive rate of 18% or higher.

2\. At least 1 unstable and risky escape route must be secured.

3\. There are fatal danger factors within the Special Zone, but a possibility of survival must also exist.

4\. 「Railway Station Where the Siren Sounds」, 「Kids Café That Kills Red」, etc.

════════

Danger grade refers to zones where sacrifice is inevitable.

Screening is required to enter these places.

Also, entry will not be approved unless it is an extremely important matter.

So even if you discover citizens, you must judge coldly.

Even if you go in, the number of corpses will only increase from one to two.

Next is the most dangerous Quarantine grade!

════════

■ Quarantine (S) Grade

1\. No escape route secured.

2\. Survival method unknown.

3\. 「National Museum of Korea Where a False God Looks Down」, most Type 3 Special Zones.

════════

Quarantine-grade Special Zones are zones with a return-alive rate of 0%.

The only time our agents approach Quarantine-grade Special Zones is when resetting the Special Zone.

Quarantine-grade Special Zones can cause mental damage through monitoring alone.

So always take care not to approach them arbitrarily.

Lastly, Pending grade refers to zones where exploration is still in progress or zones with special characteristics.

Special Zones in the early stages of exploration, and 「Circulating Library」, fall under this category.

Now, we have looked at the danger levels of Special Zones.

For Caution grade, be active. For Alert grade, be swift. For Danger grade, be cautious.

And for Quarantine grade, do not approach!

Make sure to remember these four things!

Ta-dadada-daa—!
