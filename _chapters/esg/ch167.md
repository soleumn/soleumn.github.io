<br>“The older sister of 「Big Cat」.”

At Cha Sieon’s murmur, I vigorously nodded my head.

It was to emphasize just how unavoidable my situation had been.

“So you’re saying that she made a deal with Seo Doun, and on the condition that our agents be spared and let out, Seo Doun chose to stay behind here and be killed by that ‘sister’?”

I nodded once again.

An exceptional summary.

However, I would appreciate it if he could refrain from pronouncing the word ‘sister’ with that tone of voice as much as possible.

After making me sit in front of him as I nodded diligently, Cha Sieon fell into thought for a moment.

Then, looking back at me, he quietly expressed his anger.

“And you truly had no time to say even this single sentence?”

“…….”

At Cha Sieon’s blatant lingering resentment, I abruptly stopped nodding.

He might as well have hit me one more time instead.

Instead of hitting me, Cha Sieon kept giving me pointed looks and subtle pressures with his words and gaze.

However, knowing my crime, I answered with a humble attitude.

“If I were to make an excuse, I really didn’t have any breathing room either…….”

A bombardment fell from the sky, and both Team Leader Cha and Baek Sara died.

Even I ended up dying, but suddenly a woman calling herself ‘the older sister of 「Big Cat」’ exposed my repeated deaths and made a strange proposal.

“Everyone had died and come back to life, so I thought escaping from here was the top priority, and I didn’t have the confidence to explain the situation sufficiently either.”

Honestly, if I had told them the truth as it was, would people have believed me?

They probably would’ve thought that bastard got contaminated and dragged me away tied up.

Even I would do that.

That was why I only gave a vague hint to Team Leader Cha before running off, but now that I’ve cooled my head and thought about it, my actions must have felt like a massive bolt from the blue to Team Leader Cha.

One of the members of the operation team he was leading suddenly called him, grinned, and jumped out of the train.

From Team Leader Cha’s perspective, wasn’t it a scene straight out of a horror movie with absurd logic?

Cha Sieon seemed to get more pissed off the more he thought about it, and after suppressing his anger for a moment, he opened his mouth.

“Thanks to that, both I and my team members saved our lives, so I have nothing to say, but at least consult with me before acting from now on. And as for the conditions of that deal…….”

However, quickly getting fired up again, Cha Sieon let out a sigh.

“……I understand why Seo Doun made that judgment. But while exchanging a single life for twelve lives might make sense arithmetically, considering the special traits and strategic value Seo Doun possesses, Seo Doun returning alive might actually be more beneficial in some ways. So from now on, even if a proposal like that comes in, absolutely do not make decisions on your own. Do you understand?”

“Yes…….”

“And we are also people who are prepared to die. Not only are you not someone whose life Seo Doun needs to risk everything to save, but we have already received more than enough lives handed over to us.”

“…….”

“Do not add your own life to this. That is, if you know why we volunteered. We are already people with more than enough weight on our shoulders.”

That was right.

They were people who had come to settle their grudges.

They were people who wanted to play that role themselves rather than just cheer on Agent Otter, and people willing to risk their lives to do so.

Yet, I had almost added another burden onto them.

Realizing this, I spoke humbly.

“I’ll make sure nothing like this happens again.”

At my answer, Cha Sieon let out another long sigh.

Sensing that Team Leader Cha's displeasure had softened somewhat, I casually added my thanks.

“And thank you, Team Leader. I survived thanks to you as well.”

“You haven’t survived yet, so don’t let your guard down.”

This uptight bastard... A guy tries his best to thank him, and he...

“...By the way, aren’t you acting alone right now as well, Team Leader?”

I asked, hoping Cha Sieon would feel stung.

I was crawling on the floor because I was in the wrong, but I wondered if he was really in any position to lecture me so boldly.

However, Cha Sieon scoffed instead.

“Do you think I’m like you? I handed things over properly to my team members before coming.”

Huh, really?

“They just let you go willingly?”

“When I said I was going to drag Seo Doun back, everyone understood.”

“...”

Come to think of it, aside from his own gear, Team Leader Cha was carrying a large bag.

I had wondered what that extra bag was that he didn't have before.

Turns out the people had pooled gear and rations for him, telling him to go bring me back.

I had been secretly relieved, thinking Team Leader Cha was an accomplice just like me.

I was wrong.

I was the only criminal, and he was the slave hunter tracking me down.

Looking at the large bag resting beside Team Leader Cha, I felt a fresh chill run down my spine.

Just how long was Team Leader Cha planning to wander around here, packing a bag like that?

With that much, he could easily hold out alone for a week...?

“Anyway, you’re alive, so that’s that. We’ll rest for fifteen minutes and then make our escape.”

Saying that, Cha Sieon looked a bit exhausted.

Well, he had been running around like crazy earlier, hunting down entities.

He said he had breached twelve spatial segments before reaching me, and five of them had erupted into brawls like that.

More than enough to wear anyone out.

So Team Leader Cha set his weapon down for a moment and sat back on a chair in the waiting room.

“We’ll use the same method the Deputy Director used to escape the nascent-phase 「Hotel」.”

Based on what we had observed while moving around so far, the 「Railway Station」 was also following the exact characteristics of a nascent-phase Special Zone.

Thus, Cha Sieon intended to find the 「Railway Station」 from the time period when the Special Zone formed' and escape through it.

Since it was an already proven escape method, the judgment itself was sound.

However, the problem was...

“If we leave now, the chances are high that we won’t be able to get back in.”

“Most likely.”

Cha Sieon readily agreed with my statement.

I understood Manager Kwon Museop's words—that 'you can tell the Special Zone is using its brain'—down to my very bones.

Seeing it warp space and pour down bombardments, there was no denying the malice of the things hostile to us.

Along with their clear sense of purpose.

Type 1 Special Zone does not want us to defeat the 「Railway Station」.

In that case, the probability of them locking the 「Railway Station」 in this state was high.

Just like how the 「Island」 was tightly hidden away without opening again.

From a human perspective, this could be considered a victory of sorts.

It meant that a few spaces that used to lure and kill humans were now semi-permanently closed.

However, if that happens, our initial goal will never be achieved.

Lee Sol would remain abandoned just like this.

As if noticing my thoughts, Cha Sieon opened his mouth.

“The top priority for this operation is the survival of everyone. And if we die, there is no one left to protect Lee Sol.”

This was also true.

“So right now, getting out alive is our priority. Besides, the information we've secured this time is not insignificant. Using this achievement as a stepping stone, we can explore the next operation again.”

At Team Leader Cha's words, I had no choice but to nod.

Right, excess is as bad as deficiency; it's about selection and concentration.

If I get greedy just because the situation has improved a bit, I'm bound to meet disaster instead.

Deciding to take a break and cut things short will be necessary too.

“The 「Railway Station」 does not have its spaces divided by doors from the waiting room to the platform. Therefore, if we try to escape through the platform, we might become targets of the 「Giant Siren」. So this time, we will move to the 「Circulating Library」 and escape via the 「Library」.”

I nodded at Cha Sieon's explanation.

15 minutes later, we stood up from our seats.

To move to the 「Circulating Library」, we first need a book.

And the place with the most books in the 「Railway Station」 is the 2nd-floor business center.

We need to find a painting connected to the 「Circulating Library」 there.

We arrived at the business center and opened the door.

However, stars were twinkling outside the door.

“……?”

Stars twinkling in the deep blue void.

And clouds scattered like feathers.

It wasn't difficult to deduce what the spectacle before our eyes was.

“Isn't this... the night sky?”

“…….”

That's right, this was the night sky.

The problem, however, was that this pretty night sky was spread out right where an indoor space should be.

Cha Sieon carefully observed the scene, then took a candy out of his pocket and threw it outside.

*Whoosh-*

The candy flew sideways instead of falling down.

Then, without hitting anything anywhere, it drifted infinitely far away and disappeared.

*Clack-!*

Cha Sieon shut the door as if he had seen something he shouldn't have.

Then, he calmly opened the door once more.

“…….”

Fortunately, the night sky had disappeared.

Instead, a blue sky was visible this time.

Glancing downward, clouds were spread out far below.

It was a scenery that could only be seen by riding an airplane.

*Thwack-!*

This time, Cha Sieon closed the door without even trying to throw anything through it.

Then he spoke to me as if nothing had happened.

“Let’s try another door.”

We opened the door of the station security center next to the business center.

Then a bright light greeted us.

Fortunately, the heat did not cross over to our side.

*Rumble- Rattle-*

Filling the other side of the door was red lava, writhing and moving.

“……”

Cha Sieon took out a candy once again.

Just how many candies were inside Team Leader Cha’s pockets?

While I was having such leisurely thoughts, Cha Sieon casually tossed a candy into the lava blocking the door.

*Sssss-*

The candy melted away nicely.

Cha Sieon turned back to look at me and asked with an absurd expression.

“What on earth is the meaning of this?”

“Are you asking me that?”

That was when it started.

The moment we began wandering around the 「Railway Station」, frantically opening and closing doors.

However, every single door there betrayed our expectations and revealed bizarre spaces to us.

Rock faces, inside the dirt, the deep sea, the interior of a living creature's body, and so on.

Places that we could not enter, or places that would mean certain death if we did, kept appearing.

Even the very door that Cha Sieon had been using normally just before this was no exception.

Three hours passed like that.

Only after opening and closing doors thousands, perhaps over ten thousand times, did we realize.

It seemed we were trapped here.

“Team Leader.”

“Yes.”

“By any chance, did you pass along my request to destroy my will?”

Cha Sieon quietly turned to look at me.

It was an expression that said, *As if I would.*
