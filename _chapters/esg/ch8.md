<br>"There's nothing particularly wrong with it."

"What did you say?"

"It's something we've been doing all along, so what's the problem? You said public opinion has changed, too."

"You're more shameless than I thought."

"I apologize, but my life is on the line right now."

I can see a chance of survival, so I won't just die.

I looked at the woman with confidence, and she looked back at me, then let out a laugh.

"Amazing, you're a kid who knows how to throw a tantrum like this."

The woman muttered to herself.

I was treated like a child throwing a tantrum, but this wasn't the time to be embarrassed.

I confirmed that the woman's atmosphere wasn't particularly bad and continued speaking.

"Besides, our Director was really on the verge of death, and now you're being strict all of a sudden, which seems inconsistent."

"……."

However, when I added that, the woman's smile became somewhat subtle.

The woman looked at me with ambiguous eyes, as if she had a lot to say.

Then, she whispered in a voice that seemed to be holding back laughter.

"You don't know, so it's pitiful."

"……?"

As the woman spoke in a way that made no sense, the area behind her also sparkled.

What is this noise?

The woman changed her tone and spoke again.

"Besides, that's not something I did."

"What do you mean……."

Now that I think about it, that woman and 「Crow」 are separate entities.

It was probably 「Crow」 that revived Director Go.

"The method is different, too."

"What method?"

"The method of reviving your Director. That's not really reviving, but rather skipping death. So, it's fundamentally different from what I did to you. That's a crude trick that uses space."

What is she talking about?

"Then, is Director not revived?"

"Rather than being revived, he's more like someone who never died."

"……?"

When I asked in surprise, the woman explained with a generous attitude.

"Think of life as a thread."

She nodded.

"When you're alive, that thread will continue to follow time. And if that thread is cut off at some point, that's what you consider death."

Death. She nodded again.

"What I did to revive you was to unwind the cut thread and reconnect it one by one. But that 「Crow」."

The woman smiled for a moment as she mentioned 「Crow」.

"That 「Crow」 didn't reconnect the cut thread. Instead, it pulled the cut end and covered up the time period when the thread was cut."

Covered up?

"That way, it made it as if the event of the thread being cut never happened."

Reconnecting the cut thread and erasing the fact that it was cut.

The former is the method the woman used on me, and the latter is the 「Crow」's method.

So, the 「Crow」's method is why there's a 13-second gap in Director Go's time.

But...

"Isn't erasing the fact that it was cut better?"

"You're annoying, trying to justify it after I went through the trouble of reconnecting it."

I realized my mistake and closed my mouth.

Is there some kind of philosophy behind the method of reviving humans?

I glanced around, worried that I might have made a mistake.

The woman smiled again.

"It's a joke. Well, from your perspective, it might seem that way. But that's really a crude trick. It's a cheat that can only be used in the Spring of Memories, or the state you call nascent-phase Special Zone, where time distortion is easy to occur."

It's true that opening a door to pull out a dead person's 13 seconds prior is only possible in the nascent-phase Special Zone.

Of course, that requires the help of beings like 「Crow」 or this woman.

But the method used to revive me is completely different...

"Then, what about the method used to revive the Bureau Agents earlier?"

"Hmm?"

"You opened a door and pulled out the people who were about to die, but isn't that the same trick 「Crow」 used?"

"Correct."

"You just called it a 'crude trick'..."

"If there's an easy way, you should use it."

The woman answered my question calmly.

"And my breath is not something I give to just anyone. You're using it without realizing its value, but."

The woman scolded me, her tone similar to a parent chastising their child.

Is this being naturally affectionate towards humans, or is she treating me specially?

It's probably the latter.

She said, "My breath is not something I give to just anyone."

It seems that her breath is some kind of material or ability used to revive humans.

I thought about the woman's new words.

I still don't know what differences there are in the methods of reviving, or what's important.

But I can tell that this woman is being kind to me.

So, I decided to be even more shameless and asked.

"If you've gone to the trouble of reviving me, wouldn't it be better to let me live a bit longer?"

*Sparkle, sparkle-*

The lights responded to my words again.

Thank you, everyone.

And the woman, still smiling at me.

The atmosphere isn't bad.

Maybe I can get away with this...

As I was thinking about my chances, the woman, who had been looking at me with Author Na Bit's face, spoke again.

"You lack consistency. Your request right now is..."

"Consistency is..."

"Do you remember what you thought when you died this time?"

Thought?

"You said your death was 'just something that happens'."

The woman's words made me quietly surprised.

Then, she burst out laughing.

"Don't be surprised. That's what reviving someone means. To revive you, I had to put your pieces back together one by one. So, I know. I know more about you than you do yourself."

Her words made my heart feel strange, as if something had been exposed.

I felt like I'd been found out, and the woman looked at me with a hint of pity.

"I know how much you fear death."

"……."

"So, I'm asking you about your attitude, which lacks consistency even more than my whims."

"What do you mean……."

"Being revived means you'll die again. For humans, experiencing death once is enough, but you're willing to go through it again. Why are you so desperately begging for your life?"

"Because I want to live."

"Don't lie."

The woman denied my words.

And without giving me a chance to retort, she said.

"If you really wanted to live, you wouldn't have sent your friends away. Well, that's in the past. Right now, you're asking me for mercy, but is that really because you want to live?"

The woman looked at me, and I couldn't meet her gaze.

But even without looking, I could sense her eyes piercing through me.

I finally realized why she had been laughing all along.

It was because I was pathetic.

I was trying to negotiate with her, but she saw right through me.

I regretted my pointless attempt and confessed.

"Yes, it's not just about living. I want to do what I've been doing."

"What's that?"

"My work."

"Is it the same as what you said earlier? Now that you've sent your friends away, you want to save Lee Sol, too?"

"……Yes."

The woman laughed again.

I guess she thinks I'm shameless.

It's like I'm asking for more after being saved from drowning.

But I've already become shameless, so I asked again.

"If you've gone to the trouble of reviving me, couldn't you just let me live a bit longer? And I think this story will be interesting. Many things are intertwined, after all."

I tried to speak as calmly as possible, so I wouldn't seem like I was begging.

And I prepared myself for her criticism.

But instead, I received an unexpected response.

"I've already seen it."

"……?"

"I've seen that you'll save Lee Sol."

The woman was still smiling, and her tone was affectionate.

But her calm statement made me feel a strange, unsettling feeling.

As I stood there, speechless, the woman added.

"I put you in the 「Railway Station」 for that reason."

I didn't understand immediately, so the woman explained again, as if pitying me.

"I put you in the 「Railway Station」 because I wanted to see that."

I looked up at the woman, and for some reason, I was no longer afraid of the abyss.

The woman's revelation had taken away my fear.

I stared at the woman for a while before finally speaking.

"You're not「Crow」."

"Yes, 「Crow」 is 「Crow」."

What is she talking about?

It was 「Crow」 that brought me to the Special Zone.

But the woman said she was the one who brought me to the 「Railway Station」.

Was it this woman who caused the initial incident that messed up my life?

"You can resent me."

The woman smiled and said.

"I'm a bit sorry to you, too. That's why I've been keeping you alive until now, but I think it's time to stop."

"……You can't decide that unilaterally."

I barely managed to speak, unsure whether I was feeling resentment or anger.

The woman tilted her head and countered.

"As I said earlier, my rights over you are all legitimate. I've already paid the price for bringing you here."

What price could she have paid, and what have I been through?

"If you had chosen to live as a normal editor, I might have revived you again. But not you, as you are now."

The woman was still being kind to me, speaking in a gentle tone and smiling.

"You won't need me to fill your life with breath from now on, right?"

As she spoke, I felt a chill run down my spine.
