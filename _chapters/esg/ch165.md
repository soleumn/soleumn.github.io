<br>“There’s no need for me to replenish every single life you use as a tool. Isn't that right?”

I could not properly follow the words the woman poured out.

What on earth was she talking about?

She was the one who dragged me into this hell.

Feeling a shred of responsibility for that, she had kept me alive until now, but now she was going to quit.

And the reason was that I had not spared my life in this living hell.

I had thought of myself as an abandoned animal waiting to be euthanized, and that turned out to be an accurate description.

Picking me up as she pleased, using me, pretending to care for me for a while, and now deciding to kill me.

It was so absurd that I couldn't even get angry properly.

Instead, in the midst of extreme confusion, I muttered without realizing it.

“Why me, of all people?”

“You were there.”

“……”

I felt something crumble inside.

At the same time, the suppressed anger finally surged up.

For a moment, I couldn't even breathe.

“……I was only acting according to the situation as well.”

Yet I barely gathered my reason and feigned composure.

“I, too, want to live as an ordinary editor. But you made a mess of my situation, and because that situation hasn't ended, I had no choice but to do this. I'm struggling just to process it all somehow, so what on earth are you talking about…….”

But the more I spoke, the more my emotions churned.

In the end, unable to finish my sentence, I shut my mouth.

I kept thinking.

Why me, of all people.

What did I do wrong.

I couldn't bring myself to believe that something like this had happened for no reason at all.

So I searched for a reason.

I had even blamed an innocent God.

And yet, this was all it was.

Because an absurdly unreasonable being had discovered me.

That was all.

The more I thought about it, the more I felt like I was losing my mind.

I gritted my teeth to suppress that feeling, then spoke up again.

“……You said I could hold a grudge.”

“Yeah.”

The woman nodded willingly.

I said to that being, whose kindness or cruelty I couldn't tell.

“I won't hold a grudge. Instead, help me one more time. I know you've helped until now, but it was all done unilaterally by yourself. I'm the victim who suffered the damage, so why are you helping alone and exempting yourself from responsibility alone? This doesn't make sense.”

“And if it doesn't make sense?”

“Comply with my demand at least once. Then I won't hold a grudge either. Take responsibility with that.”

Holding a grudge or anything of the sort wouldn't improve the situation.

So I stated the best thing I could do.

The woman smiled softly toward me and replied.

“As expected, you're wrong.”

It was a matter-of-factly affectionate voice.

“I won't keep you alive, and you cannot overturn my decision.”

“……That's irresponsible.”

“I am fickle, but not irresponsible. The irresponsible one isn't me, it's you. Yet you act all diligent. While turning away from what you actually need to take responsibility for, and clinging to things you don't need to.”

“…….”

“What you need to take responsibility for right now is none other than the promise we made. You don't have the authority to break that promise.”

The woman only had an affectionate voice.

With that kind of voice, as if she would grant anything, the woman began to stoke despair.

“And you aren’t a story that must be completed. Look, did the deaths here have a perfect narrative arc?”

As if reacting to the woman’s words, the space surrounding me turned into ruins.

A space entirely shattered, entirely burnt.

And countless humans, burned like trees.

“Having a narrative arc all the way to completion and tying up every piece of foreshadowing is something only possible inside narrow stories. The causality of this world isn’t that simple. There are lives that simply end in misfortune, and you are no exception. Nor are all such stories unfinished. It’s merely a common tragedy, overflowing in this world.”

At the woman’s words, I let out a breath without realizing it.

Yet the woman paid no mind and continued speaking.

“Besides, you already know it well. That this is ‘just one of those things that happens.’ That humans only desperately assign meaning to things because they cannot bear their own worthlessness.”

“…….”

I realized why this thing had taken the author's appearance.

Did it want to tell me that my life was meaningless using the form of the author who had taught me how to live?

If that truly was its intention, perhaps I should applaud its brilliance.

“If you survive and make it out of here despite all of this, would that truly be a plausible story?”

I merely looked at the woman.

Yet this time, too, the woman smiled as if she had already heard my answer.

And my body plummeted into the abyss.

It was because the single line holding me had snapped.

<br>

I was running.

With a young body not even ten years old, I ran breathlessly.

Just then, something blocked my path.

As I ran, I fell straight into its embrace.

The one holding me in her arms was a woman.

Something in the form of a certain Author I knew well.

Still panting for breath, I looked up at the woman with confused eyes.

Holding my young self tightly in both arms, the woman asked.

“Why are you running like that?”

“There’s someone over there!”

“A kid named Lee Sol?”

Wanting her to release her arms, I nodded hurriedly.

Yet the woman ignored my urgency and murmured nonchalantly.

“Is that your final lingering attachment?”

“……?”

“Why is that child holding onto you this much?”

What was she asking?

Not knowing what she meant, I looked up at the woman.

The woman slowly released my body.

Yet, without even thinking to run again, I stood still in that spot.

The woman lowered her body and rested her hands on her knees.

Locking eyes with me like that, she asked again.

“The friends who came with you did have plenty of resentment built up. So their motives and plausibility were sufficient. But what about you?”

What about me?

“Why are you getting mixed up in that and grinding yourself away? You said so with your own mouth. That kid is nothing to you, and you're not a hero.”

The woman's question was too difficult.

No, rather than the question, my answer was what was difficult.

So instead of answering, I asked the woman back.

“Why won't you let me go?”

“To save you.”

“Save me?”

“I'll make sure you aren't murdered by you.”

“…….”

“So tell me. Do you want to be a hero, by any chance?”

“It's not that.”

“Or is it out of pity?”

“That's not it either.”
I shook my head repeatedly at what the woman had assumed.

“Then why are you doing this?”

At the woman's question, I hesitated before finally opening my mouth.

“The situation can't be helped, but actions can be chosen.”

At my words, the woman pondered for a moment.

The woman thought it over carefully and asked back.

“Are you perhaps choosing the most ideal action?”

“No.”

When I shook my head, the woman looked at me again.

As if asking for a proper explanation.

However, young as I was, I couldn't explain this well enough.

So I merely struggled to recite the sentences within me.

“The situation can't be helped, but actions can be chosen. But my choice is someone else’s situation.”

Seeking the woman’s understanding, I said again and again.

“Because of my choices, other people's situations change.”

Did she understand what I meant?

A smile spread across the woman’s lips.

The woman asked back with a warm smile.

“And so?”

“So, I want to give them a situation they can endure.”

“Why?”

“Because everyone has to fight their own situation.”

“I see.”

The woman nodded.

Clutching the hem of my clothes, I looked at the woman.

For some reason, I waited nervously for her reply.

“Me too. I also want to give you a comfortable death.”

The woman said, embracing me once more.

It was a cozy embrace, but it was not what I wanted.

“So you don't have to run anymore. There won't be any more suffering.”

Held in the woman's arms, I looked beyond her shoulder.

There were still things to do.

I had to get out of this embrace.

So, just as I tried to push the woman away, she whispered in my ear.

“My mercy isn’t something you can refuse.”

Those words ensnared me in an instant.

As if possessing physical form, they stiffened my body, and the woman whispered once more to my frozen self.

“Because now, I am your situation.”

I felt something snap inside me.

As the strength drained from my body, the woman stroked my hair and smiled.

“Good boy.”

I had no choice but to accept it.

There was no other option.

It was because this woman was already my situation.

“Thanks to you, it was fun. You don’t need to be disappointed that your attempt failed. This isn’t your fault. It’s just that I wanted to kill you so very badly.”

At the woman’s consolation, I quietly accepted it.

The woman had made a choice.

My death.

And so, that became my situation.

It was something that happened everywhere in the world.

If you trace a given situation upward, you will always find someone’s choice.

Even a trivial choice turns into an overwhelming situation once it circles the world, and humans call it fate as they are crushed to death beneath it.

I did not fail to understand that truth.

That was why, without resisting any further, I closed my eyes.

“So now, let’s go.”

The woman whispered, cupping my cheek in her hand.

*Press—*

However.

*Press, press—*

“Why are you seriously being like this?”

……Those were my words.

I opened my eyes again at the hands pressing against my cheeks.

I had turned back into an adult, and the woman wearing Author Na Bit’s face was poking and pressing my cheeks with her hands, just as she had done earlier.

“It seems like it’s mostly wrapped up, so why won't you budge? There’s no way the bond could still be maintained to this extent……”

The woman muttered unintelligible words.

Why does this woman seem subtly clumsy, reminding me even more of Author Na Bit……

I looked at the woman with mixed feelings.

I didn't know whether to rejoice that my lifespan had been extended even briefly, or to tell her to just end it quickly if she was going to kill me anyway.

It was then.

*Ch-r-r-r-ak-!*

The dark space surrounding us suddenly moved.

Suddenly, floors were laid and walls were erected, transforming in an instant into the waiting room of the 「Railway Station」.

“……?”

I wasn't the only one who was surprised.

As if this situation was unexpected to her as well, the woman looked around with surprised eyes.

*Zzzt- zzzzt-*

Then, the swell spread behind the woman's back also began to gradually shrink.

As if displaying such power as it did now was no longer possible.

Time and space, which had been wandering wildly in an amorphous state, were solidifying once again.

“Why is the space…….”

It was just as the woman muttered in confusion.

*Boom-!*

A roar heard from afar.

*Bang-! Boom-!*

Was a bombardment falling somewhere?

The woman frowned slightly and opened a nearby door.

Then, looking beyond it, she let out a low sigh.

“Ha.”

Wondering what was going on, I looked beyond the door and was equally flustered.

The person beyond the door was someone I knew all too well.

It was Cha Sieon, who was literally smashing the anomalous phenomena of the nascent-phase 「Railway Station」.

It was a new situation given to me.
