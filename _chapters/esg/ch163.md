<br>I barely understood the woman's words, still in a daze from the shock.

So, she's going to <span class="tooltip-container">euthanize<span class="tooltip-text">Means to end the life of a person or animal in a painless and humane way, usually to relieve suffering from an incurable illness, extreme pain, or old age.</span></span> me now.

Not for any other reason, but because I'm to her liking?

The output value of her kindness is actually murderous intent?

It was a thought process that my brain couldn't comprehend.

I had become a stray animal waiting for euthanasia.

What's more, I had to wait indefinitely for my disposal.

...What is this?

I was quite perplexed by my situation.

I wasn't dying immediately, nor was I locked up or tied down.

Moreover, I had the pistol that Cha Sieon had given me earlier.

"Don't do anything unnecessary."

The woman said, as if she had read my mind.

"You can't escape from me, and you can't harm me either."

"...What do you mean?"

"It's not just an empty phrase. The causality here doesn't work like it did in the world you lived in, where things unfold slowly."

The woman's voice was calm and affectionate.

My attempts at resistance or escape seemed to pose no threat to her.

And her composure wasn't due to carelessness or arrogance.

"You guys can attack my younger sibling because it killed you first. That's why that child became someone who can be killed by you, but I'm not. I'm still intact, and you can't harm me."

Is this the principle that governs them?

"...Then wouldn't it be better to stay intact? If you kill me, you'll also become someone who can be killed."

"No, killing you wouldn't be a flaw or a sin. I'm just taking back the life I gave you."

"......"

"We made a promise, didn't we? You'd stay here and become mine if I spared your friends' lives."

That's right.

The woman had given me a choice, and I had made my decision.

I had to follow through on my promise, no matter what.

Especially since I didn't see any other way out at the moment.

"My ownership of you is clear. So, killing you is my rightful right and perfectly legal. It's this binding that's restricting my rights that's illegal."

The woman's declaration was straightforward.

Simply stating the facts without needing to emphasize them.

I lost my words and stared at the woman, who was smiling at me with a blank expression.

It was a face I knew well, that of a certain author.

It was then that I wondered if this was some kind of nightmare.

The white aura behind the woman began to flash wildly, like camera flashes on a red carpet.

The woman muttered, "It's so noisy."

Noisy?

Right, the flashing was like "talking" or "making a fuss."

"...Are they talking now?"

I asked, recalling what I had heard earlier.

The woman nodded in agreement.

"Yes, the reaction is bad again."

"What reaction?"

"They're all protesting my words, saying I should spare your life."

Is this some kind of internal conflict?

I looked back and forth between the woman and the flashing lights behind her, then spoke in a calm tone.

"Are those lights also conscious beings?"

The woman didn't respond, just smiled and looked at me.

So, I asked again, trying to hide my tension.

"What are those lights, anyway?"

"They're just bored souls."

The woman's casual remark made the lights flash even more wildly.

The woman sighed, looking uninterested.

"It's pointless, so just go in and rest. The fun part is over."

"......"

The woman, who claimed to be the sister of the 「Big Cat」, seemed to have a generous personality, unlike the monster.

She had her own principles and rules.

It didn't seem like she was purely evil.

Moreover, her comment earlier, when she recited my will...

<br>

-Isn't it cruel to mention it and then die?...

<br>

It wasn't a common way of thinking, guessing someone's reaction while reading their will.

It was something that people who worked with the public might do.

Perhaps there's still a chance.

If this woman and the lights are in a relationship where they exchange complaints...

If they're like an author and their readers, showing and demanding something...

Especially since those "bored souls" are fixated on interesting things...

"I disagree, the fun part is just beginning."

I interjected, responding to the woman's words.

"This was just a frustrating part, and now it's finally time to do something. If we stop here, it'll be a waste."

At my words, the lights flashed again.

Hoping that was support for my statement, I continued.

“To finish it this crudely would be discourteous to the peop…….”

A suitable word other than people.

“……to the readers who have been watching until now.”

*Sparkle, sparkle-*

The blinking lights seemed to be applauding.

As if this was not merely my misunderstanding, the woman covered her ears with her hands.

Then she looked at me with somewhat dumbfounded eyes.

“Everyone seems to agree with me, no?”

*Sparkle, sparkle-*

And the woman pressed a hand to her forehead.

The woman seemed to suffer from that flashing for a while, then soon let out a deep sigh.

Then she laughed dryly toward me.

"Are you trying to be an editor even here?"

"It's an occupational disease."

“Wonderful.”

The woman smiled, and I also smiled faintly, though it was a forced smile.

But how much does this woman know about me?

Of course, my previous job isn't a secret.

However, the fact that a non-human entity knows and mentions it is a different story.

And this woman's appearance...

Now, she's Na Bit, the author.

Earlier, she was Baek Sara at the 「National Theater」 and Lee Sol in a surgical gown.

Is it just a coincidence that she's mimicking these appearances?

Moreover, she read my will earlier, a will that hasn't been made public yet.

Is she somehow reading my mind?

I found the woman's abilities unsettling, but I continued to speak, pretending to be unaffected.

"It's literally a disease. When I see authors who just leave their work unfinished and run away, I lose sleep. I think about the readers whose expectations turn to disappointment."

"Mm, but didn't you have any authors like that under your care?"

"No, fortunately, the authors I worked with were all diligent."

Is she asking because she can't read my mind directly?

Why is she asking this, anyway?

And why does she seem relieved by my response?

...Is this woman Na Bit, the author, herself?

No, that's absurd.

I trust that Na Bit is incompetent.

Na Bit couldn't possibly look down on me with such confidence.

Besides, I saw her change her appearance in real-time earlier.

This must be something that's just mimicking my memories.

I shook off the unnecessary suspicion and continued speaking.

"So, even in death, I won't be able to rest. If my story ends like this, it'll be a mess."

"A mess, perhaps, but..."

The woman reacted to the word "mess," which seemed to be a term from our line of work.

"A mess, yes. We've been building up to something, but the payoff is nonexistent. That's worse than not starting at all. The readers who've been following us will have their time wasted."

The woman's expression turned slightly serious, and the lights behind her continued to flash.

Let's think about this.

The woman who declared that she would put me down.

She claimed it was her rightful authority, but that's just nonsense.

Since I agreed to the deal, I have to accept her authority.

...So, what if she doesn't go through with it?

The woman who decided my fate didn't seem particularly solemn or serious.

It was as if this was no big deal for her, just a casual decision.

And the lights behind her, flashing and chattering away...

They wanted an interesting story, and they'd been watching me.

Their tastes didn't seem that different from humans.

Perhaps I could stir them up and reverse the woman's decision.

If her resolve isn't that firm...

I thought this and spoke to the woman, or rather, to the beings she was carrying.

"No, it's a mess. Look, I've just been dragged around, doing nothing, until now. I've finally made it here on my own, and now you're going to end it like this? This isn't a narrative with a beginning, climax, and conclusion; it's just a beginning and a conclusion. You've cut out the most interesting part, which is a fatal mistake, even for an aspiring author."

*Sparkle, sparkle-*

"......"

"If you end it like this, the narrative you've built up from the point you revived me will all be for nothing. What was the point of reviving me if you were just going to end it here? Especially since you gave me a special ability that can stop the core. You should at least give me a chance to use it. Dropping it like this, with so many people watching... It's not just my story anymore; it's not just the author's story. You have to take responsibility, not just claim ownership."

*Sparkle, sparkle-*

"......"

"If I die here, the Bureau will go back to square one. We've already seen that happen several times. Do you want to keep watching that? If you're not on either side and just want something interesting, then sparing me is the better choice. My death here would be a waste, both subjectively and objectively. As an author, you should be able to make that judgment yourself, unless you're a complete beginner."

*Sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle-!*

The lights flashed wildly, and the woman's expression turned gloomy.

It was as if she was wearing Na Bit's face, which made her look even more dejected.

It wasn't that hard to stir her up.

"Stop it, don't complain so much..."

The woman wasn't talking to me, but to the flashing lights.

She looked like she had a headache, shaking her head.

Then, she let out a deep sigh and muttered.

"Could it be?"

"...?"

"The binding that's holding you here."

The woman examined me carefully and said.

“I wondered what was holding onto something that should naturally fall into my hands…… In a certain sense, this is amazing. An occupational disease that overwhelms causality.”

The woman smiled wryly, as if she found it ridiculous.

What is she talking about?

Is she saying that the reason I wasn't taken away earlier was because of my occupational disease?

...No way. I'm not that obsessed with my work.

"Alright, if the reason you're stuck here is your aversion to a mess and incomplete stories, I'll take care of it."

The woman spoke, as if sighing.

Then, she smiled again and asked me.

"So, is it possible for you to survive and make this story more interesting?"
