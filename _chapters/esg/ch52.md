The huge shadow blocking the doorway.

Is that 「Alpha」?

Huh?

Is that really…… 「Alpha」?

This guy really is Jae-gyeom.

“Haa…….”

That was not 「Alpha」, but Cha Sieon.

That Cha Sieon’s pride had been rather hurt because I chose Baek Sara as my assigned instructor.
