<br>I stood frozen like an electrocuted bird before wiping my nose with the back of my hand.

It was an unconscious gesture, but something came away smeared on my skin.

Blood.

A nosebleed was flowing.

“…….”

As I stood there receiving the dripping blood with my hand, Kwon Museop hastily handed me a tissue from the table.

“What’s wrong, are you okay?”

“My memories…….”

My eyes stung.

With a pain in my head as if it were burning up, I closed my eyes tightly and continued.

“……I remember. Back then, I saw the “three creatures”.”

“What?”

At that time.

The moment I escaped from 「Sports Complex」.

I hadn't been dragged off to various places in Special Zone.

Because entities from different domains had come to see me, space had overlapped.

“Multiple spaces were overlapping. And they were right in front of me.”

A skinless four-legged beast bearing the Type 1 Special Zones.

“「Big Cat」.”

A giant horned black silhouette riding upon a brilliant world like something out of a game.

“A horned monster.”

And a man stepping into a supreme dimension that one dared not even look upon.

“And a man was looking down at me.”

The memories of that moment came alive before my eyes like an illusion.

Through the gaps of fragmenting space, 「Big Cat」 snarled at me.

GRRKKKK—!

A bizarre, nervous cry.

That sound overlapped with what Ryu Dana had translated for me some time ago.

<br>

-I'll tear you to shreds! Seo Doun, I'll tear you apart piece by piece! You demon-like bastard!

<br>

「Big Cat」 was filled with hatred.

Why?

The me back then hadn't done anything yet.

I had simply been dragged into Special Zone and was just busy trying to survive.

I had never attacked 「Big Cat」, nor had I touched the core of the Type 1 Special Zone.

Yet 「Big Cat」 was already showing intense hostility toward me.

<br>

-Wait, don't touch him right now.

<br>

The man stopping it.

His face was not visible.

Yet the delicacy of that attitude was branded into my mind.

<br>

-I should go back, I'm hungry.

<br>

And the giant horns, muttering indifferently from afar while standing by as a spectator.

<br>

-I'll kill you. I'll kill you! Die, die, die die die die!

-Not yet.

<br>

Despite the man's restraint, 「Big Cat」 ultimately put me in its mouth and chewed.

CRUNCH—!

Sitting in my chair, I stomped my foot without realizing it.

RATTLE-!

Kwon Museop said something to me, but I could barely hear it.

My nosebleed poured out uncontrollably.

And, what happened?

Ah, I remembered.

I died.

I died in agony, and I fell.

To a pitch-white space colder than death.

There, I met someone.

<br>

-Author……?

<br>

A woman smiling strangely at me.

Some existence with the face of Author Na Bit.

The me back then didn't doubt that woman was Na Bit.

So groaning at the sight of that face, I realized it once again.

That meeting her wasn't the first time.

That even when I died for the very first time, she had come to find me just like this.

<br>

-What is the meaning of this……? Why haven't I remembered…… until now…….

<br>

I asked in a distant despair.

How could I forget something like that.

No, is it even right that I remember everything now?

I don't know.

I don't know.

It was a promise, a bargain, and a contract.

Yet I don't remember.

I even forgot the fact that I had forgotten.

Stuck with a helpless feeling that tangled up my brain, I desperately asked.

<br>

-Just what are you, really?

-Aren't I a god to you? Since I made you and brought you back to life several times.

<br>

How had I reacted to the woman's playful reply?

“Ugh……!”

I hurriedly covered my mouth.

The me in my memory acted the exact same way.

It was because her words made me recall further.

My death.

Those miserable deaths.

The worst memories a living organism could possibly hold.

Some deaths are a form of rest, but others are not.

The deaths I experienced were not like that.

They were acts of splitting a seed to force out a sprout, acts of breaking an egg to let a fledgling spill out and ruin.

I died like that, came back to life, and so I died again.

I groaned at the belatedly recalled pain.

<br>

-Why of all people me……?

<br>

At my resentful question, the woman looked at me with warm eyes.

Then she beamed brightly and answered.

<br>

-Because I made you.

<br>

I scratched my arm with my fingernails.

Don't forget, remember.

Telling myself that, I left wounds on my arm.

<br>

-You don't have to struggle like that.

<br>

Na Bit smiled as if pitying me.

Looking at my blood-stained arm, she whispered gently in my ear.

<br>

-You'll find out everything anyway. When the time comes for you to know.

<br>

With those final words, I ultimately lost consciousness.

Even within my memories.

Even in reality.

<br>

When I opened my eyes again, I was lying on a bed.

Next to the white ceiling, an IV bag was visible.

Feeling a discomfort on the back of my hand, I saw an IV needle stuck into it.

“Doun, are you awake?”

The person asking was Kim U-in.

Behind him, Cha Sieon and Kwon Museop were also visible.

“How long have I been lying here?”

“It hasn't been long. It's only been 10 minutes since you got here, and your nosebleed has stopped.”

If it was that much, it was about the time it takes to briefly close and open your eyes.

I sighed in relief and spoke to Cha Sieon, who was standing by the wall.

“Team Leader, that recording file from earlier. Please play it for me just one more time.”

“Doun, you should rest a bit right now…….”

“It'll only take a moment. There's something I need to check.”

Kim U-in tried to stop me before letting out a sigh and stepping back.

Cha Sieon approached my side holding his tablet.

Lying in the hospital bed, I lent my ears once again to the sound coming from that tablet.

“So you're saying none of you can hear the woman's voice.”

People nodding at my question.

“I don't know the reason, but I can hear it. This was a sound I couldn't hear originally either.”

That's why I had asked an infected person named Ryu Dana to interpret this sound.

In that case, did I become an infected person just like Ryu Dana?

However, they said that not every infected person could read the broken text of Special Zones or hear the voices of entities.

Besides.

“Yet I still can't understand the sounds of the other things.”

I dragged the playback bar of the recording file forward.

SCREEEE—

TSSSSS—

HSSSS—

A bizarre noise rang out.

It was the sound made by the things that had looked down at me.

Those moments were now clearly remembered, but that was all.

The sounds they made were still just noise.

“It seems I've only become able to understand the woman's voice.”

Messages left by Lee Seop who appeared at the nascent-phase 「Hotel」.

What on earth did he want to say to repeat the word "They" like that?

Knowing whether the contents that follow would be revealed once the blinds are lifted was the Bureau's biggest interest these days.

Looking at it now, there were a few other noticeable sentences as well.

‘Everyone is watching you.’

If the "you" in this sentence is really me, this "everyone" might refer to those lights that were twinkling behind the sister of 「Big Cat」.

The "golden calf" is the horned thing I saw.

The "weeping lion" is 「Big Cat」.

Then what about the "great angel" and the "Bird that guides souls"?

It seemed plausible to substitute the man who stopped 「Big Cat」 and the woman who was 「Big Cat」's sister, but it was unknown which one corresponded to which.

More than that, it bothered me that it was specifically an "angel."

As I examined those cryptic messages, I closed the tablet and swallowed a sigh.

I wanted to clear my head a bit, but it wasn't easy.

Why on earth did I become able to understand only the woman's voice?

Because of the "breath" that woman talked about?

It felt as though I had somehow become subjugated to that woman, making my stomach churn.

<br>

-So I know. I know enough about you, and some parts I know better than you do.

-How much you fear death, you might not know, but I know.

<br>

The words the woman had said came back to me.

Looking back on them now, there was no lie in those words.

Because of that, I started to get confused.

<br>

-I'm the same. I want to give you a comfortable death, too.

<br>

The thought crossed my mind that perhaps the woman's one-sided and violent mercy really was meant for me.

……No, that's insane.

In the first place, it was that woman who pushed me into that living hell.

To drop a person who was living a normal life down into the abyss, and then call it mercy to offer euthanasia just because they broke down?

I swallowed a deep breath to shake off these unpleasant thoughts.

Just then.

*Tap, tap.*

Along with the sound of knocking on the door, Kwak Jaegyeom's voice could be heard.

"Hyung, can I come in?"

"Yeah, it's fine."

I answered, sitting up on the bed.

Kwak Jaegyeom, who opened the door and walked in in the meantime, said to me with a somehow grinning face.

"How about taking a vacation?"

"Suddenly?"

"Manager told me to ask you? He said if you need a vacation, I should go out with you for a bit."

Such a kind manager.

The moment a kid suddenly vomits nosebleeds, he hands you a vacation right away.

Now that I think about it, it's been a while since I've been home.

Besides, I really needed to drop by home at least once.

My cell phone that I left at home.

There had been an instance where a message was sent to Oh Taeo using that phone.

Because of that, agents went to our house and retrieved the cell phone.

Other people had been coming and going through my house while I was away, so I needed to go and check it out at least once.

"My vacation probably won't be very fun even if you tag along."

I told Jaegyeom, who was already looking forward to it, beforehand.

However, Jaegyeom grinned without caring at all.

"As long as I can skip work, anywhere is fine."

It was a statement I couldn't possibly refute, having once been an office worker myself.
