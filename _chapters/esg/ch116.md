Feeling somewhat strange, I looked at myself on the screen.

It was good that I had handed Lee Sol the leaflet back then.

No, it was fortunate.

It was truly fortunate that I had found Lee Sol.

Seo Doun and Lee Sol on the screen headed toward the Lost and Found Center.

The agents watched us in a considerably lighter atmosphere.

But the situation after that had not gone very smoothly.

“Why is the 「Cloth Doll」 in that state?”

The 「Cloth Doll」 gradually brightened in front of Lee Sol, who was lying face-down.

“Uh……!”

Flames pushed out from the Lost and Found Center.

And black forms.

“What happened? Has a phenomenon like that ever been reported?”

“No, it has not. This is the first time it has been observed!”

The agents shouted in panic.

On the screen, Lee Sol and I began running away.

But the flames spread faster than that, and to make matters worse, most of the CCTV inside 「Railway Station」 went dead.

Now, the only ones operating normally were the CCTVs on the platform.

WEE-OOO—!

And the siren sound rang out again.

The agents flinched and checked the time.

Then they hurriedly counted each other’s numbers.

Only 20 minutes had passed since the previous siren.

So they were counting to see whether anyone had been dragged into 「Railway Station」.

“The two people are back on screen!”

At that moment, the agent examining the screen shouted.

Just as they said, the platform-side CCTV screen caught sight of me and Lee Sol.

And the black forms savagely chasing behind us too.

Back then, I had only run while looking ahead, so I had not realized.

It had been an even more incredible situation than I thought.

Behind us, black masses like a sandstorm were swarming in.

Chased by them, Lee Sol and I ran across the platform.

The agents watched us with their mouths firmly shut.

Of course, they also knew there was only one Ticket.

“What were you thinking back then?”

I asked the current Cha Sieon, who had been silent for a while.

Unexpectedly, Cha Sieon answered readily.

“I was regretting not moving sooner.”

This is why people say he is so desperate to die he has gone mad…….

The February 3 Cha Sieon was watching.

Watching Lee Sol move toward death by the second, his jaw clenched tight.

But something they had not expected happened.

“Ah!”

Lee Sol was thrown into the train.

“Later.”

And my voice transmitted while mixed with static.

“Pay it back to anyone.”

CRUNCH—!

“Mister!”

Along with Lee Sol’s scream, even the final CCTV broke.

The screen went black.

And silence flowed.

The agents froze as they were, having lost their words.

All of them were swept into shock, only staring endlessly at the empty screen.

A girl who moved her steps while swallowing sobs.

Then she found Cha Sieon standing beyond the partition and widened her eyes.

“Uncle……? Is that you, Uncle Sieon?”

Lee Sol asked faintly.

At that, Cha Sieon answered after a long while.

“Yes.”

“Uncle! Call the police, call the police and 119!”

“I was in a strange station, and there is still someone there! Some mister stayed behind there alone because of me. We have to hurry!”

Hzzzt— hzzzt—!

Lee Sol’s face distorted, and noise occurred.

With a face where errors had occurred, Lee Sol kept shouting.

“That mister saved me! Uncle, Uncle……!”

When the staff in protective suits tried to take her away, Lee Sol called for her uncle even more desperately.

But the February 3 Cha Sieon said nothing more.

He did not tell her to be at ease, nor that it was all right now.

Even as the frightened Lee Sol was half-dragged away by the staff, he did not open his mouth until the end.

With that sight as the end, the memory completed reconstruction and collapsed.

“…….”

I looked back and forth between that door and Cha Sieon, who stood silently.

Because the atmosphere somehow felt heavy, I asked jokingly,

“You were her uncle?”

“Almost every member of Kwon Museop’s team was Lee Sol’s uncle.”

Almost?

Ah, Agent Lee Seop would be her dad.

Baek Sara would have been her aunt, or unni.

In any case, you did know her well, you bastard.

Judging by how the past Cha Sieon had acted earlier too.

That child had come out alive from a Special Zone.

But why had the memory just now been full of noise?

Was it because the process itself had been terrible, regardless of the result?

“Mr. Seo Doun.”

“Yes?”

“You asked me before. Whether one becomes a dangerous entity if contamination becomes severe.”

“……?”

Memories from inside here could not be carried outside.

Was that why?

Cha Sieon told me something unexpected.

“What did you say?”

“What on earth does that mean, Team Leader?”

“What do you mean, Team Leader! Then is Lee Sol still……!”

WEE-OOO—!

Inside terrible noise, a siren sound rang out as if tearing my eardrums apart.
