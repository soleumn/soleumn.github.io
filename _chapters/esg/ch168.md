<br>Humans fear everything that causes them pain.

That is why isolation is always near the top of the things humans fear most.

Humans are not built to live in isolation, and they feel extreme pain when they are isolated.

And right now, we are isolated.

“What kinds of spaces have appeared so far?”

“4,071 instances of the sky, 1,911 of underwater, 813 of soil, 705 of rock faces, 426 of lava, 660 of muscle cross-sections, 444 of darkness, and fewer than 100 instances each of vertically dropping corridors, rail-laid tunnels, stalactite caves, empty fields, and the gaps between organs.”

I answered Cha Sieon’s question while scrolling through the photos saved on my tablet.

So far, I had categorized over 10,000 photos.

They were all photos of the scenery beyond the doors.

This place is the waiting room of the 「Railway Station」.

We were isolated here.

In a very bizarre form, at that.

For some inexplicable reason, opening a door would reveal one of the spaces listed earlier.

Randomly, endlessly.

We took pictures to figure out the pattern.

However, after 10,000 attempts, the only fact we figured out was this.

That we were trapped right here.

“It probably isn’t just a coincidence that this phenomenon started right after I joined up with Seo Doun.”

“Most likely.”

“Could it be that 「Big Cat」’s older sister is taking out her anger?”

“That wouldn’t be it. The older sister said this space belongs to 「Big Cat」.”

"If she is an older sister, she could seize her younger sibling's things as much as she likes, could she not?"

"Such tyranny is common in the human world, but the case seems a little different here. The older sister did not seem able to enter the younger sibling's space comfortably either."

"That said, the possibility of this being 「Big Cat 's doing seems even lower."

“My thoughts exactly.”

If 「Big Cat」 could isolate us like this, it would have done so from the start.

Rather than desperately buying time until the bombardment fell.

As I nodded while thinking this, Cha Sieon added.

“However, we cannot say for sure either. Unless this phenomenon is permanent.”

“You mean this is a temporary phenomenon?”

“Yes. Perhaps this is also a way of buying time. Just like earlier.”

Just like earlier, he said.

Did that mean another round of bombardment could pour down after buying time?

The deduction itself was plausible, but the content was too brutal.

So, to silence Team Leader Cha, I muttered a light complaint.

“Team Leader, words can become seed—”

It was right then.

*BOOM-!*

“……!”

Along with a faint roaring sound, a vibration was felt beneath our feet.

As if an explosion had occurred somewhere far away.

Cha Sieon and I deliberately tensed up.

Just in case a bomb really was falling.

About three minutes passed like that.

“……It seems to be a temporary phenomenon.”

Cha Sieon said, breaking the silence.

“Let's rest for a bit. It doesn't seem like opening doors blindly will give us any answers, so from now on, let's make it a rule to open the doors every hour.”

I nodded readily at Cha Sieon's suggestion.

For the past three hours, we had been wandering around opening and closing doors like madmen.

The action itself wasn't difficult, but once it piled up, the physical toll was considerable.

Moreover, it was even worse in that it was labor without an end in sight.

Therefore, it was preferable to limit the start and end times like this.

That was the only way to endure a situation where you never knew when it would end.

I casually sat on the floor, and Cha Sieon handed me some water and a protein bar.

I chewed on it slowly while looking up at the glass ceiling.

“Should we secure a door that leads to a cave or a tunnel and leave it open?”

“Why?”

“Just in case a bomb falls so we can run away.”

“You were the one who said words become seeds.”

“We still need to come up with a countermeasure, though.”

At my answer, Cha Sieon let out an absurd snort.

Then he suddenly changed the topic.

“You said you've died and come back to life several times so far, Seo Doun.”

“According to the older sister. And I also have a few things that come to mind.”

“…….”

I answered nonchalantly before glancing at Cha Sieon.

For some reason, Cha Sieon was just quietly watching me.

Wondering what kind of reaction that was after he brought it up first, I asked him back.

“By the way, Team Leader Cha, didn't you feel anything strange while watching my heart rate?”

“It was irregularly erratic at times. But it went back to normal just before stopping completely, so I suspected arrhythmia.”

<span class="tooltip-container">Arrhythmia<span class="tooltip-text" style="left: 0 !important; right: auto !important; transform: none !important;">A medical condition involving an irregular, too fast, or too slow heartbeat caused by a problem with the heart's electrical signaling system</span></span>…….

Should I think of this as meaning she revived me so quickly that it did not even show up on a heart-rate check?

As I was having these thoughts, Cha Sieon brought up an unexpected remark.

“Actually, there were many agents who felt a sense of self-doubt because of you, Seo Doun.”

“Self-doubt?”

“Comments like ‘What have we even been doing this whole time if a civilian is doing that?’ came up frequently.”

Oh…….

“So there were many agents saying things like ‘Is it luck rather than skill?’ or ‘Is luck a skill, too?’ But if it’s the case that you didn’t just survive by luck, Seo Doun, but rather died and came back to life, in a way, it actually makes sense.”

Cha Sieon's voice was flat.

It wasn't a tone of secretly being relieved or hiding amusement, but rather a voice stating facts as they were.

So I accepted it and replied as well.

“Even when I think about it myself, I felt like I was way too lucky. Though that special treatment seems to be over now.”

“…….”

At my answer, Cha Sieon's gaze lingered on me again.

I wondered why he was acting like this since earlier.

“If you have something you want to say, just spit it out.”

“Does it really not bother you at all, Seo Doun, that you've actually died?”

Cha Sieon struck right at me the moment I finished speaking.

I found it quite absurd and let out a hollow laugh as I replied.

“It doesn't feel good, of course.”

“Does it just end with feeling bad?”

“To be honest, it still doesn't feel quite real. If I had died and seen hell or something, maybe it would, but I don't remember a single thing.”

I said calmly, then recalled what the woman had said earlier.

<br>

-You don't even know how afraid of death you are, but I do.

<br>

……Certainly, it wasn't a situation that could simply be brushed off as 'unpleasant.'

Still, well.

What good would it do to panic now?

“Besides, I'm not the only one who died and came back to life. Knowing I'm not alone gives me courage.”

This time, Cha Sieon looked dumbfounded.

However, I nodded and affirmed my own words.

“And honestly, knowing that I died and came back has its advantages. I used to wonder why only I kept getting dragged into Special Zones to stop cores, but now I feel like I've caught a lead. Frankly, this is better than when I knew nothing at all.”

“Do you believe Seo Doun's unique constitution is due to the fact that you died and came back to life?”

“Isn't that the most likely possibility? There's no other way to explain it.”

Besides, the woman had also said this.

<br>

-My breath isn't something I give to just anyone. Though you use it carelessly, not realizing how precious it is.

<br>

Breath.

I figure the answer probably lies in that word.

Even when God created humans, He breathed life into them, and there are major religions that claim only humans have a soul for that very reason.

Wondering if this was enough of an answer, I looked at Cha Sieon.

Then, Cha Sieon dropped yet another unexpected remark.

“The field team and the agents weren't feeling a sense of self-doubt solely because of Seo Doun's survival capability.”

“……?”

“Actually, many agents expressed doubts about Seo Doun's mental fortitude as well.”

“Why would they…….”

“Because Seo Doun has never once wavered or broken down inside Special Zones.”

“They must have no idea how much I suffer.”

“I think so too. What I've felt while watching Seo Doun until now is that Seo Doun is not a person devoid of fear.”

That goes without saying.

I feel fear.

That's why, being dragged into Special Zones, I've never been okay even once.

“Seo Doun definitely knows terror. The only difference between you and others is that your adaptation and recovery are bizarrely fast. And that level is detached from the realm of the ordinary.”

Suddenly, I found myself being treated like some bizarre anomaly who wasn't normal.

Isolation comes in many forms.

There's physical isolation, social isolation, and the isolation of identity.

So, I'd really appreciate it if they didn't treat me like some endlessly weird person out of nowhere.

“Didn't you, Team Leader, constantly say things like 'Get a grip' on a daily basis? You'd snap at me with things like 'Chaos comes later,' 'Face the situation right now,' so I just did as I was told.”

“I told you to stay calm because it was a situation where staying calm was difficult. But I didn't truly expect you to actually become calm.”

Oh…….

“Yet you managed your emotions and agitation beyond all expectations. Almost like shutting off a machine.”

“That's…….”

“I’ve heard a similar story once before, even before entering this place. Back then, Seo Doun, you said, ‘I ended my inner conflict through a rational process and adapted to the situation.’”

That was true.

When I stopped reacting to the subject of contamination, Cha Sieon had expressed doubt over my heart rate remaining normal.

“I understand the process, but I still have doubts.”

“About what?”

“The main cause of death for citizens isolated in Special Zones is carelessness. It is commonplace for people to lose their lives because they get nervous even after reading the rulebook. There is no way those people didn't think, ‘Let’s stay calm.’ But they fail because they can’t manage it. However, once Seo Doun's agitation switches over, you almost never get nervous again. That remains the same even now. Are you currently facing the situation properly?”

“I think I am.”

“If so, it would be normal to be under severe stress. I say this again, Seo Doun, you are not fearless. You are not someone who doesn't know tension or anxiety. I know that just by looking at your heart rate. But right now, it is right after you found out that you died several times, we are isolated in this space, and we don't know what is going to happen. Is it really possible to have already ‘ended your inner conflict and adapted to the situation’?”

“Are you any different, Team Leader…….”

“I have told you repeatedly, do not compare yourself to me.”

Cha Sieon even clicked his tongue as he spoke.

Right, Team Leader Cha is the Bureau's ace.

He truly wasn't someone to compare myself to.

“So are you saying this with the intent that I should be agitated right now……?”

“There’s no need to deliberately do what you aren’t doing. What I'm curious about right now is how that is even possible.”

“That is…….”

To question someone down to the unit of their thought process.

Thinking it was unfair, I pondered for a moment before answering.

“Because I am an editor……?”

Cha Sieon’s gaze, which had been serious down to the ground, suddenly blurred.

It was the look that said I was spouting nonsense again.
