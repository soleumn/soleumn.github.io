<br>*CRASH—! BOOM—! RATATATA—!*

A series of splendid rang out one after another. 

The waiting room of the 「Railway Station」was swarming with unidentified entities. 

And at its center was Cha Sieon, grinding down anomalies like a shredder. 

If I hadn't known Team Leader Cha, it was a sight that would have left me confused not just about which side was friendly to me, but even which side was on humanity's. 

“……Is that the real him?” 

“Yeah…….” 

Confused, I asked the woman, and she answered somewhat gloomily. 

Thanks to that, I learned that Cha Sieon over there wasn't an illusion, but my confusion did not end. 

If that really was Cha Sieon, how did he get in here? 

This place, the 「Railway Station」, had connected with the nascent-phase 「Island」 and taken on the characteristics of a nascent-phase Special Zone along with it. 

So the path entering from the outside should have been blocked as well—that was our hypothesis. 

Could the passage still be open? 

……No, wait. 

He's alone right now.

Because he was smashing his surroundings so nonchalantly, I had overlooked it, but Cha Sieon was currently acting alone. 

No way. 

No, it wasn't just "no way." 

If he had regrouped outside and re-entered, he naturally would have come in as a team. 

There was no way he would be roaming around alone like that. 

I was slowly overcome with horror at the realization that dawned on me. 

Did that bastard jump off the train……? 

Following me? 

“Ha…….” 

Is he completely insane? 

No, after I went through the trouble of reviving him and sending him out, he should have just left quietly……. 

I stared blankly at that rebel before suddenly snapping back to my senses. 

If so, then the bond that was supposedly keeping me tied here was……. 

At the thought that crossed my mind, I turned back to look at the woman. 

The woman whose expression hadn't looked good for a while now. 

And the woman's white surge, which was shrinking smaller and smaller, contrasted with the lights flashing clamorously. 

As every circumstance seemed to point to a single truth, I cautiously spoke up. 

“Isn't this different from what we agreed on?” 

“…….” 

At my words, the woman's mouth fell open into an 'O' as she stared at me. 

And the lights sparkled even larger and more noisily. 

That blinking looked almost as if they were bursting into laughter. 

*Sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle-!*

I continued speaking, fueled by their enthusiastic reactions.

“You promised to save 'all' my friends and send them out. But why is that person acting like that over there?”

“……Is that how you're going to play it?”

The woman muttered, sounding aggrieved.

“It's not my fault your friend is a lunatic.”

“Anyway, it's a fact that you broke your promise.”

“Ha…….”

The promise the woman made to get her hands on me.

I will let your friends out alive.

Using this promise, the woman asserted her ownership and rights over me.

However, that promise had been shattered quite neatly.

By Team Leader Cha, who was rampaging instead of leaving this place.

As if confirming my rough guess, the woman sighed in a voice mixed with dismay.

“I was wondering what this binding was all about…….”

That was it.

The reason the woman couldn't take me away.

She had tried twice, but ultimately failed to carry it out because she hadn't fulfilled her promise yet.

Whether it was by the woman's own volition or against it didn't matter.

What mattered right now was that Cha Sieon was here.

And that meant our promise had become void.

“This is truly absurd.”

The woman lamented into the empty air.

Then, as if she couldn't understand it at all, she asked me.

“Are you close with him?”

“No, not really.”

“Then why?”

“I think it’s just that person's personality.”

“Ah, I'm upset.”

*Sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle-!*

Behind the woman saying she was upset, lights flashed almost as if they were running wild.

Everyone seemed to be exploding with dopamine at this twist.

*Bzzzt- Bzzt-bzzzt-*

And the space where that frenzied light gathered was steadily shrinking.

The place we were in right now was the domain of 「Big Cat」.

The woman couldn't come here, so she said she dragged her own domain along.

And that was precisely the pure white gap resembling wings.

However, seeing that it was gradually closing, it seemed she didn't have much time left to stay here.

“It's a pity.”

The woman murmured, sounding regretful.

As expected, the woman had to leave this place now.

Giving up on me, who was still bound to this space, just like this.

Fine, it seems I survived……!

It was when I was inwardly cheering at this fact.

*Crash-!*

The woman suddenly pounced on me.

I fell backward without even a chance to react, and the woman climbed on top of me, wrapping her hands around my throat.

“……!”

Startled by the grip tightening around my neck, I grabbed the woman's wrists.

However, I couldn't shake those hands off.

Those slender wrists didn't budge an inch, as if they were cemented in place.

In that position, the woman applied pressure with her fingertips and began to gradually choke off my breath.

“Ugh……!”

Panicked, I glared up at the woman.

The woman was looking down at me expressionless.

She wasn't smiling, nor was she angry.

She simply looked down at me, delicately squeezing the life out of my windpipe.

What is this? The deal was already…….

Could it be, does she plan to just kill me?

Throwing out agreements and bonds alike?

With a spine-chilling premonition, I struggled desperately.

However, my actions were nothing more than a meaningless thrashing.

Unlike me, desperate, the woman strangled my neck without the slightest hint of effort, and I slowly began to suffocate.

Damn it, let go……!

Over top of me as I thrashed my body to survive, the woman lowered her upper body.

I looked in despair at the woman's face drawing closer to me.

That face gradually blurred.

I could see the lights the woman carried shimmering.

It was as beautiful as stars perched across the Milky Way. 

At the very moment my consciousness was about to fade away. 

The woman whispered into my ear. 

“I’ll let you go this time.” 

Suddenly, the pressure released. 

“Cough! Gasp, gasp...!” 

Panting heavily, I twisted my body. 

Then I scrambled backward to escape from beneath the woman, but there was no need. 

By the time I pulled myself up, the woman had already vanished. 

Only I was left here, gasping for breath. 

Death had passed me by. 

My death had been certain, but the situation had changed. 

Realizing this reality, I touched my neck. 

Then I raised my head and looked beyond the door. 

The person whose choice had changed my situation was still utterly absorbed in smashing everything around him.

*BANG-! BOOM-* 

At last, the deafening roar ceased. 

The vibrations that had lingered like aftershocks gradually subsided as well. 

Realizing that the space beyond the door had fallen silent, I cautiously poked my head out. 

Team Leader Cha was looking around like a conqueror from the center of the waiting room. 

I wanted to join him right away, but stepping out rashly might get me shot. 

Calling out to him in a loud voice all of a sudden was just as dangerous.

So I lingered near the doorway and waited for Cha Sieon to spot me.

After a little while.

Cha Sieon's gaze, scanning the perimeter, landed on me.

As if waiting for this moment, I frantically waved both arms and made it clear I knew Cha Sieon.

“Team Leader!”

Cha Sieon raised his gun at me for a moment, but upon confirming my face, he turned the muzzle aside.

“Team Leader, it's me!”

I shouted once more, raising both my hands to show him.

Then, Cha Sieon, who had been observing me from afar, began striding toward this direction.

It seemed he recognized me.

Perhaps because I had narrowly escaped death, I felt an inexplicable sense of relief.

To think this person could look so welcoming.

If he asks for the passcode, I should tell him I'm glad to meet him again.

With these thoughts, I walked toward Cha Sieon to meet him halfway.

However, as the distance closed and I reached a range where I could make out his expression.

I instinctively stopped in my feet.

What is this? The atmosphere is somehow…….

Just as I felt something was seriously wrong.

Cha Sieon reached right in front of me with rapid strides.

And then.

*Thud-!*

“……!?”

A heavy impact struck down on the top of my head.

Caught off guard, I took the blow squarely and froze in shock.

My head throbbed with a dull ache. 

However, the mental shock was far more overwhelming than the physical pain. 

So, I stared blankly at Cha Sieon with a dazed face. 

What was that just now? Did he hit me? 

Did he strike the top of my head with the edge of his hand? 

Team Leader Cha? Me? But I’m not Kwak Jaegyeom......? 

I couldn't believe what had just happened to me. 

As I looked at Cha Sieon with trembling, earthquake-like eyes, Cha Sieon monotonously muttered. 

“I thought you were the enemy.” 

“Don't lie!” 

I, who had been frozen solid, finally came to my senses and shouted angrily. 

However, Cha Sieon did not offer any particular explanation. 

No, he didn't even pretend to listen. 

It was as if to say, so what are you going to do about it? 

I stared stupidly at Team Leader Cha again, then barely managed to calm my mind with two sighs and a deep breath. 

Right, what does getting hit on the head once matter right now? 

It's true that I was being annoying, so let's let that slide and figure out the situation first. 

“What is going on?” 

“What on earth is going on with you?” 

“Pardon?” 

“First you suddenly jump off the train telling me to destroy the will, and then what, you show up with a fine face and pretend to be glad to see me?”

Though I had been genuinely glad to see him, for some reason I ended up feeling awkward. 

Somehow, the atmosphere was more serious than I had thought……. 

No, it wasn't just serious, it was outright grave. 

Cha Sieon was royally pissed off, and he showed no signs of letting it slide depending on the situation. 

As I belatedly began reading the room, Team Leader Cha continued his interrogation. 

“Are you out of your mind, or have you been contaminated? Whichever it is, what on earth is this situation?” 

“There were circumstances.” 

“So you did this of your own sound mind? When you pull off solo acts like that, what are the rest of the personnel supposed to do?” 

“There wasn't time to explain properly. But if I just disappeared without a word, it would cause confusion, so at least to the Team Leader…….” 

“Is that why you summoned people and then jumped off the train?” 

“…….” 

At Cha Sieon's words, I belatedly realized my mistake. 

Earlier, there really hadn't been any time or leeway. 

So I had just done what was urgent, and only now did I realize what kind of situation my choice had created. 

And I also realized what kind of choice Cha Sieon had made, being pushed into that situation. 

There was no room for excuses here. 

“I'm sorry.” 

“Hoo…….”

I admitted my fault, and before me, Cha Sieon only let out a deep sigh. 

As the sound of his breath still seemed quite irritated, I subtly added. 

“You can hit me one more time.” 

“……Forget it.” 

Cha Sieon clicked his tongue as if dumbfounded. 

However, I saw it. 

Cha Sieon's hand rising halfway before dropping back down.
