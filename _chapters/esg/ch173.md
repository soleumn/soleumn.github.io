<br>“To be honest, the beginning made me wonder, ‘What on earth is he doing?’”

“But from the middle, I thought, ‘Oh?’ and by the end, ‘Ah…….’”

“That was the first time I've ever seen a will with a proper introduction, body, and conclusion.”

“This is the true power of a humanities major!”

They read it.

They had definitely read it and were talking about it.

I was utterly dumbfounded—so bewildered I wondered if this was all a dream—but I hid my agitation and asked back.

“How did you......see that?”

The time I was trapped inside 「Railway Station」 and the space bound to it was a mere 57 hours.

It was far too early to confirm my death.

There was no way they would randomly unfold a will while waiting for us to return alive.

If anyone pulled a stunt like that, Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong would roar at them for bringing bad luck.

Then just how?

Why?

In response to my question, the researcher answered.

“The Field Division requested an analysis.”

Analysis?

“Ah, Agent. While we're on the subject, you didn't leave any sort of code in your will, did you?”

“They suddenly told us to analyze it, so we checked the paper and ink components, but nothing special came out of it…… So there was just a mix-up, right?”

“…….”

Team Leader Cha, what is the meaning of this?

I turned to look at Cha Sieon without a word.

In response, Cha Sieon returned a look that suggested it was news to him.

Right, even though Team Leader Cha occasionally exhibited psychopathic tendencies, he wasn't twisted enough to purposefully broadcast to the entire world a will someone had asked him to destroy.

Then what on earth was this situation?

The full story of the incident, as told by the researchers, was as follows.

<br>

<center>*&emsp;*&emsp;*</center>

<br>

Three days ago.

When the agents, excluding me and Cha Sieon, emerged from the 「Railway Station」.

“Seo Doun got off the train?”

Manager Kwon Museop asked back with a stiff face.

Han Seungyo, who was acting as Cha Sieon's deputy, answered.

“Yes. That's why Team Leader Cha also smashed the train window and jumped out. He told us to go ahead first since he would find Seo Doun himself.”

“So the two of them are the only ones left in the Special Zone?”

“Yes, that's correct.”

At Han Seungyo's reply, Kwon Museop let out a low sigh.

Then, with an even stiffer face, he probed for details.

“Why did Seo Doun suddenly get off the train?”

“That hasn't been ascertained.”

“Didn't he say anything before Cha Sieon followed him?”

“It seemed Team Leader Cha hadn't fully grasped the situation yet, either.”

“……And yet he blindly got off the train? And you people just stood there and watched?”

Rage mixed into Kwon Museop's voice.

Just as everyone grew tense at the imminent sign of a fiery temper outburst,

One agent cleverly chimed in.

“Agent Seo Doun told us to check his will!”

With the new information, Manager Kwon Museop swallowed back the anger he was about to unleash.

Taking advantage of the opening, the agent quickly continued.

“I was near the entrance of the passenger car and heard Agent Doun's voice. Before getting off the train, Doun called out to Team Leader Cha and told him to check his will.”

If I had been there, I would have corrected it immediately.

I would have said clearly, ‘It’s not check, it’s destroy’ and ‘Aren’t check and destroy way too far apart in both pronunciation and meaning?’…….

But I wasn't there, and the misunderstanding was smoothly completed.

“So if we check Agent Doun's will, we should be able to figure something out!”

……Humans have two ears and one mouth.

They say that reason means to listen well and speak cautiously.

Unfortunately, there was someone in the Bureau who listened roughly and spoke with absolute conviction.

His name was Jeong Jinu.

<br>

<center>*&emsp;*&emsp;*</center>

<br>

And thus, my will became an object of analysis, passed around the Bureau.

During the two days I went missing.

I felt my sanity slipping away at this unexpected turn of events.

“Then…… you only checked it in Analysis Team 5……?”

I asked, swallowing my agitation, and the researchers simply nodded brightly.

The distribution scope of my will.

The Bureau's command leadership.

The participants of the 「Railway Station」 suppression operation.

And Analysis Team 5.

Was it roughly around this much?

For my words asking them to destroy the will to be distorted and come back like this—

I couldn't pull myself together in the face of an irony worthy of Greek mythology.

Still, if there was one comforting thought right now……

“Nothing came out of Agent Seo's will, so we analyzed Team Leader Cha's will as well.”

“But nothing much came out of Team Leader Cha's will, either. As expected, the message was just passed along incorrectly, right?”

It was that I wasn't alone.

The person who advised me to write a shameful will.

His will had also been revealed to the whole world, so at least I wasn't lonely…….

I forced a smile toward the chatting researchers and glanced sideways at Cha Sieon.

Cha Sieon looked calm on the outside, too.

However, a strange tension seemed to grip the fist resting on his knee.

A moment later, we got up from our seats.

We didn't say a single word as we walked back to the main building.

As a result, an awkward silence hung over the return trip, even more so than when we went to meet Lee Sol.

And amidst a strange sense of deflation, I chewed over the reality I was facing and the contents of the will I had written.

I felt dizzy.

To put it more accurately, the self-loathing rising all the way to the top of my head made me want to bash my head against a wall.

I felt like I was going to die, but since it was entirely my own doing, I had nowhere to direct my anger.

Why on earth did I leave a will like that?

Did I really have to make my resolve to return alive in such a fashion?

Is resenting Team Leader Cha here too cowardly a thing to do……?

While I was writhing in silence, Cha Sieon abruptly broke the silence.

“Everyone will have forgotten about it.”

“…….”

“We succeeded in suppressing 「Railway Station」, so attention will be focused there right now. There is a lot of newly updated information, and there are many things to sort out going forward. Therefore, no one will be paying attention to a will.”

Team Leader Cha spoke in a grave tone, as if he had attained some form of enlightenment.

Was those words meant for me just now, or were they meant for himself?

With a gloomy mood, I stared at Cha Sieon's back.

“Furthermore, the Field Division won't casually bring up the will either. So if only Seo Doun remains calm, it will blow over quietly.”

Is this atonement for giving me the wrong advice?

Or is it solidarity toward a colleague suffering the same agony?

Whichever it was, I listened to Cha Sieon's advice and nodded deeply.

Right, a will wasn't an essay submitted to a writing contest.

Nobody was going to say to my face, ‘I saw that you wrote a will.’

Somehow it had turned into a situation where my name was stamped all over a rambling text I had hastily thrown onto an anonymous board or a private blog.

A text I had rambled on and on about, intending never to reveal that it was me or to show it to a single soul, had been exposed to the people who knew me.

……Just thinking about that situation made me break out in a cold sweat and want to bite my tongue off.

This too shall pass.

Like every wave of hardships I had already weathered, this one would too.

Above all else, I was not alone right now.

<br>

<center>*&emsp;*&emsp;*</center>

<br>

“Agent Doun! I saw that you wrote a will!”

“…….”

I had forgotten for a moment.

That the hit rate for Team Leader Cha's words was well below fifty percent.

No matter how common-sense a thing he said, for some reason, whenever Team Leader Cha spoke, an exceptional situation that completely blew past it would crop up.

In the afternoon, at the Field Division meeting room.

The members of the 「Railway Station」 suppression operation gathered in one place.

There, Cha Sieon and I received a passionate welcome from the agents who had arrived first.

And the agents were riding high on a bizarre misunderstanding.

“Leaving a will like that and trailing off at the end is foul play…… Seungyo cried reading that, you know.”

“I didn't cry.”

“Honestly, your heart ached, didn't it? Am I wrong?”

“…….”

“Hmm? Agent Doun, why are you sweating so much?”

At Jeong Jinu's question, I forcibly curved the corners of my lips upward.

For now, it seemed the Field Division had long since dismissed the assumption that there was vital information in my will.

Instead, incredibly absurdly, they believed that through my will, I had…… conveyed my convictions to them.

I had no clue where to even begin straightening this out, and while I was just streaming cold sweat, that was when it happened.

“Hey, sit down. What is this, a middle school classroom?”

Right on cue, Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong's voice rang out.

Manager Kwon Museop and Deputy Director Yoon Ujeong were walking into the meeting room.

At the appearance of their superiors, the agents hastily took their seats.

Before long, Manager Kwon Museop, having settled into his seat, opened his mouth.

“Everyone worked hard. And you did well.”

Just two short sentences settled heavily in the air.

“Thanks to you guys, I feel like we're finally getting rewarded for all the hardships up until now. You must have felt frustrated, too. You probably felt the most frustrated out of all of us. Thank you for enduring so well up to this point, and I am truly grateful that everyone came back alive. Sol is recovering, so you can rest easy, and Sol's father…… Agent Seop will also be sent off properly once again. And the Director was extremely pleased as well. So he decided to come directly to award commendations.”

“Oh……”

The agents gasped in admiration at the mention of the Director.

Director Go, you were dead set against it, but now that results are out, you're honestly happy.

People are certainly like that, but well, this is still better than holding up useless pride and throwing tantrums.

“Things are going to change quite a bit from now on. Since this is a feat you built by risking your lives, you can take pride in it for now.”

At Kwon Museop's praise, the agents let out cheers.

Kwon Museop watched the scene with a smile, then said to Yoon Ujeong.

“Deputy Director, would you like to say a word as well?”

“Don't get too carried away.”

At Yoon Ujeong's single word, the cheers abruptly stopped.

In response, Yoon Ujeong shook her head and added.

“I know you guys did well. You did well, yeah. So maintain your beginner's mind. Don't get ahead of yourselves trying to do even better, and don't get frustrated if you fail to pull off this much next time. You did exceptionally well this time; it's not the baseline. Got it?”

“Yes!”

“Ah, and you guys saw Seo Doun's will, right?”

Out of nowhere?!

At Yoon Ujeong's ambush, the cold sweat that had temporarily stopped began to flow once again.

“Since we’re on the topic of keeping your original intentions in mind, you guys need to show some effort when writing your wills. Huh? It’s not like you’re advertising that you’re risking your lives out there, but God forbid something happens, what do you think your families would think if they received a half-assed will? Write it sincerely, like Seo Doun, huh? Read a book once in a while.”

Hold on, Deputy Manager. Why this timing…….

I felt cold sweat sliding down my spine.

But, ugh, this too shall pass.

So let me take comfort in not being the only one…….

It was right then.

“Do you know why I’m saying this? Team Leader Cha, where in the world is the guy who writes just a single line saying ‘No special remarks’ on his will? Especially when he’s the head of the team.”

“……?”

I doubted my ears.

No special remarks?

Just a single line?

“Isn’t that a bit much? Doun filled out three full pages in his own handwriting, kid.”

I turned my head following Yoon Ujeong’s gaze.

“I will correct it.”

Cha Sieon, humbly accepting Yoon Ujeong’s criticism.

……There is a poem that goes, *Though life deceive you*.

I was now understanding that verse deep down to my bones.

Ah, so this is what it feels like.

The feeling of being scammed by the world.

Perhaps feeling my gaze, Cha Sieon glanced over at me.

I asked him with my eyes.

What the hell are you……?

To that, Cha Sieon answered with his eyes.

It just turned out that way.
