■ Escape Directives for 「Ant Hill That Steals the Five Senses」

Survive for 29 hours.

If you endure for 29 hours, you will return to reality.

However, several fatal dangers exist here.

Please familiarize yourself with the contents below and ensure your safety.

\[Major Hazards\]

1\. Cold

The temperature in this area is -1°C. Beware of hypothermia.

2\. 「Ant Lines」

These are white lines covered in mucus, scattered between trees or across paths.

Due to their strong adhesive power, they easily stick to clothing or the body, and they are extremely tough, making them difficult to cut even with knives or scissors.

It is possible to melt them with fire, but you cannot expect much effect from the firepower of an ordinary lighter.

If 「Ant Lines」 attach to your body, escape quickly, even if you must cut away clothing or skin.

If you shake the lines trying to remove them blindly, they will become more tangled.

If you are caught by these 「Ant Lines」, you will not return to reality even after 29 hours have passed.

In addition, if 「Ant Lines」 touch your skin, bodily deformation may progress.

3\. 「Execution Ants」

「Execution Ants」 are dangerous entities that roam this hill and bite humans to death.

They are ant-shaped entities reaching 1.8m in body length, and are characterized by their greatly developed sickle-shaped mandibles.

These mandibles can easily sever human bones.

There are 28 entities in all, and they regard humans as prey and track them.

The human detection range of an 「Execution Ant」 is approximately 100m, and in this Special Zone, which has a total area of 2㎢, most places fall within the detection range of 「Execution Ants」.

If two or so adults work together, it is possible to kill an 「Execution Ant」, but such action is not recommended.

Practical methods for surviving 「Execution Ants」 are as follows.

1\. Find the 「242 Stone Towers」 scattered throughout the 「Ant Hill」.

2\. When you touch this 「Stone Tower」, a red brand will be engraved on your wrist. Only one brand can be engraved per 「Stone Tower」, and a 「Stone Tower」 that has granted a brand will collapse.

3\. 「Execution Ants」 cannot attack a person who bears this brand. However, the duration of this brand is at minimum 21 minutes and at maximum 50 minutes.

4\. Before the brand disappears, find a new 「Stone Tower」 and renew the brand.

5\. Repeat the above actions for 29 hours and survive the 「Execution Ants」.

Then, good luck.

In short, tag with your life on the line.

We had to avoid traps and monsters and find 「Stone Towers」.

For 29 hours, nonstop, even with one of the five senses stolen.

At that time, the chance of survival changed greatly depending on which sense was taken.

In particular, if sight was taken away, the situation became hopelessly difficult.

“I can’t see in front of me.”

And of all people, Manager Kwon Museop had drawn that.

-Ant No. 6 has already caught the scent and is coming. For now, move to a 「Stone Tower」.

To make matters worse, tracking had begun too.

-The nearest 「Stone Tower」 from your current location is 80m away. Another 「Stone Tower」 requires going 130m farther from there.

“Manager, take my hand.”

For now, I grabbed Kwon Museop’s arm and pulled him.

But he had so much luggage that he could not stand properly.

“I’ll just stand up on my own.”

Kwon Museop smiled awkwardly and stood by balancing himself.

But then.

“Manager, beside you……!”

THUD—!

Kwon Museop slammed his head into a tree and sank back down.

This is bad. What do I do?

As I fretted, Yoon Ujeong said,

-Doun, if the Manager gets in the way, you can just abandon him.

“Are you joking?”

I was dumbfounded and asked back with a straight face.

Then Kwon Museop laughed unseriously.

“Stop teasing, Deputy Manager. Doun is serious right now.”

That made me even more dumbfounded.

What is he saying when he can’t even get up alone…….

“Doun, put this on first.”

When every second was urgent, Kwon Museop searched through his luggage and pulled something out.

It was a lightweight padded jacket.

“It’s below freezing here. You need to maintain your body temperature.”

Because a necessary item had suddenly appeared, I was rather bewildered.

I put that padded jacket on under my vest and held my hand out to Kwon Museop again.

“I’ll hold you from the side.”

“Mm, if you take care of me like this, it’s a little embarrassing…….”

-The Manager is getting senior-citizen treatment.

“See? This is definitely going to last a long time.”

Yoon Ujeong teased endlessly.

Kwon Museop muttered unseriously.

They could not read the mood.

Is now really the time to be this relaxed?

What exactly are they trusting to act like this…….

“Deputy Manager, you can give directions, right?”

-Goodness, making me work at this hour. Turn on the body cam.

As Kwon Museop stood again, he turned on the body cam attached to his chest.

Then he asked Yoon Ujeong in return,

“You said Ant No. 6 earlier, right?”

-Yes. Your current location is D-6 Area.

“Then this must be the entrance to the walking trail.”

-Try moving for the first time in a while. Straight ahead at two o’clock.

“Doun, let’s go.”

After finishing the conversation, Kwon Museop took a long step forward.

Then he began running without hesitation.

What is this?

Startled, I followed behind him.

At first, I chased awkwardly, then soon panicked and ran with all my strength.

Even so, I was only keeping pace with Kwon Museop.

No way. Is he really running?

Was saying he could not see in front of him perhaps a joke?

The instant I suspected this.

CRASH—!

Kwon Museop, who had been running ahead, tripped over a tree root and fell.

“Deputy Manager…….”

-Ah, I’m sorry. I was drinking water for a moment.

Kwon Museop stood with a sigh.

Since he had fallen once, one might expect him to hesitate, but he began running again at the same speed.

Far from holding him, I was busy keeping up behind him.

-Keep going straight. Rising stone-step section. Turn at 11 o’clock in 5m.

Yoon Ujeong guided the way like a navigation system, and Kwon Museop continued forward.

-Stop!

SCRAAAAAPE—!

Kwon Museop stopped abruptly, making the sound of gravel scraping under combat boots.

Then he asked back in a voice without the slightest disturbance.

“Why?”

-Ant No. 8 is approaching, and it’s a little fast.

“How long?”

-I don’t think you’ll have time to mark two 「Stone Towers」.

“Then one first.”

Kwon Museop ran without caring that an 「Ant」 was coming.

After I followed him up the hill path for a while.

A small stone tower came into view.

It was a stone tower one commonly saw when walking mountain paths, roughly built by placing small stones on top of a large stone.

“Doun, touch it.”

I wanted to yield first to the person who could not see.

But I had no shame to say that when I was panting for breath.

I placed my hand on the 「Stone Tower」 as instructed.

Then the 「Stone Tower」 collapsed like dust, and a red band appeared on my wrist.

A mark as if I had been tied with rope. Is this the brand?

Now I was safe even if an 「Ant」 appeared.

The problem was Manager Kwon Museop, but there was no time to ask what he would do.

“Deputy Manager. Tell me the direction and distance of Nos. 6 and 8.”

-No. 6 is 130m at 7 o’clock, and No. 8 is 60m at 10 o’clock.

“Continue relaying Ant No. 8’s position.”

Kwon Museop spoke calmly and put down his luggage.

Then he took out something like a round rod and attached it to the muzzle.

Is that a silencer?

-24m, 22m, 19m, 17m…….

Meanwhile, Ant No. 8 was closing the distance more and more.

Rustle—

At the presence that had finally arrived, I lifted my head.

It was on top of the hill.

It was literally an ant.

Like a sculpture by an artist with good powers of observation, it was a horribly enlarged ant.

The 「Ant」 shook antennae like steel rods and charged toward us.

“Manager……!”

PFFT—!

Just as I was about to shout, one of the 「Ant」’s eyes shattered.

PFFT— PFFTPFFTPFFT—!

With suppressed noises, its legs were blown off, its head burst, and the 「Ant」 became riddled with holes.

Kwon Museop was calmly sniping with a gun equipped with a silencer.

Dumbfounded, I looked back and forth between Kwon Museop and the dying 「Ant」.

The Deputy Manager had told him the direction and distance.

But even so……?

-Ant No. 8, silenced.

Only after Yoon Ujeong’s declaration did Kwon Museop stop shooting.

At the sight of him casually waving away the smoke pooled in the silencer, I asked in disbelief,

“Manager.”

“Mm?”

“Is it true that you really can’t see?”

“Would I lie about something like that?”

Kwon Museop burst into laughter.

In the middle of that, Yoon Ujeong cut in.

-At this rate, you can conserve more 「Stone Towers」.

“Yes. I was worried because it’s been a while, but it’s going better than I expected.”

At their peaceful voices, I grew even more confused.

Perhaps having seen my expression, Yoon Ujeong cackled and said,

-Doun, that place was originally the Manager’s front yard.

“Front yard?”

-It’s the place he was in charge of back when he was a team leader. Back then, he wasn’t holding himself back like now, so he went in and out almost like it was his own home.

Huh…….

Yoon Ujeong cut off the small talk and informed him again.

PFFTPFFTPFFTPFFTPFFT—!

Another 「Ant」 was riddled with holes.

If it had happened once, I would have called it coincidence.

But if he did it like this in succession…….

-Whew, your skills, of course. What else would there be?
