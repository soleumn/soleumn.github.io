<br>“I'm not even asking you to write like Doun. But you should at least show some basic human decency, you punks! Huh? Don't pour all your time and effort solely into muscle!”

Yoon Ujeong's scolding continued.

But those words didn't stay in my head, going in one ear and straight out the other.

It felt like I had fallen into a trap created by the entire world acting in unison.

The world was harsher than I had believed, and to make matters worse, I was the only one caught in this cruel snare.

For now, I had to deal with this situation.

But how could I go against this flow?

How on earth could I stand alone against an atmosphere so firmly established...?

Just then.

Suddenly, someone raised a hand high.

“There is something we need to check first regarding Agent Seo Doun’s will.”

The person who said that was none other than Baek Sara.

Check something? What now...?

“At first, Agent Jeong Jinu said there would be some clue in Agent Seo Doun’s will. However, upon close examination, Agent Seo Doun’s will was confirmed to be nothing more than an ordinary will.”

Baek Sara said in a calm voice.

Everyone listened in silence, as this was something they already knew well up to this point.

“Then why did Agent Seo Doun ask for his will to be checked? If he died, the will would be revealed anyway.”

Yet the moment Baek Sara raised that doubt, the atmosphere shifted.

Agents began to tense up at a certain sense of incongruity.

Stirring up those ripples, Baek Sara asked me.

“Agent Seo, did you really ask Team Leader Cha to check the suicide note?”

Ms. Baek Sara…….

At Baek Sara’s question, a strange sense of overwhelming emotion washed over me.

It felt as though the dark clouds of the world were parting and a single ray of light was shining down.

The opportunity to reveal the truth had arrived out of nowhere, and overcoming all frustration, I gladly testified.

“I asked him to destroy the suicide note.”

“……!”

“……!”

“……!”

In order: Yoon Ujeong’s astonishment.

Jeong Jinu’s astonishment.

The astonishment of the other agents watching us.

It felt like an unnecessarily solemn reaction, but I steadfastly continued.

“I told him to get rid of it, but it seems that got communicated incorrectly.”

A commotion broke out in the room at my confession.

“Wasn’t this given to us to read?”

“No wonder it felt a little weird…… especially the beginning.”

“How do you mistake ‘destroy’ for ‘check’? Are your ears blown out?”

After murmuring amongst themselves for a moment, the agents finally seemed to grasp the mood and began watching me out of the corners of their eyes.

Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong, in particular, wore a deeply flustered expression.

“Destroy……?”

Yoon Ujeong’s gaze drifted aimlessly as he chewed over my words.

Then, with an awkward expression, he apologized to me.

“Uh, um. My bad.”

“It’s fine, Deputy Manager. It was just a misunderstanding…….”

I tried to accept the apology like a mature adult.

Since there was a mistake, it's, i-it's fine…….

No, honestly, it's not... fine at all…….

“Please give me back my will, even now…….”

I eventually groaned, covering my face.

“Jeong Jinu, you son of a……!”

It was when Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong, even more flustered by my reaction, raised his voice.

Baek Sara raised her hand again and spoke.

“And there is one more thing we need to address.”

Gaze levels turned completely resolute as they focused on Baek Sara.

Baek Sara was fully displaying the sharpness of the Exploration Team right then.

“In Agent Seo Doun's actual will, there is a passage stating that he was coerced to ‘write the will as humiliatingly as possible.’”

“……!”

“……!”

“……!”

“Isn't this a trick the seniors used to use back in the day to mess with newbies?”

Right. Life often deceives me, but that didn't mean my entire existence was a deceit.

Dazzling truths do exist in this world, and those truths always wait for the moment to come to light.

“Who was the bastard who told Seo Doun to write a humiliating will!”

Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong immediately set out to hunt down the culprit.

At that, Jeong Jinu and the other agents looked around with innocent expressions.

As if protesting that it wasn't them.

Naturally, their gazes gathered in one place.

What was captured in the agents' field of vision was Cha Sieon, resting his interlocked hands on the desk while quietly bowing his head.

“……Don't tell me it was you?”

Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong asked Cha Sieon.

The voice asking that was close to a groan.

As if his foot had been chopped by the axe he trusted.

As if he never expected you to do such a thing.

At that, Cha Sieon raised his head.

Then, he confessed in a low, calm voice.

“I didn't expect you to listen so well.”

A heavy silence fell.

For how long did only my blood pressure surge silently in the quiet.

Yoon Ujeong said.

“……Cha Sieon, Jeong Jinu. You two, stay behind for a moment.”

Justice existed in this world.

And Baek Sara, who had showcased a one-shot, one-kill to set justice right, turned back to look at me and smiled brightly.

As if telling me to rest easy now.

While I was happy at that beautiful smile, I couldn't help but feel sorrowful.

Because my current situation felt like an elementary school student being bullied and then rescued by the class president and the homeroom teacher.

And so, despite this realization of justice, I had to endure a considerable amount of cringe and pettiness all over again.

<br>

A short while later.

Cha Sieon and Jeong Jinu, having received a kick to the shin from Yoon Ujeong, came over to me and apologized.

30 minutes later.

All of us came out of the Bureau.

Another 1 hour later.

We arrived at a quiet countryside safe house with a garden, and the after-party dinner started there.

And 4 hours later.

The agents succeeded in building a pyramid out of soju bottles.

“Hey, that's dangerous…… Clean up the pyramid before you guys leave…….”

“We're taking it to the dorm.”

“It's our achievement.”

“Hey, get up. You gotta stack one more layer…….”

Jeong Jinu waking up Han Seungyo, who was sleeping under the pyramid.

As fitting for a celebration of success, the agents partied without holding back.

I watched their otherworldly energy for a bit before quietly getting up and stepping outside.

"Hoo..."

The publishing world has its fair share of heavy drinkers, but this crowd is no joke either...

I had a glass or two just to be polite, but that was nowhere near enough to keep up with those unbridled people.

So, as I stepped outside to catch my breath, a breeze blew by at that exact moment.

The zelkova tree casting its shadow over the wooden porch rustled, and the smell of a summer night filled the air.

It's peaceful.

As if everything that happened in the past was a lie.

But it shouldn't be a lie.

Because the things we barely managed to achieve are too precious.

*Rustle-*

As I was enjoying the summer night, a sound came from my clothes.

Ah.

I rummaged through my pocket and pulled out a crumpled piece of paper.

It was my suicide note, returned to me by Deputy Manager Yoon Ujeong.

"..."

The product of my embarrassment.

I considered just tearing it up and throwing it in the trash, but stopped my hand.

I'm going to throw it away.

I am going to throw it away, but I felt a little reluctant to toss out something I had pressed down and written with such firm strokes.

So, hesitating for a moment, I unfolded my suicide note.

<div style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold;">

To the Bureau personnel,<br><br>

After rethinking a level of humiliation that wouldn't make you uncomfortable, I make this confession.<br><br>

I am actually in a secret affair with Team Leader Cha Sieon.<br><br>

Is that really true, you ask?<br><br>

How could it possibly be true?<br><br>

This sentence, born purely for the sake of humiliation, performed its role admirably.<br><br>

Just to hide this sentence, I will desperately survive.<br><br>

I'm going crazy...

What on earth were you thinking writing something like this and leaving it in someone else's hands, past Seo Doun...

I squeezed my eyes shut at the fact that this nonsense had been made public.

Enduring the pain for a moment, I faced the will again after taking a deep breath.

My nonsense continued to fill up the second page.

Since I wrote it thinking absolutely no one would ever see it, the brainless rambling was overflowing to the point of freezing.

So, after speed-reading it with glazed eyes, I paused my gaze on the last page.

<div style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold;" markdown="1">

---<br><br>

I am trying this desperately to live.<br><br>

However, on the other hand, the thought that it's all futile sometimes crosses my mind.<br><br>

Because human efforts and wishes are betrayed far too easily.<br><br>

Even so, humans foolishly hold onto expectations, suffering all the more for the price of refusing to let go of hope.<br><br>

Wouldn't it be better to resign oneself beforehand, to give up so completely that one can remain cynical in the face of any catastrophe?<br><br>

Since our lives have already been stolen, and it is exhausting just to dodge the deaths that keep coming, wouldn't it be better to calmly choose death than to struggle so vainly?<br><br>

Burying thoughts like these in a corner of my heart, I was actually weighing things in the balance.<br><br>

Wondering if not just me, but all of you here, might be frogs slowly boiling in a pot.<br><br>

Wondering if we are simply enduring meaningless time, much like Sisyphus receiving an eternal punishment.<br><br>

If so, I dared to think, wouldn't it be fine if we all just died together?<br><br>

Yet, the moment I actually imagined death, it was funny how I still wanted to live, which made me change my mind.<br><br>

I had thought my life was stolen, but even this was life.<br><br>

The time I considered mere endurance in this place is also my life; enduring today in anxiety because I don't know tomorrow is a day's worth of life; and because there is a day's worth of life even without perfect salvation, I was able to exist for yet another day.<br><br>

This was neither meaningless nor futile.<br><br>

So, it was an honor to share this life with all of you.<br><br>

Thanks to all of you, I lived a few more months of life, and that life…….<br><br>

*Rip, riiiiiip-!*

Unable to endure any longer, I tore the suicide note into long strips.

I tore it vertically, then horizontally, dismantling even the printed characters within it piece by piece.

Farewell, paper stained with my humiliation.

It would be perfect to scatter these pieces colorfully.

However, I couldn't litter the garden with tiny scraps of paper.

Just as I was looking around for a place to throw them away,

"What are you doing?"

"Agh!"

Startled by the voice suddenly coming from behind me, I let out a yell.

Damn it, I almost spat out my heart.

Turning around, I saw Cha Sieon standing there.

Cha Sieon sat down casually on the wooden porch, looking as if he had just come out to get some fresh air like me.

"I heard a rough summary of the will."

"......"

"I've properly noted your determination not to die alone."

"As if you're in any position to criticize me for that, Team Leader."

"......"

After exchanging those sharp remarks, silence fell.

A breeze blew in and filled the empty space.

Then, Cha Sieon spoke up again.

"Thank you."

"......?"

"Thanks to you, I was able to do what needed to be done."

Why is he acting like this out of character?

I pondered for a moment on how to reply.

Since we were mutually indebted, I wondered if I should express my gratitude as well, but upon second thought, it seemed a bit premature.

So, after a brief moment of hesitation, I came up with an appropriate response and replied.

"I look forward to your continued cooperation."
