<br>Thankfully, the rain had stopped.

Cha Sieon and Kwon Museop, dressed in black suits, used shovels to dig into the wet earth beneath a small tree.

Two years ago, that tree had become the grave of Lee Seop, established even without a body.

Lee Sol and a woman, both dressed in mourning attire, approached the tree holding a small urn.

The two carefully lowered Lee Seop's remains between the roots of the tree.

The agents who had accompanied them silently watched as the grave, which had previously been nothing more than a name, finally became whole.

Perhaps it would have been better if the rain had kept falling.

Once the burial was finished, the woman who had been with Lee Sol bowed her head to Cha Sieon and Kwon Museop.

Then, she and Lee Sol came over to where I was standing.

"Mister, this is my mom."

Lee Sol introduced her in case I didn't recognize her.

Lee Sol's mother bowed her head to me as well.

"Thank you, Agent Seo Doun. For saving our child twice and even finding her father..."

As the wife of Lee Seop, the woman spoke with a calm face and a quiet voice in which sorrow and relief were blended.

"It's nothing. You must have been through a lot of heartaches, so please accept my deepest condolences."

At my reply, the woman smiled faintly.

Her smiling face looked a lot like Lee Sol's.

"We hung up the family photo again yesterday."

A family photo?

"A family photo taken with the child's father."

"Ah..."

At the words added by Lee Sol's mother, I nodded.

The redaction of information concerning Lee Seop had not been limited to the Bureau.

The family members had also been restricted from even missing the deceased.

"Now we can finally breathe and live. Thank you so much, Agent."

Lee Sol's mother bowed deeply at the waist.

Seeing that, I had no choice but to remember it once again.

The final moments of Lee Seop, or rather, 「Giant Siren」.

As I expected, Lee Seop was a good father and a good husband.

Why did such a person have to become a monster and remain bound to that unfamiliar space?

We still did not know the reason.

<br>

<center>*&emsp;*&emsp;*</center>

<br>

All agents deployed to the 「Railway Station」 suppression operation underwent thorough medical examinations.

"Everyone received a clean bill of health."

Cha Sieon reported the examination results.

At this, Kwon Museop nodded with a look of relief.

"Good. Since the Director is fine too, you guys must not have any major issues. That's a relief."

And thank goodness, there didn't seem to be any particular aftereffects from the resurrection.

In truth, because of Director Go's precedent, there hadn't been an atmosphere of deep worry.

However, I was the problem.

"But Doun's case is different..."

Kwon Museop muttered as he flipped through the report placed in front of him.

"I thought he was a kid with incredible luck, but it turns out it wasn't something to be taken so lightly."

Kwon Museop's voice was bitter.

It was because he had found out that I had died and come back to life multiple times.

"Share this only with Director Kim U-in and Analysis Team 5, and put an information lock on it."

"Yes."

"And you, Doun, don't talk about this anywhere else either. It won't do us any good if the number of people who know increases."

"Yes."

At Kwon Museop's request, I nodded as well.

Just as he said, once it got out that while other people only had disposable lives, I had a reusable one, there were only downsides and no upsides.

Furthermore, the woman who used to grant me this special privilege had already decided not to look out for me anymore.

Under these circumstances, there was no need to deliberately let those around me know the fact that my life was made of recycled paper.

After binding us to secrecy, Kwon Museop looked down at the report once again.

Before long, he sighed again.

"Anyway, the fact that this 「Big Cat」's older sister is..."

"She might be the exact same entity that Senior Lee Seop encountered."

Cha Sieon chimed in quietly at Kwon Museop's self-talk.

"Senior Lee Seop also said regarding the entity that helped him, 'I can't tell if it's an angel or a human.' And the appearance of the woman Seo Doun described was also something that would typically be considered an angel."

"Well... Didn't the one Doun saw openly look like an angel? If he had seen something like Lee Seop did, he probably wouldn't have said he was confused."

That was true.

The woman I met had spread out a snowy-white space behind her back like wings.

So I wasn't confused at all.

Even though she took on a form very familiar to me, I was instantly certain that she was not human.

"Besides, what Lee Seop saw was a young man."

"A young man?"

This was my first time hearing about it.

Since the gag order on Lee Seop had been lifted, Kwon Museop answered without hesitation.

"He said it was a young and frail man. What Doun saw was the appearance of a woman he already knew. Sara, Sol, and what was it, the author he was in charge of?"

"Yes."

"He's close with Sara, and Sol was the objective of this operation. The author? Were they someone he worked with for a long time?"

"In many ways, yes."

"Then we have to assume that the 「Big Cat」's older sister imitated the appearances of women who were impressive to Doun. Given that she referred to herself as 'older sister', she seems to definitely perceive herself as a female."

"However, Team Leader, judging by the information so far, what rules the Special Zone are the 'four living creatures'. If those split into a faction of “three creatures” and the 「Big Cat」's older sister, the only one among them who is friendly to humans is the 「Big Cat」's older sister. Therefore, to fit the diagram, the entity that gave hints to Senior Lee Seop must also be the 「Big Cat」's older sister."

The sound recorded when I escaped from the 「Stadium」.

The performance I saw at the 「National Theater」.

And the message Lee Seop left at the 「Hotel」.

Putting all of this together, Cha Sieon's words made sense.

However, Kwon Museop shook his head once more.

"Lee Seop said that man was kind. But is this woman kind? From what I can see, she seems to be on the vicious side."

"..."

"And are the entities we need to figure out necessarily the 'four creatures'? If the 「Crow」 is separate yet again, that diagram might not be the whole picture."

Kwon Museop's words were also correct.

It was still too early to piece the situation together with just the information we had secured.

Kwon Museop, who had been flipping through the report again, suddenly asked me.

"By the way, Doun. Did you write down this transcript from memory?"

"Pardon?"

"I mean the conversation you had with the 「Big Cat」's older sister. The length is quite substantial, so how did you write it down so specifically?"

"Ah no, I wrote it while listening to the recording on my smartwatch."

I answered Kwon Museop, looking bewildered.

Just as he said, the time I spent having a conversation with that woman was quite long.

There was no way anyone could memorize and write down conversational spoken language verbatim just by listening to it once.

Besides, with a perfectly functioning recording available, why on earth would he need to do that?

“You wrote this while listening to the recording?”

Yet, Manager Kwon Museop sounded as if he couldn't understand my words at all.

When I stared blankly at Manager Kwon Museop, Kwon Museop added in a stiff voice.

“There was nothing on the recording except your voice.”

“Pardon?”

“I couldn't hear anything other than Seo Doun's voice either.”

“…….”

Cha Sieon even chimed in to back up those words.

What on earth does this mean?

I had looped that recording for hours in order to write that transcript.

The recording I had been listening to clearly contained a woman's voice—that exact same voice of Author Na Bit.

“Just a moment.”

I immediately turned on my tablet.

Then I played the audio file saved inside it.

<br>

-Playing the editor even here?

-It's an occupational disease.

-How wonderful.

<br>

The voice of Author Na Bit playing in alternation with my own voice.

Sure enough, the woman's voice could be heard clearly.

“There must have been an error in the shared file.”

I said this after letting Cha Sieon and Kwon Museop listen to the recording.

Yet the expressions of the two men looking at me grew even stranger.

“……?”

When I reacted with confusion to their response, Kwon Museop, who had been staring at me steadily, opened his mouth again.

“There's only your voice, you know.”

“Pardon?”

“I can only hear your voice. You can't be serious, right?”

“……You're not joking right now, are you?”

I laughed awkwardly and turned to look at Cha Sieon.

Yet Team Leader Cha shook his head with a rigid face.

What is this, a joke?

I know you guys like to play harsh pranks, but pulling something like this right now is a bit much, isn't it?

“You really can't hear this?”

Moving the playback bar on the recording file, I isolated just the woman's voice and played it again.

<br>

-Hmm, but none of the authors you managed were like that, right?

-I was wondering what was holding back something that should naturally fall into my hands…….

-For you to make it out of here alive, the plausibility is…….

<br>

“You really can't hear this?”

Figuring I would actually get angry if they claimed this was a joke too, I asked back with a straight face.

At that, Cha Sieon answered with a hardened expression.

“I cannot hear it. Only faint static is audible, and apart from Seo Doun's voice, there are no recognizable sounds.”

“…….”

At Cha Sieon's assertion, I felt a strange chill.

It didn't seem to be a joke after all.

Then what on earth is it?

These people couldn't hear what I was hearing in real time?

As I sat frozen and speechless, Cha Sieon abruptly pulled out his own tablet.

“Seo Doun, by any chance, do you hear this sound too?”

Cha Sieon played yet another recording file.

<br>

-What on earth is going on here……?

<br>

What suddenly came through was none other than my own voice.

This was the audio recorded when I came out of 「Sports Complex」.

<br>

-Why up until now…… couldn't…….

-……What on earth is this ……entity…….

<br>

My voice continued in sporadic snatches.

 I strained my ears to listen to the sound amidst my confusion.

A recording where, when I listened to it previously, only my voice could be heard.

Yet the next moment.

<br>

-Isn't it like a god to you? After all, it created you and brought you back to life several times.

<br>

The woman's laughter-laced voice was heard clearly.

Goosebumps broke out all over my skin at that gentle voice.
