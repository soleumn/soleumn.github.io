<br>I froze where I stood.

Cook the dragon…?

In SL2PL’s true ending, the Fire Dragon Infernum fought the player as the final boss.

Once the player drove out Infernum, who had ruled as a tyrant, the twins became the dungeon’s new masters and peace was restored.

But although I’d seen the defeated Infernum flee countless times, I could swear I’d never once killed him, captured him, or cooked him.

“As for the cooking method, I’ll leave it entirely to your discretion, Roasted Chestnut. However, I’d like the eyes and the heart served intact, without discarding any part of them.”

“…”

“Roasted Chestnut, there’s no need to worry so much. Hunting a fire dragon won’t be difficult. After all, I’m your companion, aren’t I?”

He seemed to have misunderstood why I’d gone stiff.

He encouraged me, saying he’d help with the hunt and that even young Roasted Chestnut would be able to do it.

As I listened, I carefully considered my answer.

*‘I can’t refuse.’*

What I really wanted to ask was whether he absolutely had to eat a fire dragon. Couldn’t he just eat whatever I served?

But if I said that, there was no telling what Dominic—already displeased because of the Fire Dragon’s Seal—might do.

The choices available to me were practically predetermined.

“I can’t say I’m confident… but I’ll do my best, Dominic.”

The real problem was figuring out how to deal with this going forward.

I held out my hand for him to climb on, but even after hearing my answer, Dominic remained motionless.

Then I realized why.

The Fire Dragon’s Seal was branded on the hand I’d extended.

Apparently he didn’t even want to touch a place bearing someone else’s seal.

I offered my other hand instead.

Only then did the pudding slime settle comfortably into my palm.

Feeling his soft, squishy body against my hand, my heart finally settled.

Maybe I should buy something this squishy once I get out of SL2PL.

The moment the clock struck six, a black silhouette silently appeared at the staircase.

Je Hyun-oh descended two or three steps at a time.

The instant his feet reached the first floor, he came to an abrupt stop.

“Lieutenant Colonel Je, did you sleep well—”

Before I could even finish my greeting, he was suddenly standing right in front of me.

Realizing where his gaze was fixed, I obediently held out the back of my hand, displaying the Fire Dragon’s Seal.

A moment later, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein came downstairs as well.

The first thing Kwak Hanmuk checked was today’s slime.

“…I can still see it. Fuck.”

After confirming the morning slime, he cursed before muttering in resignation,

“I’ll check my contamination level again once we’re out. Doesn’t seem like there’s gonna be a cure….”

While he mourned the possibility of spending the rest of his life as the only person who could see slimes, Mo Haein noticed the Fire Dragon’s Seal.

“Trainee. What’s that? When did the restaurant owner show up?”

“I woke up about thirty minutes early because of what seemed to be a system error, so I met the owner first.”

“Oh, hey, Little Chestnut.”

Kwak Hanmuk bumped into me hard enough to make a *thud*.

“Let’s work through the strategy together, alright? You keep doing things by yourself.”

“I’m sorry. I was in too much of a hurry.”

“Yeah. You should be sorry.”

Looking down at me, he warned,

“Goyo, don’t forget weird stuff always happens around you.”

“Yes. I’ll be careful.”

I didn’t bother mentioning that Infernum was eagerly waiting for me to end up on his dining table.

Why?

Because that was never going to happen.

With three members of the Special Mission Team and one pudding slime backing me up, I was feeling extremely confident.

*‘Ah, the hologram badge.’*

Samra had told me to wear the hologram badge after entering SL2PL.

But with everything going on, there were too many things I wanted to hide.

If communications were connected, I wouldn’t even be able to talk freely with Dominic.

For the time being, I decided to pretend I’d simply forgotten.

I explained to Je Hyun-oh and the captains that our party’s combat strength had impressed Infernum enough for him to unlock the daytime dungeon, and that it was far more difficult than the nighttime dungeon.

The captains didn’t actually know what conditions unlocked the daytime dungeon.

And this was their first time entering with a party this powerful.

They had little choice but to accept my explanation.

Crossing her arms, Mo Haein murmured,

“So the dungeon opens during the day too. I guess your combat power has to reach a certain level.”

“Yes. That’s right.”

Chewing noisily on an apple he’d gathered from the orchard outside the village yesterday, Kwak Hanmuk said,

“Well, since we’re here anyway, let’s clear all ninety-nine floors today.”

Mo Haein nodded.

“I don’t know how hard the daytime dungeon is, but with us, it shouldn’t be much of a problem.”

“Captains, there’s actually no need to go all the way to the ninety-ninth floor yet.”

The goal of SL2PL wasn’t simply conquering the dungeon.

Running the restaurant was just as important.

Clearing the dungeon alone accomplished nothing.

I began briefing everyone on my plan.

When I finished, neither captain spoke for a moment.

Mo Haein turned toward Kwak Hanmuk.

“Is this how you sold that leaf tea yesterday too?”

“Captain Mo. That was honest money.”

After hearing that we’d put on a play for the dungeon gatekeepers in exchange for money, Mo Haein wore the face of someone who had achieved enlightenment.

“…Alright. Let’s give it a try.”

After receiving her approval, I turned to Je Hyun-oh for the final decision.

“What do you think, Lieutenant Colonel Je?”

Could Dominic see my inventory window too?

*‘I’m curious what he and Infernum talked about earlier, too.’*

I’d have to find a chance to ask him discreetly later.

The dungeon in SL2PL was structured as a tower.

Once all normal monsters on a floor were defeated, a boss monster appeared.

Defeat the boss, and the next floor became accessible.

In the daytime dungeon, hidden bosses also appeared on floors with repeated digits.

Normal monsters and boss monsters dropped cooking ingredients upon death.

Hidden bosses, however, fled after defeat, leaving only ingredients behind.

Today’s objectives were:

\- The normal monster on Floor 32, the Flame Lizard.<br>- The boss monster on Floor 32, the Lava Golem.<br>- And the hidden boss on Floor 44, the Flame Mage.

Crack.

Cracking his knuckles, Kwak Hanmuk grinned.

“Let’s blast our way to Floor 44.”

Then he looked at Je Hyun-oh.

“Lieutenant Colonel Je, fighting!”

The plan was simple.

Je Hyun-oh would clear the way at top speed.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein would farm materials on Floor 32.

Je Hyun-oh and I would continue on to Floor 44.
