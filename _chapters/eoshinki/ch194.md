<br>As soon as the words left my mouth, I stopped breathing.

The very idea of demanding something from that being made no sense at all.

Calling it bold was a massive understatement.

In a fraction of a second, I could be crushed like an ant and meet a vain, bad ending.

No, that possibility was practically a hundred percent.

Yet, I threw myself into a probability that might be even smaller than 0.002%.

A low chuckle emanated from the blue eyes right in front of me.

<big>“Do you truly believe I won’t erase Roasted Chestnut from existence?”</big>

At that moment, goosebumps crawled down my spine.

A warning to run away shook my brain.

I almost stepped back on reflex, but I endured with all my might.

I couldn’t show weakness. Looking straight into his eyes, I answered.

“Yes.”

<big>“Why is that?”</big>

“Because… you want something from me.”

I understood that Dominic’s actions were a form of affection expressed in a way different from humans.

The golden cracks that had formed on my body were the signs of a Bloom.

Yet, the reason they looked similar to the previous traces of destruction was simple.

It was because I ultimately had to break.

Only by splitting the seed’s shell and pushing through the hard soil could I fully Bloom.

Dominic had fed me Infernum under the guise of children’s nutritional supplements, along with the eyeball of <span class="tooltip-container">Batial<span class="tooltip-text">the god from The Cell X, who used to be called ■■■</span></span>.

It was a semi-forced act, but thanks to that, I repaired my cracking body and managed to endure the shock of the Bloom.

Otherwise, my flesh would have shattered to pieces long before I even reached the Bloom.

The things Black Block and Dominic had done to me here in COF must have been help in a similar context.

They were trying to carve me down to fit the frame of a Young god.

It was because I, on the verge of Blooming, kept refusing and failing to accept the Young god.

I used the god's power in various situations as led by Black Block, drawing closer and closer to being a god.

However, even Dominic couldn't forcibly tear my humanity away from me.

Merging halfway with the Young god was the maximum limit.

It seemed that was likely because the choice to ‘discard humanity’ was something I had to decide for myself.

Even though I understood their affection, doubts still remained.

Black Block’s desire regarding me was clear.

Though twisted, it was the desire of a fan who wanted to watch every part of me.

However, Dominic’s purpose was still unclear.

He cherished me and wanted me to grow into a proper god, but...

It felt like there was some other desire that couldn't be explained by that alone.

“I figured you wouldn’t erase my existence until you achieved what you wanted.”

Having come to understand the gods a little better, I realized this to be true.

The ancient gods were omnipotent. They held far too much information.

It was close to impossible to catch the interest of beings who had mastered all logic, yet Dominic was an entity that even gods dared not fathom.

And I was the target that had sparked enough interest for such an entity to want something.

Therefore, I judged that Dominic would never easily make me vanish.

<big>“Roasted Chestnut.”</big>

The blue eyes slowly shifted their shape. They were molded once more into the appearance of imitating a human.

A three-meter-tall man looked down at me.

“He is still young and has much to learn.”

He touched my forehead with his fingertips. He slowly moved his fingertips downward, tracing a line.

An illusion that my skin was peeling off followed the sensation of the touch.

“I could force Roasted Chestnut to shed his skin right now, if I wanted to.”

The finger that had traced down my face passed my neck and reached my chest.

Pressing gently against me with his finger, Dominic leisurely uttered terrifying words.

“Even if I don’t make Roasted Chestnut vanish, I could make him choose to discard his humanity on his own.”

I couldn't breathe.

His plain tone carried no intent of a threat.

It merely announced, as it was, an unfathomable gap that couldn't even be perceived.

I could feel how much restraint *He* was exercising and how much *He* was ‘sparing’ me so as not to break my existence.

Making me climb the Tower had been despairing and agonizing to me, but to him, it was an indulgent method, so merciful that it was no different from playing with a young child.

“Do you believe I will let Roasted Chestnut do as he pleases without any price?”

The casual question pressed down on me. Unable to breathe, my face pale, I finally forced out an answer.

“Yes…”

The one who had taken in every fragment of my terror pulled away the hand pressing down on my chest.

Only then did the time of the paused world begin to flow again.

“It seems there is still something worth receiving from the Young god.”

Dominic held out his hand to me.

“It will suffice for Roasted Chestnut to make a new contract with me.”

A gentlemanly attitude permeated the gesture, reminiscent of someone asking for a handshake.

However, I knew the consequence I would face the moment I took that hand.

I had already made a contract with him once. The promise to stay together until he fully regained his existence.

If I made an even greater contract here, there would be no turning back.

“...Mr. Dominic.”

I painstakingly parted my lips.

Looking into the terrifying eyes beneath the guise of a human form, I forcibly moved my tongue, which wanted nothing more than to stay still.

“To be honest, I can't make a sponsorship contract. Even so, I...”

I poured my sincerity into my words toward my unreasonable understanding.

“...consider Mr. Dominic my very first Sponsor and guardian.”

Undeniably a terrifying disaster, but still, I... didn't hate Dominic.

Even when his methods were often wrong, he ultimately always looked out for me.

Recalling the time I had spent with Dominic, I asked.

“Can't we make a promise instead of a contract?”

“......”

“A promise that we will stay together even after Mr. Dominic fully regains his existence.”

The gaze looking down at me held a peculiar nuance.

In that gaze, I could feel it once again: he was trying to understand me.

At the end of an eternity-like fraction of a second, Dominic muttered briefly.

“A promise...”

He continued slowly.

“In that case, Roasted Chestnut, you must make a promise to me as well.”

Dominic slowly tilted his head toward me. His face drew close within a hair-raising distance.

<big>“If Roasted Chestnut's planet forsakes its master once more.”</big>

The words he uttered felt as though they were being carved into my brain one letter at a time.

<big>“At that time, I will help Roasted Chestnut in my own way.”</big>

And then, he demanded of me.

<big>“Will you promise?”</big>

I didn't understand all of Dominic's words.

That was because I had no way of knowing what circumstances could possibly lead a planet to forsake its master.

*'Besides, the fact that I'm Earth's god is a secret known to only a tiny few.'*

The only ones who properly understood and believed in the concept of a 'god' were probably the Special Team members.

It seemed I needed to grow even more than I had now to fully understand Dominic's words.

Anyway, I had to accept at least this much. This was a proposal where Dominic had backed down and compromised a lot.

I answered Dominic.

“Yes. I promise.”

He gazed at me in silence. Then, he pulled back the face he had pressed close.

After letting out a sigh-like laugh for a moment, Dominic soon spoke to me in a stern voice.

“You cannot postpone the skin-shedding forever. Someday, Roasted Chestnut, you must let go.”

Do I need to become a 3m alien too? Daily life is going to be so inconvenient....

As I looked up at Dominic with a slightly helpless expression, a smile crossed his eyes as if he had read my mind.

“You won't be my size just yet.”

“Then...?”

“After molting, you should be about the size of Roasted Chestnut's palm.”

That in itself would be terribly inconvenient. I wanted to molt when I grew to around 181cm.

It seemed like a distant future event anyway, so I first gave Dominic a polite bow.

“Anyway, I'll be in your care from now on.”

I wanted to ask if he had any plans to go back to being a Pudding Slime, but I held it in and asked politely instead.

“And if you don't mind, can you please forgive Double M and the subordinates too?”

I asked him not to punish them, even though they had gone against Dominic's wishes.

At that, Dominic smiled silently for a moment.

It was a smile that made it seem like Double M would have been erased from Akasha if I hadn't brought him up.

“If Roasted Chestnut wishes it, I will do so.”

Having barely obtained his permission, I felt a wave of inner relief and quickly called out to Double M.

“Double M. Come out.”

Then, an absurd thing happened. Something small and squishy dropped right out of thin air.

I instinctively reached out my hand and caught it.

“How is this, Little Lamb!”

A small square block shimmering in deep navy blue squished around on my palm.

“It's a form that perfectly matches the nickname the Little Lamb gave me!”

And in the middle of all that, the sight of him wearing a tiny monocle was... actually kind of cute....

I tried calling out to the small block presumed to be Double M.

“Double M...?”

“Please call me Black Block!”

“.......”

He wasn't even black, yet he was shamelessly demanding that title.

Perhaps reading my gaze, his body squiggly-wiggled.

Then, showing off his transformation, he boasted that a sparkling navy blue stood out much better than black and would surely win the Little Lamb's heart.

Watching such a hypocritical display, I felt the gratitude I had been feeling toward Double M evaporate a little.

However, since I was in a position where I needed to ask for a favor, I just called him what he wanted for now.

“Black Block. Give me just a little bit of your territory.”

I didn't bother using overly polite honorifics. He seemed to like this better.

The moment he heard my words, Black Block started jumping up and down excitedly on my palm.

“Territory! You're saying you'll make the Little Lamb's territory inside my Tower!”

Even though I was the one asking a favor, somehow it felt like I was doing Black Block a huge favor.

“It's not like I'm asking for your help for free, you know.”

I knew that receiving help without any compensation was dangerous.

This wasn't even a minor matter; I was trying to create my own territory right in the center of Hyper Eden.

There had to be a commensurate price paid to avoid any trouble.

That was why I set a suitable condition.

“I'll make the Young god channel part of Black Block's agency.”

Double M was the god of mass media.

If I expanded a new media channel for someone like him, there could be no more appropriate compensation than this.

Besides, I had been planning to keep broadcasting to aliens in the future anyway.

*'I need to earn Star Fragments.'*

So I figured it would be better to have an agency that could manage my cosmic broadcasts.

Though of course, the profit split would probably be around 99.999% for me and 0.001% for Black Block.

“Since we already have that image rights contract planned, I'll just include it in there...”

I trailed off, startled.

The Black Block on my palm was flashing with a fluorescent sky-blue glow and growing larger.

<big>“L i t t l e  L a m b!”</big>

It was when he had grown to the size of a teddy bear.

“Roasted Chestnut.”

As soon as Dominic called my name, Black Block instantly *WHOOSHED* right back down to his small form.

“Isn't he a bit noisy?”

It sounded like a remark that would make him shut his mouth forever. I quickly stopped Dominic.

Black Block spoke again in his tiny voice.

<small>“Little Lamb is my star! The contract is already prepared. I'll send it as soon as Little Lamb returns to Earth!”</small>

After whispering those words, he opened up a portion of his territory to me.

I could feel Black Block pulling back his power. I quickly pushed my own power into the newly emptied gap.

It was an unpolished yet brightly sparkling power.

When I repainted the ownerless small territory according to my will, a neat golden square formed beneath my feet.

The territory formed by pushing away another god's divine power was entirely under my control.

Black Block, who had scrambled back up to the top of my head in the meantime, opened up the space where the subordinates, the sponsors, and Winter were trapped.

I moved the people into my territory while using my power.

*'Resurrection.'*

The territory was barely the size of a <span class="tooltip-container">single pyeong<span class="tooltip-text">approx. 3.3 square meters</span></span>, so I created several small golden spheres. 

Then I placed each of those trapped in a state of suspended animation into a sphere.

Only after confirming that the percentage was rising smoothly did I feel a bit at ease.

Floating the spheres in midair, I hurriedly checked on the person I was most worried about.

*'Captain Kwak....'*

The prayer beads made of lightning-struck wood that always hung around his wrist were nowhere to be seen.

This time, he had definitely broken the seal completely.

When I peeked slightly inside, the blue lightning tattoo was rapidly spreading.

The god had returned back inside Kwak Hanmuk, but once broken, the seal had now slackened.

The divine power leaking out through that gap was slowly eroding his body and soul.

The words "collapse due to fatal damage" kept echoing in my head.

I stared at the golden sphere containing Kwak Hanmuk and thought.

If I pulled the god out right now, he wouldn't be able to withstand the shock and would break apart.

First, I needed to find a way to stop the collapse, but...

*'Galactic Trading Post.'*

There was a decent item among the Trading Post's subordinate items.

It was an item I had been eyeing for a while, thinking it would be good to give to Kwak Hanmuk.

As for Star Fragments, I figured I could just ask Black Block for some as an advance payment, but the problem was that I was legally incompetent.

I had to find somewhere called an interstellar financial institution to resolve the frozen account issue.

Clutching the golden sphere to my chest, I cautiously looked up at Dominic.

“Mr. Dominic....”

“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”

“Do you remember the time my account was frozen the other day?”

“I remember.”

“Of course, it was mostly my fault for depositing a huge sum of money into the account all at once, but don't you think Mr. Dominic, my guardian, also bears a tiny bit of responsibility for failing to give me proper financial education?”

After deploying the 'guardian responsibility theory', I tentatively gauged his reaction.

Dominic smiled with his eyes lightly narrowed. I quickly got straight to the point.

“Please take me to the cosmic bank.”
