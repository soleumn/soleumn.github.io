Following that, other words were written down in a continuous stream.

The words continued like some sort of spell.

Since the palm was entirely filled and there was no space left, the writing continued onto the wrist.

I rolled up my sleeve slightly.

<br>

Once again, 3 characters were written down, and below that, other words continued up to my forearm.

<br>

The number of characters gradually decreased, and the final word was a solitary 1 character.

The handwriting was neat and pretty, making it nice to look at.

I waited, wondering if they would write something more, but there was nothing.

While I was thinking it was somehow a bit disappointing, Black Block called out to me.

<big>“Little Lamb?”</big>

At the same time, I was startled.

I had just realized that I had naturally tried to share a meal with Black Block.

Dog food made with these suspicious ingredients, at that.

I hid the palm where the words were written.

“I appreciate the offer, but I'm fine. It's a dish I made for Mr. Black Block, after all.”

So I said I hoped Mr. Black Block would enjoy it to the fullest.

<big>“…….”</big>

For some reason, Black Block was silent.

The hand that had been covering the top of the studio moved slowly.

The shadow cast over my head rippled along with the movement.

Black Block was taking off his glove.

At the exposed hand, my lips parted.

It possessed a faint, glowing dark indigo color. It was a skin color humans could not possibly have.

Tattooed on the back of the hand was a fluorescent sky-blue eye-shaped mark.

The lines connected to the fingers, and there was also a small eye-shaped tattoo on each individual finger.

There were 6 eyes on a single hand.

The pupils drawn on the back of the hand and the fingers turned simultaneously toward the stew and the bread.

Then, the fingertips of Black Block touched the stew.

Starting from the part where his hand touched, the dish crumbled into golden light.

Golden sparkles, like small swarms of stars, seeped into his hand.

The hand surrounded by the golden light shivered finely.

Watching him look as if he were savoring the taste, I didn't feel particularly good.

*‘I should have made it more like dog food.’*

It felt like I had made it too well.

Only after a long while did Black Block open his mouth.

<big>“It was a perfect news broadcast….”</big>

It was a voice as satisfied as if he had enjoyed a magnificent banquet.

<big>“No matter how outstanding a report may be, it cannot capture the innermost emotions of the target! Being able to know Little Lamb inside and out was immensely flavorful.”</big>

I wanted to see what kind of face Black Block was making, but it was blocked by his hand and couldn't be seen.

*‘Why is he staying building-sized, anyway?’*

Surely he wasn't going to keep talking in this state.

As I looked up with my neck straining, Black Block called me.

<big>“Little Lamb.”</big>

“Yes, Mr. Black Block.”

<big>“Little Lamb will become a magnificent god. There are already many who hold Little Lamb up as a god.”</big>

The hand withdrew, revealing Black Block's previously hidden face. He tilted his head toward me.

<big>“There is no reason to cling to trivial parts. Little Lamb, too, looked back on memories just like me. Aren't you wishing for it deep within your heart?”</big>

Black Block spread both hands. He spoke as if speaking for my heart.

*<big>“To become a perfect young god!”</big>*

That wasn't necessarily true.

I remembered the NPCs I had saved.

It was a work that wouldn't have been achieved without the power of Young god.

However, it was also work that wouldn't have been done if I had been a perfect Young god.

*‘What I truly want to become.’*

I looked up at Black Block, who looked like he would rush in like a tidal wave at any moment.

The difference between him and me was felt anew.

Black Block was so colossal, and compared to him, I was still endlessly small.

The palm with the writing on it itched.

I felt a certain weight continuing from the palm to the wrist, and then to the forearm.

It was a weight pulling me downward.

A memory came to mind.

I was looking for an anchor.

An anchor that wouldn't be swept away even by the disaster called a god.

It was the advice Black Block had given me when I said I didn't want to lose my humanity.

When I didn't answer, a suspicious mutter dropped from Black Block.

<big>“This is not enough.”</big>

He pulled back the head he had tilted toward me and said playfully.

<big>“Little Lamb, from now on it will be even more fun. First, let's keep our promise! Starting from number 666!”</big>

I waited with anticipation. But no change occurred.

<big>“Ah! Who was number 666!”</big>

“…….”

I had known he would treat Cecilia like a mere insignificant creature, but I hadn't expected him to be this indifferent.

When I stared at him with an absurd look, Black Block hurriedly waved his hand.

<big>“Don't worry, Little Lamb. I possess convenient things that will take care of troublesome matters.”</big>

He extended a finger and tapped lightly on the floor in front of me with a thud.

Then, a fluorescent sky-blue light rippled from the floor, and a woman appeared.

“…!”

I, who had been about to smile brightly and call out ‘Noona,’ had my expression freeze.

It wasn't Cecilia.

Yet, she bore a striking resemblance to Cecilia. The only difference from her was that she possessed white skin.

The woman immediately knelt on one knee toward Black Block.

“Master! My merciful Messiah!”

Thrilled with ecstasy, she shouted a hymn of praise to her god.

“I will not forget this grace and will strive and strive even further in the future. So that the entirety of Akasha may rest within Master’s frame, I shall dedicate only the most provocative and glorious scoops!”

Far from answering, Black Block didn't even react.

Even though she received not a single glance, the woman was filled with fervent and blind loyalty and love for her god.

Soon, she rose to her body and turned back to look at me.

A chillingly familiar title struck me.

“Player!”

I….

Couldn't say a single word.

“Ah, we decided to call you by a different title now! I'm sorry, Little Lamb.”

Sa Gongyeon, who looked identical to Cecilia, said with a bright smile.

She looked at me with genuinely affectionate eyes and spoke.

“Thank you. Thanks to Little Lamb, I was able to bring my sister back to life.”

It was absurd. 

Cecilia's longed-for younger sibling was Sa Gongyeon?

*'That doesn't make any sense.'*

Yet I couldn't even breathe properly.

In front of me, standing stiffly, Sa Gongyeon bowed deeply in a Korean-style greeting.

She clapped her hands together with a sharp sound and shouted.

“Sister!”

Then, fluorescent sky-blue particles appeared from the empty air.

Scattered like fireflies, they gathered together.

They first tried to form the shape of eyeballs.

However, they failed to take shape and scattered, creating a heart instead.

The heart, formed of muscle, made a thumping, pulsing sound.

From then on, it was in an instant.

A web of red blood vessels exploded outward like a net from the heart.

Ribs spread left and right like tree branches to wrap around the thoracic cavity, and the vertebrae of the spine connected sequentially, stretching long up and down.

Tough muscle fibers tangled around the bones like skeins of thread, forging the shape of limbs.

Tendons attached to the bones, and muscle layers piled up in layers, swelling smoothly to create a human outline.

Amidst the blindingly flashing fluorescent sky-blue light, skin tissue finally covered the muscle surface, forming smooth flesh.

However, Cecilia could not open her eyes.

It was because the parts where her eyeballs should have been were completely empty.

As if someone had plucked them out.



“There is something a god does first when taking a subordinate.”

“It is plucking out the eyes. So that they look only upon the master. In the best state to instill loyal, blind faith.”



Sa Gongyeon pulled a case from her bosom and popped the lid open with a click.

Then, she inserted artificial eyeballs taken from the moist culture medium into the empty sockets.

Cecilia slowly moved her pupils and looked at me.

Our gazes met.

Sa Gongyeon hugged Cecilia and rejoiced.

“I'm glad you're back, Sister! Let's serve Master even more sincerely in this life!”

It was words that showed not a single hint of resentment toward Black Block, who had actually killed Cecilia.

Cecilia simply stared quietly back at me, embraced in Sa Gongyeon's arms.

I felt like something snapped in my head.

I turned my head and glared at Black Block.

“Did you make Cecilia into a subordinate just now?”

A subordinate is the property of a god.

The beginning and end of life were tied solely to the god's hands.

If the master desired, they had to welcome even death, and if the master commanded, they had to perish without leaving a single breath behind.

That was why Sa Gongyeon was able to revive Cecilia by receiving the master's authority.

Simply, as if making a toy.

Could this be what it meant to revive Cecilia?

It felt like my head was getting hot.

However, the answer came not from Black Block, but from Sa Gongyeon.

“Sister has been Master's subordinate for a long time.”

Sa Gongyeon said to me, still holding Cecilia close.

“Didn't Little Lamb create a new life for Sister? With the Physical Regeneration Package!”

The moment I heard her words, I realized.

The one who turned Cecilia into Black Block's subordinate.

Was none other than me.

*‘Resurrection...’*

No matter how advanced science became, there was no way to bring back someone who was already dead.

The Physical Regeneration Package was a means to forge a subordinate contract with Black Block in exchange for ‘Resurrection’.

That must be why unauthorized labor grades were demoted to F-level when they used it.

“It was hard because I couldn't persuade Sister for a long time, but thanks to Little Lamb making her a subordinate, we can work together for Master.”

Because of me, Cecilia became Black Block's subordinate.

Even though she hadn't wanted it so much that she even rejected the persuasion of the younger sister she had longed for.

Cecilia called out to me.

“Little Lamb.”

Using a title I had never heard before.

“N-Noona.”

I stammered like a fool and called out to her.

However, Cecilia continued coldly with a expressionless face.

“Thank you for attending the Masquerade Ball. It was what Master desired and wished for. I apologize for the rudeness of approaching Little Lamb while hiding our purpose.”

“…….”

“Thanks to Little Lamb, I was reborn and am living happily while serving my Master.”

*‘Lying.’*

The affection Cecilia had shown me was something that could never be faked.

A foul emotion welled up.

It was a sense of helplessness, knowing I had forced Cecilia into having to lie.

The reality of what I had thought was saving her, of being helpful, was horrific.

I had burdened Cecilia with an inescapable yoke.

Then what was it that the human Han Goyo had accomplished?

I am…….

What kind of usefulness do I have?

<big>“Little Lamb.”</big>

A deep navy hand reached out toward me as I stood blankly.

A single finger gently pushed me. The finger, with me at the center, traced a circle on the ground.

<big>“It hurts, doesn’t it? It’s alright! I will guide you so that Little Lamb no longer hurts or suffers!”</big>

“…….”

<big>“Come with me. Only fun and exciting things will await you. I solemnly promise you!”</big>

A fluorescent sky-blue circle surrounded me. I could feel the gazes of Cecilia and Sa Gongyeon watching me.

Everyone was waiting for the obvious choice I had to make.

However, I couldn't readily answer. My lips wouldn't open.

When I failed to reply in the end, the finger that had been leisurely circling around me stopped moving.

<big>“Oh my goodness…. It’s only because you are still young. I shall help the Little Lamb's judgment!”</big>

The finger lightly pressed down on the floor.

My eyes widened. The floor collapsed along a circular arc, and my body plummeted.

Along with a fall of unknown depth, I saw screens covering all directions.

They were all about me.

The screens, changing frantically by the second, felt like someone was wildly flipping through TV channels.

A voice that shook the space dug into my brain.

<big>“Little Lamb! Climb the Tower! If your mind does not change even after climbing to the top of the Tower, I too will cheer for the Little Lamb!”</big>

I fell to the very bottom of the Tower.

I squeezed my eyes shut, bracing for horrific pain. But no pain came.

“…!”

The Han Goyos of the 1st floor of the Tower were holding me up.

They were twisted into shapes humans couldn't possibly manage, as if they had rushed out in a panic.

I hadn't expected the Black Block's possessions to help me. I blankly shifted my gaze.

A structure that hadn't existed before caught my eye.

It was a spiral staircase.

Climbing endlessly, it seemed to lead all the way to the very top of the Tower.

As I looked up at the stairs, the Han Goyos held me and spoke.

“You mustn’t go.”

“Stay here.”

“Stay with us.”

Limp and utterly drained of strength, I asked slowly,

“…Why?”

Then, the Han Goyo wearing a player’s uniform revealed the truth to me.

“Because no one is waiting for you.”

I hesitated, my lips parting slightly.

“But… someone keeps calling out to me…”

For a while now, I had been hearing a voice—a voice that sounded as if it were desperately waiting for me to reach the top of the tower.

*'It might just be an auditory hallucination I made up, though.'*

As the Han Goyo in the player’s uniform had said, if no one was actually waiting for me, it was highly likely that this was nothing more than a delusion I had created on my own.

Because, after all—

The human Han Goyo is someone who always fails.

“It’s alright.”

“If you stay here, you won’t have to feel any pain or suffering.”

“We’ll protect you.”

The Han Goyos set me down on the floor, each offering these words.

As I listened in silence, I suddenly glanced at my own palm.

As soon as I saw the letters whose meaning I couldn't understand, I muttered to myself without realizing it.

“…I have to go up.” 

I didn’t know why. It just felt like something I had to do.

Countless hands reached out to hold me back, but I pushed them all away and finally set foot on the stairs.

In that instant, the profound silence vanished like a mirage.

Left all alone, I looked up toward the sky.

There were so many stairs…

I took a deep breath and started running up the stairs.

It was around the time when I figured I had climbed five or six floors.

A hologram screen blocked my path.

The screen showed me wearing a player's uniform.

I recognized it immediately. No, I had no choice but to recognize it.

The last match before retirement.

It was me on the day of the finals.
