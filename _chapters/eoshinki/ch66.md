I ended up going out to sell Grass Leaf Tea together with Kwak Hanmuk.

When he heard me say that I was going into the dungeon to do business, Kwak Hanmuk was confused.

That was because Sla2Plate’s dungeon only opened at night, and entry was only possible from level 10 or higher.

I explained the future course of action to Kwak Hanmuk in detail.

Then, while bringing along the person who kept sneaking glances at Dominic, I passed through the village and arrived at the dungeon in the eastern forest.

It was a place that did not match the peaceful atmosphere of the village at all.

A massive door made of black obsidian overwhelmed anyone who approached.

Complex patterns were deeply engraved into the surface of the door, and vivid red magma flowed through the grooves like veins.

Its faint red glow made it look like a living creature breathing.

As I approached the door that resembled the entrance to hell, I could feel heat hot enough to burn my skin.

I looked up at the door and shouted loudly.

“Is anyone there? Please open the door!”

Then flames shot up on both sides of the door.

Two fluffy, adorable children appeared from within the pillars of fire.

The children had androgynous appearances, bat wings on their backs, and two red horns growing on either side of their light pink hair.

Their long, drooping lizard tails gracefully swept across the ground.

Their pink eyes with sharp, reptilian pupils stared at me.

They were the demon-type Fire Dragon Tribe twins who guarded the dungeon entrance.

The female, Ella, had eyes with raised outer corners, while the male, Ellun, had eyes with lowered outer corners.

Aside from that, their faces and height were completely identical, as if they had been copied.

The powerful level 99 twins served the role of filtering out visitors to the dungeon.

Because they cruelly executed the unqualified with flames, they were a nightmare to beginners who came to the dungeon without knowing better.

The twins both spoke with equally cold expressions.

“Foolish human. You dare come seeking the entrance to hell.”

“You still lack the qualifications to enter this place. Atone through death.”

However, they had what could be called a single weakness.

I fell to my knees in front of the twins and shouted.

“It was a mistake! Please spare me!”

Perhaps because they were still young, they were weak to someone pretending to be pitiful.

Even in the game, the twins’ hearts were easily swayed, and after helping the player in many ways, they eventually came to truly consider them a friend.
