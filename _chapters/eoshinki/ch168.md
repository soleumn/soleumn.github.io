<br>The moment I called out to it, half-doubting, the white glove eagerly clung to my hand.

A speech bubble with a flashing star dot graphic appeared above the white glove.

I recalled the name Black Block had used to address me during our last communication.

<br>

—Little Lamb, my star! Let's communicate again. As your fan, I will continue to watch you. Farewell for now...

<br>

Given the circumstances, it was definitely Black Block.

I had suspected a connection between the current situation and Black Block from the start. After all, the Event Quest in this arcade was similar to the one I'd received from DeZonDeal's broadcasting station.

But I never expected him to intervene so directly.

*Why would he do something like this?*

Black Block knew all too well how much I cherished my subordinates. Yet, he had still done something that would infuriate me.

I wondered why he was revealing himself without even trying to hide. His boldness suggested he didn't even think his actions were wrong.

*'So much for being my fan.'*

Perhaps because it was a god, it acted completely differently from any fan I knew.

I pushed the Young god's thoughts, which were raging in a corner of my mind, aside. Honestly, I was furious too, but this wasn't a matter to be solved emotionally.

I calmly assessed the situation.

*'Could it have heard my voice?'*

The way it reacted even though I'd spoken outside the game made me think so.

Just as I was about to try talking to it, pixelated text appeared.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh hopes the god will not speak with that.
</div>

<br>

The pixel character on the screen spread its metal hand wide.

This time, my fingers might actually get cut off.

I cautiously spoke up, reading the room.

"Return my subordinates to their original state."

A teary-eyed speech bubble floated above the white glove. It seemed to be a refusal, but I wasn't surprised—I had expected as much.

"Then let me Clear the game."

Since it clearly had no intention of returning my subordinates, I wanted to Clear the game as quickly as possible, following the rules set by Black Block.

*'If you're going to show up as the final boss, then act like one.'*

I poured all my frustration into those words, not wanting to waste any more time, but the white glove seemed to have no intention of doing its job as a boss.

Just as I was about to push away the hand that kept clinging to me, Je Hyun-oh moved first. He rained down crescent moons on the wound where glitch fragments were leaking out.

The white glove flinched and retreated.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh was hoping the god wouldn't come into contact with that thing.
</div>

<br>

Je Hyun-oh had every reason to distrust me after everything we'd been through.

After all, the last time we entered The Cell X together, I'd made contact with another god and started acting strangely.

Fortunately, the white glove that Je Hyun-oh had attacked regained its composure. It limped back to its original position, leaving a trail of glitch fragments in its wake.

It was finally about to fulfill its role as a Boss.

The white glove gave a light wave.

The announcement signaling the start of the boss battle appeared, and the music changed.

I should have withdrawn my hand from the screen now, but I hesitated. I was worried.

*'Maybe I'll just see how the boss battle unfolds before I pull out,'* I thought.

But something was off.

Its attack style and patterns were completely different from any Boss we'd faced before. It felt... abnormal.

While other bosses attacked by firing laser beams or light beam bullets...

The white glove shot hearts.

I quickly stepped in front of Je Hyun-oh to block the countless hearts flying toward him.

A slight sting shot through my hand as a heart struck it. Of course, it hurt far less than Je Hyun-oh's attacks.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh was hoping the god would withdraw its hand immediately.
</div>

<br>

Pixelated text appeared, conveying what would have been said as, "Goyo. Put it away."

Perhaps because it was inside a game, the white glove's attack was relatively mild.

It seemed Je Hyun-oh could deal damage himself, so there was no need to worry too much.

I slowly pulled my hand away from the screen. The white glove displayed a teary speech bubble, as if pleading with me not to go, but I ignored it.

And the moment I placed both hands on the control panel—

*Rumble!*

A deep bass, loud enough to tear the speakers, rumbled through the room.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  !!WARNING!!
</div>

<br>

The word flashed across the center of the screen.

The white glove moved its hand with a sudden, fluid grace. An onslaught of attacks—insanely difficult—began to rain down.

The same white hand that had just been shooting hearts and sparkles now filled the entire screen with a laser beam.

Neon cyan flashes formed a grid of light as they poured down.

I jerked the joystick, and Je Hyun-oh narrowly slipped through the grid's intersection.

The moment we dodged, a fan-shaped beam of light erupted, threatening to swallow the entire screen.

Along with it, a swarm of neon cyan homing missiles poured out, exploding all around us.

Neon light flashed from every direction.

There was no time to even catch my breath, but I refused to let a single hit land.

I gritted my teeth, attacking with the main weapon whenever I found a gap to dodge.

That was when Je Hyun-oh slipped from my grasp.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh never prioritized his own safety. He deemed the god's overprotection unnecessary.
</div>

<br>

He flew toward the white glove, weaving through the air like an acrobat. Every time a beam he couldn't dodge grazed the tips of his wings, his health plummeted.

I hated seeing my subordinate get hurt—it was unbearable. I frantically moved the joystick, but Je Hyun-oh refused my control.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh wanted the god to watch him.
</div>

<br>

At last, he broke through the relentless barrage of attacks and reached the white glove. His health was nearly depleted—a single hit would be fatal.

Just as I resolved to force control, Je Hyun-oh lunged at the white glove and violently swung his metal hand.

The wound, already leaking glitch fragments, tore open even wider.

Before the white glove could unleash a new attack, the black crow dove into the gaping wound and vanished.

"...!"

I shot up from my chair, my heart pounding.

*'This wasn't a move you'd see in a shooting game.'*

I froze for a moment, stunned by the rule-breaking attack. Then, through the blaring boss battle music, a sickening *skreeeech*—the sound of something twisting and tearing—echoed repeatedly.

An exclamation mark bubbled up above the white glove.

Black lines slashed across its surface, carving into it like a thousand knives. Then came a series of sharp *cracks* as it began to shatter along those very lines.

As the glove tore apart, a torrent of glitch fragments erupted from the gaps.

From the blinding storm of glitches, a dark figure emerged, spreading its wings and gliding out.

I pressed a hand to my chest, confirming Je Hyun-oh was safe. My heart was racing—not good for me at all.

Yet, a slow smile crept across my lips. The sheer power of my subordinate filled me with pride.

Just as I was about to shelter him with a Golden Veil out of a desire to praise him, the entire screen suddenly washed white.

The blinding white light gradually faded, giving way to large, bold letters.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  ★ALL STAGE CLEAR★<br>

CONGRATULATIONS!
</div>

<br>

It was a Clear.

A message announcing a new high score appeared, followed by a taunting prompt:

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  CONTINUE?
</div>

<br>

Continue my ass.

As I scowled at the absurd message, the text slowly dissolved.

*Clatter.* A coin rolled out of the dispenser.

I picked up Je Hyun-oh's Clear Coin.

Only after confirming Je Hyun-oh's pixel character face on the coin could I finally relax.

I had been worried because the final boss battle was against Black Block, but it seemed the Clear had been successful.

*'Is Lieutenant Colonel Je okay?'*

I was also worried about what had happened to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein after the Clear. I wanted to return everyone to their original state as quickly as possible.

"Good job, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic congratulated me on my Clear. I thanked him and glanced at him sideways.

He had expressed displeasure during my communication with Black Block before. I wondered if he was going to say something this time, but instead, he seemed satisfied with the situation.

It was probably because I had used so much of the god's power...

But the thought of Dominic being pleased made me uneasy. I had a bad feeling about it.

At any rate, the most urgent task was to restore the Special Team to their original state.

I went straight to the exchange and hesitated for a moment.

They had said to inquire directly if I wanted a different prize.

But the exchange consisted of nothing more than a coin slot and what looked like a prize dispenser.

There was no space where a person could be, nor did I sense any presence.

Though it seemed empty, I spoke up anyway.

"Excuse me. I would like to exchange my Clear Coins for a different prize. I want to get my subordinates back."

Then, with a *clatter*, something dropped into the prize dispenser.

I picked it up and checked. It was popping candy, a participation prize.

The flashy wrapper, adorned with an illustration of a bursting star, bore the name "Supernova Pang Pang."

*'Would Captain Kwak like this?'*

Knowing his fondness for snacks, I stowed it in my inventory for now.

Meanwhile, the text on the prize information board shifted.

The entire list of prizes vanished, replaced by a new sentence.

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
  One (1) Abnormal Clear Coin has been detected. The exchange for a subordinate will proceed, excluding the aforementioned Clear Coin.
</div>

My eyes widened at the message.

The two Clear Coins in my hand glowed and then vanished.

Only Je Hyun-oh's Clear Coin remained in my palm.

I froze, staring at the single coin left behind.

Why?

My head felt like it was about to explode.

Of course, Je Hyun-oh *had* cleared the game using an attack method that didn't exist in the game's mechanics.

But before that, hadn't Black Block been the one to act out of line first?

*'Black Block was the one who interfered abnormally in the first place.'*

Even in Kwak Hanmuk's racing game, the NPCs had been acting strangely. The criteria for what constituted an abnormal Clear were completely biased.

Just then, the sentences on the prize information sheet were erased clean.

*Scratch, scratch.*

The sound of writing on paper echoed in real-time.

A sentence appeared in elegant cursive. It was a classic and dignified script.

<div class='f-satisfy' style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 1.6rem !important;">
  Little Lamb, my star! Congratulations on your Clear. Your performance was truly magnificent!
</div>

I asked in a strained voice, "...What the hell do you think you're doing?"

But the other party didn't answer my question.

Though it must have heard me perfectly, it simply continued to say whatever it wanted.

<div class='f-satisfy' style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 1.6rem !important;">
  I am glad Little Lamb enjoyed it. You also seemed to find pleasure in managing your subordinates!
</div>

"..."

For a moment, I was speechless. I couldn't bring myself to deny it.

After all, even though the game had been forced upon me, there were moments when it truly brought me joy. The thrill of feeling a profound sense of unity with my subordinates still burned within me.

While I hesitated, a new sentence appeared.

<div class='f-satisfy' style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 1.6rem !important;">
  Come find me, Little Lamb! I will even exchange the Abnormal Clear Coins for you.
</div>

It was a blatant demand, with no attempt to hide its true intentions.

As I glared with a stony expression, the text continued to scrawl across the screen with a soft, scratching sound.

<div class='f-satisfy' style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 1.6rem !important;">
  I have so many things in store that will make the Little Lamb's time even more delightful and entertaining!<br><br>

This would also be the perfect opportunity for Little Lamb to treat me to a meal.<br><br>

We will have such a wonderful time together. I am eagerly looking forward to it!
</div>

I stared blankly at the self-indulgent ramblings before finally speaking.

"Give me back my subordinate."

I had forced myself to stay calm before speaking.

But as always, Black Block ended the conversation on its own terms.

<div class='f-satisfy' style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 1.6rem !important;">
  I will be counting the days until you find me, Little Lamb. Farewell...
</div>

"Wait! Don't go...!"

I screamed, reaching for the prize guide.

But as the final period was placed, the world collapsed.

A fluorescent sky-blue, so bright it hurt my eyes, flooded the space and poured down.

When I came to, I was outside a fast-food restaurant.

I slowly pushed myself up from the cold asphalt.

Still slumped on the ground, I breathed in and out, over and over.

But my seething emotions refused to subside.

"Goyo!"

Kwak Hanmuk, who had been lying nearby, also woke up and approached me.

I slowly turned my gaze. Mo Haein was still unconscious.

Kwak Hanmuk, who had been approaching me, paused. I could feel the golden light emanating from myself.

"Captain Kwak..."

As I murmured his name, Kwak Hanmuk abruptly grabbed my shoulder.

"Goyo, are you all right?"

I couldn't answer that I was fine. I was trying my best, but I couldn't hold on any longer.

My face contorted as I finally spoke.

*"They took my subordinate..."*

The thought that I shouldn't act strangely in front of the captains flashed faintly through my mind.

But I couldn't bear it any longer. The golden light enveloping me grew stronger.

"Roasted Chestnut. It's all right. I'm right here with you, aren't I? You can do whatever you want."

A gentle voice whispered to me.

The moment I heard those soothing words, I finally let go of what I had been desperately clinging to.

A sensation of falling into an abyss washed over me as a golden light, piercing the darkness, erupted outward.

A circular wave of energy, originating from my body, swept across all of Hyper Eden.

The city of night, once ablaze with garish holographic advertisements and neon signs, froze.

Thousands, tens of thousands of screens went dark simultaneously, and a brief silence fell.

Then, everything changed.

The billboards that had spanned the sky, the mega-strips on the building exteriors, even the floating displays suspended in mid-air—every screen in Hyper Eden was now covered by a single person.

It was me.

On the screens, I spoke.

*"Black Block... where are you?"*

My bright golden eyes scanned Hyper Eden.

Then I *commanded*, 

*"Come out. Now."*
