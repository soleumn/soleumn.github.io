I never imagined I'd see the day my photo and an article about me would appear in the Galactic Newspaper.

And on the front page, no less, taking up the entire spread.

I read it again, slowly, unable to believe my eyes, but there was no mistake.

It was my face. It was my story.

Once the reality fully sank in, only one thought crossed my mind.

*'Is it really this big?'*

The Administrator had once told me that a Young god like myself would naturally draw interest. He had also warned me to be especially cautious of the Old gods, since I had a domain and thus lacked protection.

But I never imagined I'd be a figure of such universal interest that I'd land on the front page of a newspaper.

*'But I couldn't even read the Day News in CallOVo...'*

Back then, everything had been blocked, and I could barely read the general knowledge section. Now, both the photo and the article were fully visible.

I had definitely grown stronger.

<big>—Sa Gongyeon—are you—out of your—mind—?! How—could you —use a young god—as a news story—!</big>

The Administrator was trembling with rage, his hair standing on end in all directions.

—Huuu, kid. Sorry for shouting.

He finally calmed down, looking regretful for raising his voice in front of me.

Though I was young by cosmic standards, I was an adult by Earth's standards, so his outburst didn't really bother me.

"It's fine. But does this article put me in danger now?"

—Yes. Because the existence of a young god has been publicly exposed.

The Administrator turned the page of the newspaper to show me the next one.

It was a section that required a paid subscription to read, yet it contained a detailed account of my situation from an alien's perspective.

It even laid bare the fact that a young god with a domain was living completely defenseless.

The only saving grace was that the planet's name and coordinates hadn't been leaked.

The special feature on the Young god was accompanied by a wide variety of photos: extreme close-ups of his face, full-body shots from head to toe, and even a zoomed-in shot of his golden eyes. There were photos of me sitting on my bed, dazed and just waking up—not just for PTB, but for everyone to see—and even one of me wearing my uniform loosely thrown on before heading to work.

*'And this one is from this morning!'*

No paparazzi had ever been this insane.

The article's content was also masterfully crafted. It seemed to reveal everything, yet it left just enough out to pique curiosity—even mine. It had perfectly set the stage for all sorts of aliens to take an interest.

I read the article carefully, considering it from every angle.

"Can we take the article down if the subject didn't consent to it?"

—It's going to be difficult because the kid isn't under our protection...

"I understand."

After listening to the whole explanation, I finally understood.

Having reached a conclusion, I spoke to the Administrator.

"I'll handle this matter."

—What?

The Administrator asked, his expression turning serious.

—Kid. You're not planning to contact the Butlers, are you?

I hadn't expected the Cat Butler and Rabbit Butler to be mentioned so soon. It seemed the Butlers wielded considerable influence, perhaps because they were Dominic's subordinates.

"That's not it."

I bowed politely to the anxious Administrator, thanking him for the information, and ended the call.

I let out a long sigh.

I was currently sitting in an empty conference room.

The recruit training had already begun some time ago. To catch up on the theoretical training that had already taken place, I was scheduled for a separate, intensive tutoring session today.

So I was waiting for the training in the meeting room alone when I suddenly received a call from the Administrator.

"Roasted Chestnut."

The Pudding Slime, who had been watching me calmly until then, called my name.

I spoke before he could say anything else.

"Mr. Dominic. I think I can handle this."

Normally, I would have asked him something.

But right now, I didn't feel like it.

I knew Dominic had no ill intentions when he gave me advice.

Even though he had recommended I pull out my own subordinate’s eyeballs, that was simply the right advice by his standards.

It was just that I found it difficult to understand, let alone accept.

I placed a collect call to Black Block.

He answered the Interstellar Communication immediately.

—Little Lamb! To think you would contact me first! What joy, what delight!

He spoke as if he had been watching me all along.

I addressed him in a calm tone.

"Mr. Black Block."

—Yes, please speak!

"I am truly disappointed."

—What? Disappointed!

Black Block cried out in alarm.

Before he could start sobbing, I spoke in a cold voice.

"I never expected you to publish an article about me in the Day News."

—The Day News! It is a most excellent newspaper. A true press with noble journalism. But Little Lamb...

I drove a wedge into Black Block, who was trying to feign ignorance and slip away.

"Reporter Sa Gongyeon is your subordinate, isn't he, Mr. Black Block?"

Silence.

I waited expressionlessly in the void where sound seemed to have been severed.

After a few seconds, a loud laugh erupted. It was so thunderous it momentarily deafened me.

— Hahaha! Correct, Little Lamb!

Just as I thought.

I had once received a quest from the broadcasting station in DeZonDeal, where zombies roamed.

<br>

It was a quest sent by Black Block, and the text read: 'The broadcasting station is a minion of ■■ ■■■.'

Furthermore, CallOVo had contacted me through Day News.

This was enough to make me suspect that Black Block was an entity connected to the media.

But that wasn't all.

Sa Gongyeon's behavior—throwing himself at me as a fan and obsessing over sponsorships—shared certain similarities with Black Block. I hadn't concluded they were the same person, but I had suspected some level of connection.

The incident on the front page of the newspaper was what finally gave me certainty.

It seemed obvious that Black Block was the superior being, with Sa Gongyeon as its subordinate. Yet, a mere reporter couldn't have broadcast Black Block's target of interest to the entire universe on his own.

The fact that Sa Gongyeon had flamboyantly decorated the front page of Day News with the birth of a young god meant he was acting on Black Block's orders.

At that moment, I guessed that Sa Gongyeon was a subordinate of Black Block, and I also realized the hidden scheme behind it all.

*'A sponsorship contract.'*

All sorts of Outer gods would be interested in the Young god and try to track the planet.

As a newcomer to Akasha, there was no way I could deal with so many Outer gods.

If I were to be cornered, I would look for someone to ask for help. Right now, there were only two beings around me with enough power to do so:

Dominic, or Black Block.

Since one could have multiple Patrons, even if I were to sign a sponsorship contract with Dominic, Black Block would have been looking for a gap to intervene.

All while flaunting his influence over the Cosmic Media to me.

If I hadn't known the truth, I might have signed sponsorship contracts with both of them just to defend Earth.

*'There's not a single alien in the universe I can trust.'*

The Pudding Slime had frozen my bank account, and Black Block had sold my face to the entire cosmos. The Administrator's description of this place as a lawless zone was spot on.

Surviving in this harsh universe, where children are given no special treatment, was going to be a struggle.

— Little Lamb. Let's sign a sponsorship contract.

Black Block no longer bothered to hide its true intentions. I wasn't surprised; I'd expected this to happen.

"What happens if I refuse?"

— Refuse? That would be a poor choice.

Black Block's voice began to distort eerily.

—What can a* Little Lamb* do all alone?

The words, laced with laughter, were drawn out slowly.

—Every day, new articles will be published, and more and more people will take an interest... Your small, cute Planet **might just get crushed!**

I frowned without even realizing it.

Just then, a short laugh rang out.

It was a sound teetering on the line between a dry chuckle and a sneer—low and not particularly loud.

Yet, it pierced my mind with startling clarity.

*And then, That Person spoke.*

<br>

*<big>"■■■■■■■■."</big>*

<br>

I heard nothing of what the voice said.

But I felt the tremor in space that rippled through the air at that moment.

If only a little more—just a tiny bit more—power had been infused into those words, the very fabric of reality around us would have twisted and torn asunder.

A distant terror shivered through my body.

I froze, my mind going completely blank.

Slowly, I regained my breath and confirmed that I was still here.

Only then did I very slowly turn my gaze.

On the desk, a small, squishy slime was shimmering.

It still looked like the cute little thing I knew.

A polite apology followed.

"I'm sorry, Roasted Chestnut. I've startled you."

He said he had made a mistake, as this wasn't something Young Chestnut should have heard yet, and comforted me, asking if I was very scared.

My lips trembled, but I could only manage to shake my head.

"That's a relief," he replied softly.

The person on the other end of the Interstellar Communication was just as frozen.

After a long silence, Black Block spoke.

—Little Lamb. I have been watching you for a very long time. Only you, Little Lamb...

His voice, once distorted, had returned to its original tone, and his previous arrogance seemed to have completely vanished. I didn't know what Dominic had told him, but he was clearly subdued.

Yet, he still refused to give up on me.

By now, I had finally recovered enough to think straight.

*'Now's my chance.'*

Seizing this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, I quickly spoke up.

"Mr. Black Block."

I started by dangling a carrot.

"I never imagined you'd been watching me for so long. You really are a devoted fan of mine."

—Ah, Little Lamb!

Just as he was about to be swept away by emotion, I immediately threw cold water on him.

"But to act like this..."

*'In truth, I was the one who shouldn't have been acting like this.'*

I was a low-level god, while he was a max-level god with full gear.

But?

I had Dominic, the oldest veteran god, backing me up.

So, I scrapped my original negotiation plan and decided to just dig my heels in.

"I'll send the food soon. Let's leave it at that."

I was telling him I'd send the meal as a parting gift, so he should stop contacting me.

Black Block went into a frenzy.

—Please, anything but that! I was wrong!! 

At this point, I wanted to send him a meal kit and tell him to cook the promised dish himself.

But I couldn't completely alienate Black Block.

He was a being who had been watching me for an unknown amount of time.

Moreover, he was already exerting his influence on Earth through Sa Gongyeon.

Even if I cast him aside now, there was a high probability I'd end up entangled with him again eventually.

*'And besides, Outer gods must be fought with Outer gods.'*

I now had a vague idea of what the Outer gods meant by "interest."

Judging by his actions so far, Black Block might have put me in danger, but he wasn't likely to throw me to other aliens as prey.

Unless, of course, he wanted to play the savior.

Crucially, he hadn't revealed the planet's name or coordinates.

—Little Lamb. What must I do to earn your forgiveness?

"Hmm, I suppose there might be a way..."

As I slowly trailed off, Black Block grew anxious.

—What is it? Please, tell me at once!

I waited long enough to make him sufficiently desperate before speaking.

"Settle the payment for my photos."

My face was already sold. I might as well earn a few Star Fragments.

How could I ever retrieve my face, already scattered across the universe?

Since things had come to this, I figured I might as well collect what I was owed.

"I'll keep providing you with photos," I said. "So I'd like to sign a formal contract."

— Little Lamb...

Black Block called my name, his voice trembling.

*'He's not actually upset over those Star Fragments, is he?'* I wondered, just as a deafening sound erupted from the communication channel.

— Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! Little Lamb! **<big>LITTLE LAMB!</big>**

A voice laced with madness screamed my name.

Through the static that tore through the line, a burst of laughter erupted, sounding like millions of people shrieking in unison.

Then came a sharp, piercing *beeeeeep*. Before long, the ear-splitting noise vanished.

In the ensuing silence, Black Block let out a deranged, wheezing chuckle. *Heh... hehehe...*

—Little Lamb, you are my star. As your longtime fan, every time this happens, ■■■■■■■....

Once again, he said something I couldn't understand.

Muttering to himself, Black Block finally addressed me.

—I will prepare a contract. A contract worthy of my star.

He made the promise with a look of profound emotion, then immediately returned to his delirious state, trying to tempt me again.

—But Little Lamb, a mere photograph won't be enough, will it? You need more Star Fragments. I have an excellent method!

"Mr. Black Block."

—Please, speak!

*Stop it. You're annoying me.*

—My apologies, Little Lamb....

Though I hadn't actually said those words aloud, the effect was so immediate that I decided not to correct him.

"Contact me when the contract is ready."

I planned to show the contract to the Administrator as well.

And since things had already come to this, I decided to ask the question I'd been meaning to.

"Mr. Black Block, you're interested in my current state, aren't you?"

—Indeed.

"Do you know of a way for me to maintain this state?"

—What state are you referring to?

I wasn't sure how Black Block would take it, but I carefully voiced my desire.

"I... I want to be a god who is still human."

I wanted to be a god who retained his humanity. If I had to accept the ego of the Young god, I wanted to be the one in control.

—How intriguing.

And to my dilemma, Black Block replied cheerfully.

—But the Little Lamb has never been human!
