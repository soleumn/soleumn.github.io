<br>I thought the spelling of Mad Messiah was strange, but it turned out to be an anagram of Mass Media.

Once I got the answer right, the System Window spat out a completely uninteresting explanation.

{% include system.html text="Both Mad Messiah and Mass Media refer to the master of Hyper Eden.<br>Those who <i>understand</i> him call him Double M!" %}

It seemed there were multiple names representing Black Block.

Just as I was called Little Lamb, Roasted Chestnut, Young god, and so on.

*'Double M….'*

I muttered it to myself, but it felt terribly awkward.

I decided to stick with calling him Black Block.

Dominic called me Pudding Slime, so Black Block was nothing.

He even said he liked it and considered it a nickname.

Honestly, Black Block was a little more approachable than Dominic.

Not that I’d go so far as to call him a total pervert… but, well, that was the general vibe.

*'I got the password right, so isn't it going to open the door?'*

I needed to find my subordinate quickly.

My heart wanted to press the skip button and bypass the explanation.

However, since the quest objective was interviewing Black Block, I endured it.

It was when I was holding out with the mindset of checking every single detail meticulously without missing a thing.

Finally, the door began to open.

Faint cutting lines formed on the solid metal surface, and soon it split into blade-like segments.

*Clatter, click.*

With intricate mechanical sounds, the metal plates rotated past each other.

The small gap that had formed at the center widened into a large circle.

The layers of metal shutters slid and disappeared into the wall.

It was a movement resembling the aperture of a camera lens locking onto a subject.

Brilliant light poured out from beyond the roundly opening door. It was electronic light emitted by hundreds of screens.

I checked the Acorn Compass. The needle was still pointing in the direction of the control room.

The Hide-and-Seek Event was not over yet.

As I stepped fully inside the control room, the door closed behind my back.

I looked around once, but no figure presumed to be Black Block was in sight.

The needle of the Acorn Compass was rotating slowly, as if signaling that we had arrived at the destination.

I could use it until I encountered the seeker, but it didn't seem like it would be of much use inside the control room right away.

The control room was much wider and deeper than I had imagined. 

It was a place with a thoroughly cyberpunk vibe, completely different from the classical atmosphere of the banquet hall.

The ceiling was dizzyingly high, and an entire wall was densely covered with hundreds of screens.

Monitors, big and small, relentlessly displayed all sorts of scenes.

CCTVs showing every corner of the banquet hall in real-time, wide-angle screens overlooking the skyscrapers of Hyper Eden, and all kinds of provocative scoops broadcast by the media poured down along the wall like a waterfall.

Standing and taking the waterfall of dizzying screens felt surreal.

It was the control room of the banquet hall, yet it encompassed the entirety of Hyper Eden.

It was a space where I could not only simply watch, but also output video tailored to the frame I wanted.

It felt like I could ‘control’ Hyper Eden from here.

It felt a little omnipotent.

Did Samra always feel this way?

There were times when I felt like Samra was looking down on humans.

I thought that if I lived like this forever, I might unconsciously start looking down on them too.

Mimicking Samra, I examined the screens.

However, since this wasn't the genuine Samra after all, it was hard to keep up with the screens that changed in real time.

Even when I tried to select and view only the banquet hall CCTVs, they were mixed among hundreds of screens, making them hard to spot.

*‘Isn't there any way to fix this?’*

I wanted to select and view only what I wanted.

However, no control panels were visible in the control room. There were only screens.

After pondering, I arrived at the thought that this was a place used by Black Block.

I felt like I knew how it should be used.

I *commanded*.

The screens responded to me like living creatures. And they began to display only what I wanted.

The screens in my field of vision all captured the banquet hall CCTVs.

I saw the Captains and Cecilia, who were located inside the banquet hall.

My initial surprise at the unexpected combination was brief, and as I saw the entity they encountered in the banquet hall, which had emptied out after the guests left, my eyes widened.

It was the Eyeball Alien.

If that thing, which tore apart security androids like toys, were to do the exact same thing to my subordinates...

I watched with a wary gaze, but for some reason, the Eyeball Alien's attitude was quite different.

Unlike when it had brutally destroyed the security androids, it only stared intently at my subordinates.

And at the scene that followed, I almost passed out.

“…!”

The Eyeball Alien extended its hand toward Kwak Hanmuk.

Cecilia, who was with them, looked at them with eyes mixed with fear that went beyond bewilderment.

Young god and I were also freaking out together, but Kwak Hanmuk, conversely, was calm.

Smiling affably like a waiter guiding a guest, he said something.

Meanwhile, Mo Haein took a tray containing champagne from another waiter and held it out.

The Eyeball Alien looked down at the politely supported tray.

Then, all of a sudden, its body shook as if laughing.

Instead of picking up a glass of alcohol, it poured out a clatter of jewels.

After piling the jewels at his feet, the Eyeball Alien walked past the Captains and left.

The faces of Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein instantly turned expressionless.

They looked at the pile of jewels as if they were garbage and exchanged a brief conversation.

Mo Haein even kicked away the rolling jewels with her foot.

Cecilia rambled on at length with sweat beading on her face, but the Captains didn't seem to pay much attention.

Letting out a sigh of relief, I quickly sent a message. However, there was no response from the Captains.

*‘Is the message not going through?’*

It seemed the control room was judged to be a special space.

Suddenly, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein looked up into the empty air.

Their gaze looked precisely at the camera, as if their eyes met mine from beyond the screen.

However, it was clear the Captains couldn't see me.

That was because both of their faces bore expressions failing to hide their anxiety.

*‘My subordinates. Just wait a little bit.’*

After looking affectionately at my cute and pretty subordinates, I shook my head to snap out of it.

To return quickly, I had to move busily.

I immediately went to look for CEO Cha.

CEO Cha still had his Cognitive Decline set to 100%, so he didn't even show up on the screen.

Instead, I saw entities tangled up disgustingly.

They were greedily scouring the place where the Young god's power had twinkled.

Are all beings called gods like this?

If the gods exercising influence in Akasha, including the Eyeball Alien, were only bastards like those...

*‘The universe is such a mess.’*

It was at an unbelievable level.

Or maybe Black Block had deliberately gathered only bastards like that.

The kind who possessed great power, but didn't make me want to contract with them.

Considering Black Block's sinister nature, it was a sufficiently plausible scenario.

As I was guessing his intent, the Young god chimed in on my thoughts.

He claimed there was no god here as perverted as him.

The Young god bounced around, declaring that he could fight and win against all the gods invited to the banquet.

His confidence was piercing the universe. Yet in front of Dominic, he had cried in fright.

If left alone, he looked ready to make a major contribution to this chaotic situation.

I let the Young god's words go in one ear and out the other and resumed my search.

*‘Where is my subordinate?’*

I scoured the screen hoping to see even a metal fragment, but I couldn't find any.

Just in case, I also looked for Kang Byeokrin, but she wasn't caught on the screen either.

The fact that neither of them could be seen even though they should be within Hyper Eden seemed to be because the master of the control room was Black Block.

After all, Black Block's orders would take priority over mine.

*‘It means I can only see what Black Block allows.’*

“…….”

I narrowed my eyes for a moment.

From the retro arcade and the Western-style Masquerade Ball to this control room faithful to cyberpunk.

Under the pretext of reporting, there was no common denominator among the places Black Block had guided me to.

It felt like he was sending some kind of message to me.

However, nothing came to mind right away.

I needed to extract more information about Black Block.

*‘Information I can get from the control room's screens...’*

Staring intently at the screens reflecting various parts of the city, the thought suddenly struck me that the control room's abilities wouldn't be restricted to Hyper Eden alone.

“Show me Double M's territory.”

When I gave the new command, the images contained on the wall screens rippled and shook.

The screen, trembling like ripples on water, at one moment projected the universe.

It was a universe map containing galaxies.

And upon it, fluorescent sky-blue dots were stamped innumerably.

I immediately recognized that those were territories owned by Black Block.

While most of the fluorescent sky-blue dots were clustered together, one stood apart, separated from the rest.

I followed the straight path along which this lone dot was traveling.

As my gaze traced the dots, a sudden chill ran down my spine.

Black Block's communication, which had become increasingly clear as time passed, crossed my mind.

And the fluorescent sky-blue dots abruptly cut off at a certain place.

I zoomed in on the universe map.

Near where the last dot cut off, a familiar galaxy was situated.

It was the galaxy to which my territory, Earth, belonged.

Black Block had already created his own territory near mine.

Probably through methods that weren't entirely gentle.

The words spoken by the aliens in the banquet hall came to mind.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"The butlers recently conquered a new territory."</span>

<br>

The Cat Butler and the Rabbit Butler had said they were moving to a new planet.

Even though their master hadn't returned home yet.

Could it be….

It was a moment when I was lost in thought about the butlers and momentarily zoned out.

Young god didn't miss the opportunity and pushed me away. Then, placing his hand on the screen, he shouted.

"My subordinate!"

It was absurd—as if he were casting some sort of spell.

Yet, golden ripples spread out centered around the hand touching the screen.

I opened my eyes wide.

It was because my subordinate had genuinely appeared on the screen.

While checking to see if there were any injuries, I belatedly recognized the bizarreness of the screen.

It was Mass Media's prison.

The ceiling, walls, and floor had dissolved into one another, a chaotic jumble of every conceivable form of media.

Newspaper pages poured down rapidly. Black letters incessantly assembled and disassembled sentences.

The pouring newspapers were mixed with video footage flickering so brightly it hurt the eyes.

A serious face moving a joystick in front of an arcade game machine.

The golden eyes that had momentarily occupied Hyper Eden's screen.

Wearing the Era Agency uniform and eating snacks in the Special Team office.

The hand throwing away the championship ring into a trash can.

As the crimson letters of ‘Breaking News’ crossed the top of the screen, the footage changed.

Young god's personal broadcast and my footage of facing Trials mixed together.

Me falling into the boss's mouth in DeZonDeal, me grilling a lizard's tail in SL2PL, me shouting at CEO Cha in PTB appeared in messy, fragmented pieces.

A buzzing noise came from the screen, which should normally have been playing on mute.

Along with the noise of an old radio's frequencies aligning, the voices of announcers overlapped and echoed like a round song.

<br>

*"This is truly an unprecedented incident! Young god's n, next move! Ah, ahh, Han Goyo! Little Lamb! All eyes are fixed on him! It's an e, e, e, exclusive scoop! Will he abandon his subordinate and run away, or will he become the prey of the gods?"*

<br>

It was a sea of media composed entirely of Han Goyo and Young god.

Right in the middle of it, a pitch-black figure stood motionless, submerged.

My subordinate, with his metal arms hanging down, didn't even twitch.

To the point where it was hard to believe he was a living creature....

The light of the video reflected chaotically over the pitch-black goggles.

It was then.

One of the videos swelled up, crackling with glitches.

Its surface undulated like waves, and something popped right out of the video.

It was me, wearing the Era Agency uniform.

The moment ‘Han Goyo’ positioned himself in front of my subordinate.

The metal hand moved.

*Crunch.*

The pitch-black hand pierced straight through ‘Han Goyo’s chest. And then pulled out indifferently.

Red blood surged and scattered from the hole where the metal hand had pulled away.

‘Han Goyo’ looked at my subordinate with disbelieving eyes before meeting his death.

That was the beginning.

Countless ‘Han Goyo’s and ‘Young god’s crawled out from all directions.

A Young god flashing golden eyes and smiling arrogantly, a blood-soaked and pale Han Goyo, and a trainee calling Lieutenant Colonel Je with eyes full of curiosity threw themselves at my subordinate.

<br>

*“My subordinate!”*

*“Lieutenant Colonel Je.”*

*“Je Hyun-oh!”*

<br>

Though they were clearly fakes, their appearances were so strikingly similar that they were indistinguishable from the real ones.

And my subordinate.

Mercilessly slaughtered the ‘Han Goyo’s and ‘Young god’s rushing toward him.

<br>

*“Lieutenant Colonel Je…. I’m sorry….”*

<br>

He cut out the heart of the ‘Han Goyo’ who said it was his fault.

<br>

*“My subordinate. I’m sorry….”*

<br>

He slashed the eyes of the grieving ‘Young god’.

The things taking on my appearance screamed and wailed in agony.

Yet there was not an ounce of hesitation in his slaughtering hands.

‘Han Goyo’ and ‘Young god’ appeared endlessly.

They multiplied infinitely every time a new video, a newspaper scoop, or a radio news broadcast was created.

When black metal was soaked in red blood to the point of losing its original color from the endless slaughter.

A change occurred in my subordinate.

With a sound like bones being crushed, a new pair of wings grew out from behind his back.

Wings of jet-black metal feathers spread wide.

Now possessing eight wings, my subordinate slashed, carved, and crushed me countless times.

And the more he killed me, the more he lost his human form.

Metal feathers gradually covered my subordinate's body.

Even as his flesh collapsed, there was no reaction from my subordinate.

The more he changed, the faster and stronger he moved instead.

As if returning to his very essence.

The generation speed of ‘Han Goyo’ and ‘Young god’ also became even faster.

At a certain moment, they surged like an explosion and covered my subordinate all at once.

They filled the screen so much that not a single feather of his wings was visible.

It was when I clung to the screen in shock.

A black crescent moon swept through, and hundreds of fakes crumbled all at once.

And what appeared was….

A form where the human shape was completely dismantled.

A giant sphere consisting only of black metal wings and eyes.

Thirty-six metal wings surrounded and enveloped a large eye positioned right in the center.

The eye in the center was not of ordinary appearance.

Dark purple covered the place where the whites should have been, and the pupil flashed with a metallic golden light.

The thirty-six wings moved individually, but the flapping of the wings did not stir up a soft breeze or anything like that.

Only the chilling sound of cleaving space echoed.

Small eyes sprouted in the gaps between the wings where the metal feathers overlapped layer upon layer.

I gazed spellbound at the holy yet overwhelming form.

*‘Like an angel….’*

This thing, possessing eyes that looked only at me and wings meant solely to protect me, was.

My beautiful subordinate.
