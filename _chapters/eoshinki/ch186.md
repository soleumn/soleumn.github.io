<br>I could tell the moment I saw him.

Black Block was also wearing a wrapper that hid his true essence.

Just like the aliens at the Masquerade Ball.

"How is it, Little Lamb!"

Black Block stepped forward.

Stopping at a reasonable distance, he looked down at me.

The Han Goyos standing behind him stared at me just the same.

Trapped within their gazes, Black Block exclaimed triumphantly.

“Do you like what you see?”

I found myself facing Black Block up close.

"……."

As I watched his shameless attitude, my stomach felt like it was twisting.

The emotions from the moment Cecilia shattered, the anger of someone daring to suppress my subordinate and make them lonely, writhed vividly.

I slowly took a deep breath.

Shouting and getting excited here would only add to Black Block's amusement.

Besides, I hadn't entered the Tower alone.

As the master, I had the responsibility to protect my subordinates.

If the white human died right away after I even made him a Sponsor, it would hurt my pride.

I had to do well.

Suppressing my feelings calmly, I examined Black Block once more.

It was a neat hairstyle with the hair brushed back to reveal his forehead.

By contrast, his eyes were as flashy and unusual as his attire.

They sparkled flashily with deep navy like his hair, as if studded with stars.

Additionally, a fluorescent sky-blue line was drawn along the round edge of his iris.

Looking at him draped in a thin veil, I came up with a hypothesis.

Until now, Black Block had approached me with voices and letters, but had never revealed his appearance directly.

Even when I entered Hyper Eden, he had only gradually made me understand his existence.

The situation fit together plausibly if he had worried that I would melt away upon encountering him.

It seemed he had tested it even at the Masquerade Ball to see what level of wrapper was needed so I wouldn't suffer harm.

By having gods lower in status than himself wear masks and attend the ball.

His appearance in front of my subordinates while I stayed in the control room seemed to be in a similar vein.

After confirming that the subordinates didn't melt, he must have been convinced it was alright to show himself to me.

Certainly, encountering an existence of that magnitude without any veil would have probably taken quite a toll on me.

Dominic was also an existence of a distant standing, but my first meeting with him had exceptional specificity.

*'Because he was staying among NPCs.'*

Dominic was already wearing an NPC disguise, which was why I was safe.

A thought suddenly occurred to me that when he made his wrapper, he seemed to have imitated Dominic somewhat.

Probably because they were the exact same height and even dressed in suits.

Of course, even if they were the same suit, Dominic had a classic feel while Black Block had a modern feel, making a big difference.

In front of me, Black Block spread his gloved hand wide open.

Then he made crying sounds with his mouth and pretended to weep.

"I wanted to make it similar to the Little Lamb, but I couldn't reduce the size any further than this."

Given that he was the same height as Dominic, it seemed 3m was the minimum size higher gods could maintain in a human form.

It appeared that smaller human forms had trouble containing their status.

When I still didn't respond, Black Block stretched out his index finger and lowered his voice, whispering secretly.

“However, a very small and cute shape is possible. The Little Lamb especially likes soft things!”

Saying that he could change differently for me, Black Block asked me to tell him whatever I wanted.

Black Block showed off the wrapping he had made, but I looked at it with an unenthusiastic face.

*'I thought we were going to start with a fight.'*

I had arbitrarily altered his domain, shaken the Tower, and invaded.

On top of that, I had insulted him by forming a sponsorship contract with a human.

However, Black Block didn't mention any of that, nor did he try to forcibly suppress me.

He was just acting as leisurely as if I had come to play at the Tower upon his invitation.

As if nothing had happened between him and me.

I opened my mouth for the first time toward the pudding slime hopeful.

“Black Block.”

At that, Black Block's eyes widened.

Since he just stared with his eyes wide open, I called out to him again.

️“…Black Block?”

Black Block clasped both hands in front of his chest and cried out frivolously.

“Oh my, Little Lamb!”

He spoke in a voice full of emotion.

“To be called by a nickname directly from the Little Lamb…!”

I no longer wanted to converse. However, since I was the one in the weaker position, I asked with patience.

“What do you want from me?”

The moment my words dropped, Black Block's eyes shone as he cried out.

“What I want! There is so much!”

The thought that I shouldn't have asked crossed my mind, but I first waited to see what he was going to say.

As if he had never been excited, Black Block changed his attitude to a composed one.

“However, Little Lamb! Isn't eating well during your growing period the most important thing of all? A banquet for the Little Lamb has been prepared.”

I openly displayed wariness toward him.

Black Block made an exaggerated gesture and spoke.

“There's no need to worry, right? Because I am a huge fan of the Little Lamb, and I will do whatever the Little Lamb wants. That remains true even now.”

Black Block's gaze veered away from me and headed behind me.

“I will be kind to the Little Lamb's toys, too.”

At the end of his gaze were positioned my subordinates and the white human.

Everyone was in a state of extreme tension, more than ever before.

No matter if he had covered himself with a wrapper, Black Block was a god.

The dark subordinate might be fine, but for the others, having a god right before their eyes could not possibly be easy.

I anxiously checked on my subordinates.

I could see cold sweat trickling down the small subordinate's temple.

The red-haired subordinate was holding up the struggling small subordinate. Even though he must have been having a hard time himself.

*'Still, they're enduring well.'*

Having previously witnessed Black Block in their own separate ways seemed to have helped a little.

Proud of my subordinates inwardly, I suddenly looked over at white human.

*'Has white human ever seen Black Block before?'*

Just as I was wondering because he was enduring it better than expected, Black Block suddenly spoke up.

“This is a crow’s toy! I was keeping it for him for a bit.”

His tone was like someone greeting a teddy bear a child was playing with.

Even though he shamelessly claimed he was just keeping it, dark subordinate didn’t even flinch at the words referring to him.

He didn't show any fear even in front of a god, but Black Block didn't find that strange.

Brushing it off naturally, as if he knew all about Little Lamb's toys, he pointed to the other subordinates one by one.

“This is something warm and nice for Little Lamb to play with, and haha, doesn’t this toy have a unique structure?”

His gaze, which had passed small subordinate and the red-haired subordinate, finally reached white human.

“And…”

Black Block looked at white human. The cheerful flow of his words cut off for a single moment.

It was just a split second, but I saw it.

The strong displeasure that flashed in Black Block’s eyes.

He didn't say anything, but he briefly let slip his desire to punish CEO Cha.

He wanted to make the human who dared to take the Sponsor’s seat pay the price, but he quickly hid his true feelings.

As if nothing had happened, Black Block said casually,

“This is a new toy Little Lamb has brought in.”

Equipping his smiling face like a mask once again, Black Block reached out toward the Han Goyos near him.

After gently patting the heads of the ones gathered close by, Black Block waved his hand lightly.

At that, the Han Goyos floated up into the air.

With their limbs limp, the Han Goyos zipped into their respective positions like figures going back into a storage box.

As they were neatly placed back in their original spots, the Han Goyos began performing their assigned tasks once again.

I looked away from the Han Goyos, who were clacking their keyboards and mice busily as they played a game that would never end.

Black Block slashed his hand through the empty air.

A door was created at his fingertips. As the door appeared in the empty space and swung wide open, a banquet hall was revealed.

A long dining table filled with appetizing dishes came into view.

Stepping boldly inside, Black Block looked back at me.

“Now, follow me! There is plenty of a banquet for Little Lamb’s toys to join in as well.”

I gave a signal to my subordinates and white human.

At that, dark subordinate moved his feet first and approached my side.

At his action without a shred of hesitation, the red-haired subordinate wiped a hand down his face.

Looking as though he was swallowing curses to himself, he followed behind dark subordinate.

small subordinate looked at me with a somewhat complicated expression.

She had remained in a state of mental turmoil ever since seeing the wall of newspapers created by Black Block.

Yet, she eventually began walking toward me.

The last one to move was white human. It wasn't because he was hesitating.

White human stared at the Han Goyos for a moment before turning his gaze to look at me.

I turned around without bothering to check if he was stepping away.

It was because I knew he would follow me anyway.

Upon entering the banquet hall, the seat of honor at the very end was prepared as my place.

On both sides, small subordinate, the red-haired subordinate, dark subordinate, and white human took their seats.

There was no seat for Black Block.

Instead of sitting in a chair, he stood beside me and volunteered to wait on me.

He pulled out the chair for me with his own hands, and even unfolded the napkin and placed it on my lap.

Then, he poured the aperitif into a very small glass.

The golden liquor was poured into the transparent glass. The name of the liquor suddenly popped into my head.

*'Amontillado?'*

It was a liquor I was seeing for the first time, but I had no idea how the name came to mind.

The appetizer placed alongside it was a pomegranate.

Though I had no intention of eating it, I was curious why on earth they had brought out this kind of liquor and pomegranate.

It was because they were things I didn't particularly prefer.

I checked the plates of the others as well.

Various kinds of dishes were spread out on the table, but strange foods were placed on the plates right in front of each person.

They were things that didn't suit the appetizer of a splendid banquet at all.

A hamburger was placed in front of small subordinate, and a strawberry whipped cream cake in front of dark subordinate.

It felt like they had served the food each person liked.

Perhaps because she realized that fact, small subordinate's expression wasn't good.

It was right when I was about to check the plates of the red-haired subordinate and white human as well.

Black Block, standing behind my back, rested his hand on the backrest of the chair I was sitting in and spoke.

"Does the food not suit your palate!"

Did he really think I was going to eat?

No matter what kind of banquet Black Block brought out, I had no intention of letting a single drop of water pass my lips.

I handed him a fork and said,

"You try it first."

"Little Lamb...."

Black Block muttered in a purposely gloomy tone.

"There is no dish for me here."

After acting as pitiful as he possibly could, he placed one hand on the table and looked at me.

"Perhaps it is time for Little Lamb to keep his promise to me...."

Something came to mind at his low murmur.

I had told him I would make him a dish in exchange for receiving Dark Star Remnants.

It was a promise I couldn't break since I had already received the payment.

*Screeech*, the sound of something scraping was heard.

It was the sound of dark subordinate scraping the table with her metal hand.

The subordinates, as well as white human, couldn't quite speak up in front of Black Block, but they were expressing their intent with their eyes.

It was the look asking what kind of nonsense I had pulled.

I belatedly remembered that this was a promise I had made behind the subordinates' backs.

*'Well, it couldn't be helped though.'*

I felt the subordinates' gazes on me, but since things had turned out this way, it would be better to just get it over with quickly.

If I didn't keep the promise, I didn't know what he might weave out of it as an excuse.

It could end up being a pretext to torment my subordinates.

I slowly opened my mouth.

"I'll do it now."

"Goodness, Little Lamb! My star!"

As soon as my words dropped, Black Block showed a violent reaction.

“Such tenderness! The time spent with Little Lamb is always full of wonder and emotion!”

He truly acted like a fan chosen by a star.

Just as I was about to ask Black Block what he wanted to eat,

“…!”

Fluorescent sky-blue sparks flashed across the banquet hall. I saw the subordinates and white human disappearing right before my eyes.

The moment I jumped out of my seat to shout,
