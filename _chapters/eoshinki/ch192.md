<br>The time spent falling was not long. It was short to the point of being futile.

It was as if the effort of climbing so hard was a lie, and I had returned to the 1st floor of the Tower.

My vision was entirely red. It seemed to be due to the blood spreading.

It was all blood flowing from me.

I lay looking upward. I thought with a hazy mind.

*'How did I survive?'*

It was a height that was more than enough to cause instant death. Yet, I was alive.

While feeling relieved about my survival, I felt a terrible sense of emptiness.

It wasn't simply because of the fact that I had to climb the stairs again from the beginning.

The moment I saw Dominic, I felt relieved.

I believed that he would comfort and help the injured me.

Foolishly so.

Even though Dominic's desire had been clear from the beginning.

Pushing aside all thoughts, I first tried to move my limbs.

However, twitching just my fingers was the best I could do.

It felt as though my entire body was completely crushed.

Though I couldn't be sure, having fallen from that height, I must have ended up in quite a miserable state.

It was fortunate that I couldn't see my own state, at least. It felt like it would have hurt even more if I had seen it with my own eyes.

When the pain surpassed the limit, no sensation could be felt.

As if my body and mind were separated, I judged my condition like it was someone else's business.

Meanwhile, a certain… pupil came into view in my vision.

The man with an unreal appearance looking down at me had calm eyes, like an adult scolding a spoiled child.

Looking up at Dominic, *I was able to realize.*

Just how massive that thing was.

Despite being this overwhelming, the entity before me was merely a single, extremely minuscule cell of the essence possessed by *Him*.

A mass impossible to even imagine, embracing hundreds of billions of galaxies and eons of time.

The primal and absolute universe itself.

Even a single blink of *His* eye would cause galaxies to perish without a single trace.

I newly realized.

Even if it was only an extremely small part, packing *Him* into a human form of three meters was close to being impossible.

This too must have been *His* power.

How arrogant it was of me to name *Him* Dominic as I pleased.

However, I….

I opened my lips.

Very softly, like the sound of escaping wind, I called him "Mr. Dominic".

I thought he might not answer. It was absurd to answer such a trivial title.

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

However, Dominic answered.

He spoke affectionately, looking down at me as I was covered in blood.

"I am very late. You must have been lonely all this while."

Dominic knelt on one knee and sat beside me.

His eyes drew even closer.

"I am sorry for disappearing without telling you beforehand. I was examining the records regarding Roasted Chestnut here."

He explained step by step that if he had told Roasted Chestnut first, I would have disliked it, so he had intended to go quietly, and that he had finally arrived now because it took quite a bit of time.

I could not believe it.

I had noticed from the moment I stepped onto the 1st floor that Double M had gathered records regarding me in the Tower.

Even so, it was just a human lifetime. For Dominic, he should have been able to grasp all records in a fraction of a second.

This was no explanation for why he had been away from my side for so long.

Was he not even together with Double M?

I remembered what Double M had said to Dominic.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"So I changed it to be a little more fun! You will definitely like it."</span>

<br>

There was no doubt that Dominic's tacit approval or instructions were mixed into what Double M was doing now.

Even if that weren't the case, the moment he pushed me from the top of the stairs, it became clear that what Dominic wanted was the exact opposite of my hope.

*'He probably figured I'd give up on my own while climbing the stairs.'*

Because I had climbed all the way to the top in the end, he must have made me fall once more.

I no longer trusted Dominic.

Had he read the betrayal and resentment lingering in my gaze?

Dominic called out to me once again, Roasted Chestnut.

"I have seen all of Roasted Chestnut's past."

"……."

"I have come to *understand* Roasted Chestnut."

The word 'understand' struck me as eerie.

It was because I instinctively felt that this was an understanding in a direction I did not want.

"Roasted Chestnut's planet is a place unworthy of existence. For it committed an unforgivable sin against god."

The past me was human. Therefore, saying that I had committed a sin against god was contradictory.

However, I could not refute Dominic.

He was enraged at the humans who had insulted god.

"How did they react when Roasted Chestnut revealed such divine power?"

That was anger for my sake.

Just as I had seen my past while climbing the stairs, Dominic too had seen everything about me here in the Tower.

His blue eyes rippled like a nebula.

I felt as though I was being sucked in….

"They merely tried to covet god even more. In their boundless, presumptuous greed, did they not ultimately attempt to destroy what was divine?"

Dominic made a resolute conclusion.

"Such things deserve to be *punished*."

After speaking firmly, as if teaching the right path to an ignorant child, he did not forget to soothe me.

"Roasted Chestnut has done no wrong. Since you were still young and immature, you couldn't have known any better."

Concluding that everything was the sin of the microscopic creatures who tried to handle god as they pleased, he declared Roasted Chestnut innocent.

He also said that if I wished, he would punish them on my behalf.

Sweet words followed, promising to cleanly clear away those trivial and filthy things and gift me a new planet.

Warm and affectionate comfort wrapped around me.

"It is alright, Roasted Chestnut. We can set things right from now on."

Dominic lightly stroked over my heart with the tip of his finger.

Golden light leaked out from my body. It seemed that the split cracks were appearing once more.

His long fingers traced the cracks before soon digging into my chest.

"You have been busily making trouble while I was gone. Even forming contracts…. Do not worry. I will not scold you. You haven’t become a god yet. I can resolve this as well. First, I must do ■■."

I wanted to writhe at the sensation of my heart being touched.

However, my limbs were broken, so I couldn't even budge.

Even when I screamed, only the sound of wheezing breath came out from my throat.

All I could do was squirm like a bug.

Dominic, who had meticulously rummaged and inspected inside me, pulled out his hand.

"■■ is soon. Even though I paid special attention, seeing as it is an important moment that will determine what kind of existence Roasted Chestnut will become."

A long-glided finger gripped my wrist.

It was a hand with letters written on it.

"You must no longer expend your strength on such trivial matters."

I parted my lips.

No.

However, my resistance was meaningless. The letters written from my palm all the way to my forearm vanished smoothly.

I hurriedly tried to recall the names I had been memorizing.

Nothing came to mind….

*'I had been memorizing them.'*

I had certainly kept those who called to me stored in my head, but everything had completely evaporated.

I felt the sensation of being dropped alone into a distant universe.

It was an infinite void with nowhere to lean.

Only Dominic remained—he, and he alone.

For everything else had ceased to exist.

Trembling with fear, I looked up at *Him*.

"I believe you *understand* now."

A being made of the universe asked me.

“Will you come with *me*?”

A thrill ran through my whole body. Overwhelming joy filled my head.

*He is calling me!*

*I must answer the glorious call!*

*'But.'*

I closed my lips and shook my head. Even while fearing what would come, I ultimately refused.

The reason was unclear. It just felt like I had to.

Dominic let out a shallow sigh.

“To be so stubborn.”

He patted my head once and rose to his feet. Dominic looked down at me and smiled.

“Can you come back up, Roasted Chestnut?”

His body floated lightly into the air. The human-like form collapsed, revealing a bizarre shape.

The largest eye was positioned at the top of the tower, looking down at me.

<big>“Show me.”</big>

The blue eye containing galaxies said to me.

<big>“What the little Roasted Chestnut can do.”</big>

I wanted to avoid its gaze.

However, there was no place to run, and my body could not move even a single finger.

It felt as though a string made of beads was wrapping around me and pulling me.

The hand that had been lying haphazardly on the floor came into my field of vision.

It felt as though lightning flashed inside my head.

I was able to remember everything.

I raised my body with a strength I didn't know where I'd gotten.

No, I thought I had raised it.

However, flipping over my lying body was all I could manage.

A hot sensation violently surged from within. The moment I opened my mouth, a mouthful of blood poured out.

“Haa, haa....”

As soon as I recovered my memories, fear washed over me.

I couldn't believe how I had been awakened.

*'What on earth did Captain Kwak do? To wake... me up...'*

I crawled forward through the pool of blood.

Along with my movements, a long red trail was drawn across the floor.

One by one, they floated into my head.

Those who had vanished while consuming ■■■ for my sake.

*'No.'*

No matter what it took, I had to climb back up.

However....

“.......”

I gritted my teeth and crawled, but I couldn't even reach the stairs.

Before long, I collapsed facedown onto the floor.

I managed to lift only my head and look up at the sky.

Only after witnessing the distant staircase and the giant eyeball once more did I understand.

This was not a Trial meant for humans in the first place.

I let go of the foolishly clinging attachment and sank into the deep darkness.

Soon, a familiar space appeared.

A place where an old TV, the latest gaming console, and an antique bronze telephone were mixed together.

It was the place Dominic had called my mindscape.

There, I met the one I had been looking for.

“...Hey.”

And I said to the Young god.

“Help me.”

It could never be resolved by human strength alone. Ultimately, to deal with a god, I needed the Young god.

I clenched my fists so hard that my fingernails dug into my palms and waited.

I intended to listen no matter what the Young god said.

Yet for some reason, it was quiet. I had thought it would go wild, demanding we attack right away.

“What are you doing? Now is not the time to be playing around. Come out already.”

I called out to the Young god who was hiding behind the TV and refusing to come out.

The Young god cautiously poked his head out over the TV.

I confronted an unexpected face.

“...I can't.”

The Young god, wearing a terrified and frightened expression, cried out to me.

“I'm scared. I'm really scared....”

Taken aback for a moment, I rushed right over to the Young god. Pulling him out by force from behind the TV, I shouted.

“What are you talking about! In a situation where we don't even know what happened to the subordinates, you, you...!”

I spoke, soaked in a sense of futility.

“A Young god shouldn't be like this....”

It had always been like this.

The Young god had saved everyone in moments of crisis, facing things head-on without fearing or dreading anything.

Running away and hiding was the role of the human Han Goyo.

So now, naturally, the Young god had to step up.

In a situation where he actually had no other choice.

“Get out here right now! We have to stand up to Dominic. I know it's not easy. I know it's impossible, but we still have to try!”

“I can't do it!!”

The Young god shouted right back at me. Tears were welling up abundantly in his golden eyes.

“You're saying that because you don't know! We can't. We really can't.”

The Young god squeezed his eyes shut and shook his head.

“I'm scared. I want to run away....”

“You did well until now!”

Unable to hold back, I grabbed the Young god by the collar and yelled. My whole body trembled with despair.

“We need to save the subordinates too.... Everyone is waiting only for you right now. If you don't step up...”

“Why do you keep saying that?”

The young god sniffled. Letting his tears drop steadily, he shouted back at me.

“I didn't do it alone!”

“Then!”

“We always did it together….”

Young god finally burst into tears and said.

“I told you several times, why do you keep saying you did it alone….”

I was left speechless.

*‘Did it together?’*

I retraced the actions I had assumed Young god had accomplished all this time.

Without a doubt, those had been Young god’s power.

However, it hadn’t been entirely Young god’s power alone.

Clearly… my will had been mixed in as well.

I had regarded Young god as a separate entity and separated us, but we were not two.

“It was *me*…?”

Young god sniffled and nodded. Gazing quietly at Young god, I slowly parted my lips.

“…Wipe your tears.”

“Hic, *SNIFF*, I’m scared…. You’re not scared because you don’t know what it is. You’re really awful….”

Even now, I probably still didn’t fully understand Dominic’s true nature.

Yet, it didn’t matter even if this was sheer bravado born of ignorance.

“No. We have to do it.”

I roughly rubbed Young god’s whining face with my hand. Then I opened both arms.

“Come here.”

Young god stared blankly at me. With eyes still red from crying, Young god asked me.

“The subordinates… can we save them?”

“Yeah.”

“Really?”

“Really.”

It wasn’t a lie. I was going to keep trying until I succeeded.

I would not give up.

Even if I were to perish.

“So let’s go.”

Young god blinked several times. Hesitating for a moment, Young god reached out and hugged me.

A small reply followed.

“…Okay.”

Along with it, I heard a sound.

It was the sound of *Bloom*, where a fractured fissure split wide open and a hard shell broke apart.

A dazzling radiance poured out from *me* and covered the world.

It was a golden light, both complete and serene.
