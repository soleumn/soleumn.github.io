<br>I stared at the chat screen, unable to even blink.

I felt as though something was on the verge of coming to mind—it was right there, just out of reach.

Yet, by the time the screen vanished, nothing had surfaced.

It felt like a persistent mental block.

Even so, my determination to climb upward only grew firmer.

I didn't want to disappoint those who said they were waiting for me.

“Winter, Yuhae, Hyunhyun….”

The names I had to memorize had increased, yet I remembered them even better.

The names seemed to connect in my head and bind together solidly.

Muttering like a spell, I passed the Young god's screen as well.

As I headed upward, I glanced at the chat screens floating around me.

That was because a man named Hyunhyun was talking inside the chat window.

I hoped anyone would speak to me again.

Climbing the endless stairs alone felt a little lonely, too.

At that moment, I felt someone pull on my clothes.

*‘He's going to be okay, right?’*

A sudden wave of worry washed over me, making my heart race. There were three people waiting for me.

Muttering the names once more, I diligently climbed the stairs.

Hyunhyun had now disappeared from the chat window, leaving only people I didn't know.

They continued to exchange words that still made no sense.

Were they perhaps people I knew, too?

I tried to keep the names from the chat window in my head, but I failed every time.

A sense of fatigue washed over me as I followed with my eyes things I didn't even understand.

I tore my eyes away from the chat window and climbed the stairs in silence.

Even when the Young god's large screen appeared, I walked past it without any particular agitation.

As I passed the screen several times, the stairs suddenly shook.

“…!”

They shook left and right as if an earthquake had hit, so I dropped to the ground to avoid falling.

*CRACK*, the stairs split apart. A fluorescent sky-blue light leaked through the cracked crevice.

The light created a new screen.

The video captured on the screen was completely different from anything before.

It wasn't high-definition where even a single eyelash was detailed, but a chunky dot-graphics video.

Just like an archive game.

Watching the video, I felt a sense of familiarity.

*‘It's &lt;City of Frame>.’*

‘Me’ and Cecilia, as tiny dot characters, were approaching the tower.

The dot character carefully breached the heavy security and approached step by step with caution.

When we stopped in front of the tower, a square window popped up at the bottom of the video.

Hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of eyes drawn all over the tower moved their pupils in unison to look at ‘me’ and Cecilia.

It was a moment not found in my memories.

I definitely didn't have an experience like that when I played it as a game.

All I remembered was opening the door, and Cecilia melting and running away….

While I was bewildered, the video continued uninterrupted.


<div class="caixajogo" markdown="1">

Cecilia melted away in an instant.

Just before she completely melted.

Every single pupil on the tower turned toward ‘me’.

Only one of Cecilia's pupils was left alone on the floor.

Though I received the gazes all by myself, ‘I’ was fine.

I was cowering and screaming, but at least my body didn't melt.

It was then.

The pupils vanished, and a door formed at the very bottom of the tower.

A door resembling a camera aperture opened, and the tense background music shifted into an upbeat tune.

A large hand wearing a white glove reached out from the door and snatched the tiny dot character.

‘I’ became trapped inside the hand and completely hidden from view.

The hand dragged ‘me’ inside the tower.

The eyes vanished from the tower's surface.

The cheerful music continued, and the video showed the quieted tower once more.

The screen grew darker. Just like announcing a bad end.

Right before total pitch blackness, the screen suddenly flashed brightly.

A door formed once again at the very bottom of the tower.

As soon as the aperture-like door opened, a dot character bounced out from inside.

It was ‘me’.

Rolling out like a tumbleweed, ‘I’ frantically snatched Cecilia's eyeball rolling on the floor and bolted.


<div class="caixajogo" markdown="1">

However, his search failed.

I knew the reason. It was because I had quit the game.

Shocked by Cecilia's death, ‘I’ shut off the game the moment I escaped.

Failing the search within Hyper Eden, the tower's light stretched out into space.


<div class="caixajogo" markdown="1">

The light searched every region touched by its power, but failed to find what it wanted.

Even though it thoroughly scoured every space where Mass Media existed and sentient beings with civilization lived.

The light began to flow backward through time.

It was the power of a god.

As it crossed spacetime and traced back into the past, the graphics of the video began to change.

Depth was added to the flat colors, and the jagged, step-like outlines were smoothed into graceful curves.

The dot graphics transformed into an ultra-high-definition screen before I knew it.

Within the vivid screen, the light scanned the universe once again.

And finally.

*'How?'*

*'Could the games I played all be—'*

Warm liquid trickled down from beneath my nose. Red blood dripped down *thud, thud*.

The truth, too heavy to bear, washed over me.

*'Is this reality?'*

Uncontrollable thoughts surged through my head like a rampage.

I scratched the floor with my fingertips.

*'Then me.'*

*'Me, who collected even all the bad endings—'*

Letters flickered like a vision before my eyes as I struggled to breathe somehow.

My heart pounded *thump-thump*. My head felt like it was splitting, making me want to smash it against the stairs.

Breathing heavily, I forced my hand to reach out.

I had to go up.

I felt like things would be okay if I met the ones waiting for me.

I wanted the reassurance that all the choices and actions I had made weren't entirely bad.

“Winter…, Yuhae, *ugh*, Hyunhyun….”

Cold sweat kept dripping down.

Crawling up using only my hands was not easy.

Dragging my unmoving legs, I crawled for a bit before stopping my hands. Then I stayed still.

It wasn't that I didn't want to climb. It was just that my strength kept draining away.

Thinking of Cecilia and the NPCs of the Archive, I curled up my body on the stairs.
