Wondering if no one was chatting at all, I widened my eyes and peered closely.

*'With so many aliens who entered, how could that be?'*

Thinking it made no sense, I checked the chat window, and a notice message was displayed.

It seemed too many aliens had sent chats at the same time.

In any case, since I had no intention of listening to what the aliens had to say anyway, I only checked whether the broadcast was streaming properly.

Black Block must have seen my broadcast clearly as well.

To Black Block, who had been looking forward to becoming my Sponsor, there could be no greater insult than this.

After all, the position of first Sponsor was stolen not by Dominic, but by a mere human.

Originally, I had wanted to take in a powerful god who could be helpful to my life in the universe as a Sponsor.

I had wanted to find an entity that would shower me with Star Fragments and items, but after experiencing the Masquerade Ball, my mind had changed a bit.

Beings called gods were all completely selfish.

I wanted a Sponsor whom I could control as I pleased.

For now, I would try keeping the white human as the first Sponsor, and if needed later, taking in a few more would be fine.

*'It doesn't matter if I have multiple Sponsors anyway.'*

However, my subordinates seemed very surprised.

In particular, the red-haired subordinate and the small subordinate gasped in horror.

The red-haired subordinate urgently said to the dark subordinate,

"Lieutenant Colonel Je, hasn’t he turned into a complete madman??"

To his argument that they had to stop the broadcast right now and hold him back, a leisurely answer came.

"It's fine. CEO Cha will have his uses."

The dark subordinate didn't view the Sponsor contract negatively.

At his reply, the red-haired subordinate and the small subordinate frowned all at once.

The small subordinate lowered her voice and said,

"But it's CEO Cha."

A clear sense of wariness was contained within that short sentence.

Then the dark subordinate answered, clinking his metal hand.

"You saw the things that came to the Masquerade Ball. He's better than them. At least we can communicate with him."

"…That's true, but."

The small subordinate muttered reluctantly.

"Other than that, they seem about the same…."

The red-haired subordinate raised a hand and spoke with a serious expression.

"CEO Cha can't be reasoned with sometimes either, though."

Claiming that he had spoken in dog barks before, he once again argued that CEO Cha was useless.

I watched affectionately as my subordinates bickered and debated.

It seemed they were jealous, fearing my attention would be stolen by taking in a Sponsor.

Thinking that I should take better care of them and pay more attention to them, I glanced toward the Tower.

Even after I announced the Sponsor, the Tower was still standing firm.

Personal broadcasting was a realm referred to as new media.

It was an entirely different category from legacy media such as radio, newspapers, and TV that Black Block owned.

My goal was to create a crack in the Tower, which was composed of the existing Mass Media.

I judged that it was entirely possible if I dropped an impactful scoop while all the aliens were gathered in my broadcast.

Of course, no matter how much of an uproar it caused, it was merely a temporary disturbance.

A single broadcast wasn't enough to tear down what Black Block had built up until now.

What I wanted right now wasn't the collapse of the Tower or the occupation of the territory anyway.

I needed a small opening—a way to get inside the tower.

*‘I’m going to need something more.’*

I looked back at the white human.

When I declared myself a Sponsor, I had been all set to teach him a harsh lesson if he threw a tantrum or rejected me.

Yet he was much more obedient than expected.

Even while the subordinates debated right in front of him, the white human just stared quietly at me.

He might have been struck speechless, deeply moved by the fact that he was getting a Young god as a Sponsor.

It certainly was a glorious thing.

I held out my hand to the white human.

“Let's make a contract now.”

Normally, making a sponsorship contract required exchanging a written agreement with specific terms.

But all I wanted from the white human was the position of Sponsor.

Forget any conditions—this was a contract without even a piece of paper.

The white human stared at the hand I offered, then let out a sudden, scoffing laugh.

And he grabbed my hand as if snatching it.

“You don't know when to stop just because I go easy on you.”

He met my eyes. The mechanical voice spoke to me insolently.

“Go ahead, try it.”

His tone was as if he were the one in the position of power.

I easily ignored it and *finalized the contract*.

Dazzling golden light burst forth.

The light illuminated everywhere brightly before digging straight into the white human's heart.

*A sense of connection followed.*

I felt like I understood a little bit now.

Why Dominic and Black Block both wanted to form a sponsorship contract with me so badly.

This was an indelible brand upon one another.

The moment when existence connected with existence would be recorded in Akasha and never fade.

Even after enough time passed for stars to be born and die away....

I looked at the white human.

Beyond the mask, very pale sky-blue eyes were visible.

The corners of his eyes distorting with pain were amusing.

I wanted to take off the mask and see his face.

I wanted to check what kind of expression he was making, but I held back.

My subordinates were waiting, so I couldn't waste time on trivial matters.

One unexpected thing was that the white human endured it quite well.

It was a contract between a god and a human.

Since it was a contract with an immeasurable gap in status, it had to hurt quite a bit, yet he held on without even letting out a groan.

Other than a brief twitch in our clasped hands, nothing showed on the outside.

It was commendable, as it wouldn't do any good to show off a feeble Sponsor.

I was contemplating whether to even praise him.

Right as the contract ended, the white human shook off my hand as if swatting it away.

Then he shoved me backward.

Wondering what he was up to, I stepped back a couple of paces, and the white human thrust his face right into the camera.

“Get lost already.”

And then he threatened the aliens of Akasha.

“Because I don't share my spot.”

At that moment, the frozen chat window unfroze and the viewer count skyrocketed.

Just as all sorts of aliens were screaming in shock,

*Rumble*, a vibration was felt.

I quickly looked up at the Tower. A flash of light burst from the Tower, dyeing the sky into a fluorescent sky-blue.

I hurriedly turned off the broadcast and shouted.

“Now!”

Simultaneously with sending out the broadcast-end notification, the dark subordinate grabbed me and flew like a bolt of lightning.

At the lowest floor of the Tower, a small gap was open.

Without a moment of hesitation, I threw my body right into that fluorescent sky-blue rippling space at the exact speed we were flying.

A flash like a bursting camera bulb glared, and as the light faded, a vast space appeared.

The dark subordinate slid a long way across the floor and came to a stop.

Following right behind, wrapping prayer beads around the dark subordinate and skillfully chasing after us, came the red-haired subordinate and the small subordinate.

And just before the door closed, the white human also succeeded in entering.

My subordinate turned on the lights in the goggles and welcomed the white human.

The white human didn't even look that way and scanned the interior.

It was a space with a bluish glow.

It was made of metal, but there were no joints to be seen, as if it had been melted and forged in one massive piece.

Everything from the ceiling to the floor was perfectly smooth.

And I….

I was met with a sight that was quite hard to accept….

“What the fuck, shit.”

The red-haired subordinate immediately burst out swearing.

All sorts of versions of Han Goyo were strewn all over the Tower.

They probably were androids, but they weren't just sitting there like robots.

The 1st floor of the Tower was divided into spaces like a filming studio.

There were no walls, so each space could be seen at a glance, and they were all familiar places.

An orphanage, a school, an athlete's lodging, a stadium, a convenience store, a studio apartment, and even parts of the Trials I had cleared.

Every single place that existed within my memories was implemented without missing a single one.

Dozens of Han Goyos were busy doing actions suited to each place there.

Playing games, doing homework, picking out triangle kimbap, diligently cleaning the mansion, and so on.

It was while I was staring blankly at that sight, frozen in place.

The Han Goyos simultaneously turned their heads toward me.

Then, they suddenly started running over like crazy.

Just as I was about to prepare for an attack.

A Han Goyo wearing the work clothes of &lt;Happy Smile Factory> threw an arm around my shoulder.

He grinned and said to me.

“What took you so long! I've been waiting.”

His voice was identical to mine, too….

Without answering, I turned my head.

The Han Goyos hadn't just latched onto me.

They were swarming all over my subordinates and the white human as well.

There was even a younger version of Han Goyo.

Child Han Goyo chased belatedly with short legs and clung to the leg of the red-haired subordinate.

The red-haired subordinate, who already had several Han Goyos hanging off him, flinched in surprise and looked down.

The situation was similar for the other subordinates.

The small subordinate was practically buried under the Han Goyos due to the difference in build.

The dark subordinate stood still with several Han Goyos hanging off him, but perhaps because he had already experienced a similar Han Goyo bomb, he didn't show much reaction.

For some reason, the white human was completely frozen and being overwhelmed by the Han Goyos.

Countless Han Goyos kept poking and prodding the white human.

And I was grabbed on both sides by Han Goyo wearing a professional gamer jersey and Han Goyo wearing a butler suit.

“Welcome!”

“I missed you.”

The Han Goyos smiled and gave me a welcoming greeting.

I kept a blank expression and only slightly frowned.

I knew Black Block was a pervert, but I didn't think it would be to this extent.

*'So it was true when he said he’d been a fan for a long time.'*

However, his fan heart wasn't pleasing at all.

This sight, which seemed to capture my entire life in detail, felt unpleasant.

It was just as I was thinking about how on earth I should deal with these androids.

Something in my arms vibrated noisily.

I took out the Acorn Compass, which I had forgotten about.

The needle of the compass pointed straight ahead and popped up a notification.

That I had been using the Acorn Compass completely backwards all this time.

Originally, the goal of hide-and-seek is not to track the seeker.

It is to run away from the seeker.

It was then. *CRACKLE—!*, fluorescent sky-blue sparks rose in the empty air.

The Han Goyos who had been tormenting me, my subordinates, and the white human burst into joyful laughter.

Dozens of Han Goyos ran toward the place where the sparks had risen.

A hand popped out from between the fluorescent sky-blue light.

The being that appeared next...

Stood tall even among the countless Han Goyos.

A man with a giant height of 3 meters shouted.

A cheerful voice rang out loudly.

“Little Lamb, my star!”

My lips parted in a daze on their own.

It was a presence that stuck in the mind to the point of being stimulating.

And it wasn't just because of the clear voice like a radio.

The flashy attire like a TV, contrasting with a monocle giving off a conservative newspaper-like vibe.

The man wearing a suit with fluorescent sky-blue stripes had slightly cunning, thin eyes.

Yet objectively, he was a wonderfully handsome man.

He reached out both hands and shouted.

“The Little Lamb has finally arrived at my Tower! I am moved, and I am touched!”
