<br>I couldn't answer Kwak Hanmuk's words.

In reality, it made no sense for me to die before Kwak Hanmuk.

After all, he was my subordinate.

The moment I perished, all my subordinates would inevitably meet the same fate as their master.

Kwak Hanmuk couldn't possibly be unaware of that fact.

Even if his mind didn't register it, his instincts would perceive it, so I couldn't understand why he was talking like this.

As if I had truly died right before his eyes...

While running through logical deductions to replace something that had never happened, I soon came up with a reasonable explanation.

In COF, Kwak Hanmuk must have watched the whole process of me climbing the tower.

That probably made him unnecessarily anxious about it.

Well, if I were in his shoes and watched someone fall from the top of the tower, I'd probably start worrying about things that hadn't even happened.

I stared at the band-aid, pondering how to reply.

The star-shaped band-aid glittered over my heart, and gold filled the roots of the blue lightning tattoo.

I belatedly gave him my answer.

“I don't want to be the only one left behind.”

This, at least, was the absolute truth without a shred of a lie.

I had always wanted to live.

But since I didn't want to be alone, I wanted to find a way to survive together. No matter the situation.

Even while suffering through the pain, Kwak Hanmuk let out a soft huff of a laugh. He called my name in a subdued voice.

“Goyo.”

“Yes.”

“Han Goyo.”

“Yes...”

“Don't cause any accidents and stay put.”

“Yeees...”

Kwak Hanmuk swallowed a groan for a moment. He smiled, his face slick with cold sweat.

“Lie to me again and you'll get a smack on the head.”

He had figured out that the claim that I received Star Fragments from the Young god Channel was a lie.

While I stood there unable to say anything, Kwak Hanmuk ultimately failed to withstand the pain and lost consciousness.

I supported him and laid him out straight on the bed.

The gold glittering on the Starlight Hug Band-Aid slowly faded, and the lightning tattoo returned to its original blue.

I could feel my own eye color returning to normal as well.

I let out an inner sigh of relief and looked down steadily at Kwak Hanmuk.

Though he had lost consciousness from the pain, his closed face looked much more relaxed than before.

Thinking it was a relief, the Young god started pushing its subordinate-praise theory on me again, sliding in smoothly.

I tried to just ignore it, but it was way too noisy.

As he kept fussing, insisting he would come out and do it himself, I had no choice but to open my mouth.

“Captain Kwak... Are you sleeping...?”

After calling out to check on the sleeping Kwak Hanmuk, I gave his head a quick, stealthy pat.

My heart hammered even as I rubbed it briskly three or four times.

A trainee secretly patting their captain's head while he slept—what a sight.

If Kwak Hanmuk woke up, flattening myself facedown on the floor wouldn't even be enough to make up for it.

With my heart in my throat, I successfully completed Young god's mission.

*'Stop making me act like a social misfit.'*

I warned the Young god, but it didn't even listen to a word.

Since the primary treatment was done, my work for the day was finished.

I checked Kwak Hanmuk's condition one last time before slipping out of the hospital room.

Walking down the quiet hallway, I sank into thought.

The collapse had been averted, but his body had already been shattered once. From now on, he had to digest the divine power, even if it had to be forced.

It would be terribly tough on Kwak Hanmuk, who was a human, but it couldn't be helped.

Still, the subordinate growth system gained through the Bloom ability should prove helpful.

However, there was still a part of me that couldn't understand.

Just how on earth had Kwak Hanmuk sealed a god inside his body?

By accepting the Young god, I had come to see and know much more.

It made me realize all the more just how absurd it was to contain a god within a human body.

While I wondered how he had managed it, the fact that another god resided inside him poked sharply at me.

Even now, if it weren't for the risk of hurting Kwak Hanmuk, I would have wanted to rip the god out of him by force.

Trying to summon some patience, I trudged along.

The Young god begged me to go check on Mo Haein's hospital room, too. But this was the one thing I absolutely couldn't grant.

*'Hey. Look at me.'*

I was wearing a hospital gown and even holding a bloody utility knife. It was the exact kind of appearance that would make Mo Haein slice me apart with the Black Moon Sword the moment she saw me.

When I taught the Young god the basic common sense that one shouldn’t just barge into a space where a woman is alone, he looked quite dejected.

Even though he knew Mo Haein was fine, it seemed he wanted to check for himself.

Actually... I felt the same way myself, though...

*'Want to check the Status Window?'*

At the Young god's compromise, my resolve wavered mercilessly. I hesitated before checking it for just a single second.

In the past, the current status would only output a single, very simple word, but now the contents had become quite detailed.

I felt reassured, figuring Mo Haein would recover consciousness without any trouble.

The Young god whined, begging to see the small subordinate's dream, but I utterly ignored him.

Shaking off my final remaining worry, I headed back to my hospital room.

And the next morning.

“......”

Granted permission to discharge, I slowly stripped off my hospital gown and changed into my uniform.

There was still no contact from the person I was waiting for.

*'This won't do.'*

I wanted to meet Samra somehow today.

I was worried, and frankly feeling frustrated in all sorts of ways.

Whenever I came out of a Trial, I'd usually hear all kinds of stories from Samra about what had been happening on Earth in the meantime, but this time I wasn't getting any information at all.

Had he really gotten infected with a virus, or had some other problem cropped up?

I decided I needed to check for myself.

I picked up my phone and sent a rough notice to Samra.

“Samra. I'm going to come find you today, so make time to see me. I want to talk face-to-face.”

This didn't feel like quite enough, so I added one more line.

“I'll keep searching until I can find you.”

I trusted he would intercept and listen to it properly.

Since I already had my phone out, I sent a text message as well.

Still, since CEO Cha had called me before entering COF, it probably wouldn't matter.

*'He even became a Sponsor, so at least he won't hit me.'*

After sending the message to Sa Gongyeon, I slipped my phone back into my pocket.

Now all that was left was finding Samra.

Just as I was about to step out of the hospital room with a resolute determination,

“...?”

An interstellar communication came in.

It arrived as a video call, but from an unrecorded communication code.

There were very few aliens who would communicate with me, and I had all of their codes saved.

Could it be Sa Gongyeon? I picked up the call.

—Roasted Chestnut.

However, an entirely unexpected person appeared on the screen.

“Mr. Dominic...?”

I had expected it to be at most Black Block with a changed communication code.

Seeing my astonishment, Dominic let out a low chuckle.

—I figured you might be waiting, so I reached out.

He explained that since he had made Roasted Chestnut wait without any promise last time, he thought he would reach out in advance this time.

Nodding blankly at the person who was taking such warm care of me, I quickly scanned the screen.

It was too dark to see clearly, but the background behind him looked like some sort of mansion.

I didn't have time to examine it closely, though.

Because Dominic pulled the screen a little closer.

Blue eyes carrying a subtle light gazed at me.

—Did you treat the red-haired subordinate safely?

“Ah, yes…. Thanks to you, Mr. Dominic. Thank you.”

A compliment fell, telling me I did well. If he had been right beside me, I was sure he would have patted my shoulder, too.

—Things took longer than expected, but I should be able to return to Roasted Chestnut soon.

Did you take care of business?

Are you willing to come back as a Pudding Slime?

Might you possibly give me just a little bit more allowance? I only have thirty billion won right now.

I tightly swallowed down the urge to ask various questions and opened my mouth.

“Mr. Dominic, then….”

I tried to ask when he might return in a bit more detail, but I couldn't finish the question.

It was because Dominic had briefly turned his gaze to the side.

His blue eyes turned cold, devoid of any warmth. With an air of boredom, he cast a look toward someone else.

But the instant he turned his gaze back to me, he had returned to the Dominic I knew.

Smiling, Dominic gently soothed me.

—Sorry for making you wait alone, Roasted Chestnut. Just wait a little longer.

The communication ended before I could ask anything.

I saved Dominic's communication code and thought.

Where on earth was Dominic right now?

It didn't seem to be an interstellar financial institution or Hyper Eden.

Rabbit Butler and Cat Butler suddenly popped into my head, but I forced them out of my mind.

The only reason Dominic was respecting my boundaries and trying to understand me even a little bit like this was because he had lost a part of his existence.

If Dominic had recovered his existence enough to want to 'go home,'

he wouldn't have bothered contacting me to say he'd be right back, so I should wait.

He had no need to ask anyone for understanding.

*‘If it were the original Dominic, I doubt he would have followed the Young god around in the first place.’*

He might have just started by destroying Earth, which was my territory.

A number of horrific thoughts crossed my mind, but they were all just guesswork.

For now, until Dominic fully recovered his existence, making it so he could understand humans a little better was the best course of action.

A hollow laugh escaped me at the square window that flashed into view.

*‘It has to be useful for me to trust it.’*

Then, an arrow immediately appeared in the void.

It was an arrow pointing to the place where Samra was.

To think it could tell me a location even though Samra wasn't a subordinate. Its performance really seemed to have been upgraded.

*‘This is nice. Keep doing just like this in the future.’*

After offering praise, I followed the arrow.

Leaving the hospital ward and entering the administrative building, the atmosphere was chaotic.

The Era Agency hadn't been a place that ran with extreme systematic precision to begin with, but today felt especially distracted.

I quietly slipped out among the buzzing crowd.

The arrow was pointing toward the basement.

It was the same underground area where Samra and I had once fled when CEO Cha tore open the side of the Era Agency’s complex.

Feeling a sense of familiarity, I diligently chased the arrow and reached the 5th basement level.

There was an evacuation passage here that connected to another building.

I knew the location of the hidden iron door, but I didn't know how to open it.

The arrow was pointing straight at the iron door.

That was when I stood in front of the wall and hesitated for a moment.

“…!!”

I stepped back in alarm.

Sharp metal suddenly shot out from behind the wall.

An all-too-familiar black metal silently pierced through the wall surface and rapidly withdrew.

Following that, black lines were slashed messily across it, and the iron door was torn to pieces.

As I gazed beyond the crumbling fragments, I let out a scream.

“Restriction!!”

The black metal hand that was lunging forward again came to a breathless halt.

Samra slowly turned his eyes to look at me.

He was in a gruesome state with one arm torn off.

The inside of his artificial skin, which was normally as smooth as a human's, was starkly exposed.

The frame of his forcibly ripped shoulder joint was twisted crookedly.

Crushed parts and torn bundles of fiber optics spilled outward, spitting out crackling sparks.

Gasping for breath, I stammered as I opened my mouth.

“Lieutenant Colonel Je, why are you doing this to Samra…?”

Je Hyun-oh, freed from the Restriction, called out to me with his metal hand hanging down.

“Goyo.”

At that moment, wind stirred.

*CRUNCH.*

Moving too fast for me to track, a metal hand pierced through Samra's torso.

Along with the sound of merciless, devastating destruction, Je Hyun-oh whispered.

“This thing is broken.”
