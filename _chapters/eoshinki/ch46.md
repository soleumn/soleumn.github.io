<br>Banana fell into the river of Aid.

The struggling figure quickly vanished, respawning back at the starting point.

I was walking carefully on the wafer boards, trying not to slip. Just then, the recruits who had crossed the bridge by holding onto the ropes turned to look at me. They had just stepped onto solid ground.

Exchanging glances, they suddenly shook the wafer bridge I was on. The narrow bridge swayed violently, and I couldn't help but fall.

I quickly grabbed the rope to stop my descent.

The recruits clicked their tongues and ran forward.

I watched their backs as they entered the castle. Just as I was about to move, gripping the rope...

One recruit remained.

He was a recruit with small angel wings on either side of his head. His shaggy hair was so long in the front that it covered half his eyes, and his hunched shoulders made him look timid for his height and build.

For some reason, he stared at me with intense nervousness.

Using the rope's elasticity and recoil, I quickly crossed the chasm. As I neared the ground, Angel Wings hurriedly reached out to me. I grabbed his hand and stepped onto the flat ground.

Then, while still holding his hand, I immediately spun my body and shoved Angel Wings downward.

"Ack! Trainee Han Goyo...!"

Angel Wings, now dangling from Cookie Cliff, called out my name in desperation.

*'Trainee?'*

The bizarre title made me stare down at Angel Wings.

"Who the hell are you?" I asked.

Every other recruit was hostile toward me, so it was only natural to be suspicious of someone suddenly pretending to help. I decided I'd hear him out before deciding whether to pull him up from the cliff.

Angel Wings' voice trembled as he stammered, "I-I'm...!"

But he couldn't finish his sentence.

This wasn't a situation where I could simply trust that he was offering a hand out of goodwill.

As I gazed down at him, he remained silent. Without a second thought, I turned to walk away.

Just as I was about to leave, Angel Wings shouted desperately, "I'm the one who pushed you!"

"..."

I never expected to find the culprit this way. Or to find out he was *this* kind of bastard.

I stared at Angel Wings, my expression a mix of disbelief and exasperation.

Under my gaze, Angel Wings shook his head frantically.

"I-I didn't want to do it either! But no matter how much I thought about it, it just didn't seem right..."

I pressed him for specifics. "What wasn't right? Tell me everything."

When I promised to report him to his superiors if he explained properly, Angel Wings began to recount the situation, his voice trembling on the verge of tears.

Listening to his story, I finally understood why the recruits were so hostile toward me.

Entering the Trial of the Suitable was a matter of risking one's life.

Since it was a task no sane person would volunteer for, the Era Agency heavily emphasized a noble spirit of sacrifice. They inflated the recruits' pride by stressing the need for invisible devotion to the nation, much like the NIS's black agents.

But then, a thorn in their side appeared.

There was no way the recruits hadn't looked up Han Goyo.

They all shared the same thought: While they had thrown themselves into the Trial with a sense of duty, some guy who did nothing but click a mouse and tap a keyboard had waltzed into a Special Team position without any resolve, just by sheer luck.

*'So, a jobless guy just rolled in and kicked out the stone that was already firmly embedded.'*

Angel Wings, who had just confessed everything to me, looked even more aggrieved, as if he were the one who had been wronged.

I summarized his story for him.

"So, you're saying you're the one who pushed me, but the other recruits are the real villains here?"

Angel Wings muttered despondently, "I'm sorry..."

"And why the sudden confession to me now?"

"...Well."

When his answer took too long, I reached out to throw him off the cliff.

Angel Wings shrieked, "I—I was a fan!"

"..."

"I really liked you a lot. I watched all your matches... only on video, of course... So I thought it would be better if I pushed you off safely myself, rather than letting the others do it..."

His way of thinking was a bit strange.

As I remained silent, Angel Wings tentatively called out to me.

"Trainee..."

I silently extended my hand. Angel Wings's face immediately brightened as he grabbed it and pulled himself up.

"Thank you for saving me!"

Then, he declared with great enthusiasm, "I will help you, Trainee. Just tell me what to do."

Though I would be the one helping him, I didn't bother to correct him. Even if he had some ulterior motive, Hopdpop wasn't a dangerous Trial, so letting him follow me around seemed fine.

I turned my gaze aside. While I had been talking to Angel Wings, the revived trainees were crossing the bridge again.

At the very front, I saw a Yellow Banana furiously charging forward, gripping the rope.

Our eyes met, and he seemed to be cursing at me, but I couldn't hear him clearly from this distance.

It would be best to enter the castle before Banana arrived.

I took the lead, and Angel Wings quickly followed behind me.

Just then, the Sheep-Head Slime on my shoulder spoke.

"Roasted Chestnut."

He wobbled his squishy body, making a suggestion.

"How about taking this one in as a subordinate? He's quite impressive. He has a few flaws, but they can be easily corrected."

*'Impressive enough to catch Dominic's eye?'*

I glanced at Angel Wings.

He certainly didn't seem lacking in ability, considering he was the only one who hadn't fallen off the Wafer Legs.

But he was a recruit.

When I thought about the list of people Dominic had taken a liking to so far, it was a highly suspicious matter.

*'But why isn't the System Window telling me to register?'*

It used to be so annoying all the time, but lately, it felt like it was slacking off.

Harboring doubts about the Angel Wings and the System Window, I first entered the Castle of Sweets.

As I stepped inside the castle, the actual stage appeared.

In the first stage, I had to advance while dodging rotating sweepers on a slippery floor.

Several giant churros sticks were spinning in various places.

Every time a churro spun, shedding cinnamon sugar, ripples formed in the pink syrup covering the floor.

I had to keep moving forward while dodging the spinning churros sticks, but the syrup-slicked floor made it easy to slip and fall. Once I went down, it was hard to get back up. I'd just be flailing on the ground until a churros stick came by and knocked me far away.

That wasn't all; I also had to watch the patterns on the floor's square tiles. The checkerboard-like floor kept changing its patterns at regular intervals, and stepping on an X mark would launch me randomly into the air.

Up ahead, I could see the other recruits struggling across the syrup-covered floor. Just then, one of them got hit by a churros stick and sent flying. He managed to stop right at the edge of the floor, barely avoiding a fall, but he was left flailing pathetically in the syrup.

The other two were advancing steadily.

At this rate, they'd enter the Second Stage before me.

*'Not on my watch.'*

I stepped into the Syrup.

But instead of moving forward, I watched the Churros stick's movements and found the perfect spot to stand.

One of the recruits ahead turned back, saw me, and wore a look of utter disbelief. It was understandable—I was just standing there when we should have been rushing forward.

"Trainee...?" Angel Wings called out, sounding just as bewildered.

I turned my head slightly toward him. "Just watch what I do and copy me."

Hearing me speak so formally, Angel Wings stammered, "What? But I can't do things like you, Trainee Han Goyo..."

"If you can hold on to the Wafer Legs, you can definitely do it."

The Churros stick moved behind my back.

I waited a little longer. Then, when the perfect timing arrived...

I jumped, adjusting my angle.

*Thump.*

The moment I felt the heavy impact, my body shot forward like an arrow.

If you got the stick's force, angle, and timing just right, you could jump across the entire stage.

This was my first time trying it with my own body, so I had even considered a second attempt. Fortunately, I succeeded on the first try.

The recruits watched me fly in a parabolic arc, their eyes wide.

I cleared the stage in a single bound and landed lightly on the ground.

At the same time, curses erupted from all around.

"What the hell is that guy? What just happened?!"

A wave of immense satisfaction washed over me.

I smiled at them as they flailed around in the syrup.

Then, Angel Wings used his Churros stick to cross over to our stage. He arrived panting heavily, his eyes wide with shock.

"Huff... huff... So this actually works...!"

"Let's go."

I immediately moved to the next stage with Angel Wings.

The Second Stage was a massive candy disc. We had to cross a bridge to reach it, but anything outside the disc's perimeter was an instant-death fall.

The disc was littered with miscellaneous items. I strode across the bridge, picked up a Marshmallow Glove from the disc, and equipped it.

"Find the same one and put it on."

"Yes, sir!"

After Angel Wings found and equipped a glove, he turned to me and asked,

"So, what are we supposed to do here?"

I slammed my fists together with a loud *thwack*.

Just as I was checking the gloves' condition, a recruit entered the arena. He was the one who had been in the lead during the first stage.

He immediately crossed the bridge and ran toward the platform. Seeing him reach straight for the item, I was certain.

*'He knows the strategy.'*

Someone must have told him how to beat Hopdpop. The three who had been in the lead definitely seemed to know, and I suspected the other recruits did too. After all, they might have known the strategy but simply lacked the reflexes to react before the bridge collapsed.

I lightly jogged toward the recruit. And before he could grab the item, I smacked him with my glove.

*Thwack!*

The recruit went flying with a satisfying *crack*.

"Aaaaargh!"

As the recruit tumbled off the platform, I tilted my head and gave Angel Wings a simple explanation.

"Just beat up everyone who comes in."

Angel Wings gaped. I calmly added, "We're going to beat them all up here once before moving on to the next stage."

*'Lieutenant Colonel Je, Captains, Samra. I'm sorry.'*

I had tried to be just the right kind of "stumbling block" to avoid tarnishing the Special Team's reputation, but it seemed I'd have to become a massive one.

Judging by how things were going, this wasn't a situation where I could just finish with a decent rank. If I didn't win by an overwhelming margin, the Special Team would be accused of giving preferential treatment to a parachuted recruit.

Having changed my objective, I explained the item usage and the stage's gimmick to Angel Wings.

Before long, the other two recruits arrived.

"Huh? What's this?"

It was the same two who had been swimming in the syrup earlier.

Sensing something was off, they approached the platform with their guards up.

I grabbed one of the Jelly Balls I'd stacked in a corner earlier. Though soft, the baseball-sized balls packed a serious punch upon impact.

I wound up like a pitcher and hurled one.

*Thwack!* A clean hit.

"Urgh!"

The recruit who took the hit stumbled and fell. As he stared at me in shock, I unleashed a rapid-fire barrage of Jelly Balls.

*Thwack-thwack-thwack-thwack-thwack!*

After five consecutive hits, he stumbled backward, unable to even fight back, before tumbling off the Candy Disc.

"Aaaargh! You cheap bastard—!"

The loser's pathetic cries faded as he fell.

The other one was taken care of by Angel Wings.

I threw a rope made of String Jelly, which whipped around the opponent's body and sent him plummeting.

Of course, I had taught him how to use the item, but his rope-handling skills were impressive.

After taking down one enemy, Angel Wings seemed to surge with confidence. He stepped beside me and declared solemnly, "I will protect you."

I thought it should be the other way around, but I kept my mouth shut again.

After a short wait, the other recruits arrived in a swarm. It was Banana and his crew.

Screaming curses, they flooded onto the platform. Since the platform wasn't very large, we were at a disadvantage with so many of them.

I tied a String Jelly to a ring on the floor and gripped it in one hand. With my other hand, I scooped up a Jelly Ball and hurled it toward the ceiling.

The Jelly Ball struck exactly where I wanted, and a cheerful voice announced over the intercom:

<br>

***"Three, two, one, start!"***

<br>

*Vrooooom!*

A giant exhaust fan on the ceiling roared to life.

Everything on the Candy Disc floated into the air.

Suddenly airborne, everyone flailed wildly.

They couldn't have known about this Hidden Gimmick.

Only Angel Wings and I, holding onto the String Jelly Rope I'd anchored to the floor, managed to stay grounded.

When the fan abruptly shut off, the recruits tumbled back down to the platform.

The impact left them paralyzed for a few seconds.

"Agh!"

"Damn it, ah...!"

I rushed in and started pummeling them with my gloves.

The Marshmallow Glove had a surprisingly satisfying impact.

I was focusing my assault on Banana when he roared, "Fuck! Drop Han Goyo first!"

Banana roared. The remaining recruits all turned their sights on me at once.

A smirk tugged at the corners of my lips.

Just as I was about to throw down and start a brawl...

"Trainee! I'll help you!!"

Angel Wings came running toward me, calling out.

He was clutching a Popping Candy Bomb to his chest.

Before I could even react—

*Pop!*

The Popping Candy Bomb exploded, blasting Angel Wings and all the nearby recruits right off the platform.

Only Banana and I remained.

I stared blankly at the scattering candy debris.

"Aw, fuck."

Banana spat out the curse and sneered at me.

"Hey. You're a Special Team recruit, but you're fucking terrible."

"...You're right."

I readily admitted it.

"I need to work harder."

And I changed my plan.

I'd just give up on taking first place.

Ever since he entered Hopdpop and Han Goyo, Won Taebeom felt as if he were on the verge of losing his mind.

After The Cell X, he had never ceased thinking about Han Goyo for a single moment. The ecstatic memory of being enveloped in perfect silence refused to let him go.

The auditory hallucinations that returned after he escaped the Golden Influence felt even more agonizing than before.

But it wasn't just the longing for silence that tormented Won Taebeom. The protective instinct to shield the young god also plagued him.

He couldn't be sure if this was truly his own genuine emotion. Considering he had been captivated by the young god's Blind Faith, it was possible that its influence still lingered within him.

Yet, Won Taebeom...

From the moment he witnessed the miracle of resurrection after the Death of Three Days, he had felt a vivid, undeniable temptation.

He was willing to be blinded by blind faith once more, if only to dwell beneath that golden light.

Why bother thinking about anything else?

He only needed to hope for and desire the Young god. That alone would be the ultimate happiness.

Amidst his confusion, he entered Hopdpop with Han Goyo.

With Han Goyo right before his eyes, the fanatical thoughts he had barely managed to suppress went wild.

Won Taebeom struggled desperately to maintain his neutral instructor's demeanor.

The surge of worry, coupled with his protective instinct, also stemmed from his knowledge of the current situation.

The recruits' opinions of Han Goyo were far from good.

Since instructors did not intervene in Hopdpop, there was every chance the recruits would bully Han Goyo.

Even knowing it wasn't a dangerous Trial, he was still worried.

He was just a young kid, being dragged around helplessly.

I clearly remembered how he had rejoiced over a single sandwich in The Cell X, and how he had offered a slightly awkward salute and a gentle smile when our eyes met in the classroom today.

At the time, I was struggling to maintain my mask as a fair and impartial Instructor.

"That bastard is a complete psycho!"

"Crazy. Fucking crazy."

"What the hell is that bastard trying to do right now?!"

Recruits kept respawning at the starting point.

They would pour out a stream of curses towards Han Goyo, only to fall silent as they glanced at Won Taebeom. This cycle repeated itself.

Even worse, an increasing number of recruits were using up all their hearts and becoming Spectators.

Something was clearly happening.

No matter how I looked at it, Han Goyo seemed to be getting bullied by the other recruits.

The moment the thought struck me that he was barely enduring their cruel torment alone, I couldn't stand it any longer.

Won Taebeom immediately began tearing through Hopdpop.

Having cleared the stage countless times, he flew through it in an instant.

But even after passing several stages, Han Goyo was nowhere to be seen.

*'Where the hell is he?'*

He finally found Han Goyo at the Final Stage.

Han Goyo was perfectly fine. He was simply standing before the finish line like a gatekeeper, holding a giant lollipop.

"Fucking hell!"

"Just finish it already!"

"Just go in already! Just win first place!"

The recruits screamed as they charged toward the finish line.

Won Taebeom's heart sank.

Just as he was about to stop them—

*Whack!*

Han Goyo swung the lollipop like a baseball bat.

A recruit was struck squarely by the candy and sent flying out of bounds.

Watching the recruit soar into the distance, Han Goyo grinned.

"Wow, a home run."
