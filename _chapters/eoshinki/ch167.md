<br>Manifestation of god.

It was a term used to describe the moment a god appeared before humans.

I couldn't understand why he was suddenly talking about waiting for the Manifestation of god.

I thought about it for a moment, but the more seriously I considered it, the worse my thoughts became.

The god he was waiting for must be the one he met in &lt;Zero>.

After all, Je Hyun-oh and I hadn't known each other for very long.

No matter how hard I tried to force the pieces to fit, it was impossible for me to be the god he was so eagerly awaiting.

*Why do I keep thinking of another god...?*

I quickly shook my head to clear the intrusive thought.

Am I a child? To demand he only like me?

While it was unpleasant that he thought of another god while looking at me, I could tolerate it.

No, is this also the Young god's way of thinking?

The thoughts were so intertwined that I couldn't tell them apart.

But I, too, hated the thought of my subordinate belonging to another god.

*'Ah, please.'*

I forced my mind to wander elsewhere to stop these crazy thoughts.

Come to think of it, "Manifestation" was also the name Je Hyun-oh had received from the Trial.

The names given by Trials either reflected the Adaptee's traits or held some symbolic meaning.

If so, since he had been waiting for the manifestation of the god he met in &lt;Zero>, his name...

"..."

I took another deep breath and chased away the impure thoughts.

Then, I tried to adopt a more positive mindset.

Since my subordinate had been hurt by another god, couldn't I just treat him well?

*'So well that he'd forget all about his previous god.'*

It seemed like a good plan.

With my heart finally at ease, I carefully withdrew my hand from the screen.

I couldn't waste any more time. I had to get Je Hyun-oh out.

I worried for a moment that he might slice me as I withdrew my hand, but fortunately, Je Hyun-oh let go without a fight.

He seemed to be in a slightly better mood now.

When I finally pulled my hand out, I stared at it in shock.

My hand, which had been covered in wounds, was completely healed the moment it left the screen.

Come to think of it, I had crushed the Mafia to death in the racing game earlier, yet there wasn't a single drop of blood on my hand when I withdrew it.

I looked down at the control panel, wondering if I had healed myself or if the game's effects simply didn't apply to reality.

The arcade machine's control panel consisted of a joystick and two buttons.

The joystick controlled the craft, while the buttons were used for the main weapon and the special move.

I pressed a button to test the attack.

The main weapon fired black crescent moons in a straight line, like ammunition.

Picking up items would upgrade the weapon, increasing the number of projectiles to two or three lines.

Using the special move would draw black lines across the entire screen, like a beast's claw marks, slicing all enemies to pieces.

However, the special move could only be used once the gauge was full.

Just as I finished my quick review and was about to start the game, an unexpected thought struck me.

Je Hyun-oh's pixelated character began to move on its own.

I stared blankly at the screen, still clutching the joystick.

*'I'm actually pretty good at this shooting game...'*

Honestly, I prided myself on my skill. I was confident I could play well enough to satisfy Je Hyun-oh, so I felt a tiny bit stung that he wasn't giving me the chance.

Why does he keep refusing?

If it were &lt;Zero>'s god instead of me, he would have accepted it without a fight, wouldn't he?

My mind threatened to fill with strange thoughts again, so I forced myself to focus on the game.

As the game began, spaceships appeared on the screen.

Several ships, seemingly crewed by aliens, unleashed a barrage of light beam bullets at Je Hyun-oh's character.

The sheer volume of the attack made me flinch involuntarily.

But Je Hyun-oh was still Je Hyun-oh.

He dodged the countless beams with minimal movement.

At the same time, he counterattacked with his own black crescent moons.

Every single attack struck its mark. A spaceship, hit by a series of black crescent moons, was destroyed.

As three ships were sunk in a row, the word "BOOOOM!" flashed across the screen with an explosion effect.

A weapon item emerged from the debris of the spaceships.

*'I have to get that.'*

It was an item that upgraded the main weapon. If I missed it now, I had no idea when the next one would appear. I absolutely had to get it.

But Je Hyun-oh was too busy attacking other ships.

I hesitated, then moved the joystick. The words immediately appeared on the screen:

<br>

*'I'm just going to grab the item.'*

I quickly moved the joystick, guiding Je Hyun-oh's pixelated character to weave through the light beam bullets and collect the item.

Suddenly, the main weapon—the black crescent moon—multiplied from a single line to three.

The spaceships on the screen began to be destroyed in an instant.

I waited with a faint sense of anticipation.

I hoped for pixelated text like 'The new weapon is satisfactory' to appear, but the screen remained silent.

Je Hyun-oh simply used the three-lined black crescent moon to shoot down the spaceships even faster.

I just watched the aliens falling from the destroyed spaceships.

The aliens kept popping up speech bubbles, but they were all blocked out, making them impossible to read.

In fact, before I could even try to read them, Je Hyun-oh shot them down with the crescent moon.

Having cleared the stage in the blink of an eye, Je Hyun-oh reached the first boss.

The first boss was a seafood monster.

I felt a flicker of familiarity at the sight of its massive eye and wriggling octopus tentacles, but it was gone in an instant.

A black line slashed diagonally across the screen, slicing the octopus into pieces.

After starting by cutting the octopus's health in half, Je Hyun-oh began mercilessly pummeling it with black crescent moons. He was practically mincing it for sashimi, and I almost felt sorry for the creature.

Yet, at the same time, my heart swelled with pride at my subordinate's overwhelming power.

Having swiftly dispatched the octopus, Je Hyun-oh took flight through space once more.

I watched him on the screen. Unlike the games I had played before, Je Hyun-oh's game was set in outer space.

Despite the completely surreal space, Je Hyun-oh showed no sign of panic.

He simply drifted silently through the cosmos, his black metallic wings fluttering.

I was quietly observing him when—

"...!"

A massive hand suddenly reached into the screen.

It was the same white-gloved hand I had seen in Mo Haein's game.

I watched the hand with intense wariness. If it dared to lay a finger on my subordinate, I would dive in immediately.

But the hand did nothing to Je Hyun-oh.

It merely swept across the screen.

As the hand passed, the ordinary spaceships were transformed into colossal warships.

My eyes widened at the ominous sight, but before I could even process it, the screen went black.

At first, I thought the arcade machine had lost power.

But Je Hyun-oh's character remained on screen.

Light beam bullets streaked through the black void that had swallowed the display.

My vision had vanished.

The bullets, hidden in the darkness, only flickered into view just before impact.

The alien spaceships were invisible, of course, and their attack patterns had become insanely difficult.

It was an unbelievable level of difficulty for just the Second Stage.

This was a place designed to kill you.

If I had memorized the patterns, it might have been manageable, but this was my first time playing.

And I didn't have enough coins to spare for multiple attempts.

I had to finish this in a single coin clear. With only one chance, failure was not an option.

I watched Je Hyun-oh with tense eyes.

He moved nimbly, dodging the light beam bullets. He was relying on pure reflexes to evade them, timing his movements to the very last instant before impact.

But facing the second boss in this state seemed too dangerous. I couldn't bear to see my subordinate get hurt.

Just as I was hesitating to intervene—

"Huh...?"

A vacant sound escaped my lips.

A meteor shower was falling. The golden meteors filling the black screen were identical to the one I had created for Mo Haein.

And the moment he witnessed the golden meteor shower...

Je Hyun-oh froze for a split second.

It was truly just a fraction of a moment, but in a situation where flashbangs were blanketing the entire area, it was fatal.

Je Hyun-oh was mercilessly bombarded by the flashbangs.

As the small character was buried in the flashbangs, his health bar plummeted in an instant.

I couldn't watch any longer. I grabbed the joystick.

An exclamation mark speech bubble appeared over Je Hyun-oh's head, but I ignored it and took control of him.

First, I focused on dodging the light beam bullets.

They only became visible just before impact, making the visibility range narrow. Even a graze on the wingtips would trigger a hit, making them difficult to deal with.

However, I was able to narrowly dodge them by a mere 1-pixel difference.

The moment I dodged them by a hair's breadth, I launched a crescent moon whenever I had even a slight opening.

I recalled the positions of the ships I'd seen just before the screen went dark and attacked by instinct.

Fortunately, the gauge was slowly filling up, so I seemed to be hitting my marks.

The moment the gauge was full, I immediately unleashed my special move, striking across the entire screen.

Speech bubbles announcing the destruction of spaceships popped up one after another on the black screen.

After taking down enough enemies to catch my breath, I quickly withdrew my hand.

I wanted to sweep away the black screen, but the white glove had created it.

Since it felt like an entity similar to a game Administrator, this might be linked to clearing the stage.

It seemed best not to touch it.

Instead of clearing the darkness, I created a new star for Je Hyun-oh.

I coated him in my golden light so he wouldn't be confused by the fakes.

A golden outline formed around the black crescent moon fired by the dot character.

The crescent moon, now wreathed in gold, carried its own light as it pierced through the darkness. It was as if Je Hyun-oh himself were creating a meteor shower.

Adding the light had also proven far more helpful for gameplay. His attacks, which had previously been swallowed by the pitch-black background, were now clearly visible. Furthermore, the trajectory of the golden crescent moon temporarily cleared the darkness, securing his field of vision.

Just as I was thinking this would be enough to conquer even the boss battle, an ellipsis bubble appeared above his head as he watched the procession of golden light.

Then, pixelated text materialized.

What memory?

I wanted to know more, but the pixelated text vanished.

I waited for new words to appear.

Then, Je Hyun-oh looked up at the sky.

In the star-filled expanse... he gazed at me.

A sensation washed over me.

A sense of complete unity, as if I had become one with my subordinate.

The same thrilling synchronicity I had felt with Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk before surged through my entire body.

The golden light streaming from my eyes seemed to grow even more intense.

I moved Je Hyun-oh.

And I knew what he desired.

I could feel the direction he wanted to turn.

He moved in perfect accord with my will.

We soared through the star-studded universe, crossing the Milky Way.

No matter how overwhelming the ships that appeared, no matter how many light beam bullets rained down to cover the entire screen, they could not hinder my subordinate, who carried out my will.

He was a being who could fly freely and cut through anything.

*Because I made him that way.*

*Because he is my subordinate.*

Together with Je Hyun-oh, I reached the final stage.

But even after clearing all the stages, the final boss did not appear.

Instead, the giant hand that had repeatedly intervened in the game appeared.

The white glove brushed away the black darkness that covered the screen.

Then, with the universe at its back, it stood majestically as if it were the final boss.

"What is it trying to do?"

I reached my hand into the screen immediately.

I was only trying to protect my subordinate... but something felt... a little off...

The moment the white glove saw my hand enter the screen, it trembled violently.

Then, without warning, it grabbed my hand.

Just as I was about to let go, suspicious of its motives, the white glove started shaking my hand on its own.

As it shook my hand, it spammed heart-shaped speech bubbles.

*'What is this?'*

I had always considered the white glove a hostile entity because it constantly acted creepily and interfered with the game.

Yet, its actions now were brimming with affection for me.

When I finally snapped out of it and tried to shake my hand free, a torrent of teary speech bubbles erupted above the white glove.

The white glove squeezed my hand tight, refusing to let go, while about ten tear-shaped speech bubbles floated around it.

Just as I was about to force my hand free from the inexplicable gesture—

A ferocious claw mark tore through the cosmos.

A golden-streaked strike slashed sharply across the entire screen.

With a sound like space itself being ripped apart, the back of the white glove's hand was gouged open.

Broken glitch fragments erupted from the gash.

I stood there, mouth agape, trying to make sense of the situation, when Je Hyun-oh's character took flight and soared toward me.

Pixelated text materialized below.

Still, it seemed I'd have to take this thing down to clear the game.

I tightened my grip and shook off the white glove.

The white glove stumbled as if it had been struck.

It started spouting a flurry of teary-eyed speech bubbles again, then suddenly displayed a massive one containing a pixelated image of a little lamb.

I narrowed my eyes at the speech bubble that now filled half the screen.

Then, on a whim, I called out to the white glove.

*Pervert?*

"Mr. Black Block...?"
