<br>While receiving trainee instruction at the Era Agency, I came to realize something all over again.

Trainees were normally in no position to even look a captain in the eye.

That wasn’t all. There was a seven-year age gap between the captains and me.

In a country that prided itself on courtesy, a recruit seven years younger to pat a Captain on the head and declare them "good boy"….

*‘Aaaaaagh!’*

I screamed, but no sound escaped me.

My hands were still busy stroking their heads.

I’d once flicked the Young god on the forehead. Now our positions had reversed.

I deserved to be pummeled with prayer beads—endlessly.

No, this was the kind of insubordination that wouldn’t be resolved by a few smacks with prayer beads.

The moment I regained control of my body, I’d probably have to prostrate myself before them.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein glanced at each other as I petted them.

At last, my hands stopped stroking their heads.

Kwak Hanmuk grabbed my hand.

Then he placed it right back on top of his head.

Grinning, he asked, “Master. I’m fucking good, aren’t I?”

I screamed inwardly again.

But regardless, my mouth was moving on its own once more.

“Yeah. I’ll give you a present.”

I pulled Supernova Pang Pang out of my inventory.

Kwak Hanmuk raised an eyebrow at the popping candy that had appeared out of nowhere.

I gave the candy as a present to my cute subordinate, who loved snacks.

*‘This is driving me crazy.’*

The train of thought that had led me to think Captain Kwak was cute had felt perfectly natural.

Kwak Hanmuk took the popping candy and replied without a hint of surprise.

“Master, what about Captain Mo? Don’t you have one for her?”

“I don’t….”

“Aw, I’ll share mine with her.”

He was playing along with this utterly ridiculous game of pretend.

Meanwhile, I felt bad that I didn’t have a present for Mo Haein.

As Kwak Hanmuk rustled open the candy wrapper, I met Mo Haein’s eyes. She was watching me.

“Is there anything you want? I’ll get you a present.”

Mo Haein blinked a couple of times.

Holding a few pieces of candy Kwak Hanmuk had given her, she replied a little awkwardly.

“Hmm… There’s nothing I want… But you’re feeling all right, aren’t you?”

I was touched by how she put her master’s well-being ahead of her own desires.

I reached out and cupped Mo Haein’s cheek.

“My pretty subordinate.”

At that moment, I finally regained control.

“I’ll make it up to you by pampering you even more from now on— I’m sorry!”

But I still didn’t have complete control. My hand was still rubbing Mo Haein’s cheek.

With her face cupped in my hand, she stared at me in surprise, lips slightly parted.

“C-Captain, my condition’s a little strange right now!”

As I spoke through my cold sweat, Kwak Hanmuk asked while eating the popping candy, “Is it Chestnut?”

“Yes. I’m Chestnut right now.”

Only after answering did I realize that, in my panic, I’d used one of the chestnut names myself.

Without saying anything, Kwak Hanmuk gently removed my hand from Mo Haein’s face.

Just when I thought I’d managed to sort things out—

“Were you feeling left out? I’ll pet you, too.”

My tongue spouted another strange, nonsensical line, and this time my hand went for Kwak Hanmuk’s face.

I did everything in my power to push the Young god out of me.

But I couldn’t stop myself from kneading Kwak Hanmuk’s cheek.

“I’m so sorry, Captain Kwak…. I’m trying, but, um, I can’t control my body just yet….”

“Sure. Take your time.”

Kwak Hanmuk offered up his face as if letting a child play with a toy. Licking the crumbs of popping candy from his lips, he said, “I should at least do this much for my master.”

“I really am sorry….”

After that, I continued to show the captains the sight of the Young god and me splitting apart inside my own mind and fighting it out.

After a fierce struggle, I managed to gain at least a slight advantage.

All the while, Kwak Hanmuk quietly played the Young god’s victim at my side.

It took a long time of kneading Kwak Hanmuk’s face, but I finally managed to push the Young god back. I had no idea when he might surge forward again.

My eyes were still gold, and I could feel the Young god grumbling resentfully inside me.

“What exactly happened?”

Once things had calmed down after all the commotion, Mo Haein asked.

“I think it’s because of the power I have.”

Having already gone through something similar at the arcade, I was able to explain it a little more easily this time.

As I explained things to the captains, I also thought about what had caused this situation.

*‘This is all Dominic’s fault, isn’t it?’*

He had forcibly fused me and the Young god back together after we’d been separated.

But it seemed like he’d gotten the ratio a little wrong.

Normally, I held control over my body and mind, and the Young god’s influence only increased when I used my powers.

But right now, it felt like we were evenly split, fifty-fifty.

I thought for a moment about the Young god inside me.

Until now, I’d vaguely imagined the Young god as a little kid.

But the Young god I’d encountered was nothing like I’d imagined.

He looked exactly like me.

He had my face, but he talked and acted like a child.

That didn’t feel strange. Somehow, I accepted it naturally.

I just couldn’t believe I had to become one with a social misfit like that.

Now that I’d met him separately, I found it even harder to imagine how we could coexist.

“I’m not completely back to normal, so I think I’ll be acting strangely for a while….”

Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk readily told me it was all right.

Mo Haein even said I could touch her face if I needed to.

“It’s not like the trainee doing something weird is anything new. I’ll just think of it as a child touching me.”

“……”

It seemed she still didn’t understand just how dangerous the Young god was.

I asked Kwak Hanmuk to please stop me if I started doing anything crazy.

One small mercy was that the captains didn’t seem too put off by my condition.

Maybe that was possible because they were my subordinates.

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">“Everyone likes me better anyway.”</span>

<br>

I was just finishing explaining things to the captains, thinking back to the Young god’s words, when Kwak Hanmuk asked, “By the way, where’d your pet slime go? I haven’t seen it around.”

Only then did I realize Dominic was missing.

I looked around in a hurry, but there was no sign of the glittering slime.

“…Oh. He went for a little walk.”

I made up an excuse, but a chill spread through my chest.

Where had he disappeared to?

Whenever he’d vanished and then returned before, it had never been a good thing.

And I was even more uneasy because every time Dominic disappeared, *something*… changed about him.

The timing couldn’t have been worse.

He’d fused me together however he pleased, then disappeared. I wouldn’t be able to get any explanation from him.

But, as always, my relationship with Dominic was one-sided.

He could always watch me and come find me, while all I could do was wait.

The countless eyes that had *stared into me* suddenly returned, like a hallucination.

I clutched the blanket for a moment, then spoke to the captains.

“…Lieutenant Colonel Je is in the tower.”

Before I lost consciousness, I’d surveyed all of Hyper Eden and confirmed that my subordinate was in the tower.

“We need to get into the tower.”

Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk’s faces grew slightly tense. Mo Haein asked cautiously, “Is that possible?”

“Not right now.”

In COF, the tower was an area that was normally impossible to enter.

No matter which ending you got in the game, the tower’s doors never opened.

It was safe to say there was no way to get in from outside.

“We have to make the owner of the tower open the door himself.”

But if he were willing to open it that easily, Black Block wouldn’t have told me to come see him in the first place.

*‘Being a Stringer won’t be enough.’*

Even if I worked hard on quests and closed things out with the True Ending, the tower’s door still wouldn’t open.

There had to be something more I could do besides my work as a Stringer.

I was turning the problem over in my head when an Interstellar Communication video window abruptly appeared in front of me.

An alien with long hair flying in every direction shouted, 

*<big>—Kid! What did you just do?!</big>*

It was the administrator of the Galactic Trading Post.

Before I could even react to the sudden call, I first checked on the captains.

“…What is that?”

Kwak Hanmuk muttered in disbelief at the unexpected sight of an alien.

Mo Haein narrowed her eyes, watching the administrator warily.

The captains had already seen the key NPCs during the COF briefing.

So they immediately recognized that the administrator was someone unfamiliar.

The administrator looked past the screen at the captains and acknowledged them.

—Those are the kid’s subordinates, aren’t they?

But after that one remark, he immediately lost interest.

Maybe I was reading too much into it, but it felt as if he didn’t consider them people.

The administrator scolded me.

—Did you know you were even livestreamed on Day News?

I was so distracted trying to keep the Young god from popping out that my mind was all over the place.

I’d been secretly messaging the administrator, telling the captains he was an alien I knew well, and was so startled that I only managed to answer belatedly.

“Excuse me?”

—Haah…. So the kid didn’t agree to it after all. They went and did whatever they wanted again….

The administrator showed me the terrible thing Day News had done.

I watched the video playing on the screen, unable to close my mouth.

It was footage of me clearing the Event Quest at the arcade.

The chat had been flying by throughout the broadcast, and it felt strangely familiar.

The messages reminded me of my days as a professional gamer.

But the stream was choppy. It kept cutting out and coming back, with static breaking up the image now and then.

The moments when the broadcast stopped followed a clear pattern.

When Dominic came out of the arcade machine. And when I used my divine power.

*‘They didn’t even capture the Pudding Slime.’*

All the footage of me putting my hand inside the arcade machine and exerting my influence had been cut. Only the parts where I was playing the game were broadcast.

Something caught my eye as I watched the livestream.

*‘M-Tube?’*

The most famous video platform on Earth was also called M-Tube.

Of course, it might have just been a coincidence that the space video platform had the same name.

But after remembering what Sa Gongyeon from Day News had been doing on Earth, it was hard to think of it as a coincidence.

After the video ended, the administrator spoke, his eyes simmering with anger.

—Because of this, there’s practically no one in Akasha who doesn’t know about the Young god….

It seemed he’d called right away because he was worried about me.

Thinking he really was a kind alien, I slipped in a question I’d been curious about.

“Who are the people sending messages in the chat? And why are they so interested in me?”

It was hard to believe they were this excited just because a Young god had been born for the first time in a long while.

But maybe I’d asked the wrong question, because the administrator looked at me with deep pity.

He wore the expression of someone looking at a child who didn’t even know the basics.

—…Right. You said there weren’t any adults around you. I’d better start by explaining what Akasha is.

The administrator explained it to me in very simple terms.

Akasha was, in a word, the society where aliens lived.

And “gods” were like nobles or royalty, by Earth’s standards.

They had their own domains in the universe and ruled over them as their masters.

They were like stars that had existed for unfathomable spans of time, beings so far beyond ordinary aliens that no one could dare approach them.

—But a Young God is different.

A Young god could be “touched.”

Because they were inexperienced, even beings who weren't gods could approach them, just as Infernum had done in the past.

And since I was outside the rules of protection, I was the perfect target for the Akasha aliens, who were already obsessed with gods.

*‘So that’s why the aliens are buying newspapers from Day News and watching the livestream in real time.’*

If gods were celebrities, I was like an easily accessible star, which explained the frenzy.

Thinking about it that way, I could more or less understand why the aliens had been sending those kinds of messages during the broadcast.

—Kid. Why did you go to Hyper Eden?

“…Oh. Well.”

Lost in thought, I gave the answer I expected would get a certain reaction, feeling awkward.

“I came to work as a Stringer.”

The administrator clapped his crystal hands over his mouth.

It wasn’t hard to imagine what was going through his head.

He probably thought I was a poor kid taking on all sorts of part-time jobs to earn Star Fragments.

The administrator’s hair drooped, then slowly lifted again.

—Let’s go back right now. Return to your planet immediately. And you should set up a defense system on the planet first.

He even offered to help me with everything he could, so I told him honestly, “I can’t return just yet. I’ve lost one of my subordinates.”

The administrator scolded me sternly.

—Kid. I know toys are precious at your age, but now’s not the time for that. You can get a new subordinate anytime. What if you end up coming face-to-face with the owner of Hyper Eden…?

I hadn’t come face-to-face with him, but I’d communicated with him and even shaken his hand.

We’d even made a verbal deal for the rights to my image.

But if I told him that, the administrator would just scream at me with his hair flying everywhere.

So I pretended not to know and answered, “There are other things I need to take care of besides my subordinate. Thank you for worrying about me.”

—Kid, you…!

Before the administrator could start scolding me, I hurriedly asked another question.

“Do you think any high-ranking gods saw my broadcast?”

The administrator looked displeased, but answered anyway.

—The ancient gods? They definitely saw it. Though I doubt they joined in with the viewers.

At that moment, a good idea came to me.

An idea that would make Black Block open the door whether he wanted to or not.

“Administrator, could you help me with something?”
