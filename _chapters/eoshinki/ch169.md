<br>Contrary to its name, which meant 'Broken Sound', the Representative's Office at Paseong was excessively quiet.

This was because it had been tailored to CEO Cha's preference for solitude.

The man sitting still in his chair, wearing headphones, looked no different than usual.

However, Mok Saehwa, who had observed CEO Cha for a long time, could sense his somber mood.

It was always like this.

He was always subdued on the days he dreamed.

Yet, CEO Cha had never let his mood affect those around him.

He behaved consistently, but Mok Saehwa was the only one who could often read his atmosphere.

She always hoped CEO Cha would be in a good mood.

It was only natural for her to wish for her master's happiness.

Mok Saehwa sighed inwardly.

*'It must be because Mr. Goyo entered COF.'*

From that point on, CEO Cha cleared his schedule again and waited at Paseong Headquarters in case of an emergency.

Paseong's losses were mounting in real time, yet the directors voiced no complaints.

Mok Saehwa was waiting more desperately than anyone else for Han Goyo's safe return.

Even if only for CEO Cha's sake.

Seo Munchan, who had been dispatched to Cheongang, felt the exact same way as Mok Saehwa.

Just as she was recalling the messages she had exchanged with him—

"!..."

Mok Saehwa's gaze darted sharply.

The phone on the desk in the Representative's Office was vibrating.

Very few people knew CEO Cha's personal number.

CEO Cha lowered his gaze, stared at the phone for a moment, then took off his headphones and draped them around his neck.

He checked the screen, which displayed "Unknown Caller," and nodded to Mok Saehwa.

Mok Saehwa immediately summoned her weapon, *Blooming Funeral*.

She shouldered the long, flower-entwined rifle and approached the window. She pushed aside the blinds with the muzzle and scanned the outside, but saw nothing.

He always acts like he's watching from nearby whenever he contacts us, so I checked... And sure enough, there was nothing to be seen.

Mok Saehwa stowed *Blooming Funeral* back into her inventory and shook her head slightly.

CEO Cha reached out and picked up the phone. The moment he answered the call from the unknown number, he addressed the person on the other end.

"Reporter Sa Gongyeon."

His voice, low and measured, continued slowly.

"I told you I won't do the interview."

The person on the other end of the line seemed to be trying to persuade him.

Mok Saehwa's brow furrowed slightly. Normally, she could easily overhear a conversation from this distance.

Yet, calls from this being called Sa Gongyeon were always unclear, as if filled with static.

CEO Cha was the only one who could hear Sa Gongyeon's voice properly.

"An exclusive interview... I don't know. It's only 'exclusive' for you, reporter."

CEO Cha looked at Mok Saehwa while speaking half-heartedly into the phone.

Mok Saehwa shook her phone. She had already reported the situation to Samra at the Era Agency.

Soon, a screen appeared in front of CEO Cha. An expressionless android was displayed on the screen.

Ever since they had speculated that the being named Sa Gongyeon was connected to a god, they had been cooperating with the Era Agency on related matters.

Samra had immediately wiretapped CEO Cha's phone.

But it seemed they had heard nothing as well.

Although no emotion could be read from the android's inorganic eyes, Mok Saehwa felt a faint sense of irritation lingering there for some reason.

Meanwhile, the person on the other end of the line seemed to be spouting nonsense.

CEO Cha leaned back against his chair, his posture radiating annoyance. He detested wasting time on useless chatter. He was only listening in the hope of extracting some useful information from Sa Gongyeon, but his patience was clearly wearing thin.

Mok Saehwa inwardly guessed the call would be cut off any second.

Just then, the laptop on the desk powered on by itself, and a video began to play before CEO Cha's eyes.

Han Goyo appeared on the screen.

Bound with thick rope, Han Goyo was covered in blood. Through the tears in his shredded shirt, his bare skin was visible, scored with red scratches and bruises. Beneath his matted hair, soaked in blood and sweat, Han Goyo's eyes trembled as he barely managed to look up at the camera.

<br>

*"CEO Cha... please help me..."*

<br>

The whisper was thick with terror and dread. The sound of labored breathing followed.

Mok Saehwa clenched her fists. Forcing herself to stay calm, she glanced at Representative Cha.

But for some reason, he seemed completely unimpressed. Instead of showing any alarm, he asked back as if bored.

"Reporter, aren't you too old to be lying for attention?"

Mok Saehwa blinked.

*'It was fake...'*

The video had been so convincing that she hadn't even considered it could be fake. She was simply amazed that the CEO had recognized it instantly.

Sa Gongyeon, on the other end of the line, burst into laughter.

Then, the screen changed.

It was another image of Han Goyo.

A golden-hued Han Goyo was now dominating every screen in the city.

Mok Saehwa unconsciously held her breath, her gaze fixed on the screen.

On the display, Han Goyo—his eyes gleaming with an otherworldly light—scanned his surroundings before his gaze seemed to lock onto the screen.

She felt as if their eyes had met.

His brilliant golden pupils were anything but human. The mismatched colors of his irises vividly marked him as an alien being.

Mok Saehwa, mesmerized, stared back before snapping out of it with a start.

Her phone vibrated briefly in her hand.

Checking the message, she turned to CEO Cha.

"..."

He couldn't tear his eyes away from Han Goyo on the screen.

Mok Saehwa cautiously approached him and held out her phone.

The screen displayed a brief report from Seo Munchan:

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
  Kang Byeokrin is missing from her residence.
</div>

Seo Munchan had been dispatched to protect Kang Byeokrin from Geumcheon.

*'But to go missing inside her own home...'*

The timing coincided perfectly with the call Sa Gongyeon had received. It couldn't be a coincidence.

After reading Seo Munchan's report, CEO Cha finally spoke. "Why are you dragging Kang Byeokrin into this?"

At that moment, Sa Gongyeon's voice came through clearly for the first time.

—For a special interview!

A primal chill ran down Mok Saehwa's spine. Just as an inexplicable revulsion made the hair on her neck stand on end, CEO Cha's voice cut through.

"Let's do the interview."

Mok Saehwa's eyes widened.

"But you'll have to pay the price for a story this big," Sa Gongyeon continued. "As you know, my services aren't cheap, so I'll be taking something expensive."

CEO Cha rose from his chair and drew the Daeil Yeorae. Sheathing the sword, he added in a flat tone, "Where is Han Goyo?"

Absolute trust, affection, and protection.

Such overwhelming emotions, crashing down like a bombardment, would have been too much for ordinary people to bear.

In truth, it would have been more accurate to describe it as something bordering on madness.

But for Mo Haein, it was salvation.

"..."

Han Goyo had given his all until the very end. He had pushed the power of god beyond its limits to find Je Hyun-oh, only to lose consciousness.

Now, he lay there, with no telling when he would wake up...

Twenty-four. The same age as her younger sister.

The thought struck her anew—how young he was—while a wave of profound worry and anxiety churned within her.
