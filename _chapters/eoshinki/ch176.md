<br>After confirming CEO Cha's badge, I raised my gaze once more.

The Cognitive Decline Mask, which still betrayed no hint of the eyes behind it, was still looking at me.

This was a Masked Ball where 666 divine beings had gathered in Akasha.

CEO Cha was the one who fit this place the least.

I stared blankly at CEO Cha, who sat there like a jagged stone.

He seemed to scan me up and down for a moment, then turned his gaze to the Eyeball Alien.

The Eyeball Alien was still keeping three of its eyes fixed on me while staring at CEO Cha with the remaining three.

I forced down the Young god, who kept trying to burst out, and swallowed dryly.

A chilling tension flowed.

A human cannot stand against a god.

Even so, CEO Cha was acting as if he could easily handle someone like the Eyeball Alien.

*'Crazy...'*

Putting aside the shock of the unexpected encounter, I screamed inwardly asking if he was out of his mind while surveying the situation once more.

I thought I could understand what CEO Cha was thinking by coming out like this.

He was wearing a Number Tag.

It meant he was a guest invited by Black Block.

Although the Hide-and-Seek Event had been staged as a bit of entertainment for the Masked Ball, Black Block had presented clear rules.

Do not look inside.

And the Eyeball Alien had been on the verge of violating those rules.

Unless it eliminated the witness, there was no way Black Block wouldn't learn of its actions.

*'But it can't kill Black Block's guest.'*

Whether it violated the rules or killed a guest.

Either way, it would be hard to avoid Black Block's wrath.

The fact that the attendees were concealing their existence beneath masks also worked in CEO Cha's favor.

Because he was suppressing his power, CEO Cha could avoid being influenced by the Eyeball Alien.

Likewise, the Eyeball Alien had no choice but to mistake CEO Cha for someone also hiding his power.

CEO Cha seemed to have charged in recklessly, but it was all calculated action.

Having thought this far, it seemed that Black Block's reason for hosting the Masked Ball wasn't simply due to his tastes.

Because they had hidden themselves beneath masks, I too was relatively less affected by the other gods.

The Eyeball Alien's gaze moved. It was checking CEO Cha's number.

After staring at the Number Tag for a while, the Eyeball Alien lowered the hand that had been stopped by the sword sheath.

Given its aggressive disposition, I'd braced for a fight, but it seemed it knew how to act rationally after all.

All six of the Eyeball Alien's eyes turned toward me.

It adopted a fairly courteous demeanor and said something.

"■■■■."

Its attitude was as if the rude thing it had been about to do moments ago had never happened.

I couldn't understand its words this time either.

Seeing my expression of not understanding, the Eyeball Alien slowly spoke again.

"Come... with me..."

It stammered as if pronouncing an awkward foreign language, but spoke in a language I could understand.

Then it showed me its palm.

The air split open, and a large gem dropped onto its hand with a plop.

Actually, I wasn't sure if calling it a gem was correct.

Because it was a mineral of a shape I had never seen in my life.

Beneath the dazzlingly transparent surface, something that looked like a nebula was contained within.

The Eyeball Alien held it out to me.

I worried the Young god might covet the gem, but fortunately that wasn't the case.

The Young god already had the worst impression of the Eyeball Alien.

I politely declined the Eyeball Alien.

"It's fine. And I'm not the one you're looking for."

I spoke with a courteous attitude, but somehow the Eyeball Alien smiled as if dealing with a child pretending to be an adult.

It said something to me.

"■■■■■■."

I couldn't be sure, but it seemed to be something similar in meaning to not getting angry or not being scary.

It wasn't clear, but it was just input into my head with that kind of feeling.

It felt like if I heard just a little more, I could understand what it meant.

Without realizing it, I was about to focus a bit more when I felt a gaze glaring at me from the side.

It was a gaze that seemed like it would pierce through me if I got distracted even a little more here.

I carefully took a step toward CEO Cha.

Then I greeted as politely as I possibly could.

"...I'll take my leave now."

With that, I nudged CEO Cha with my elbow.

CEO Cha glanced at my hand for a moment, then wordlessly re-secured the Daeil Yeorae at his waist. And followed after me.

The Eyeball Alien didn't grab me. It only stared intently with all six eyes.

It seemed unable to recklessly grab me because of the near rule violation.

After all, the end of the hide-and-seek was supposedly 'being directly chosen by the Young god.'

Even so, I could feel a sticky gaze clinging to my back.

I could easily imagine the six eyes that would be watching me.

As I walked, a security android carelessly thrown in a corner of the corridor caught my eye.

The android, whose appearance was no different from a human's, had its internal mechanical parts fully exposed beneath the surface.

The sight of the android with its limbs cruelly torn off made me uncomfortable.

Perhaps because it reminded me of Samra.

I briefly imagined having the Eyeball Alien as a Sponsor and quietly furrowed my brow.

It was starkly contrasted with the alien I had spent the most time with.

Dominic sometimes took coercive actions toward me, but there was always a clear reason—acting for my sake.

He didn't unnecessarily use his hierarchy and power.

Perhaps even that appearance was Dominic adjusting his behavior to suit me, but...

Anyway, I felt that Dominic was far better than the Eyeball Alien.

Even after I turned the corner and the alien's figure completely disappeared, the residue of its gaze lingered unpleasantly on me.

Just as I was about to brush my shoulder with my hand for no reason.

CEO Cha grabbed my forearm and pulled me.

"...!"

I positioned myself with him behind a holographic screen.

The holographic screen that occupied one entire wall served as a mural.

After we hid behind the screen where the universe rippled, not long after, I felt the presence of aliens passing by in droves.

They all spoke in languages I couldn't understand, but I could feel that they were looking for the Young god.

Only after the presence of the aliens had ceased did CEO Cha speak.

"Wait a bit before moving. Right now, everywhere is crawling with those kinds of bastards."

At his words, spoken in a low whisper, I nodded for now.

Then I subtly raised my gaze. The CEO Cha before me felt unfamiliar.

The suit, which had metallic decorations along the lapels, was the kind aliens would wear.

Looking at the suit tailored perfectly to CEO Cha's body, I wondered where on earth he'd gotten it.

The number on the badge on his chest caught my eye.

Number 6....

It was an uncanny number.

Because the Trial &lt;Wheel of Karma>, which I had experienced with CEO Cha in the game, was structured around passing 6 stages.

I let my gaze rest on the number that seemed to evoke memories, then spoke.

"Why are you here, CEO Cha?"

"What are you doing crawling into a place like this?"

Because we spoke at the same time, a brief silence occurred.

We'd both thrown out questions, but neither asked again.

"......"

It was the first time we'd met since PTB.

We'd exchanged a few texts in the meantime, but all of it was through Mok Saehwa.

And even that was nothing but trivial matters.

As if we'd made a promise, neither of us brought up the topic of PTB.

It was the same in the phone call we had right before entering COF.

In the oddly awkward atmosphere, as I hesitated, not knowing what to do or how to act—that was when trouble erupted.

The Young god seized control of my body, not missing the moment of carelessness.

*'Hey!'*

I shouted inwardly.

The Young god lunged straight at CEO Cha, his hand reaching for the Cognitive Decline Mask.

Finding the gap between the mask and the face, he hooked his fingertips and pulled with all his might.

Since it was equipped as an item, there was no way it would come off.

I shouted in a hurried whisper as I pulled at the Cognitive Decline Mask.

"CEO Cha! This isn't what I'm doing right now... well, it is me, but!"

I'd been trying to have a serious conversation, but the Young god had thoroughly ruined it.

I'd have no excuse even if I was beaten with the Daeil Yeorae.

But for some reason, CEO Cha didn't move at all.

He left the hand that was tampering with the Cognitive Decline Mask alone.

He gazed at my eyes, which were clearly golden, then grabbed my wrist very slowly.

And as he slowly removed it, he said.

"Kang Byeokrin is here."

I, who had been struggling to push away the Young god trying to take off the Cognitive Decline Mask, was startled and asked back.

"...Kang Byeokrin is?"

The granddaughter of the Cheongang Chairman had been kidnapped into COF.

I had heard once before that Kang Byeokrin was an Adaptee.

The reason Kang Cheonseo, the Cheongang Chairman, had spent a fortune collecting Trial items was also for Kang Byeokrin's sake.

I was dumbfounded after hearing that CEO Cha had obtained the Era Agency's approval to enter in order to rescue the kidnapped Kang Byeokrin.

*'Why would Kang Byeokrin be...'*

CEO Cha said the kidnapping was the work of Sa Gongyeon.

But there was no way Sa Gongyeon would act on his own—it must have been Black Block's intention.

I couldn't begin to guess what Black Block wanted.

At least CEO Cha's reason for entering was understandable.

But everything else was a question.

Why on earth was he wearing a Number Tag?

A stringer was supposedly from the slums.

There was no way he could receive an invitation through normal gameplay, and I was curious what CEO Cha had done.

"CEO Cha's Number Tag is..."

"You answer first."

But CEO Cha cut me off.

"Why are you sticking your head into a dangerous place?"

"Lieutenant Colonel Je is being held hostage."

CEO Cha was silent. Then he asked back sharply, as if dumbfounded.

"You did all this crazy shit to actually rescue Je Hyun-oh?"

His tone was as if he couldn't believe it even as he said it.

But CEO Cha faltered right after saying it.

He added in a somewhat softened tone.

"...He's a guy who doesn't need that."

He meant that if left alone, Je Hyun-oh would find his own way out, so why bother.

I felt a slight sense of resistance.

I know best how strong my subordinate is.

But the opponent is a god, isn't it?

The photo I'd seen on Day News lingered in my mind.

Even though a black line had been drawn over the tower, my subordinate couldn't get out.

One thought came to mind at that moment.

*'Bloom...'*

The ability I'd nearly obtained by tearing out Kwak Hanmuk's eyes was said to be able to grow my subordinates.

If I used Bloom.

*Perhaps the metal hand could truly cut through everything.*

But I couldn't use Bloom, just like Resurrection.

Was it only by breaking myself down, by growing closer to being a god, that I could use Bloom?

Just as I had with Resurrection.

While I was lost in thought, the Young god finally pushed me out completely.

CEO Cha, who recognized the changed atmosphere the moment the Young god took over my body, reacted immediately with agility.

He released my wrist, which he'd been holding until then, and took a step back.

But behind the holographic screen, there was nowhere to run.

I followed him as far as he retreated. My mouth moved on its own.

"Don't you want to be my Sponsor?"

The Young god smiled as if faced with an amusing toy.

He placed his hand on the mask again. This time, CEO Cha couldn't remove it.

"If you take this off, I'll make you my Sponsor."

"......"

In the silence of the one who couldn't answer, a faint wavering could be felt.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"Don't change any further. Stop here."</span>

<br>

That was what CEO Cha had said in PTB.

But I had become even closer to a god than I was then.

A mere human, commanding a god not to change. It was truly arrogant.

As I fiddled with the mask, wondering if I should just command such a paltry item to be removed right this instant, a low voice was heard.

"Han Goyo."

The eyes beyond the mask looked straight at me. For some reason, that unwavering gaze made my interest plummet rapidly.

I wanted to leave the one who was no fun and go to my subordinates.

Just as I was about to step outside the holographic screen.

CEO Cha pulled me strongly. As I raised my eyes curiously at the tension felt in his hand.

I heard a cheerful voice.

"It's an honor to have you come!"

It was Black Block. But he wasn't alone.

A voice as elegant and pleasant as an old instrument followed.

"■■■■■■."

It was an incomprehensible language, but there was absolutely no way I couldn't know who the owner of the voice was.

Dominic was with Black Block.
