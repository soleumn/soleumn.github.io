<br>It was a proposal I’d racked my brain to come up with.

First of all, I didn’t know the location of the interstellar financial institution, nor did I have a spaceship to get there.

On top of that, although I was twenty-four on Earth, in Akasha I was treated as a child.

There was clearly no way going alone would lead to a good result.

*'They’d probably just tell me to come back with an adult.'*

Even if I somehow managed to handle business there, there was another problem.

I had no idea how alien finances worked.

Even if they scammed me into signing up for dangerous financial products, I wouldn’t know.

Besides, who knew if the aliens at the bank might take an interest in a Young god and do something strange?

From experiencing the Masquerade Ball, there were far too many insane aliens out there.

I wanted to bring someone aliens wouldn't dare mess with.

Therefore, Dominic was the most perfect adult to accompany me to the cosmic bank.

<small><small>“Little Lamb!”</small></small>

With a bit of anticipation, I waited for Dominic’s answer.

<small><small>“Little Lamb!”</small></small>

Looking down at me, Dominic still had a smiling face for some reason.

“I will do as Roasted Chestnut desires.”

“…!”

Just as I was about to quickly thank Dominic—who had so meekly accepted his guardian duties—before he could take his words back,

“Little Lamb!!”

Black Block atop my head called out to me loudly.

“What?”

“Make the offer to me as well!”

Leaping down into the air, Black Block floated right in front of me.

*POP!* A fluorescent sky-blue bowtie popped into existence on the small, squishy block.

Wearing the bowtie, Black Block puffed himself up and shouted.

“I shall be the perfect, flawless, supreme companion!”

Black Block had certainly watched me for a long time. He saw through my tastes to a frightening degree.

Suppressing the urge to touch him, I forced my eyes away.

“You stay here and help make sure the Resurrection proceeds safely.”

Until the Resurrection was finished, the golden spheres could not be removed from the domain.

Someone had to watch over them while I went to the bank.

Yet if I just told him to stay behind, he’d likely throw another huge tantrum.

He really might end up being annihilated by Dominic, so with a serious expression, I said something plausible.

“Other than you, there's no one I can trust with this favor.”

It wasn't an empty compliment, either.

After all, the safest place in Hyper Eden was wherever its master resided.

“How could this be... Little Lamb trusts me so much...”

As Black Block muttered in disbelief, I quickly added,

“Cecilia—well, I mean, don't bully the subordinate you sent the message to too much, either.”

I figured he wouldn’t recognize her name anyway, so I phrased it differently.

Black Block spun around me once and whispered,

“There is no need to worry, Little Lamb! I will act in a manner worthy of Little Lamb’s tremendous trust.”

I tried hard to remind myself once more of the man with treacherous eyes hiding inside that squishy block.

Then, before heading out to the bank, I took a final look at the golden spheres.

Seeing them resting in such tranquil stillness made my heart ache, as if it were being squeezed tight.

Everyone had fallen into a state of suspended animation to save me.

For human Han Goyo.

If not for them, I would never have reached the top of the Tower.

Stroking each sphere one by one, I checked their interiors.

*'Captain Mo...'*

Once I cured Kwak Hanmuk, I wanted to do something about the meteorite embedded in Mo Haein's stomach.

Remembering what she had told me about her younger sister, I stared at the sphere for a long time before shifting my gaze.

After confirming Je Hyun-oh's safety, I remembered that his Growth Level had been 0.

Considering that in games one usually starts at Level 1, it was bizarre.

Even growth was impossible. I would need to examine it closely as soon as I had the chance.

When my eyes reached the sphere containing CEO Cha, I blinked for a moment.

He was someone who hated gods so much, yet ended up becoming my Sponsor.

Circumstantially, he’d had no other choice... but it was still hard to believe he had readily stepped up for my sake.

For no particular reason, I pressed down firmly on CEO Cha’s sphere.

After dwelling on the words he had spoken and getting lost in thought for a while, I checked on Winter.

“…?!”

With wide eyes, I pressed my face close to the sphere.

Flustered as I looked at the person sleeping peacefully, it suddenly hit me.

*'No way.'*

The moment I realized a truth I hadn't even suspected until now, my cheeks instantly flushed red.

*'Winter was... Ms. Kang Byeokrin?'*

I hurriedly searched my memories, wondering if I had behaved properly in front of Kang Byeokrin or if I’d shown her any strange behavior.

At least it was a relief that I met her not as an unemployed bum, but as an Era Agency trainee.

Even so, the embarrassment remained.

It felt mortifying that I’d kept an appropriate distance and adopted such a businesslike attitude toward her.

The questions Kang Byeokrin had showered me with flashed through my mind.

Her urgent voice, pressing and interrogating me about whether I ate three meals a day, slept at least seven hours, or took supplements and herbal medicines.

A feeling of fullness swelled deep in my chest.

*'Then... she must have come to watch the final match too.'*

Since Winter hadn't uploaded photos of the finals, I had assumed she couldn't come in person.

Yet having heard that a Cheongang VIP had attended, she definitely must have watched my last match as Kang Byeokrin.

I was glad I’d been able to show her my victory.

Playing that match really was the right choice after all.

I slowly pulled my face away from the sphere.

I couldn't waste any more time. I needed to hurry to the bank and back.

*'I'll be right back.'*

Silently bidding farewell to the golden spheres, I looked at Kwak Hanmuk one last time.

I added one more word of parting to him.

*'Please wait just a little longer, Captain Kwak.'*

And so began my short journey to escape being declared legally incompetent.

<br>

Though I had reached the top of the Tower, COF's Clear message had not yet appeared.

It was because I had not fulfilled the final Clear condition.

Since I had a hunch about what was missing, I intended to Clear it after returning from the bank.

Leaving the Resurrection in Black Block's hands, I headed toward the interstellar financial institution with Dominic.

Surprisingly, the means of transportation Dominic chose was a spaceship.

Here I was vaguely thinking he might do something like teleportation.

The reason he opted for a physical means was entirely because of me. It was because I had not yet grown enough to traverse space-time.

While I marveled at the spaceship—a first in my life—Dominic explained this and that.

“Even among gods, those who command space-time are rare. Roasted Chestnut will be capable of it as much as you like once you grow, so there is no need to feel ashamed.”

I wasn't feeling anything like that at all.

After all, I had never even imagined the possibility that I could teleport.

I merely nodded.

According to Dominic, the spaceship provided by Black Block was a compact vessel, not very large.

He explained that he had chosen the fastest possible means of travel, and since it was set to autopilot, there was no need for any manual operation.

“I will ensure we arrive as quickly as possible without straining Roasted Chestnut's body.”

Dominic manipulated something further. For me, as a spectator, it was simply fascinating.

I thought that once I earned a lot of Star Fragments later, I wanted to buy a spaceship too.

While I was examining the spaceship with covetous eyes, a sudden, uneasy thought struck me.

“Mr. Dominic.”

“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”

“Have you ever been to a bank?”

When I stopped to think about it, the idea of Dominic personally going to a bank felt extremely strange.

It felt like the kind of mundane, ordinary behavior that just didn’t suit someone like Dominic.

He even had his own currency that he created himself.

*'Didn’t he just have his butlers take care of everything until now?'*

Hearing my question, Dominic let out a hmm and fell into thought for a moment.

“It is not a complete memory, but it has probably been quite a while.”

“About when was it?”

“It seemed to be around the time Roasted Chestnut's planet was formed.”

“…….”

I should have brought Black Block along.

I felt a very slight regret, but still, it had to be much better than coming alone.

Even if Dominic didn't know the latest financial jargon, at least the bank clerk would be polite.

“Is the reason Roasted Chestnut needs Star Fragments because of the broken subordinate?”

Worried that Dominic might scold me for spending money on useless things, I answered yes.

Fortunately, Dominic did not scold me and simply asked what item I was trying to buy.

I showed him the subordinate item I wanted.

Dominic narrowed his eyes slightly and asked.

“Have you checked how to use it, Roasted Chestnut?”

Hearing Dominic's words, I belatedly checked the usage instructions. And I ended up feeling utterly hopeless.

*'This… feels like he's going to call me Crazy Chestnut…'*

It felt like I would be tied up with prayer beads and get hit with a flick to the forehead ten times.

While I was gripped by despair, an announcement suddenly signaled that the spaceship had arrived at our destination.

<br>

*“Target coordinates reached. Docking at outer docking ring, Gate 002.”*

<br>

Following that, an arrival broadcast from the interstellar financial institution's control tower could be heard.

<br>

*“Notifying incoming ship from the control tower. This is a neutral zone. Visitors, please disarm your weapons before disembarking. We inform you that attempts at mind control directed at the staff will be immediate grounds for deportation.”*

<br>

It seemed aliens caused a lot of trouble.

I listened to the broadcast and looked out the window.

A gigantic artificial celestial body resembling a space station came into view.

From crude cargo ships to sleek streamline ships, hundreds of spaceships were tightly interlocked with the artificial celestial body's docking rings.

The spaceship carrying me and Dominic also entered the docking gate, and we were able to disembark quickly.

The bulkhead of the gate with the number 002 written on it opened.

The central lobby unfolded directly before my eyes.

Beneath a transparent reinforced plasma ceiling where space was visible, holographic electronic boards covered in various figures floated densely in the middle of the lobby.

Below them, sleek silver metal counters were arranged in a circle.

At each of the hundreds of counters sat an identical-looking alien teller with a pale face.

From the wrinkle positions of their freshly pressed silver-gray uniforms to their blink cycles and the angle of their professional smiles toward customers, everything matched perfectly.

It was a bizarre sight, resembling infinite copies of a single object placed facing mirrors.

The clerks at the counters were all repeating the exact same words.

“Today's business is now closed. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.”

And in front of the counters, aliens were making a huge fuss.

“Do you know who I am! You dare close business like this and expect to get away with it!!”

The one shouting was a giant alien whose entire body was covered in red exoskeletons.

Every time he struck the counter with a front paw like a mass of iron, a roaring sound echoed, yet not a single scratch appeared on the silver metal.

The situation was similar at the other counters.

“This is ridiculous! I traveled through three wormholes to get here! Can't you open the counter for just 5 minutes, exactly 5 minutes?”

“My waiting number was next in line!! The newly invested galaxy I funded is right on the verge of evaporating from a supernova explosion, are you going to take responsibility?!”

From a mollusk-like alien flailing dozens of tentacles in the air to a gaseous being flashing in various colors out of sheer rage, all manner of bizarre creatures clamored in protest.

Despite that, the bank clerks repeated the exact same lines with uniform harmony as if they were a single person.

“Today's business is now closed. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.”

Dazedly staring at the chaotic scene, I spotted a familiar face amidst it.

The Administrator of the Galactic Trading Post was walking out from what appeared to be a VIP area.

He was shouting at the top of his lungs at the staff member who was practically dragging him out.

“You crazy bastards! A kid's account has been frozen! You promised it would be sorted out if I came in today!! The kid is starving!!”

With his hair sticking out in every direction, he stopped mid-shout, his eyes flashing.

“To pull this kind of stunt against the Trading Post Administrator….”

His gloomy words sounded ominous. At that moment, the Administrator's gaze and mine locked directly.

“Kid…!”

The Administrator cried out, startled—and at that very moment,

Dominic, standing behind me, took a step forward.

And everyone stopped moving.

A chilling silence fell.

After a fleeting moment where even the sound of settling dust seemed audible, the first to move were the bank clerks.

There was not a single 0.1-second margin of error.

As if connected by a single massive neural network, hundreds of them stepped out to the front of their counters simultaneously, dropped to their knees, and deeply lowered their heads.

This was no mere display of professional courtesy; it was a gesture of absolute submission.

The bodies of the bank clerks, who had seemed devoid of any emotion, were trembling faintly.

Following that, the aliens filling the lobby collapsed onto the floor.

The giant red-exoskeletoned alien who had been pounding the counter with his fists until just moments ago shrank to a pathetic size.

Overwhelmed by awe and terror, the alien became smaller than a young child and crawled on the floor.

The tentacles of the gelatinous aliens lay flattened and quivering, while the gaseous alien that had been flashing with anger also dimmed its light and sank as low as possible.

The Administrator of the Galactic Trading Post was no exception. He swallowed even his scream and knelt on the spot.

He looked at me with a despairing face before hurriedly lowering his gaze to the ground.

The lobby, which had been boiling like a marketplace, was crushed by overwhelming silence.

Dominic called me in his usual calm, tranquil voice.

“Roasted Chestnut.”

Too startled and frozen, I answered belatedly.

“…Yes?”

He straightened the collar of the uniform I was wearing and gently placed a hand on my back.

Then, as if teaching a child, he spoke.

“Shall we start by getting a number tag?”
