I shouted as if I were deeply moved by the twins.

“Thank you! Since I knew I would be meeting the two of you, I brought a little human food with me in advance.”

Ella crossed her legs and sat down on the chair as she commanded.

“You have prepared quite well. Let us see what you have brought.”

Together with Kwak Hanmuk, I lined up 50 cups of Leaf Tea on the table.

The twins each drank a cup of Leaf Tea.

Ellun, who had maintained a languid expression the entire time, suddenly widened his eyes. With a shocked expression, Ellun spoke.

“It tastes bad….”

Ella also shuddered the moment she took a sip and harshly criticized it.

“Terrible! You have no talent for cooking, so choose another profession immediately. This is all garbage!”

Kwak Hanmuk did not forget to put on an act.

“Ah…! My younger brother Chestnut stayed up day and night, putting his heart into making those Leaf Teas…!”

*Thud.*

When he collapsed face-first onto the ground, Ella shouted angrily.

“Get out of here!”

Ellun quietly blinked while looking at me.

A welcome notification appeared.

This time, after seeing Kwak Hanmuk pretending to be pitiful, Ellun had added 10G as a tip.

After earning a total of 60G, I lowered my gaze and spoke sadly.

“I’m sorry…. Next time, I will definitely bring food that satisfies Ella and Ellun.”

I felt bad, but tomorrow would also be Leaf Tea.

At the beginning, when money was tight, I had no choice but to feed them only Leaf Tea.

After promising the twins that I would return tomorrow as well, I left the dungeon.

“Wow….”

Kwak Hanmuk let out an impressed exclamation and spoke with a very satisfied expression.

“As expected of you, hyung. I’m freaking rich now.”

“Make me do something like this again. This is insanely fun.”

“That was a touching performance. Roasted Chestnut, you would have done well as an actor too.”

*‘It wasn’t that impressive, Dominic.’*

After receiving overwhelming praise and bringing the play to an end, I headed toward the village general store together with Kwak Hanmuk.

It was while I was happily thinking about what to buy at the general store with my 60G.

Kwak Hanmuk narrowed his eyes.

“What’s that?”

With a bright, cheerful pyorong sound, a notification appeared.

The bandit event normally almost never happened in the very early stages.

However, on rare occasions, the event could occur after overcoming its low probability.

If you encountered bandits at the very beginning, before you could properly level up or obtain equipment, that run could be considered ruined.

But right now, I had Kwak Hanmuk.

And what did the bandits have?

*‘Money.’*

“Customers, Captain Kwak!”

Having a non-violent hobby was a good thing. Especially since he had hands suitable for gardening.

“Tip.”

There was no bandit who could refuse the tip request of such a kind employee.

Since we were judged as a five-person party, some experience was divided, but with just a few small quests completed here, the entire Special Mission Team could reach level 10 by today.

That meant we would be able to enter the dungeon starting from the second day.

Just thinking about starting ingredient gathering tomorrow night naturally brought a smile to my face.

“I’ve lived my whole life and this is the first time I’ve seen someone extort money from bandits.”

“Captain Mo. Let’s think of it as rehabilitation costs.”
