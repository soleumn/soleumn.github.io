<br>As soon as the words left my mouth, fierce opposition came from both sides.

It was an operation that neither the Young god nor CEO Cha was fond of.

However, it couldn't be helped.

In our current state, our range of movement was far too limited.

We had gone through the trouble of pulling out the Acorn Compass, but we were in a situation where we couldn't even try anything.

I scolded the Young god, asking if he couldn't even endure this much when his subordinate was waiting for him.

After half-coercing the Young god into agreeing, I explained to CEO Cha how efficient this operation was.

It involved temporarily concealing divine power within him, then revealing it far away from the banquet hall.

If the Young god's power leaked out, the gods would swarm toward it, and I planned to track down Black Block in the meantime.

Even if he fled immediately after revealing the power for just a moment, it was still a task that meant dealing with gods.

It was a difficult operation, but I felt CEO Cha could pull it off.

*'Just like during the Eyeball Alien incident.'*

I ended up looking at him with eyes full of a bit of anticipation.

And then I realized it right away.

That every time this happened, I was expecting game-like reactions from real-life beings.

However, I thought CEO Cha was partly to blame for this too.

After all, wasn't he the one who kept acting strange first?

"How do we do it?"

Just like this.

Because he showed an attitude of being willing to comply with anything I said, I inadvertently began acting like I used to.

Recalling my memories of PTB, I said.

"You just have to accept it."

It meant not to reject the power, but CEO Cha merely gave a silent nod.

At his nod telling me to go ahead and try, my eyes narrowed before I even realized it.

Without even knowing what I might do....

It seemed his distrust of humans didn't apply to me at all.

I quietly stared at CEO Cha.

The terrace seating, configured as a separate freestanding space, was not particularly spacious.

Since the light was not bright, the shadow cast over his white mask was dark.

I reached out and placed my hand over CEO Cha's chest.

My palm made contact. Beneath it, the thumping heartbeat was transmitted completely.

"......"

I felt an urge to plunge my hand in just like this and grasp his heart.

It felt like it would be fun to watch an insolent human suffer.

Fortunately, it wasn't a strong urge, so I could easily ignore it.

Although the Young god grumbled, he didn't stubbornly insist and quietly gave up, given that saving his subordinate was urgent.

I soon began pushing the power into him. Golden energy flowed in along my hand.

The moment the light touched him, he held his breath. I could tell from his body stiffening rigidly.

That all this time, CEO Cha had been suppressing his repulsion.

He had been accommodating me while holding down his feelings toward the Young god.

Yet at the exact moment the energy directly touched him, he couldn't completely hide his true feelings.

Feeling his intense repulsion, I thought at the same time:

Why does he keep denying me?

I could guess what CEO Cha was thinking.

He wanted to separate the Young god from me.

Still unable to accept me, he was looking for a way to detach the Young god.

I stared intently at CEO Cha, who was sitting amidst the rippling golden light.

If I poured more of the young god's power into him...

Wouldn't he have no choice but to acknowledge me?

“……”

I suddenly felt a gaze upon me.

Meeting the direct stare from within the white mask, I stiffened in a start.

A belated sense of incongruity washed over me.

I had been handling the young god's power as if it were the most natural thing in the world.

This wasn't even a method Dominic had taught me.

Acting as naturally as instinct, I only realized something was amiss right before I nearly caused an accident with CEO Cha.

I hurriedly scolded the young god, but his reaction was somehow strange.

Instead, he threw a tantrum, asking why I was only getting angry at him.

*‘Then was it me?’*

I warned the young god to stay quiet once again and slowly reduced the power.

The golden light vanished, and the terrace seating regained its darkness.

CEO Cha lowered his gaze for a moment following my withdrawing hand, then looked up at me.

“Why.”

The mechanical voice prodded me indifferently.

“Go on.”

I answered a bit slowly.

“……I’m done.”

Seeing him accept my power so obediently, if I tormented him just a little bit, he’d become submi—well, he wouldn't, but anyway, I added a lame excuse.

“I am perfectly fine and in my right mind.”

A short, dry chuckle came back instead of an answer.

CEO Cha rubbed the hilt of the Daeil Yeorae out of habit.

Following that, his figure vanished. He had turned Cognitive Decline up to 100%.

The unseen person spoke to me.

“You’re trying hard, Han Goyo.”

I had no idea how to respond to those casually tossed words.

Not even knowing where the opponent was, I vaguely looked up at the empty air, when the door to the terrace seating opened and closed.

CEO Cha had gone outside.

Left alone, I looked down at the banquet hall.

It was still filled with the young god's media. Interspersed among the videos, my own appearance occasionally showed up.

As if to prove that he had been watching me from a very long time ago, old footage of me wearing a player uniform was also being broadcasted.

The past me, focused on the game with a blank expression, was there.

Having won the match, I took off my headset and smiled with self-confidence.

Perhaps because of the stadium lights, I even looked as if I were faintly sparkling.

I quietly turned my eyes to a different video.

I let some time pass while watching the videos featuring the young god.

It was barely a few minutes, but perhaps due to the tension, it felt much longer than that.

I waited while looking in the direction CEO Cha had likely headed.

And exactly when the promised amount of time had passed, I revealed the power I had contained within CEO Cha.

Far away from the banquet hall, my presence flashed for a split second.

*Screeeech!*

At that moment, a sharp noise rang out from the cybernetic violin playing the symphony.

Following that, the sound cut out across the entire banquet hall.

Every single guest, dressed in luxurious attire and behaving gracefully, stopped moving in unison.

The hands tilting their glasses, the softly laid-back conversations—everything came to a halt.

Everyone turned their heads toward the same direction.

A breathless, absolute silence.

And then, I *saw* it.

As time stretched out, space melted away.

The shells that the guests had wrapped around themselves like attractive gift wrapping were stripped away.

What was revealed were forms that human common sense dared not explain.

The mask of the dignified gentleman heaved, and the skin of the face hidden beneath it split vertically and horizontally.

An esophagus embedded with layers of twisted fangs spread wide like a full-blossomed flower.

Mucous membranes spewing bluish slime, skin boiling black while emitting the scent of sulfur, dozens of legs sprouting crustacean joints, wings twisted at impossible angles, violently rippling darkness, and eyeballs!

<br>

**Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes! Eyes!**

<br>

Countless eyeballs targeted the same spot.

Anything positioned beneath that distant gaze would have been crushed to pieces.

Without leaving behind a single trace of a form, unable to even recognize the fact that they themselves had been erased from the world.

Before long, the violin performance resumed once more.

The melted space found its place again, and the nightmare-like truth faded away like a hallucination.

The banquet hall returned to its previous appearance as if nothing had happened.

The guests once again put on a noble shell.

However, a joy that could not be concealed even by masks was blanketing the banquet hall.

They moved with grotesque gestures.

It was a gait where their joints bent backward and their strides tangled together, as if they had forgotten what form the outer skin they wore took.

The sight of them tangling and rushing toward the place where the Young god's power had twinkled was surreal.

Staring blankly, I snapped back to my senses as if I had been struck by a bucket of cold water.

My heart was beating too fast. Pressing down on my chest with my hand, I swallowed dryly.

*‘Cecilia….’*

And I came to understand.

What had happened in the game when I tried to climb the Tower with Cecilia.

While playing <City of Frame>, I made a name for myself as the most famous reporter among the bottom-dwelling slum's Stringers.

Confident that only the True Ending remained, I declared that my final report would involve entering inside the Tower.

Though frightened, Cecilia could not let go of her desire to report on the Tower for the very first time.

She ultimately headed toward the Tower with me.

And upon reaching that place.

*What did I do? Ah! Someone opened the door for me? Who? Who? Who was it? Who? Who opened the door for me? Why did they make me look inside? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why?*

I hadn't even set foot inside once.

Cecilia melted the moment she looked inside the Tower.

She literally failed to preserve her form and melted away, and I barely managed to grab the remaining eyeball of Cecilia and fled.

It was a miserable defeat.

Barely surviving, I poured all the wealth I had gathered while working as a Stringer to purchase a physical body regeneration package.

In COF, if only an eye or a heart remained, even the dead could be brought back to life.

However, such an action was not permitted for the working class.

Though I wouldn't be locked away in prison or executed, a price had to be paid.

The revived Cecilia burst into tears the moment she saw me.

<br>

**“Why did you do that! Why…!”**

<br>

It was because my labor grade, which I had raised up to the S-level, had plummeted to the F-level.

The F-level meant that one was discarded waste who had failed in Hyper Eden.

It was a level where one could not engage in any activities within the city, meaning one was pushed out to the outskirts and left with nothing to do but die as trash.

Cecilia had managed with great difficulty to secure a transport ship and sent me off to another planet.

Stringer, having failed his final reporting assignment, leaves Hyper Eden vowing to someday reveal the truth of the Tower.

The game ends with the final scene of looking down at the gorgeous night view of the city from the transport ship.

It was COF's True Ending.

At the time, I thought it happened because I had entered a restricted zone.

I believed that if COF2 came out, I would be able to enter the Tower with Cecilia then.

But now, I felt like I knew.

Cecilia must have come face-to-face with a god in the Tower.

*‘She probably saw Black Block.’*

That must be why she melted away.

I traced the corners of my eyes with my hand.

The moment the gods revealed their true essence was less than a single second, a mere split second.

Even so, it was enough time to melt away, but I had endured it.

It didn't seem to be just because the young god was mixed in.

It felt like I had been able to protect myself because I had witnessed Dominic transform into a non-human form numerous times.

I examined the banquet hall from which the gods had departed.

The melted waiters were scattered sparsely across the floor like filth.

A few guests who were not gods had also pooled into puddles.

Glitches flickered like corrupted data from those gelatinous forms.

Unlike the waiters who had melted without even a trace of their form, the guests still had parts of their bodies remaining.

Security androids and other waiters entered the empty banquet hall to tidy up the interior.

The melted waiters were roughly disposed of as waste, while only those who were guests had things like eyeballs collected.

The guests would probably revive using Hyper Eden's body regeneration package.

I searched the banquet hall several times, but neither the captains nor anyone who could be presumed to be Cecilia was visible.

Fortunately, it seemed they hadn't been in the banquet hall.

While feeling a sense of relief, I also worried about CEO Cha.

Even though I knew he would handle things well on his own, I couldn't help but feel a lingering sense of apprehension.

However, going to find CEO Cha right now would only result in both of us dying.

According to the original operation, I had to track the Black Block.

Just then, a faint vibration emanated from the Acorn Compass in my hand.

“…!”

The needle was spinning.

The needle, which had been spinning frantically in circles, came to a dead stop.

I immediately tried to follow the direction pointed by the Acorn Compass.

However, as soon as I opened the door to the terrace seats, I ended up spotting an Eyeball Alien wandering around the second floor.

Unlike the other aliens who had swarmed toward CEO Cha, this Eyeball Alien was wandering around this place alone.

Out of the six eyes painted on its mask, three were currently gazing in the direction where CEO Cha would be.

The Eyeball Alien opened the terrace doors one by one. It would reach the place where I was in no time.

I needed to quickly track Black Block and find my subordinate, but….

Anyway, it was a troublesome bastard.

Young god insisted that we needed to teach that bastard a lesson, but I rejected the idea.

If I made a flashy display of power here, I would immediately draw attention, which would be like jumping into a trap of my own accord.

Instead, I decided to use the secret passage in the terrace seating.

It was a waste disposal duct that was no longer in use.

When I lifted the metal finish plate embedded at the bottom of the service console in the corner, a passage barely large enough for a person to crawl through appeared.

I squeezed my body into the duct. As soon as I closed the metal finish plate, the door to the terrace seating opened.

I stopped moving right as I was about to crawl into the duct.

*Thud, thud, thud….*

The footsteps walking inside were heavy.

*Clank*, the area right above my head vibrated finely. Of all places, he had stepped right on the metal plate.

I even held my breath.

The Eyeball Alien stood still like that for a while.

While he stood there for a long time as if gauging my location, light of an unpleasant color flowed in through the gap in the metal plate.

When the light brushed against my cheek, Young god struggled, but I held him down firmly.

While I waited without reacting, a noise was heard from the direction of the banquet hall.

The Eyeball Alien turned its steps in response to the sound.

Only after I heard the footsteps heading toward the banquet hall did I begin to crawl through the duct.

Scattered here and there in the narrow, stuffy duct were old wastes.

Most of them looked like android parts.

Crawling bit by bit, I checked the direction of the compass.

After getting stuck in the duct and moving forward for a while, I listened closely to the surrounding presence.

Confirming that no one was around, I slipped out of the duct.

Pushing the metal plate aside and climbing up, a place I had never seen before—not the terrace seating—lay before my eyes.

*‘Black Block is here?’*

I had thought he would be with Dominic in the VIP zone.

It seemed like it would be the wrong answer, but I tried saying the name I knew anyway.

“Mad Messia?”

Beep, beep, a system window popped up along with an alert sound.

Wait, there’s only one attempt left?

Isn’t it basic to give you about three tries?

Flustered by the prompt abruptly declaring my final chance, I hurriedly tried to deduce the answer.

The Black Block knew that I was trying to interview him.

It probably meant he would only allow me access if I could figure out something like his name.

I fell into deep thought.

Hyper Eden, the city of provocative media. And Black Block, the owner of that place.

The strange spelling of Mad Messiah.

There had to be a clear reason why the final h was missing.

As I stayed in Hyper Eden, I learned more about him and also came to understand.

The moment I snatched up the floating thoughts and gathered them together.

The spelling of Mad Messiah reassembled inside my head.

I muttered the name of the Black Block as if possessed.

“Mass media.”
