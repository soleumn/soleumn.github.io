<br>I had mentioned financial education, and he was truly teaching me right from step one.

I never dreamed I would have to take a number tag even in a cosmic bank.

When I nodded blankly, the plasma ceiling sparkled, and a beam of light scanned me.

A translucent, three-dimensional holographic interface unfolded right before my eyes.

<br>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
  <strong>Hello. This is the Customer Reception System.<br>
Please state your business.</strong>
</div>

<br>

As I read the dry text, the hologram suddenly flickered with static, and the letters began rearranging themselves on their own.

<br>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
  <strong>Customer Young god! Please tell us what you'd like to do!</strong>
</div>

<br>

Child mode had been applied even at the bank.

And by this point, I felt like I understood why all the employees had closed for business.

*'It was because of Dominic.'*

Black Block had probably notified the interstellar financial institution of Dominic's visit in advance.

So they were in the middle of closing up shop to welcome Dominic, but we had arrived too early.

Because Dominic had tinkered with the spaceship, we had arrived far ahead of the estimated time.

I had known all along that Dominic would receive VIP treatment.

That was part of why I accompanied him in the first place.

But I never expected such a massive incident to unfold.

Struck by the natural disaster known as Dominic, the bank tellers and aliens were trembling so hard it was almost pitiful.

That wasn't all. I truly never imagined I would run into Administrator here.

I felt like a juvenile delinquent caught red-handed doing something bad.

The Galactic Trading Post's Administrator had previously warned me about the butlers' master.

He had advised me to be wary of Old gods, especially the oldest god.

Judging by what he shouted earlier, he had even come to the bank on my account, only for me to walk in alongside Dominic.

I kept recalling the look of despair the Administrator made the moment he saw me.

Though I had dealt with various aliens, Dominic was still relatively communicative and reasonable in his own way.

Of course, that was setting aside the fact that he killed and resurrected me nine times in a row, painfully rummaged inside me with his hand whenever he got bored, or pushed me off the top of the Tower to crush every bone in my body....

"......"

Come to think of it, maybe he wasn't reasonable at all.

Either way, it was a situation beyond my control.

Before the countless bank employees and aliens, I stated my business in a small voice.

"Unfreezing my account...?"

With a *DING*, new text appeared on the hologram before me.

<br>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)); border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; text-align: center; margin: 20px 0;">
  <strong>Please follow the staff!</strong>
</div>

<br>

The kneeling bank employees rose in unison.

Smiling brightly, they made the exact same gesture. The creepy part was that not a single one made even the faintest sound.

With beaming faces, they pointed toward the interior of the bank without a word.

As if they had lost their vocal cords.

Hesitating, I first looked up at Dominic. He explained kindly.

"From now on, no matter which financial institution in Akasha you visit, you will never have to wait."

It meant that unless an Old god personally came visiting in an extremely exceptional case, I had an unconditional free pass.

Then, he waited for me to take the lead.

Burdened by the pressure, I stepped forward. As I slowly walked ahead, I soon arrived before the Administrator.

It was the moment my pace unconsciously slowed down.

"That is not necessary right now, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic's usual leisurely voice drifted over. At the same time, the Administrator's crystal hand pressed against the floor twitched violently.

Dominic had once remarked that it might be useful to get to know the Administrator.

So I wondered if he might treat the Administrator somewhat like a person, but as expected, that wasn't the case.

Lingering here any longer wouldn’t do the Administrator any good.

After all, the Administrator's very presence might displease Dominic.

I simply quickened my pace.

*'I'm sorry, Administrator. I'll contact you later.'*

I offered an unspoken apology to the back of the Administrator’s head.

Since I planned to contact him to ask if he could check my contract with Black Block, my silent apology was doubly heartfelt.

Suddenly, another thought crossed my mind.

Previously, Dominic had encountered the Administrator while in his Pudding Slime form.

But at that time, he hadn't revealed his true self.

It had been the same at the Masquerade Ball. He had instructed Black Block not to announce his visit.

But his attitude now was completely different.

He had zero hesitation in appearing before aliens.

Wondering what had caused Dominic to change his mind, I entered the VIP room.

Rather than a room, the VIP room was closer to a massive spaceship cabin.

The surrounding walls, ceiling, and even the floor were made of perfectly transparent reinforced plasma, giving the sensation of floating right in the middle of vast outer space.

Beneath our feet, a deep blue nebula swirled and drifted slowly, and when I turned my eyes, countless stars sparkled like jewels.

In the center of this magnificent and fantastical space, a sofa floating gently in midair and an elegant crystal table were set.

I sat down on the sofa, which looked plush despite its unknown material.

Dominic naturally took a seat beside me as well.

Since the sofa adjusted its size on its own to accommodate him, he didn't look uncomfortable.

On a golden tray placed upon the table, transparent star-shaped jellies, rainbow-colored candies, and luminous treats resembling fluffy cotton candy were neatly arranged.

It was an overly adorable assortment for a 24-year-old adult man, but it certainly looked appetizing.

Amidst it all, I spotted a familiar candy.

When I picked up a piece of Supernova Pang Pang, the bank tellers watching from the side subtly lowered their shoulders in apparent relief.

*'What's this?'*

Reading the room, as soon as I tried to put the candy back down on the plate, the tellers stiffened again as if tensing up.

Hesitating, I popped the candy into my mouth.

Rolling the popping candy around inside my mouth, I glanced at them, and only then did everyone seem to relax.

To think they would be nervous not just around Dominic, but around me as well.

Perhaps some kind of instruction had come down from above.

Was the owner of the financial institution an Old god too?

To own something on this scale, they couldn't be an ordinary being.

Just then, the bank teller standing at the very front cautiously stepped forward and spoke.

"I understand you have visited regarding the unfreezing of your account. I am the general manager here."

He looked identical to the other employees, but a ringed planet badge was pinned to his chest.

A translucent holographic document window floated up before him, who appeared to be the branch manager.

"We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your identity verification was already completed during the scan in the lobby. Without any need for additional paperwork or explanatory procedures, we will unfreeze it immediately."

Strictly speaking, it was undeniably my fault.

After all, ten billion Star Fragments had suddenly landed in a brand-new account.

Yet the branch manager apologized repeatedly, as if everything were entirely their own fault.

The branch manager placed his hand on the hologram in midair.

An aurora-like light shimmered over his pale hand, and the holographic letters shifted rapidly.

Along with a cheerful notification chime, the familiar System Window popped up in my vision as well.

<br>

{% include system.html text="You can now freely use your account.<br>Current balance: 10,000,000,000 Star Fragments" %}

<br>

The moment I saw those ten neatly printed zeros, I was on the verge of tears from sheer joy.

All I had to do was get an advance from Black Block to cover whatever money I lacked and buy the items.

However, cold water was poured right over my hopeful future.

"You must not receive payment in advance."

Dominic stepped in to object.

"Why can't I…?"

I looked at him while making the most pitiful expression I could muster.

Then, Dominic answered in a strict tone.

"Because Roasted Chestnut refused the shedding."

Since I had chosen an incomplete physical body that hadn't gone through shedding, I was taking continuous damage.

I didn't particularly feel any pain, so I hadn't worried about it for the time being, but Dominic hadn't missed that detail.

It seemed he intended to use all the eyeballs and hearts of the guests attending the Masquerade Ball—which Black Block had prepared as a gift for me—to repair and maintain my physical body.

The plan was to distribute the revenue from the Young God's channel by mixing it with Star Fragments; however, if I were to receive the Star Fragments first, it would complicate everything.

I had been right in the middle of listening to the explanation. At that moment, the Young god, who had been quietly watching from inside me until then, suddenly threw a tantrum.

He demanded that I receive the eyeballs Dominic had promised to give me if I behaved well during treatment.

*'Why on earth would he want something like that?'*

One moment he was crying because Dominic was scary, and now that he felt he had some breathing room, he was lying down and whining for eyeballs. It was baffling.

When I remained silent, the Young god came out to the surface and insisted that he would speak for himself.

I ignored the Young god.

*'To think something like this is a part of me.'*

It reached a point where I couldn’t even call him a social misfit anymore.

As I inwardly lamented this unbelievable reality, Dominic offered a remark that piqued my interest.

"Instead, I will give Roasted Chestnut a small allowance."

A small allowance? What is the standard for small?

A hopeful feeling welled up reflexively, but instead of eagerly accepting it, I put what I had learned earlier into practice.

"But I haven't even formed a sponsorship contract yet, so if I receive help…."

Dominic curved the corners of his lips slightly.

At that crafted, motionless smile, a faint chill inexplicably crept over me.

He explained to me in a neat tone.

"You have judged well. If you do not pay a fair price, a debt piles up in the soul of a god."

He seemed to ponder how to explain it in a language I could understand, then brought out an appropriate expression.

"Would you understand if I called it… karma?"

*'Karma?'*

It meant that receiving help without giving anything in return would eventually turn into a debt and come back to haunt me.

"Karma moves in both directions."

It meant it piled up not just on me, who received the help, but also on the one providing it.

Dominic said he could take on my share of karma up to a certain point, but since this was ultimately an act of receiving help, it would become dangerous the moment it crossed a certain line.

"If you make a single mistake, another karma could form and affect Roasted Chestnut's divine status."

After hearing the explanation that it was even more dangerous because Roasted Chestnut was still young, I finally understood completely.

That must have been why he said it was difficult to help carelessly without a sponsorship contract.

The reason Black Block couldn't give me Star Fragments and nutritional supplements in advance must also be connected to this very issue.

Too much advance payment could be perceived as unearned assistance.

Where on earth was I supposed to come up with the lacking Star Fragments then?

I was in a rush because I needed to use the item on Kwak Hanmuk right away. At that moment, a low chuckle was heard.

"When I said I would give you an allowance, it means I will take on the karma. Only enough so that it does not cross the line, and thus causes no harm to Roasted Chestnut."

He gave a light wave of his hand.

At the same time, a broken System Window appeared before my eyes.

<br>

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong><span class="glitch-static">The Exalted One! The Exalted One! The Exalted Onnnnnne has given to the Young god!</span></strong>
  </div>
</div>

<br>

"It has been a while since I last did this, so the presentation looks rather poor."

Dominic's words didn't properly register in my ears.

It was because the numbers written on the deposit notification window that popped up right after were shooting up like crazy.

The numbers that seemed to be pouring down finally stopped, and I hurriedly counted the number of zeros.

*'Ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand.'*

The destination of the endlessly climbing zeros was...

One hundred billion....

The kindest alien in the universe and the absolute best adult asked me gently.

"Is this enough for a day's allowance, Roasted Chestnut?"

<br>

Having become a billionaire of a hundred and ten billion Star Fragments, I boarded the spaceship triumphantly.

Right before boarding the spaceship, the bank manager cautiously handed me a transparent card.

In the exact center of the card, the pattern of a planet with a ring was embedded.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"The Master requests to meet with you next time."</span>

<br>

The owner who possessed the interstellar financial institution wanted to see me.

Dominic had encouraged me, saying it was fine to accept the card.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"There is no harm in knowing. I will introduce him to Roasted Chestnut next time."</span>

<br>

I put the mysterious card into my inventory and boarded the spaceship.

The trip back was alone.

Dominic had said he had some business to attend to briefly, and that he would follow right after taking care of it.

During the solo spaceship ride back, I immediately purchased the item for Kwak Hanmuk.

Once I cleared COF and got out, I would explain things to Kwak Hanmuk—meaning I would explain very thoroughly that I was not in my Crazy Chestnut state and that this treatment was absolutely necessary—before equipping the item.

As soon as I returned to Hyper Eden, I checked the golden spheres first.

Resurrection was complete.

However, I didn't wake the sleeping ones right away.

Instead, I asked Black Block to call Cecilia.

It took a little while to explain what kind of being Cecilia was, but I successfully brought her before me.

Only, Cecilia wasn't alone. She stood before me alongside Sa Gongyeon.

"You called for us."

After looking up at the sisters who were paying strict respects, I reached my hand out to Cecilia.

"Noona. Lend me a camera."

"I will prepare one right away…."

"It has to be that exact camera."

Cecilia couldn't say anything for a moment, but soon quietly took out a camera.

Sa Gongyeon revealed a puzzled look.

Black Block, still maintaining its small and cute appearance, circled around me with curiosity.

"What is this, Little Lamb!"

I stroked the old camera.

Every Archive Game I had played was reality.

If I hadn't accepted Young god.

I probably wouldn't have been able to handle the truth and would have gone slightly crazy.

Because even now... the more I thought about it, the more I felt my sanity shaking slightly.

There were still far too many unresolved questions.

What were the people from South Korea I met in the Archive Game?

How were my playthroughs back then remembered by the NPCs as endings?

The joy that NPCs were real beings whom I could meet again was dimmed only by the guilt of the things I had done to see bad endings.

"……."

The Young god inside me grumbled, telling me not to think strange thoughts.

Listening to the absurd claim that everyone would like me brought a slight smile to my face.

In any case, what I had to do was clear.

*‘Take responsibility for my play. And…’*

Find the Creator.

In the end, the Creator was the beginning of everything.

Tightly gripping the camera, I soon gestured to Black Block floating in the air.

“Come here.”

Though puzzled, he came closer for my sake.

*CLICK.*

I took a photo of the Master of Hyper Eden.

“Little Lamb… Could it be that you want my photo card?”

Black Block babbled nonsense, but I ignored him and held out the camera to Cecilia.

Cecilia, who had been staring blankly at the camera, took it with trembling hands.

I looked at her and spoke.

“I’m really late, aren’t I?”

I handed the greatest mystery of Hyper Eden into her hands.

Captured with the camera I had held on that day long ago when we promised to climb the Tower together.

“I finally kept my promise, Noona.”

I had come to understand the Master of Hyper Eden, and I had climbed to the top of the Tower.

However, one final task still remained.

It was to broadcast the story I covered as a stringer to the media.

The greatest scoop I could offer was captured in the camera.

“If it’s all right with you… I’d like to keep in touch with you.”

I gauged her reaction and continued speaking.

“Since Hyper Eden is a place that exists somewhere in Akasha, I thought that even after I Clear this and leave, I could still keep in touch with you, and maybe see each other in person from time to time….”

It took a bit of courage to say the final words.

“Can I just contact you as Roasted Chestnut?”

Cecilia bit her lower lip hard.

In the end, she couldn't hold back.

Cecilia embraced me tightly.

In that moment, caring neither about her master nor her sisters, she spoke only to me.

“You will always be my Castañita.”

At the same time, the System Window popped up.
