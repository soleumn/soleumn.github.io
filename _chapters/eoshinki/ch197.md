An Old god who held the reins of cosmic journalism.

It was something I had pretty much expected.

After all, it didn't seem like Black Block would let me leave Hyper Eden in a mere 80 seconds.

Cecilia flinched in surprise and let go of me. Only then did she glance warily at Black Block.

Right before letting go, however, she quickly slipped something into my hand.

Without even looking, I shoved it straight into my inventory.

Black Block stepped up close to me.

“Little Lamb!”

As if unconcerned with the item I had received from Cecilia, he simply bounced his squishy body.

“As an excellent Stringer, Little Lamb has explored every nook and cranny of Hyper Eden! But there are still very many places you haven't been to.”

We had even climbed all the way to the top of the Tower, yet there was still stuff I hadn't seen?

I had even completed every single Stringer quest without leaving a single one out.

I lent an ear to Black Block's words.

At that, Black Block awakened me to a fact I had forgotten.

“You haven't covered the adult topics yet!”

That... was true.

It was something I had only learned after entering the trial this time around.

Dominic had chosen the adult category, but because he vanished right after, I hadn't even gotten to see what the quest's content was.

“Aren't you curious about the forbidden zones, too? Now Little Lamb has a small territory in the Tower. That means you've practically obtained all authority over Hyper Eden.”

He openly seduced me, saying I could examine Hyper Eden inside and out.

He leisurely waved his hand through the air.

All sorts of provocative media generated along Black Block's movements were thrust right in front of my nose.

They were videos that looked like they could make you lose your mind and trap you for hours even if you only spared a fleeting moment of distraction.

“You can satisfy your newfound curiosity and then return to Little Lamb's planet!”

After laying out all sorts of flashing, interesting videos, Black Block slid closer and clung squishily to my cheek.

Then he whispered secretly.

“Little Lamb, my star! Let's spend a pleasant time together....”

I liked the sensation. Should I say it felt a bit chewier than a Pudding Slime?

As expected, I felt like I needed to knead it with my hands to know for sure.

Anyway, for him to say something like this the moment Dominic stepped away.

It made me think he was truly on the lookout for any chance to stay by my side.

While Black Block's proposal was tempting, I had a lot of things to do.

There were trials piling up that needed to be closed due to Epic Quests, and above all else, I was worried about Samra outside.

During my time in COF, I had tried contacting him a few more times with the hologram badge, but each time the connection failed.

I pulled Black Block off my cheek and said.

“Sorry. I have to go back.”

Clinging to my hand, Black Block let out a sobbing sound, *SOB, SOB, SOB!*

When he was three meters tall, it was just absurd, but seeing him do that as a tiny square made me quite concerned.

When I inevitably maintained a firm attitude, Black Block detached himself from me.

“Understood, Little Lamb....”

He flew toward the golden spheres and took a spot between CEO Cha and Kang Byeokrin.

“I will take these ones with me. Guest No. 6 must also be given a truly remarkable prize!”

It was surprising that Black Block recognized CEO Cha.

Understandably so, since he couldn't even memorize his own subordinates and constantly went around saying, *'Who was it again!'*, I hadn't expected him to remember a human like CEO Cha as a guest.

Though CEO Cha had won first place in finding the Young god at the Masquerade Ball, I had assumed Black Block would just pretend not to know and gobble up the prize....

With unexpected things overlapping, Black Block felt suspicious.

Reading my gaze, Black Block hopped up and down atop a golden sphere as if wronged.

“Little Lamb! To suspect and distrust me to this extent! Truly, the problem was that I didn't make ears for myself....”

He whined that he would attach small, triangular ears to his head right now.

I warned him absolutely not to do that. That appearance seemed way too dangerous for me.

Anyway, he was Black Block's guest, and CEO Cha had to receive his prize, too.

It seemed right to trust Black Block here.

*'If he wanted to torment me, he would have already tried when I went to the bank.'*

I casually asked something I was curious about.

“By the way, what's the prize?”

“It concerns Young god!”

It was an obvious answer, considering it was a gathering of those who wanted to become sponsors of the Young god.

I pressed Black Block, who was stating the obvious, a bit further.

“I mean, what is it specifically?”

“Young god is Little Lamb!”

Uttering sheer nonsense, Black Block let out a strange chuckle—*heheh, heheheh*—and hid behind a golden sphere.

The world melted away in brilliant colors.

“Little Lamb, my star! I'll contact you before long.”

I hurriedly scanned my surroundings, but nothing was visible.

Amidst the collapsing world, only a booming voice echoed everywhere.

“I'm looking forward to it! To all the fun things that will soon take place on Little Lamb's planet…!”

And with that, I emerged outside the Trial.

The unfamiliar woman, who was still unconscious and passed out, was dressed in a gown.

Her face looked familiar for some reason.

I was soon able to recall where I had seen her.

*'That's the person who was playing the violin.'*

She was the violin player I had seen at the banquet hall of the Masquerade Ball.

Along with a sense of gladness that she was also an Adaptee who had been trapped in COF, a sense of doubt arose.

*'Why didn't she melt?'*

There had been a moment when the gods gathered in the banquet hall revealed their true essences.

Even if she had somehow endured it since it was a split second, she shouldn't have been in her right mind….

“…….”

The woman slowly opened her eyes.

Pitch-black pupils, contrasting with her pale face, were revealed.

Eyes so dark that it was hard to distinguish the pupils from the irises stared straight at me.

The woman smiled gently, raised her upper body, and—
Crawled toward me….

She approached slowly, her red lips moving.

“Is this the True Ending Clear?”

I knew there were plenty of oddballs among the Adaptees, but her behavior was certainly startling.

Still, acting as a representative of the Era Agency, I answered faithfully.

“Yes, it's the True Ending.”

Hearing my voice, the woman's smile deepened. Right as her lips were about to part again—

A black combat boot stamped hard onto the floor.

Je Hyun-oh, letting his metal hand hang loosely, had stepped right between the woman and me.

Je Hyun-oh looked down at the woman.

The prone woman slowly tilted her chin back to face Je Hyun-oh.

The gaze of them staring blankly at each other grew prolonged.

She seemed a bit mentally unhinged, but she was a valuable Adaptee nonetheless.

Just as I was about to stop her so she wouldn't get sliced up by Je Hyun-oh's metal hand—

—Mr. Goyo.

A holographic screen floated up before me.

I widened my eyes and cried out happily.

“Samra!”

—Mission complete. Congratulations on your return.

Normally, upon clearing a Trial, I would be the one contacting Samra through my earpiece.

It seemed to be the first time he had contacted me first and even pulled up a screen like this.

“I was worried.”

I was about to ask why I hadn't been able to reach him through the hologram badge all this time, but I pushed that thought aside for now.

It wasn't really a story to tell in front of a freelance Adaptee.

Yet, for some reason, Samra gave no reply. Ordinarily, he would have congratulated me on the clear and even worn a slight smile.

Samra's gaze shifted next to me.

—Lieutenant Je Hyun-oh. Cease any actions threatening a civilian Adaptee.

Yet Je Hyun-oh didn't even twitch.

He simply kept his footing and tilted his head slightly to the side.

A terribly raspy bass voice spat out a short retort.

“Civilian?”

Samra on the holographic screen didn't show any emotional change. He merely answered a little slowly.

—…Yes. A civilian Adaptee. Verification is complete, so cease your threatening behavior in accordance with regulations.

Je Hyun-oh turned his head toward Samra.

After staring for a moment, he slowly shifted his foot away.

Before long, he turned one hand into a raven glove and roughly grabbed me by the scruff, hoisting me to my feet.

Following that, Era Agency soldiers, who must have been waiting nearby, entered the special soundproof room.

Je Hyun-oh left the soundproof room with his grip still fastened to the back of my neck.

Being dragged along by him, I looked back.

I made eye contact with the woman. She mouthed words with her lips.

See you again.

I didn't answer to the silently delivered words.
