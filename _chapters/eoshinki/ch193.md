'*Don't worry. I'm going to pull it off.*'

I slowly opened my eyes.

The Tower, which had become sickeningly familiar, was visible.

However, I wasn't lying on the floor with my entire body crushed.

I was cleanly healed and standing upright on my own two legs.

Still in a human body.

I had accepted the Young god, but I felt it would be hard to endure if I shed my skin and even my body changed.

This was my best option right now.

Exhaling a small breath, I checked my status.

My restored body was wearing the Era Agency uniform.

When I was in the cooking studio, the outfit had changed to a chef's uniform on its own; when I fell down the stairs, it had changed to a banquet suit.

Right now, however, it was an outfit I had created for myself.

I slowly raised my head and looked up at the giant eye occupying the sky.

The gaze watching me was still heavy enough to be cruel.

Even so, I didn't feel any terror or dread that I would melt away.

I leaped toward an opponent I could never defeat.

I didn't climb the stairs.

There was no need to.

A long golden trajectory was drawn across the dark space.

Flying toward the uppermost floor of the Tower, I looked back at the stairs I had climbed.

And the large hologram screens set up like barriers everywhere.

They were things that could no longer exert any influence over me.

I arrived at the top of the Tower.

Landing on the uppermost floor, I gazed at the giant eye.

The blue eye I met up close was... smiling.

At that moment, I *understood*.

I knew because I had accepted the Young god.

The overwhelmingly unilateral and violent actions poured onto me when I was human.

All of this was the *god's affection.*

"...!"

Simultaneously with this realization, Dominic *peered* into me.

It was nothing more than a very brief, light gaze.

Yet before my eyes unfolded a hollow chaos where all form, time, and meaning had completely vanished.

A mass of information too difficult to endure poured out from the chaos where nothing existed.

Dizziness swept over me, and I rapidly lost my sense of balance.

Unable to endure, I plummeted back to the ground.

Yet I didn't even know I was falling.

"Hah, gasp...!"

Tumbling across the floor of the Tower, I lay face down and hurriedly gasped for breath.

Thanks to wrapping my body in gold, I thankfully wasn't hurt too badly.

However, strange thoughts kept trying to occupy my head, such as *'It is wrong to go against His will!'*

Bloody tears were flowing from my eyes.

I wiped away the bloody tears with the back of my hand.

The Young god inside me was frightened. Telling myself to pull it together once more, I made a calm judgment.

I was certain of it from the act just now.

Dominic had no intention of genuinely attacking me.

Having experienced something similar to a degree at CallOVo, it felt even clearer.

If there had been even a shred of such intent, my existence would have already vanished.

Right now, Dominic was waiting for me to give up.

I staggered to my feet and immediately flew toward the sky once more.

The same thing repeated. I continuously challenged him, yet every single time I was plummeted to the ground by Dominic.

My body wasn't hurt much, but my mind had long since been in a state of overload.

The voice of the Young god whimpering inside me was also gradually losing strength.

The blood flowing from my eyes no longer stopped. Era Agency's blue uniform had long since been dyed deep with blood.

I was still far too lacking.

The mere act of flying to the uppermost floor of the Tower could not satisfy what Dominic wanted.

'*What else am I supposed to do here?*'

Gasping for breath, I briefly looked at my palms. After confirming my name written in bright blue, I clenched my teeth again.

I wasn't alone now.

Just as I was about to face Dominic once more,

Fluorescent sky-blue sparks cracked, and two women blocked my path.

Sa Gongyeon, and... Cecilia.

"Little Lamb! Congratulations on your blooming. From now on, we shall attend to Little Lamb. We have prepared delicious food, warm bathwater, and comfortable bedding. Please let us know if there is anything you desire."

Sa Gongyeon smiled sweetly and reached out her hand politely, as if offering an escort.

"Would you like to take a rest with us?"

Cecilia neither smiled nor spoke. Yet she waited with her hand outstretched to me as well.

'*Double M sent them.*'

Since I kept charging at him dozens of times without giving up, he must have thought he needed to calm me down.

Seeing the underhanded move of sending Sa Gongyeon and Cecilia, a hollow laugh escaped me.

I looked at Cecilia's hand.

Through any moment, I had grabbed the hand she extended without hesitation.

Because both Cecilia and I held the belief that we would hold onto each other.

Right now, however, I couldn't take her hand.

I straightened my body alone. And I said to Cecilia.

"I'm sorry, Noona."

A faint crack appeared on her expressionless face.

Watching the slight agitation briefly revealed, I continued speaking.

"I was too weak and made things hard for Noona."

It was entirely my fault.

If I hadn't been a young and immature god, Cecilia wouldn't have ended up like this.

"I'll try doing it alone now."

As I passed them, Sa Gongyeon startled and tried to grab me.

"Little Lamb!"

I stared at Sa Gongyeon with gold-oozing eyes.

No commands were necessary. I merely looked at her like a pathetic, insignificant creature.

Sa Gongyeon's complexion instantly stiffened pale.

She hastily bowed her head and fell to her knees before me.

I swallowed a sigh inwardly.

I had chosen an easy way to make Sa Gongyeon submit, but I still hated this sort of thing.

I firmly grabbed the forearm of Cecilia, who was about to kneel after her.

“Noona, don't.”

Held by me, Cecilia looked at me. I could feel her trembling gaze, but I didn't look back at her.

Feeling that I might have held her a bit too hard, I carefully let go and stepped forward.

Taking slow steps one by one, I called out to the entity gloomily watching me from somewhere in Tower.

“Double M.”

Double M was not a fan in the sense that I knew.

Because to me, a fan was someone like Winter.

However, seeing Double M send subordinates to persuade me, I clearly realized it.

What his desire toward me truly was.

As I climbed the stairs, I also saw Double M's memories.

Double M had watched me for a very long time.

He had methodically collected my anguish, pain, despair, and all my misfortunes in Tower.

Yet, it was not only misfortunes that he gathered.

He had also recorded in Tower every single moment I rejoiced, had fun, and tasted victory.

Examining my entire life without leaving out a single detail, from Young god to human Han Goyo, and still watching me even now while calling himself a fan...

What such an entity wished of me.

“In the end, you just want me to be happy, don't you?”

At that moment, Tower shook with a heavy *BOOM*.

From the already cracked stairs, a dangerously loud *CRACK* resounded as they split even further.

I continued calmly.

“Even if I'm troubled, struggling, suffering, and despairing...”

I *understood* Double M a little more.

“In the very end, you want me to smile.”

Though a bit twisted, I was certain that Double M also ultimately wished for my happiness.

“Are you going to keep acting like this until the end?”

*BOOM!* Tower shook violently once more. I spoke to master of Tower.

“No matter what you do, I won't give up.”

The moment I finished speaking, the stairs began to collapse.

Every fragment of the stairs turned into small pieces of media: shredded newspapers, snippets of radio broadcasts, clips of TV news.

In an instant, all of Tower was filled with things about me.

Amidst them, I waited for Double M's answer.

Then suddenly, someone grabbed my hand.

“…?”

At the desperate and earnest touch, I looked back.

Cecilia was panting heavily for breath.

“M-Master sent a message...”

Holding my hand, she stammered.

“He said... Little Lamb has accepted himself... So things you couldn't do before... would now be possible...”

I stared at her with rigid eyes.

I had only hoped Double M wouldn't interfere. I never expected him to help me by sending a message like this.

Double M was a powerful Outer god, but he was beneath Dominic after all.

Going against his will was by no means a light matter.

Right now, Double M had helped me, prepared to pay a massive price.

Cecilia, his subordinate, was likewise trembling terribly because she knew the gravity of what he had done.

Yet even while steeped in fear and dread, she had delivered Double M's message to me in the end.

It wasn't out of loyalty to her master.

This was... for my sake.

Cecilia burst into tears. Clinging tightly to my hand, she spoke.

“You must not go.”

Without realizing it, I let out a soft smile.

I knew how foolish and reckless I must look to her right now.

Still, I was glad Cecilia was worrying about me.

Holding Cecilia's hand in return, I answered.

“Noona. You always said it.”

“Little Lamb...”

“That if it's me, I can reach the top of Tower.”

Then, slowly floating into the air, I said goodbye.

“I'll be back.”

“Roasted Chestnut!!”

Letting go of Cecilia's hand, I soared into the sky once more.

Having heard Double M's words, I felt like I knew what to do.

*'Protection.'*

I used Protection on myself.

It was an ability I had only ever been able to use on subordinates.

Yet now, Golden Veil of protection draped over me as well.

Had I put a bit too much power into it? A veil far larger than what I usually created formed.

Behind me as I surged toward the sky, a long veil trailed like the tail of a meteor shower.

The sweeping edges of the sprawling veil gently brushed past the media fragments filling Tower.

The fragments began to transform into radiant light.

The voices that had mocked my fall and struck my ears turned into passionate cheers, echoing through the empty air.

Wherever the Golden Veil touched, the altered fragments of media poured down like stars.

Wrapped in those sparkling moments, I soared toward the highest peak.

And I reached the giant eye.

The blue eye gazed into me, but now, wearing the Golden Veil of favor, I could endure it.

Having held on without falling, I called out to the entity before me.

“Mr. Dominic.”

Dominic did not answer. He merely watched me quietly.

Within that gaze, I opened my mouth. 

“Thank you… for helping me all this time.”

It was something I never would have said, even if the universe had split in two, if I hadn't understood him.

Looking into his eyes, which rippled like a nebula mixed with mysterious colors, I continued.

“However, I don't think I can become the god Mr. Dominic wants.”

His pupils shone as faintly and distantly as the universe. A dizzying chill rose across my skin.

Yet, I did not stop.

“From now on, I intend to walk the path I desire. If Mr. Dominic finds that difficult to accept, and therefore wishes to forcefully stop me once again.”

Clenching the hand inscribed with blue text into a tight fist, I gambled on a very slim probability.

“Annihilate me right here and now.”
