<br>The pixelated character's long hair swayed gently. The deep green, high-slit dress, which revealed her thigh, also fluttered slightly with her movements.

Mo Haein stood frozen, staring at the woman opposite her.

*'If only I'd had a moment to even slightly calm down...'* But before I could even catch my breath, Round 1 began.

I gritted my teeth and tried to control Mo Haein.

But no matter how frantically I mashed the buttons and moved the joystick, she wouldn't budge.

My heart sank.

I stared at the screen in desperation.

The opponent was just a created character.

Because her younger sister was still asleep inside the meteorite.

Even knowing the truth, Mo Haein seemed to be struggling to keep her composure.

It was nearly impossible to think rationally while watching her own sister move right in front of her.

While Mo Haein stood frozen, an attack came immediately.

A straight-out leg strike hit her squarely, and with a sound effect, Mo Haein was sent flying backward.

The one-sided assault continued.

The controls still wouldn't respond.

I could only watch helplessly as Mo Haein, on the screen, failed to even raise her guard and took the hits.

The younger sister's attack skills were identical to Mo Haein's.

Even her ultimate move was the same, as if they were the exact same character.

Mo Haein was being defeated in the very same way she had subdued ■ in the previous match.

Her health bar vanished in the blink of an eye, and Mo Haein collapsed powerlessly to the ground.

And she couldn't get up.

Round 1 was over before I could even react.

The victorious younger sister character spun around in a circle and flashed a V-sign to the crowd. A wave of enthusiastic cheers erupted for her.

In stark contrast, a chilling atmosphere settled around Mo Haein.

It had been a boring match—she'd just taken hit after hit without even trying to dodge.

Round 2 began shortly after, but Mo Haein's condition remained the same.

Once again, she was utterly dominated.

If she lost Round 2... she would be trapped in the game forever.

And that wasn't all.

The crowd might jeer at her helpless defeat. The Giant Hand had appeared in response to the audience's boos before.

This time, Mo Haein would be crushed into a bloody pulp.

*'No.'*

I mashed the button repeatedly, desperate to force her to move.

The same notification kept flashing on the screen.

"..."

I gripped the joystick tight, staring at the monitor.

Her body, thrown into the air, was struck repeatedly before being slammed back onto the ground.

Even knowing she was heading toward a miserable end, Mo Haein remained motionless.

I knew her younger sister was important to her. I knew she was a precious being she would give her life to protect.

But to me, Mo Haein came first.

I could not tolerate her sacrificing herself for her sister. *I could not accept my subordinate valuing someone else over me.*

Besides, this wasn't even real.

Before I knew it, my eyes had changed color, but I felt no urge to suppress the change.

*'How can I make Mo Haein snap out of this?'*

Neither my Golden Veil nor my messages were working. I needed another way.

*My subordinate coughed up crimson blood. I couldn't stand to see her like this any longer.*

I moved my hand from the control panel to the screen. Beneath my palm, I felt the hard texture of glass and a surge of heat.

The golden light streaming from my eyes flickered.

Slowly, I reached into the screen.

My hand appeared within the display—a hand so colossal it seemed poised to cover the entire arena, like a white-gloved giant. I barely restrained the urge to *crush everything to dust*.

Instead, I gently caressed the sky.

So that Mo Haein would wake up.

I wanted her to feel that this entire world was a virtual simulation.

With a wave of my hand, the bright daylight sky was stained with the colors of sunset. The crimson glow blanketed everything, but it wasn't enough.

I swept my hand across the sky once more. This time, night fell instantly.

The sudden darkness plunged the stadium into a panic. The crowd trembled in terror.

I checked on Mo Haein. Her face was frozen as she stared up at the sky.

*I should show her something beautiful, too.*

For her sake, I conjured countless stars in the night sky and sent them cascading down. A golden meteor shower embroidered the heavens—a truly magnificent sight.

I watched the display with satisfaction, and once Mo Haein had seen enough, I restored the sky to its original state.

The bright daylight returned to the stadium.

I smiled the moment I saw the notification.

*Good girl.*

She truly was my subordinate. A little help was all she needed to overcome it.

Before withdrawing my hand, I extended my index finger and pointed at the crowd.

Then, I slowly swept it over to point at Mo Haein.

The gesture was clear: 'Cheer for her properly, or I'll crush you all.'

The crowd, frozen in a silence so profound they dared not even breathe, erupted into a forced cheer.

I lowered my hand from the screen and placed it back on the control panel.

Mo Haein, who had been staring at the sky, slowly lowered her head to look forward.

The situation remained dire.

Though she had finally regained her senses, Mo Haein's health bar was completely empty.

The remaining health bar was so thin it was barely visible. A single graze would mean death.

Mo Haein took a deep breath, then let it out. She clenched her fists and fell into a fighting stance.

Though she was already battered and broken, she refused to give up.

Even knowing that despair was far closer, she clung to hope.

And her god had not given up either.

She had learned something from her earlier match against ■.

When the opponent attacked, some damage would still seep through even if she blocked.

In her current state, with only a single pixel of health remaining, even a blocked attack would kill her.

But there was a way to reduce the damage to zero.

If she pushed the joystick forward exactly as the opponent attacked, she could perform a "Blocking" that completely nullified the damage.

The only problem was the timing—she had to react within approximately 0.25 seconds, making it incredibly difficult to pull off.

Still, it was a fight worth taking on.

The younger sister character unleashed a multi-hit special move. A fifteen-strike kick combo surged forward, raining down at dozens of frames per second.

I had to successfully block fifteen times in a row.

I pushed the joystick with perfect timing. Every time Mo Haein's arm blocked a kick, a flash of light erupted on the screen.

*Thud, thud, thud…* and finally, the fifteenth.

I jumped and blocked the final aerial strike. Then, I immediately went on the offensive.

I continued my aerial assault, staying suspended in the air.

I unleashed every combo I had, including my ultimate move.

Every single strike landed with pinpoint accuracy.

The younger sister's full health bar vanished in an instant.

Mo Haein's fist slammed into her solar plexus, launching her body into the air.

And when she crashed back down to the ground—

Mo Haein was declared the victor.

The crowd, which had been breathless and silent, erupted in a deafening roar.

It was a thrilling comeback victory.

Yet, amidst the frenzied cheers that shook the entire arena, Mo Haein remained calm.

She stared at her younger sister lying collapsed before her for a moment, then looked up at the sky.

<br>

From that moment on, Mo Haein feared nothing. She continued to win without hesitation.

She won the third round, eliminating her younger sister's character.

Fortunately, her sister's character was not crushed to death by the giant hand.

However, in subsequent matches, Mo Haein's overwhelming perfect victories led to several opponents being jeered by the crowd and dying as bloody pulps.

Of course, it was more important to me that my *precious subordinate didn't take a single hit*.

That's why I chose to keep winning perfectly, turning our opponents into bloody pulps.

The ten matches were over.

As I knocked down the final opponent, pixel fireworks burst across the screen, and a cheerful electronic fanfare rang out.

After the "Clear!" message, my score appeared on the screen.

I'd set a new high score.

The other players' scores were listed below mine.

The gap between my score and the one right below me was huge. The second-place score started with a 6, so it seemed unlikely my record would ever be overturned.

The Pudding Slime, who had been watching the game from above my head, exclaimed in pure admiration.

"Roasted Chestnut... you're truly amazing."

I thanked him for the compliment on my incredible skill and stood up from the chair.

*Clatter.*

A neon blue coin rolled out of the coin return slot.

I picked up the coin.

The front of the coin featured a pixel art character of Mo Haein's face, and the back was inscribed with the word "CLEAR."

*'I'll just collect all the Clear Coins first, then visit the Exchange Center,'* I decided.

I began searching for Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk among the arcade machines.

With so many arcade machines, I couldn't find them as easily as I had when looking for Dominic or Mo Haein.

After wandering among the machines for a while, a racing cabinet caught my eye.

Equipped with a heavy steering wheel and foot pedals, it was the most cutting-edge machine in this arcade.

I approached the racing cabinet and checked the screen.

The scenery on the screen was made of pixel art.

Usually, a machine of this caliber would at least implement 3D graphics with low polygons.

As I stared intently at the game screen, my eyes widened. The Pudding Slime also recognized him.

"Roasted Chestnut's red-haired subordinate is over there."

I remarked that my subordinate was putting quite a bit of effort into the act, adding that I had spotted him successfully.

I immediately sat down in the racing cabinet's seat.

Inside the screen, a rough wilderness unfolded.

In front of the line of rally cars on the off-road track, Kwak Hanmuk's pixelated character was moving back and forth.

He seemed to be looking for a way out.

I gripped the steering wheel and turned it gently. As I did, Kwak Hanmuk's character moved to the left.

A question mark appeared above his head.

A speech bubble appeared above his head, containing a pixelated image of a chestnut.

I smiled, my hands still gripping the steering wheel.

*'How could my subordinates be so kind and obedient?'*

I wanted to clear this game in one go and get Kwak Hanmuk out of here as soon as possible.

The racing game was a bit unusual. It was a best-of-five match, but instead of facing multiple cars, I had to race against a single opponent.

Unlike Kwak Hanmuk, who wore a frayed shirt and torn jeans, the opposing driver was fully geared up in proper racing attire. He even had a helmet tucked under his arm and a car that looked incredibly high-end.

*'This is so unfair,'* I thought.

I was dissatisfied that the rally cars I could choose from looked so much older and more worn-out than the opponent's.* I wanted my subordinate to drive a cool car too.*

Part of me wanted to steal the other driver's car.

I frowned, then quickly shook it off and turned the steering wheel, moving Kwak Hanmuk.

Every time I stopped in front of a rally car, a description of that vehicle appeared.

I was carefully inspecting the vehicles.

When I stopped in front of a worn, rugged rally car with a boxy hatchback, two exclamation marks popped up over Kwak Hanmuk's head.

Though it looked ordinary on the outside, it was a vehicle equipped with a high-output engine and four-wheel drive.

*'This looks good.'*

The driving difficulty would be that much higher, but its exceptional acceleration made it perfect for racing.

I selected the car Kwak Hanmuk wanted.

Just as the notification appeared confirming the selection was complete—

The door of the building, which had been partially visible at the edge of the screen, slammed open.

Mafia in black suits poured out of the makeshift shack, a structure hastily thrown together with wooden planks.

Every one of them was armed with a gun.

The last ones out were dragging people bound in rope.

The moment the racing opponent saw the woman and two children being hauled out, he screamed.

They were likely his family, but he was forced to shut his mouth immediately. A mafia member had pressed a gun to the head of the weeping woman.

Seeing this, I understood the price of defeat.

The lives of the hostages.

The moment we lost the race, holes would be blown through their heads.

Then came Kwak Hanmuk's hostages...

They were two men and one woman.

The pixel art was too crude to be certain, but they seemed to be wearing blue uniforms similar to those of the Era Agency.

A speech bubble containing the curse word 'FXXK' floated above Kwak Hanmuk's head.

But unlike the opposing player, he remained calm.

Just as he was relieved thinking he only needed to focus on the racing, the final hostage was dragged out.

It was a young child.

A small boy, bound with rope, stumbled forward, barely able to walk.

He looked at Kwak Hanmuk with eyes wide with terror.

Kwak Hanmuk dashed forward almost simultaneously.

He ran frantically toward the child, but the Mafia immediately blocked his path.

I forced the agitated Kwak Hanmuk back to calm him down.

Initially resisting, Kwak Hanmuk stumbled backward as the muzzle of a gun was pointed at the child's head.

I couldn't understand who the boy was to make Kwak Hanmuk react so violently.
