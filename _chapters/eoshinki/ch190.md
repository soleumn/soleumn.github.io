<br>The chat window screen slowly blurred before disappearing completely.

I stared into the empty space.

I waited and waited, but no new chat window appeared.

The last sentence I saw felt burned into my mind, impossible to forget.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">[Winter: You are my god]</span>

<br>

Why would they call me a god?

I am not a young god.

Winter… what is that?

The moment I tried to think of the word 'Winter', my mind went completely blank.

It was as if someone had cleanly excised just that part.

I muttered softly to myself.

“Winter….”

At that moment, like a flash of light, a vivid memory appeared.

She had said it was a fan name chosen from my name and nickname.

She told me that because of me, her favorite season had changed.

How could I have forgotten?

It made no sense. To have forgotten Winter.

I felt it anew: my condition was not normal.

*‘Could the person waiting for me at the top be Winter?’*

Wondering why she would be in a place like this, the messages Winter had sent me came rushing back.

She had said she hadn't forgotten me. And that even now… she was waiting.

I yanked my body up to a standing position.

“…!”

A stabbing pain forced me to grab my leg and sit right back down.

I was in no physical condition to climb stairs.

Yet I had to go up.

I wanted to make sure Winter was safe, to confirm if she was truly waiting for me at the top.

If she was trapped in this place, I had to pull Winter out.

I carefully climbed one step at a time.

My heart wanted to run up, but I couldn't do that.

There were still endless stairs left to climb.

I paid close attention so I wouldn't misstep and worsen my leg injury.

I had to pass the holographic screen that had once made me decide to give up, but this time I managed to get past it.

As I limped upward bit by bit, a strange chat window appeared.

At first, I rejoiced thinking it was Winter's chat window, but it wasn't.

It was filled only with unfamiliar names and incomprehensible content.

Even after reading through the chat window carefully, I couldn't make sense of it.

On top of that, screens as small as flying bugs kept clinging to it, blocking the chat window.

To check the ongoing chat, I had to look at the small screens as well.

Since they only contained things that were no good to look at, I decided to focus solely on climbing the stairs for now.

At this rate, even if I climbed all day, I wouldn't reach the top of the Tower.

As fatigue made my steps gradually slower, the small screens got in my way even more annoyingly.

However, these no longer had a major impact on me.

Thinking that Winter was waiting made me feel like I would be fine.

When I ignored them steadfastly and pushed forward, a large holographic screen newly appeared.

It wasn't a particularly special scene.

It was just a memory of my 20th birthday, the day I became an adult.

It was also the day the club, having watched my rehabilitation up to that point, finally issued a notice of contract termination.

After receiving the call, with nothing to do, I just stayed lying down in my room and slept all day.

After watching myself sleep like a corpse on the bed, I walked past it without caring.

Though I wobbled a bit, I didn't tumble or fall down.

I felt like I had climbed quite a bit, but I still had a long way to go.

My mind kept going numb.

Fearing I might forget even Winter, whom I had managed to recall, I muttered out loud.

“Winter, Winter….”

Then, someone's voice brushed past me.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">“What are you going to do if you forget ■■ too, huh?”</span>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">“You shouldn't forget things like that.”</span>

<br>

Whose words were those?

I couldn't remember what I had been told not to forget.

It was a time when I had been anxious all by myself, wanting to remember.

When I stopped being agitated, the screens chasing me began to tremble finely.

*BZZT, BZZT*, as if causing glitches on the surface, the small screens suddenly merged into one.

Then they became a screen larger than ever before, washing over me like a wave.

In an instant, I entered the memories within the screen.

The ensuing scene was familiar.

'I' stared blankly at the screen after turning on the computer, then flopped down onto the bed.

The moment I saw that sight, I felt like I knew.

*‘Ah, it's the day I started playing the Archive Game.’*

For two years after my retirement, I had played all sorts of games.

On that day, when I was feeling a bit bored from having played almost all decent games, I encountered the Archive Game for the first time.

Because of the Archive Game, I was able to pass the time without being bored.

I thought I would soon sit back in front of the computer and download the Archive Game, but….

'My' movements were strange.

Instead of going to the computer, out of the blue, I headed for the living room.

It was definitely 'me', but moments unknown to me were unfolding.

'I' stood in front of the living room's display cabinet and made a phone call to someone.

The other person didn't pick up.

Even so, 'I' kept calling several times before finally giving up and putting the phone down.

For me, it was nothing short of puzzling.

That was because not even once had I ever reached out to anyone first since my retirement.

It was around the time I was dismissing it as a fabricated illusion.

'I' reached a hand out toward the living room display cabinet. I carefully clutched the cloth covering the top of the cabinet.

My hand flinched several times, but in the end, I couldn't pull the cloth away.

Just like the phone call, I had given up.

'I' stood in front of the display cabinet like a stone statue before staggering toward the kitchen.

Repeating the action of opening and closing drawers, I searched for something.

‘I’ finally got what I wanted.

I stared in bewilderment at 'me'.

Because 'I' sat down with my back to the display cabinet, and—

A moderately low, calm yet powerful voice spoke to me as if giving an order.

<br>

*“Han Goyo. Don’t look.”*

<br>

I couldn't see anything, but I smelled a fishy, damp scent.

Strangely, it felt intensely familiar.

The scene that had unfolded before my eyes was clearly a memory that didn't exist.

Even so, the smell felt so vivid, as if I had actually experienced it.

I wanted to check on 'myself,' but I couldn't.

Only the man blocking my sight looked at everything.

After stopping me from seeing, he himself seemed to struggle looking at the sight before him.

I could feel anguish from the hand touching me.

I wanted to ask what he had seen, but his hand pulled me along and sent me up the stairs.

Before I knew it, I was on the other side of the screen.

As I tried to look back, a small chat window screen appeared.

Once again, someone grew quiet.

I stared blankly at the disappearing chat screen.

*'Why on earth are they worried about me?'*

Even while suffering, this person named Yuhae was thinking entirely of me.

Their emotions were conveyed so vividly that it was almost overwhelming.

I began moving my feet again.

If I go up, I will probably be able to meet that person too.

I wanted to know why this person is waiting for me. And ask why they were worried about me.

Perhaps, like Winter, they were someone very important to me whom I had completely forgotten.

I moved forward, repeating the written names together in my mind.

“Winter, Yuhae, Winter, Yuhae...”

I didn't think I was that bad at remembering things, but right now, it felt like I would forget everything the moment I looked away.

The speed at which I climbed the stairs slowed down a bit more.

I was practically dragging my legs as I went up.

The cameras tenaciously filmed me like that.

I felt a bit embarrassed wondering if Winter might have seen me like this.

Because I only wanted to show cool sides of myself to Winter.

*'Have I come about a third of the way?'*

Glancing down, the floor was dizzyingly far below. But there were still many more stairs left to climb.

Just then, the small screens that had merged into a giant screen and disappeared began to reappear one by one.

Feeling a little nervous, I braced myself.

It was right when I thought it would just show old memories again.

The newly appeared screens were not memories of the past.

It was Young god.

Young god, with glittering golden eyes, scattered light across the world.

Descending at every desperate, critical moment, Young god possessed the power to save everyone.

What others needed was Young god.

Not human Han Goyo.

Since it was a fact I was well aware of, I wasn't particularly hurt by it anew.

I was just a little worried about whether it was all right for me to be so stubborn.

*'If I had to choose between the two.'*

Then, naturally.

“...”

*'Still, Winter must be waiting for me.'*

Yuhae... seemed to be waiting for me too.

Wondering if this was selfish behavior, I slowly climbed the stairs and came to a stop.

And I looked at the large screen blocking my way.

In the screen, Young god granted Resurrection to everyone. Reviving the dead and breathing new life into them.

Following the moment of miracles, a scene showing Young god delivering punishment appeared.

Toward the giant eye in the sky, golden light shot out like stars.

With the help of a man as pitch-black as a crow, Young god flew across the sky.

My gaze was completely captivated by that sight.

Looking at the brilliant light created by Young god, my confidence to move forward waned even more.

My feet felt heavy, as if someone had tied lumps of lead to my legs.

It was when I was bound helplessly before the screen, unable to do this or that.

Another screen appeared over the screen undulating entirely with golden light.

What was displayed on the screen covering Young god was my studio apartment.

Just as I was feeling a bit flustered since it wasn't particularly interesting scenery—

A black line was drawn silently across the veranda's glass door.

Then, along the line, the plate glass was severed and plummeted to the floor.

*CRASH!* The glass shattered with a clamorous sound. Black combat boots trampled over the shards with a *CRUNCH!*

The one who entered the studio apartment along with the moonlight was the pitch-black man who had been with Young god.

My eyes widened.

I couldn't understand why that man was in my studio apartment.

The man, pitch-black from head to toe, clacked his metallic hand as if out of habit—*CLACK, CLACK!*—and stood in front of the display cabinet in the living room.

Then, turning one hand into a glove, he pulled the cloth.

The cloth covering the display cabinet slid down, *SWOOSH!*

Standing in front of the cabinet, the man tilted his head slightly to the side.

Opening the cabinet as he pleased, he took out the items inside one by one and looked at them.

My heart sank at the sight of him carelessly handling the gifts received from fans.

I watched the man with bated breath.

Just as he had smashed the glass, it seemed as if he would tear the gifts to shreds at any moment.

However, the man merely examined all the gifts filling the cabinet.

His attentive gaze was quite serious, even.

The man's next movement after finishing looking at the cabinet was even stranger.

The man lightly kicked the trash can with his combat boot, *THUD!*

As the trash can fell over, an unexpected object rolled out.

It was the case containing the championship ring.

The man crouched down and picked up the case.

Opening the lid, he closely observed the ring inside.

Watching him hold the ring up to the moonlight, I was swept by a strange sensation.

This time, too, the man didn't damage the ring.

I thought he wouldn't care at all about things belonging to human Han Goyo.

Even if he felt a fleeting interest, I assumed that would be all, and he would naturally destroy it.

Yet the man didn't destroy the fans' gifts, nor the championship ring.

He simply slipped the ring, case and all, into his chest.

The man walked back to the veranda, unfolded his wings, *SWISH!*, and leapt into the darkness.

The video ended, and the screen disappeared.

Young god's screen appeared before my eyes again, but I couldn't even pay attention to it.
