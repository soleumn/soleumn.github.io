A red glow erupted from the Flame Dragon’s body after it swallowed the twins.

It was the light signaling the recovery of its health.

The regenerated scales covered the Flame Dragon like a sturdy armor, then soon changed into a dark crimson color.

At the same time, thick spikes began growing all over its body with cracking sounds.

Spikes also grew between the horns on its head, forming a shape resembling a crown.

A dark crimson energy flowed down from its once-bright red eyes.

The Flame Dragon fully recovered its health and entered a berserk state.

It was power gained by killing its own kin.

*“Hahahaha! Little one!”*

The Flame Dragon burst into laughter and looked down at me with eyes shimmering with madness.

Despite the considerable distance between us, the Flame Dragon’s eyes captured me perfectly.

*“Don’t you want to see my underlings?”*

The Flame Dragon revealed countless teeth as it laughed.

*“Meet them inside my stomach.”*

I stared blankly at the Flame Dragon.

Strangely, I wasn’t angry. My mind was endlessly cold, and I was in an unusually calm state.

*Only my eyes were unbearably hot….*

The heat surging through my eyes made my head throb.

But I took my first step more calmly than ever before.

I ran again.

Je Hyun-oh and Captain Kwak were retreating while putting away their weapons.

They were trying to assess the battle situation after the Flame Dragon’s sudden recovery.

The berserk Flame Dragon roared, shaking the space itself.

When the Flame Dragon spread its wings and flapped them once, a powerful gust of wind erupted and pushed my body backward.

Following the wind, flames spread across the entire floor. There were no longer even any footholds left to avoid them.

A system window appeared.

*‘Divine Protection.’*

I kept running.

*“How foolish! Your end has already been decided.”*

His words clearly revealed the confidence that something like this would never be able to restrain him.

Kwak Hanmuk, sweating profusely from the intense heat, burst into laughter and cursed roughly.

“Bullshit, fuck…!”

Mo Haein, holding the Black Moon Blade, leapt up nimbly.

Instead of running across the floor filled with lava and flames, she ran along the long-stretched rosary beads.

She moved without any shaking, as if she were running on flat ground despite standing on thin strands made of rosary beads.

After running close to the Flame Dragon, Mo Haein jumped down.

It looked as if she had jumped into boiling lava, but the flames had no effect on her wrapped in Divine Protection.

Mo Haein swung the Black Moon Blade toward its lower abdomen.

It was the area where the scales were the thinnest, making it a vital point.

Clang! The scales crumpled under the Black Moon Blade, then soon became ragged and fell away.

The Flame Dragon tried to twist its body as if annoyed, but it couldn’t move at all because it was bound by the rosary beads.
