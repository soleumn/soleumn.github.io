<br>Since I'd gone and declared that I would open a personal broadcast and choose a Sponsor, I'd expected some kind of reaction from Black Block.

I'd braced myself for something fairly unhinged, but I never imagined he'd pull something on this scale.

I couldn't believe Black Block had gathered 666 Sponsor candidates from across Akasha.

I wanted to proudly say that I could simply refuse to choose, but the presence radiating from the guests gathered in the ballroom made that impossible.

It wasn't certain since they were concealing themselves, but I could sense beings that truly made one think, "This is a god."

Beings capable of forcing a choice even if I refused.

Even the Young god was watching with keen curiosity.

Just what kind of being was Black Block?

Despite being more obsessed with a contract with me than anyone else, he'd publicly held a charity event.

Gathering guests of this caliber felt like a statement that he was confident he'd be chosen.

"Dominic..."

I couldn't know whether Dominic would let this slide.

If, by some chance, I actually ended up signing a Sponsor contract...

"......"

A faint chill crept eerily down my spine.

Even the Young god, who until a moment ago had been picking out candidates to squeeze Star Fragments from while muttering that the author would serve him well, fell silent.

The Young god had always been afraid of Dominic, but ever since he'd been forcibly merged with me inside that eyeball last time, he'd been even more frozen.

Unlike my complicated feelings, Black Block continued speaking cheerfully.

<br>

*"To the guest who first finds the Young god, I shall present a truly magnificent prize. Namely..."*

<br>

The rest was lost to me.

I strained to hear Black Block's voice.

But all I got was noise mixed with exaggerated laughter and applause.

I looked at Cecilia to see if she'd caught it, but she gave a small shake of her head.

Whatever Black Block had said, a wave of murmuring spread through the ballroom.

I couldn't be sure, but it seemed to be a prize more than enough to pique the guests interest.

<br>

*"And a simple rule! You must not peek inside!"*

<br>

A voice warped in warning rattled my brain.

I nearly stumbled back but barely held my ground.

The Young god threw a tantrum, demanding how I could be shaken by something so trivial.

When he tried to push me aside, claiming he'd handle it, I told him to be quiet.

Black Block also mentioned he'd applied a bit of his power throughout the ballroom to keep the Young god from being found too easily.

It felt like Cognitive Decline.

Well, thinking about it, even with a mask, it'd be easy for other aliens to find me.

My pupils alone were golden, after all.

Black Block shouted his final remarks.

<br>

*"Let us all enjoy a night full of joy, ecstasy, and delight!"*

<br>

As his greeting ended, a voice came from the screen.

<br>

*"Who wants to be my Sponsor?"*

<br>

The words the Young god had spoken echoed through the ballroom.

The screen vanished, and the lights came back on.

Soft classical music once again flowed through the ballroom.

Laughter and conversation mixed with the music here and there.

I surveyed the guests one by one.

He'd held a Young god hide-and-seek as part of the Sponsor selection, but Black Block himself didn't seem to be here.

Only the masked guests were enjoying the special event.

It was certainly an appropriate amusement for a charity event.

It's just that the prize on the line was me...

The number of guests gathered in the ballroom wasn't exactly 666.

Because some, like Cecilia with number 666, had brought partners.

Also, Black Block had given me a small advantage.

He'd said the Young god was among the 666 guests, but I wasn't a guest.

I had no number tag because I'd attended as Cecilia's partner.

But who would complain to Black Block about a minor discrepancy?

Those in power have no obligation to tell the truth.

Anyway, since attention would be focused on those wearing number tags, I had a bit of a loophole to slip through.

"Roasted Chestnut."

Cecilia whispered my name and offered her hand.

I took her hand.

The only option available to us now was to act naturally.

Like guests at a ball.

As with any ordinary charity event, this was a social gathering where people exchanged conversation.

But the more I talked, the more disadvantaged I became.

Then again, if I hid away without saying a word, I'd stand out even more.

I had to stay in the background and somehow endure until the event ended.

I crossed the ballroom with Cecilia, surveying the interior.

I'd been to the Central District ballroom before, when I'd infiltrated as a cleaner or a cook in the game.

But this was the first time I'd looked around the inside of the ballroom as a guest.

The ballroom was a strange space blending classical grandeur with cutting-edge cyberpunk.

Marble pillars, gold-gilded moldings, carved ceilings, and massive chandeliers all matched the Western-style palaces I'd seen before.

But look closely, and every element of the ballroom felt futuristic.

Between the crystals of the golden chandeliers hanging in tiers from the ceiling were embedded microscopic optical components.

The crystals sparkled as if stars had been plucked from the night sky and affixed to them, and each time light rippled across their surfaces, the digital frescoes on the ceiling changed.

Right now they were showing the cosmos.

The ornaments blending gold and nano-coated metal and the pillars etched with intricate circuits were unique, but the most beautiful thing was the black marble floor.

The faintly gleaming black marble floor shifted its light in real time, following the guests crossing it.

It looked like walking across the Milky Way.

My domain would look so pretty if I decorated it like this... but Earth didn't have this level of technology yet.

I turned my gaze elsewhere.

I spotted a woman playing a cybernetic violin made of titanium.

I watched the soloist's elegant fingerwork for a moment, then turned back to Cecilia.

Cecilia, standing with her back to the dancing aliens, gave a faint smile and asked.

"Do you know how?"

I could only give a rather feeble answer.

"No..."

I'd done it plenty in the game, but this was my first time in reality.

Actually, even in the game, all I'd done was press a button like "Dance," and the character would move on its own.

Social dancing was especially unfamiliar, so I'd probably be even more clumsy.

But if I learned now, maybe I could teach my subordinates later.

*'I could throw a party in Akasha too, inviting tons of guests.'*

I was imagining a party to show off my subordinates when I barely shoved the Young god back down.

After telling the Young god to behave during the dance, I said casually to Cecilia.

"Teach me, noona."

Cecilia let out a small laugh.

"Alright."

When I placed my hand over hers, Cecilia gently wrapped it in her grasp.

Because of our height difference, I ended up looking up at her slightly.

Cecilia bent down to meet my eye level.

"Relax your shoulders. Just follow my feet."

Her other hand gently rested on my shoulder and guided me.

I stepped along with her. With each step, ripples of light spread across the marble floor like waves.

When Cecilia turned gracefully, the hem of her dress scattered a soft glow.

"How is it? Not hard, right?"

"That's because you're leading the whole way. I'm worried I'll step on your feet."

"It's fine, Roasted Chestnut. If you make a mistake, I'll just scoop you up."

Cecilia whispered with a playful glint in her eyes.

I stifled a laugh at her claim that she'd lift me right up.

Dancing to the melody among aliens felt a little strange.

Because even though this world was the far future compared to Earth, the way people behaved was so similar.

As I glanced at the dancing aliens, Cecilia suddenly asked me.

"Are you doing okay these days?"

"...Yeah."

I answered first, then thought for a moment.

Whether I really was okay.

But the more I mulled it over, I really was okay.

I liked things as they were now.

Cecilia said teasingly.

"My Castañita has grown up now. You used to cry about not wanting to be alone."

"No, I didn't cry..."

Cecilia laughed merrily again.

I remembered the days I'd spent at her place, staying together.

Because Cecilia had been by my side every single moment, I'd been able to return to my usual self quickly.

Even now, thinking back on that time, all I felt toward Cecilia was gratitude.

I'd always liked NPCs, but after meeting Cecilia, I'd come to give them even more of my heart.

Because I'd learned that NPCs could truly care for me.

That was why I loved Archive games.

Not just because they were fun, but because the vivid NPCs that felt alive were the greatest appeal.

Because they didn't seem like mere pieces of data...

"Roasted Chestnut."

Cecilia turned and subtly shielded me from someone's gaze.

"Can you get the interview?"

"I have to."

Tonight's charity event was, for me, a game of hide-and-seek to find Black Block.

Originally, the quest to interview the Mad Messiah was designed to be impossible.

There was no way into the Tower, and no way to even know if he was inside it.

But now he'd come out of the Tower himself.

I'd even been put up as a party prize, so Black Block ought to pay a price of his own.

I was determined to succeed at the interview I'd never been able to pull off in the game.

Black Block had no publicly known appearance.

My plan was to capture at least a part of his body and sell it as an exclusive scoop.

If the media outlets of Hyper Eden wouldn't take the article, I had other methods in mind.

*'Anyway, since he invited guests, Black Block should be somewhere in the ballroom.'*

I looked up at the second floor.

Sections of the corridor running along the outer wall of the second floor were partitioned into private terrace seats.

Holographic curtains hung over them, so the inside couldn't be seen from outside, but those within could look down over the ballroom.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein, who'd infiltrated as waiters, also planned to check the second-floor terrace seats first.

Radios would've been nice, but they risked being caught by security scans, so we planned to rely on messages as much as possible.

The music ended, and those who'd been dancing exchanged greetings.

I was exchanging greetings with Cecilia and stepping back when—

"Ohh, Cecilia!"

A very short alien came bustling over.

An alien whose entire body was made of gemstones approached with a wine glass in hand, acting familiar.

"Long time no see! Still blessed with a dazzling refractive beauty!"

Cecilia smiled at the compliment phrased in terms of optical beauty.

Cecilia stepped forward, subtly blocking me from his view, and replied.

"Good to know my efforts tonight paid off."

"Cecilia is always beautiful. By the way, what is that adorable little organism you have beside you?"

My first thought was that he was the adorable one.

The gemstone alien was even shorter than Mo Haein.

"He's my partner."

Cecilia answered smoothly and took my hand.

She pressed a smacking kiss to the back of my hand and said.

"Darling, would you bring me a glass of champagne?"

The gemstone alien also wore a number tag. It was a signal that I'd better slip away before he took more interest in me.

Besides, it was about time for me to start moving for the interview anyway.

I answered Cecilia like a devoted lover.

"How could I refuse when you ask so sweetly?"

Cecilia beamed and sent me a wink wishing me luck.

I escaped the gemstone alien's lingering gaze and stepped out of the hall.

The Central District ballroom was a massive building with hundreds of rooms in addition to the party hall.

They'd prepared spaces where invited guests could stay freely.

I headed to another floor, pretending to go take a short rest.

There was a meeting spot where the Captains, who'd infiltrated ahead, were supposed to leave notes if they found clues.

They must have seen everything that just happened, so I sent a quick message in case they were worried.

I was mentally smacking the Young god, who was whining about wanting to be with my subordinates, when—

"...?"

I bumped into something.

I'd been distracted sending the message, but the corridor was long and completely open.

Even though I could see everything ahead, I hadn't felt the slightest presence.

I looked around belatedly, but there was nothing to see.

As I wondered what had happened, a loud noise came from behind.

Along with a scream came the crash of something toppling over.

I quickly realized what was going on.

A towering alien was walking down the corridor, tearing apart a security android like a toy.

Machine parts dropped behind its steps like splatters of blood.

I watched it with a hint of tension.

The alien, wearing what looked like a space uniform with a soft sheen, had a mask covering its entire face.

Six eyes rendered in neon circuits adorned the mask, each moving in a different direction as they ceaselessly searched for something.

The moment I saw the number tag on its collar, I immediately stepped aside.

I lowered my gaze, pretending not to dare look at the alien noble.

Fortunately, the eyeball alien passed me by.

I glanced at its retreating back, deciding to wait a little longer before moving—

*Thud thud thud thud thud!*

The eyeball alien, having sprinted back at incredible speed, stopped right in front of me.

Before I could even draw a breath, it bent low and thrust its face into mine.

All six pupils stared at me at once.

"■■■■■■■?"

At that grotesque voice, my body froze on its own. I couldn't understand a single word it said.

The alien tilted its head very slowly from side to side.

It tossed the broken android it'd been holding to some random spot in the corridor.

Then it pressed its face all over me, as if sniffing me.

I was just staring wide-eyed when—

The alien placed its hand on my stomach.

The instant that hand, large enough to cover my whole body, touched me, I knew.

It was going to reach inside me and look within.

Just as the Young god, enraged, was about to burst out—

Three of the six pupils shifted to look to the side. I turned my head as well.

Standing there was a man wearing a white Cognitive Decline mask.

A white scabbard roughly struck the alien's hand away.

A mechanical voice spoke in a commanding tone.

"Get your hands off."

It was CEO Cha.

And on his suit...

A Sponsor number tag was pinned.
