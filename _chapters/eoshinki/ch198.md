<br>One hour before the infiltration into Kwak Hanmuk's hospital room.

After being transported back to the Era Agency in a container alongside Je Hyun-oh, I first received a medical examination.

During the latter half of the COF walkthrough, my eyes remained gold, but the moment I stepped outside, they reverted to their original state.

Thanks to that, I passed the physical examination without any issues.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein were also transferred to a general ward.

In truth, Kwak Hanmuk's condition was critical, but since very few people were aware of the seal, we were able to conceal the truth.

Otherwise, he would have long since been locked in a special isolation cell.

Left alone in the hospital room, I intended to meet Samra first.

Usually, whenever we returned to the Era Agency, Samra would either come to see me in person or open a screen to talk.

However, I waited on the hospital bed for a while, but the silence persisted.

No contact came from Samra.

*‘Is he busy because of the international organization?’*

I wanted to reach out first, but I had no way to do so.

Although we belonged to the same Special Team, as long as I was not the Director of the Era Agency, I did not have the authority to summon Samra on my own volition.

Moreover, above all else, Samra's state seemed strange.

*‘It can't be that he's infected with the virus….’*

My worries refused to subside as I thought of Samra, who had been on edge over Geumcheon before I entered COF.

I would have to try to meet Samra, even if I had to ask Je Hyun-oh as a favor later.

In any case, my attempt to inform Samra about Kwak Hanmuk's condition and request support had become difficult for now.

*‘I wanted to ask about that woman, too.’*

No matter how I thought about it, she was a bizarre woman.

While she had been playing the violin, she must have seen my face displayed throughout the banquet hall, yet her reaction had been far too calm.

I was also curious about why Samra had concluded that she was a civilian Adaptee.

She would inevitably have been dragged to the Era Agency for investigation due to the closure of COF.

Deciding to handle that matter later, I checked the time.

It felt like about thirty minutes had passed, yet the sound of people moving could still be heard outside.

Without Samra to help and no Cognitive Decline Mask at my disposal, I had to wait for an appropriate timing.

As I waited for the surroundings to settle, I checked the Epic Quest.

<br>

{% include system.html text="◆&lt;Han Goyo> Epic Quest: Close 7 Trials within two months.<br>*Currently Closed Trials (3/7)<br>(Warning! If the quest fails, your eyes will be gouged out! If you succeed, you keep them.)" %}

<br>

I had cleared The Cell X, PTB, and COF with a True Ending, while Hopdpop was not included since it was a normal ending.

It had taken about 14 days in Earth time to clear COF.

I had about one month remaining.

And yet, four Trials remained.

The thought that I was in serious trouble crossed my mind.

At this rate, I would end up going to Hyper Eden to get artificial eyeballs installed myself.

I was absently rubbing my eyes with my hands when I suddenly recalled the final reward of the Epic Quest.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">[Upon completing all Epic Quests, the final reward &lt;Han Goyo> will receive is:</span>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">The recovery of &lt;Han Goyo>'s body to its most perfect state.]</span>

<br>

The word "recovery" felt suspicious.

*'In the end, won't it be meaningless once I shed this shell?'*

I was harboring complaints, feeling that no matter how hard I worked on the Epic Quest, it would all be for nothing.

<br>

{% include system.html text="You firmly believe that the System Window is presenting a wise and astonishing path.<br>If there is one single entity you must place your trust in, I believe it is the System Window." %}

<br>

The System Window immediately sent out a lengthy rebuttal.

The Young god grumbled in irritation at being lectured.

I felt a bit of sympathy, but I suppressed my own complaints, reminding myself that the System Window had helped me in many ways.

*'But why is it speaking now?'*

It had been quiet lately, so I had wondered if it was somehow being restricted.

<br>

{% include system.html text="You have accepted Young god. I judge that your own growth has also had an impact on the System Window." %}

<br>

Did that mean that the more I grew, the more smoothly I could handle/receive the System Window?

But before I could decipher the full meaning, a new rectangular window appeared.

<br>

{% include system.html text="Furthermore, you believe that now is a good time to go and check on your subordinates." %}

<br>

I was just about to venture out anyway.

I got out of bed, following the System Window's guidance.

I gathered the items I needed, then slipped quietly out of the hospital room.

By the time I left after closing up COF, it was already the dead of night.

Past midnight, the hospital hallway, with no daily lighting, was dark.

Not knowing which room Kwak Hanmuk was admitted to, I had originally intended to open the doors one by one.

But the System Window indicated the direction with arrows, allowing me to reach my destination easily.

I put my ear close against the door of the hospital room.

After confirming that there was no sign of movement inside, I slowly turned the stainless steel doorknob.

*Click*, the faint metallic sound resonated unusually loudly.

I opened the door just enough for my body to squeeze through and quickly stepped inside.

Amidst the dim outlines of the dark interior, I could hear breathing that was regular but somehow labored.

I hurried toward the bed.

And I gazed down at the sleeping Kwak Hanmuk.

Faint light seeped through the large glass window, where the blinds were drawn down, and settled onto his face.

Was it because the shadows in his features had deepened?

He felt like a completely different person from the one who normally laughed cheerfully and cut into me.

I stepped a little closer to the bed.

Noticing his slightly furrowed brow, I idly reached out and gently rubbed it.

It weighed on my heart all the more when compared to the sight of him sleeping peacefully inside the golden sphere of Resurrection.

The reason Kwak Hanmuk had broken his seal within COF was because Double M and Dominic were present.

He took a gamble for my sake, believing that even if he broke the seal and the god came out, the situation would be resolved somehow because there were other super-higher beings.

Even though he knew the future would bring his own collapse and death, he had thrown himself into it.

“…….”

It was already the second time. The second time Kwak Hanmuk had risked his life to protect me.

It was no exaggeration to say that I only existed because of his sacrifice.

Guilt over this happening because of me squeezed my mind mercilessly, while a rather twisted thought also rose to the surface.

*'Will you just leave me behind?'*

I hated that he had tried to disappear on his own accord when he couldn't die unless I permitted it.

Even if it was all done for my sake.

I moved the fingertips that had been rubbing his brow toward the corner of his eye.

A memory from the past resurfaced.

Following Dominic's advice, I had tried to dig out Kwak Hanmuk's eyes.

Back then… I believed it was all the work of the Young god.

I resented him greatly, thinking that I had almost done something insane because of him.

But now that I have accepted the Young god as a part of myself, I think I understand.

I was anxious that Kwak Hanmuk might leave me.

It seemed that the desire to leave a mark that would ensure he could never escape me led to my crazy behavior.

Of course, it was not a normal way of thinking, and I simply felt I could understand it in retrospect.

As I pondered over the past, the Young god suddenly gasped in surprise, shouting that I shouldn't dig out his eyes.

*'Do you think I'm you?'*

Hearing such words from the Young god slightly bruised my pride.

I *looked closely* at Kwak Hanmuk.

I could see him gradually collapsing.

I called out to the one disappearing into the void.

So that he would return to me, and so that he could not even face death without my permission.

Bright golden light added a gentle glow to the dark medical room.

And at the moment Kwak Hanmuk returned to me.

“…!”

Immediately, my wrist was grabbed roughly.

A sharp gaze pierced through me, then abruptly softened.

“Captain Kwak.”

Seeing his obvious panic, I hurriedly informed him of my condition.

“I am Han Goyo. I am not Chestnut.”

Not knowing how else to phrase it, I simply said I wasn’t Chestnut.

Kwak Hanmuk slowly closed his eyes, then opened them.

It looked as though he were trying to determine if this was a hallucination.

He remained seated against the backrest, still holding my wrist.

“Goyo.”

“Yes.”

Kwak Hanmuk gestured briefly with his chin toward my other, free hand.

“You don't plan on using that to peel fruit, do you?”

Oh.

I realized my mistake too late.

I had brought out a large knife to use the item, but for a completely unaware Kwak Hanmuk to suddenly be confronted with it, it was undoubtedly a startling object.

“Um, Captain Kwak. I intend to treat you.”

“With the cleaver?”

“No, I mean…. This is just an auxiliary tool.”

I hurried to begin my explanation.

“I obtained an item to treat you. Because the seal is damaged, a collapse is in progress, but this item can prevent it.”

I diligently explained that it could not only halt the collapse but, if performed correctly, would also allow him to gradually adapt to the god's power.

Kwak Hanmuk looked skeptical. He regarded me with the same distrust one has for a travelling salesman, so I pulled out the item first.

<br>

{% include system.html text="Starlight Hug Band-Aid: A premium protective Band-Aid for hurt toys. It tenderly hugs and protects the damaged parts with sparkling starlight." %}

<br>

It was a star-shaped Band-Aid.

Kwak Hanmuk's expression grew even more doubtful after checking the item's description, which featured a small star pattern inside.

Admittedly, it did look suspicious even to me. To boost his confidence, I revealed the item's source to Kwak Hanmuk.

I told him that gods could access a Trading Post in the universe, and since I had purchased the item there, it was trustworthy.

Kwak Hanmuk, who had been flipping the Starlight Hug Band-Aid over to inspect it, narrowed his eyes sharply.

“Chestnut.”

“Yes?”

“How much did you pay for this?”

I hesitated before unfastening my lips.

“Eight…”

“Eight hundred thousand won?”

“Eight hundred…”

“Eight million won?”

I answered in a very, very small voice.

“Eight hundred billion won…”

Kwak Hanmuk silently covered his eyes with his hand.

In truth, it would have been even more expensive if converted to Korean won. The value of a Star Fragment and the won did not correspond in a one-to-one ratio.

Subordinate items were also ridiculously expensive compared to other items.

Of course, I didn't tell Kwak Hanmuk about such minor details.

Instead, I tried my best to persuade him just how necessary and reasonable this expenditure was.

"It was an item absolutely essential for treating Captain Kwak."

"……."

"I also got a special discount. Five percent."

The standard adhesive bandages were at full price, while the children's bandages came with a five percent discount.

The kids' bandages only featured a star shape with colorful star patterns; there was no difference in their performance.

It was a bit embarrassing for an adult man to stick them on his skin, but I couldn't afford to pass up a five percent discount opportunity.

I was getting impatient to begin the treatment, but Kwak Hanmuk remained completely expressionless and simply refused to give his permission.

He interrogated me again.

"Where did you get the money?"

I added a small lie to my explanation.

"It's the payment I received through the Young god Channel."

"Haaah……."

Kwak Hanmuk let out a long sigh. He swept his hair back with his hand before asking,

"Do Lieutenant Colonel Je or Captain Mo know about this? It's about us being the same type of subordinate… right?"

"No, not yet…"

The Young god had strongly insisted that we had to operate in secret to prevent the other subordinates from getting jealous.

He was so filled with worry and concern that I had barely managed to calm him down by promising to do whatever the Young god wanted.

I called out to Kwak Hanmuk anxiously.

"Captain Kwak. Please, let me treat you first. I promise to answer every single question you ask later."

I had restored him using Resurrection, but it was merely a temporary emergency measure.

Deep down, I wanted to forcibly administer the treatment right then and there.

I called out to him earnestly.

"Captain Kwak……."

Kwak Hanmuk slowly released my wrist, which he had been gripping firmly until that moment.

At last, he gave his approval.

"Alright, I guess… A Master has to be obeyed, after all."

The title 'Master' was a bit awkward, but securing his permission was the more important matter.

"Thank you, Captain. Then, please remove your upper garment."

Kwak Hanmuk immediately grabbed the hem of his clothes.

He crossed his arms in an X-shape to pull the garment off, leaving his upper body bare as he looked at me.

*'He only needed to take it off enough to expose his chest…'*

However, it did seem more convenient for assessing his condition this way.

The tattoo now covered his entire upper body.

The man, wrapped in blue lightning, asked me,

"What are we doing now?"

I casually glanced at Kwak Hanmuk's wrist. After double-checking that the prayer beads made of lightning-struck wood were gone, I replied.

"I will need a small amount of blood."

When I checked the item's instructions for use, Dominic had been displeased.

He had been displeased with the necessity of me spilling my own blood to treat a subordinate, but items that did not require blood were far more expensive.

Due to budget constraints, I had no choice but to purchase the Starlight Hug Band-Aid.

I gripped Kwak Hanmuk's shoulder and brought the blade to rest over his heart.

Before Kwak Hanmuk could say a word, I quickly carved a shallow gash in the skin, creating a wound.

Bright red blood trickled down across the blue tattoo.

Holding the blood-stained kitchen knife, I watched Kwak Hanmuk.

With a knife pointed at his heart, I had expected him to call me crazy and give me a solid bonk on the head by now.

But Kwak Hanmuk was calm. His demeanor, which suggested complete trust in me, gave me a slight boost in confidence.

"I'll be done in a moment."

Just as I was about to bring the kitchen knife to my palm,

*CLANG!*

The kitchen knife clattered to the hospital room floor. Kwak Hanmuk had roughly slapped my hand away.

I stared at Kwak Hanmuk with wide, surprised eyes. He glared at me with a rigid expression.

Frozen in a state of bewilderment, I finally stammered out late.

"I was only going to take a little cut..."

Kwak Hanmuk drew in a deep breath that expanded his chest, then exhaled sharply.

"...Hah. Chestnut. You should have explained that sooner."

"I'm sorry."

It was a shallow wound, but it was in a location close to the heart. The strong scent of blood spread through the hospital room.

I tiptoed over to retrieve the kitchen knife that had fallen on the floor. Then Kwak Hanmuk extended his hand toward me.

"Give it here."

Kwak Hanmuk snatched the kitchen knife from me and asked.

"You only need a small cut, right?"

"Yes..."

Kwak Hanmuk grabbed my wrist and, in my place, brought the kitchen knife to my palm.

He made a shallow cut. Following the smooth motion of his hand, a red line was drawn across my skin.

I tore open the bandage and and smeared the blood from my palm on it.

Then, I pressed it firmly onto the wound above his heart.

"..."

Kwak Hanmuk's eyes briefly crinkled in discomfort. The sensation he felt seemed somewhat unpleasant.

"I'm almost done."

I slowly pushed my power into the bandage. The Starlight Hug Band-Aid began to glow with a golden light, and the radiance started to spread along the lightning tattoo.

The golden light began to seep into every nook and cranny of the fractal pattern.

I could feel Kwak Hanmuk's breathing become heavy. Since it was a matter of forcibly sealing the split, it must have been extremely painful.

The Young god inside me insisted that I should praise my well-behaved and dignified subordinate once the treatment was finished.

I let the Young god's words slide out of one ear and opened my mouth.

"Excuse me, Captain Kwak."

The previously downcast gray eyes lifted to meet mine.

I looked down at Kwak Hanmuk and opened my mouth.

"I would prefer if you didn't... do that again."

As if he had seen something within me, a trace of amusement suddenly seeped into his gaze.

A voice, cracked by pain, came back.

"...So you want me to watch Chestnut die?"

Baring his sharp fangs, the one who smiled sneeringly spoke to me.

"Do not die in front of me, Goyo."
