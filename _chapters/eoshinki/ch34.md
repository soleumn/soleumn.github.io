<br>Including the ones that hadn't hatched, there were hundreds of thousands of Meteorites of Trial that had fallen to Earth.

It was only natural that I wouldn't know about all the Trials.

It was also only natural that CEO Cha would know about Trials I didn't.

After all, as the CEO of Paseong in the real world, he was constantly entering Trials.

Unlike me, who only entered Trials within Korea, CEO Cha also accepted many requests from abroad.

But to think there was a Trial where Sa Gongyeon appeared as an NPC...

As the one who had received the text message Sa Gongyeon sent, my bewilderment outweighed the joy of finding a clue.

*'Could an NPC from a Trial affect reality?'*

I quickly shook off the uncertain thought.

Nothing was confirmed yet. I would have to hear more from CEO Cha.

"Let's go inside and talk."

CEO Cha turned around immediately. Kang Cheonseo, having achieved her goal, smiled and released my arm.

She walked beside CEO Cha, who was leading the way, and winked at me.

I flashed a business smile at the Chairman and followed them, keeping an eye on Je Hyun-oh.

After confirming he was okay, I approached Kang Byeokrin.

The last time I'd seen her briefly at Chairman Cheongang's residence, she had fallen and sprained her ankle.

It had always bothered me that I couldn't even talk to her properly back then because I was too busy treating her leg.

Though it was in the past, Cheongang had helped us a lot by becoming the team's sponsor.

I felt nothing but gratitude toward Cheongang, so I wanted to be kind to her as well.

"How have you been?"

For some reason, Kang Byeokrin flinched the moment I spoke to her.

Her face, which had been faintly flushed for a while, deepened in color, and she suddenly covered her mouth with her hand.

After a long moment, she finally replied.

"Yes... Um, Goyo... How have you been?"

I knew Kang Byeokrin was supposed to have a formidable personality, just like her grandmother Kang Cheonseo, but strangely, she seemed incredibly gentle.

To think I had been mistaken, I recalled the words Kang Byeokrin had said while stepping out of the car during our first meeting.

*'She said she hated commoners.'*

I wondered if she was being kind to me now only because of Kang Cheonseo.

"I've been doing well. I felt I hadn't properly greeted you back then."

I was glad to see that her leg, which she had injured when she fell, seemed to have completely healed.

Just as I was about to bring up a casual topic like the weather, Kang Byeokrin spoke first.

"Have you... eaten?"

"Yes, I have."

I was about to ask if she had eaten too, trying to keep the conversation going, but a barrage of questions suddenly poured out.

"Are you eating all three meals? You need to make sure you eat well every single day. Are you getting enough sleep? At least seven hours, right?"

My brain momentarily froze at the torrent of words. I didn't know how to answer. Kang Byeokrin's gaze was far too serious to just laugh it off as a joke.

After a moment's thought, I gave the most neutral response possible. "I'm taking good care of my health. Thank you for worrying."

But Kang Byeokrin's questions weren't over yet.

"What about supplements? You aren't taking any herbal medicine, are you? I could have some tonic brewed for you..."

"Byeokrin."

Kang Cheonseo called her name without even looking back, her tone a clear warning.

Kang Byeokrin's lips parted as if to say more, but she ultimately shut her mouth tight.

Yet, she kept staring at me with desperate eyes.

I felt a bit awkward, suddenly being subjected to a health survey. I couldn't understand why Kang Byeokrin was taking such a keen interest in my well-being.

But it didn't seem like she was asking with any bad intentions. For some reason, this seemed to be an important matter to her. I got the feeling that I shouldn't give a careless answer.

First, I softened my gaze and forced the kindest smile I could muster. Then, I lowered my voice and whispered softly.

"I'll make sure to take my vitamins and even have some restorative herbal medicine made."

Hearing my answer, Kang Byeokrin's eyes widened. Then, her face crumpled as if she were about to cry.

I, who had tried so hard to give a good answer, flustered and stumbled. I felt like I'd made a mistake, but since I didn't know what it was, I just hesitated. Kang Byeokrin immediately composed her expression.

Kang Byeokrin spoke to me with a serious expression.

"You promised."

...I had intended to feign ignorance. I thought I could just let it slide, since I'd hardly ever have to see her again. But it seemed I couldn't do that.

*'I might not be able to get a restorative tonic, but I really should at least make sure to take my vitamins,'* I thought.

Our conversation naturally tapered off as we arrived at the CEO's office at Paseong.

The directors waiting outside the office bowed in greeting.

Je Hyun-oh held out the coffee and desserts he was carrying to Mok Saehwa, who was standing nearby.

Mok Saehwa looked at him with a puzzled expression.

Then CEO Cha gave a slight nod, and Mok Saehwa immediately switched into guest-hosting mode, taking the items with a bright smile.

"I bought these for the directors," I explained, quickly stepping forward. Mok Saehwa's smile turned genuine.

"Mr. Goyo? Thank you."

"Oh, we should be the ones treating you, Mr. Goyo..."

Seo Munchan looked genuinely touched.

I'd only bought these small gifts because I felt awkward coming empty-handed, but seeing how pleased they were made me a little embarrassed.

"Would you like me to take your bags for a moment?" Mok Saehwa asked kindly.

I declined. The duffel bag was light, and the box tucked under my arm was the gift—I wanted to hand it over personally.

The CEO's office, decorated in clean, neutral tones, reflected CEO Cha's personality.

I spotted a pair of white headphones resting on the inner desk. He must have been wearing them before the guests arrived.

"We're all busy, so let's get straight to business and get out of your hair quickly," Kang Cheonseo said, settling onto the sofa as if she owned the place.

"What Geumcheon wants is *‘Good-Looking’*."

*‘Good-Looking’* was a small bronze hand mirror.

It changed the Adaptee's appearance to the one most preferred by the NPC of the Trial they had entered.

It could only be used once per Trial. I had used it in Clean3 myself, but it had resulted in no change to my appearance at all.

Still, there was no better item for safely gaining an NPC's favor, so it was no wonder Geumcheon coveted it.

"What kind of deal would I make with criminals? I refused immediately. So, they approached Linyi instead."

Geumcheon had offered Rin an item in exchange for 'Good-Looking'.

Surprisingly, the offer was for something that pierced through a matter Cheongang had been keeping hidden.

Cheongang had been searching for the insider who had leaked the information, but they were having more trouble than expected.

"When Geumcheon figured out something we had only shared among people we truly trusted, I was too scared to even live."

"What kind of item was it?"

At CEO Cha's question, Kang Cheonseo glanced at Kang Byeokrin.

Kang Byeokrin slowly opened her mouth and answered.

"He said it was an item that perfectly restores a damaged body."

Did Kang Byeokrin need a body restoration item?

*'She doesn't seem to be injured anywhere.'*

Separate from his puzzled thoughts, Geumcheon's offer was a lie.

Recovery items themselves could certainly exist.

But to receive such an item as a Clear reward, one would have to be the top contributor to a True Ending Clear.

If it were a Normal Ending Clear, even the top contributor couldn't obtain such a recovery item.

*'But just what kind of Trial is it?'*

He was curious. If it was a Trial I had already played through in the game...

I had wondered why no window related to the Epic Quest appeared when I closed The Cell X, but it seemed the System Window had been sending it directly.

After letting the System Window vanish without a trace after delivering its message, I refocused on the conversation.

Kang Byeokrin had rejected Geumcheon's offer.

And while they were investigating Geumcheon, Sa Gongyeon had contacted them.

It was absurd that a mere reporter could track Cheongang's internal movements in real time.

But the content of the message was even more unbelievable.

"He'll provide information on Geumcheon, but in exchange..."

Kang Byeokrin looked at me and spoke cautiously.

"I requested support from Han Goyo, an Adaptee from the Trial Response Agency."

Every gaze in the CEO's office instantly locked onto me.

I was utterly bewildered to hear my name mentioned so abruptly.

*'So this is why Kang Cheonseo said I needed to be informed.'*

The situation was unraveling in a completely unexpected direction.

"That's enough of our discussion for now. So, CEO Cha, what's an NPC? I'm curious, tell me."

Kang Cheonseo beckoned to CEO Cha with a flick of her hand.

CEO Cha, as if lost in thought, finally spoke up after a moment's delay.

"It was an interview request."

He explained that while he was in the middle of the Trial, he had received a phone call from a reporter who introduced himself as Sa Gongyeon.

Thinking it was just a simple event, CEO Cha had ignored the call and continued his Trial, eventually clearing it without any issues.

The moment I heard that, I was certain.

*'Sa Gongyeon is not an NPC.'*

Just as Mr. Black Block had contacted me while I was inside the Trial, it seemed Sa Gongyeon had approached CEO Cha in a similar way.

I didn't know why he'd specifically requested an interview, but...

*'He'd asked me for a planning interview too.'*

It didn't feel like Mr. Black Block and Sa Gongyeon were the same person. Besides, Mr. Black Block had Interstellar Communication, which allowed him to contact me directly.

He wouldn't have gone out of his way to send a message on his phone.

As I was trying to guess the connection between the two beings, Kang Cheonseo called my name.

"Goyo."

"Yes, Chairman."

"Be careful. Of course, the Era Agency and Paseong will handle things just fine, but still."

Kang Cheonseo glanced at Je Hyun-oh.

Leaning against the wall with his arms crossed, Je Hyun-oh's face remained expressionless, even after such a shocking conversation.

Kang Cheonseo smiled at me again and said, "The older you get, the more things you have to fear."

I still didn't know why Sa Gongyeon had approached me.

But Geumcheon would certainly have an interest in me.

After all, I was an Adaptee who could obtain the expensive Trial Items they desired.

Still, I think the reason I wasn't too worried was thanks to the Special Team. Especially with Samra here, I felt even more at ease.

"Anyway, CEO Cha. Given the situation, don't just give me a mercenary. Assign one of the Directors to me. I'm really scared, you know."

Kang Cheonseo joked around. But beneath her words, wrapped in humor, her anxiety was clearly visible.

This was because she had realized that not only Geumcheon, but also the suddenly appearing Sa Gongyeon, were no ordinary beings.

CEO Cha called her softly, "Chairman Kang."

However, he didn't offer a detailed explanation or long words of reassurance. He merely threw back a short question.

"Has Paseong ever disappointed you?"

Kang Cheonseo's eyes widened for a moment, then she broke into a bright smile.

"Of course not. I knew I could count on you, CEO Cha. I'll leave you to it then."

She rose from the sofa, her expression relieved.

"We'll head out now and let you young people talk. Goyo, don't forget to visit us next time."

Kang Byeokrin glanced back at me one last time. I offered her a slight smile, and she nodded in acknowledgment before following Kang Cheonseo out.

The sound of the directors and Kang Cheonseo talking outside faded as they walked away.

And in the CEO's office...

"..."

Now, only CEO Cha, Je Hyun-oh, and I remained.

With only the three of us left, a sudden wave of pressure washed over me.

The moment Kang Cheonseo and Kang Byeokrin vanished, CEO Cha had flipped the mood like he was turning over his palm.

"I didn't expect you to bring a guardian along to a hospital visit," CEO Cha said, his gaze fixed on Je Hyun-oh. "Before that, are you even capable of acting as a guardian?"

Je Hyun-oh had been acting indifferent until now, but he wasn't the type to back down from a fight. He slowly narrowed his eyes, then pushed himself off the wall he had been leaning against.

"CEO Cha!" I called out quickly, trying to break the tension.

I lifted the large box I'd placed on an empty chair and handed it to him. "This is a get-well gift."

CEO Cha let out a dry laugh.

My get-well gift was a box of cup noodles.

Six cups of instant noodles were neatly packed under the plastic wrap covering the top of the box.

I watched CEO Cha's reaction closely.

Objectively, I'd chosen an insane get-well gift, but there was a reason for my choice.

In *The Cell X*, I'd seen CEO Cha's affection level for me.

Not that I'd meant to see it... but still, the abnormally high number had sparked my suspicion.

So, after much thought, I'd brought him something that might be meaningful to the CEO inside the game.

"Is my life that cheap?"

I felt like Daeil Yeorae was about to slice me up.

*'I guess I was wrong.'*

I breathed a sigh of relief.

Yet, despite his disbelief, CEO Cha didn't throw the cup noodle box. He simply placed it on the desk.

Seeing this, I was even more confused.

Just as I was staring at the unfamiliar figure before me, CEO Cha pulled out a large, hard-shell suitcase.

"Open it."

I gave him a suspicious look, and he added, "It's better than ramen."

"..."

I quietly walked to the desk and opened the suitcase he had placed there.

Inside the suitcase lay a Meteorite of Trial.

Meteorites from one or two-star Trials could be moved using special items.

The Era Agency had also collected a few low-grade Trials to train Adaptees.

Could CEO Cha have gone to the trouble of acquiring the meteorite just so I could close the Trial?

I'd only brought a box of cup noodles as a get-well-soon gift.

A pang of guilt pricked my conscience.

Just as I was staring at the meteorite, which glowed with a split of red and blue, CEO Cha suddenly grabbed my hand. At the same time, a metallic clang echoed.

Je Hyun-oh, reacting instantly, shed his human form and lunged forward.

But the world melted away into a mottled, fluorescent haze.

Je Hyun-oh, thrown back from the entry zone, slashed through the CEO's office with his metallic hand. I watched, eyes wide, as the sofa and table were brutally torn apart.

Then, in a flash, I was thrust into a new Trial.
