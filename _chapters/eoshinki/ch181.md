<br>As I stared without being able to look away, I belatedly realized that my train of thought was a bit peculiar.

Objectively speaking, it was a bizarre sight.

It would have been normal to be terrified or afraid.

However, I didn't feel even a shred of those emotions.

It was simply breathtakingly beautiful.

I wanted to see it closer, but the screen blocking the way was annoying.

I took a Clear Coin out of my pocket.

Holding the only Clear Coin I couldn't return in one hand, I slowly pressed my other hand down onto the screen.

It wasn't much different from when I had shoved my hand into the arcade machine screen.

The Clear Coin vanished, and my body was sucked in as if sinking into something soft.

Before I knew it, I was inside the screen.

In this place, fakes were still being born endlessly.

However, they couldn't even approach my subordinate anymore.

That was because black lines, like scribbles, had been violently slashed across them, shattering them to pieces.

The screams and the smell of blood from the fakes were far too realistic.

Amidst blood and flesh flying from all directions, I approached my subordinate.

Our eyes met.

The moment I was completely reflected within the large pupil at the center, it felt as though everything paused for a moment.

The entity formed of metallic wings was dozens of times larger than my body.

Countless pupils gazed at me all at once.

What woke me from being captivated by that persistent and unwavering stare was the voice coming from the radio.

<br>

*“What can a little lamb, a young god do! Being so weak! So immature!”*

<br>

The media, noisy like a swarm of flying bugs, was annoying.

As if I had any time to spare—when I should be focusing entirely on my subordinate.

Manipulating the space had become effortless for me now.

I changed things one by one, just as I had done in the arcade.

I turned off the radio, closed the newspaper, and paused the video.

The space, which had been noisy with all sorts of media, grew silent.

The fakes that had been born without rest also vanished. Only me and my subordinate remained in the snow-white space like a blank sheet of paper.

I looked up at my subordinate and thought.

*‘It'd be hard to walk around like this.’*

It looked cool, but daily life seemed like it would be inconvenient. The captains would probably be startled, too...

Just as we were quietly facing each other.

The metallic wings spread wide and instantly enveloped me.

While I caught my breath in surprise, I became trapped amidst numerous metal feathers and pupils.

Amidst the dim surroundings, a cool sensation constantly brushed against my skin.

It was the feeling of metal feathers brushing against me.

Each time they grazed past my vital points, a chilling sensation washed over me.

From the blade maintaining a precarious boundary, raw emotion could be felt.

It was hatred toward me and the intent to kill, wishing to carve my flesh to pieces.

However, the blade did not cut me.

Unlike when it slaughtered the fakes without hesitation, it only caressed me with a dulled edge.

All that I could feel was a cool and solid texture.

The emotion of gazing only at me was pleasing.

No matter what kind of emotion it was, the most important thing was that it was thinking only of me.

*‘Why did Black Block say it like that?’*

Claiming it wasn’t the form I remembered, of all things.

***But I remembered it with perfect clarity!***

How could he speak so confidently when he didn’t even know the truth?

Buried within the metal feathers, I savored the cozy moment for a brief while.

And slowly, I opened my mouth.

“I'm back.”

The thirty-six wings tightened around me even further.

A groan escaped my lips—but the crushing force did not lessen. Rather, it only grew fiercer.

As a metal feather brushed against my throat, a slight sensation of suffocation crept in.

Even if I weren't cut, it felt as though I could be crushed to death.

Even though I couldn't flick a single fingertip, I was not afraid. Rather, it felt completely comfortable.

Feeling a pleasant dizziness, I asked.

“Are you going to stay like this?”

Naturally, no answer came. I muttered as if talking to myself.

“At this rate, you won't even be able to eat the cake...”

The crushing pressure eased, just a little.

I could sense the sheer disbelief coming from my subordinate, and I couldn’t help but laugh.

The metal feathers shifted with a rustling, clinking sound.

My body was swept along helplessly until, before I knew it, I was face-to-face with the largest eye positioned at the center.

I gazed at it, full of curiosity.

The massive eye scrutinized me thoroughly.

It was a gaze that seemed ready to swallow every last trace of emotion surfacing on my face without missing a thing.

Being observed with my entire body subdued, I gently rested my cheek against the feather pressing against my face.

I knew what my subordinate wanted.

“It's fine if you hurt me.”

While I couldn't permit it to kill me, I could give this much up freely.

The metal feather touching my cheek loosened and shifted its angle.

The blade traced down my cheekbone, swept across my jawline, and slid down to the pulse throbbed at the side of my neck.

Lingering for a long time as if choosing the most delicious part, the feather returned to my cheek once more.

And very slowly, pressing against the skin, it slid across.

A thin scratch opened, and blood stained the black metal.

Yet before a drop of blood could even form, the blood vanished without a trace.

A bizarre pulsation could be felt from the contacting metal feather.

With a somewhat unrefined metallic screech, the constriction grew even tighter.

As my breath was choked off, my vision blurred. Even while feeling the dizzying pressure, the corners of my lips naturally curved into a smile.

“Do you want more?”

In my blacking-out vision, I smiled.

“Take a lot...”

I wanted to stroke it, but I couldn't.

I twitched the only fingers I could still move.

When my fingertips brushed against somewhere soft, the feathers pressing down on my body reacted faintly.

It seemed I had touched a small eye located at a joint.

I whispered in a faint voice.

“My subordinate...”

Just before I lost consciousness.

Suddenly, my airway burst wide open. I took a deep, heavy breath.

At the same time, a chilling yet languid sound of metal friction echoed from all around.

From the outermost edges, the thirty-six massive wings dismantled into thousands, tens of thousands of individual metal feathers.

The metal feathers interlocked and overlapped in midair, coalescing toward the center.

It was a spectacle akin to a black whirlwind flowing backward inward, devouring itself.

Amidst the bizarre storm, the silhouette of a human was sculpted.

Cold, hard metallic feathers fused smoothly, forming pale skin and a well-proportioned skeletal frame.

The blades of the feathers shaped the lines of the forehead and bridge of the nose, and the small eyes embedded in the gaps of the wings transformed into dense eyelashes and dark hair.

The transformation continued unhindered downward as well.

The familiar gas mask formed the lower half of the face.

The collarbones, the broadly spread shoulders, and the muscles of the firm chest and abdomen were created in turn.

As the black feathers covered the skin, they instantly changed form into pitch-black combat gear and a hooded jumper that wrapped around the body.

The cluster of feathers extending long on both sides made a rattling sound as they intertwined to create human arms.

However, reaching the hands, they did not turn into soft skin.

The sharpest feathers remained intact at the joints, forming metal hands.

Finally, the feathers that had been floating about in disarray gathered together behind his back.

And they were structured into six wings.

The reassembled body landed lightly.

As if the complete dismantlement of the human form had been a lie, right before my eyes stood my usual subordinate.

He moved his six wings in turn, as if checking his own body.

Then, lightly clacking his metal hands, he approached me.

The dark purple iris and golden pupil nestled within his long eyes were calm.

His gaze turned toward the scratch on my cheek.

Since the wound wasn't deep to begin with, no blood remained, only a red line.

Averting his gaze from the scratch, my subordinate looked down at me.

A rough bass voice that sounded like it was scraping the bottom of the abyss echoed.

"Goyo."

"Yes!"

His familiar voice was welcome.

I answered quickly, but my subordinate looked at me intently and whispered words of unknown meaning.

"Not yet."

What does that mean?

I stared at my subordinate in bewilderment.

My subordinate looked at me as well.

Our gazes intertwined deeply. It was a look that seemed to verify our existence.

The longer our contact lasted, the more the murderous intent slowly faded from his metallic pupils.

Just around the time he returned to his usual languid gaze.

A low whisper was heard from behind the gas mask.

"When will you grow..."

"Pardon?"

I asked right back, but my subordinate put on his goggles without answering.

The one equipped with the crow set stood out alone in the pure white space.

The form full of wings was good too, but of course, his current appearance was nice as well.

The Young god insisted that he had looked cooler earlier, but I ignored him.

*That form is inconvenient, you see.*

Putting forward the practical reason that it was also inconvenient to stroke his hair, the Young god accepted it roughly.

Anyway, it was just when I was thinking that all we had to do was get outside.

I realized a crucial fact.

"Um.... Do you happen to know the way out?"

I didn't know the way out.

When coming inside the screen, it had just felt natural.

I had slipped right in as if breathing, but now that I tried to leave, I had no idea what or how to do it.

It was just like smoothly hitting complex notes in a rhythm game, only to miss the beat the moment you became conscious of your finger movements.

Come to think of it, the control room was a place I had never even visited in the game.

Normally, riding the waste ejection duct in the terrace seating should have led to the hallway.

While I was pondering a method, a gentle question poked at me.

“Why did you wander in so carelessly?”

It meant, what on earth were you thinking entering without even knowing how to leave?

Still engrossed in figuring out an escape method, I replied.

“Because my subordinate was here…. Ah, Lieutenant Colonel Je was here.”

I belatedly corrected my slip in title, but it was already out of my mouth.

I checked his reaction, but there was no particular response from the other person.

He merely stared intently at my eyes, which must have been sparkling with gold, and then looked down at his own metal hand.

“…So that was why.”

My subordinate, uttering yet another incomprehensible sound, equipped a crow glove onto one hand.

Then, without warning, he snatched me up and tucked me under his arm.

As soon as I was tucked in like a sack of rice, my body floated up into the air.

Flying upward, my subordinate stared straight ahead for a moment.

Was he trying to split the space?

However, he had drawn a black line in the Tower before as well, yet failed to escape.

Just as I grew tense, fearing my subordinate might be harmed, a soft, gentle voice reached my ears.

“Act like a Trainee, Goyo.”

He called me a Trainee, not a Young god.

I closed my eyes for a moment and then opened them.

And to my subordinate…. No, to Je Hyun-oh, I spoke with concern.

“But it might be dangerous.”

This was a worrisome point even for a Trainee. It was because I knew the failure he had experienced earlier.

Je Hyun-oh applied a bit more strength to the hand holding me.

And as if announcing an established fact, he spoke calmly.

“We can get out now.”

His metal hand spread wide toward the empty air. After seeming to measure it for a moment, he swung his hand.

A line formed, like a black brush traversing a blank sheet of paper.

A pitch-black cut line, resembling a horizon, was drawn long horizontally.

And at some moment, it slowly began to widen.

“…!”

I opened my eyes wide.

Beyond the widening cut line, all sorts of worlds were unfolding.

The swirling, distorted and tangled scenery was enough to make one lose their wits just by looking at it for a brief moment.

My subordinate leaped right into it.

Sparks flew as my vision flashed brilliantly.

Lacking goggles, I had no choice but to close my eyes.

Light flashed even beyond my tightly closed eyelids.

When the light finally subsided.

Opening my eyes again, I had arrived at an unexpected place.

It was a banquet hall.

Tucked under Je Hyun-oh’s arm, I was floating in mid-air with my back to the chandelier.

An even more unexpected event was unfolding beneath my feet.

The time spent inside the control room and the screen hadn't been that long, but perhaps the flow of time differed from the outside?

The banquet hall, which had clearly been empty when seen from the control room, was now packed with all sorts of aliens.

They were all staring at me at once.

It didn't feel like they were simply looking at a suddenly appearing entity.

A primal wariness surged up from the arrow-like gazes flying toward me.

CEO Cha was also standing among the aliens.

For some reason, he had deactivated his Cognitive Decline.

Clutching the hilt of Daeil Yeorae, he glared at me.

The gaze of the stiffly rigid man turned toward my cheek.

Mindlessly wiping my cheek with the back of my hand, I felt a stinging sensation. I had completely forgotten about the wound by then.

Just as I was touching and examining my face, I froze right there.

The mask was gone.
