<br>My bare face had been completely exposed.

The half-mask that had been covering my face had vanished somewhere.

*‘When did it disappear?’*

Did it vanish when I entered inside the screen?

Je Hyun-oh also seemed not to have expected this situation, as his body stiffened slightly.

It appeared he had not intended to come out into the banquet hall.

Something must have gone wrong; as he cut through space and teleported, he seemed to have ended up in an unintended location.

In the brief moment, all sorts of thoughts rushed through my head.

Gasps burst out across the banquet hall.

“It’s the Young god!”

“Seeing him in person, he’s even younger.”

“He is just, so, small.”

“■■■■!”

The guests in the banquet hall had not revealed their true essences.

They suppressed their power with masks and maintained their outer shells remarkably well.

As if trying to keep a frightened child from running away.

They looked at me as if they wanted to play the role of wonderful, adult gods, performing only the part of moderate sponsors.

However, I had already witnessed their true essences.

I knew it all the more because I had seen the gods' greed directed at me.

I must never be caught...

It was the moment the clinking sound of metal was heard ringing in my ears.

“Je Hyun-oh!”

CEO Cha roared and unsheathed Daeil Yeorae.

Je Hyun-oh was already flying before CEO Cha could even shout his name.

Je Hyun-oh and I cut across between the chandeliers embedded with optical elements.

The chandelier, sliced by Je Hyun-oh’s metal wings, poured crystals down like rain.

In the blindingly sparkling rain of crystals, countless hands reached out.

They took on the bare minimum shape of "hands," but that was all.

Stretching out in ways impossible for a human, the hands tried to grab me.

Just as I was performing a acrobatic flight to avoid the hands reaching out like a net, a semicircular white light rushed in.

Brighter even than the crystals of the chandelier, the light ruthlessly sliced through the hands.

A hand in a leather glove narrowly missed touching me, but was severed by the light of Daeil Yeorae and fell down without achieving its purpose.

A hand in a lace glove finally grazed me as if licking my skin, then dropped with a faint convulsion.

Blood and flesh rained down over the table piled with champagne glasses.

The transparent liquor was dyed red, and a severed hand smashed down and collapsed the neatly stacked champagne tower.

Following the white light, Je Hyun-oh savagely swung one hand.

Black lines were drawn across the marble pillars of the banquet hall, slicing them to pieces in an instant.

*Boom!*

The pillars engraved with complex circuits collapsed like dominos, and blue sparks popped like firecrackers.

As the central axis collapsed, the banquet hall building shook as if an earthquake had struck.

The digital fresco on the ceiling also shattered from the shockwave.

The ruined panels were crushed into thick pixel noise, and a few surviving panel fragments endlessly displayed incomprehensible system error codes and red warning windows.

The building looked as though it might collapse at any moment, but no one thought of fleeing.

Instead, they only reached out their hands all the more persistently.

In fact, building collapses and the like would not even pose a minor threat to them.

Amidst the pouring debris and sparks, the stretched-out arms once again completely covered my field of vision in pitch black.

*SCREEECH!*

Je Hyun-oh gripped one of his metal hands.

A crunching sound was heard from his grasp, and immediately hundreds of black crescent moons were scattered.

The fired crescent moons indiscriminately pierced the opponent, halting their movement.

The trajectory of pure white light gracefully penetrated between the spread-out crescent moons in a wide area.

CEO Cha, leaping off a crumbling marble pillar as a stepping stone, crossed paths with Je Hyun-oh in mid-air.

Yet, not even in that brief moment did they look at each other.

Je Hyun-oh stared straight ahead, while CEO Cha glared at me.

Had there been more time, he surely would have cornered me to toss out some snide remark.

I couldn't fathom what was so displeasing to him.

Having done nothing wrong except diligently follow the Acorn Compass, I felt a fleeting sense of unfairness.

Daeil Yeorae glowed brightly, emitting light on its own.

A dazzling white light instantly carved away the hands that had penetrated the gaps of the black crescent moons.

*Slash, CRUNCH!*

Deformed, twisted hands lunged like a surprise attack from behind CEO Cha as he landed.

However, CEO Cha did not look back.

Instead, he simply adjusted his grip on his sword and unleashed a wave of white sword energy in another direction.

Je Hyun-oh fiercely swiped his metal hand toward the direction of his head.

The black crescent moon narrowly grazed CEO Cha's white mask, dismantling the enemies behind his back.

This bizarre cooperative attack, devoid of even a single word or an exchange of glances, was flawless.

When Je Hyun-oh slashed all around to block the reaching hands, CEO Cha unleashed a sharp light to precisely sever the wrists.

Even while watching this seamless offense and defense, I anxiously scanned my surroundings once again.

This was only possible because the gods were humoring the Young god.

If they hadn't held back and treated him like a child, CEO Cha, Je Hyun-oh, and I might have already been torn to shreds by the countless hands.

This was practically no different from a predetermined ending.

It would end the moment CEO Cha and Je Hyun-oh grew exhausted.

Even so, if I were to leave it to the Young god...

"......"

I wasn't confident I could return to my original self once I completely handed over the initiative.

Anxiety crept in, whispering that I might be pushed aside forever.

It was an anxiety rooted in a certain intuition, not just simple worry.

It was then. Amidst the unfamiliar and bizarre hands, I spotted a familiar hand.

It was Cecilia.

Positioned on the second-floor railing, she was desperately calling out to me.

"Roasted Chestnut!!"

The moment I heard her cry, I turned back to look at Je Hyun-oh.

Je Hyun-oh also spotted Cecilia. I urgently called out to Je Hyun-oh.

"Lieutenant Colonel Je...!"

He squeezed me tightly once.

Then, he hurled me directly toward Cecilia.

It was a blind spot that deftly evaded the reaching hands.

CEO Cha, who had been busy dealing with the aliens, didn't miss the movement and turned around.

It seemed as though the thought *'Has that raven bastard gone mad?'* could be read in his gaze.

However, in the current situation, it was the best method.

Cecilia reached out her arm toward me with all her might.

I, too, reached out my hand to her without hesitation.

And the moment our hands touched.

Sparks flew from Cecilia's body, and she and I instantly teleported.

*Crash!*

Cecilia rolled across the floor with me still held in her arms.

She gasped for breath, panting heavily.

"Haa, haa..."

The surrounding scenery had completely changed.

Because she had used her ability in a rush, we hadn't been able to travel far. We were still inside the building where the banquet hall was located.

Looking around in a slightly dizzy state, I belatedly realized that this was a corridor.

Hologram screens acting as murals were displaying the night view of Hyper Eden.

"Captains...?"

And there were Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein.

Cecilia had teleported straight to the location where the captains were.

As she staggered and let me go from her embrace, Kwak Hanmuk immediately grabbed my hand and pulled me up.

My head was spinning, likely because I had been performing acrobatic maneuvers right up until the moment of teleportation.

When I couldn't stand straight, Mo Haein grabbed my other arm this time.

Leaning on the captains' hands for a moment, I struggled to steady my body.

Before I could even figure out why the captains were here, Cecilia opened her mouth.

"Roasted Chestnut..."

She took off the half-mask that had been covering her face.

And looking at me with trembling eyes, she said,

"Run away."

The eyes that had shone while handing me a banquet hall invitation, asking if I could cover the greatest mystery in Hyper Eden, were nowhere to be found.

Now, her eyes were filled only with primal terror.

"Run away together with your siblings. You should be able to get out of the building from here."

She even went as far as saying things that sounded like she was entrusting me to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein.

"The subordinates in the banquet hall will be able to escape on their own, so you don't need to worry about them. Get out of here first."

It was speech and behavior that the Cecilia I knew would never have exhibited.

Left speechless, I barely managed to open my lips.

"...What about you, Noona?"

At my short counter-question, Cecilia smiled faintly.

"I was invited as a guest."

Meaning she couldn't go outside.

Even though she had simply received a Number Tag and put it on, it was an absurd state of affairs.

"No. We're all leaving together."

Even if I had to give up on the coverage, I couldn't leave Cecilia behind in a place that was obviously dangerous.

And in the banquet hall, not only Je Hyun-oh, but CEO Cha was there as well.

To begin with, I didn't want to leave CEO Cha, who despised divine entities, among the gods.

"Captains. Lieutenant Colonel Je has come outside as well. So..."

"No."

I tried to suggest a new plan, but Mo Haein resolutely cut me off.

And staring straight at me, she said,

"We have to leave right now."

Mo Haein held out a mask toward me.

However, my gaze went to her hands before the mask.

As I looked at her hands shaking pitifully, Mo Haein applied pressure to her fingertips.

She pressed hard enough to turn her knuckles white, forcefully stopping the tremors.

I slowly raised my eyes to look at Mo Haein, and then at Kwak Hanmuk's face.

Who on earth had tormented my subordinates?

Though they were maintaining their composure, they couldn't hide their hardened gazes.

I lowered my gaze back to the mask.

I felt as if I had been hit on the head.

“...!”

It was the mask the Eyeball Alien had been wearing.

I couldn't understand why it was in the captains' hands.

What on earth could have happened while I was staying inside the screen...

It was when I was blankly staring down at the mask.

“Goyo. I'll explain later, so let's move for now.”

I looked at Kwak Hanmuk. When our eyes met, he grinned as if it were nothing.

“If you can't move, should I carry you on my back?”

His playful words made me realize.

The captains were the same. Just like Cecilia, they were trying to send only me outside.

No, they were trying to help me escape, ‘even if it meant just me.’

It was something I could never accept.

The moment I was about to retort, the holographic screen showing the night view of Hyper Eden shifted, and a cheerful voice rang out.

<br>

“Ladies and gentlemen!”

<br>

Everyone turned to look at the holographic screen.

As if their gazes were forcibly captured even in this time-critical situation.

The holographic screen showed the banquet hall, which had been turned into a shambles.

The banquet hall, where black and white constantly intersected, was no different from a ruin.

As soon as I disappeared, the gods' targets were fixed on Je Hyun-oh.

They must have judged that if they just caught Je Hyun-oh, they could summon me back.

The masks the gods wore rattled constantly as if they were about to fall off at any moment.

Amidst the precarious tension, when the Black Block's voice echoed, everyone stopped moving.

Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha also came to a momentary halt.

<br>

*“Everyone is spending a night full of joy, ecstasy, and rapture! It is a magnificent night, but unfortunately, the hide-and-seek is over! That is because a guest has found the young god first!”*

<br>

Black Block cheerfully declared.

<br>

*“It is none other than number 6!”*

<br>

Along with those words, a spotlight clicked and shone down on CEO Cha.

In the light beating down like the protagonist of a stage, CEO Cha looked up into the air while gripping Daeil Yeorae.

He probably seemed to be looking for the location of the speaker.

However, I could tell. This was not a voice coming through a machine.

It was a sound directly created by Black Block within his domain.

<br>

*“As promised, we will give you a tremendously great prize!”*

<br>

Even though a god's mouth promised a great prize, CEO Cha showed no reaction.

The sight of him adjusting his grip on Daeil Yeorae looked less like he was about to receive a prize and more like he was about to receive a punishment.

<br>

*“And the rest of the guests excluding number 6... Ah, my disappointment is huge. You should show a cultured appearance in front of the young god... Such vulgar, reckless, and barbarical behaviors!”*

<br>

Black Block stopped hurling accusations and suddenly let out a chuckle, heh, hehehe.

A sense of ominous dread crept over me.

<br>

*“It can't be helped! So that we are not ashamed before the young god, we shall inflict a rational and appropriate punishment!”*

<br>

Cheers and applause erupted.

It was a sound made by no one in the banquet hall. But accompanied by shouts that shook the entire space...

The public execution began.

Fluorescent sky-blue flashes flickered from the bodies of the gods, and in an instant, they began to disintegrate into various shapes.

When the neck of the person who had turned black and white was severed, the letters making up his existence surged like a fountain.

His body collapsed, spewing out movable type instead of blood.

Stretching and dissolving into a glitch like a broken film, his existence was compressed like a photograph, torn to shreds, and overwritten and erased by censorship text reading ‘Adults Only’.

The spectacle of hundreds of beings perishing simultaneously without offering any resistance fell short of being described as a living hell.

Yet, no screams were heard. The uproarious laughter of non-existent spectators replaced all sound.

Only desperate, clamoring gestures were visible on the screen.

I stood frozen, unable to move, gazing at the sight.

I had already guessed that Black Block was a powerful Outer god.

However, the power to treat hundreds of other gods like mere toys...

It was when I was completely out of my wits at the sight of them being offered up like living sacrifices.

Suddenly, a chill ran down my spine.

It was because Cecilia, too, as a guest, had received the Number Tag 666.

However, she did not act like the other guests.

Instead, she had even taught me social dancing as my partner, so she would be fine.

I tore my eyes away from the screen and hurriedly checked on Cecilia.

She remained intact.

Just as the strength was about to drain completely from my whole body in relief...

“...!”

I saw scattering pixel fragments.

Cecilia was shattering.

Her body breaking apart into dot graphics.

“Noona.”

I called out to Cecilia. She answered me.

Yet, even though her moving lips were calling my name, no sound was heard.

Only a black box floated beneath her.

Stiff pixel text, like dialogue from a game, was outputted.

> “Roasted Chestnut.”

Her lips moved in silence once again.

> “My Castãnita. I'm sorry.”

I reached out my hand.

But I caught nothing.

Dot graphic fragments slipped through my fingers.

Once again, dialogue appeared.

> “I'm sorry for leaving you behind alone.”

That was the end.

Cecilia crumbled away without a trace.

Not even a single eyeball was left behind.
