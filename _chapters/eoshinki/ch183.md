<br>I stared into empty space.

I thought I had grasped it, but when I opened my palm, not a single fragment remained.

Staring blankly ahead, I slowly lowered my hand.

I blinked once, but my field of vision remained completely empty.

Warmth slowly drained away from my fingertips.

I was certain of it.

*'He killed her on purpose.'*

Black Block had seen me clearing Trials every step of the way.

He must have naturally known that I valued NPCs as well.

Targeting NPCs after subordinates, he kept creating situations meant to shake me up.

Earlier, enraged by his interference with my subordinate, I had even faced the crisis of almost losing my sense of self to Young god.

And because of that, I had ended up half-merged with Young god.

It was likely the same this time.

Black Block wanted me to be violently shaken emotionally and let go of my humanity.

I had no desire to move as he wished.

I held onto my reason and struggled to think calmly.

Bringing Cecilia back with a physical regeneration package was impossible.

It was because her entire body had crumbled into pixel graphics.

Even if a fragment of her body had remained by some slim chance, it would still have been difficult.

Even if I somehow managed to scrape together the money to buy the package, the moment working-class people used it, they plummeted straight to Level F.

In Hyper Eden, Level F was waste unable to conduct any activities within the city.

Besides, there was an even more fatal problem.

Level Fs cast out of the city gradually transformed into monsters.

The monsters that Mystery Stringers covered were once Level F laborers.

That was why Cecilia had somehow sent me outside the city when I became Level F.

Level Fs could not even use transport ships originally, yet she had spent a fortune to smuggle me out.

*'Level F won't do.'*

If I became Level F, it wouldn't just end as my own problem.

If the Trial was not closed, COF would explode.

*If Black Block invaded*, a tiny planet like Earth would be devoured in an instant, and that would simply mean total doom.

I considered a second method.

Resurrection....

But that was also impossible.

It was not a skill I could use at will just because I wanted to.

Setting aside using power of a god, there was the condition that it had to be 'my domain'.

Unless I subdued Black Block and turned Hyper Eden into my domain, it was impossible.

I hurriedly tried to come up with other ideas, but the conclusion of impossibility only followed one after another.

In between my efforts to think rationally, memories of Cecilia continuously surfaced.

I hadn't been close with her from the beginning.

Cecilia and I had maintained a moderate business relationship as a stringer and an information broker.

It was Cecilia who crossed the line first.

She had found me throwing fists with another stringer over coverage data.

When I pulled a data chip from a broken camera and asked how much she would buy it for, Cecilia burst into loud laughter.

> “For such a little guy, you've got quite the temper, huh?”

From then on, we gradually became close.

I went on several reporting assignments where the conditions were just right for dying, assignments that other stringers wouldn't even look at.

In truth, I only had to do them because they were game quests.

However, Cecilia always worried about me and helped me every time.

> "I can't let you go alone."

Though we crossed the boundaries of life and death together, she had never left me behind and gone on alone in any situation.

She always put me first and protected me.

On the day I succeeded in a reporting assignment that no other stringer had managed to pull off, the toast we shared on the rooftop of Cecilia's house remained vivid in my memory.

Clinking cheap beer cans together, she and I rejoiced and also had a good time.

> "Roasted Chestnut. You'll be able to climb the Tower. You're going to become the greatest first-generation stringer!"

What had I replied back then?

*‘I said I wanted to go up together.’*

Not knowing what terrible things might happen, I had said I wanted to go with Cecilia.

It was the same now.

Because I had asked Cecilia to help me….

She had met death once again. This time, it was a death from which there was no turning back.

I panted heavily, struggling for breath.

I felt a gaze.

It was the gaze of an entity watching me. The watcher was waiting.

An auditory hallucination echoed in my ears….

<br>

*"Roasted Chestnut."*

<br>

A voice brimming with deep sympathy called out to me.

<br>

*"You must be terribly upset. It's alright."*

<br>

Soothing me to the utmost as if he knew my heart inside and out, he presented me with a solution.

<br>

*"Roasted Chestnut, you know what needs to be done, don't you? There is no need to be afraid. Because I am always watching over Roasted Chestnut. Just like I always have."*

<br>

He subtly persuaded me, promising to back me up no matter what happened.

I genuinely felt a strong temptation at Dominic's words.

Is there any need to feel a sense of aversion just because things go the way Black Block wants?

Is there any reason to be scared that my humanity might shatter and never return?

*‘I might be able to pull it off again.’*

It is an extremely small possibility, and highly dangerous, but still, if I use the power of a god.

I might be able to find a way to bring Cecilia back.

Because a god would be able to do what a human cannot….

"Goyo."

"……."

"Goyo."

"……."

"Han Goyo!"

"…Ah."

I came to my senses.

My entire body was soaked in cold sweat. Kwak Hanmuk was staring at me, gripping my shoulder tightly.

"Captain Kwak…."

I called out to him with trembling lips, then looked down at the gazes piercing into my face.

Mo Haein was watching me without blinking once.

Her gaze clearly conveyed that I shouldn't do anything reckless over an NPC.

However, to me, Cecilia was not merely a simple NPC.

"Don't do it."

I hadn't said anything, but Mo Haein spoke first.

"Getting out of here is the top priority."

"Yes…."

Her words were the rationally correct judgment.

I answered, but her words didn't properly register in my ears.

I muttered with a slightly foolish expression.

"But Captains…."

The dizziness I had been feeling since earlier grew stronger. I felt like surroundings had become brighter.

"I think I'm a bit strange…."

Trailing off, I touched the wall with my hand and staggered.

The part I touched turned into Supernova Pang Pang, a popping candy, and collapsed with a clatter.

Surprised, I looked down at my hand, but it was fine.

Candy packets just kept appearing continuously.

Along with that, sparse holes formed in the space around me.

The places where holes were punched distorted into pixel art shapes and repeated returning to normal.

Kwak Hanmuk spat a curse and called Mo Haein.

"Fuck, Captain Mo!"

Mo Haein immediately pulled out the Black Moon Sword, and Kwak Hanmuk abruptly gave me a piggyback ride.

He wrapped his body with prayer beads in a blur and started running.

Spaces were distorting all over the ordinary hallway, creating traps.

Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein ran while avoiding the pixel art traps.

Supernova Pang Pang dropping with a thud behind Kwak Hanmuk stretched out long in a line as if marking footsteps.

Bound at the waist by prayer beads and being carried on a piggyback, I glanced at the popping candy packets constantly popping out of my hand, then stared at the distorting spaces.

*'What is this.'*

However, it was bothersome to think about complicated things.

For now, it didn't seem dangerous to my subordinates.

Neither the subordinate carrying me nor the subordinate running alongside me turned into pixel art.

I thought, hanging limply on the subordinate's back.

*'I just want to break everything.'*

If I smash everything and remake it all….

"Chestnut."

"……."

"Chestnut?"

"……."

"Master Chestnut?"

"…Huh?"

When I answered while in a daze, for some reason my subordinate let out a sigh.

"Ah, this is driving me crazy."

Muttering to himself, he stopped his feet. It was because of a voice that sounded like it would burst eardrums.

<br>

*"Little Lamb!!"*

<br>

An urgent voice called me. At the voice of Black Block, a faint agitation stirred among my subordinates.

I could feel the body of the subordinate carrying me stiffen.

<br>

*"I am sorry! I even killed the toy cherished by Little Lamb! It was a huge mistake!"*

<br>

I showed no reaction, and the subordinates ignored the voice and started running again.

Then, a hologram with a 'STOP' sign written on it appeared and blocked the way.

My subordinate immediately swung a long and sharp weapon, splitting the hologram.

The subordinates sprinted fast, but the hallway grew bizarrely long.

Stretching out so far that the end was not in sight, the voice was heard repeatedly.

<br>

*"Little Lamb, my star! It truly was a mistake. I was only trying to make a gift for Little Lamb! Will you hate me?"*

<br>

Saying that, Black Block crazily created obstacles blocking my way.

Walls made of newspapers sprouted everywhere.

On them, pictures of Cecilia and me were printed.

The two of us side by side eating hamburgers and chewing, the appearance of getting caught by guards, barely photographing interview material, and teleporting in the nick of time, and the appearance of me dozing off sitting on a sofa after finishing an all-night interview and Cecilia sleeping with her head on my lap….

Articles below the photographs detailed every single thing that had happened between her and me.

<br>

*“To think that I tore apart such an affectionate relationship! I have committed a capital crime!”*

<br>

My subordinate, who had been about to slice down the wall of newspapers using a weapon, paused.

Her eyes scanned the photograph and the article before looking at me for a moment.

Then, as if snapping back to reality belatedly, she swung her weapon.

However, the speed at which they newly generated was faster than the speed at which they were wiped out.

In the end, the subordinates had no choice but to stop their running feet, blocked by the wall of newspapers.

<br>

*“I beg of you. Please give me a chance to make amends! I will bring her back to life!”*

<br>

I reached out my hand forward and replied.

“Shut up.”

The golden light extending from my fingertips struck the wall of newspapers.

The newspaper turned into dot graphics and collapsed in a clatter of pixel blocks.

<br>

*“Aaaah! Little Lamb!!”*

<br>

After destroying all the walls in a single breath, I spoke to my subordinates.

“Let us go to the Tower.”

At that, the subordinates wore slightly flustered expressions.

As I simply stared at them intently, not knowing why they were acting that way, the small subordinate gripped the weapon in one hand, looked at me, and then glanced sideways at the red-haired subordinate.

It was a gaze filled with bewilderment, not knowing what to do or how to act.

The red-haired subordinate called out to me.

“Master.”

“Yeah.”

“What is my name?”

“……”

I could not answer.

Name? Is something like that important? What matters is that you are my possession.

I will always cherish and love you all equally, after all.

However, the complexions of my subordinates darkened all at once. It was just as I was carefully observing their reactions.

“Captain Mo, front!”

Simultaneously with the red-haired subordinate shouting, the small subordinate fiercely swung the weapon.

A hologram hand that had been reaching toward me was sliced by the blade and vanished.

However, following that, a thick band inscribed with a TV news headline appeared and wrapped tightly all around us.

Along the red band, white letters announcing a breaking news bulletin scrolled by.

<br>

“Damn it, Chestnut…. Master Chestnut.”

In the meantime, the red-haired subordinate said something strange to me.

“What are you going to do if you even forget your own name, huh?”

I tilted my head slightly. Subordinate uttered yet another incomprehensible remark.

“You shouldn't forget things like that, you know.”

Why?

I simply could not understand at all.

As I pondered over what could have made my subordinate upset, the small subordinate came close to my side and spoke.

“Training…, um, first of all, let us go outside.”

“Why?”

At my counter-question, the small subordinate was briefly at a loss for words. Then the red-haired subordinate answered in her stead.

“Master. If we fight that bastard, it feels like it’s gonna hurt like crazy, you see?”

“I am strong, too.”

“…No, I mean.”

Could it be that my subordinates considered me weak?

I moved my hand once again.

The red band turned into dot graphics and crumbled down with a clatter.

Thinking this was not enough, I altered the space a bit more.

A door materialized upon a wall where nothing had existed. The door, composed of square pixels, felt incongruous.

When I flicked my hand, the door opened by itself.

I could feel my subordinates flinching in surprise at the place beyond the door.

It was the outside of the building.

It connected two separated spaces. The spaces in between must have been broken, though...

*'It doesn't matter.'*

The subordinates panicked, but rushed outside first.

As soon as they went outside, a barrage of camera flashes poured down.

Along with flashing lights, the sound of camera shutters echoed like mad.

All the stringers gathered around the banquet hall had rushed over.

The red recording lights of the cameras caught the eye.

Right now, every single moment of this should be broadcasting across the mass media of Hyper Eden.

I looked at those aiming for provocative scoop material and thought.

*'It's noisy.'*

They were as annoying as bugs.

*I turned off the radio, closed the newspaper, and paused the video.*

The radio broadcast that had been noisily chattering about the deeds of Young god suddenly emitted only static noise, as if it had lost its frequency.

The newspapers all burst into confetti of dot graphics with a pop, and censorship phrases floated above the screens reporting exclusive scoops.

I waved my hand lightly as reporters holding broken cameras panicked and screamed.

They turned into dot graphics and crumbled away.

I looked at Tower.

I feel like I could change that too...

It was just as I tried to slowly reach out my hand.

"Han Goyo!!"

A loud shout came from behind.

At the same time, the subordinate carrying me twisted my wrist with prayer beads, and the small subordinate also urgently grabbed my forearm.

"...?"

I looked down at my body in bewilderment.

Cracks broken in pure golden light were crudely tangled all over it.
