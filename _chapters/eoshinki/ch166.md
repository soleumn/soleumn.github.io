<br>Dead?

They were deceased individuals transformed into game characters.

I stared blankly at the pixelated figures.

I wanted to know more. What these people meant to Kwak Hanmuk. Why they were so precious to him.

I wanted to dig into every detail and uncover the truth.

Even if they were already dead, their importance to him shouldn't outweigh his feelings for me.

After all, subordinates are meant to look only to their master.

As I glared at the screen, a speech bubble with an eyeball icon appeared over Kwak Hanmuk's head.

He then formed a circle with his hand and held it over his eye like a monocle.

<br>

Well... I do want to pluck them out...

But I knew I couldn't.

As if reading my hesitation, Kwak Hanmuk burst into laughter.

A smiling speech bubble appeared, and his character approached my hand.

Then, it patted me gently. It felt like a gesture of encouragement.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  He thought it was best to trust and follow the Young god.
</div>

<br>

I read the words again.

*It was only natural for my subordinates to like me. They must have recognized my greatness even more now.*

Even though it was a given... I still couldn't believe it. I felt a strange, indescribable emotion.

Kwak Hanmuk's character looked up at me.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Kwak Hanmuk wanted to know if the Special Team was safe.
</div>

<br>

Mo Haein had already cleared it, and now only Je Hyun-oh remained.

I was debating whether to send this as a message when text appeared.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Kwak Hanmuk is certain that Han Goyo, as the Young god, will save them.
</div>

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Just as he had saved him.
</div>

<br>

I stared at the pixelated letters for a long time.

I looked at the characters made of tiny squares over and over again.

As I stared, unable to look away, Kwak Hanmuk gestured to me.

A speech bubble with a dot-graphic cake appeared over his head, and he mimed punching the air.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Kwak Hanmuk wants to go eat cake with the god.
</div>

<br>

My lips curved upward. I couldn't help but smile.

I wanted to grant my subordinate's wish.

And I wanted to achieve what the Special Team hoped for.

I slowly withdrew my hand.

My hand vanished from the screen.

Kwak Hanmuk's character waved at me as if in greeting.

The other characters were politely expressing their gratitude. The opposing player's characters and his hostages, all represented in pixel art, were especially so. Some knelt in prayer, while others raised their arms in praise.

After Kwak Hanmuk said something to his former teammates, they snapped to attention and saluted me.

Finally, the young boy...

He had been clutching Kwak Hanmuk's sleeve, but then he gave me a small wave.

The screen changed.

Electronic fanfare blared as pixelated fireworks exploded.

Clear.

I had been worried because it ended so abnormally, but thankfully, it was cleared without a hitch.

Letters announcing a new high score appeared on the screen, displaying my record.

Once again, my score was far ahead of the second-place finisher.

But for some reason, I didn't care about the record.

I picked up the neon-blue coin that had rolled out of the coin return slot. It was a Clear Coin with Kwak Hanmuk's pixelated face on it.

"..."

Suddenly, I pressed the back of my hand against my eyes.

The color of my eyes, which had changed when I played Mo Haein's game, still hadn't returned to normal. I realized that the *Young god* had completely merged with me.

Normally, I would have been terrified. I would have been scared of what I might do, afraid I'd cause harm to my team.

But right now, I was fine. In fact, I felt surprisingly good.

Even though I had merged with a god, I wasn't being treated as a calamity. I was still trusted as a member of the Special Team.

*'I have to do this.'*

Just as Kwak Hanmuk had said, I was the only one who could resolve this situation—both as Han Goyo and as the Young god.

I gripped the Clear Coin tightly. I could feel a faint warmth emanating from the flat coin.

"You've become much more proficient in using your power, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic's praise reached my ears, his voice brimming with satisfaction. He squished himself against my cheek, as if pleased.

He seemed to have thoroughly enjoyed watching me intervene in the game world.

*'What does Dominic see in me now?'*

He probably thinks I've grown much closer to becoming a god.

"Dominic."

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

"I think we only need one more Clear. Let's get out of here quickly."

At my words, he let out a low chuckle for some reason.

"I can wait."

His reply felt ominous.

I let the Pudding Slime roam my face as it pleased, searching for the game console where Je Hyun-oh was trapped.

My heart was racing. I couldn't stop thinking about what he had told me before.

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">"I hate waiting."</span>

<br>

Unlike the Captains, he was someone who might do anything. My anxiety spiked as I remembered the last time I was trapped in PTB with CEO Cha—Je Hyun-oh had torn through the Trial just to get to me.

*'Could he have already broken out?'*

I scanned my surroundings, but saw no dark figures. Assuming he must be furious, I hurried through the rows of arcade machines.

Finally, I spotted him at the very last console in the corner.

It was a flight shooting game.

It was a flight shooting game where the player controlled a fighter jet, moving forward while dodging attacks from all sides and destroying enemies. Like a classic arcade game, the screen was a top-down view.

But the fighter jet the player was supposed to control was nowhere to be seen on the pixelated screen.

Similarly, Je Hyun-oh was nowhere to be seen.

As I stared at the screen, I was momentarily speechless. Dominic described the situation in a calm, composed tone.

"It seems one of Chestnut's subordinates has caused some trouble."

The world inside the game was a complete mess.

The space that served as the background was torn open in several places.

Beyond the tears, other games were visible.

Fighting games, racing games, action, sports, puzzles...

Countless different types of games were partially revealed through the cracks.

These were the traces of attempted escapes.

Yet, despite my frantic search, Je Hyun-oh was nowhere to be found.

*'Where could he be?'*

I scanned the screen again, searching every inch. The black-and-white space background made his dark silhouette nearly invisible. I sat back in my chair, feeling like I was playing a game of 'Hidden Objects', but I still couldn't find him.

Just as I was starting to think it was strange that I couldn't see him, even after looking this hard...

"...!"

In the far left corner of the screen, I spotted a black figure emerging from a crack that had been obscured by the health bar.

He had entered another game and just come back out.

I quickly checked the status of the flapping pixel character. He was too small to see clearly, but outwardly, he didn't seem to be injured.

Only after the relief of finally finding Je Hyun-oh washed over me did other thoughts begin to surface.

*'Wait, the games were connected?'*

By tearing through space in the universe, he had linked it to another game, and Je Hyun-oh had been crossing between them.

As I re-examined the game's background, which was now practically shredded, I noticed something strange.

Je Hyun-oh's character, who had been flapping his wings, was now stumbling.

Then, at some point, he stopped moving.

Since the game was set in space, stopping his wings didn't make him fall. But Je Hyun-oh just remained there, motionless. As if it didn't matter if he fell or not.

The moment I saw the pixelated character floating alone on the screen, I quickly inserted a coin into the machine. Then, to announce my presence, I moved the joystick on the control panel.

Je Hyun-oh's character moved up slightly.

But there was no reaction. Not even an exclamation mark or a question mark popped up in a speech bubble above his head. I tried moving the joystick a bit more, making him travel a longer distance, but the result was the same.

Dominic let out a displeased "Hmm," his annoyance clear. From his perspective, Je Hyun-oh must have been acting incredibly arrogant, but he didn't say it to me.

Even if he had mentioned it, I wouldn't have heard it properly. I was that flustered.

I scrambled to try everything I could.

First, I cast Divine Blessing.

A golden veil of pixelated graphics draped over his head, but Je Hyun-oh remained unresponsive.

I pressed the joystick and buttons, typing out a message letter by letter.

<br>

<br>

But the result was the same. There was no reaction. No speech bubble appeared, nor did any text.

I even tried casting a restriction, but it had the same effect.

*'Was this related to how badly he had broken the game world?'*

I was panicking, having never anticipated such a situation, when text finally appeared beneath Je Hyun-oh.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh does not desire the touch of the god.
</div>

<br>

In that instant, an unbearable surge of emotion welled up within me.

*Why?*

Even if he didn't want me, he still belonged to me. He wasn't allowed to refuse or push me away.

"...Hah."

I let out a short breath and closed my eyes.

I tried to calm my turbulent emotions. I strove to think not as a young god, but as Han Goyo.

Once I regained my composure, I reassessed the situation.

Je Hyun-oh had torn the place apart. The numerous cracks he'd created made it seem impossible to proceed with the game normally.

I had to figure out how to fix this first.

Taking a deep breath, I reached my hand into the screen.

At that, Je Hyun-oh's pixelated character moved just a tiny bit.

He lifted his head and looked at my hand.

But that was all. After a brief glance, he averted his gaze as if he'd already lost interest.

Leaving Je Hyun-oh behind, I diligently repaired the game world.

At first, I wondered if I could even do such a thing, but my hands moved as if by instinct. Just as I had changed Mo Haein's sky, I gently stroked the jagged cracks. Then, the torn space began to mend itself, sliding back together.

The repairs took a considerable amount of time. Je Hyun-oh had torn apart too much of the space. Moreover, I spent even more time being careful not to accidentally touch him.

Once I had mended every last trace, the world returned to its original state—a shimmering cosmic backdrop. I had never done anything like this before, yet I seemed to have fixed it well.

Satisfied, I stole a glance at Je Hyun-oh. He remained completely still, as if his power had been shut off. I couldn't just leave him like this if I wanted to pull him out of the game.

With no other choice, I cautiously reached out to Je Hyun-oh.

It was a desperate gesture, hoping for any kind of response.

But the moment I extended my hand, I flinched, trembling all over.

In a flash, Je Hyun-oh had lunged, driving his metal hand into mine. Then, he dragged it down.

A sharp sting shot through me as a long gash tore across my skin.

The thought that he would attack me had never even crossed my mind. Paralyzed, I could only recoil, my hand clutched tight.

In that instant, Je Hyun-oh unleashed a merciless assault on my hand.

When he pinned me against the black crescent moon and turned me into a pincushion, the pain was so intense it brought tears to my eyes.

Still, I felt a flicker of relief that he hadn't severed any of my fingers.

He only ceased his attack after my hand was a bloody mess.

Above Je Hyun-oh's head, a speech bubble appeared, containing only "...".

It was the first time he had shown any reaction. Just as I was about to seize the opportunity and try anything, a pixelated character fluttered its wings slowly and landed on the back of my hand.

Then, it drove a metallic feather deep into my skin and displayed a message:

<br>

  Je Hyun-oh desires the Divine Blessing. He believes the god must grant his wish without fail.

<br>

Though I winced in pain, I cast the Divine Blessing over him.

Once he was draped in the golden veil, Je Hyun-oh slowly pulled the metallic feather from the back of my hand.

For a moment, he stood beneath the veil, and then new words appeared below him:

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  Je Hyun-oh no longer wishes to wait. He believes the god must grant his wish without fail.
</div>

<br>

*'I'm sorry I'm late,'* I apologized fervently in my mind.

Immediately, more pixelated text scrolled across the screen.

<br>

<div class='f-vt323' style="background-color: var(--bg-card) !important; border: 1px solid var(--borda-suave); font-size: 1.5rem !important; padding: 10px !important; margin: 0 !important;">
  It was because he had waited for so, so long. Je Hyun-oh had waited. He waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited. Waited....
</div>

<br>

The word 'Waited' continued endlessly.

I stared at the words with wide, bewildered eyes.

It was true that I had taken my time coming to Je Hyun-oh, having been busy clearing Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk's game.

But my subordinate shouldn't have become this unstable.

The pixelated letters that had filled the screen vanished all at once.

All that remained was Je Hyun-oh's dot character standing on the back of my hand, set against a backdrop of the cosmos.

The dot character, its metal hand hanging limp, slowly flapped its wings.

After a moment of silence, a single line of pixelated text reappeared.
