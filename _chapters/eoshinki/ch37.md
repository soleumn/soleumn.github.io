<br>I had always known a day like this would come.

Just as I had seen through the holographic badge in CallOVo, I knew there would be a moment when Dominic would notice the Signet Ring hidden in my inventory.

But I never expected it to be now.

My entire body went rigid.

I didn't want to give the ring back.

Yet, contrary to my will, my body was already moving on its own. As if obeying an irresistible command, I retrieved the ring from my inventory.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>The Masterless Signet Ring: A signet ring once worn by a noble. Any non-master who dons this ring will be overcome with profound sorrow.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

I had known from the moment the butlers handed it to me. This item was never meant for me.

Dominic had already reclaimed a piece of his being. I could feel it—he was different now.

*'What if he changes even more after receiving the signet ring...'*

I gasped for breath, my trembling hand barely able to grip the ring.

*'The best I can do right now is to keep it from him.'*

Seeing my state, Dominic asked softly, "Are you that scared?"

"..."

"It's just an object. Giving it to me won't cause the things you're worried about to happen."

"..."

"Because it truly means nothing to me. But it will be meaningful for you, Roasted Chestnut. If you return the ring, I can do a few things for you."

He whispered gently, mentioning that he could help with things Roasted Chestnut wanted, like opening an interplanetary trade account.

"But if you're still scared, you can put the ring back. Let's just say you'll take it out whenever you're ready to give it to me."

The voice, tinged with a faint smile, was no longer purely gentle.

Dominic spoke like a man who already possessed everything.

"You're going to give it back to me eventually, aren't you?"

I realized it then.

He was pretending to give me a choice, but there was only ever one destined answer.

Resisting now was a meaningless gesture.

Once a master recognized his possession, he was bound to reclaim it, sooner or later.

In truth, Dominic could have taken it by force; he was merely showing me mercy.

Returning it now would be the better move for our future relationship.

Besides, as Dominic had said, a single ring wouldn't be what truly restored his essence.

I parted my lips.

"...Mr. Dominic."

"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."

"The ring... I should have returned it to you long ago. I'm sorry."

"No. You must have gone through a lot of trouble."

Dominic was the one comforting me instead. He even praised me for keeping it safe all this time, knowing how terrifying it must have been to possess such an object. He spoke like a teacher soothing a child who had confessed the truth without lying.

I felt myself becoming infinitely smaller, just listening to him.

After reassuring me for a moment, Dominic said, "Then, I will take it now."

Though I tried not to be tense, my body stiffened on its own.

The ring on my palm melted away, vanishing completely.

When it was gone, a chilling silence descended.

The moment all sound in the world was severed.

Even the light was sucked away, and absolute darkness enveloped the space.

An absolute darkness, woven from the deepest obsidian, enveloped my body, while pure disorder and chaos crushed my mind.

The blinding remnants of a supernova's explosion flashed across my vision.

Hundreds of billions of galaxies swirled and vanished.

The deaths of countless stars.

The screams of those stars, trampled by That Person, poured over me.

*Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary. Scary.*

I wanted to run.

But in the grip of this primal terror, all I could do was endure.

Wait for this horrific calamity to end.

But... was there an end?

*Is there an end to the infinite void?*

"Roasted Chestnut."

"...!"

I jolted awake.

It took a moment to realize I had escaped the endless abyss of the void, and that I was back in this small room.

That fleeting moment had left my mind in tatters.

I stared at Dominic, frozen in place.

He hadn't grown to a monstrous size, nor had he issued a command as my master. He remained a small, squishy slime, speaking to me just as he always had.

"See? I am exactly as I was, Roasted Chestnut."

I think I understood what Dominic wanted. When he had reclaimed the Signet Ring, I had felt the vast, insurmountable gulf between us. But Dominic didn't want me to fear him. He wanted to keep pretending to be my gentle Pudding Slime.

And that was exactly what I wanted, too.

Taking a deep breath, I reached out my hand to him.

His soft, squishy body pressed against mine. The familiar sensation finally allowed me to relax a little.

"Would you wait a moment?"

After asking for my understanding, the Pudding Slime remained still on my hand for a while, as if checking something.

As I waited for him, I thought to myself.

*'What does it mean that he went to look around my planet?'*

He wouldn't have left my side for such a simple reason. Dominic was interested in the Young god, not my domain.

*'He must have had some other purpose...'*

But Dominic would never tell me if I asked. If he were willing to share, he would have already told me.

While I was trying to guess his motives, Dominic finished his check.

He hummed softly and said, "This should be enough."

I hesitated before asking, "Enough for what?"

"Enough to become Roasted Chestnut's sponsor."

The Signet Ring must have been linked to his Cosmic Account.

Dominic asked me politely, "May I access Roasted Chestnut's Galactic Trading Post?"

"Please do."

He then touched a shimmering powder to me, and starlight seeped into my being.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Entering the Galactic Trading Post.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

As we entered, Dominic—who was now sharing my vision—glanced at the System Window and remarked, "It seems a special feature for Young gods has been applied. The Akasha System will be quite accommodating, so I suggest keeping it active."

Then, he chuckled the moment he saw my shopping cart.

His laughter made me feel a little embarrassed.

While I was trapped for 72 hours, I had nothing to do, so I had added everything I wanted to buy to my cart. Even though I had tried to be selective, the list was endless.

The total value of the items in my cart had surpassed the trillion-won mark and was now in the quadrillions.

However, this was also because the items at the Galactic Trading Post were incredibly expensive. Anything even remotely useful cost at least several billion won, and anything decent easily cost tens of billions. The items I truly wanted often ran into the hundreds of billions.

After scanning my shopping cart, Dominic made a proposal.

"How about signing a sponsorship contract with me? I can afford everything in Roasted Chestnut's cart. I'll also be able to do much more for you."

This was the second sponsorship offer I'd received, following the one from Mr. Black Block.

Both of them acted as if they had so many Star Fragments that they could buy me any items I wanted.

It reminded me of the happy times I spent emptying Dominic's wallet in CallOVo.

I asked cautiously.

"I'm already in a contractual relationship with Mr. Dominic. Is a sponsorship contract separate from that?"

"To freely transfer goods or currency from the Galactic Trading Post to Roasted Chestnut, a different type of contract from the current one is required."

He explained that this was because they had to use the Akasha System. He also added that under the current contract, it was difficult to give something without receiving anything in return.

Come to think of it, back in Infernum, I had hunted after receiving quests from Dominic, and during ■■■, my power had partially contributed to the annihilation.

*'A sponsorship contract...'*

The concept of a sponsor was familiar. But why would he sponsor me when I wasn't competing with a Pudding Slime logo on my back?

I couldn't help but ask.

"I'm curious what the god who sponsors me would gain from this contract."

"It depends on the type of contract, but the one I'm offering you, Roasted Chestnut, provides no benefit to me at all."

"What? Then..."

"It simply means our relationship will last a little longer. After all, our current contract has a fixed term."

*'Until Dominic recovers his memories.'*

That was the nature of our current agreement.

But now, if I signed a sponsorship contract, my relationship with Dominic would be extended, and I would receive an immense amount of financial support in return...

The deal seemed too good to be true, and I couldn't shake my suspicion.

Besides, I didn't know what it truly meant for our relationship to be "extended."

In the end, I turned down the sponsorship offer once again.

"For now... I'll try doing this on my own."

"Understood."

As always, Dominic let me have my way.

He even offered to help me open a Cosmic Account. I watched him intently, trying to learn how to do it myself, but my efforts were pointless.

A glitch flickered in the empty air, and suddenly, an account materialized out of nowhere.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    An Akasha-exclusive account has been created for Han Goyo of Planet Earth.
</div>

I was just wondering if this was normal when another message appeared.

<div class="system-window system-content" style="text-align: center; box-shadow: -3px -3px 0px #ff0055, 3px 3px 0px #00fffa !important; color: var(--texto-principal); text-shadow: -1px -1px 0 #ff0055, 1px 1px 0 #00fffa; font-weight: bold;">
    Dark Star Remnants have been deposited into the account.
</div>

The Dark Star Remnants that had been sitting quietly in my inventory were automatically transferred to the account.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>Deposit Notification: 10,000,000,000 Star Fragments</strong>
  </div>
</div>

My eyes widened as I stared at the number of Star Fragments displayed in the System Window.

*'Ten billion...?'*

Before I could even process the staggering amount, a piercing alarm blared.

*WEEEEEEENG!*

Suddenly, a warning alarm blared, and an urgent System Window popped up.

<div class="system-window" style="text-align: center;">
  <div class="system-content">
    <strong>&lt;Notice of Abnormal Transaction Detection and Account Freeze><br><br>
    An unauthorized high-value fund inflow, drastically deviating from your typical transaction patterns, has been detected in your account. Emergency security measures have been implemented.<br><br>
    - Inflow Amount: 10,000,000,000 Star Fragments<br>
    - Sender Code: Unidentifiable (Unauthorized Method)<br>
    - Action Taken: Planetary assets frozen; all withdrawals, transfers, and payments completely blocked.<br><br>
    To lift these emergency security measures, please provide proof of the source of these funds.</strong>
  </div>
</div>

...Account freeze?

The Pudding Slime I had trusted had just stabbed me in the back.

I called out to Dominic, who was sharing my vision.

"Dominic...?"

I think I'm screwed. What do I do?

That was the sentiment behind my call, but Dominic replied as if he had no idea what was going on.

"I didn't expect Roasted Chestnut to have this much cash. You won't be able to use it for the time being."

Apparently, unless you set it up separately, any funds in your inventory are automatically deposited when you open an account.

The way he spoke, as if it were his own mistake, was highly suspicious.

I couldn't help but think he'd deliberately kept this from me because I hadn't signed a sponsorship contract.

My ten billion star fragments...

"Still, it never hurts to know the Administrator. Especially in the case of someone as young as you, Roasted Chestnut."

Dominic was saying something, but I couldn't really hear him.

My star fragments were flying away right before my eyes.

For once, I felt like I could crush a Pudding Slime to death.

Just as I was staring blankly at the account freeze notification, a new square window appeared, and a stranger materialized.

A forced video call connected without my consent.

Startled, I stared at the screen. A strange alien appeared.

The alien had long hair that flowed in the air, swirling with the colors of a nebula—teal and purple. Its skin was a stark white, and its features were androgynous, making it impossible to discern its gender.

It wore a stifling, high-collared military coat. The hands emerging from the sleeves were made of a transparent, crystal-like mineral, with light constantly circulating within them like fiber optics. Its eyes, with diamond-shaped pupils, darted around restlessly, as if analyzing something.

"This is the Galactic Trading Post Administrator. We have initiated this communication to proceed with an explanation procedure regarding the abnormal transactions detected in your account. We expect your full cooperation."

Hearing the voice, I lowered my guard slightly.

The stiff, dry tone was nothing more than that of an exhausted office worker.

So, I just have to explain things to this alien?

A spark of hope flared—maybe I wouldn't have to lose ten billion won after all.

Since this was my first time meeting an alien other than Dominic or Mr. Black Block, I decided to start with a greeting.

"Hello."

As I bowed politely, the Administrator, who had been staring into the void, finally looked at me.

A flicker of surprise crossed his face.

After a moment of silence, he spoke again, his voice now noticeably softer.

—Kid. Is there an adult on the planet? Can you go get one for me?

I almost turned to look at Dominic on my shoulder.

Restraining myself, I replied to the Administrator, "I am the Master of the Planet."

The Administrator hadn't noticed Dominic.

He seemed to be a high-ranking official, at least for a Trading Post manager, yet Dominic had apparently concealed his presence. For some reason, Dominic must have decided it was better to remain unseen.

The moment I answered, the Administrator shoved his face toward the screen. His diamond pupils dilated wildly, and his hair billowed out in all directions.

*<big>—You’re—the—Master—of—the—Planet—? Without—a—Guardian—?!</big>*

His voice, previously heavy with exhaustion, now boomed with shock.

I flinched slightly as I replied, "Yes... I'm sorry, but... what do I need to do to unfreeze my account?"

But the Administrator wasn't listening to me at all. He was frantically moving his hair like a hundred hands, while his crystal hand tapped the air incessantly.

Light flickered in the air wherever he touched.

—Planet Earth... Akasha has just begun... Child filter applied... To think they entrusted a planet to this little one. They must be insane. A Young god is such a precious being...

He muttered to himself as he checked the data, then announced to me:

—Kid. I'm going to take a look at your Galactic Trading Post transaction history.

And before I could even answer, he opened my records without permission.

Of course, my transaction history was practically empty.

—First transaction... 10 Event Star Fragments...

The Administrator's hair drooped sadly. He looked at me with a somewhat devastated expression.

When I just stared back blankly, the Administrator sighed and asked in a surprisingly gentle tone.

—Where did you get the Dark Star Remnants? Did someone force you to do something bad?

I pondered for a moment.

*'How should I answer this?'*

The problem was that the amount of Dark Star currency was far larger than I had anticipated. A massive sum had been deposited into my account immediately after its creation, without any proper transaction history. This was bound to trigger a red flag in the System.

If I couldn't explain this properly, my ten billion star fragments would likely be reduced to cosmic dust.

I wished Dominic would whisper a hint to me, but he just squished around on my shoulder.

I slowly opened my mouth.

"I received it for doing cleaning work. My employers took a liking to me."

I decided to be honest, fearing that lying would only make things worse if I were caught.

At that, the Administrator looked at me with even more pity in his eyes.

It seemed the gods of other planets didn't do things like cleaning part-time jobs....

—Dark Star Remnants are a rare currency, so I need to verify this. Where did you do the cleaning, kid?

This was the first I'd heard of them being a rare currency. I'd assumed they were a common currency since they were usable throughout the entire universe.

*'How am I supposed to explain that I got this from a Trial?'*

The Administrator probably wouldn't even recognize the name of the now-defunct Clean3, but I decided to tell him the truth anyway.

"I cleaned a mansion...."

*<big>—A-mansion-?</big>*

The moment the word left my lips, he reacted strongly.

The Administrator shoved his face back into the screen and asked a question I never expected.

—Kid, did you get this from the Cat and Rabbit Butlers?
