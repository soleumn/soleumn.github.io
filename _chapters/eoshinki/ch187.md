“Then why do you keep doing this?”

“Little Lamb! I am certainly not tormenting Little Lamb, nor Little Lamb's subordinates! I have prepared this for my star!”

<big>“■■■■■■! ■■■■■■■■■!”</big>

“Please believe me! It's going to be a very interesting thing for Little Lamb! Of course, <small><small>it might hurt a little and be a bit tough</small></small>, but….”

If judged with human reasoning, it was easy to interpret them in the wrong sense.

It was likely the same now.

There was more than enough possibility that the phrase ‘interesting’ held a meaning I couldn't guess.

I had thought so ever since I saw the spectacle of all sorts of Han Goyos gathered in the Tower, but he seemed to want to collect various facets of me.

Even the moments when I was miserable.

*'The System Window even issued a warning, so it won't just be a little painful and tough.'*

It was a probability of 0.002%.

Even though I had entered fully prepared, I was slightly scared. Because there were those I needed to protect.

I hurriedly turned my thoughts over and over.

“All the ingredients here are ready as well…. It would be fine to throw them all in and boil a stew…. This is the moment when Little Lamb must keep your promise to me….”

“You're the one who broke the promise, Black Block.”

“Return my subordinates and Sponsor safely, and bring Cecilia back to life.”
