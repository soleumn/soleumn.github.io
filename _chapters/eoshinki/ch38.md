<br>This time, I was the one who was stunned.

As my face hardened, the Administrator's expression turned to one of regret, and he pulled his head back.

—I'm sorry. You must have been surprised... I'm not trying to scold you, I'm just asking to confirm.

The Administrator waited for my answer, pressing down on his flowing hair with his hand.

*'How does the Galactic Trading Post Administrator know the Rabbit Butler and the Cat Butler?'*

Until now, I had thought that when a Trial was closed, the NPCs within it also disappeared. I assumed that since the Meteorites of Trial vanished, everything else vanished with them.

But... thinking back, the Butlers from Clean3 were NPCs beyond the norm.

They had asked me for my Cosmic Account and requested the code for Interstellar Communication. They were already utilizing the civilization of Akasha.

It was entirely possible that they existed outside the Trials.

I also knew of a case where an NPC from a closed Trial was still alive.

*'I saw DeZonDeal's Paranoid Researcher in PTB.'*

If NPCs didn't completely vanish just because a Trial was closed...

And if they could exist outside as well...

Smiley, the researcher siblings, Edel, the Fire Dragon Twins, the Creatures...

They might also exist somewhere in the universe.

Of course, the probability was slim.

I didn't even know what conditions had to be met to escape a Trial.

Still, the important thing was that it wasn't 0%.

Every time I said goodbye, I'd only felt a sense of hopelessness. Now, I could at least hold onto a sliver of hope.

At the same time, a new thought occurred to me.

*'Then what does "closure" actually mean?'*

*'No... what exactly is a Trial in the first place?'*

I felt as if I had reached a fundamental question.

But it was a problem that couldn't be solved with the knowledge I had right now.

First, I answered the Administrator, who was waiting.

"The Dark Star Remnant... the Butlers gave it to me."

As soon as he heard my answer, the Administrator nodded as if he'd expected it, yet he also seemed surprised.

—*Those guys, of all things...*

Judging by the Administrator's reaction, it seemed the Butlers had never given a Dark Star Remnant to anyone before.

The Administrator called out to me gently.

—Kid.

"Yes."

—I'm going to connect with the Butlers for a moment to verify the facts. Is that okay? If you're reluctant to contact them, I'll check separately.

I first looked at the Pudding Slime on my shoulder.

Dominic showed no reaction.

Now I think I understand.

The Rabbit Butler and the Cat Butler are likely Dominic's subordinates. They had guarded the mansion as his subordinates, waiting for their master to return.

Now that he had even recovered his signet ring, there was a high probability that Dominic recognized his subordinates.

Yet, he didn't even betray a hint of emotion, even as the butlers were being mentioned directly.

Was it because he hadn't fully recovered his essence yet, or...

Did he simply have no interest in his subordinates?

The latter seemed more likely.

I pondered for a moment.

Should I establish communication with the butlers?

I was the one who had sealed off Clean3, where the mansion was located. And instead of returning the butlers' master to them, I had turned him into a cute little slime.

The butlers had every right to blast me with their beams.

If the Administrator connected with them in this state, they would say things that would send me straight to the Space Prison. It would be better for me to join the call and beg their forgiveness.

*'At least it's a video call, so they can't shoot beams through it.'*

I needed ten billion star fragments.

I suddenly missed the Dark Star Remnant that Mr. Black Block had given me back in SL2PL. I wished I could sneak back into the already-closed SL2PL just to grab some spare change.

"You can connect the call."

—Great. Connecting now.

The Administrator moved his crystal hand, and a new screen appeared. Now I had two screens before me.

As I waited for the connection, my heart began to race.

It wasn't just fear or tension.

The anticipation of seeing beings I thought I'd never see again clearly enveloped me.

The ringing tone dragged on, suggesting the Butlers weren't answering the call. The Administrator's face contorted in frustration before he used his Trading Post Administrator privileges to force the connection.

And then, for the first time in a very long time...

—I'm busy. Hang up.

...I heard the Rabbit Butler's voice.

Though I had initiated a video call, the screen remained black, as if he hadn't activated the camera. Only his voice came through.

The Administrator, his face flushed with anger at the Rabbit Butler's response, snapped,

—Hey. You think I'm not busy?

—I'm busier.

Just as the Rabbit Butler was about to cut the line, the Administrator quickly spoke up.

—Cooperate with the Summons. Did you distribute the Dark Star Remnant?

—...Dark Star Remnant?

As the Rabbit Butler fell silent for a moment, I softly spoke.

"Butler..."

Before I could even finish my sentence, the screen flickered on. I was met with a sleek rabbit's face made of jet-black metal.

— Roasted Chestnut!

A mouth full of sharp teeth split open in a wide grin.

— It really is Roasted Chestnut! You made a communication code? How clever of you.

The rabbit's face, which had filled the screen, was suddenly pushed aside.

— Good heavens, Roasted Chestnut!

The Cat Butler gently nudged the Rabbit Butler out of the way and smiled at me.

— It has been a long time indeed, Roasted Chestnut. Unexpected reunions are always a joy. Have you been well? I have thought of you constantly, time and time again. Of course, I'm sure you've been thinking of us as well!

— Roasted Chestnut. I thought of you more.

—Oh! Come to think of it, I should be calling you Little Lamb now... but it's hard to break old habits. Would you mind if we keep calling you Roasted Chestnut as a nickname?

—Is it okay if we call you Roasted Chestnut?

I froze as the words poured out the moment the connection was established.

*'Why are they so full of affection? I had braced myself for a cold reception...'*

I glanced at Dominic, but he was still just quietly watching.

I stammered a reply to the Butlers' questions.

"Roasted Chestnut... is fine. Um, how have you all been, Butlers?"

—Very well.

The Rabbit Butler pressed his face close to the screen.

—We're building a new mansion on a new planet. You'll love it, Roasted Chestnut.

—It's incomparably more magnificent than the last one. I can't wait to show it to you.

Just then, the Administrator cut into the conversation.

—Hey, you Butlers. I connected this line to have you explain the Dark Star Remnants...!

—Explain? You mean the Dark Star Remnants we gave to Roasted Chestnut? That was just his fair compensation for working so hard. It's clean money, with no issues at all.

—We did give it to Roasted Chestnut.

The Butlers answered the Administrator curtly before immediately refocusing their attention on me.

A chilling question was directed my way.

—Roasted Chestnut. When are you coming back?

A shiver ran down my spine. This was a situation I couldn't lie my way out of.

So, I gave an ambiguous answer—neither a lie nor the truth.

"I... haven't kept my promise yet. I'm sorry."

I had promised the Butlers that I would find Master and bring him home.

I had found Master, but I hadn't brought him home, so it was true that I hadn't kept my promise.

This time, I expected the Butlers to get angry or scold me.

But their reaction was... far from what I had imagined.

After glancing at each other for a moment, the Butlers all grinned, their mouths stretching wide.

—*Hahaha!*

The sound of their loud laughter sent a shiver down my spine.

They stared at me in unison. Even though their smooth metal faces had only mouths, I could feel their gaze.

—We believe in you, Roasted Chestnut. And we know how to wait.

—It's alright.

Their words were feignedly gentle, as if they would have patted my head if they had been standing in front of me.

The Butlers filled the screen with their faces and said to me:

—We'll be waiting for the day you return to the Mansion, Roasted Chestnut!

—Roasted Chestnut. We'll be waiting.

Then, they cut the communication.

The screen vanished, leaving only me and the Administrator.

The Administrator stared, his eyes wide, before muttering in disbelief.

—What a bunch of troublemakers.

I stood still, chewing over the words the Butlers had spoken.

Just like the Administrator, they hadn't noticed Dominic perched on my shoulder.

So why weren't they asking about their master?

Was it because they trusted me that much? Or did they think they'd already found him?

Even so, since I hadn't returned to the Mansion, it seemed only natural they'd ask at least once. I couldn't fathom what the Butlers were thinking.

As my mind raced, the Administrator called my name.

—Kid. Do you happen to— He stopped himself.

He looked like he was about to say something, but then gave up.

I didn't even have time to wonder what it was. I was about to hear some shocking news.

—The verification is complete, but according to procedure, you must visit a branch in person and submit the documents to lift the account freeze.

"...What?"

—You won't be able to visit the nearest Interstellar Financial Institution... will you?

The Administrator's voice was filled with pity as he realized I had no means of space travel.

I had always been poor, but now I was a pauper stripped of all financial means.

At that moment, my violent urges toward the Pudding Slime grew even stronger.

—I'll try to find a solution for this.

"Yes..." I replied listlessly.

I expected the Administrator to end the communication, but for some reason, he kept staring at me.

—Kid. Akasha originally has regulations to protect Young gods. But you are the Master of the Planet. Do you know what that means?

Of course I didn't. As I waited for his next words, the Administrator explained in a calm voice.

—It means a lawless zone has opened up for you, kid. Gods with domains aren't protected targets.

The Administrator's hair now writhed and swayed on its own.

His diamond pupils stared straight at me.

—Be especially wary of the Old gods. They'll do anything if they find someone intriguing.

I felt I'd heard these words before. Unable to contain my curiosity, I asked, "Why is it harmful if the gods take an interest?"

— Well... to put it simply, they'd eat you. Or turn you into a toy, or keep you as a pet. And in even worse cases—

The Administrator continued, but I couldn't hear him. His voice had become grotesquely distorted.

A screeching sound, like a broken machine, poured out, mixed with the static of a radio tuned to the wrong frequency.

The Administrator apologized, seemingly unaware that I had failed to understand him.

—I'm sorry. I shouldn't have said that in front of a child.

The things I had just heard sounded bad enough, but to think there were cases far worse... I couldn't even begin to imagine what those unheard events could be.

Dominic, who had been listening quietly, let out a low chuckle. He spoke in a tone I had never heard from him, even before the Butlers.

"There is no need to worry so much. Don't you have me, Roasted Chestnut?"

He reassured me, promising that he would never let such things happen. It seemed that even for Dominic, the events were truly terrible.

The Administrator stared at me and lamented, 

—It's been so long since I've seen a Young god... Even I find this intriguing, which is why I'm truly worried...

I finally understood why Dominic had said it would be good to get to know the Administrator.

Still, the Administrator seemed like a fairly kind and conscientious alien.

I stole a glance at the Pudding Slime before cautiously asking the Administrator another question.

"What if I used the Patronage System?"

— A Patron...

The Administrator's hair flickered. He spoke with careful deliberation.

— That... depends heavily on the terms of the contract. But for a Young god, having a Patron isn't a bad choice... You need protection, after all...

After a moment of deep thought, the Administrator spoke, his brow furrowed in concentration.

— If you do sign a sponsorship contract, show it to me. I'll review it for you.

"!..."

I couldn't believe such a kind alien existed.

Having gained a dependable ally, I quickly thanked him.

"Thank you so much. I'll be sure to show it to you, Administrator."

—Alright... I'll see what I can do about the account freeze as well.

The Administrator spoke, his expression grim.

—And kid, take this.

His hair seemed to tap the air, and a rift tore open above my head, dropping an object.

It was a cubic device, a blend of turquoise and purple.

—It's a Disposable Pager. Call me if anything happens. I'll help you as much as I can.

*'The Administrator of the Galactic Trading Post is helping me?'*

The gloom that had settled over me from the account freeze lifted a little.

As I clutched the cube, the Administrator gave me one final warning.

—I'll say it again: you must beware the Old gods. Got it?

The communication cut off.

But then, a new message appeared before me.
