<br>First, let's look on the bright side.

The newly assigned Group X accommodation in the 800s... was surprisingly decent, like a proper lodging facility!

It means it actually feels worth sleeping in.

*‘The bedding is clean and the quality is good...!’*

Instead of a bunk bed, it's one bed per person. Yes, this is exactly what an ordinary adult in society would want. I nodded my head blankly.

“The rooms are usually for three people, but you get a single room.”

“Really?”

What kind of unexpected stroke of luck was this? Just as I was internally cheering...

“Yeah. The workers who used to be in your room have all gone missing.”

“…….”

“Nice, isn't it? Since it's comfortable.”

It's terrifying.

Of all things, did two of the one-third of workers who 'vanished' during the last courage test *have* to be my roommates?

*‘Wait... No, could it be that all three vanished, and I was brought into one of the empty spots?’*

That actually makes more sense. It seems those three roommates must have formed a team together for the test.

*‘Ha...’*

For the record, it seemed the explanation given to the other workers was that the missing individuals were relocated to another area because it had become difficult for them to carry out their duties.

*‘The fact that it isn't completely a lie makes it even scarier.’*

Pretending nothing is wrong while completely blocking any chance of me quitting within 30 days—the Namhae Branch is truly specialized in deceiving its workers.

Because of that, the mood to enjoy the pleasant room condition vanished in an instant.

*‘It's scary being alone too...’*

After roughly tossing my luggage onto the bed near the door and walking out, I was subjected to what they called a 'closing interview'—

—which basically consisted of me having to listen to a researcher who had called me out again in the afternoon say things along the lines of, 'I've taken such good care of you, so be grateful.'

And then, I heard this phrase:

*‘Rest Area Pass.’*

<br>

<span style="color: var(--texto-secundario);">—Originally, it is a place open only one day a week, on Sundays. However, Group X receives a weekly pass, allowing you to go there even on weekdays.</span>

<br>

To think they would give such a huge opportunity to me.

*‘Seeing how they talk about it... is there something good there?’*

First, I asked Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

Her answer was as follows:

“I've never been there either.”

“Then would you like to go together...?”

“Not interested.”

“…….”

Yes.

And so, I decided to use my free time today to make use of that Rest Area Pass!

*‘Strike while the iron is hot. Who knows, there might be a chance to fish up some useful items, so I should scout it out in advance.’*

If I pull a good item early, it just gives me more options to use when a crisis hits.

Even putting that aside, going to a brand new area of this Namhae Branch that I've never seen on the Wiki made my heart flutter in a strange way.

It felt like a locked map was finally opening up.

<br>

*-Pass verified.*

*-The Rest Area is opening. Fight on this week too! Get rich!*

<br>

With that, the closed fire door opened before my eyes, revealing the sight of the 'Rest Area'...

“…!”

It felt... exactly like a very tacky, old-fashioned rural nightlife district at night.

That was the vibe.

An arcade, a comic book cafe... places that barely scraped by in appearance were selling things at absurd service rates.

Even so, since it was practically the only form of entertainment and nightlife here, it seemed workers would naturally end up buying into it. Especially since they had cash on hand.

Following the hallway, the doors on both sides were wide open, and inside, I could see light bulbs and old storefront-style light fixtures.

*‘Did they actually try to create an atmosphere here?’*

When I first opened my eyes in this place, I was practically on the verge of death, and it was probably closing time, so I must not have noticed because most of the doors were shut.

*‘There are no... staff members.’*

Maybe it's because it's a weekday. It looks like there's not much going on, but first...

“Hey.”

“…….”

“Hey, look at this bastard, pretending he can't hear me?”

Wow.

The moment I arrive, someone is picking a fight with me again.

*‘Right. This is exactly why I said I wouldn't walk around alone...’*

With dead eyes, I turned my head. Some guy from Group X was pointing his finger right at me.

“Why, why are you doing this...?”

“Oh, why? You think I'm doing this for no reason? It's because some piece of trash walking around with Z999 attached to him just crawled in here!”

He pokes me in the neck as he lets out a bizarre shout.

“Where do you think a Z-group piece of trash is crawling into?!”

Ha...

Should I just smash his face in?

Maybe sorting out the hierarchy right now would make things easier for me in the long run.

Just as I was about to open my mouth—

“Hey, you stupid idiot! The two of us belong to Group X now, not Group Z.”

“Uh, yeah?”

“Ah, this guy doesn't look at notices and doesn't listen to rumors. All he does is whine all day about how the internet doesn't work when he's alone.”

“Our Kanghoon, go over to the massage chairs and get a good night's sleep. Okay?”

The two men bantered playfully, putting their arms around the shoulders of the guy who had been shouting, holding him back.

Then, after sending that fellow off to the 'Deep Sleep Management Center,' they turned around to look at me and spoke.

“Wow~ look who it is, Pigeon.”

Surprisingly, their identities...

Were the two hopeless Group X guys I had seen the moment I first opened my eyes here.

*‘What?’*

Why are these guys acting all friendly?

And the next second, I realized the reason.

“Hey, I heard the person you hang around with rides around in a bus.”

“…….”

“Why don't you introduce us? Huh? Our luck has turned around.”

“How much did you make over the last two days? You piece of trash, don't be scared. Since you're here, let's have a drink with your seniors and have a little chat.”

It was because they realized there was more for them to leach off of.

Since I was earning money much more stably now than when I was Z999.

But because my social standing had risen a bit, they seemed to think they could build a bit of rapport, make it easier to threaten and coax me, and extort some cash.

*‘Oh.’*

You guys walked right into it.

I was actually planning on taking care of this eventually anyway.

“That, that is...”

“Let's go, I said.”

Right here, I was just starting to form a plan to pretend to give in, tag along, get them drunk, and take care of them in a less troublesome way—

Just as I was putting that plan together.

“Uh, young man!”

“Come over here! What are you doing over there?”

*‘...?’*

When I turned my head, a group of older-looking workers was gesturing toward me.

“You said you'd help with this. Hurry up!”

Me?

But the next moment, I looked into those workers' eyes and understood.

They were signaling me to run away.

“Yes, ye... yes?”

Okay.

First, I bolted. Since there were witnesses, executing my original plan would have to wait until next time.

“Hey! Where is this bastard going again...?”

“Hey, bro, bro.”

The Group X duo tried to follow me like typical disruptive Karens throwing a tantrum,

But seeing that the group calling me over consisted of four or five people, they didn't recklessly rush in.

Instead, they spat out some choice words.

“Wow... mister, ma'am, you're living here at that age? Your lives are totally screwed up, aren't they?”

“Young man, come over here~ Yeah? Even the 4050s won't get picked for part-time jobs, but here they're going to act like bosses~”

This crossed the line.

*‘If I don't respond here, the people who helped me are going to look like fools.’*

While I was debating what to do, the people grabbed my shoulders.

“Sigh.”

“Dear me, why do people in similar situations want to act like that?”

“Ignore them, ignore them.”

“Thugs like that ought to be kicked out so they can't work at all.”

While the Group X duo stood there giggling amongst themselves, the people quickly guided me away to the opposite side.

“Are you okay? Don't get involved with those guys for no reason. Don't go even if they call you.”

“Exactly. No matter where you go, there are always strange people like that. We've made it all the way here, so we have to be careful, make our lump sum securely, and leave!”

It made me realize all over again.

*‘...There are good people here too.’*

And... it was a relief that they were lucky enough to preserve their humanity to that extent.

Looking at the four or five people wearing Group X stickers, I felt a strange sense of relief.

Of course, this hospitality was likely only possible because I had advanced to Group X and looked like a 'sane young man,' but even so, it was something to be grateful for.

“Sigh, he always walked around completely emaciated and skeletal, but he turns out to be such a fine young man. Yes, indeed.”

“Making a living these days is no ordinary struggle, after all.”

“Still, since you're young, you're better off than us. When you leave, don't waste your money carelessly, make sure to save it, and learn a trade. Okay?”

“...Thank you.”

...If possible, I hoped these people would endure safely in Group X, get their money, and leave successfully.

“There actually are a few things here. Everything is expensive so mostly we just look around, but sometimes there are things actually worth buying.”

Afterward, I toured the Rest Area together with these middle-aged workers.

Most items were priced absurdly high, as if trying to bleed the workers dry of their money, and occasionally, items that looked like bait products were priced at about the regular retail price outside.

The workers I was walking with would keep an eye out and buy those things occasionally, but for the most part, it seemed they held back to save their money.

“Fifty thousand won for just two hours in a comic book cafe?! They're pure thieves.”

“Mr. Kim, didn't you say you wanted to read some comics?”

“Ah, no. Gosh, I'm not a kid, why would I hang my life on comics! Let's move on quickly.”

And after pondering for a bit, I quietly purchased a bottle of very strong vodka from the tavern.

The alcohol content was practically at the level of hand sanitizer.

*‘Alcohol with this high a percentage might actually come in handy during an anomaly event.’*

Remembering how useful that beer was against the wooden doll, it was hard not to buy it.

The price was completely insane, looking as if a tenfold liquor tax had been slapped onto it, but I swallowed my tears and endured it.

*‘Am I just... not fated to save money?’*

Aside from that, most things were purely for entertainment.

“When the weekend comes around, there's always a fool or two who shows up and throws their money away like water. Good grief...”

Expensive western liquor, premium snacks not available at the regular commissary, and domestic chilled meats for grilling were also on display.

Even though it was an unmanned shop, no one even dreamed of stealing; it seemed everyone had already witnessed what kind of price someone paid after getting caught.

*‘Doesn't look like I'll be spending money here...’*

But then.

There was one spot that made my feet come to a halt.
