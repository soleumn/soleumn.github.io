<br>Daydream Co., Ltd. 

To think I’d be thrown into this garbage company a second time. 

*‘Save me.’* 

Moreover, this wasn't even the headquarters where I used to work. 

Namhae Branch...? 

What even is that? 

There wasn't a single entry about this on the Wiki!

Granted, after returning home, I had successfully managed to recall the things I couldn't remember about the Wiki before. 

Even though I had distanced myself from &lt;Darkness Exploration Records> , that much was certain. 

Just like how I suddenly remembered entries that I previously couldn't post on the Wiki—such as the Sekwang Special City or the Disaster Management Bureau.

*‘Perhaps back then, as I assimilated into and became buried within the world of ghost stories, I gradually started to forget them...’* 

I had hypothesized. Or maybe it was because of the situation regarding that 'Good Friend' I was dealing with.

Whichever it was, it left behind a bizarre, unsettling aftertaste. But regardless, it meant I could now remember the Wikis I had read without any sense of cognitive dissonance.

But the Namhae Branch? 

*‘What on earth is that? I don't know...’* 

Of course, I knew that Daydream Co., Ltd. possessed regional branches. 

...In a sort of, *‘Ah, so that's how the setting goes,’* kind of way.

Daydream Co., Ltd. consisted of the Seoul headquarters, various regional branches scattered throughout the provinces, and a few specialized overseas divisions... 

For a pharmaceutical company that massive, it would actually be stranger if it *didn't* have any regional branches and only had a headquarters.

Besides, it would be even weirder if ghost stories only occurred exclusively around the Seoul area. Therefore, it was a setting closer to natural probability.

But here's the thing. 

*‘...The specific details for each regional branch were never established.’* 

If the lore had gotten that meticulously detailed, it wouldn't be a ghost story Wiki universe anymore—it would be a corporate whistleblowing exposé.

At the end of the day, it was a Wiki meant for writing ghost stories, and for Daydream Co., Ltd., the most important aspect was gathering dream essence. Even so, actually seeing a branch with my own eyes like this was fascinating, but…

A heavy, chilling sensation—unique to when things are going horribly wrong—swept over me. 

Furthermore. 

*‘...Isn't the environment here way too different from the headquarters?’*

For the record, I was still sprawled out on the floor in front of that decrepit ritual altar. 

This was a series of thoughts that crossed my mind in the span of about six seconds after looking at the 'Daydream Co., Ltd. – Namhae Branch' nameplate. 

While soaked completely in water, with absolutely zero strength left in my body.

I'm going crazy.

"Ugh..." 

What exactly is wrong with my body right now? 

Letting out a sound that was practically a death groan, I barely managed to push my body up.

As I did, the pig's head, the rice cakes, and the dried fruit on the altar fully entered my field of vision. 

...For some strange reason, it caught my eye. 

"......"

*‘Am I... incredibly starved right now?’* 

For a split second, the thought crossed my mind that my complete lack of bodily strength might be due to extreme hunger... 

*‘An altar... Touching something belonging to a ghost story company within a ghost story world is a straight-up suicide mission.’* 

That was a realistic judgment born out of pure dread.

<br>

Leaving behind the altar with its shriveled food, I shuffled out the door on trembling legs. *‘I’m gonna die, seriously...’*

Beyond the door lay a dilapidated hallway. 

It was completely sealed off, carrying a strange atmosphere that felt like a subterranean tunnel.

*‘It almost feels like a closed-down 1980s mine shaft...’*

<br>

*— Religious activity time will conclude shortly. All workers, please check your assigned workload for this week.*

*— When you are in a rush, Dream Money, Dream Life!*

<br>

Moreover, what was up with this intercom announcement? 

*‘Workers? Workload?’*

 It felt somewhat similar to what I used to hear back when I was on the Security Team, but it also felt bizarrely like an advertisement and lacked any sort of sincerity. What should I call it... *‘It feels like a loan shark operation.’* 

Private moneylenders, to be exact. 

"Ugh..."

Then, down at the far end of the long hallway, I spotted a group of people trudging along. Perhaps the person who poured water over my head was among them.

*‘Is it... that way?’*

Reflexively, I followed behind them while maintaining a bit of distance. Fortunately, the few people who glanced back at me didn't seem to care and just went right back to talking amongst themselves.

​Everyone was dressed exactly the same.

*‘A short-sleeved t-shirt and pants with an elastic waistband...’*

The muddy, yellowish-brown color made them look like prison uniforms, or maybe indoor uniforms for some kind of mass communal living facility.

​And there was one thing that particularly caught my eye.

*‘...A sticker?’*

Without exception, everyone had a red sticker affixed somewhere on their body.

*‘What does it say?’*

Squinting my eyes, I was barely able to read it.

I have one too.

Carefully taking my hand off the sticker, I managed to force my mouth open.

*‘Are they monitoring us?’*

However, the voice just now sounded entirely too ordinary to bear any traces of anomalous contamination.

It didn't give off the vibe of a Security Team either.

*‘...Hmm.’*

At any rate, I had my destination.

*‘They told me to go to the designated break room.’*

​I started walking again, and as I kept following the crowd, the indoor layout shifted slightly.

It was a corridor that vividly brought to mind the hallway of a rundown, old apartment building.

<br>

*—The rest area is being closed. Fighting for this week too! Get rich!*

*​Thud.*

The moment I stepped into that apartment-style corridor section, a heavy fire door slammed shut behind me.

The mechanical beep of an electronic door lock engaging echoed through the space.

*‘Confinement...?’*

"Fuck..."

"I fucking hate work."

​Others complained, but as if they were used to it, they scanned their stickers on the door frames of the hallway one by one and entered their rooms. 

I looked at the door the last group had entered. 

Steel plates with small barred windows, like prison doors, with nameplates attached.

​With a brief BEEP sound, the door opened.

​Immediately, the environment inside caught my breath.

*​‘Wow.’*

​It was a small space, barely the size of a single normal room. 

Cobwebs, mold, and exposed concrete flooring everywhere. On one side, the dirt wall was fully exposed, dug out to create a primitive hole that served as a toilet, which reeked with a foul stench. 

Several cheap foam mats, torn and spilling cotton everywhere, were scattered around.

​...That was it. No fridge, obviously. Not even a chair.

*​‘What is this?’*

​It was questionable if even prisons back in the day, or actual dungeons, were this poor. 

Furthermore, if that nameplate meant this space was intended to house Workers Z990 through Z999...

*‘This room is supposed to hold ten people?’*

​It wasn't even like some ancient POW camp. This was definitely not a ‘break room’ in any sense. 

This was an abandoned prison site. 

I looked around the dilapidated interior with wary eyes. 

And when my gaze landed on one of the mattresses in the pitch-black corner...

​...I noticed a silhouette that was different from the others.

"Huh?"

​Someone was lying on that mattress.

​It was a corpse.

​"...!!"

​I was about to bolt out of the room, but having no strength left, it only resulted in me stumbling backward.

*‘Crazy.’*

What on earth was this now?

​I wanted to scream, I wanted to cry, but that wouldn't help me assess the situation. Swallowing my internal screams, I examined the corpse...

*​‘Ha.’*

​Staring blankly at the air with eyes wide open was a man who looked to be my age. Like other workers, he was wearing Earth-toned clothes.

A chocolate wrapper was peeking out of his pocket. Until that detail, it could be considered a somewhat ordinary sight, but...

*​‘Condition... his condition...’*

​A dark ash-water mixture was flowing and dried up from all the orifices of his face. His right hand was clutching his chest near his heart.

​It appears he died from a heart attack.

"Wow, please..."

It’s unusual. Anyone could tell this is a ghost story-style death.

I fell into a heavy silence as I read up to the text written on the sticker attached to the person's exposed wrist: 'Classification: Worker, Z993.'

It seems they were likely one of the nine roommates of my current body...

“Ah…”

This is truly driving me crazy.

And what’s driving me even crazier is that my body is slowly reaching its limit.

*‘...I’m dizzy.’*

Now I know for sure.

I felt like I was going to lose my mind from hunger.

...And my eyes began to steal glances at the corpse.

“……Ha.”

A scream naturally wanted to burst out, but there was no other choice.

I ransacked whatever was inside the corpse’s pockets.

“Ugh.”

Foil-wrapped chocolates, candy, and... a single notebook.

Unable to hold back any longer, I first stripped the wrapper off the chocolate and shoved it into my mouth.

“Phew.”

Insane.

The sugar rushes up all the way from the depths of my stomach to my head.

Everything becomes goosebump-inducingly clear and sharp. My body, having instantly regained its vitality, moves quickly as I take big, successive bites of the chocolate.

*‘Information.’*

I opened the notebook that the corpse had been carrying.

*​Thud.*

​I closed the notebook.

Cold sweat gathered between my fingers.

“Ha…”

​This is, anyone could see, a record of a civilian dying after repeated deployment into ghost stories without knowing anything about them.

*‘Do they... do this in Daydream Co., Ltd. branches?’*

​Luring people with no family ties using high pay, trapping them, and just tossing them indiscriminately into ghost stories? 

Even forcing them to work without being able to quit?

*‘Did such a setting... exist?’*

​When trying to recall if there was mention of the branches, nothing specifically came to mind, but a strange sense of déjà vu washed over me.

​If it is a sort of déjà vu...

*‘Come to think of it, early Darkness Exploration Records Daydream Co., Ltd. was sort of like this vibe.’*

Early Wiki days, when security chief J3 from Daydream was the B-team leader. 

Deployment into ghost stories mercilessly, bare-handed, without any item or equipment support. 

It tasted of how things used to be standard back then...

Formerly, that was standard...

​"......"

*​‘Wait a minute.’*

*‘The timeline... doesn't match.’*

The fact that it was written in the early stages of the &lt;Darkness Exploration Records> Wiki doesn't necessarily mean it's a story from the chronological past. 

In the case of the Disaster Management Bureau, there's some of that due to the history of the agents, but Daydream is different.

*'In the first place, there were many ghost stories from the 2020s perspective in the early Daydream ghost stories.' *

That couldn't be a story from the time of the Wolf Leader of B-team whom I met.  

If so, those nameless, numbered Daydream employees in the ghost stories who were dropping like flies without a single safety measure, treated as mere consumables...

*‘They were from the branches.’*

​Unlike the headquarters, the regional branches had preserved the raw, unforgiving flavor of the early Daydream Wiki lore...!

*‘While the headquarters hires full-time employees, designs standard manuals, and systematically harvests dream fragments, this place operates a cheap sweatshop designed to grind human lives into profit...?’*

​The absolute plausibility of it left me completely speechless.

I felt like I was going to lose my mind.

To think up such a twisted, hyper-capitalistic spin on a ghost story concept…

Are you saying I'm the sole survivor of this room?

What kind of insane survival rate is this?

Is this why it’s called the Wrap-up Team?

I never thought the day would come when I'd actually look back fondly and cry out that things were better when I was a full-time employee at the headquarters.

​Compared to this, the fatal drawback of not being able to let my guard down because of a dangerous roommate back then felt like absolute paradise. 

At least over there, the roommate was actually alive. And the place to sleep was a state-of-the-art officetel right next to a subway station...!

Using the darkened hallway outside as a makeshift backdrop, I tried to look at my reflection through the glass pane of the barred window on the door.

A haggard, hollow face stared back at me. It seemed to be me, but it was too dark to be completely sure.

*​‘No, wait...’*

Why did I pick up a Nameless Radiant Cult ID card in my dream, only to wake up here in a Daydream regional branch?

*‘Granted, waking up in the Nameless Radiant Cult would probably mean dying on the spot, so this might be better, but still...’*

​Curses threatened to spill past my lips, but as the sudden spike of blood sugar from the chocolate wrapper began to fade, I just let out a heavy sigh instead.

​Next, I checked the sticker on my neck.

“Cannot leave before completing a full month of service, huh…”

​If I calculated the remaining time based on the notebook's metric of "500 million won for 100 days of work"...

The remaining time meant I had 14 days left.

"Exactly two weeks."

​In other words, I just had to endure and make it to the 30-day mark to legally get out of this insane place. 

Once I did, I’d have both the freedom to get my tattoos and a massive lump sum of cash.

​I took a deep breath.

*‘...I know how to go home now.’*

​There was a profound sense of reassurance that came from simply knowing that the place I needed to return to actually existed.

I also knew exactly how I ended up here and what state my body was in.

​Yes.

*‘...I understand myself well now.’*

​Even now, as the memories of the countless trials and tribulations I had faced in this ghost story world flashed through my mind, a natural wave of anxiety and sighs followed. 

But at the end of all those experiences, I also knew who had helped me, and exactly what I had managed to pull off...

​"......"

​Several faces drifted across my mind.

Because of them, I could confidently declare my situation in a grounded, stable state of mind:

*‘If anything, I'm in a much better position than before.’*

​If I could just get past these 14 days, it meant I could do whatever I wanted without being bound or confined.

And...

*‘...I can look for the others, too.’*

​The acquaintances I originally knew and interacted with in this world.

*‘I wonder how everyone is doing.’*

​Having barely made it back home only to be dragged right back into this urban legend, an unexpected sliver of anticipation almost welled up inside me—but the moment my eyes locked with the corpse in the corner, that warmth instantly evaporated.

​Please!

*‘First, I just have to survive and escape...!’*
