"I'm supposed to draw from this, right?"

Deputy Jin Nasol reached her hand deep into the box without a moment's hesitation and selected one.

*'Let's see, 8th place was the participation prize.'*

Jackpot.

**Ebony Prayer Beads, Silver Ornaments**

The corners of Deputy Jin Nasol's mouth twitched upward as she picked up the black prayer beads, which featured two subtly gleaming silver ornaments woven in at intersecting points.

"Not bad. I guess 3rd place is on the higher tier?"

"Yes. Congratulations."

*'As expected of an elite team member, is her luck good too...?'*

"Then I will submit mine as well."

*Riiiiiiip.*

"…!"

The poor-quality fabric barely tore under the weak grip of my hand. Stamped across the fabric like a flyer were the three individual stamps I had collected.

"Just in case, since I didn't have the original flyer, I stamped them here. ...Here you go."

I placed it into the security guard's palm.

Rather than throwing or pushing it away, the guard's hand froze for a brief moment as if paralyzed, before disappearing back into the window.

"…You certainly know how to exploit a situation."

"Don't mention it."

Watching the prize box emerge from the window once again, I felt a slight thrill.

One prize per person. A truly wonderful rule...

"Thank you. I'll take whatever comes out of this one."

"Sure."

*'Perfect.'*

Now that the ownership of the prizes had been sorted out, let's think fast.

Are there any relevant exploration cases I can use as a reference...?

*'Hmm.'*

First off, considering that duplicate prizes have occurred before, the prize drawing must be the type that 'restores to its initial state' every time this anomaly triggers.

There was no indication that people won based on their specific abilities or that anyone used a trick.

However, looking at it from an overall perspective... in terms of general trends, that is.

*'It did seem like the people who felt they had a bit more leeway before escaping drew the higher ranks.'*

For instance, the civilians in the exploration record who previously drew 3rd place belonged to a mountaineering club.

Meaning they were the type of people capable of moving through this environment while being relatively less panicked and less terrified.

Besides them…

"Did you figure something out?"

*'The events at this training center don't exactly seem elaborate or grand.'*

Because from the very beginning, it looked like they were just trying to maintain outward appearances.

If so, let's assume the prize drawing setup was put together with a similar level of effort.

The training center staff would have made this prize box and then thrown the drawing balls inside.

How would they have put them in?

*'The probability is highest that they put them in numerical order.'*

Quite naturally.

In order from number 1 through number 9.

*'Then the smaller numbers would have gone in first, meaning they'd end up buried at the very bottom.'*

In conclusion, the higher the rank, the higher the probability it's sitting at the absolute bottom of the box...!

*'However, in this kind of situation, it's incredibly difficult to actively reach all the way down to the bottom of a prize box to make a selection.'*

Explorers who don't understand the situation are driven by fear, while explorers who *do* understand are driven by the pressure of the light constraint.

Either way, they are consumed by the urgency to grab a prize quickly and get out.

*'The same applies even if someone tries to choose very carefully.'*

In case they are aiming for an item, picking up different balls wouldn't show any noticeable weight difference, making it hard to distinguish them. The more they rummage through, the more the original pattern breaks, turning it purely into a game of luck.

Ultimately, the high ranks are drawn by people like this:

People who, with a slight sense of excitement or anticipation, shove their hand deep into the box and are capable of searching the very bottom to pull one out.

*'This perfectly aligns with the established trend.'*

People who felt they had a bit more leeway before escaping.

Therefore.

*'I'm going to pull a proper one on my very first try...!'*

I shoved my hand straight in.

Reaching as deep as physically possible into this cursed prize box, I swept my hand toward the very corner of the bottom surface to grab a ball that was buried away.

And then, I pulled it out.

*'Please.'*
