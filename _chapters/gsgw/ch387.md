<br>A normal voice echoes through the corridor where white hoses stretch out eerily across the floor.

From inside the jar I am holding.

"By the way, where are we going right now?"

"...The Off-Duty Zone."

*Splat.*

"And because the situation is dangerous, we're trying to go as quietly as possible."

"Oh dear, I'm being a nuisance. I'll keep my mouth shut!"

However, my mental state made me think that it might have been better to keep listening to them speak.

Or perhaps it's because I keep thinking about it.

What is inside this jar I'm carrying in front of me....

*'Stop thinking about it.'*

I grit my teeth and moved forward.

Since my body was in poor condition, carrying a crock heavier than a person made it feel like my shoulders were going to pop out, but my nerves were so frayed that I didn't even notice.

Only the destination.

I focus only on that. And then....

*'...We've arrived.'*

*'It's dawn, surely it won't be deactivated.'*

…….

<br>

*-Pass confirmed.*

*-The Off-Duty Zone is now open. Good luck this week! May you become rich!*

*'Phew.'*

I almost collapsed from the sense of relief.

I hurriedly entered the Off-Duty Zone and scanned the floor first.

*'It's not here.'*

There are no hoses. Good! Now I just need to find the door....

***Gurgle***

<br>

I looked back.

Beyond the open isolation door of the Off-Duty Zone, a mass of white strings was twitching.

And then ....

<br>

***Zip!***

Deputy Jin Nasoll is pointing at me. No, at the jar I'm holding.

***Gurgle***

*Beep.*

<br>

In the meantime, fortunately, the door began to close.

Keeping my body tense so I could run again at any moment, I stared at the hose through the narrowing gap of the door.

...The white string fixed inside the bottle stopped moving instead of gurgling.

Does it only eat...liquid made of melted people?

A new white string extending from the thick hose stem, approaching the narrow gap of the door....

*'......!!'*

The moment that string touched the door.

<br>

*Thump.*

<br>

The isolation door locked completely.

But I couldn't be relieved.

"It came in through the bottom of the door and got caught in the gap."

I looked back at Deputy Jin Nasol.

"It will definitely stretch its way in."

"Move, now."

Without a word of protest, I immediately ran.

The first place I went to was the door that had been in the religion room.

<br>

However.

"It won't open."

The doorknob wouldn't budge, as if it were frozen.

Rattle, rattle, crack! Even though Deputy Jin Nasol's hand—hidden by the box—must have had veins popping out from the force she used to nearly break it, it didn't work.

It was the same when I put down the jar and tried.

There was no keyhole, nor even an authentication device.

How on earth do the authorized personnel get in?

"To open this by force, we'd need someone like your previous Section Chief."

“........”

It meant that if we were to use physical force, human strength wouldn't be enough. Damn it!

What do I do? I'm in a bare-bodied state with no usable supernatural gimmicks right now!

*'In that case ....'*

Ah.

I remembered the doorknob part in my pocket.

...What if I find a door where I can actually install a doorknob?

"Oh dear, oh dear.... "

"...!"

Just then, the voice from the jar I was holding was heard again.

After taking a deep breath, I spoke to it.

"Excuse me, could I ask you a favor?"

"Uh, what, what should I do, young man?"

"Do you remember the doors in the Off-Duty Zone that say 'Authorized Personnel Only'?"

"Uh ...yes! There was one in the religion room! Are we there now?"

"Yes. We are there now. Do you remember any other doors? Specifically, any that didn't have a doorknob?"

"Uh......."

And for some reason.

"I don't know. Uh, the lottery shop? Or next to the lottery shop? Yes, I think there was one there. Yeah."

The words coming from the 'worker inside the jar' seemed to be getting more slurred....

"...Yes"

Swallowing hard, I picked up the jar again and walked out of the religion room.

...It felt like my shoulder was going to pop out.

"It's really disgusting."

Back in the main corridor of the Off-Duty Zone.

White threads were leaking through the gap of the isolation door at the end.

The way they leaked out in clumps reminded me of ghost stories about parasitic worms like Gordian worms.

“........”

'The isolation door...I don't think it'll hold for long.'

Anxiety tightened around my throat.

"The lottery shop is that way!"

"Right."

We moved faster.

As we ran, Deputy Jin Nasol, who was running beside me, ducked into a meat restaurant as we passed it to grab sharp objects like kitchen knives and scissors.

*'Ah.'*

I also quickly found some yellow tape inside and made a disposable shoulder strap. I did this to distribute the weight, like when moving watermelons, so I could hang the crock in front of me.

*'This makes my hands a bit more free.'*

Earlier, when I held the jar with one hand to grab the liqueur bottle, my shoulder almost popped out.

However, Deputy Jin Nasol narrowed her eyes as she looked at me, whose both hands were still occupied by the weight of the crock.

"Think again about whether you're not just taking a loss here."

“........”

I clearly understood that she meant my survival rate would be higher if I just dumped it and took other items.

*'...I don't know how the situation will change.'*

I'll keep it as long as I can handle it.

I nodded, pretending not to know, and started walking again.

But I faced a rather unfavorable situation.

"Hey. There's nothing."

...No matter how much I searched, every door with a 'Authorized Personnel Only' sign had a doorknob properly attached.

*'Damn it.'*

I kept glancing back at the door, wanting to gauge the remaining time.

The area around the door was now almost completely covered in white, looking like paint. Numerous white ends, like tentacles, were stretching out and slithering.

*'Damn it!'*

"Um, Elder! Could there be anywhere else?"

"I don't know...Oh, but I can't see anything. Why can't I see? Ah, I'm in the crock....That's why I can't see. That's it."

“........”

"Young man, can you see me?"

This is driving me crazy.

"Hey, is this right? Either find a way to pick the lock or find a ventilation shaft a person can fit through, change direction now."

"That...just a moment."

"I've put up with enough of you hugging that thing and listening to nonsense."

“........”

Deputy Jin Nasol spoke irritably, and without even looking back at me, she once again pulled the handle of the 'Authorized Personnel Only' door as if trying to rip it off. She knew it wouldn't open, but it seemed to be a mix of venting her frustration and trying one last time.

But then......

"Assistant Manager."

"Shut up and choose."

"No, it's not that."

I swallowed hard.

"Look at your hand."

“........”

Deputy Jin Nasol looked down at her hand gripping the doorknob.

Fingers wearing two layers of black latex gloves brought from the infirmary.

“........”

*Squish.*

<br>

“........”

“........”

<br>

She was infected.

<br>

"Wait,"

I felt my blood run cold.

*'Where....'*

How did she get infected?

She was wrapped in boxes like that, and she had the same protection as I did, so why only her?

Wait, could it be...that I just haven't noticed yet?

‘...!!’

***Crack.***

<br>

“........”

"Was that...a sound from the isolation door?"

Instead.

"Hey."

A butcher's knife was held out in front of me.

"Wait,"

"Stop saying 'wait' and just do it. Why are you being so stupid?"

"Use a painkiller first!"

She could faint from shock!

***Crack.***

<br>

"Can't you see the situation? Can't you hear the sound? Do it!"

Fuck.

"I said do it!"

I finally swung the butcher's knife downward.

<br>

*Thud.*

*Thud, thud!*

With a short, blunt sound, Deputy Jin Nasol's limp ring finger fell away along with the glove.

"...!!"

Deputy Jin Nasol clutched her hand, her body trembling as she grit her teeth.

I held her convulsing body, which seemed unable to bear the pain, and managed to do something like hemostasis with the remaining glove.

*'Quickly, quickly!'*

Jin Nasol's finger lying on the floor.

At first glance, it looked normal. But.

<br>

*...Splat.*

A viscous liquid-like substance gushed out from the severed end of the finger, and soon the skin tore as well, and everything was swallowed into the liquid....

Squish, squish, a yellowish mucus with a reddish tint.

Just like the inside of this jar I am currently hugging and carrying....

"...!"

A human voice is heard.

"What was that sound just now?"

"...Ah, I dropped something."
