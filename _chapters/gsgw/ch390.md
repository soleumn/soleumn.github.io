"Yes, yes, Branch Manager! When will the containment be... Oh, the warning alarm has been deactivated? Yes, yes, the Branch Security Personnel are confirming the situation and re-entering... Ah, then Headquarters won't need to send a security team!"

The Researcher, answering the lab phone with a somewhat subservient tone, kept repeating "Yes" in agreement...

"...Yes? Not a security team... but a real Headquarters official is coming... you say?"

He had just heard some news.

"Inspection... I see. Yes."

"Ah, they're just coming to look around because of the incident, so we'll just... put in some effort and do our best. Yes, yes. Don't worry. We'll make sure our branch doesn't get a bad reputation. Yes! Goodbye, Branch Manager!"

*Slam.*

"Fuck!"

The researcher slammed the phone down on the floor, then snatched it back up, panting with rage.

"Those bastards at Headquarters..."

Whenever I'm on the verge of making a breakthrough, they always find a way to swoop in and take a cut!

They wait for something to happen, then stroll down here with an arrogant air, talking about an ‘inspection’ and trying to leech off the Points, money, and Darkness Manuals the branch office had managed to scrape together. It was nauseating to watch.

In severe cases, they'd even try to hijack my work entirely.

*'Absolutely not!'*

The Researcher gritted his teeth.

*'I'm the one who's going to research this... no matter what!'*

It wasn't out of a genuine passion for research, but rather a raw greed for Points. Still, his resolve was firm.

He had to hide and protect this golden goose at all costs.

If he played his cards right, he might hit a Points jackpot a few more times!

*'First, I have to hide him.'*

If that guy had actually survived...

No, he must have survived!

He had to have survived!

...And yet, the Researcher couldn't help but run a delusional "happy circuit" in his mind.

How could any worker possibly survive a Grade A Darkness Uncontained Incident?

*‘It's impossible.’*

He's not some god of luck, after all.

But even as he told himself this was no different from dreaming of a shitcoin jackpot, he couldn't stop planning his next moves based on that very assumption... The Researcher swallowed hard, already scheming for his future gains.

Then, a moment later, an email popped up on his monitor. He checked the details of the "Headquarters Personnel Visiting."

"Section Chief Baek Saheon from Team H..."
