<br>A normal voice echoes through the corridor where white hoses stretch out eerily across the floor.

Chased by the hoses, we finally found and opened the door to the 'Staff Only Zone' within the Off-Duty Zone.

“…….”

“…….”

Inside was dark.

The only light came from the faint green glow of emergency lamps. Materials were piled up haphazardly, giving off a strong impression that the place was under construction….

“…It really is a bathhouse.”

Indeed.

We walked in somewhere between the locker room and the baths.

I swallowed hard as I looked at a worn-out, unmaintained sign that read 'Namhae Hot Springs,' and moved my feet forward.

My heart was still pounding.

*'The hoses….'*

For now, they weren't coming inside this door.

Whew.

“Let’s head further in for now. If we can find an emergency exit here, we might be able to get outside.”

“…….”

Jin Nasol walked with heavy steps.

As I passed under an emergency light, I caught a glimpse of a notice.

*'Whatever, I'm just glad to be away from those damn white hoses for now….'*

“…You mean how people are lured by the melted body fluids.”

“Yeah. Why do you always ask back things that are obvious!”

“…….”

“No, seriously, why are you always smirking? It's annoying. We're stuck in this gutter, so what's so funny that you're smiling while eating and smiling at a bed? What the hell is wrong with you?”

A torrent of verbal abuse poured out.

*'No….'*

It seemed that the pain and the bleak situation were triggering impulsive behavior, just like back at the spring.

It was quite scary… but stay calm, don't panic.

“I just… um.”

I organized my thoughts and spoke slowly.

“I'm trying to enjoy the good times while I can and make an effort to relax. That way, I don't break down even in difficult situations.”

“…….”

Yes.

“That's been my experience.”

Several people and events flashed through my mind.

The people who had made me continue to find affection for this world, which was objectively horrific, and the people who had seen me off.

“Are you saying you're trying to rationalize things by finding some comfort even in a trashy situation?”

“No. I'm just acknowledging that even if the situation is a mess, there are moments that are actually good.”

I finished speaking cautiously.

Mixing in a bit of Deputy Jin Nasol's vocabulary.

“I believe that is the healthiest and most efficient way.”

“…….”

The woman continued to walk in silence.

I quietly matched her pace. The sound of footsteps echoed through the abandoned bathhouse facility….

“I talked nonsense.”

“…!”

“It wasn't necessary in this situation, and it was only a distraction.”

“It wasn't that bad.”

But she didn't seem to care.

“I can't speak like I used to. You know, right? Because I've become trash.”

Jin Nasol shook more painkillers into her mouth.

This time, I couldn't hold back and asked.

“…Um, are you suffering from constant pain?”

“Yeah.”

After a brief silence.

Jin Nasol tapped her temple with the hand holding the painkillers.

“A piece of iron got embedded in my brain during an accident. In the thalamus—the area that constantly stimulates pain sensation.”

……!!

“Is that why you joined the company? To get it removed?”

“I already did.”

“……What?”

“I removed it, but this goddamn pain won't go away.”

*Crackle*. Veins popped in Jin Nasol's hand as she crushed the empty painkiller packaging.

“Phantom limb pain or whatever; even the doctors don't know the cause. No matter what I do, it's only temporary. When you keep shoving painkillers in and reach a point where they're no different from a placebo due to tolerance, a person goes crazy.”

“That's…the reason you entered Daydream.”

Because the pain wouldn't go away through realistic methods.

“Yeah. The Dream Essence painkillers produced in the Daydream are quite decent. If I take those, I can live comfortably for about fifteen days.”

Her voice softened slightly, as if she were imagining it.

“…Um, how many points does that cost?”

“Five thousand points.”

I almost screamed.

One Wish Ticket for every hundred times you endure the pain?

*'Is that really a painkiller?'*

It felt like it was some other potion that Deputy Jin Nasol was simply using as a painkiller, but no, that wasn't important. Conversely….

…It meant that using painkillers a hundred times would squander enough points for a Wish Ticket.

*'If she was spending points like that.'*

While working in Team A, since she was in Director Ho's line, she would have been able to pull in and process high-efficiency tasks, creating a structure where her points wouldn't go into deficit.

*'But if Director Ho disappeared in that situation….'*

“…Um, how did the Team A Leader handle the situation?”

“He went missing.”

“…!”

“The Darkness wasn't strong enough for that… it was C-rank at most.”

Deputy Jin Nasol's voice was indifferent, but the content was not.

“But he went missing because of a single error in the Manual. Well, it's that kind of company.”

“…….”

Was it… really just a disappearance?

No matter how much of 'that kind of company' it was, the circumstances were suspicious, but regardless of the inside story, the result for Deputy Jin Nasol would have been the same.

All her existing connections had vanished.

*'If that's the case.'*

If the Team A Leader, who was at least a middle manager and someone she could have aligned with, disappeared, and she found herself floating within the company while her points ran dry….

“Yeah. Even though the situation was bad, there were no painkillers like those, so I overspent points and ended up stuck here.”

“…….”

“That's what you were curious about, isn't it? Why I ended up like this.”

I couldn't affirm or deny it.

It was true that I had asked even though I knew the past three years Deputy Jin Nasol had experienced wouldn't have been pleasant.

However.

“Then… you're currently holding on with ordinary painkillers.”

Isn't it amazing that she's kept her sanity to this extent after going through all that?

“Does this look like 'holding on' to you? I'm just enduring it.”

“Once we get out of here, let's look for pain-related items. Like the painkiller item I gave you on the train.”

“Sure.”

Deputy Jin Nasol's voice softened a bit.

“That's enough useless talk.”

Because there was a bigger problem.

“There isn't one. An emergency exit.”

“…….”

We had been moving around the bathhouse while having this conversation, but no matter how much I looked, there was nothing that could be called an exit.

We were just endlessly circling inside the eerie bathhouse facility with its broken tiles.

“…Since the interior is so poorly maintained to begin with, the emergency exit or other hidden exits might be buried under materials.”

“So, from now on, we're just going to rummage through everything to find a possible exit?”

“I'll try to think of a way.”

“Why think? You have one.”

“Pardon?”

“Your item.”

…!

I pulled out the glasses case from my pocket.

The first-place prize from the courage test.

“Usually, the effect of an item follows the concept. And glasses are….”

“Conceptually, they are objects that show things that are invisible.”

“You're quick.”

It was exactly as she said.

There was a high probability that these glasses had the function of 'showing something that is normally invisible.'

*'…If we stay in here, those white strings might eventually cover everything and reach us.'*

If the Staff Only Zone had been completely safe, the staff wouldn't have fled in the first place.

*'In that case, while we have some time, trying to use them here….'*

It was a gamble worth taking.

“…If a problem occurs, you must remove the glasses immediately.”

“Yeah.”

I took a deep breath and took out the black-rimmed glasses from the case labeled 'Clear Water Optician.'

And the moment I put them on.

“…!”

“What is it?”

I gasped.
