<br>The morning after meeting Baek Saheon in the restroom.

“Ma, Manager!”

Once again, during roll call, I witness Manager Baek Saheon crossing the workers' corridor without hesitation.

It seemed he was making up for the roll call check he missed yesterday.

And naturally, this time, he didn't even acknowledge me.

“Th-that's the end! There's nothing here!”

“Yes, I know.”

The Manager from headquarters strode across the corridor, scanning the lined-up workers and the interior of the breakroom, passing naturally right in front of me.

And then, in front of me—the very last room—he naturally turned around and paused for a moment.

“…….”

“…….”

Baek Saheon looked back and forth between me and the room, and I had the feeling that his eyebrows twitched behind the mask.

I know. Hey, this place really isn't fit for a human to live in, is it?

Moreover, because my face had swollen overnight, I was in a state that was truly hard to recognize.

*‘I have no cash because of the bills….’*

I'm worse than a beggar.

It was just as I was thinking that.

Baek Saheon, likely standing with his back to the CCTV… slightly moved his lips.

*‘…!’*

He showed me only the shape of his mouth.

*‘Don't eat it.’*

“…….”

What does that mean?

“Yes! This is the end of the breakroom, and, well, that's it. Haha… there was no parasite damage up to the work zone.”

I watched reflexively as the person in charge guided Manager Baek Saheon away, thinking frantically.

Could it be that my cheeks were so swollen that it looked like I was eating something?

Of course, it wasn't such an absurd meaning.

I would soon deduce exactly what he meant by intuition.

“Let's go eat~”

It was after roll call, when a simple breakfast distribution took place.

Under the pretext of a 'special meal to comfort hearts startled by parasite damage,' the same meal composition was distributed to every team.

And it was quite substantial and good.

‘The rice balls are glistening….’

I received white rice balls filled with tuna mayo and stir-fried kimchi, seaweed, and a plum drink.

However, next to the foil, there was one more thing that wasn't food.

*‘…Medicine?’*

The transparent pink pill looked like a liquid softgel capsule.

I held it up to the light. There was a slight scratch-like mark near the center….

<br>

**<center>[This is parasite medicine. Please take it regularly for the next few days. We are providing this as a preventative measure, just in case.]</center>**

<br>

One pill was distributed to every single person.

“Phew, they're finally acting rationally.”

“Do we just take it with water?”

The workers whispered, but feeling it was an appropriate measure, they were all popping them into their mouths.

<br>

**<center>[Yes, yes. Just don't chew them. The effectiveness disappears if it touches saliva.]</center>**

<br>

“…….”

If the effectiveness disappeared upon touching saliva, it probably wouldn't have been developed as an oral medication in the first place.

I realized instinctively.

This is what Baek Saheon meant when he said not to eat it.

“…….”

Casually, I pretended to swallow the pill with the drink, but instead let it slip into my palm.

Then, I tucked it into the deepest part of my pocket.

<br>

***

<br>

After finishing the morning work and returning, I observed the workers.

…Nothing seemed wrong, but the feeling continued to be bad.

“…….”

<br>

**<center>[Z999, come get your daily wage.]</center>**

<br>

However, while receiving the money, an unexpected joy occurred.

“Are, are you… returning it?”

Surprisingly, as the branch Security Team employee gave me my daily wage, they returned all the money they had taken yesterday for the bills.

This was while having me sign a bill that stated, ‘Due to deficiencies found in the cause of the claim, the corresponding bill is nullified.’

*‘Oh.’*

I scribbled a signature that looked like initials but meant nothing—intentionally messy—and took back the bundle of cash.

It felt like free money, so I felt quite good….

*‘Since I did something crazy to the Team X bastards yesterday, they won't come to rip me off today.’*

I'll keep it safe. I'll need money to spend once I get out.

And since there was no additional work in the afternoon, I was able to return to my room early.

Not that there's much to relax with even after entering the room.

“Hah.”

I suppressed a sigh looking at the state of the room.

The mattress now smelled of rot, and the poor environment of the narrow room had not improved at all.

I swallowed a groan while looking at the unfinished dirt wall on one side, the filth pit serving as a toilet, and the dead bugs and cobwebs.

*‘The Rest Zone opens tomorrow, right.’*

It was supposed to be today, but was it pushed back a day for maintenance? It would be more relaxing to go there….

But since I have things to do, it's actually a good thing that I have the afternoon free.

Because I decided to properly search this room.

*‘Let's do it now.’*

I even waited a day on purpose to look less suspicious.

From the backpack in the corner, I took out various small items, like a child comforting themselves while looking at their treasures.

This was an action done very consciously of the CCTV.

Then, finally, I grabbed the glasses case.

*‘Alright.’*

The moment I was about to take them out….

<br>

*Thump.*

<br>

The breakroom door opened behind me.

“…!!”

Who is it.

Startled, I pretended to stand up while stuffing the glasses case back into the backpack. As I hurriedly turned my head….

Manager Baek Saheon was standing in the open doorway.

“…….”

“…….”

The breakroom door closed again.

“Wh-who… Ah, the person here for the inspection… Wh-why are you here…….”

“I turned off all the CCTVs.”

“What do you want?”

“Ha!”

Baek Saheon snorted with an incredulous expression and stepped into the room without hesitation in his dress shoes.

“You certainly know how to act.”

“Yeah. Thanks.”

“…….”

“Anyway, being able to turn off the CCTVs—a headquarters Manager really is different. But will it be okay?”

“Are you comparing me to a worker right now? Don't you think you're being a bit arrogant?”

“Because if the reason is vague, you might be suspected.”

“I told you my situation is different from yours, you worker.”

A crooked smile spread across Manager Baek Saheon's face.

“I'm currently checking several branch worker breakrooms as random inspections. Did you think I was wasting my time because of you? Your self-consciousness is excessive.”

Sure, whatever.

Well… since he's so confident, fine.

“Anyway, for about 5… no, 10 minutes, staying in this breakroom won't arouse suspicion.”

And Baek Saheon shrugged.

“Forget those useless worries. …You got the money, right?”

Hmm?

“I mean the nullified bill.”

“Of course I meant that. Who do you think did that for you?”

Wow.

This time, I was genuinely impressed.

*‘Thank you.’*

I really needed the money…!

Just then.

“Hand it over.”

“Hmm?”

“Naturally, half of that money is mine, so have it ready.”

“I spent it all, so I don't have any….”

“...!”

“Just kidding.”

“…….”

“But I do hate the idea of giving it to you.”

Baek Saheon clenched and unclenched his fist!

“Look at you, risking your life so pathetically over pocket change. And you're even joking about money? Even if a civil servant embezzles…….”

“Well. I'm not a civil servant anymore, so wouldn't it be fine?”

“…….”

The other party suddenly went quiet. I used that gap to organize my backpack.

The other's next words came out abruptly.

“Were you originally an agent who infiltrated Daydream?”

“No.”

I said calmly.

“It's exactly as you knew. A spy.”

“…….”

For some reason, the next silence was longer.

*‘…Awkward.’*

Only after I finished organizing the backpack did I continue speaking.

“What you said this morning—you meant don't take the nutritional supplement, right?”

“…It's a relief you still have that kind of intuition. Correct. I told you not to take the supplement.”

…….

“Because it's a Darkness.”

As expected.

The workers were thrown into a new Darkness, completely unaware.

The Darkness we were to be deployed into this week was mediated by those pills.

I took out the pill that was still in my pocket.

Baek Saheon also looked at it.

“Why are you touching that with your hands? Throw it away.”

“What kind of Darkness is it?”

“That part was censored in the documents. They were desperate not to let it be known, but what can a researcher working in a branch like this do. I'll find out soon.”

“…Don't do anything too conspicuous.”

Between the bills and everything else, I wondered if he was going too far, but Manager Baek Saheon snorted.

“Didn't you ask me to smuggle you out safely in a week?”

I don't recall asking to that extent…?

“Now you're pretending to be considerate, but it's unnecessary. Who do you think I am, still a Supervisor?”

“Still, I'm just saying be careful. I can be worried.”

“…….”

Baek Saheon let out a suppressed voice.

“…I really don't know what you're thinking.”

That's what I want to say.

“……Did you infiltrate to investigate this Namhae Branch?”

“Not exactly, but since I'm trapped, I'm looking into it now. It's suspicious in many ways.”

I pondered for a moment, then completely took the glasses case out of the backpack and opened it again.

It was best to take advantage of the opportunity while the CCTV was off.

“For starters, this room is a bit suspicious.”

“The room?”

“Yeah.”

I looked around the room.

“So I want to see what it looked like before it became a worker's lodging.”

And I took the glasses out of the open case and held them up.

A strange light flashed in Baek Saheon's eyes.

“…'What it looked like before'? Don't tell me those glasses have an effect that shows the past?”

I nodded.

“That's the item you mentioned yesterday, right? The first-place prize.”

“Correct.”

A greedy glint passed through one of Manager Baek Saheon's eyes.

But unlike before, that glint was quickly suppressed, and he became nonchalant again.

Though his words remained the same.

“Well, enjoy it while you can. Because on the day we leave here, you'll have to politely hand it over as the price for your escape.”

He can dream.

In the place where the dirty and poor scenery of room Z999 had been….

Candidate number 1.
