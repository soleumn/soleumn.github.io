## [Just Let Them Go]

: A ghost story appearing in the &lt;<span class="wiki-red">Darkness Exploration Records</span>>, the identification code for <span class="wiki-red">Daydream Inc.</span> is Qterw-C-2554.

The entity is primarily discovered buried beneath the soil in close proximity to bodies of water.

Its size distribution ranges from 12cm to 37cm. The material is non-standardized—consisting of cotton, wood, brass, parchment, or ■■—but every variation takes the shape of a <span class="wiki-red">human form</span> possessing both hands and feet. It can be verified as an active instance of Qterw-C-2554 by the presence of an accompanying <span class="wiki-red">talisman.</span>

The talisman is severely damaged and scribbled upon. After restoring and interpreting the text, it was confirmed to be a <span class="wiki-red">ritual chant</span> for a <span class="wiki-red">"water-sending" ceremony</span> for those who died by drowning. 

(The four pillars of destiny for the drowned person are written, but the name is erased, and <span class="wiki-red">no matching death record can be found.</span>) 

Anyone who comes into contact with the doll begins to experience hallucinations of ghosts, waterfronts, and drowning from that day forward. If the phenomenon is left unchecked, the subject <span class="wiki-red">dies</span> within 7 days. 

Unless it is a suicide, the <span class="wiki-red">autopsy</span> result is determined to be drowning.
