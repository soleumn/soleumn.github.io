<br>In front of the restroom sink.

Out of nowhere, I found myself face-to-face with a headquarters employee who had come to inspect the Namhae Branch. But….

It was Baek Saheon.

*‘…?!’*

Why the hell are you here?

*‘Wait, the person coming from the headquarters was you…?’*

If I had been standing, I probably would have slipped while gripping the sink.

I was considerably flustered.

And it seemed the other party felt the same.

Behind the mask of the guy dressed in a sharp black suit, one eye looked as if it had seen something impossible.

It was like the feeling of witnessing a pig you ate last year suddenly walking back into the livestock pen perfectly fine.

*‘But he actually recognized me.’*

I didn't think it would be easy to match a drenched day laborer with a bruised face to Supervisor Kim Soleum.

Especially since the last time he saw me was three years ago.

Hmm.

I sat up abruptly.

“…!!”

And I stared intently at Baek Saheon.

I couldn't read the expression on the face behind the goat mask, but he seemed to flinch for some reason.

Oh?

“Did you notice?”

“What?”

“You noticed.”

“……!”

At that moment.

“Chief!”

Another person rushed into the restroom.

A figure covered from head to toe in a private protective suit with a white lab coat thrown over it; they were relatively short in stature.

*‘Is that the person in charge of the announcements?’*

It seemed they had no choice but to enter the worker zone to guide the headquarters auditor.

“The contamination here has been completely remov…?!”

The branch official, seeing me looking like I'd been beaten and water-tortured, became visibly agitated, even through the protective suit.

“U-um….”

“I told you not to open your mouth during roll call! This is a penalty point, not a warning—I'm actually going to impose it!”

The moment I opened my mouth, the researcher practically had a seizure.

“Sorry….”

Instead of saying, ‘Roll call hasn't started yet and we're in a restroom, what are you talking about,’ I quickly mumbled and backed away.

“…!”

Then, the official blocked ‘Manager Baek Saheon’ and began to speak, trying to smooth things over.

“Ah! Workers with the highest numbers are usually in that kind of state. You know how it is, haha….”

“I don't.”

“…Pardon?”

“I said I don't know.”

“…!”

The single eye behind the black goat mask stared intently at the person blocking his path.

“I suppose the person in charge can tell at a glance whether it's contamination or not.”

“……Well,”

The headquarters employee in the black suit looked down, scanning the official in the clumsy protective gear.

Then, he asked back as if truly puzzled.

“But why are you at the branch?”

“…….”

The Manager uncrossed his arms.

“I'll write you a recommendation. With that great discernment, you should join the Field Exploration Team.”

“P-pardon…?”

“For now, I'll call you over to my team.”

A gloved hand grabbed the branch official's shoulder and pressed down hard.

“A supervisor happened to die three days ago, so there's a vacancy.”

“……Well,”

“It was recorded as a disappearance, but they definitely died. I went in with them and watched them die.”

“…….”

“Just in case you were doubting it.”

“I-it's fine! No, that couldn't be. Doubting? I believe everything! But I feel that being the research lead for the branch suits my temperament….”

“Ah, I see. Since this lifestyle suits you so well, it must be so comfortable that you've made a mistake in judgment. Right?”

“…….”

“Right?”

“O-of course.”

*…Thud.*

Only after hearing that did Baek Saheon release the official's shoulder.

He dusted off his hand and crossed his arms again.

“Well, don't worry. That's why I'm here for the inspection.”

“…….”

“Roll call is unnecessary. I can just collect testimonies directly from the workers.”

Then he gestured with his chin.

“Wait outside.”

“Pardon?”

“I said get out.”

“No….”

“If you have complaints about the audit procedure, you can tell the headquarters directly. Or I can tell the Branch Manager myself. That the person in charge seems dissatisfied with the audit.”

“N-no, that's not it!”

The official grit his teeth, tore his gaze away from me, and hurried out of the restroom with a forced smile.

“Haha, I'll be waiting outside!”

<br>

*Bang!*

<br>

The sound of the restroom door closing echoed loudly, and silence returned to the space in front of the sinks.

Baek Saheon's mouth slowly opened.

“You….”

“You?”

“…!”

This guy is flinching like it's a spinal reflex.

Hmm. It still works well.

And the other party seemed to have reached a conclusion at the same time.

“…What the.”

“…….”

“It is you.”

I saw the single eye of Baek Saheon tremble behind the mask as he scanned my appearance.

Then, Baek Saheon approached me and asked in a low voice.

“You, after eating up my points… why the hell are you stuck here…! And why do you look like that…?”

“I-I'm scared….”

“…!?”

“I-I don't know what you're talking about….”

As I said that, I gestured toward the restroom.

Then I mouthed the words.

Eavesdropping.

“……Hah.”

At that, Baek Saheon stood up, took a small earplug-like object from his suit pocket, went to the restroom door, and shoved it into the keyhole.

“It's a sound-blocking item.”

“…….”

“Satisfied?”

“Yeah.”

I smiled and stood up.

“…!”

“It's been a while.”

Baek Saheon's pupils trembled for a moment, but he quickly became calm as if trying to hide his agitation.

I continued.

“So… has it been three years?”

“…….”

“Have you been well?”

Baek Saheon scanned my state.

“Doesn't look like you have.”

“…….”

“Did another worker do that to you?”

“Pretty much.”

I admitted it readily.

Baek Saheon sneered, though I couldn't tell if he was mocking me or angry.

“Are you living like that to avoid drawing attention? I guess you got beaten less since I wasn't around.”

That's not it.

But Baek Saheon seemed to gain some energy from his own words, returning to the attitude of a superior on an audit.

Then he asked me.

“Now, you are a day laborer and I am the Manager who came for the inspection. Do you think that attitude is appropriate right now?”

Huh?

“Of course it is.”

“~!”

“I'm quitting in a week anyway.”

Baek Saheon's expression turned strange.

“If you're quitting, why are you here……. You disappeared for three years; did you gamble or something?”

“I don't know either. I woke up and I was here.”

“…….”

“Anyway, my mind is set on quitting. Though I'm not sure if they'll let me go quietly.”

“What, do they kill employees who resign here?”

“I don't know about that. I just don't think they'll let me go in the first place.”

I grinned.

“Because I pulled a B-rank Dream Essence with my bare body last week.”

“…?!”

“And in this containment failure incident, I managed to survive after contacting the Darkness perfectly fine.”

“No,”

“I'm saying I've drawn some attention.”

I shook my wet hair to tidy it.

“They might not let me go.”

“……Hah.”

Baek Saheon crossed his arms, and a mocking smile appeared at the corners of his mouth.

“So, you're saying you need my help.”

“…….”

I didn't deny it.

“Wow, three years is a long time. Right? While I became a Manager, you've been stuck here as a day laborer. I went through the trouble of saving you, and you're not even embarrassed.”

Then he tilted his head.

“But… is this the attitude you use when asking for a favor?”

“…….”

“How are you going to intimidate me now? From what I see, you have nothing.”

“Oh, I have equipment items.”

“…?”

“They're great. It was the first-place prize. I won it as soon as I entered the Darkness.”

“Liar.”

“I'm serious.”

I shrugged and straightened my work clothes.

“And I have no intention of intimidating you. There's no need for that anymore.”

“…….”

“I was just glad to see you.”

The other party flinched.

I looked at Baek Saheon calmly.

“Why are you still working at Daydream?”

“…….”

“Did something else happen back in your hometown—,”

“It's not that.”

The answer came as if cutting me off.

Then Baek Saheon brushed his swept-back hair and spoke with a sigh.

“Forget it. Anyway… just wait. It's easy for me to find out if you can leave.”

…!

“Thanks.”

“You answer as if it's free.”

Baek Saheon frowned.

“You said you have items. You can pay me back with those. If we count the interest, there's more than one or two things you owe me….”

His voice trailed off.

“Well… anyway.”

“…….”

This guy seemed to have noticed that I was that agent from Jisan Village.

His recent comment confirmed it.

*‘Even though he's sure, he seems unable to figure out how to treat me.’*

I couldn't tell if he wanted to mention it, show it, or pretend he didn't know.

For now, I changed the subject.

“I'm not the only one here; Deputy Butterfly is here too. We're cooperating.”

“Deputy Butterfly? Ah, that person from Team A.”

Baek Saheon spoke indifferently, but he avoided my gaze.

“I heard she went to a branch after incurring a debt; I guess this is the place.”

“…….”

After some thought, I spoke.

“Then what happened to the others?”

“Who?”

“The people who were on Director Ho's project team.”

“…….”

After a silence.

“I'm not sure about that. No one's acted like they knew me since the project was dismantled.”

“Don't lie. You've even been promoted; you're telling me you lived without hearing company rumors?”

“You've lost your intuition. I guess life was too easy for you?”

“What?”

“I'm changing the subject because there's nothing good to hear.”

…A bad feeling ran down my spine.

Baek Saheon whipped his head around.

“But if you really want to hear it… well. Most of them aren't at the company. They either resigned or went missing….”

What?

“Who resigned and who went missing?”

“I told you I don't know because I tried not to get involved!”

“…!”

“Why? You have such a disappointed look in your eyes. What, were you going to say something like I should have helped them?”

I don't know why he's speaking so defensively. He almost seems desperate.

“It's not that. I was just worried about the others and wanted to find out how they were.”

“……Hah.”

Baek Saheon raised his hand and pressed his eyelid.

“You really were this kind of person….”

…….

“…More people just resigned than went missing. For now, Manager Lizard is still with the company. Though he's not a Manager anymore.”

“Then,”

“It's time, so I'll be going. I told you to wait. Don't do anything strange. It's not like you're in a position to save anyone anyway.”

And before I could answer, he turned quickly as if ending the conversation and tried to leave the restroom, but then he stopped and asked.

“Do you still have the paper I gave you?”

…….

“No, I mean, if you have it, it'll be easier to contact you….”

“I don't have it now.”

“……Well, alright then.”

*Thud.*

<br>

With the sound of the restroom door closing, Manager Baek Saheon disappeared.

Once again, I was left alone in the restroom.

*‘Hah.’*

I don't know what's happening all of a sudden.

*‘I mean, why the hell did Baek Saheon pop up here?’*

It felt so surreal that I almost washed my face again to snap out of it, despite having been water-tortured.

To meet ‘Manager’ Baek Saheon, who had been promoted rapidly like in the &lt;Darkness Exploration Records>.

And to encounter him in this state.

*‘Still… I don't think he'll report my location to the higher-ups.’*

It was actually a bit good to see him.

He was definitely more friendly than I expected, right?

Of course, I also suspect he wouldn't choose to report me and get entangled in the mess for the sake of his own self-preservation… but regardless of that.

“Hmm.”

Anyway, it's good.

If I can get help from a head office Manager, it will definitely help me get out of the branch.

Of course, the worry for the other people from Director Ho's project team surged up, but….

I took a deep breath.

*‘Baek Saheon isn't wrong.’*

Finding anyone's whereabouts is something I can only do after I get out.

What I can do now is….

*‘Focus on getting out of here safely.’*

Alright.

I made up my mind to do that.

…But I really don't know how I should treat Manager Baek Saheon.

What on earth does he want?
