*'I'm glad I grabbed a backpack from the commissary yesterday!'*

*SLAM!*

I shoved everything from the bathroom—including towels and a single water bottle—into the pack and sprinted into the living room.

"........"

"........"

"Oh, for heaven’s sake."

"Oh, you heard the noise and came out startled too?"

***[EMERGENCY ALERT!]***

"Ah, it's fine. It's fine. This happens sometimes."

"Seriously, if it's malfunctioning, they should fix it... waking up people who have to work in the morning. What is this?"

...Malfunction?

"Wow, uh, how did you know it was a malfunction...?"

"Huh? Didn't the supervisor tell you on the first day? The facilities here are old, so back when this was a coal mine, the evacuation alarms used to go off whenever they felt like it. He said to ignore them..."

Ah.

"Why are you acting like you've never heard that before?"

Dammit.

"I-I don't know. I guess it wasn't audible in the Team Z room...."

"Ah."

Glancing at the Z999 sticker still stuck on my neck due to the rapid promotion, the worker nodded as if roughly convinced.

They seemed to let it slide, thinking i must have been out of my mind and missed it. Phew.

"Anyway, the facilities here are so old that false alarms like that go off sometimes. You can just stay in your room."

"Sigh, there goes my sleep."

"Same thing happened last time. They let us sleep in the morning and made us work in the afternoon anyway. Probably be the same this time."

Casual conversation drifted through the gaps of the blaring siren.

I spotted Assistant Manager Jin Nasol sitting near the entrance with an extremely sullen expression and slunk over to sit near her.

"Um, by any chance... did you know about the malfunction?"

"No."

"......Would you like some water?"

"No."

Hmm. I guess she's cranky from lack of sleep....

Just as I thought that, a quiet, low voice reached my ears.

"It doesn't make sense."

"...Pardon?"

"Would this company, of all places, leave an emergency alarm malfunction unattended?"

"...!"

I finally realized the source of the lingering unease I'd been feeling.

Without any ulterior motive?

***[EMERGENCY ALERT!]***

<br>

<br>

<br>

"Good grief."

"Bothering us again. Honestly."

And as one person sauntered toward the door.

"Wait a moment."

"Excuse me, you usually don't talk at all... but you're quite well-spoken now...."

<br>

—Pardon?

<br>

<br>

"Hey! Why are you saying something to provoke them! You're going to make things difficult-urk!"

<br>

—I don't speak if I don't have to. I'm telling you to open the door because I need to inspect it now.

<br>

"Ah, I see...."

<br>

—Yes. If you delay any further, penalty points-

<br>

"But we don't really need to open it for you, do we...?"

<br>

"You open the automatic doors with your device from the outside. You opened the door that way this morning too."

"So why are you... asking us to open it for you now?"

—Ha.

........

<br>

***BEEEEEEEP!***

<br>

*'...!'*

The door opened automatically, just like it had in the morning.

Beyond it was....

"Stand in a single line. We will conduct a head count."

A person really was standing outside, wearing the security team uniform.

........ 

Huh?

"Sigh."

"Why the hell are you causing trouble, you piece of shit."

"Seriously, ever since he moved in here, he's been so full of himself. We can't stand him, sir."

The workers in the same room each threw a comment at me while groveling to the security employee.

Then they pushed me aside first.

"Hey, get to the back!"

"S-Sorry!"

Blatant disregard and contempt were revived.

I felt them shoving me back or sneakily hitting me—the Z999 sticker still on my neck—as a way to vent their annoyance, but I didn't retaliate.

I just stood in the back obediently.

Next to me, Jin Nasol was quietly observing the situation.

"........"

*'What is it?'*

There was definitely a sense of dissonance, yet the situation was proceeding peacefully.

He looked like a real security guard, too.

I scrutinized the security employee as he approached, checking the people starting from the front of the line.

There was nothing strange about his clothing or behavior... but.

*'Ah.'*

One thing did catch my eye.

'*...A string?'*

From the back of the security team's jumpsuit, a white string was hanging out, as if it hadn't been tied properly.

It was in a position where the person couldn't easily see it, so it was a plausible wardrobe malfunction. However....

'*......Isn't it too long?'*

That string stretched on and on.

Further and further.

Past the entrance, through the door, and into the hallway.

"........"

The head count continued.

"Is there any personnel who stutters unnaturally, or hiding away and refusing to come out? Anyone trying not to show their face?"

"Huh? Oh, there's no one like that!"

"Ah, that brat over there does stutter, hahaha!"

Thanks to the person turning around to point at me, my vision cleared, and the string became even more visible.

........

"Did you see it?"

"Yes."

What I had thought was a string....

Was pulsating.

<br>

***Throb.***

<br>

*'Shit.'*

I broke into a cold sweat and started to slip out of the line-

"Ah- that brat again!"

Dammit.

"Stand in a single line. The head count is not over."

"...Mr. Security Guard."

I caught my breath.

"What is that string attached to your back?"

"Pardon?"

The security guard's head turned.

"That thing on your waist."

"......."

"It's pulsating."

The security guard's head snapped back to the front.

"Stop talking nonsense and stand in line."

"Right! Hurry up and get in li-"

"Sta-ta-ta-tand-in-line-head-count-not-sta-ta-tand-in-line-count."

"......Pardon?"

"Li-li-line-ah-aaaah-me-why-aaaah-this-is-weiiiiiird."

<br>

He collapsed.

<br>

*Splat.*

As if the inside of the jumpsuit had melted into something mushy, the security guard's form crumbled and fell downward in a viscous heap.

"Wh-Whaaaaaack!"

"Hieek!"

<br>

**Throb.**

<br>

The string now looked like a hose.

Thick enough to burst, it throbbed and sucked greedily as if swallowing something.

The security guard's volume decreased.

<br>

***Throb.***

<br>

Leaving only the security uniform like a hollow shell, the contents were sucked inside.

As if being drunk.

<br>

**...*Gulp.***

<br>

We're screwed.

"Get out!"

I sprinted out of the breakroom with Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

"W-wait for us!"

"Aaaaah! What is that!"

"It's weird it's weird it's weird."

As we dashed down the hallway, I could see the rooms opened on either side, roughly every other door. In every room, I saw head counts being performed, people convulsing, and those who noticed the anomaly stumbling backward.

"Hey! Watch out!"

"A-Aaaaaah!"

Screams and chaos.

And all of it....

<br>

***Throb.***

<br>

Was connected to those long, white strings stretching across the hallway....

*'This is insane.'*

What the hell is this?

My stomach churned. I ran desperately, avoiding the strings.

The paranormal phenomenon was spreading rapidly from person to person.

Is it an infection? An outbreak?

*'For now, stay away from people...!'*

Thinking quickly, a place that was currently unpopulated but sealed would be.......

There!!

"The infirmary!"

As soon as we rounded the corner, I tore open the door to the infirmary and burst inside. Assistant Manager Jin Nasol followed, and as we went to close the door....

"P-Please...!"

"Agh!"

A few workers from our room desperately forced their way in behind us.

*'Dammit!'*

It looked like they had followed us in their escape!

"This...!"

"It's faster to just let them in!"

We were in the same room anyway; if they were infected, there was a high probability we were already finished too!

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol furrowed her brows in annoyance, but she grabbed the two workers who were already halfway through the door and violently yanked them inside.

At the same time, I succeeded in slamming the door shut.

<br>

THUD!

<br>

"...Phew."

Cold sweat poured down my face.

...Hell had been temporarily blocked out.

"Wh-what was that just now??"

"There was something weird attached to their backs!"

“Uh, Mommy.......”

I hurriedly checked the floor. It was clean. No strings, not even a thin thread was visible.

*'Ha.'*

...For now, we were safe.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol, who had immediately started raiding the infirmary's painkiller supply, swallowed two capsules at once and approached me.

"Infectious Darkness?"

"Infectious Darkness, yes."

There was no other explanation for it.

Ghost stories about zombies, vampires, or parasites flashed through my mind.

"Source of infection?"

"Unconfirmed."

That was the biggest problem.

We didn't know the exact cause of why people were turning out like that.

At the very least, what I could guess was....

"That white string earlier, didn't it look like a hose or a straw?"

"It did."

I knew it.

"Do you think that's the source of infection?"

"I couldn't be certain."

"Me neither."

It looked like the 'result of infection.'

If I were to imagine the cause based on that result.

Something that sucks them dry....

"It... reminds me of a mosquito."

"A mosquito?"

"Yes. When a mosquito sucks blood, it injects proteins from its saliva to make the blood easier to consume."

It causes itching, but that's not the important part; it's the method itself.

"So, you think it's making humans easier to consume?"

"...Something like that."

Wow, the direction of this conversation is brutal and nauseating....

But this was the only way to talk with Assistant Manager Jin Nasol. And it was true that such a framework helped in thinking rationally.

*'A mosquito... huh.'*

"If that's the case, defense should be our priority for now."

I tore open all the boxes and bandages in the infirmary and wrapped them around our limbs.

Basic quarantine.

To prevent infection, the best thing to do is not to expose bare skin.

"Wh-what are you doing?"

"Looking at the situation, I think it's better to cover our bodies. You two should do the same. Hurry!"

However, the two people who followed us in only half-heartedly mimicked us before giving up.

Apparently, a crazy thug and his lackey duo didn't inspire much confidence. Dammit!

"Do it more thoroughly!"

I could only hope they'd realize the gravity of the situation soon. No, actually, I didn't even know how to escape or what this thing was.

*'Are there any hints?'*

While busily plastering boxes onto my body, my brain—now accustomed to anomalies—rewound the afterimages of what I'd seen so far.

What did I see on the way here? Was there anything unusual?

*'...No.'*

Wait a minute.

There was definitely something.

Not in what I saw, but in what I heard.

An earthenware jar.
